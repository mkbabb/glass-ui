# Aristotelian proportion

Use these axes to challenge the rendered whole. They are review language, not a fleet
of mechanical gates.

1. **Proportion.** Radii, spacing, padding, type, and region balance should relate to a
   small number of named anchors. √φ and φ are useful when they clarify hierarchy; they
   are not excuses to preserve awkward geometry or proliferate tokens.
2. **Motion.** Spatial change carries spring weight, interruption, anticipation, and
   follow-through where earned. Effects use restrained easing. Reduced motion preserves
   state and meaning while removing unnecessary travel.
3. **Chromatic identity.** One principal procedural or spectral gesture may carry the
   composition. Surrounding glass stays quiet, legible, and materially coherent; color
   and decoration do not compete for attention.

Review each changed surface twice: first as the feature in isolation, then in its routed
product and tranche context. Ask whether the original specification remains the simplest
correct design, whether implementation friction exposed a better owner or abstraction,
and whether any duplicate element can be removed. Confirm action affordances where
behavior would otherwise be illegible. Native Browser inspection and interaction are the
binding visual evidence.
