## The 3-axis acceptance verdict

Each enrolled gestalt surface (the roster at `docs/tranches/BG/audit/reflect/
bg-gestalt-roster.md`) owes a per-surface VERDICT on three design-language axes, filed in
the edict-verdict ledger `docs/tranches/BG/audit/reflect/bg-edict-verdict-ledger.md`:

1. **√φ-proportion** — the surface's radii, spacing, and padding step off a named √φ
   anchor (nothing arbitrary). The model is the `--card-pad` ladder (`Card.vue`): ONE
   `--card-pad-inline` anchor drives the sqrt-φ (1.272) / φ (1.618) / φ² (2.618) rungs,
   the block-over-inline lift, the footer cadence, the intra-header gap. The verdict reads
   whether the surface's proportion is √φ-consistent, not arbitrary rem.
2. **animation-laws** — the surface's motion driver carries the animation laws
   (anticipation · follow-through · squash-and-stretch · the weight the liquid-glass
   identity reads), compositor-only on the per-spring settle clock. The verdict reads
   whether the driver has weight, not a flat linear tween. See `docs/design/motion-canon.md`.
3. **technicolor-cartoon-punch** — the surface reads as warm cartoon-technicolor punch, not
   a flat washed grey. The verdict reads the composited-whole warm-cream identity (the
   `proof:warm-identity` dominant-hue band is the mechanical companion). See the CLAUDE.md
   §"The warm-chroma floor" / §"The dark register as a luminous transmissive material".

The verdict tokens are `PASS` · `FAIL` · `PENDING`. A surface's row is COMPLETE when all
three axes carry a token; the review is COMPLETE when every enrolled surface has a
complete row. `edict-verdict-present` locks that completeness, born-RED until the ledger is
filled — it does not itself judge PASS/FAIL (the FABLE review does, below).

self-judged builder assertion. This is the same non-authoring fence PE-FABLE (GA-3) draws

against the committed dual-engine captures, not the pixels the building agent judged. The

verdict against the committed captures; the completeness fence is enforceable in both

## The light proportion-census — the model, not a fan-out

The aristotelian √φ census is LIGHT: it names ONE model anchor (`--card-pad`) that the
review reads, NOT an off-ledger-rem gate fan-out over every declared length in `src/`.
Minting a mechanical gate per proportion axis is the ceremony disease GA-9 forbids — the
census is acceptance LANGUAGE the review speaks, never a gate fan-out. The `--card-pad`
ladder is the model universalized: a surface whose proportion the review reads as arbitrary
gets a `FAIL` on the √φ axis, re-worked toward the named ladder, and re-filed — the paint
loop, not a red build. `edict-verdict-present` asserts the model exists on disk (the ladder
constants live in `Card.vue`) so the canon reference is not a phantom, and asserts the
review roster is complete against the enrolled surface set. It asserts nothing about any
other file's rem values.
