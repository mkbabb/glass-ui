1. **G1, the Slider's default fill paints near-black on a coloured card.** `slider-range glass-liquid-fill` computes `oklab(0.216 … / .88)` on value's gradient Direction slider. Fix 8's O-167 cure (a calm dark fill) may cover it. Measure it on value's geometry, a fill over a saturated card in light and dark, and say whether it is cured. If not, cure it so the fill reads as the card's own calm ink, never near-black on a light or coloured surface.
2. **G3, the focus-ring utility draws nothing at `:focus-visible`.** It computes `outline: none` with a 0 px transparent shadow, seen on value's `/browse` palette-card disclosure trigger. This is an accessibility failure (WCAG 2.4.7). Cure it at the utility, so every consumer of `.focus-ring` paints a visible ring. Gate: every focusable atom in the demo, under keyboard focus in both engines, paints a ring whose contrast is at least 3:1 against its surround. The O-201 rule (never clipped) holds as well.

Reproduce fourier's geometry from its records, measure it on v10.1.4 and on the branch, and cure it at the root if it reproduces.
