**(a) the morph animates `inline-size`, not a compositor transform — per-frame relayout (C3).** `layers.css:53-61` lands the live scalar on the layout-axis box size directly: `--dock-morph-size: calc(--dock-morph-from + (--dock-morph-to - --dock-morph-from) * --dock-morph-t)` then `inline-size: var(--dock-morph-size)` (vertical: `block-size`, `:70-80`). Animating `inline-size` per frame forces a relayout EVERY morph frame — the CDP Layout track is busy through the whole window (the user verbatim, C3: "the dock transitions are too slow and laggy"). The morph must paint as a COMPOSITOR transform (the box reserves its SETTLED footprint, the in-flight frames `transform: scale()` between the from/to footprints) so the CDP Layout track stays FLAT through the morph — a GPU-composited size simulation, not a per-frame layout solve.

**(b) the reveal seats at a partial-size interim — the blank-icon dock (C3, D1-F1).** Because the box grows `inline-size` frame-by-frame, the active pane is REVEALED through the clip aperture AS the box grows (`layers.css:52`, "the active pane is REVEALED through the clip aperture as the box grows"). For the morph's leading frames the dock paints at a partial size with its controls clipped/half-painted — "a blank icon partially sized dock in the interim" (the user, C3). The reveal must seat at the SETTLED target geometry (the box reserves its end footprint; the content reads complete; the morph is a compositor transform OVER the settled footprint, never a content reveal through a growing layout box).

**(c) PRM has NO synchronous seat — a P0 TERMINAL failure (C3 / WS1.3).** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the spring's `respectReducedMotion: true` jumps `--dock-morph-t` 0→1 in one frame (`dockMorphContext.ts:205-206`, `useLayerTransition.ts:141-142`). But the SPAN — `--dock-morph-from`/`--dock-morph-to` — is seated by the rAF MEASURE-DEFER (`dockMorphContext.ts:347-370`: pin at `from`, ONE rAF later measure the true `to`). The scalar snaps SYNCHRONOUSLY; the span seats a rAF LATER. So a collapse→expand under PRM paints the box at the pinned collapsed `from` while the scalar already reads 1 — TODAY's measured terminal failure: the PRM dock collapses to a **10×74 blank sliver with every control (Start/Next/Settings) painting OUTSIDE the box** (no control anywhere reachable). Under PRM there is NO morph to play, so the geometry must seat SYNCHRONOUSLY at the target — measure the true `to` and write it in the SAME synchronous step as the scalar jump, no rAF defer, no morph window. The PRM path must be a synchronous seat, not a zero-duration animation of an unseated span.

**(d) `DockLayerGroup` does NOT self-reserve its peak-layer block-size — the consumer guesses (DOCKLAYER-RESERVE / G4).** A multi-layer `DockLayerGroup` swaps panes of differing intrinsic block-size; the group never reserves its PEAK-layer footprint, so a consumer hosting it hand-rolls a reserve guess. The live consumer (speedtest `Dock.vue:225` `min-block-size: var(--dock-host-reserve)` + `:489-497` the `calc(max(4rem·scale, touch) + 0.5rem·scale·2 + 2px)` literal) over-reserves ~70% (WS1.3) — a guess the consumer should never have to make. `registerGroup` already exists (`DockLayerGroup.vue:93` `morphHost.registerGroup({ containerEl, activeLayer, axis })`) — the group has the descriptor registry; it just never measures + reserves the peak. The group must self-reserve its peak-layer block-size on its OWN root so the consumer `--dock-host-reserve` interim RETIRES (consume-and-delete at speedtest W7).

**(e) `--dock-local-scale` does NOT thread from a consumer scope — the 3rd recurrence of the substitution-vs-inheritance trap (DOCKSCALE).** `--dock-scale` (`offsets-sizing.css:264`) and `--dock-icon-glyph` are DECLARED only on `:root` and are NOT `@property`-registered, so their `calc(--ui-scale * --dock-local-scale)` is substituted ONCE at `:root` against the `:root` identity `--dock-local-scale: 1` (`:265`). A consumer override of `--dock-local-scale` on a dock SCOPE is inherited but never re-evaluated — the dock keeps `--dock-scale: calc(1 * 1)` and stays at the unscaled audacious base (speedtest `Dock.vue:510-528` documents the exact failure, witnessed `audit/w22-witness.md`; the consumer's conformant workaround is to RE-HOME the library formulas on its own ancestor). This is the EXACT class CLAUDE.md already records for the `@media (pointer: coarse)` block (the dock-coarse-scale re-declare) — but the lever is meant to be a `:root` PRESET a consumer can override on any scope. The fix is `@property`-registering the dock-scale axis (inheriting, `<number>`) so a consumer-scope `--dock-local-scale` override RE-EVALUATES the `calc()` at the dock and threads down — the documented-but-broken `:root` preset made real.

**(f) the VERTICAL collapse↔expand morph moves `block-size` but its plate CHROME snaps discretely — the chrome-interpolation is gated out across the whole axis (DOCKMORPH-VERTICAL-CHROME / BB-1 + BB-4).** CLAUDE.md states "a vertical dock collapses its `height`" and "the collapse/morph/shrink machinery applies on BOTH orientations" — the box-SIZE arm IS wired (the orchestrator is axis-aware, `dockMorphContext.ts` maps a vertical inner stack → `block-size`; `layers.css:70-72` lands the live `--dock-morph-size` scalar on `block-size` for `.glass-dock.vertical[data-morphing] .dock-layers`; `alwaysExpanded` defaults `false` so a vertical dock genuinely collapses).
