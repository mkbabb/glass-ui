## The defect / charge

The button register has every iOS-27 liquid-glass token already minted but COMPOSES almost none of them on the LIT surface, and its two best mechanisms — the W55 adaptive-darken legibility seam + the moving specular — silently no-op the moment the pointer lands. The user's ask is the iOS-27 both-at-once: *more* glassmorphism on buttons WHILE legibility *rises*, never the tradeoff every other web liquid-glass library ships unsolved. glass-ui owns the solved-legibility seam (W55 `--glass-tint-*` darken-over-light + the `contrast-color()` ink flip + the sampled-luminance observer) — the wave's whole job is to REACH it on the lit button, turning "more glass AND more legible at once" from a tradeoff into glass-ui's differentiator. Five mechanism gaps, all token-first + COMPOSE-don't-author, all compositor-only + PRM-safe, the calm-CTA + GL fences held:

**(a) the hover/active fills paint PRE-substituted raw glass tokens — the W55 legibility darken + the `contrast-color()` flip drop exactly when the pointer lands (the substitution-vs-inheritance trap, live on the button register).** The glass button variants (`button/index.ts` `default`/`glass`/`primary-audacious`) paint `hover:bg-(--glass-bg-resting) active:bg-(--glass-bg-floating)`. Those tokens are the RAW `:root` recipe — `glass.css:139` `--glass-bg-resting: color-mix(in srgb, var(--card) …, transparent)` / `:140` `--glass-bg-floating: …` carry NO `color-mix(in oklab, …, --glass-tint-source --glass-tint-strength)` wrapper (only `--glass-bg-dock` at `:147` carries the oklab tint at `:root`). The element-level `.glass-wash` REST background (`ladder.css:69` composes `color-mix(in oklab, var(--glass-bg-resting), var(--glass-tint-source) var(--glass-tint-strength))`) DOES compose the oklab tint — so a button at REST inherits the 4% content-tier floor (`ladder.css:217`) AND the bright-bucket darken + `contrast-color()` flip reach it. But the moment the user hovers/presses, the bg swaps to the non-tinted raw `--glass-bg-resting`/`--glass-bg-floating`, so over a bright/busy backdrop the button DROPS its adaptive darken exactly when the pointer is on it. This is the dock's documented morph-root/vertical-plate trap (CLAUDE.md §"the substitution-vs-inheritance trap"), unfixed on the button register — the 3rd-named recurrence class. The literal "more glassmorphism would COST legibility" the iOS-27 ask inverts.

**(b) the press is a single flat scale leg — no SQUISH, no coupled beat; `useSpringPress` ships exported-but-DEAD.** `button/index.ts` press is `active:scale-(--scale-press-btn)` (= `--scale-press-sm`, `scale-paper.css:41`) on the `.tap-squish`/`.btn-pill` `scale var(--spring-smooth-duration) var(--spring-smooth)` channel. That is a UNIFORM shrink — iOS-27 controls SQUISH (a volume-preserving X/Y reciprocal deform, exactly the `useLiquidFlex` primitive the tabs indicator already uses) and the press couples a subtle specular/brightness lift. `useSpringPress` (`src/composables/motion/useSpringPress.ts` — response 0.25, ζ 0.7, ~5% overshoot, PRM-safe) is the PURPOSE-BUILT engine and has NO in-library binary consumer at HEAD (the visual-load-bearing risk — a shipped-but-dead primitive). The press reads correct-but-inert, not liquid.

**(c) the moving-specular gleam is DEAD on buttons — Button.vue never writes `--mouse-x/--mouse-y`, so the pointer-tracked catch-light paints a static centred fallback.** `material.css:89-90` wires `.glass-wash::before` (which the glass buttons compose) to track `--specular-x: var(--mouse-x, 50%)` / `--specular-y: var(--mouse-y, 50%)` — the iOS-26 illuminate-from-within behaviour. But `Button.vue` (read in full, 42 lines) binds NO pointermove handler and writes neither var. The `var(--mouse-x, 50%)` floor means every glass button's gleam sits frozen at centre and only the INTENSITY lifts on hover (0→0.10, `property-regs.css:153`). The single most iOS-27 glass affordance — the highlight that slides toward the pointer/grazing edge — is unwired on the primary interactive surface. **The boundary:** W-LIQUIDHOVER AUTO-ARMS the position write at the tier root (so a bare `<Button variant="glass">` follows the pointer with zero wiring) and W-LENSING MINTS the `useSpecularPointer` leaf — this wave CONSUMES both + adds the button-scoped intensity bump; it does NOT mint the leaf NOR re-author the auto-arm (see §Do-NOT-touch).

**(d) the glass button reads the FLATTEST wash shadow rung — no DEPTH layering the "increased glassmorphism" move calls for, and no button-scoped hover-specular bump.** `.btn-glass` (`surfaces.css:182`) is just `backdrop-filter: var(--glass-blur-btn)` (`= blur(10px) saturate(1.05) brightness(1.02)`, `glass.css` btn rung). The glass TIER carries the `--glass-edge-light` rim + `--glass-specular` top catch-light + the chromatic fringe on the LADDER rungs, but the button's `box-shadow` depth is only the THINNEST wash rung. glass-ui has the rim+specular tokens but the button reads the flattest shadow rung and never lifts its hover gleam past the global 0.10. The "increased glassmorphism" ask wants the button to read unmistakably as a lifted glass lozenge with a brighter grazing-edge gleam — from ALREADY-MINTED tokens, a `--glass-btn-*` depth + a button-scoped `--glass-specular-intensity-hover` bump (0.10→~0.14), no new compositing axis.

**(e) the refraction edge is unreachable on the button — the iOS-26 edge-bend has no opt-in here.** W-LENSING mints the `--glass-refract` axis + the `.glass-lens` opt-in + the `--press-t` lens-swell (the backdrop pinches under the press) as the §2 refractive tier. The button should be able to OPT IN to the refraction edge (the §2 "buttons (refraction opt-in)" consumer) — a `<Button :liquid>` / `.glass-lens` arm that, on a supporting engine, reads the edge-bend + the `:active` lens-swell coupled to the press squish on the SAME `--press-t` drive; off-Chromium degrades cleanly to today's blur+tint (the no-workaround floor). This is a CONSUME of W-LENSING's axis, NEVER a button-local lens fork.
