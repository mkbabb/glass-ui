## The defect / charge

The house has TWO glass-surface tint axes today and a THIRD it lacks. The W55 `--glass-tint-source`/`--glass-tint-strength` axis (`ladder.css:38-109`) is the WHOLE-SURFACE LEGIBILITY/darken axis — it points the entire plate background toward `--glass-tint-ink` (a darken-over-light for AA, an over-dark lift). It is the BACKGROUND mix, AA-bounded, and re-points toward INK — never a consumer's chromatic DATA hue. There is no seam for a consumer to say "tint THIS glass instance's rim + catch-light with the datum's color" — so the §F1 data-keyed colored hover (a chart cell whose surface should glow with that cell's series hue, a HoverCard whose rim should carry the hovered datum's color) is hand-rolled per consumer: the Atlas threads the accent through bespoke inline `:style` + a per-surface `border-color` override, re-paying the OKLab mix at every call site (the same N-pastes-of-one-recipe anti-pattern `<IconChip>` collapsed for the section-color POP).

The charge: mint a per-INSTANCE CHROMATIC-rim axis — a `--glass-accent` (a complete color, default the NEUTRAL identity so an unset surface is byte-identical) + a `--glass-accent-strength` (a bounded knob, default 0%) — that tints a glass surface's RIM + its `::before` specular catch-light with the consumer's DATA hue, OKLab-mixed, so the data-keyed colored hover becomes a ONE-LINE seam (`--glass-accent: <datum-hue>; --glass-accent-strength: <N%>` on the instance) instead of a hand-threaded `border-color` + inline catch-light per consumer.

This is a DISTINCT axis, NOT a fork of W55:
- **W55 `--glass-tint-source`** = the WHOLE-PLATE BACKGROUND legibility darken (toward `--glass-tint-ink`, AA-bounded, the adaptive-glass seam). **`--glass-accent`** = the RIM + CATCH-LIGHT chromatic tint (toward a consumer DATA hue, per-instance, decoration not legibility). They are the SAME "distinct register, not a fork" relationship **W-ON-GLASS-FG** has to the adaptive-glass seam (a distinct foreground-legibility register that does not fork the backdrop-darken seam). Three disjoint glass axes after this wave: **level** (opacity+blur, AX.W54) · **tint** (whole-plate legibility darken, W55) · **accent** (per-instance chromatic rim+glint, this wave) — coordinated, never collapsed.

It also COORDINATES with — never folds into — W-LENSING + W-LIQUIDHOVER:
- **W-LENSING** mints `useSpecularPointer`/`--glass-specular`/`.glass-lens` + the EDGE-anchored specular glint, and OWNS the `::before` catch-light pseudo. **W-LIQUIDHOVER** auto-arms that specular at the tier root. This wave tints the SAME `::before` catch-light (the rim where the glint grazes carries the accent hue). Folding the accent INTO either would OVERLOAD them (W-LENSING is the refraction depth + the angle-keyed glint geometry; W-LIQUIDHOVER is the position auto-arm; neither is the chromatic tint). The accent is a THIRD disjoint axis on the shared specular surface — it COMPOSES W-LENSING's resolved `::before` recipe (a `color-mix` on the warm-cream core), it does not re-author the catch-light layer.
