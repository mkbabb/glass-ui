## The defect / charge

The audit (R1 finding 2, R3 finding "the spring clock isn't threaded into the entry keyframes"): "presses spring, but reveals don't" — the ENTER register the §6/W-MOTION-CANON doctrine names (`--spring-bouncy`/`--spring-snappy`) is only HALF-applied.
