## Triumvirate Dispatch

Per BB inv (the cardinal lesson) + the AZ/BA W-REFLECT loop, a FAIL deploys three roles in sequence then re-reflects, looping until operative-PASS. The orchestrator may NOT re-dispatch the failing reflection alone; the triumvirate is mandatory for these triggers:

- **A recorded gestalt FAIL on any roster surface (incl. a BB primitive).** A surface whose whole-page both-mode × both-viewport read draws a "wtf" (a mis-alignment, an occlusion, a register that reads wrong as a page, a primitive that reads broken in its demo home) is a FAIL even if its per-mechanism gates are green. This deploys: (1) RESEARCH — an opus lane root-causes the miss (the mechanism, the SOTA bar where aesthetic, the constraint map; no fixes); (2) PLAN — an ad-hoc wave spec authored per WAVE_SPEC (§0 RE-GROUND, defect table, born-RED gate, scope fence) joining `docs/tranches/BB/waves/` + PROGRESS; (3) REDRESS — a build lane executes under the full discipline (gates, captures, typecheck). Then the surface RE-REFLECTS from protocol step 4 and the record gains a RE-REFLECTION stamp (the last-line-supersedes verdict).
- **A diagnostic loop whose third iteration has not isolated the gestalt miss.** If a surface FAILs, redresses, re-reflects, and FAILs again three times without isolating WHY the page still reads wrong (the same mechanism-green/page-wrong gap that re-opened R3→R8, the EXACT class W-DOCK-RAIL-SEAT-FINAL's five seat-Y chases embody), halt and triumvirate the ROOT — the suspect is a cross-surface interaction (a dark-register backdrop the surface composites over, a shell-hold race, a stale capture, a topology the single-surface redress cannot reach) the single-surface redress cannot reach. Do NOT loop on a value tweak on one surface (the seam-Y-dodge anti-pattern).
- **A freshness RED under `--strict-freshness`.** If a surface's reflection capture goes stale (its declared surface re-mutated after capture, e.g. a sibling triumvirate redress touched a shared file), the capture re-takes; if the re-take changes the gestalt read FAIL↔PASS, that is a new verdict and the loop continues — a fresh capture can only ever RAISE the bar, never paper over a regression.
- **A mobile-only FAIL (the W-GESTALT-GATE2 G2 case).** If a surface reads PASS on desktop but FAIL on mobile (or vice-versa — the dock chip-over-title was the inverse), the surface is a FAIL (the verdict is the AND of both viewports) and routes to the triumvirate. Do NOT flip a surface PASS on a desktop-clean read while the mobile capture reads broken — that is the exact WEAK-2 the hardened gate exists to forbid (the gate reds a PASS over a desktop-faked mobile, but a genuinely-broken mobile read that ships a real mobile PNG passes G1/G2 and must be caught by the HUMAN gestalt verdict, not deferred).
- **A roster-schema conflict.** If flipping a verdict, adding a capture path, or growing the roster to a BB primitive requires a roster-ledger SCHEMA change `proof:ba-gestalt` cannot read (a column rename, a verdict outside {FAIL, PASS}, a capture-cell shape the W-GESTALT-GATE2 parser does not accept), that is the gate-design miss W-GESTALT-GATE2's triumvirate trigger named — halt and triumvirate the schema (coordinate with W-GESTALT-GATE2's bound, which owns the parser), do not fork a second ledger.
