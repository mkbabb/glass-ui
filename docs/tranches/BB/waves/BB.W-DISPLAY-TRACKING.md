## The defect / charge

The deep SOTA audit's display-type finding (`audit/sota-deep/findings.md:10`, `:37`): Apple's live display type carries a tight **NEGATIVE** tracking ≈ **−1.5% of size** (measured: 80px → −1.2px; home 34px → −0.374px ≈ −1.1%) + a tight **~1.05 line-height** (84px over 80px). This is THE signature that separates "designed" display type from "just big" — the single highest-leverage type harden. glass-ui owns a magnificent √φ audacious ladder (`text-display` / `-2..-5` / `-mega` / `-hero` / `-audacious`, peaking at 352px — the fast.com peg) but the ladder's TRACKING + LEADING are NOT Apple-calibrated: the display reads BIG, not DESIGNED.

This is a TYPE-ONLY token refinement on the typography cascade — the display rungs ONLY; body/caption/small/prose UNTOUCHED, the heading/title register UNTOUCHED. No glass, no motion, no component. It is the §1 HARDEN-wave's lowest-risk, highest-leverage item.

**§0 CORRECTION (re-grounded at HEAD — the charge sharpened by the real source).** The naive read of the charge ("add negative letter-spacing to the display ladder") is HALF-stale — the display rungs ALREADY carry a negative tracking. The real state + the two true gaps:

1. **The display ladder ALREADY tracks negative — but on the SHARED `--type-tracking-tight: -0.025em`** (`scheme-motion.css:56`), which is ALSO the heading/title register's tracking. All nine display rungs read `letter-spacing: var(--type-tracking-tight)` (`semantic.css:27,63,73,83,93,103,113,123`) AND `text-title` reads the SAME token (`semantic.css:136`), as do `configurator.css:26`, `typography/utilities.css:96`, `MetricRow.vue:228`. The harden MINTS a display-ONLY rung (`--type-tracking-display`) and re-points ONLY the nine display @utilities onto it (clean break, no shared-token mutation).

2. **The em-relative `-0.025em` IS proportional, but the LEADING is the real miss.** `-0.025em` is −2.5% of size at every rung (em-relative tracking is proportional-in-px by construction) — TIGHTER than Apple's −1.5% at any size, so the tracking is over-aggressive, not under. The display **line-height** is the un-Apple value: `--type-leading-display: 1.1` (`scheme-motion.css:54`) vs Apple's measured ~1.05 (84/80). 3. **`text-hero` is its OWN poster register and is OUT OF SCOPE.** `text-hero` (`semantic.css:41-57`) is a SEPARATE one-off poster register with its own three knobs (`--text-hero-leading: 0.84`, `--text-hero-tracking: -0.03em`) — already tighter than Apple by design (the fast.com hero peg). It is UNTOUCHED (the poster register keeps its own knobs — the harden is the LADDER, not the bespoke poster).

The fix is gestalt + idiomatic + non-bleeding: mint the display-ONLY proportional tracking + tight leading rungs in the SAME `:root` block the existing `--type-leading-display`/`--type-tracking-*` rungs live (`scheme-motion.css:48-61`), re-point the nine display @utilities onto them (the `text-title`/heading/subheading/body/caption/small/prose register reads its EXISTING tokens, untouched), bridge the new tracking rung to Tailwind alongside the existing `--tracking-*` family (`bridges.css:48-54`) so a consumer can read it, and record the Apple-calibration rationale. ## Triumvirate Dispatch

- **The em-relative tracking does not reproduce the Apple −1.5% across the full ladder.** Apple's measure is −1.5% at 80px but −1.1% at 34px (slightly LESS negative at the smaller size — optical-sizing's natural taper). A single em value is a CONSTANT −X% at every size, so it cannot reproduce the size-dependent taper exactly. Do NOT ship a per-rung hardcoded px tracking (it breaks the clamp() vw-fluidity — the tracking must scale WITH the fluid size) and do NOT widen into editing the size clamps.
