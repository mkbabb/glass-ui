## Consequences for perfected BI

- Liquid Glass remains a functional plane, not the content substrate; adaptive material claims need live sample provenance and explicit failure.
- Browser receipts name exact version/build and feature probes. Safari stable is the product lane; beta is forward-compatibility research unless explicitly promoted.
- Native feature presence never closes its historical bug classes. Scroll timelines include pause, boundary, bfcache, nested-scroll, resize, and input tests.
- The Dock's 44 px coarse target is a deliberate product floor. WCAG's 24 px minimum and exceptions remain separately measured; no fallback may disguise a broken token cascade.
- Modal isolation, form-error linkage, GPU loss/error channels, animation writer ownership, and preference resolution are behavioral properties owned by existing durable families, not reasons to mint more gates.
