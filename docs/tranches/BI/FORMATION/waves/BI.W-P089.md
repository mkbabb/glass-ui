# BI.W-P089 — Toggle

**Status:** SUPERSEDED — DELETED

The consumerless standalone `Toggle` runtime, root export, and specimen are deleted.
ToggleGroup retains only an unexported `toggleVariants` recipe for its own items, limited
to the live default/outline and sm/md/lg axes. The legacy card recipe and compatibility
aliases are deleted; the P090 ToggleGroup contract remains intact.
