# First-principles product and actual-demo assay

No source or proof script earns product credit by existing. Every retained material, Dock facility, motion mechanism, procedural scene, and component concept is assessed through a semantic/physical contract and an actual current demo witness; the owning wave must then render the full state matrix in Safari-current and Chrome-current (or explicitly establish a device-free property).
