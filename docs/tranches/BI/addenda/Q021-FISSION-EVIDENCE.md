## B. The DOCK-LADDER honest-goo bounds + the current dock state

Source: `docs/tranches/BI/design/ios27-reference/DOCK-LADDER.md` — the iOS-27 dock measured
frame-by-frame from Apple Music V1 (60fps) and V2 (120fps). Confidence tags in the ladder: [H]
direct pixel measurement, [M] tracked-with-bias, [L] qualitative.

**The honest goo spec (§3, [H]).** — DOCK-LADDER.md:111-139.
- Fission deformation runs ~4 frames total; **the fused waist lives 1-2 frames (17-33ms)** as a
  lobe-bulge with a shallow concave crease (a ~10-15% inset per edge), then clean separation with
  a ~140px gap and no visible strand (DOCK-LADDER.md:113-124).
- Merge is a mirror: a 1-2-frame contact-fillet meniscus, then absorb-with-interior-ghost; the
  glyph handoff is a dual-instance crossfade, not one reparented node (DOCK-LADDER.md:127-133).
- The spec verbatim: "lobe-bulge → shallow waist (≤2 frames) → clean separation; contact-fillet
  (≤2 frames) → absorb-with-interior-ghost. ... Nothing ever stretches into a taffy thread; there
  is no long-lived neck to render. ... **a persistent stringy bridge is fan-fiction.**"
  (DOCK-LADDER.md:135-139); prior-correction C3 restates it (DOCK-LADDER.md:241).

**The morph kinematics (§2, [M→H]).** — DOCK-LADDER.md:62-108.
- Measured iOS-27 dock-morph spring: **response ≈ 0.25-0.32s, ζ ≈ 0.80-0.90**, overshoot
  **1.1-1.4%** of travel, full settle to ±2px **≤ ~420ms** (DOCK-LADDER.md:72-86).
- "The 'weight' in the reference is carried by the ~150-200ms overshoot-recovery TAIL and the goo
  deformation — not by a slow travel. If the liquid-weight edict wants more visible squish, add it
  in the deformation channel, never the clock." (DOCK-LADDER.md:94-96, restated §8 :258-260).

**The traveling selection lens (§4, [H]).** — DOCK-LADDER.md:143-169. Mid-travel the lens swells
rounder and slightly proud (~1.21× slot pitch), with rim chromatic dispersion, a rim-gated ink
wipe (not a timed crossfade), and near-unity clear transmission ("selected = the clearest glass on
the bar"). This is the eyeglass/tabs surface, distinct from the fission topology.

**Geometry + library-binding bounds (§1, §8).** — DOCK-LADDER.md:26-58, :251-283. Bar 1102×190 @
1206px (stadium r=h/2), slot pitch 206px, triad circles d≈153 (0.8× bar height), capsule 645×137,
inter-piece gaps 45-47px. §8 explicitly bounds a rebuild: "The neck spec (§3) bounds the goo
filter: waist visible ≤2 frames, no persistent strands; content = double-exposure crossfade gated
to the silhouette; dual-glyph handoff. PRM ⇒ instant topology swap stays correct."
(DOCK-LADDER.md:261-264). C7 corrects the BD spring tune: the shipped 0.68/ζ0.64 "moved AWAY from
the reference it cited" (DOCK-LADDER.md:245).

- Fission/merge waist: **1-2 frames = 17-33ms at 60fps**, then clean separation — DOCK-LADDER.md:

  sustained strand.
- The traveling tab goo-worm (a related but distinct phenomenon, the selection indicator): the
  iOS-27 corpus measures it at **+30% elongation (304→394 px, 1.11→1.44 slots) at ~⅓ into a ~270ms
  travel, then catches up and contracts** — `/Users/mkbabb/.claude/projects/-Users-mkbabb-Programming-glass-ui/bi-addenda/reports/ios27-corpus/CODEX.md:192` (§3.1 timing table; session-tree report — subsequent `CODEX.md:NNN` cites are this same file),

  60fps); both are "goo," but only the DOCK-LADDER waist is the fission-topology number.

  connective tissue, not in scroll-snap — CODEX.md:206-207, headline #4 `:350`.
