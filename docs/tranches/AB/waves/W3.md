## Rationale

AB.W3 is the LIVING-UI HEADLINE — the two variants (aura + sectioned) collectively define what "living UI" means as a glass-ui substrate:

1. **`<Pulse variant="aura">`** lifts the ambient breathing-motion vocabulary into a tokenized + reduced-motion-respecting variant. The aura is composed by the consumer surface (the host owns `position: relative` + `border-radius`; the aura inherits both); the primitive itself does not mount on cards/chassis. Speedtest reserves the variant at 5 surfaces — the cap is a deliberate scarcity rule (overuse defeats the "living" intent).

2. **`<Progress variant="sectioned">`** lifts the phase-bus vocabulary into a state-distinct multi-cell variant. Where `default` and `gradient` variants present a single linear progress, `sectioned` reads as a phase-bus — N colour-coded cells with living gradient seams, spring-grown active fill, catch-light sweep on the active cell. The `ProgressSegment` shape gives consumers a structured payload for `{key, label, color, state, weight}` cells; the `currentSegmentKey` + `activeProgress` props decouple "which phase" from "how far through".

Both variants share a discipline: **every motion vocabulary is tokenized + reduced-motion-gated.** No raw animation values; no hard-coded durations; no motion that survives reduced-motion preference. The token surface (`--animate-ambient-pulse-*` + `--pulse-aura-opacity-*` + `--progress-sectioned-*`) is the consumer override path; the `@keyframes ambient-pulse` is the shared substrate other primitives can reuse without redefining.
