## 9 The close checklist

- Every wave's status in §2 rests on a commit and a reading taken at the close.
- `git grep` over `src/` for wave ids, row ids and seat vocabulary in comments: empty.
- The overfitting audit: every `src/` artefact BM added has two sites, is exported, or is a private demo helper.
- `data-motion-count` ships in markup because the shipped library is what G-3 reads, and the attribute costs a few bytes on the roots that carry it; the close says which roots carry it, and strikes it from any root whose count is two or fewer.
- The precepts as amended by BM-0 still describe what shipped; any line that no longer does is amended in the close commit, citing its ruling.
- The retro is written at `audit/BM-retro.md`.
