A hovered seat beside the current one

Each card starts with a still: Pin hovered beside Home, in the text’s colour and then with three seats in colours of their own, as a site may tint them. Below it the same two runs answer a mouse, and a press shows the on plate.

The strengths

Each letter is one fill at its on strength, with the hover drawn as a share of it by opacity, so no colour changes while a plate comes and goes. A: hover 5 %, on 12 %. B: hover 12 %, on 12 %. C: hover 6.5 % in light and 5 % in dark, on 10 %. On covers the current seat, an open one and a pressed one. C is the dock’s ladder as measured and built for 11.2.0, carried to every control.

Everything here is a model drawn by this page alone, not glass-ui itself.
