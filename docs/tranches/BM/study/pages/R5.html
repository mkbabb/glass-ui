The menu grows out of the button you tap and settles over it. Tap outside the menu, or the button again, to close it. The same menu each time; only the spring changes.

C and D overshoot. glass-ui’s rules keep overshoot off menus and allow it only on the dock’s split and join, so picking C or D asks to change that rule.

The menu starts as the button’s own round shape and grows into the panel over it; the button lends the menu its glass, so only one piece of glass stands there at a time, and takes it back with a short fade once the menu has gone. The words fade in once the shape has mostly arrived. Closing is the same for all four: a quick fade. The page draws the menu’s size itself each frame, a stand-in for the final method: read the timing and the shape, not the smoothness.
