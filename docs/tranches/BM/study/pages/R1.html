Reading your Safari version…

Reduce Motion is on in your settings. Turn it off for this reading: the folds are what you are looking at.

Each shape is one frosted glass plate over the same stripes. The stripes behind a plate blur only where the glass is. If the blur spills past a rounded edge into a square, that square is the ghost this page looks for.
