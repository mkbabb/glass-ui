Script frames per second: –

All four dots run one spring (response 0.3 s, damping 0.88), started as if a finger had flicked it, on the same clock. A second Go while they move sends them back from where they are: A and C keep their speed as they turn, B and D start again from still. “Busy page” keeps the page’s own work running three quarters of the time, the way a heavy page does. Low Power Mode halves the page’s own frame rate. The reading is of the Safari this phone runs.
