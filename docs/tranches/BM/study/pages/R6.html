Each number is the best whole second of your drag. Do the first drag with Low Power Mode off and the second with it on: WebKit halves the page’s frame rate in Low Power Mode, and the second set of numbers shows whether the touch events slow with it. A ProMotion iPhone’s screen runs at 120 a second; a number near 60 means Safari is holding that event to the page’s 60 frames a second.
