What runs

B and C: a press shows the plate’s tone. The change runs two motions on one 200 ms clock: the rays draw in, then the night slides. The plate’s tone returns after the glyph lands. With Reduce Motion on, nothing moves: the rays fade as the crescent fades in, over 150 ms.
