Light and dark

Each card shows the toggle at a dock seat’s size and large. Rest a mouse on one to see its plate, a faint fill that never moves.

A · Today’s: a sun outline turns half a turn while a disc slides.

B · Proposed: a warm sun draws its rays in, then a cool night carves the crescent.

C · B’s motion in the text’s colour, to judge the colour apart.

What runs

B and C: a tap changes the glyph and nothing else. Three things run on one 200 ms clock: the rays draw in, the night slides across to carve the crescent, and the colour turns from the sun’s to the night’s (in C the colour is the text’s, which turns with the page). With Reduce Motion on, nothing moves: the rays fade as the crescent fades in, on the same 200 ms, and the colour still turns.

Everything here is a model drawn by this page alone, not glass-ui itself.
