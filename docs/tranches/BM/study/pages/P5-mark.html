Reading your Safari version…

Reduce Motion is on in your settings. Turn it off for this reading: the motion is what you are looking at.

1 · Holding it

Tap a few options, then press the chosen one and slide across the others.

A · Glides and never lifts. The option you press answers with its own soft fill. Today’s glide.

B · Lifts 6% while you drag it, the amount the library’s held controls grow, and settles back when you let go. A tap only glides.

C · Lifts 16% while you drag it, the figure a third-party rebuild of Apple’s tab bar gives for the tab under its lens (not Apple’s own number). A tap only glides.

D · Lifts on every press: it moves to the option you press and lifts there, and the choice is made when you let go over it.

E · The word grows instead: the mark stays its size, and the word or icon under your finger grows a twelfth.

2 · Stretch

Tap options far apart, then drag the chosen one past either end.

A · Keeps its size, and stops at the ends. Today’s.

B · Stretches as it travels: its front edge leads, its back edge follows, up to a fifth longer, and it lands at its own size.

C · Stretches past the ends: dragged beyond the first or last option, it lengthens toward your finger and comes back when you let go.

D · Both B and C.

3 · Widths

Tap between the short words and the long one.

A · Every option as wide as the widest. Today’s.

B · Each option as wide as its word; the mark changes width as it moves.

4 · Arrow keys

These two buttons do what the arrow keys do, on all three strips at once (the keys work too, on a strip you have tapped, if a keyboard is attached).

A · Glides as a tap does. Today’s.

B · A shorter glide (about 0.12 s).

C · Fades across: the mark fades out where it was and in where it goes, and nothing moves.

Everything here is a model of the design, drawn by this page alone. With Reduce Motion on, the mark would fade across in every strip. “Busy page” keeps the page’s own work running three quarters of the time, as a heavy page does.
