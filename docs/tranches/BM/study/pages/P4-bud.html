  1. Corners: pick each letter and tap Open (Slow ×4 helps). Which letters lose their round corners, or show a square or darker patch?
  2. Busy: turn on Busy page and open each letter again. Which letters stutter?
  3. The fold: tap Fold. Does F’s frost look like G’s, and does either show a square patch?
