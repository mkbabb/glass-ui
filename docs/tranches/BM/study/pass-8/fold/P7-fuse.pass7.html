1 · A seat that leaves the dock and joins it

Open: the dock opens and a round seat comes out of its end and parts from it. Fold: the seat goes back in and the dock folds to a circle. Hold stops each one 2.5 px apart, the middle of the neck’s life; each further tap holds at the next opening (0, 1.25, 3.75 and 5 px), and the last lets go.

A · Born underneath: the seat starts under the dock’s end and slides out; its glass fills in as it comes clear. Nothing joins them.

B · Edges meet: as A, but where the two touch their facing ends go flat, so for a moment they sit as one bar, and they round again as they part.

C · Goo: the two are drawn as one shape, so a neck forms as they part and thins until it breaks. No frost: C needs the frostless dock.

D · One piece, cut: one sheet of glass, cut each frame to the dock, the seat and a neck between them.
