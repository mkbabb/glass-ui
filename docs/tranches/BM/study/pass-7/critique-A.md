## Summary

Pass 7 meets its line in UNION §4 in form. Its first line says no reading has returned. It folds R4 unread, applies F3 and Q2's candidates to the theme seat, the fold and the call to action, proposes one live fuse at a time, builds the necks on one page and hosts a second crop, and gives a provisional answer on Q2. C and fold B carry instant changes, uncounted nodes and an undesigned rest that P7-fuse does not show (A7-3, A7-4, A7-6). The one fade the fold keeps breaks P0a rule 4 (A7-5).

Thirteen objections stand: 1 blocking, 6 major, 6 minor. Eight were dropped.
