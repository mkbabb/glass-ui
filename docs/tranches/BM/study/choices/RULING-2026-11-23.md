## The exceptions

The refinement follows the ChatGPT app's slider (`BL/design/slider/MEASURE.md` §2.2): the shell and knob concentric, the knob inset from the shell, the grow rising from the control itself, the value said while held, and in and out on the shared spring. ## The apotheosis of the reference sites

The reference sites are rida.dev, both the post and its UI library, together with iOS 27 and the ChatGPT app that the study read. For every facility they show, the final design must answer one of three ways:

- **Matched**, with its reading.
- **Surpassed**, saying how, with its reading.
- **Refused**, naming the law that refuses it.

Nothing is left unanswered. Every consumer site (atlas, color, keyframes, fourier, daraksha, gaggle, Home Assistant, dns-analysis, slides) gets a note saying what it gains and what it does to reach it.

## Addendum: the owner on glass.babb.dev, 2026-11-23 23:03 to 23:06 (INBOUND O-428)

> "we should re-design the darkmode toggle to be more like daraksha's for glass-ui, but slightly less ornate, but coloured too."

The design passes take this with the theme seat of choice 24, and it ships in 11.2.0. Today glass's toggle is a white sun with a moon. daraksha's (`~/Programming/daraksha/src/shell/ThemeControl.vue`, a private repo: take the idea, never its assets) is an eight-point star made of two squares. It turns, draws its points into a disc, and a night disc slides across the disc to carve a crescent. Every move is a CSS transform keyed off the root's `.dark`, on the lively spring forward and the settle spring back. Glass's version keeps that grammar with fewer parts (a sun whose rays draw into its disc, then the crescent carved by the sliding night), and is coloured: the sun warm and the night cool, from glass's palette tokens, legible in both themes. The motion fixes for the toggle (press stall, reduced-motion cuts, the 0.2 s fade of the words) stay in 11.1.2.
