## Three decisions that shaped the rest

1. **The theme's one crossfade answers three objections at once** (L1, E2, E3). O-353's ruling had already placed it ("the theme flip cross-fades: ground, text and glass change together over at most 200 ms through a view transition … under reduced motion a plain fade; `disableTransitions` keeps its meaning for layout but no longer makes the flip a cut"), and O-430 item 9 and O-436 (6) put it with the toggle's new face. BM-1 now wraps every flip glass starts (`useGlobalDark().toggleDark()`, which fourier's own toggle also calls) in a view transition: the page's pictures cross by `opacity` on the compositor, 200 ms, 150 ms under Reduce Motion. Under Reduce Motion the toggle's glyph does not move and its change rides that crossfade, so the glyph needs no reduced leg at all: the 200 ms page-thread crossfade that broke O-350's 150 ms (E2) is gone, and no exception goes to the owner.
2. **Every reduced form is a named `!important` carve** (E2). `release/11.1.1`'s `a11y-overrides.css:6-30` puts the reduced-motion rule in its own last layer and says "`[data-allow-motion]` is not carved". BM.md §1 gains the rule, P6 is rewritten to the mechanism as 11.1.1 has it, and each wave that keeps a reduced form names its carve.
3. **The messages say what BL's wave builds** (E1). W-I.8 gives the theme seat a corner controls dock in the dock-redesign minor and couples no two docks (R-6, R-12); the `beside` slot is 12.0.0's. The four messages that promised a seat beside the dock in that minor now say so, BM.md §7 quotes R-6 and R-12, and §8 asks BL to confirm the corner dock as that minor's answer to O-430 item 12. The messages are ready as written; if BL builds an adjacent seat in that minor, the four are amended before sending.
