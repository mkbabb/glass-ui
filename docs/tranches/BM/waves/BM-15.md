## What it builds

New defaults for the Blob's geometry and motion, chosen by a design pass on 11.1.2's reading:

- **a fuller body** and **larger satellites**, read against the separation geometry the presets keep (a satellite's orbit outside the body);
- **calmer motion**: if 11.1.2 found the motion to be the designed wobble, its amplitude and rate come down here; if 11.1.2 found a fault, its cure is 11.1.2's and this wave only sets the defaults.

The defaults change `config.geometry` and the motion's constants; a site that sets its own (value.js's hero) keeps them. The design is a default that ships unless the owner overturns it; a still and a film of it go to him with the candidate.
