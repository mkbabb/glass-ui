## What the hero draws today

The hero does not draw glass's geometry. It scales its own values by the app's value over glass's default, `hero * (appBlobConfig.geometry[key] / BLOB_CONFIG_DEFAULTS.geometry[key])` (`HeroBlob.vue:201-202`), and the app's config is a clone of glass's defaults (`demo/picker/visual/heroBlobConfig.ts:15`), so the ratio is 1 and the hero draws its own: body 0.27, orbit 0.39, four satellites of 0.12, eccentricity 0.03 (`:228-236`). A new glass geometry default divides out and never reaches it. It also arms fission at 0.8, set on the owner's order of 9 November ("satellite blobs and goo morphing needs to be more pronounced") so that "the resting colony splits on most merge windows" (`:176-182`), and sets `morphT` to 0.3 (`:192`, `:243`), with the fission armed at `:242`, where glass's default fission is 0. Its other atoms, the motion's among them, come from the clone of glass's defaults, so a change there does reach it. 11.1.2's reading as BL wrote it films "the Blob at rest on its defaults" (S112:298), a register color does not show.

## What it builds

Values for the hero, chosen by the design on 11.1.2's reading taken at the hero's own values:

- **a fuller body** and **larger satellites**, read against the separation geometry (a satellite's orbit outside the body) and the wrapper's paint containment the hero's own note measures (`HeroBlob.vue:210-220`);
- **calmer motion**: the reading says which motion reads as jitter, the designed wobble, the fission splitting on most merge windows, or a fault. A fault is 11.1.2's to cure. The wobble's amplitude and rate come down in glass's defaults, which reach the hero through its clone. The fission amplitude is the hero's own setting, and the design weighs it against the order of 9 November that set it.

**Where the values live.** The geometry ships as glass's hero preset, `BLOB_HERO` (`R111` `src/components/blob/presets.ts:41-61`, exported as "the calibrated hero preset … for a consumer mounting a large focal blob"), which color does not mount today; a changed preset is a changed default look, so it rides a minor. `BLOB_CONFIG_DEFAULTS`' geometry stays, since no second site asks for a fuller default body and the one site the owner named cancels it. value.js's note asks color either to mount `BLOB_HERO` in place of its geometry overlay (`HeroBlob.vue:228-236`) or to set its overlay's values to the design's, and whether its fission stays at 0.8. Until color answers, the hero keeps its own values, and FINAL §5 records that the owner's ask reached it only where glass's defaults do.
