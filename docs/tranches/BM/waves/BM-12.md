## First: a neck a person can see

The order is for goo a person sees: "animated that fission and separation in goo with safari compat" (IN O-279). As pass 10 sampled it, the neck draws on 3 frames at 60 Hz and 1 at 30 on an open, and on the dock row's close the gap runs 8 → 5.02 → −1.73 px at 60 Hz, so a fold shows no bridge at all (D§7.4, below). P0a: "a leg is at least five frames long. A cut given two frames is still a cut"; `restraint.md`: "A motion nobody can see is a cost with no intent: cut it too." So before anything else is built, the wave's sketch is re-drawn until the neck draws on five frames or more at 60 Hz on an open and on a close. Any form that meets it keeps one velocity-continuous curve shared by the plate and the seat: the whole split re-timed together, or σ raised within Safari's limits with its cost read. A seat held through the neck's range while the plate goes on is a plateau inside the spring, a hitch that breaks the wave's own one clock (`motion-canon.md` P0: motion "keeps the velocity of the gesture that started it"), so it is not a form the wave may build. That frame count, with a reading of the speed, is a proof cell in both engines. If no form within Safari's limits meets it, or only a plateau does, the wave is held and σ, the frame counts and the cost go to the owner before it builds (BM.md §11), not as a list item behind a shipped build.

## What it builds

- **The register.** The goo exists only on the 12.0.0 dock that samples nothing: W-R.1 moves `--dock-surface-blur` to the zero rung and writes the dock's `backdrop-filter: none` (G-6). There is no second path and no development warning standing in for one.
- **The seat** is a sibling of the plate and of the controls, placed at the open plate's end plus the gap. Folded, it rests translated back by `--seat-from` (the open width plus the gap, plus the seat's width less the disc's: 244 px for a seat as wide as the disc). On frame 0 of a split it lies inside the disc (law 8), and it slides out on the window's own legs, two legs on one node, on one clock with the plate.
- **The layer.** One fusion layer per document holds the dock's plate and the seat's plate, `aria-hidden` and inert. It mounts only on a dock with a seat beside it. While fusing it carries `filter: url(#glass-fuse)` and the dock's drop-shadow: a blur of σ 4, then a threshold on alpha.
- **When the filter is on.** Set on the frame a close starts, so the first moving frame is already filtered, and held through the folded rest, so no swap lands on a still frame. On an open it is cleared on the first frame whose gap passes the built filter's pinch by a pixel, the pinch read from the shipped σ on the built filter (5 px at σ 4, so 6 px today, 200 ms in on the dock row), so a σ the first section raises moves the clearing frame with it. A reversal mid-change keeps the attribute and retargets the motion. Under Reduce Motion the fold is BM-11's fade in place and the filter never turns on.
- **What a person sees.** As pass 10 sampled it: on an open the neck draws on 3 frames at 60 Hz and 1 at 30; on the dock row's close the gap runs 8 → 5.02 → −1.73 px at 60 Hz and 8 → −1.73 px at 30 Hz, so a fold shows no bridge, only the plates meeting with filleted corners. At σ 4 the neck is thinner than iOS's (D§14.1 item 7). The first section above replaces these figures with five frames or more, open and close, or holds the wave.
- **The shadow's hand-over.** On an open the filter clears a pixel past the pinch, and its drop-shadow gives way to each plate's own shadow on a moving frame. The proof's pixel-delta cell reads that frame against its neighbours, so the hand-over is shown not to step.
- **The fold under the filter.** WebKit does not composite an element with `filter: url()` (`RenderLayerBacking.cpp:L1025-1031`), so while the filter is on the plate's window is painted on the page's thread each frame, and a busy page can stall it. That is law 5 broken for those frames by the owner's order, named in P5 and in the ceiling (BM-0). The controls' window plays on the same thread for those frames (BM-11), so the seats never run ahead of the glass. The layer's root carries `data-motion-count="5"` for an open with the split (G-3).
- **The seat's hover and press** are W-B.17's fill on its `::before` at BM-2's ladder; its glass is its plate inside the fusion layer.
- **daraksha's pill seat** (its "i" beside the khatam) is wider than the disc, so its plate is a window that widens from the disc's width, one more `translate`. gaggle's lamp, if it ever stands beside its dock, is a readout: no hover plate and no press tone.

## Proof

- **G-6** on the built dock in headless Chromium and Playwright WebKit: `--dock-surface-blur` at the zero rung and the computed `backdrop-filter` `none`.
- **The neck's frame count**, a cell in both engines: on an open and on a close at 60 Hz, the frames on which the two plates are joined by a neck of the layer's own colour number five or more, and the plate's and the seat's speeds read on every frame change by no more than the row's closed form allows from one frame to the next.
- **G-7**, frame by frame in both engines: the layer mounts only with a seat; the filter attribute is present from a close's first frame through the folded rest and is gone from the first frame of an open whose gap passes the built filter's pinch by a pixel; no element under the layer animates a layout property.
- **One thread under the filter**, Busy page on: the seat's edge against the plate's edge on every frame of a close, half a pixel at most (BM-11's cell).
- **The frame-gap reading**, recorded, not a gate: rAF frame gaps over a close in Playwright WebKit under `guarded --exclusive webkit`, Busy page on, with and without the filter. Safari's Web Inspector on an iPhone amends it when one comes. A gap the filter adds goes to the owner with σ and the layer's region; it amends the wave and never holds it.
- **The shadow's hand-over**: the pixel delta of the frame where the filter clears, against the frames on either side, within the delta of its neighbours.
- On frame 0 of a split the seat's box lies inside the disc's; the layer is `aria-hidden` and inert and takes no hit.
- The goo under the keyboard (focus moves into the seat with the filter on), forced colours (the layer draws in system colours or not at all) and Reduce Motion (the filter never set).
- G-2 (the layer is P5's named order, read by its own element), G-3 (5 on an open with the split, by `data-motion-count`) and G-5 in both modes.

**Fact checks** (D§15.2; they amend, never block): the paint cost per frame on a phone, in Low Power Mode, under a scroll with the dock folded, and in Home Assistant's app; and whether WebKit paints a translated child inside the filtered layer (Safari's Web Inspector, Layers).

## Sites it serves

A dock with a seat beside it: daraksha first, then any site that moves its theme or faces into the seat (keyframes, color, Home Assistant, fourier, atlas, gaggle, slides). atlas would fuse nothing but a dock and its own seat, and the goo loads there only if a dock of atlas's puts a seat beside it.
