## What it builds

A discrete form of the Slider, for a slider whose values are a small set of steps.

- **What the order asks**, each to be designed: the stops drawn as dots the value snaps to; a thicker track; a length set by the stop count rather than by the container. The form is the default wherever a slider is discrete (a small set of steps); the design pass names the threshold and how a site opts out.
- **What it keeps.** The grow on use (BM-5) on by default; the thumbless default and the knob prop; 11.1.2's touch intent; a step's travel at each boundary crossed (choice 22 B); the value's two events (O-432).
- **What the design pass settles before code:** the threshold of "a small set", the dots' size and ink against the fill and the ground (3:1 where a dot is the only sign of a stop), the track's thickness per size, the length rule, how the dots read under forced colours and more contrast, the reduced form, and the counts. Its critique runs against the laws as BM's critics did. The design is a default that ships unless the owner overturns it; a strip of it goes to him with the candidate.

## Sites it serves

value.js first: the slider the owner named is a "Colors" count, and the one in value.js's source is `demo/workbenches/generate/GenerateControls.vue:366` (`aria-label="Number of colors"`, 1 to 12, step 1, on `origin/master`), with Extract's twin; the wave confirms which he saw against value.js's frames. Then any site with a stepped slider. Today a site can come close with `size="lg"`, a `step` and `marks` at every step, at a width it sets (IN O-438).
