## What it builds

A discrete form of the Slider, for a slider whose values are a small set of steps.

- **What the order asks**, each to be designed: the stops drawn as dots the value snaps to; a thicker track; a length set by the stop count rather than by the container. The form is the default wherever a slider is discrete (a small set of steps); the design names the threshold and the prop with which a site keeps a slider as it is.
- **What it keeps.** The grow on use (BM-5) on by default; the thumbless default and the knob prop; 11.1.2's touch intent; a step's travel at each boundary crossed (choice 22 B); the value's two events (O-432). `marks` keeps its meaning in the minor: decorative checkpoints that never snap the value (`R111` `slider/types.ts:27`). The discrete form's dots are its own, drawn from `step` and the range, so no existing prop changes what it does.
- **What the design settles before code:** the threshold of "a small set"; the dots' size and ink against the fill and the ground (3:1 where a dot is the only sign of a stop); how a dot's ink changes as the value passes it, a tween on the plate's clock and never a step; the track's thickness per size; the length rule, and how the length tweens when a site changes the stop count (`motion-canon.md` P0: size changes get no exemption); how the dots read under forced colours and more contrast; the reduced form; and the counts. Its critiques run against the laws as BM's critics did.
- **The census.** Every stepped slider at every site's ref, read at the design and named in its record with the range and step, so the threshold's reach is known before the default ships. Read on 2026-11-27: value.js's colour counts (`demo/workbenches/generate/GenerateControls.vue:366`, 1 to 12; `demo/workbenches/extract/ExtractControls.vue:48`, 1 to 16, a spectrum when it shows a gradient); fourier's harmonic levels (`web/src/components/morph/HarmonicLevelGrid.vue:10`, `:20`, step 1 over the levels), its editor control (`visualization/EditorControlsDock.vue:121`, 0 to 10) and its equation's term count (`visualization/EquationPanel.vue:116`), all through `ui/SliderControl.vue:187`; atlas's year range (`src/filter/ui/RangeFacet.vue:62`, step 1 over its track's steps) and the sci filters' step-1 sliders (`DemandFilter.vue:63`, `EcfFilter.vue:366`, `SciFilter.vue:189`, `UsfFilter.vue:100`); Home Assistant's rails, whose step comes from each rail's spec (`frontend/src/tiles/rails.ts:246`). daraksha's scrubbers and keyframes' four step finely and are not discrete. Each site with a slider under the threshold is told in its note, with the default and the candidate that fixes it.
