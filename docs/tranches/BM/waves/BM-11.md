## What it builds

In 12.0.0 the dock's plate is the capsule window (D§3; its design and hit region in `waves/BM-8.md`), and chrome samples nothing (O-260), so the window has no backdrop to clip.

- **No seat fades.** The controls' own element becomes a second window of the plate's shape for the length of the change only (`data-morphing`). Its one child, the row of seats, stands still by the counter-translate, so the glass's edge cuts each seat exactly as it passes.
- **The one fade left is the trade at the disc.** The first seat and the folded glyph trade by P0a rule 4 (90 ms out, the arriving word from 45 ms over 120 ms).
- **The rests.** The controls' window is the plate's shape grown on the block axis by the larger of 5 px (so a 3 px focus ring at a 2 px offset is never cut) and the `--dock-active-shadow` spread (so a selected seat's ring, daraksha's 1.5 px among them, stays whole on every frame, as 11.1.2's cure for O-437 (3) has it). At the folded rest the row takes `visibility: hidden` and `inert`.
- **The clock.** It rides the dock's row as compositor keyframes (rule 2): over the 184 px fold, 159 ms to 90 % and 339 ms to half a pixel, 10.2 frames at 30 fps (`fuse.json`, `clocks`). On a dock with a seat beside it the goo's filter is on for every close, and WebKit does not composite an element with `filter: url()`, so for those frames the plate's window, inside the fusion layer, is painted on the page's thread (BM-12, under G-7). The controls' window sits outside the layer and would stay on the compositor, so on a busy page the seats would run ahead of the glass's edge (`motion-canon.md` P0a: "The surface moves before its content"). While the filter is on, the two windows therefore play on one thread: the controls' window's keyframes carry a leg the compositor does not take, or the two are driven together. The wave picks one, and a proof cell reads the seat's edge against the plate's on every frame.
- **Nothing is written per frame.** W-I.8's carrier (R-1: `composables/useDockBody.ts`, the loop, `BL/waves/W-I.8.md:48-58`, `:230`), which the dock-redesign minor ships and which writes the plate's `inset` and radius and each region's `clip-path` every frame, goes. It is BL's, so BM.md §8 sends BL its removal and asks the order of 12.0.0's dock work (W-I.8's own 12.0.0 row, BM-11, BM-12) on one branch.
- **Under Reduce Motion** the fold is 11.1.2's (IN O-430 item 8; O-364): the two faces cross-fade in place within 150 ms, and the window's edges ease on the same clock, so the box never changes size in one frame. Nothing travels but the edge the box's own size needs. It is an `!important` carve in the dock's sheet until P6's rule covers it (BM-3). The dip that D§5.3 gave the fold is struck: the fold's sign is its faces, not a transform, and a dip would blink the whole dock on every fold.
