## What it does

1. **The candidate.** A release branch with BM-9, BM-10, BM-11 (with BM-8's capsule window) and BM-12 (if its neck's count and its gates held). MIGRATION carries a row for each name BM-9 removes, with its replacement; CHANGELOG names the fault BM-10 ships with (a sheet flicked past its last detent can be slow to settle before Safari 27.3).
2. **The census** of each removed name and prop and each moved or removed stylesheet path, re-run at every site's newest ref on the day; a site that imports one is told in its message before the tag, with the line to change.
3. **G-4's reading, per entry,** as at BM-R1, with the goo's split module read where a site mounts a seat beside its dock, and daraksha's own neck credited only once its adoption lands.
4. **G-6 and G-7** on the built dock in both engines, the neck's frame count, and the frame-gap reading as BM-12 took it, each recorded in `FINAL.md`; a failing G-6 or G-7 removes BM-12 from the candidate, and a frame gap the filter adds goes to the owner with σ and the layer's region.
5. **The engine checks** each wave owes (D§15.3), re-read on the candidate.
6. **The sites are told** the release's contents and its migration lines, each in its own message.

## Proof

The G-4 table, the census table and the G-6 and G-7 readings in `FINAL.md`; MIGRATION and CHANGELOG at the tag.

Every site.
