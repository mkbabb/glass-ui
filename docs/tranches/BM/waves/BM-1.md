## What it builds

daraksha's `ThemeControl.vue` is a private repo, so its idea is taken and none of its assets.

- **The glyph,** in a 24 × 24 viewBox. The sun is a disc of r 6.5 with eight round rays from r 8.5 to 10.5 (stroke 2). Going dark, the rays scale to 0.5 about the centre, under the disc, and a night circle of r 5.75 at (15.5, 8.5) slides from `translate: 9px -9px` to 0 inside the glyph's luminance mask and carves the crescent. The night is filled black inside the mask, since a luminance mask cuts only under black. The mask keeps its own white and black under forced colours (`forced-color-adjust: none`).
- **One clock.** The rays, the night and the colour start together and ride one 200 ms clock: the shapes on present's curve, the colour on a curve that trails it, leaving rest later and landing with the shapes at 200 ms (`motion-canon.md` P0a: "Colour waits for shape"), named at the wave. The sketch's 50 ms stagger is struck, so a tap's answer ends at 200 ms (`restraint.md`: "hover and toggle feedback at 200ms or less"), and every leg on the page's thread keeps its 167 ms or more.
- **Colour.** `--section-color-5` (amber, `src/styles/tokens/light-dark.css:190`) in light and `--section-color-2` (indigo, `:187`) in dark, the same lines on `release/11.1.1`. The colour's transition sits on the `svg`, which carries `data-allow-motion` with the two groups, so it survives the flip's `html.no-transition` (the sketch put it on the button, which does not carry the attribute, so on atlas's flip the amber turned indigo in one frame). The glyph takes `currentColor` from the `svg`. `--toggle-tone`, set on an ancestor, replaces both, so a site with one ink sets it to that ink.
- **The button** becomes a `.plate` that answers hover and focus with the plate growing from four fifths as every plate does (the sketch's `--plate-from: 1` is struck: `motion-canon.md` P0's "a hover plate grows from the control" and P0a's "a plate behind a 40 px control grows from four fifths"). Its glyph is its one on-mark and its press's answer (A10-2, ratified with the study), so it carries the attribute W-B.17's plate reads (BL's row, BM.md §8): no on fill from `aria-pressed`, no press tone and no shrink. `useLiquidPress` leaves the component. The click binding is `@click="toggleDark()"`, so no event reaches the function.
- **The name** stays as 11.1.2 sets it (GL-7): a fixed "Dark mode", with `aria-pressed` for the state.

## The theme's one crossfade

- **Where it starts.** In `useGlobalDark` (`composables/dark/useGlobalDark.ts`), around the class write itself, so every way the theme changes crosses: a toggle (glass's, fourier's and daraksha's own, and the menu rows of value.js, keyframes and gaggle, which call `toggleDark()`), a site's own write to `isDark` (value.js's embed, `demo/app/embed/EmbedApp.vue:44`), a system scheme change in `"auto"` mode, which is the default (`R111` `useGlobalDark.ts:62-68`), and another tab's stored write. `isDark`'s setter starts the transition and writes the mode inside it, so a part that reads `isDark` is caught in the old picture too; a system or stored change reaches the class through `useColorMode`'s `onChanged` hook (vueuse 14, `dist/index.d.ts:1192`), which runs the default class write inside the transition. The first apply on mount does not cross. One flip runs one transition: a write made inside the running one applies its class directly.
- **Its own transition.** It is typed `theme` where the engine takes the typed form (the probe at `R111` `useViewTransition.ts:93-99`), and an `html` class stands in where it does not. The public helper `useViewTransition` and its reduced-motion branch stay as they are: it is `motion-core`'s export, which daraksha imports (`src/veil/film/Layer.vue:20`), and changing what it does under Reduce Motion would change a public name's meaning (BM.md §2).
- **What crosses.** Every theme rule is scoped to the type or the class. The page's old and new pictures, and every group a site names, cross by `opacity` over 200 ms on `--ease-standard`, on the compositor, so the ground, the words and the glass change together. `disableTransitions` keeps its meaning: the page's own transitions sit out the flip, as atlas chose, and the page still crosses.
- **The live glyph.** Each element that carries one attribute, named at the wave, takes a `view-transition-name` of its own only while the theme's transition runs, set per instance by `useGlobalDark` and cleared when it ends, with its old picture hidden and its new one live, so its glyph tweens over the crossfade and is not crossed with its old self. Glass's toggle carries it. A site's own toggle carries it on its glyph: fourier's (`web/src/components/layout/DarkModeToggle.vue`) and daraksha's `ThemeControl`, the two site toggles that flip through `useGlobalDark` with glyphs of their own. No name is fixed, so gaggle's two toggles on one page (`dashboard/src/report/page/PageDock.vue:117`, `:138`) never share one (two elements with one name skip the transition, `R111` `view-transition.css:9-10`). `toggleDark` takes no argument (BM part 3's second fold struck the element argument).
- **A flip that comes back.** `::view-transition` takes `pointer-events: none`, so the page stays live through the crossfade. A flip inside it turns it round from where it is: the running animations reverse, and the class returns on the reversed run's last frame, before the pictures are removed. No second transition starts, since a second would skip the running one to its end (P0a rule 6).
- **Engines.** Glass's stated floor is its CSS target, Safari and iOS 16.4, Firefox 114 and Chrome 120 (`R111` `vite.targets.ts:4-10`). Same-document view transitions reached Safari in 18 and Firefox in 144, by their release notes (an inference until a strip reads them). Below those, the flip is O-353's other form: a colour transition of 200 ms on `--ease-standard` on the page's ground and text, set on `html` for the flip's length.
- **Timing.** `isDark` changes when the transition's update runs, a frame after the tap rather than at once. `onFlipSettled` still drains once, one frame after the class lands; its comment's "No View Transition is involved anywhere in this path" (`R111` `useGlobalDark.ts:54-56`) is amended in the same commit. atlas (`src/charts/composables/useEChart.ts:834`) and fourier (`web/src/App.vue:65`) read the hook, and their notes say so where it matters.
- **Reduce Motion.** The glyph does not move and takes no name, so its change rides the page's crossfade, which runs 150 ms under Reduce Motion (O-350's ceiling for a reduced fade, P6; an opacity change on the compositor, so the 30 fps line does not reach it). The reduced crossfade is a `theme`-scoped `!important` carve in `styles/view-transition.css` that out-specifies its `animation: none !important` on every view-transition pseudo (`R111` `:28-34`), which stays for every other transition until P6's rule ships (BM-3). Nothing in the toggle needs a reduced carve of its own.
- **Sites' own names.** Four sites name elements for view transitions of their own, and the crossfade meets them: keyframes names its dock and every seat of the active layer under any running transition (`demo/app/dock/ChromeDock.vue:723-734`), and its own comment at `:716-720` records that a named dock is a backdrop root that blurs nothing behind it; fourier's header stands named (`web/src/components/layout/AppDock.vue:916-918`), with untyped rules that hide its old picture and leave its group and new picture unanimated (`:1040-1048`), and its canvas stage is named under no reduced motion (`web/src/components/visualization/VisualizationView.vue:889-893`); daraksha's dock stands named (`src/shell/SiteDock.vue:581-584`), with untyped rules that fade its old picture out in 89 ms and its new one in after it on its page clock (`src/App.vue:95-119`), and its ground groups have untyped rules on its page clocks (`src/styles.css:238-249`); gaggle's hero stands named (`dashboard/src/components/Hero.vue:40`). Glass's theme rules give every group the crossfade's 200 ms. A site's unlayered rule beats glass's layered one, so keyframes, fourier and daraksha are asked to scope their rules to their own transitions' types (fourier's routes are already typed `route`, `web/src/router/index.ts:14-18`); until they do, keyframes' dock blurs nothing for the 200 ms of each flip, fourier's header shows its new theme from the flip's first frame, changing only on its own transitions, and daraksha's dock and ground groups cross on its page clocks. gaggle's hero and fourier's canvas stage carry no rules of their own and cross with the page.
- **The count.** A tap runs three legs on the toggle, the rays, the night and the colour: one of the seven yields, by O-428, named in `restraint.md`'s ceiling, and the button's root carries `data-motion-count="3"` (G-3). The page's crossfade is the page's one leg, not the control's. A hover runs two, the plate's fade and its growth.

## Inputs folded

- **The theme change as one tween** (IN O-430, item 9's second half; IN O-436 (6), "With the toggle's new face: the theme flip"; O-353's ruling). 11.1.2 puts the dock's words and glyph on the plate's clock. BM-1 makes the rest of the page cross on the same flip, above. The formation's line "BM builds no page-wide colour tween: the study designs none" is struck: the ruling and both placements ask for it.
- **The specular rest light** (S112, the pointer-light section). The rest-light selector on `release/11.1.1` (`src/styles/glass/material.css:350-375`) lists the dock's own controls and not `.dark-mode-toggle-button`, so the toggle carries no rest light today, and BM-1 adds none.
- **More contrast and forced colours.** Both arms of `src/styles/accessibility.css:9-51` take `[aria-pressed="true"]`, so in dark mode they would draw a selection border on the toggle, a second sign of a state the glyph already shows. The proof reads it. If the arms ring the toggle, the same attribute that keeps the plate's on fill away narrows them too.
