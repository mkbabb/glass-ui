## The minor that brings the dock redesign

The dock's seats move to a flat hover plate in the seat's own tone, at 6.5 % in light and 5 % in dark, with the current seat at 10 %, and the glyph keeps its colour on hover. Those values come with the minor that brings the dock redesign, and you don't need to change anything for them.

Glass's dark-mode toggle gets a new face after daraksha's, less ornate and in colour, and a theme change glass starts becomes one 200 ms crossfade of the whole page rather than a flip in one frame. color doesn't mount glass's toggle, so the new face only reaches color if you put a toggle in the dock. The crossfade does reach it: your "Dark mode" checkbox rows call glass's `toggleDark()` (`demo/shell/dock/menus/ProfileSection.vue:251`, `MobileMenuDropdown.vue:160`), so color's own flips cross from this minor, and so does a flip from the system's setting. The embed sets `isDark` inside a view transition of its own (`demo/app/embed/EmbedApp.vue:44`, `:55-62`); glass's crossfade replaces it on each flip, so that transition can go when you move the pin. The same minor gives the theme seat a small round dock of its own, which a page places in a corner on the main dock's baseline. Glass doesn't couple the two docks in this minor, so the main dock stays anchored where it is rather than re-centring with a pair. The pull-apart and join I described on 12 November come in 12.0.0, below.

## The minor with the new springs, sliders and menus

- Every Slider grows while it's used, on a phone and on a desktop: on a press, on Tab, and under a resting mouse after 120 ms. That's the expanded view I asked to keep, refined after the ChatGPT app. Its own track grows out round the fill by up to 8 px a side and goes back over the same 200 ms, and while it's used the root's box doesn't move. On a touch screen the slider's own box becomes 44 px tall. With Reduce Motion on, the grown track fades in where it stands instead of growing. It reaches all six of your sliders: the channel sliders (`ComponentSliders.vue:63`), `ConfigSliderPane.vue:217`, the two in `ExtractControls.vue` (`:48` and `:77`), `GenerateControls.vue:366` and `GradientVisualizer.vue:313`.
- Sliders that have a fill are thumbless by default, as I asked. Your thumb token at `demo/styles/foundation.css:499` paints the knob in the fill's colour on every slider but the spectrum ones (`:498`), so on the scrubbers the change reads as the fill's own rounded end.
- Your six channel sliders are spectrum sliders (`ComponentSliders.vue:65`): the ramp is the track (`:237`) and there's no fill to end in a cap, so they keep their ring as the mark of the value. The ramp stays on the track's own box, where each colour still lines up with its value, and the grown margin round it is left clear. Extract's count takes the same form when it shows a gradient (`ExtractControls.vue:50`). A thin capped line in place of the ring is being designed beside the discrete form, and I'll show it to you before any code; if it isn't ready by this minor's candidate, the ring stays.
- A slider with a small set of steps gets a discrete form, after my note on your "Colors" count: thicker and shorter, with its stops drawn as dots the value snaps to. Its design comes first, and I'll show it to you before it's built; `GenerateControls.vue:366` (1 to 12) and Extract's count (1 to 16) are where I'll check it. If the design isn't ready by this minor's candidate, it comes in the minor after.
- The fuller body, larger satellites and calmer motion I asked for on your hero Blob are designed once 11.1.2 has measured the jitter I saw, and I'll show you the design before any code. Your hero draws its own geometry: `HeroBlob.vue:201-202` scales its own values by your config over glass's defaults, and that config is a clone of glass's defaults (`heroBlobConfig.ts:15`), so a new glass default divides out and never reaches it. So the body and satellites ship as glass's hero preset, `BLOB_HERO`, and the calmer motion in the defaults your clone already takes. The fission you set to split the colony on most merge windows (`HeroBlob.vue:182`) is your own, and the jitter may be that, so the measurement is being taken on your hero's own values too; my fourth question asks whether the fission stays.
- The mark under SegmentedTabs (`MixSourceSelector.vue:147`, `AdminNamesPanel.vue:28`) becomes one capsule that slides, with no lift or stretch, and arrow keys fade it across. Your DockLayerGroup hides its switcher (`Dock.vue:305`), so nothing changes there.
- Menus grow from their trigger. The finger slide on a phone is something a menu turns on, off by default, so none of your menus changes.
- Every spring becomes a single curve. WebKit's source and release notes say Safari runs curves of that kind off the page's thread in every version glass supports, though nobody has timed it on an iPhone yet. A menu's grow and a slider's growing track still run on the page's thread, each in 370 ms or less.
- Your plates land on glass's `--spring-panel` (`demo/app/composables/boot/overture.css:14-18`, `:97`, `:129`). That curve keeps its small overshoot, since glass leaves it for a site's own spatial morph, while glass's own entrances move to `--spring-bloom`, which lands without passing its rest. If you'd like the plates to land without the overshoot, `--spring-bloom` is the one to point them at.
- Glass's canvases, your hero Blob among them, arrive by the fade alone: the warm lift past full brightness at the fade's peak leaves, since glass no longer animates a filter on an entrance. The fade still never dims or greys the field, the form you asked for.
- With Reduce Motion on, glass's fades and colour changes keep a short clock of 150 ms or less instead of cutting, and nothing travels.
- By my estimate color's stylesheet shrinks by about 1.2 KB and its first-load script by about 0.8 KB, with about 1 KB loading later on a first touch. Each figure is for a part on its own, so your build is what closes them.
