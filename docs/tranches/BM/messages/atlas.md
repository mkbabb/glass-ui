# To atlas (sci-report)

Hi,

I've finished the design work on glass's next round of motion and surfaces, and I wanted to walk you through what it brings to atlas, release by release, and what I'd like to hear back. I held all of it to the bounds you sent: no box moves on hover, open or fold, nothing new runs at mount on /sci, Safari gets the same parts Chromium does, and keyboard focus, `aria-selected` and VoiceOver order stay as they are.

Glass now numbers its minors in the order they ship, so I'll name each release by what it carries and send you its number the day it's cut. 11.1.1 and 11.1.2 carry the fixes from your lists, your dropdown films among them, and they're separate from this note. The first minor after 11.1.2 carries your DataTable asks and the Dialog that grows from the button that opened it; nothing below changes either of them. Everything here reaches atlas once its peer range (`^10.1.0`, `package.json:118`) and dev pin (`:147`) move to 11, and the dashboards once their pin (`dashboards/package.json:17`, `10.1.0`) does. I read `u/s24-atlas` and `u/s24-sci` for the lines below.

## The minor that brings the dock redesign

Glass's dark-mode toggle gets a new face, in colour. It's a warm amber sun whose rays draw in under its disc while a cool indigo night slides across to carve the crescent, the rays, the night and the colour all starting together on one 200 ms clock. `DockThemeToggle.vue:29` mounts glass's toggle, so the new face reaches `Dock.vue:631`, `:717` and `DockFoot.vue:61` with no change from you.

The flip of the page itself changes too. From this minor, a theme change glass starts is one 200 ms crossfade of the whole page, ground, words and glass together, and that holds with `disableTransitions` on, as you have it (`DockThemeToggle.vue:12`): the setting still keeps your elements' own transitions out of the flip, and the page crosses from one theme to the other as one picture rather than in one frame. The toggle's glyph tweens above the crossfade. With Reduce Motion on, the glyph stays still and the crossfade runs 150 ms, so nothing cuts. A flip from the system's own setting crosses the same way, and a second tap during the crossfade turns it round from where it is. Safari before 18 and Firefox before 144 have no view transitions, so there the ground and the words take the same 200 ms as a colour change instead. One timing moves: the class now lands when the crossfade's update runs, a frame after the tap rather than at once, and your charts' `onFlipSettled` hook (`src/charts/composables/useEChart.ts:834`) still runs once per flip, a frame after the class lands.

The toggle's hover becomes a plate growing out from just inside the button, and a press is answered by the glyph alone, with no shrink. In dark mode it rests unfilled, as it does today, so it never reads as a second current seat in your docks. In the minor that brings the dock redesign, each seat's hover takes the seat's own tone at 6.5 % in light and 5 % in dark, and the current seat sits at 10 %. The glyph keeps its colour on hover.

## The minor with the new springs, sliders and menus

- Every spring becomes a single curve. WebKit's source and release notes say Safari runs curves of that kind off the page's thread in every version glass supports, though nobody has timed it on an iPhone yet. Overlays, the route zoom and chart bars no longer pass their resting place on the way in, and the carets on your Selects (`DataAside.vue:128`, `VizOptionsBand.vue:82`, `YearScrubber.vue:229`) turn to rest without passing it. Three things still run on the page's thread, each in 370 ms or less: a menu's grow from its button, a slider's growing track, and the toggle's glyph.
- Your rule's seam (`src/editorial/AnimatedRule.vue:185`) draws in on glass's `--spring-panel`. That curve keeps its small overshoot, about 4 % past full width, since glass leaves it for a site's own spatial morph, while glass's own entrances move to `--spring-bloom`, which lands without passing its rest. If you'd like the seam to stop at its full width, `--spring-bloom` is the one to point it at.
- With Reduce Motion on, glass's fades and colour changes keep a short clock of 150 ms or less instead of cutting, and nothing travels.
- The selection mark under SegmentedTabs becomes one capsule that slides, at its option's size, with no lift and no stretch, and arrow keys fade it across instead of sliding it. In atlas that's `YearScrubber.vue:213`; in the dashboards it's the five filter strips, `EcfFilter.vue:218` and `:298`, `ConsultantFilter.vue:64` and `:79`, and `UsfFilter.vue:79`.
- Under forced colours and more contrast, today's rule draws a 2 px border on the chosen tab, and a three-tab strip's boxes shift by 8 px when the choice moves to the widest tab. From this minor the sign is drawn on the mark itself, so no box moves in either mode.
- Every Slider grows while it's used: on a press, on Tab, and under a resting mouse after 120 ms. Its own track colour grows out by up to 8 px on each side and goes back on the same 200 ms. While it's used the root's box doesn't change and the grown track takes no taps, so a tap meant for a neighbour still reaches it. On a touch screen the slider's own box becomes 44 px tall, the finger-size target you asked for, and the grown track fits inside it. I'm holding the grown track to add nothing to the scrollable width of a box around it, and I'll read that on the shape of your Filters body, at a phone's width and a desktop's, before the release candidate goes out. That covers `RangeFacet.vue:62` and the five sci filters (`DemandFilter.vue:63`, `EcfFilter.vue:366` and `:383`, `SciFilter.vue:189`, `UsfFilter.vue:100`). A slider turns the growth off with `:expand="false"`. With Reduce Motion on, the grown track fades in where it stands instead of growing.
- Sliders that have a fill are thumbless by default from this minor, so the fill ends in its own rounded cap and that cap is what the finger pulls.
- A slider with a small set of steps gets a discrete form: thicker and shorter, with its stops drawn as dots the value snaps to, at a length set by its stop count. `RangeFacet.vue:62` and the sci filters step by 1, so the form may reach them, depending on where the design puts the line for a small set (my eighth question). You'll see the design before it's built, and if it isn't ready by this minor's candidate it comes in the minor after.
- Menus can take a finger slide on a phone: press, slide along the rows and lift on the one you want, and a row the finger only passes over never fires. It's something a menu turns on, off by default, and your phone section menu, the `cell` form of `DockSectionSelect.vue:117`, is one of the two menus that asked for it (my fourth question). Its code loads on the trigger's first touch.
- The dock's scroll progress line (`DockProgressRim.vue:20`) reveals its stroke by a sliding window rather than redrawing a clip on each frame. It still draws the beat progress you feed it.
- Nothing atlas calls is removed in this minor.

## 12.0.0

The dock's fold changes so the glass's edge cuts each seat as it passes and nothing fades but the first seat trading with the folded glyph. Your two detented sheets, `FilterPanel.vue:471` and `EntityDetail.vue:405`, become native scrollers that snap to their heights, and `v-model:detent` changes once the sheet comes to rest; I'm testing that shape in a non-modal dialog like yours, where a swipe on the sheet must not move the page. On iPhones whose Safari predates 27.3, a sheet flicked past its top height can take a moment to settle, and the height it reports waits for that. None of the composables 12.0.0 removes is imported in atlas or the dashboards (the `useScrollChrome` in `Dock.vue:64` is atlas's own and stays yours). The goo that joins a seat to its dock, if it ships in 12.0.0, loads only if a dock of yours puts a seat beside it.

## What I'd like to hear back

I read your sheets before writing this, and both sit in non-modal Dialogs (`FilterPanel.vue:470` and `EntityDetail.vue:404`), so I've left that question out. Here's what's still open, with what ships if I don't hear from you, and the release candidate that fixes each default.

1. `DockThemeToggle.vue:34` passes its own `aria-label` ("Switch to dark theme" or "light theme"), which overrides the toggle's fixed name, "Dark mode", and with `aria-pressed` a screen reader then hears the state twice. Could you drop that label when you move to the dock-redesign minor? The toggle keeps your label until you do.
2. The new toggle carries its own colour, and you hold to one focal colour per page. Would you like your docks to keep the amber and indigo, or to set `--toggle-tone` on the dock so the toggle draws in your ink? The coloured toggle ships unless you'd rather have your ink, settled by the dock-redesign minor's candidate.
3. Does any of those sliders sit within 8 px of something its grown track would cover on a desktop, or inside a box that clips its overflow by less than 8 px? If I don't hear otherwise by the springs-and-sliders minor's candidate, the growth stays on everywhere, and you'd set `:expand="false"` where it gets in the way.
4. Could you turn the finger slide on for the phone section menu when you move to that minor? I'll send the setting's name with the release notes. It also reads the section list's height once each time the menu opens on a phone; would that read show up in the /sci budget your suites hold at 390? Until you set it, the menu behaves as it does today.
5. `RangeFacet` is a two-value slider, so its two rounded ends become the grips once sliders go thumbless. Would you like it to keep its two knobs instead? It goes thumbless unless you say so by the springs-and-sliders minor's candidate.
6. Does `npm run check:boxes`, or any other suite, read a slider's painted size while it's held, or a strip's tab boxes under forced colours? I'll send you the minor's release candidate either way, and I'd be grateful if you could run your box-identity suites on it.
7. Does any suite read a sheet panel's box while it's being dragged, and does any page set `--sheet-enter-skew`? The detent changes only at rest, and the skew stays at 0, unless you tell me something depends on either before 12.0.0's candidate.
8. If the discrete form reaches `RangeFacet` or any of the sci filters, would you keep any of them as they are? Glass will have a prop for that, and the form ships wherever the design's line puts it unless you name one, settled by the candidate of the minor that carries it.
