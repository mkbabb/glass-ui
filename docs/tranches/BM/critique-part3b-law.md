### 2. The crossfade cannot turn round

- **Where.** `waves/BM-1.md:22`, `:43`.
- **Claim.** A second tap inside the 200 ms either lands on the root, since the view-transition pseudo-tree covers the page and takes the hit, or, if it reaches the toggle, starts a second view transition, which skips the running one to its end: the page jumps from its half-crossed picture to the new theme in one frame and then crosses back. A change that can come back inside a tenth of a second turns round from what is on screen.
- **Evidence.** `MC:93-98` (P0a rule 6). `MC:105-107` ("no more than two fifths of the travel in one 60 Hz frame"). BM-1's proof (`:43`) films one flip each way and never a reversal.
- **Remedy.** `::view-transition { pointer-events: none }` so the page stays live, and a second flip during the crossfade reverses the running animations and restores the class with no new transition. A proof cell: a second tap at 100 ms, no frame past two fifths of the travel.
