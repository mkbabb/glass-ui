## Triumvirate Dispatch

Per BA invariant 1 (the cardinal lesson) + the AZ W-REFLECT loop, a FAIL deploys three roles in sequence then re-reflects, looping until operative-PASS. The orchestrator may NOT re-dispatch the failing reflection alone; the triumvirate is mandatory for these triggers:

- **A recorded gestalt FAIL on any roster surface.** A surface whose whole-page both-mode read draws a "wtf" (a mis-alignment, an occlusion, a register that reads wrong as a page) is a FAIL even if its per-mechanism gates are green. This deploys: (1) RESEARCH — an opus lane root-causes the miss (the mechanism, the SOTA bar where aesthetic, the constraint map; no fixes); (2) PLAN — an ad-hoc wave spec authored per WAVE_SPEC (§0 RE-GROUND, defect table, born-RED gate, scope fence) joining `waves/` + PROGRESS; (3) REDRESS — a build lane executes under the full discipline (gates, captures, typecheck). Then the surface RE-REFLECTS from protocol step 2 and the record gains a RE-REFLECTION stamp (the last-line-supersedes verdict).
- **A diagnostic loop whose third iteration has not isolated the gestalt miss.** If a surface FAILs, redresses, re-reflects, and FAILs again three times without isolating WHY the page still reads wrong (the same mechanism-green/page-wrong gap that re-opened R3→R8), halt and triumvirate the ROOT — the suspect is a cross-surface interaction (a dark-register backdrop the surface composites over, a shell-hold race, a stale capture) the single-surface redress cannot reach. Do not loop on a value tweak on one surface.
- **A freshness RED under `--strict-freshness`.** If a surface's reflection capture goes stale (its declared surface re-mutated after capture, e.g. a sibling triumvirate redress touched a shared file), the capture re-takes; if the re-take changes the gestalt read FAIL↔PASS, that is a new verdict and the loop continues — a fresh capture can only ever RAISE the bar, never paper over a regression.
- **A roster-schema conflict.** If flipping a verdict or adding a capture path requires a roster-ledger SCHEMA change `proof:ba-gestalt` cannot read (a column rename, a verdict outside {FAIL, PASS}), that is the gate-design miss W-GESTALT-GATE's triumvirate trigger named — halt and triumvirate the schema, do not fork a second ledger.
