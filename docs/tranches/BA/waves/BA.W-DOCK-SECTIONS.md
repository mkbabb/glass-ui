## Anchor geometry (the BINDING concrete commitment — inv-6; no lane may re-land workaround #4)

The wave MUST commit to the concrete anchor so no lane lands the midline seat. The decided topology (hinge H3 arm a, the user's own R8-1/R8-6 language):

**The anchor element.** The rail line is co-located with a NAMED `<DockSeparator>` — NOT the dock edge, NOT the dock midline. On the SIDEBAR (vertical) dock the anchor separator is the ℱ-home `<DockSeparator>` that already demarcates the home control (`SidebarDock.vue` `#persistent` tail; measured at y=78 in `dock-rail-seat.md §1`). On the BOTTOM (horizontal) dock the anchor separator is the nav `<DockSeparator>` (the one before the trailing category-jump group, `BottomDock.vue:223`; measured x=664/729). The seam-locator reads the chosen separator's offset within the dock-frame; the decided direction is `dock-rail-seat.md §4 direction (b)` — the `<DockSeparator>` GAINS an `extend`/rail affordance so the SEPARATOR IS the rail's anchor (the divider and the rail unify into one primitive; the user literally says "where the dividing line is"), which also folds R8-9's section-divider model and R8-1's anchor-at-the-divider into ONE primitive. Direction (a) (render adjacent + read offset) is the specced fallback the impl agent may pick if (b) over-couples; the agent does NOT pick blind — it picks the one keeping the seam-position derivation deterministic.

**The inset axes (which axes anchor, which overrun).**
- SIDEBAR (vertical) dock: the rail is a HORIZONTAL line. It anchors on the BLOCK axis at the ℱ-separator's Y (`inset-block-start` = the seam's offset within the frame, NOT `50%`, NOT `100%`), and overruns on the INLINE axis past BOTH the leading (`inset-inline-start: calc(-1 * --dock-rail-extend-length)`) AND trailing (the line's `inline-size` = dock width + 2 × `--dock-rail-extend-length`) edges. The chips fan FLUSH in the trailing gutter alongside the line (butted to the seam, not 40px adrift).
- BOTTOM (horizontal) dock: the rail is a VERTICAL line. It anchors on the INLINE axis at the nav-separator's X (`inset-inline-start` = the seam's offset within the frame), and overruns on the BLOCK axis past BOTH the top (`inset-block-start: calc(-1 * --dock-rail-extend-length)`) AND bottom edges (`block-size` = dock height + 2 × `--dock-rail-extend-length`). The chips fan flush above (or below) the line.

**The overrun computation.** The line's cross-extent = the dock's content-box extent (read from the dock box) + `2 × var(--dock-rail-extend-length)`, centered on the dock so each side protrudes by exactly `--dock-rail-extend-length` (the knob re-semantics: a SYMMETRIC dual-side overrun, not the one-way `min-inline-size`/`min-block-size` floor of `rail-extend.css:139-162`). The `extent: "inset"` arm zeroes the overrun (flush at the edges). Because the line + chips paint OUTSIDE the dock's `contain:paint` via the `.glass-dock-frame` escape, the dock box stays INVIOLATE (the `proof:rail-extend` box-equality R-arm holds — the line feeds NO size into the dock's intrinsic box).

**The collapse-retract.** On collapse the chips translate along the seam axis back behind a stub at the dock edge (the rail's `inline-size`/`block-size` shrinks toward the seam, the chips' `opacity`/`translate` retract INTO it), leaving a SLIGHT protrusion (a residual `--dock-rail-extend-length × <stub-fraction>` stub past the edge). PRM-gated (snap, no travel).
