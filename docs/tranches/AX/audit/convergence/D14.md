## 1. The defect at source

The dock is the library's HEADLINE primitive (the entire AX A-band W01-W06 + the
big-dock/grid/wrap/rail/layer-group surface) yet its storybook presence is **scattered
across three categories and never reads as a coherent "dock section"**:

| Manifest row (`demo/stories/manifest.ts`) | SFC | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `navigation/dock` (`:162`) | `navigation/dock.vue` (370 ln, 8 sections) | collapsible · always-expanded · select/dropdown triggers · hover-popover · overflow=wrap · big-dock card+grid · background pause toggle |
| `navigation/dock-layers` (`:163`) | `navigation/dock-layers.vue` (267 ln) | drill-in · switcher rail · rail-hosted layer stack · collapse-while-switching · vertical overflow |
| `navigation/rail` (`:164`) | `navigation/rail.vue` (93 ln) | `variant="rail"` default pill + rounded shape |
| `foundations/dock-active-tokens` (`:86`) | `foundations/dock-active-tokens.vue` | the renamed token-ladder DEBRIS (W06 deletes) |
| `compositions/dock-with-slider` (`:237`) | `compositions/dock-with-slider.vue` | keepDockOpen contract (W06 folds into `navigation/dock`) |

So a consumer who wants to understand the dock must visit FIVE routes across THREE
categories (`navigation`, `foundations`, `compositions`), and even the three `navigation/`
routes are flat siblings with no parent "Dock" home. **There is no single section that
walks morphing → animations → layers → variants → rail as a coherent tour** — which is
exactly the D14 ask.

### What the dock axes coverage actually is (the showcase-completeness gap)

The dock prop surface (`GlassDock.vue:20-147`) is rich:
`variant` (dock | rail | instrument-strip), `shape` (pill | rounded | card),
`layout` (linear | grid), `orientation` (horizontal | vertical), `density` (4 rungs:
mobile · compact · comfortable · spacious), `overflow` (grow | wrap | scroll),
+ collapse / hold / layer-group / controls. Mapping the THREE existing stories' demoed
axes (grep of in-use props):

- **DEMOED:** collapsible morph, always-expanded, overflow=wrap, shape=card/rounded,
  layout=grid, variant=rail, orientation=vertical (layers only), the trigger controls,
  hover-popover, background pause, the layer-group drill/rail/collapse-while-switch.
- **NOT DEMOED ANYWHERE:** the `density` 4-rung axis (zero `density=` in any dock story —
  `mobile`/`compact`/`spacious` are invisible despite D15 wanting bigger mobile sizing);
  `variant="instrument-strip"` (only surfaced inside `compositions/instrument-chassis.vue`,
  never as a dock variant tour); `overflow="scroll"` (only dogfooded in the demo's own
  `BottomDock` shell, never shown as a story axis); the horizontal `shape="rounded"`
  (AW.W3b made `shape` paint on horizontal docks — `GlassDock.vue:61-62` — but no story
  exercises horizontal-rounded). The **morph + spring animation** (the W01 headline) has no
  dedicated "watch it morph" section — it is implicit in the collapsible recipe but never
  framed as the animation showcase the D14 ask names first ("morphing, animations").

This is the showcase-completeness half of D14: even after W06 consolidates the SCATTER,
the dock section is missing an axis-tour (variants/density/orientation matrix) and a
dedicated morph/animation showcase.

## 3. The gestalt fix — a Dock CATEGORY with a content-authored axis tour

The idiomatic, no-new-primitive fix composes the three waves' settled outputs into a
coherent dock section AND authors the missing showcase content:

1. **The Dock CATEGORY is W18's** (Scope-5) — a first-class top-level `dock` category in
   `manifest.ts`, sibling to Foundations/Substrates/Primitives, NOT a `navigation/dock`
   leaf. This already satisfies the "dedicated section" framing. The category's member
   rows: a `dock/overview` morph+recipes home (the consolidated `navigation/dock`), a
   `dock/layers` (the DockLayerGroup story), a `dock/variants` axis-tour (NEW), and a
   `dock/rail` (the honest rail). Reverse W06's RATIFY #1 recommendation — keeping three
   FLAT `navigation/` siblings is the scatter; the fix is a parent `dock` category WITH
   coherent member rows.

2. **W06 authors the consolidated `dock/overview` home + the honest rail** (already its
   scope) — but its RATIFY #1 should resolve toward "sibling rows UNDER the `dock`
   category" rather than "sibling rows under `navigation/`". Same files, coordinated
   category placement.

3. **Author the missing showcase content (the net-new half — the D14-specific work):**
   - A **dedicated morph/animation section** — a controlled collapse↔expand the user can
     trigger (a "Watch it morph" button driving `startCollapsed` / a held-open toggle) so
     the W01 single-scalar spring is VISIBLE as the showcase, not implicit in hover. This
     is the first thing D14 names ("morphing, animations").
   - A **variants/density axis-tour** (`dock/variants`) — one `<StorySection>` per axis:
     `shape` (pill · rounded · card on a HORIZONTAL dock, exercising the AW.W3b
     horizontal-shape paint), `layout` (linear · grid), `density` (mobile · compact ·
     comfortable · spacious — the 4-rung axis currently invisible, and the surface D15's
     mobile-sizing ask lands on), `orientation` (horizontal · vertical side by side),
     `overflow` (grow · wrap · scroll). This makes the rich prop surface discoverable.
   - This content rides the SHIPPED chassis (`StoryPage` + stacked `<StorySection>` +
     `<ShowcaseFrame>`) — NO new tabbed-sub-nav primitive (the D6 discipline: do not invent
     a chassis where stacked sections read fine).

4. **Coordination contract (the dedup-without-duplication):** the manifest `dock`-category
   rows + the IA gate re-baseline (`EXPECTED_TREE`, `proof:storybook-complete`,
   `proof:no-orphan-demo-route`) are W18's exclusive turf — D14's new `dock/variants` row +
   the morph section must land THROUGH W18 (the wave that writes `manifest.ts` dock rows +
   re-baselines the three IA gates) so there is ONE `manifest.ts` writer for the category.
   The SFC content authoring (the morph section in the overview SFC, the new variants SFC)
   couples to W06 (the dock-story-content owner). So D14 folds as: **a W06 Scope addition
   (author the morph/animation section + the variants/density axis-tour SFC content) +
   a W18 Scope addition (place those rows in the first-class `dock` category, reversing
   W06's RATIFY #1 toward category-member rows)** — neither wave is duplicated; each gains
   one explicit D14 Scope bullet on the SAME files it already owns.

## 4. Why augment, not net-new

A standalone D14 wave would race W06 for `navigation/dock.vue` + the dock-story content AND
race W18 for `manifest.ts` dock rows + the three IA gates — the exact collision both waves'
disjointness sections forbid (W06 §Disjointness vs W18; W18 §C.2 collision contract). The
machinery D14 needs already exists in those two waves:
- W06 already runs the π-lane live audit over the consolidated dock home + already touches
  `navigation/dock.vue`.
- W18 already authors the `dock` CATEGORY + re-baselines `EXPECTED_TREE` + runs the π-lane
  nav audit over every reinvented category.

D14 adds CONTENT (the morph showcase + the axis tour) + REVERSES one W06 RATIFY
recommendation (category-member rows over flat navigation/ siblings). Both are explicit
Scope deltas on existing files — augment is the no-duplication path. The only NEW decision
is "author the axis-tour content + present as ONE category" — the mechanism (manifest rows,
gate re-baseline, π audit, StorySection chassis) is wholly W06's + W18's.

## 5. Overfitting / precept check

- The new `dock/variants` SFC + the morph section are private demo helpers (≥2 sections /
  exercised axes) — passes the overfitting bar.
- NO new chassis primitive — `StoryPage` + `<StorySection>` + `<ShowcaseFrame>` is the

- The axis-tour surfaces SHIPPED props (`density`/`instrument-strip`/`overflow=scroll`/
  horizontal-`shape`) — it documents real surface, not contrivance (documentation-is-part-
  of-the-change).
- Clean break: no `MissingStory` alias, no flat-navigation/-dock tombstone — the rows move
  into the `dock` category, guarded by `proof:no-orphan-demo-route` (W18).
- Coordinated single-writer on `manifest.ts` (W18) + the dock-story content (W06) — no
  parallel manifest writer, honoring both waves' disjointness contracts.
