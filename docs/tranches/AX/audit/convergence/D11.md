## Why W09 missed it — the scope gap, source-grounded

W09 ("specular tune-to-subtle") retuned **exactly one** radial: the pointer-tracked **moving** specular at `glass.css .glass-material::before` — a `circle at var(--specular-x) var(--specular-y)` (`glass.css:118-123`) that follows the cursor and now reads the `--glass-specular-intensity-{rest,hover,active}` cohort at rest 0 / hover 0.22 / active 0.32. W09's FileBounds (the wave plan, "FileBounds" table) is `glass.css` (only `.glass-material::before`), `tokens.css` (only the new specular-intensity cohort), `dock-controls.css` (only the `--glass-highlight` hover box-shadow retire), two SFCs, one composable, one gate. **W09 explicitly carved out the rim and the curvature overlays** — SOTA-deepening item 2 states: "glass-ui's `--glass-edge-light` (AW.W22) is SOTA-correct and current; W09 must NOT touch it — only the moving-`::before` core color + the intensity ladder."

The D11 "corner-glow" is a **DIFFERENT family of radials** — fixed-anchor corner/edge gradients that W09 never had in scope:

1. **The chassis curvature overlay — `--glass-curvature-overlay` (`tokens.css:789-798`).** A `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% -20%, hsl(0 0% 100% / 0.06), transparent 60%)` composed into the `<InstrumentChassis>` background stack at REST (`instrument-chassis.css:38, :83`). The header comment (`instrument-chassis.css:4-6`) names it: "a radial-gradient curvature overlay; both persist at all phases." It is **pure white** (`hsl(0 0% 100%)`, L=100%) — the SAME pure-white-radial anti-pattern W09 fixed at `.glass-material::before` but left untouched here. Worse: its **dark arm is byte-identical** (`tokens.css:1698-1702` = the same `hsl(0 0% 100% / 0.06)`), so a screen-of-white reads HARDER over the deep canvas exactly as W09's RED-witness-1 described for the moving specular (`.dark` lifts the screen blend) — yet here the dark arm got no softening. The ellipse anchored above the top edge (`50% -20%`) produces the visible top-edge corner-glow on the chassis.

2. **The dock primary-tier corner radials — `ellipse at 30% 30%` (`dock-controls.css:303-311, :329-338`).** `.dock-tab-button[data-tier="primary"]` paints a `radial-gradient(ellipse at 30% 30%, color-mix(in srgb, var(--phase-color, var(--foreground)) 18%, transparent), transparent 70%)` on hover (`:303`) AND an always-on phase-halo `::before` at the same `30% 30%` anchor (`:329`, fires on any active `[data-phase]`). The `30% 30%` anchor is top-LEFT-corner-biased — the literal "corner-glow." At 18% color-mix with a saturated `--phase-color` (the chart hues, C≈0.20) this is a loud corner bloom on the dock's most-prominent control.

3. **The canonical `btn-audacious` corner radial — `ellipse at 30% 30%` (`utilities.css:782-786`).** The `primary-audacious` button family hover radial, `radial-gradient(ellipse at 30% 30%, color-mix(in srgb, var(--primary) 18%, transparent), transparent 70%)`. This is the source recipe the dock primary-tier (item 2) mirrors. Same fixed corner anchor, same 18% rung, bound to `--primary`.

**Root cause in one sentence:** W09 tuned the ONE moving specular it scoped and the wave/ledger declared the specular "fixed," but the library carries THREE more fixed-anchor radial glows (the chassis curvature overlay + the two `ellipse at 30% 30%` corner radials) that share the identical pure-white / saturated-at-fixed-corner anti-pattern and were never in W09's FileBounds — so the corner-glow the user sees on the dock + chassis was never addressed.

## Gestalt fix direction (token-first, no patch, no legacy)

The fix is the **same architectural transposition W09 applied to `.glass-material`, extended to the three remaining fixed-anchor radials** — there is no new mechanism, only the consistent application of the warm-cream-low-alpha + tokenized-intensity + dark-arm-softened discipline W09 already minted:

1. **Chassis curvature overlay → warm-cream + softened dark arm.** Re-derive `--glass-curvature-overlay` off pure white onto the warm-cream identity (`hsl(40 30% 96% / …)`, L<100% so the warm hue survives — mirroring the `.glass-material::before` core W09 already authored at `glass.css:120`), drop the alpha so the top-edge lift reads as a whisper, and give the `.dark` arm (`tokens.css:1698`) a genuinely LOWER alpha than light (today it is byte-identical — the unsoftened-dark defect). Better still, route the overlay's strength through a `--glass-curvature-intensity` token so it joins the same overridable-magnitude axis W09 established (one knob, override on `:root`, never a buried literal).

2. **The two `ellipse at 30% 30%` corner radials → demote the anchor + alpha to subtle.** The `30% 30%` corner-bias + 18% mix is the loud corner bloom. Tone it toward the W09 subtle-ladder magnitudes (a lower color-mix %, and consider re-centering off the hard corner to a softer near-edge or center-biased ellipse so it reads as a surface lift, not a corner hotspot). Bind the strength to the existing specular-intensity cohort (or a sibling token) so dock-primary + `btn-audacious` share ONE overridable magnitude rather than two hardcoded `18%` literals. This keeps the disco-CTA identity (still a phase/primary-tinted lift on hover) without the egregious corner glow.

3. **One radial-glow magnitude axis.** The deepest gestalt: the four fixed-and-moving radials (`.glass-material::before` moving specular, the chassis curvature overlay, the two `ellipse at 30% 30%` corner radials) are FOUR independent magnitude declarations of the same "soft inner glow" idea. W09 tokenized the first into `--glass-specular-intensity-*`; D11's remaining three should be folded onto the same token-first axis (the cohort + a curvature sibling) so the whole radial-glow family is one overridable, dark-adaptive, warm-cream ladder — never four buried literals at four pure-white/saturated anchors. This is the W09 SOTA item-4 "one specular owner per surface" precept applied at the library-wide radial-glow level.

All token-routed (`color-mix in srgb` for alpha per house pattern, warm-cream `hsl(40 30% 96%)` per the W09 precedent, a `.dark` arm that actually softens), no new magic literal, no per-component patch.
