## Purpose

Prove the new vocabulary absorbs the cross-consumer drift the W0 audit measured — without editing the consumer repos. Per user direction, consumer migrations belong to each consumer's own follow-up tranche; G's job is to produce the migration ledger that makes that follow-up mechanical. The discipline is *proof-by-ledger*: for every drift row in the W0 baseline, name the canonical replacement, cite the canon source, and project the drift drop.
