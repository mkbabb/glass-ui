## 0 — THE ORIGINAL (recalled)

**glass-ui was born as the shared glassmorphic design system** — extracted so the consumer fleet
(the TIL **slides** deck, the **speedtest** app, **fourier**, **sci-report**'s Connectivity Atlas)
would stop each re-forking warm-cream glass tokens, the √φ audacious type ladder, the dock, and the
procedural backgrounds. The founding identity, restated through every tranche:

- **Warm-cream glassmorphism** — partial-transparency glass over rich procedural backdrops, NOT a
  grey slab. (The BB regression that birthed BC is the betrayal of exactly this.)
- **The audacious √φ golden-ratio type ladder** — `text-display-1..5` + `-mega/-hero/-audacious`
  to ~352px, Plus Jakarta Sans + Fraunces + Fira Code. Large, confident, math-paper craft.
- **The dock as the staple of liquid transition** — the epigrammatic exemplar of the motion + glass
  primitives. Springy, squishy, morphing into arbitrary sizes, vertical + horizontal.
- **Procedural backgrounds** — aurora, goo-blob (metaballs), constellation, fourier, dot-flow,
  concentric, paper-grid — alive, interactive, WebGPU-first.
- **The design language** — glass · grid · math · large audacious typography · colorful audacious
  POPS (the icons) — suffused everywhere within a sense of proportion.
- **One source + one clock for motion** — keyframes.js + value.js drive all sophisticated animation.
- **Token-first, component-over-class, ≥2-consumer, no-backwards-compat** — the architectural law.

The through-line: **glass-ui should be the SOTA liquid-glass / iOS-27 / Apple-glassmorphism design
system**, and its storybook the proof. BC's job is to make the live paint finally equal that promise.
