## Goal (the gestalt)
Open `/substrates/blob` STAGE-1 story on Safari 26, Chrome, or Firefox and a single clean warm-cream blob is sitting there, breathing softly — a smooth round droplet with a few satellite droplets orbiting it, and as a satellite passes near the body they MERGE into one gooey amorphous shape (the meatball) then separate again, the bridge between them a smooth liquid neck, never a hard seam, never two unrelated discs. It is FLAT — no glassy specular, no shadow, no iridescence — deliberately minimal: the "it renders, it meatballs, it works on Safari" floor that proves the field is alive before STAGE 2 dresses it. The edge is crisp (fwidth-AA, not a blurry ring), the silhouette is one connected blob, and on Safari it does NOT flash. This is the first-principles blob: SDF circle + smin satellites + AA + fill, nothing more.
