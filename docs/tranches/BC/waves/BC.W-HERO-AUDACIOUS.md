## Goal (the gestalt)
Each category landing + each substrate hero reads as an AUDACIOUS, herostudios-grade display moment — a HUGE display title on glass-ui's √φ ladder (177-287px peak, not the starved 68px), DISTINCT per category: substrates wears a Droplet icon + an aurora-blue field, motion wears a Sparkles icon + a constellation-violet field, forms wears a FormInput icon + a teal grid — so you instantly know WHERE you are. Each of the 11 categories has its OWN newly-begotten SECTION LANDING — a `/<category>` hero at the LARGEST audacious rung (D1 `hero`, the title-size hierarchy peak) over its per-category field, with a bento grid of cards LINKING the section's stories. The cards (on the section landing AND the front door) are no longer flat grey slabs with text only: each is a bento card — a colorful per-category IconChip POP at the top, the category/story title beneath, a one-line blurb, a Fira-Code subpath chip, AND an inline LIVE mini-preview of the target (a tiny live `<Button>` row, a frozen aurora still, a mini `<GlassDock>` silhouette — not just a text link) — that lifts on hover. Color is RESTRAINED (the one-color-event rule: the per-category hue lives in the icon chip + eyebrow ONLY, the title + body stay warm ink) so distinct ≠ rainbow-noise. Every hero is the SAME standardized shape (the chassis) but unmistakably ITS OWN category — never the repeated grey-card grid the user hated; and the title SIZE tells you DEPTH (a section landing out-sizes its mains, which out-size their subs).
