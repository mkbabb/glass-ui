## Goal (the gestalt)
Open `/substrates/aurora` on Safari 26, Chrome, or Firefox and a warm-cream painterly aurora is ALREADY breathing across the full viewport the instant the page settles — a smooth iridescent mesh-gradient field by default, a thick oil-paint van-Gogh / Kuwahara finish when you pick the painterly medium, never a black void, never a frozen gradient, never a "renders slow" stutter. ## Target spec (grounded)
Five concrete moves, each token/math-grounded:

### T1 — Retire the dead-static `"auto"` fall (the #2 "renders slow")
`renderMode.ts` `resolveRenderMode("auto", …)` DROPS the `lowConcurrency` (`hardwareConcurrency <= 4`) and `saveData` heuristics (`renderMode.ts:147-160`). They predate the offscreen-park + DPR-cap + demand-gate that make a full-viewport aurora cheap, and `<= 4` is false-positive-prone in 2026 (research/viz/aurora.md §2.2). PRM is handled SOLELY by the substrate's live `matchMedia` freeze (one static frame then park, re-arms on un-reduce) — NOT a render-mode fall. The ONLY `"css"` signal that stays is the genuine software-raster guard (`isSoftwareWebGLRenderer`, `renderMode.ts:130-137`, VERIFIED present) — and even that is subsumed by `BC.W-WEBGPU-EVERYWHERE`'s try-then-rebuild. Result: aurora arms the GPU on every Safari-26/Chrome-113/Firefox-141 device. The `AV_AURORA_DPR_MAX = 1.5` sub-2× backing-store cap is KEPT.

### T2 — Await `armAsync` on the capture path (the dead-preview close)

The synchronous `renderAt` right after `createAurora(…, {mode:"capture"})` is the bug; the fix is a one-await ordering change. Every preset thumbnail bakes a real image.

### T4 — Port the painterly mediums to WGSL (the first-principles "NO FALLBACKS on Safari" close — THE keystone)
The painterly medium bodies port from GLSL to WGSL (`mediums.glsl.ts` / `oil-modes.glsl.ts` / `vangogh-medium.glsl.ts` / `brush.glsl.ts` → new `aurora-mediums.wgsl.ts`, spliced into `aurora.wgsl.ts`'s `fs_main` MEDIUM-operator slot at `:320-322`). The port is MECHANICAL — same math, WGSL syntax; the two `fwidth()` sites (the AA-edge + the structure-tensor variance clamp) become WGSL fragment-stage `fwidth()` (the goo-blob WGSL precedent did exactly this; WGSL `fwidth(e) == abs(dpdx(e))+abs(dpdy(e))`, fragment-stage-only, native on Metal/Safari — research/viz/goo-blob.md §3). The single-pass anisotropic-Kuwahara (Kyprianidis 2009/2010, research/viz/aurora.md §3.5) is the highest-value port — aurora is a PROCEDURAL field (no input texture) so the operator needs NO FBO: `mediumKuwahara()` re-samples `sampleBase` over an ELLIPTICAL kernel (4 rings × 8 angular taps = 32 procedural samples) oriented along the existing single-pass `structureTensorField`, 8 overlapping sectors, blended SOFT by `1/(1+variance^q)` (q≈4, the soft criterion → no 8-spoke pinwheel by construction). The recipe pins: structure tensor from Sobel `J = [[dot(Sx,Sx), dot(Sx,Sy)], […, dot(Sy,Sy)]]` Gaussian-smoothed; eigendecomposition → orientation + anisotropy `A = (λ₁−λ₂)/(λ₁+λ₂)`; polynomial sector weight `f(x,y) = [(x+η) − λy²]²`, η=0.1, λ=0.5 (Maxime Heckel 2025, research/viz/aurora.md §3.5 [S18]). The WGSL Kuwahara radius/sectors/q become NEW uniform scalars (extend BOTH `aurora.wgsl.ts:52-71` `Uniforms` AND `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts` `packAuroraWGPUUniforms` in lockstep — the typed-struct discipline, research/viz/aurora.md §4.3). The smooth default + van-Gogh hero + oil/oil-pastel are byte-unchanged on the GLSL fallback; the WGSL port is ADDITIVE (new uMedium dispatch in the WGSL `fs_main`).
