## Goal (the gestalt)
A `<Card variant="selection" data-hue="…">` reads as **a warm-cream liquid-glass card whose RIM glows softly with its own data hue, and which — when SELECTED — gains a slow dignified metallic-shimmer border**. At a glance: a row/grid of selectable preview cards, each tinted at its rim by its own series/category hue (a teal card, a violet card, a ruby card — the rim and the catch-light glint carry the hue, never the whole plate); the chosen one is wrapped in a swept gold/silver/bronze metal-shimmer rim that quietly sweeps once on a 6s clock (PRM-static). The unselected cards stay the calm warm-cream glass `BC.W-GLASS-IDENTITY` ratifies; selection is read by the metal rim + the brighter accent, never by a saturated fill flooding the plate. It is the iOS-27 "selected reads as glass + gilt" card the Atlas's I5 preview grid wants — glass on craft, with a chromatic data-keyed identity apple.com's web has no twin for.

## Target spec (grounded)
This wave mints ONE new component decoration — `variant="selection"` on `<Card>` — that COMPOSES the two BUILT seams. Five moves, all on the existing card (no new sub-system, no new compositing path):

1. **The selection decoration axis on `<Card>` (NOT a new SFC, NOT a new tier).** Add a `variant?: "selection"` prop (default UNSET — a bare `<Card>` is byte-identical to HEAD; `variant` is additive default-OFF). `variant="selection"` binds `:data-variant="selection"` on the card root and routes the chromatic-rim + selected-shimmer decoration through the SHARED seams — it is the EXACT shape the `surface` axis takes (a data-attr the CSS seam reads), NOT a parallel recipe. The `variant` name is the one the Atlas/I5 ask uses verbatim (`<Card variant="selection">`); `invariant 31`'s stale-prop WARN is UPDATED to recognize `selection` as a LIVE variant value (no longer a swallowed stale prop).
2. **The per-instance data-hue RIM (A-2 consume).** A selection card reads a consumer `data-hue` (a complete `<color>`) and a bounded `data-hue-strength` (default a calm `--selection-accent-strength`, ~16-22%, ≤ the W-GLASS-ACCENT bounded ceiling) → it SETS `--glass-accent: <hue>; --glass-accent-strength: <N%>` on the instance, so the EXISTING rim + border + catch-light glint tint toward the hue through the ONE landed seam. ZERO new rim recipe — the selection card is the second per-instance `--glass-accent` consumer the BB.W-GLASS-ACCENT ≥2-bar names (the IconChip-threading fold being the first). The hue NEVER tints the plate BACKGROUND (the A-2 distinct-axis fence — rim+glint decoration, not legibility).
3. **The SELECTED-state metal-shimmer BORDER (A-3 consume).** When `:selected` (a `selected?: boolean` prop + the `:data-selected` attr + `aria-selected`/`aria-checked` per role), the card composes `.metal-{gold,silver,bronze}-border` (the metal chosen by `metal?: "gold" | "silver" | "bronze"`, default `gold` — gold is EARNED for the chosen item, the BA.W-PHASE-PALETTE "gold is earned" doctrine). The swept rim rides the SAME `metal-shimmer-sweep` keyframe on the `--duration-metal: 6s` clock, PRM-static (the full static metal gradient at rest, the sweep only under no-preference). UNSELECTED cards carry NO metal border (the chromatic `--glass-accent` rim alone). 4. **The selected/unselected gestalt is rim-not-fill (the no-saturated-slab discipline).** Selection is read by (a) the metal rim, (b) a bounded accent-strength LIFT (unselected ~16% → selected ~28%, still ≤ the ceiling), and OPTIONALLY (c) a sub-perceptual `--glass-bg-floating`-tier lift (the "selected reads as glass" W-REGISTER-IOS tier, NOT a `--surface-tint` gray plate, NEVER a `--foreground`/brand-red fill). The plate STAYS warm-cream translucent — the BC.W-GLASS-IDENTITY floor is inviolate; the chroma lives at the RIM and the GLINT, never as a whole-plate flood.
5. **The a11y role contract (selection is a CHOICE, not decoration).** A selection-card GRID is a single-select chooser; the consumer wires `role="radio"`/`aria-checked` (or `aria-selected` in a listbox) on the card + `role="radiogroup"`/`listbox` on the container — the BB.W-CONTROL-TOKENS radio-semantics discipline. A `:pressable :selectable` card is keyboard-activatable (Space/Enter selects); the press composes the EXISTING `useLiquidPress` (no new press path). The selection card is NOT a `<button>` re-skin — it is a `<Card>` that announces its chosen-ness.

The KISS architecture: ONE new prop trio (`variant`/`selected`/`metal`) + two data-attrs (`data-hue`/`data-selected`) on the EXISTING `<Card>`, routing through the TWO BUILT seams (`--glass-accent` rim + `.metal-*-border`). No new SFC, no new compositing seam, no new sub-system — the "Card is the only new component" fence is honored by construction (the selection card IS a `<Card>` variant, the ONE mint).
