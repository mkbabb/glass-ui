## Goal (the gestalt)
Grab the `/dock/overview` dock and toggle it open and closed: it GLIDES — the box grows and the chrome materializes in one continuous, springy, liquid-glass motion that fills the whole gesture, settling with a tiny lively overshoot and then resting crisp. No stutter, no snap-then-wait, no mid-flight jank. The morph reads exactly like an iOS-27 control-center surface materializing: quick but never abrupt, the size and the chrome moving on ONE clock so nothing desyncs. Every transition on the dock — the morph, the hover lift, the held-state, the layer crossfade — runs on a compositor-promoted transform/opacity/filter channel, so even on a mid-range machine it is 60fps-smooth. The user's "they STUTTER / not buttery smooth" is gone because the spring curve that powered the snap now powers a glide that paints its travel across the full clock.

1. **The front-loaded spring curve (the stutter root, glass-dock-codebase.md §2.1 + §3.3).** The morph paints its travel against `--spring-snappy` (the same curve the tabs §3.3 abrupts on). The `linear()` curve in `src/styles/scheme-motion.css` (the `--spring-snappy` token) **hits 1.0 by 12.245% and PEAKS at 1.068 (16.327%)**, then runs DEAD-FLAT `1.00000` from ~49% to ~98% (verified — the curve string is literally `1.00000` across that span). So the dock box reaches its target geometry in roughly the first ~16% of the clock, then the spring sits dead-flat for the rest — the eye reads an **abrupt arrival** (the "snap") followed by a wasted tail, and on a frame-budget-tight machine the front-loaded burst (almost the entire displacement in ~3 frames) is exactly where the renderer drops frames = the perceived **stutter**.
2. **The chrome/box ARE already on one clock, but the per-property compositor-promotion is incomplete + uneven.** `src/styles/dock/layers.css:60-74` reserves `inline-size: var(--dock-morph-to)` and composites `transform: scaleX(var(--dock-morph-scale))` over it with `will-change: transform` (the CDP-Layout-flat floor — correct). The morph mechanism itself is SOUND and must be KEPT (glass-dock-codebase.md §2.7): the single-scalar `--dock-morph-t` clock, the reserved-footprint-`scale()` compositor transform, the `overflow: clip` aperture (`shell.css:178-199`), the V↔H topology-occlusion via VT crossfade. ## Target spec (grounded)

The root is that the `--spring-snappy`/`--spring-dock` `linear()` is normalized 0..1 and DISCARDS the settle time, so pairing it with the `DOCK_SPRING` 0.28s clock paints all the travel in the first ~16%.

The bounce↔damping formula (Apple official, apple-ios27.md §2.2): **ζ = 1 − bounce**, response ≈ visualDuration. The dock morph wants the iOS `.snappy` register — a crisp position morph that READS quick but settles over the whole clock with a small overshoot, NOT the front-loaded glass-ui `--spring-snappy` (response 0.35/ζ0.65, which front-loads).
