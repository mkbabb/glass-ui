## Goal (the gestalt)
Every spring in glass-ui FILLS its clock instead of snapping then sitting dead-flat. When the tab pill glides, it travels smoothly across the whole ~340ms and settles with one gentle liquid overshoot — not a hard jerk to ~90% in the first 100ms followed by a dead-flat creep nobody can see. When a dialog blooms, the bounce reads playful but controlled — the iOS ~15% overshoot, not the over-springy ~20% wobble that reads as "ringy." When a button is pressed, the squish answers in the sub-100ms iOS press window and rebounds with a tiny alive overshoot. And in EVERY case the opacity rides the SAME spring scalar as the transform — the surface materializes as one coupled liquid layer, never a fast-color-snap-then-slow-spring desync. The user reads the whole demo as "squishy, springy, quick, coupled" — the principles-of-web-animation feel — because the curves underneath have been eased so their PERCEPTUAL travel matches their visible clock.
