## The iOS-27 pull model (research/ios27-tab-switcher.md §1.2, §3.5)
This wave lands the leg that turns Register B (the PILL `BC.W-TABS-IOS` ships) into Register A (the FULL iOS-27 glass tab SYSTEM): the iOS signature is **"the glass doesn't just fade; it physically morphs from one shape to another, maintaining the translucent material throughout the animation"** (research §1.2). The pull model (research §3.5, the iOS interactive drag): the pill follows the finger ~1:1, STRETCHES along travel by VELOCITY (volume-preserving squish, capped LOW so it swells, never taffy-pulls), and on release FLINGS velocity-continuously to the NEAREST tab, settling with a small overshoot — a flick past a midpoint carries forward, a slow short drag snaps back. This is EXACTLY glass-ui's `useDragMorph` (BB.W-DRAG-MORPH) + the `:draggable` SegmentedTabs axis — the architecture EXISTS; the gap is the MATERIAL (the dragged pill reads as iOS glass — `BC.W-TABS-IOS`) + the eased fling spring (`BC.W-SPRING-EASE`) + the PAINT (verified live at this wave's close). The fling SPRING reuses a SPRING_PRESETS row (the eased `snappy`, the iOS drag clock) — NOT a new clock (research §3.5, §5 the engine-inviolate fence).

## Goal (the gestalt)
On `/navigation/tabs`, grab the active glass pill with a pointer or finger and PULL it: it follows your finger ~1:1, STRETCHES along the travel axis like a gel lozenge (a visible volume-preserving squish — wider as it moves, never a rigid slab), and on release it FLINGS velocity-continuously to the nearest tab and settles there with a small liquid overshoot. A flick past a midpoint carries forward; a slow short drag snaps back. The pill stays warm liquid glass the whole way — it physically morphs to the new location, exactly the iOS-27 segmented-control pull (research §1.2 "maintaining the translucent material throughout"). Tapping a tab still glides crisply (the click path is unchanged); the pull is the alive, primary, expressive affordance — grab → follow → squish → fling-snap. The pull is pure pointer + compositor transform (no `backdrop-filter: url()`, no WebGL), so it works identically on WebKit — the Band-3 cross-engine win, NOT a Safari-broken surface.
