## Goal (the gestalt)
The glass reads as MORE glass — richer diffusion, more light-concentration, the iOS-27 thick-material feel — AND its text stays MORE legible than before, both at once. You see more of the content through the plate (it is genuinely translucent), yet the controls and captions never drop below readable. The legibility is not bought by greying the plate opaque (the AZ disease) nor by thinning the glass to nothing — it is the iOS dynamic-range shift: the plate concentrates light and the ink rides the right register so the surface is simultaneously more transparent AND more readable. Every claim here is MEASURED (a captured WCAG contrast number over the composited plate, both modes), never "looks legible."

**The apple-glass ceiling, measured (research/apple-glass.md §1.1/§2).** apple.com is austere — glass on EXACTLY ONE layer (the nav), flat everywhere else, no lensing on the web (live-measured: the homepage carried 2 backdrop-filter elements total). So the SOTA glass-ui is "besting" is the nav recipe: `blur(20px) saturate(1.8)` over white/0.8, NO border, NO shadow, scroll-engaged — saturation is the load-bearing term (1.8 is HIGH), blur moderate (20). glass-ui is ALREADY past apple.com on five of six element rows (research/apple-glass.md §1.6 matrix): it ships glass cards (apple.com has none), glass CTAs (apple.com is solid-fill), and the OS-grade lensing/specular the web never shipped. So "more glass AND more legible" is NOT "more glass than Apple" (already have it) — it is (a) making the glass we ship read as ACTUAL liquid glass (the measured AA bar, the deep-tier push toward the apple.com nav ceiling), and (b) the differentiation: it floats over the warm-cream paper-grid craft, not over a photo or a flat `#f5f5f7` panel. The legibility bar is the apple.com-web-equivalent cross-engine floor (blur+saturate+tint — research/apple-glass.md §5, the lens is the Chromium-only superset, and Safari degrades EXACTLY onto the apple.com-web look).
