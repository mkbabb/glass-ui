## Goal (the gestalt)
No glass surface on any route carries a **spurious, unbounded radial halo over-painting it**. A viz card (constellation / aurora / goo-blob), a loading surface, an instrument chassis — each reads as its intended material with NO ghostly large soft circle bleeding across or past its bounds, no second glow stacked over the canvas, no halo spilling onto a neighboring glass plate. The user reads clean glass + clean viz; the spurious halo is gone at the root, and the structure makes it impossible to silently return.
