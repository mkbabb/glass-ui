# BC.W-CONTROL-SMOOTH — kill the control lag (quick coupled response) + square borders → rounded

- **Band:** 6 · **Status:** SPEC (tranche-dev; NOT executed) · **Sequence:** after `BC.W-RADIO-FIX` (it owns the radio STATE; this owns the CLOCK across all controls — author after so they land coherent). Coordinates with `BC.W-SPRING-EASE` (the spring re-derive — this wave reads the re-timed register, does not re-author springs) + `BC.W-BLACK-BAR` (the rim). Independent of `BC.W-DROPDOWN-FIX` / `BC.W-CONFIG-RIGHT`.
- **Owns / closes:**
  - USER-DEFECTS §F "Controls are **super laggy**; some have **SQUARE borders**."
  - Route-census `/forms/toggle` row "'square borders' witness (§F) → BC.W-CONTROL-SMOOTH".

## Goal (the gestalt)
Hover, press, or toggle any control — a Toggle, a ToggleChip, a SegmentedTab, a Select trigger, a checkbox — and it responds **instantly and crisply**, like an iOS control: the surface tints and the press squishes in one quick coupled beat, no sluggish ~200ms colour-fade dragging behind the cursor. Nothing feels like it is catching up to you. And every control's corners read **rounded** — the small toggles and toggle-chips are soft-cornered pills/squircles, not the boxy 6-10px-on-a-16px rectangles that read square. The whole control family feels light, quick, and consistently rounded — the "super laggy / square borders" complaint is gone across every form surface at once.

## Starting state (measured, file:line)
TWO roots, both shared across the whole control family (one fix each, library-wide):

**1. The lag — `transition-control` runs the surface legs at 0.2s on a standard bezier.** Live-probed (2026-06-18) on radio (`/forms/checks`), toggle (`/forms/toggle`), and select trigger (`/forms/select`): EVERY control resolves `transition-property: background-color, color, box-shadow, border-color`, `transition-duration: 0.2s`, `transition-timing-function: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
- Source: `src/styles/utilities/btn.css:55-59` `@utility transition-control { transition-property: background-color, color, box-shadow, border-color; transition-duration: var(--duration-fast); transition-timing-function: var(--ease-standard); }`.
- `--duration-fast: 0.2s` (`scheme-motion.css:77`); `--ease-standard: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (`scheme-motion.css:254` → `--motion-ease-standard`).
- 12 consumers (grep): TabsTrigger, AccordionTrigger, Checkbox, Toggle (CVA), Switch, CollapsibleTrigger, RadioGroupItem, SelectTrigger, Badge, ConfiguratorLayer/Row/root. So a single 0.2s surface fade is the control-family register.
- **Why it reads laggy:** 200ms is fine for a calm card cross-fade, but for a CONTROL the user actively pokes (hover-in, press, toggle), 200ms of colour/border/shadow easing on the bezier drags VISIBLY behind the pointer — the iOS register is a quick ~100-120ms surface beat coupled to an instant press-squish. The W-MOTION-CANON §6 doctrine (`tokens.css §2`) says surface props → `--ease-standard` bezier (correct — a colour cross-fade on a spring wobbles), BUT the DURATION for an interactive control should be the quick beat, not the 0.2s calm beat. The available shorter clock: `--duration-instant: 0.1s` (`scheme-motion.css:76`).
- The press itself: `tap-squish` (a separate utility) handles the transform-press; but the SURFACE legs (bg/border/shadow) lag behind the press → the "press is quick but the colour catches up late" desync.

**2. The square borders — mid-sized controls read `--radius-md`/`--radius-button` (6-10px) on a 16-24px control.** Live radii histogram (2026-06-18, `/forms/toggle`):
- `9999px @40px` ×18 (pills — correct), `9999px @26px` ×6 (correct).
- **`10px @16px` ×4** (Toggle reads `borderRadius: 10px` on a 16px control = `rounded-button` = `--radius-button` = `--radius` = 0.625rem = 10px → 0.63 of height, reads square-cornered).
- **`10px @18px` ×3** (ToggleGroupItem, same).
- **`4px @24px` ×3** (`--radius-sm` = 4px on a 24px control = clearly square).
- `0px @66px` ×3 (a fully-square specimen/card).
- Source: `theme/radius.css:36` `--radius-button: var(--radius)` (10px); `:45` `--radius-control: var(--radius-md)` (6px); `:18` `--radius-sm: 4px`. The Toggle CVA base reads `rounded-button` (`toggle/index.ts`). On a SMALL control (16-24px) a 4-10px radius reads square because the radius is a large fraction of the dimension. An iOS-27 small control is a near-stadium pill or a generous squircle, never a 0.26-0.63-of-height rounded-rect.

## Target spec (grounded)
TWO library-wide moves, both token-first at the ONE source:

**A — the quick coupled control beat (kill the lag).** Re-time `transition-control` to the QUICK interactive register without abandoning the §6 bezier-for-surface doctrine:
- `@utility transition-control` reads `--duration-instant` (0.1s) instead of `--duration-fast` (0.2s) for the surface legs — a quick ~100ms colour/border/shadow beat that tracks the cursor, the iOS control register. The bezier `--ease-standard` STAYS (a colour cross-fade on a bezier, never a spring — §6 binding).
- OR (the cleaner gestalt): mint a dedicated `--duration-control` token (the named control-press clock, default 0.12s — the iOS ~100-120ms quick beat) in `scheme-motion.css` beside `--duration-instant`, and `transition-control` reads it. This is the W-GLASS-CAL per-register-clock discipline: the control surface beat is its OWN named clock, retunable from one token, not piggybacking the generic `--duration-fast`. (Pin: 0.12s — bracketing the iOS 100-120ms quick-control register cited in apple-ios27.md; the press-squish via `tap-squish`/`useSpringPress` is the COUPLED transform leg on the spring's own clock, the two legs reading together per W-MOTION-CANON P3 fade-coupled-to-transform.)
- The COUPLING: the surface beat (bg/border/shadow on the 0.12s bezier) + the press-squish (transform on the spring) must read as ONE beat. `BC.W-SPRING-EASE` owns the spring re-derive; this wave ensures the SURFACE leg's clock matches the press perceptual window (the desync the radio/toggle live-probe surfaced — press quick, colour late — is closed by shortening the surface clock to the press window).
- The W-MOTION-CANON `proof:animation-coherence` (duration-aware) re-verifies the register stays §6-coherent (surface→bezier, transform→spring); the gate's EASING-TABLE-BOUND arm holds (no new ease minted — `--ease-standard` is reused; only the DURATION shortens).

**B — round the small controls (square → rounded).** Re-point the small-control radius so a 16-24px control reads as a soft pill/squircle, not a rounded-rect:
- The Toggle/ToggleChip/ToggleGroupItem `default`/`outline` base radius `rounded-button` (10px) → a control-appropriate radius. For a SMALL control the iOS register is a near-stadium pill: `--radius-control` re-points to a LARGER rung OR the small toggles read `--radius-pill` (stadium, capped to half-height by the geometry — a 16px control with `rounded-pill` is a 8px-radius pill, reading soft). The cleaner move: `--radius-control` (currently `--radius-md` = 6px) → `--radius-lg`/`--radius-xl` (10-12px) is STILL square on a 16px control; the real fix is the small controls read `--radius-pill` (the AY toggle-chip register) so the radius scales with the control (half-height), never a fixed small px that reads square at small sizes.
- Pin: the checks-atoms (radio already `rounded-pill` — correct), the Toggle/ToggleChip/ToggleGroupItem `default`+`outline` → `--radius-pill` for the inline-toggle register (a toggle is a small pill, the iOS model), keeping the `card` variant on `--radius-card` (1rem, the tile — a large surface keeps the squircle). The `4px @24px` control re-points off `--radius-sm` to the control register.
- The corner-shape squircle axis (`theme/radius.css:54+`, Chrome 139+ progressive enhancement) reads at LARGE radius only (the ROUND set = card+pill for small controls) — so the small pills stay ROUND (correct), the large surfaces get the squircle. No regression.

## Fences / invariants (must NOT regress)
- **Clean break, no alias** (MEMORY: no backwards compat): `transition-control` re-times to the new clock; no `transition-control-legacy`. The square small-radius is re-pointed, not aliased.

This wave shortens the surface DURATION, it does NOT move the surface onto a spring nor the transform onto the bezier. `proof:animation-coherence` + `proof:no-layout-animation` stay GREEN by construction (no layout property animates; the radius is static, not animated).
- **The per-spring clock fence holds** (W-GLASS-CAL Unit 3): the spring tokens (`--spring-*` + `--spring-*-duration`) are generated, READ-ONLY here; `--duration-control` is a NEW named bezier clock for the surface beat, not a spring re-tune. - **The `card` toggle tile keeps `--radius-card`** (BB.W-CONTROL-TOKENS N11): a large glass-card tile keeps the 1rem squircle; only the SMALL inline controls re-round to the pill register. The corner-shape squircle axis (ROUND set = card+pill for small) is untouched.
- **Coordinates with `BC.W-SPRING-EASE` + `BC.W-RADIO-FIX`** — SPRING-EASE owns the spring curve re-derive (the press physics); RADIO-FIX owns the radio STATE-fill; this wave owns the SURFACE-CLOCK + the small-control RADIUS. - **Token-first + component-over-class:** `--duration-control` re-tunes the whole control family from one `:root` override (the consumer-facing control-feel axis); `rounded-pill` is the component CVA register, no consumer CSS.
- **Byte-fenced (not touched):** the GL shaders, the glass tint/level/blur axes, the calm card cross-fade register (a `<Card>` is not an interactive control — it keeps `--duration-fast`/`--duration-normal`; this wave touches `transition-control` consumers ONLY, the interactive-control register).
