## Goal (the gestalt)
Both the bottom dock and the vertical sidebar dock have a real, restful collapsed state. Collapsed, each dock is a tight, crisp glass pill (a perfect circle for a content-only collapse, a small rounded capsule when persistent controls ride alongside) — NOT a sprawling bar and NOT a blurry smudge. The bottom dock at rest shows the core persistent controls (the home/category trigger) PLUS a handful of the in-category tab items as a compact summary — enough to orient you, not the whole strip. Tap or hover it and it blooms open to the full control row; leave it and it settles back to the pill. The vertical dock collapses the same way on its own axis (a column shrinking toward a circle). The collapse grows symmetrically from its center — never the right-anchored void that used to reserve empty space. On a phone the collapsed register is the summary pill alone, sized for a thumb, that taps open to reveal everything — a visible control that does exactly what it shows.
