## Goal (the gestalt)
A bare `<Button variant="glass">` (and the default + `primary-audacious` CTA) reads UNMISTAKABLY as a small slab of liquid glass — a real frosted diffusion of whatever's behind it, a bright catch-light rim on the top edge (no dark bar), a soft under-shadow lifting it off the surface, and a pointer-following gleam. Press it and it springs down with iOS physics (a quick squish), brightens at the touch point, and the gleam illuminates — "much more fun when pressed." The hero CTA (`primary-audacious`) reads as DEEP glass (the thick refractive Apple register) and on Chrome bends the light at its edge. The legibility never drops as the glass deepens — the label stays ≥4.5:1, warm-inked. It is the iOS-26 button anatomy: glass ON its background ("white on white is more white"), prominence carried by tint not size, the press alive. The user's "increase glass-morphism for buttons, while increasing legibility" — both, at once.

**The apple-glass differentiation (research/apple-glass.md §1.4/§3.3).** apple.com's web CTA is a SOLID blue capsule (`rgb(0,113,227)`, `border-radius: 980px`, `padding: 11px 21px`, `color: #fff`, `font-size: 17px`, `border: 1px transparent` — measured live, zero blur, zero glass) — Apple did NOT ship its OS `.glass`/`.glassProminent` button to the web. glass-ui's glass `<Button>` is the SUPERSET: a TRANSMISSIVE glass capsule revealing the warm-cream paper-grid craft beneath (where Apple's is an opaque slab), reading the warm tint + the pointer gleam + the press-spring + (on the hero CTA `:liquid`) the squircle lens. We KEEP Apple's transferable web signatures — the `980px`-class stadium radius (`--radius-pill`), the small `11px 21px`-proportion pad, the 17px control-text rung, **prominence-by-tint not by size** — and ADD the glass material + the deep tier + the lens. The `--glass-accent` per-instance data-hue rim is glass-ui's `.glassProminent` analogue (a CTA glows its accent hue at a bounded strength while staying ≥4.5:1 — research/apple-glass.md §3.3), over craft. The differentiation, stated: **Apple's web CTA is a solid-fill capsule; ours is a glass-on-craft capsule with the OS material the web skipped.**
