## Lane G — substrate REVERT: IconTooltip wrap-span breaks `w-full` descendants (Q-cos-13; audit-aug round-3 HEADLINE)

Per Qο Section 2 + screenshots `q-omicron-timeline-*.png`. Commit `25e1b5a` O.W6 Lane D added `<span class="icon-tooltip-trigger">` wrap-span around every `<IconTooltip>` child, styled `display: inline-flex; min-width: 44px; min-height: 44px` for WCAG 44×44 hit-area enforcement.

**Unintended consequence**: inline-flex containers do not stretch to fill their grid cells AND do not propagate width to `w-full` children. The keyframes.js `PlaybackRibbon` is the only callsite in the constellation that wraps a stretching child (`<Slider variant="timeline">`) inside `<IconTooltip>` — the Slider's `w-full` resolves against zero-width parent and shrinks to its 16px thumb-only. Visible symptom: the user-named "timeline is not correct" — the timeline pill becomes a tiny nub.

Test fix at runtime proved attribution (Qο §2): `.icon-tooltip-trigger { width: auto }` + child `width: 100%` restored the full 318px pill scrub track. The substrate revert needs to preserve the WCAG 44×44 hit-area intent (the original O.W6 Lane D goal) while NOT breaking width stretch — likely paths:
- **Path A**: replace `inline-flex; min-width: 44px` with `display: contents` + `padding: 22px` on the inner glyph (hit-area expands via padding without affecting layout).
- **Path B**: opt-in mechanism — a `:stretch` prop on `<IconTooltip>` that flips the wrap-span to `display: contents` (preserves WCAG-default behaviour for icon-glyph callsites; opts out for `<Slider>`-wrapping callsites).
- **Path C**: retire the wrap-span entirely; route WCAG hit-area enforcement through the child component's own padding/min-height contract.

Lane author picks at open per the L invariant "gestalt redesigns over incremental patches". Path C is most aligned with the invariant; Path A is the smallest correct change; Path B is the most consumer-flexible.

Cross-reference: the O.W6 Lane D audit logic that produced the original wrap-span did not include keyframes.js `PlaybackRibbon` in its consumer probe — same audit-blind-spot pattern as the rainbow-utilities D.W2.D retiral (Lane E). The substrate revert proof doc captures the lesson; W5 codification (invariant 32 / phantom-class corpus-grep gate) generalises it.
