## Lane A — scene-transition crash fix (Q-cos-15; P0 — unblocks four features)

Per Qρ F1 + Qσ D1. `demo/app/App.vue:106` wraps `<Transition mode="out-in">` around `<KeepAlive>` around a `defineAsyncComponent` scene. The async chunk resolves after first paint; the renderer null-derefs in `getNextHostNode` during the transition leave hook; the new scene's target DOM never mounts. This single bug masks the t-value scrubber, the bezier editor, easing presets, the duration control, and crashes square/amiga/easing on cold deep-link. CubeScene escapes only because App.vue statically imports it.

Fix: restructure the scene host — `<Suspense>` around the async component, with the `<Transition>` + `<KeepAlive>` ordering corrected (Vue's documented order is `Transition` > `KeepAlive` > component, but the async boundary needs `Suspense` between). Verify every scene mounts on cold deep-link. Also fix the post-crash route drift (Qρ F2 — `#/easing` spontaneously rewrites to `#/cube`).
