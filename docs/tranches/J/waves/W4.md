## Purpose

R2 surfaced two mirrored gaps:
1. Aurora's existing configurator is structurally sound but visually clipped + dark-banded at cold-load.
2. Blob lacks a configurator entirely (its story is 8 sections of static demos).

R2 proposed: lift the configurator pattern to a primitive — `src/components/custom/configurator/` + `useConfiguratorState<T>`. Aurora studio refactors to consume; blob configurator buildout stands up natively on the new primitive. DRY between the two.
