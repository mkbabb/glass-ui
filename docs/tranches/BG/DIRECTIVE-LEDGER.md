# BG — THE MASTER DIRECTIVE LEDGER (the anti-amnesia spine)

**Source:** the 9-slice archaeology corpus (`audit/archaeology/chunk-01..09.md`), mechanically
mined from the full ~1517-raw-directive historical record (the last 100+ session turns + the
AX→BF tranches), reconciled against `audit/P-historical-coverage.md` (the HEAD-verified coverage
matrix) and `PLAN.md` / `SYNTHESIS-PASS-1.md` (the locked 7-workstream partition).

**What this is.** The 1517 raw utterances dedupe to **94 DISTINCT canonical directives**. A
recurring demand ("liquid weight everywhere", "no legacy", "the dock is broken") collapses to ONE
row carrying its frequency band + first/last-seen + a STATUS judged against the **live-broken 4.2.0
reality** (not against any planning doc's self-report) + a BG DISPOSITION (the owning workstream's
convergence loop, or RETIRE-with-rationale).

**The binding rule (anti-amnesia bar):** EVERY distinct directive maps to exactly ONE workstream OR
an explicit RETIRE. No silent drop. This ledger is a binding input to every workstream's
convergence brief — each Movement-B pass must honor its directive-ledger slice.

**Status legend (judged @ live 4.2.0):**
- **ADDRESSED** — built, survives HEAD, and reads correct on a fresh capture (or is a process edict
  honored by the running cadence).
- **PARTIAL** — mechanism present, gestalt/wiring/fidelity incomplete; the user re-asked.
- **UNADDRESSED** — asked, never carried to a working state (no spec OR specced-never-built).
- **REGRESSED** — was working or specced-green, now broken/deleted/over-corrected at 4.2.0.

**Frequency bands:** `★★★★` chronic (≥6 distinct rounds) · `★★★` recurring (3–5) · `★★` repeated
(2) · `★` single.
