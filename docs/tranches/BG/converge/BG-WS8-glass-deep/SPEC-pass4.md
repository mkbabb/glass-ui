## 1 · GESTALT GOAL (unchanged — re-stated for the build agents)

**ONE glass material, every surface, every engine — that reads as a thick refractive lens of flowing structured light wherever it floats over the field.** An architectural transposition, not N bespoke shaders:

- The shell field (WS1) is procedural + script-readable. Refraction is a **second, displaced sample of the SAME field, inside the SAME GL context, with in-shader anisotropic structured-light synthesis** — no DOM rasterize, no `html2canvas` (Δ4-rejected), no second context, no staleness. The DOM glass becomes a **transparent window carrying its CSS chrome (rim+bevel+specular+border+content) AND its in-shader dimming term** (the warm-cream-plate-over-field recipe — K3); the GL composite paints the lensed, light-bent field beneath. **a11y-coupled (K8/M4):** under PRT-reduce / forced-colors / prefers-contrast:more the transparent window NEVER engages — the surface reverts to the opaque CSS plate.
- **"Suffuse component-wide" = the MATERIAL register, not glass-everywhere-maximal (the Apple HIG nuance, Δ3-validated by GlassEffectContainer).** Apple ships glass on the NAVIGATION/floating layer, NEVER on content. ONE register suffused = the CONSISTENCY of the single material register (no per-surface bespoke), the cheap material floor (frost + warm-cream tint + bright lower bevel + thin darkened separation edge + brighter moving specular) on the `.glass-material` comma-group on every surface — that alone reads as glass AT REST. The EXPENSIVE displacement stays OPT-IN per the one-deep-refractive-register-per-route budget (ONE shared field FBO feeds ALL glass surfaces).
- **The METAL FLOW read is delivered by OPTICS — anisotropic directional specular, NOT `saturate`, NOT caustics (K2).** The reference (`liquid-metal-...01.jpg`) is **warm-gold LIQUID DRAPERY: long near-vertical folds, bright specular RIDGE along the fold, dark VALLEY between — 100% LUMINANCE modulation of ONE warm hue (white→gold→bronze), ZERO chroma shift** (real-Metal-confirmed). Light-bending = rim-concentrated squircle displacement + Fresnel + a flow-aligned anisotropic specular streak (Ward/GGX directional stretch along `normalize(curlFBM)`) + a thin-rim chromatic fringe (Δ5 dispersion 0.02–0.03). The 289-tap Gaussian DROPS to ≤9-tap — sharp ridges, soft valleys.
- **Liquid-weight is POSSIBLE on the refraction itself.** Displacement/specular magnitude is a GL uniform driven by a SECOND reader of the EXISTING `--glass-btn-press-t` press spring `.value` (zero new springs), PRM-snaps to settled. The press-swell RETURNS in the GL tier (CSSWG#542 moot in GL). On mobile, the iOS-27 gyroscope-driven specular is the booked DeviceOrientation feed successor (the M9 pointer conic is the desktop analogue).

Every leg is compositor/uniform-only, PRM keeps-fade/drops-displacement, paints in Chrome AND Safari, and a11y-couples to the `--glass-level` brackets — the floor never depends on a Chromium-only filter.
