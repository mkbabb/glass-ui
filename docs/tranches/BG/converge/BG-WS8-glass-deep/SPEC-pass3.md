## 1 · GESTALT GOAL (unchanged from pass-2 — re-stated for the build agents)

**ONE glass material, every surface, every engine — that reads as a thick refractive lens of flowing structured light wherever it floats over the field.** An architectural transposition, not N bespoke shaders:

- The shell field (WS1) is procedural + script-readable. The DOM glass becomes a **transparent window carrying its CSS chrome (rim+bevel+specular+border+content) AND its in-shader dimming term** (the warm-cream-plate-over-field recipe — K3); the GL composite paints the lensed, light-bent field beneath.
- **"Suffuse component-wide" = the MATERIAL register, not glass-everywhere-maximal (the Apple HIG nuance).** Apple ships glass on the NAVIGATION/floating layer, NEVER on content. "ONE register suffused component-wide" = the CONSISTENCY of the single material register (no per-surface bespoke recipe), the cheap material floor (frost + warm-cream tint + bright lower bevel + moving specular) on the `.glass-material` comma-group on every surface — that alone reads as glass AT REST. The EXPENSIVE displacement stays OPT-IN per the **one-deep-refractive-register-per-route budget** (Apple's GlassEffectContainer model: ONE shared sampling region — the single field FBO — feeds ALL glass surfaces, dilated to sample an area larger than each element; NOT per-surface bespoke, AND NOT over-glassed content).

### 2.1 · The tier select is TWO orthogonal axes (K9 — the pass-2 "ONE oracle" sharpened)

```
                          ┌─ route HAS a named script-readable shell-field canvas?
  per-route refraction ───┤      YES → arm the field-refraction pass (Tier-1/2)
  gate (the NEW branch)   └─       NO → Tier-0 CSS material floor ONLY (no 2nd context)
                                          │
  createGpuSubstrate ────────────────────┴─ (for refracting routes) WebGPU? → WebGL2?
  (the GPU backend pick)                       Tier-2 WGSL refine     Tier-1 GLSL floor
```

- **The per-route refraction gate** is the field-canvas presence test (`document.querySelector('[data-glass-field-canvas]')` or the WS1 shell-aurora handle), NOT a capability query. A page-local-viz route (already `canvasCount=2`) does NOT arm the field pass — refraction is SUPPRESSED, the Tier-0 floor is the read. ### 2.2 · The a11y `--glass-level` coupling for the GL tier (K8 — the NEW binding precondition)

The `[data-glass-refract]` transparent-window state and the GL displacement/plate-alpha uniforms MUST couple to the `--glass-level` a11y brackets:

- **CSS half:** the `[data-glass-refract]` plate-drop is gated `@media not (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)` and `@media not (forced-colors: active)` and `@media not (prefers-contrast: more)` — under any of those, the surface KEEPS its opaque CSS plate (`--glass-level: 0`/`0.3` floor) and the transparent window NEVER engages.
- **GL half:** the registry reports a per-panel `a11yOpaque` boolean (read from the SAME media queries, event-cached on `change` — NOT per-frame); the GL pass SKIPS a panel whose `a11yOpaque` is true (passes the field texel through OR clamps `uPlateAlpha→1` for that panel), so the displacement zeroes and the dimming firms to solid. ONE source of truth (the media-query state), both halves read it.
- **The π arm (binding):** a PRT-reduce capture of a refracting surface shows the SOLID opaque plate (not a transparent window) with content AA-legible; a forced-colors capture restores the outline+silhouette. This axis is ORTHOGONAL to PRM-motion (which the substrate's one-static-frame-then-park handles) and was NOT in K1-K7.

### 2.3 · The dimming term + the ridge-local legibility valve (K3 + K12)

The warm-cream-plate-over-field dimming recipe (K3, unchanged): `plate = mix_oklab(uRungBg, uTintInk, strength); out = mix(lensedField, plate, uPlateAlpha)`, tint uniforms cached on `onFlipSettled`+resize (K4). **The K12 addition — the ridge-local legibility valve (decided at PT, conditionally minted):** IF the prototype shows the bright specular ridge core cannot clear AA ≥4.5:1 under text without killing the metal read, the dimming lifts `uPlateAlpha` where the local specular luma exceeds a knee — `localAlpha = mix(uPlateAlpha, uPlateAlphaMax, smoothstep(knee, 1.0, specularLuma))` — so the ridge stays bright at the surface edge while the plate firms under the content region. The dark-mode caveat is HONEST (flat 12% strength → no local-darken in dark; AA must clear on the worst-case bright ridge in BOTH modes regardless — the valve is the dark-mode guard). The valve is NOT minted speculatively — it ships ONLY if the PT measures the collision firing.
