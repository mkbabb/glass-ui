## 2 · I-1 · THE DOT-RING — the terminal material, built

**Spec, verbatim (EXEMPLARS-CODEX §B3 / EXC-17a):** *"nucleate → one hard dot → 7-dot ring of
deliberately unequal sizes, the inequality redistributing between frames → brighten → re-fuse to
one glow → hand off … the same luminous mass is impact flash, then pilot light, then spinner —
nothing created, nothing destroyed; the specular highlight IS the spinner."* Its *in our
language* column: *"goo beads in our palette, hue per bead, slow redistribution; blob-family
kinship explicit; filter-free."*

**What it replaced, and why that was the whole point.** The library's default work-in-flight mark
was a borrowed rotating-C border ring in `InfiniteScroll` and a rotating lucide glyph in
`EasingPicker` — the one loading idiom every CSS framework ships. The codex's design-language law
("never a trite or lifeless recreation") is not satisfiable by tuning it.

**The mechanism is ONE expression, not a score.** Seven beads hold fixed angular seats forever;
only their MASS travels. Bead k's radius scales as `sqrt(1 + c·cos(φ + k·2π/7))`, so total area
goes as

    Σ_k (1 + c·cos(φ + k·2π/7)) = 7 + c·Σ_k cos(φ + k·2π/7) = 7

for **every** φ, the seven unit vectors at the 7th roots of unity summing to zero. "Nothing
created, nothing destroyed" is therefore not a description of the animation — it is an identity
it cannot violate. The rotating maximum of that envelope **is** the spinner, which is B3's
"the specular highlight IS the spinner" read implemented literally rather than illustrated.

**The inequality depth is the house proportion, not taste.** `--dot-ring-inequality` =
(φ−1)/(φ+1) = √5−2 — the unique depth at which the largest bead's AREA is exactly φ× the
smallest's, so its DIAMETER ratio is √φ = 1.272, the same sqrt-phi constant `Toast.vue`'s overlay
padding ladder already rides.

**Every length derives from the box.** Bead diameter is pinned by tangency (at mean size the
neighbours just touch — the largest ring that is still a ring of beads and not of overlaps), and
the ring radius follows as `1/(2·sin(π/7))` BEAD DIAMETERS, a pure number. There is no pixel in
the register; sizing the host is the whole sizing API.

**Filter-free, and the goo arrives anyway.** No `filter`, no `filter: url()` (MOTION-CANON §8:
FORBIDDEN, "no new primitive may use it"). Channels are `scale`/`translate` longhands only —
§8's "safest channel we have". The blob-family kinship B3 asks for emerges FROM the conservation
law: coincident discs of conserved area separating **is** the metaball silhouette. See the
fission capture.

**Clocks, both named, neither minted.** Fission enters on `dock` (TRAVEL's row) at
`--spring-dock-duration` on `--spring-dock`; re-fusion exits on `dock`'s OWN reader
`--spring-dock-exit-duration` on `--exit-curve` — a bezier, never a spring (§7: "an exit must not
overshoot past gone"), and the exit reader belongs to the row that entered (#26's law). The
sustained redistribution rides `--duration-xl` (1s), the ambient rung: §4 REST bounds a sustained
loop at *period ≥1s, ~10× slower than any gesture beat*, and 1s is that floor, held as a token so
the tempo cannot drift from the ambient family by hand.

**Presence is the state machine.** No `active` prop and no `data-state`: mounting IS the fission
(`@starting-style`), unmounting IS the re-fusion (the `glass-dot-ring` `<Transition>` leave arm).
A prop would be a second authority over the same fact, free to disagree with the `v-if` that put
the element on the page.

**B3's hand-off has two legal endings, and both ship** — but the first one did not until the cure
cut, and §11 C-1 is the record of that. A ring inside a surface with room re-fuses and fades
(InfiniteScroll), which takes TWO arms because Vue measures a leave on the transitioned element
alone: the root arm carries the fade and IS the clock, the descendant arm draws the mass home.
A ring inside a trigger hands off to whatever lands in its place — and EasingPicker's Check glyph
**is** that token, which is the `re-fuse → hand off` clause read literally rather than
approximated by a fade into nothing. So that consumer takes no `<Transition>`.
**This shape was forced, and the forcing is worth recording** — a `<Transition>` schedules a
rAF-shim timer under fake timers, and `easing.contract.test.ts:152/:225` asserts that component
leaves **zero** timers on unmount. Amending another row's contract to fit a decoration would have
been the wrong repair; the decoration was wrong, and the spec's own second ending was right.

**Always `aria-hidden`.** Every real host already reports the state in text (InfiniteScroll owns
a polite live region; EasingPicker owns a `role="status"` line AND the trigger's own
`aria-label`), so a default-announcing indicator makes all of them say it twice.

**Not exported.** Two library-internal consumers, both of which already expose a `loading` slot
or an explicit trigger for a caller who wants their own mark. Growing the public surface for an
indicator nobody asked for is the overfit class #19 spent a row cutting. **Public entries stay

### 2.1 · TWO DEFECTS FOUND IN LIVE PAINT, BOTH FATAL, BOTH CURED AT THE SEAM

Neither is visible to any headless gate, and both would have shipped an invisible or a collapsed
indicator. They are recorded because the class matters more than the fix.

**D-1 · the beads computed `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)` — an INVISIBLE indicator.** The hue advance was
written `calc(h + <angle>deg)`. In relative-colour syntax `h` resolves to a `<number>` of degrees,
so adding an `<angle>` is a type error that invalidates the WHOLE declaration. Probed in the live
engine to fix the attribution rather than guess it:

| expression | computed |
|---|---|
| `oklch(from red l c calc(h + 12deg))` | `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)` |
| `oklch(from red l c calc(h + 12))` | `oklch(0.627966 0.257704 41.2346)` |
| `oklch(from currentColor l c calc(h + 12))` | `oklch(0.216128 0.00625756 67.9827)` |

Cured to the unitless advance. `currentColor` as a relative-colour origin was exonerated by the
same probe — it resolves fine, and blaming it would have cost the ergonomics for nothing.

**D-2 · all seven beads stacked on one radius.** The individual transform properties compose in
the FIXED order translate → rotate → scale, so a bead carrying both `rotate` and `translate` is
translated in the UNROTATED frame and then spun about its own centre. Measured: seven beads at
`cx = 0.51, cy = 0` — identical seats. The rotation has to sit OUTSIDE the translation, which on
these longhands means a parent. Cured with `.glass-dot-ring-spoke`, a wrapper that earns its node.
`transform` is not an escape: it composes INNERMOST, so the bead's own `scale` would scale the
ring radius with it.

**REFUSED, with grounds: the 4:1 as a CLOCK.** 0.33 s : 0.08 s is an exit at 0.24× its entry, and
80 ms sits **below our own floor**. MOTION-CANON §7 rules `EXIT = 0.6 × ENTRY, floored at 120ms,
ceilinged at 250ms`; ADJ-6 re-affirmed that bracket against a competing one and struck the
uncited figures to hypothesis; #26 then SHIPPED it as arithmetic —
`--spring-*-exit-duration: calc(clamp(0.12s, settle * 0.6, 0.25s) * var(--motion-tempo))`,
`scheme-spring.css:114-119`, the detector. Landing 4:1 would require breaking a floor that the
canon states, an adjudication upholds, and a generator enforces — to import a measurement of a
different system's micro-orb. **The measured 4:1 is recorded as evidence; the clock stays ours.**
