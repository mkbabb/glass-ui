**Decision 1 — bare α scalars, not pre-composed colours.** §1.2 names *one* ink (`rgb(28,25,23)`, which is

exact failure the one-ink register exists to prevent. Enforced by an arm, not left to prose.

**Decision 2 — no seventh token.** The "both" fill rung (`0.05 + 0.12 = 0.16`) and the *indicator* (2px @ 0.24) are

indicator's justification is arithmetic and is asserted as such — `2 × 0.24 == 0.48`, the same ink mass spread over
twice the height because it must be findable along a 1288px run.

1.28:1 — it fails 3:1 for a control perimeter and is 1.43× the plate edge for a grouping line. It sits between two
rungs and belongs to neither; two inks is a second colour system.
