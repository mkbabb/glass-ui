## 3 · I-2/I-3/I-4 · THE VAPORIZE

### 3.1 The mechanism, and the one clock it spends

§4b measured the Control-Centre dismissal on a background strip the panel never occupies: it is
**a desynchronised three-channel release of the backdrop filter itself**, with the element's own
ink dropped first — not mask erosion, not per-particle scatter, not displacement. sat : dim : blur
= **1 : 1.36 : 1.72**, monotone, ease-out, **zero overshoot on any channel**, saturation always
first and blur always last.

The recipe states **no duration** (an arm asserts the absence). It reads `--exit-overlay-duration`
— which #26's generator derives from the `panel` row's own settle — and spends it in the ratio.
Ratios preserved, absolutes ours, which is what stops a recede outlasting the arrival it reverses.

| channel | clock expression | live (ms) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~~saturate~~ | ~~`calc(var(--vap-clock) * 1 / 1.72)`~~ | ~~**145.349**~~ | ~~1.000~~ |
| ~~dim (the panel's own paint)~~ | ~~`calc(var(--vap-clock) * 1.36 / 1.72)`~~ | ~~**197.674**~~ | ~~1.360~~ |
| ~~blur~~ | ~~`var(--vap-clock)`~~ | ~~**250.000**~~ | ~~1.720~~ |

**[STRUCK 2026-08-05 — row-30 CURE seat, CURE-1.]** Three clocks on the presence-gated root is
exactly the defect: reka's `usePresence` unmounts on the FIRST `animationend`, so the shortest
clock was the toast's whole life and the other two channels never landed in the only consumer. The
ratio now rides the keyframe STOPS on one uniform clock — same three source numbers, same painted
gap, one end-time. §10 carries the cured table and the live re-cut.

### 3.2 Why it is a keyframe (I-4)

CWT F-11: the archive's snippet is `transition`-based and **cannot paint through reka's
`usePresence`**, which gates the unmount on `getComputedStyle(node).animationName` and has no
`transitionend` path — a transition-only dismissal is torn down in ~11ms, under one frame. The
desynchronisation forces it too: two channels sharing one `backdrop-filter` declaration can only
differ in schedule through separate **registered** custom properties (`--vap-saturate`,
`--vap-blur`; a bare `var()` in a filter function snaps). Both are registered in
`tokens/property-regs.css`. **[AMENDED 2026-08-05 — CURE-1: "three clocks" → three landings on one
clock; the reason is unchanged.]**

Neither backdrop keyframe writes a `0%` — the implicit `from` is the element's own cascaded rung, so
the release starts from whatever glass the surface actually wears and this cut restates no rung's
values.

### 3.3 The panel's own paint, in one keyframe

`glass-vaporize-panel` releases `background-color: transparent`, `border-color: transparent`,
`box-shadow: none`. The last line is load-bearing: css-backgrounds-3 interpolates a shadow list
against `none` as the same list with transparent colours, so **one keyframe releases any rung's
stack** — three layers on `.glass-floating`, one on `.glass-wash` — without restating a shadow.
Without it the cast outlives the plate by 52ms of naked shadow, which is the exact
masking-fallback-class defect the house forbids.

**DEVIATION D-3.** `--vap-dim` is **not registered**, deliberately. The archive's `--vap-dim`
existed only to carry a `color-mix` percentage; our dim channel drives three natively-interpolating
properties, which needs no Houdini dependency, restates no ladder token, and works on every rung.
TR's "`--vap-*` @property-registered or nothing interpolates" is satisfied where it bites — the two
channels that share one declaration, plus `--vap-ink-blur`.

### 3.5 THE DEFECT THE LIVE PASS CAUGHT — the resurrection

The headless arms were green and the effect was wrong. Scrubbing the paused animation showed the
ink **coming back from the dead over the last fifth of its own dismissal**:

| % of travel | ink alpha, BEFORE the cure | ink alpha, AFTER |
|---|---|---|
| 79.1 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| 84.0 | **0.5563** | 0.0000 |
| 100.0 | **1.0000** | 0.0000 |

Cause: css-animations-1 synthesises an **implicit 100% keyframe from the element's UNDERLYING
value** for any property whose stops end early. `opacity`'s last stop was 79.07%, so it interpolated
back to 1; `filter` decayed from its 23.256% peak the same way (13.15px @ 16% → 6.56px @ 40%).
Cured by pinning both channels at an explicit `100%` stop, and a **new arm** —
*"no channel RESURRECTS — every animated property is pinned at 100%"* — now holds it for every
`glass-vaporize*` keyframe, so the class cannot come back through a future stop edit.

## 4 · I-5/I-6 · THE CORNER AFFORDANCE

§3, adjudicated by O-10 against the "badge inside the icon box with a dark ring" reading: **the
centre sits ON the corner point**, nudged inward by ~6.5% of the disc's own diameter, ~70% floating
free; diameter = 0.42 × a square host's edge. It composites **flat and hard** — no shadow, no
stroke, no ring, no halo, no glass; separation is achieved **entirely by value contrast**. It is
the one element in the language that is not glass, and making it glass would destroy it.

Two grammars, never mixed: corner-straddling + opaque = system status or destructive removal;
inset + chromeless = content-owned dismissal. `ToastClose` takes the first (§3 + WAVES:662's
"the close-affordance the owner marked").

**LAW 13 (I-6)** binds in one declaration: `.glass-corner-host[data-corner=…]` sets
`transform-origin` to that corner, out-ranking `.glass-reveal`'s popper origin by specificity — the
surface blooms from, and dies into, the corner its mark sits on.

**Live geometry, measured on the toast:**

| quantity | measured | §3 |
|---|---|---|
| diameter | 24.000px | knob (0.42 × edge on a square host) |
| centre offset from the corner point | **(1.5625, 1.5625)** px inward | −d/2 + 0.065d = 1.560px |
| nudge as a fraction of diameter | **0.06510** | "~6–7% of its own diameter" |
| overhang past the edge | 0.4349 × d | 0.43–0.45 × d |
| host `overflow` | `visible` | "the parent must not clip" |
| disc `box-shadow` / `backdrop-filter` / `border-width` | `none` / `none` / `0px` | no shadow, no ring, no glass |
| polarity, light → dark | fill `rgb(28,25,23)` → `rgb(233,230,226)`; ink `rgb(251,250,248)` → `rgb(11,10,9)` | separation by value, both modes |

**DEVIATION D-6 (found live, cured live).** The first measurement read the nudge at **10.7%** of the
diameter, outside §3's band. Cause: an absolutely positioned child resolves its insets against the
**padding** box while §3 measures against the **corner point** (the border box), so the host's 1px
rim displaced the disc on both axes. Cured with a `--corner-affordance-host-border` knob (default
`0px` — most hosts have no rim) that the toast declares as `1px`. Re-measured: 0.06510.

**DEVIATION D-7.** The disc's default fill is `--foreground`, not §3's high-chroma
`rgb(254, 47, 51)`. §3's chroma arm belongs to the **status badge** (system-owned count); a dismiss
mark shouting destructive-red on every toast is the shadcn palette residual by another route. The
chroma is a knob (`--corner-affordance-fill`), which is what #87 adopts.
