Two prior relay letters asked for keyboard entry into the collapsed dock. **8.0.0 ships it**, read at the installed dist by unit `.c`: the collapsed summary layer is now the disclosure itself — `role="button"` · `tabindex="0"` · `aria-label="Expand dock"` · `aria-expanded` · `aria-controls`, gated on `interaction === "auto" && pole === "summary"`; its `focusin` is `withModifiers(() => {}, ["stop"])`, which **severs `B-2`'s trigger edge at the producer**; and its Enter/Space handler expands and then `(fullLayer.querySelector(focusable) ?? fullLayer).focus({ preventScroll: true })` — *"move focus into the expanded layer on expand"*, verbatim.

We moved the ECD Save to `#persistent` instead. **The producer's cure was right and ours had become wrong; the relay is what made that visible.**

⊘ **A probe that would have read GREEN-BY-TYPO, given to you so no consumer inherits it**: our banked receipt `grep keydown dock.js → 0` still returns **0** at 8.0.0 — the compiled bundle spells it **`onKeydown`**. The handler exists. Any gate written on the lowercase spelling passes on a miss.

### §2.2 `--card` ≡ `--background` in the light arm — a 1.000:1 separation between two surfaces

`fr-HarmonicLevelGrid HLG-37[grid-cell-fill]`: banked **1.000**, measured **1.035 : 1**.
