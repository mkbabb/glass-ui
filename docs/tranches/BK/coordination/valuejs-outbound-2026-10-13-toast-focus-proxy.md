## The finding and the ask (the ledger row, verbatim)

TOAST-FOCUS-PROXY, re-asked for **10.2.0** (F.REL `.w`, COHESION §0ew ESC-FREL-g-2; it re-opens O-32 §3.1 / erratum `E-F9b-2`). `<Toaster>` from `@mkbabb/glass-ui/toast` (10.1.0, `dist/toast-DVOwo6GB.js`) wraps reka's `ToastViewport`. While a toast is up, it renders head and tail `<span aria-hidden="true" tabindex="0" style="position: fixed; …">` focus proxies, and axe grades them `[serious] aria-hidden-focus`. fourier mounts the Toaster bare (`web/src/App.vue`, `<Toaster position="bottom-left" />`) and calls `toast()` as documented, so this is **not a consumer misconfiguration**. It reddens all four admin axe specs (`gallery-admin-a11y.spec.ts:91 · :103 · :114 · :125`). BK's 2026-09-22 reply (§3.1) declined to patch reka's DOM and offered two test-side remedies: grade only after the toast closes, or exclude the proxy selector. Neither is lawful at the consumer under §0ew (no allowlist, no masking). **Ask:** glass's Toaster renders no focusable node inside an `aria-hidden` subtree. For example, it could give the proxies `tabindex="-1"` and move focus into the viewport by its own hotkey/F6 path, or render the proxies only while focus is inside the viewport. The witness is axe `aria-hidden-focus` = 0 with one toast up. **Second, smaller ask (same component):** a viewport inset/avoid facility. 10.1.0 takes only `position`. At 1024×768, every corner meets a consumer control (bottom-right the Configurator aside; bottom-left the stage's playback dock; the top corners the app dock), so fourier now seats bottom-left as the least harmful. Held in fourier's manifest `web/e2e/producer-held.json` (4 rows, O-90, expiry "at glass 10.2.0 repin").
