## 11. Modularization execution law

The terminal topology must be produced iteratively from graph evidence, not
from a target-tree diagram:

1. sever SCCs and upward dependencies;
2. assign one coherent responsibility per module;
3. split god-modules only at independent change/ownership seams;
4. merge file sand and stock part mirrors into their owner;
5. colocate feature-private helpers, styles, stories, and fixtures;
6. strip the containing module name from child filenames;
7. publish only demonstrated family entries;
8. after source settles, move tests to
   `tests/<source-relative-path>/<behavior>.spec.ts`;
9. migrate consumers and delete old files/exports in the same clean break;
10. regenerate the graph and repeat the three-Sol challenge until no cycle,
    laundering directory, god façade, or unexplained subpath remains.

No aliases, migration shims, dual paths, source-resolution fallbacks, route
redirects, or compatibility forwarders are permitted.
