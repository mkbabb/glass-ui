## BE.W-BACKDROP-SETTLE — the opt-in backdrop-blur settle leg on `.glass-reveal`

**Band/Severity/Deps** — Band 5 (BLOOM/FLIP) · **major** · Deps: extends `.glass-reveal` (the BB.W-LIQUID-REVEAL recipe, glass/reveal.css) — no cross-band blocker; the per-channel color-bloom it owns reads BE.W-AMBIENT-TINT's `--glass-ambient-hue` if wired (OPT-IN). Sequences any time after the reveal recipe (which is at HEAD).

**Goal** — Add an OPT-IN backdrop-blur SETTLE leg to `.glass-reveal`: as a top-layer surface blooms in, its BACKDROP (the frosted plate behind it) settles its blur radius from a heavier open-state blur down to its resting radius — the iOS-27 "the glass thickens then settles" decongest on the BACKDROP (distinct from the reveal's existing SURFACE-pixel decongest). iOS-27 betters-claim: an opening sheet/dialog's frosted backdrop momentarily over-blurs (the world recedes harder behind the rising glass) then settles to the resting frost — the depth cue that the surface is RISING through the glass. glass-ui's `.glass-reveal` decongest is on the surface's OWN pixels (`filter`, NOT `backdrop-filter`) — deliberately, so it never clobbers the resting plate blur. This wave adds the COMPLEMENTARY opt-in: a `backdrop-filter` blur SETTLE that, when opted in, over-blurs the backdrop on open then settles — without breaking the resting-plate-blur fence (the settle LANDS on the resting radius, it does not zero it).

**Build** (the mechanism, compositor-safe/Safari-safe/PRM) —
1. **The opt-in token + the registered custom.** Mint `--glass-reveal-backdrop-settle` (default `0` — the no-op floor; the surface's backdrop does NOT over-blur unless opted in) + a registered `@property --glass-reveal-backdrop-t` (`<number>`, `inherits: false`, `initial-value: 0`) in `property-regs.css` (so the backdrop radius INTERPOLATES, not snaps). The settle leg is OFF by default — `.glass-reveal` is byte-identical at HEAD unless a consumer/surface sets `--glass-reveal-backdrop-settle` > 0.
2. **The backdrop-settle leg** (glass/reveal.css, gated): when opted in, `.glass-reveal[data-state="open"]` interpolates the BACKDROP blur from `calc(resting-radius + var(--glass-reveal-backdrop-settle))` (the open over-blur) DOWN to the RESTING radius (NOT to 0 — the fence: the settle LANDS on the resting plate blur, never zeroes it). The leg is added to the transition list ONLY under the opt-in (a `@supports`/scoped rule keyed off `--glass-reveal-backdrop-settle`), on the SAME snappy spring clock as the surface decongest so the two read as ONE coalescing depth cue. The backdrop radius reads the resting `glass-floating` radius primitive + the settle delta — so it composes with `--glass-level` + the saturate companion (never a parallel recipe).
3. **The per-channel color-bloom** (the owned delight): the settle leg optionally couples a backdrop HUE shift — as the backdrop over-blurs, it biases toward `--glass-ambient-hue` (the AMBIENT-TINT source) then settles (the backdrop briefly absorbs the rising surface's ambient hue). Default no-op (`--glass-ambient-strength: 0`); OPT-IN reads the ambient seam.
- **Compositor-safe**: `backdrop-filter: blur()` is a compositor paint property (not a layout property — `proof:no-layout-animation` holds; the reflow set does not include `backdrop-filter`). The radius interp rides the registered `@property` custom. **Safari-safe**: `backdrop-filter: blur()` (the REGULAR backdrop blur, NOT `url()`) IS WebKit-supported (the `-webkit-backdrop-filter` prefix the dist already ships) — cross-engine, NO Chromium-only primitive.
