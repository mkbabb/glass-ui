**Goal** — FOLD the dock text-tab register and the SegmentedTabs `pill` register onto ONE iOS-27 recessed-accent glass-capsule register. iOS-27 betters-claim: the iOS segmented control is a RECESSED track (an inset well, the selected segment a LIFTED translucent glass capsule that GLIDES + the de-selected labels read through the well) with an accent-suffuse on selection (the selected capsule briefly bleeds its accent into the track) and an icon-bloom (the selected glyph scales up ~+6% on select). glass-ui has TWO disjoint near-implementations today — the SegmentedTabs `pill` (a LIFTED floating plate, BC.W-TABS-IOS stadium register) and the dock `.dock-tab-button` (a flat de-red'd glass hover, NO recessed track, NO lifted-capsule selected register). This wave mints ONE register both arms read so a dock tab strip and a `<SegmentedTabs variant=pill>` are the SAME material, and ADDS the three iOS delights (icon-bloom-on-select, accent-suffuse-sweep, capsule-goo-pull on travel) the pill arm half-carries and the dock arm lacks entirely.

**Build** (the mechanism on the named substrate, compositor-only/PRM-safe) —
1. **Mint the ONE capsule register** in a new `src/styles/tabs/capsule.css` (`@layer components`, `@import`-ed after `segmented-tabs.css` and the dock control partials): the `--tab-capsule-*` token family — `--tab-capsule-track-bg` (the RECESSED well: `--glass-bg-quiet` + an INSET groove shadow `inset 0 1px 2px color-mix(in srgb, var(--shadow-color) 8%, transparent)`, the recessed inset the pill track lacks), `--tab-capsule-fill` (the LIFTED selected capsule, reading the EXACT `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` the pill indicator already paints — so the two arms are byte-identical material), `--tab-capsule-accent` (the accent-suffuse hue, default `transparent` no-op; an OPT-IN consumer or the BE.W-AMBIENT-TINT seam biases it), `--tab-capsule-accent-strength` (default `0%`, the no-op floor).
