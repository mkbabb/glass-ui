U2—the medium: opacity cliff on a CONSTANT blur radius, persistence across interrupts

The medium is pre-mounted and never unmounts; the radius never animates. Open: ≤100ms opacity cliff, tiles fade in at stretch/4 while sliding ~650ms decelerating, deeper rows travel +20%/row, periphery lags 100ms. Close inverts: content leaves first (~170ms), a ≥150ms empty-medium beat (HELD is a named state), then the ~420ms relax. Flick+catch re-enters mid-relax—the medium's floor never reaches 0. Scrub drives it by hand at any state.
