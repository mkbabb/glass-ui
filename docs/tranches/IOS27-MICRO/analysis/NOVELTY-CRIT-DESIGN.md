### M1 — V-ALENS contradicts its measured exemplar in three registers: it dims where iOS locks luminance, quadruples the blur dose, and inverts the annulus

- **Evidence.** The card specifies "radial progressive-blur + dim ramp"
  (`NOVELTY-ROSTER.md:172-174`) and the prototype builds it: a dim layer ramping to
  `rgba(8,10,14,0.85)` by 340px (`v-alens/index.html:96`) under a 4/8/16px stacked-blur field
  (`index.html:66-85`). The measured mark says the opposite on every axis
  (`MARKS-C-APPS.md:117-131`): "NO SCRIM: luminance ratio engaged/sharp = 0.99–1.01 in every
  non-pill band—the focus is pure blur, zero dimming" (:120-122); peak σ≈10 source px ≈3.3pt
  (~3-4 CSS px, not an effective ~18px stack); and the field is a LOCAL ANNULUS "decaying to
  zero by ~450px above and ~600px below"—most of the screen stays crisp—where ours saturates
  the far field at maximum blur+0.85 dim. The adoption is already written: "the gradient-blur
  annulus ships as the `--glass-halo-*` scalar pair (radius≈10px→3.3pt peak, extent≈1.5
  control-heights, luminance LOCKED)—engagement suffusion, not modal scrim"
  (`MARKS-C-APPS.md:188-190`); design note 2 closes it: "ZERO dimming...Never a full-screen
  scrim" (:174-176).
- **The design failure.** Mark 6's essence is "GRADIENT BLURRING...SUBTLY focusing
  attention." An 0.85 dim under an ~18px far-field blur is a modal spotlight—the trite
  inversion of the mark, and it converts an engage-rung suffusion into a takeover. The card's
  own words ("attention focused by the medium, not by a spotlight border") indict the build.
- **What survives.** The mechanism is exactly right and paint-queued correctly: 3 static
  masked layers, opacity-only ramps, one seat per engagement, no clipping wrapper, C¹
  reversal, the N3 analytic gate. Keep all of it.
- **Cure.** Re-derive the spatial profile to the measured law: peak σ ~3-4 CSS px inside
  ~±1 control-height, Gaussian-class falloff to ZERO within ~1.5-2 control-heights (re-band
  the three masks—the annulus is authored in the masks, not in new machinery); delete the dim
  layer or cap it at a sub-perceptual whisper (≤0.05); keep engagement-scrub as the declared
  divergence (clock-locked ~170ms is iOS's; the scrub is ours and better). Rename the card's
  tokens to the `--glass-halo-*` pair (see m3).
