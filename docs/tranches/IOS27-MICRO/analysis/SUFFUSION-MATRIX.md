## 1. The archetype × quality matrix

### 1.1 The abstract state ladder (the "button states, at a more abstract level" answer)

```
idle → hover → engage → active → drag/scrub → release(+settle) → idle'
                 │
                 ├─ disabled  (parallel rail, enterable from any rung)
                 └─ loading   (parallel rail, enterable from active)
```

- **idle**—material only: two-tier glass, 1px top rim light, readable transparency. No specular
  motion, no rAF (MARKS §4: no light event on a static surface). One license: the slow specular
  sweep on a primary selection lens, at most one per view.
- **hover**—intent, not commitment: pointer-tracked catch-light, sub-perceptual lift. Touch has no
  hover; the rung merges into engage there (arm question carried to pass 2, §3.4).
- **engage**—pointerdown, the press-charge: the component acknowledges touch as a body before
  anything travels (the Find My whole-bar wash). The most under-suffused rung in the library.
- **active**—the committed state (pressed, checked, selected, open), read through light and lens
  magnification (~5–8%), never color alone.
- **drag/scrub**—the finger owns position; physics owns deformation. Everything position-mapped,
  nothing time-mapped; velocity feeds smear (`--atom-drag-v`) and the transient weight boost.
- **release(+settle)**—the spring seeds with live release velocity. Fast release earns overshoot;
  slow place lands dead; synthetic bounce never. Light cools after geometry stops (~200ms).
- **disabled**—de-materialization: one glass tier down, rim off, zero deformation on press. The
  physics refusing to answer IS the relay.
- **loading**—ONE vocabulary library-wide: the slow luminance drift (skeleton/pulse) on the
  component's own surface. The field rim-glint variant is cut (critique A5); the drift wins.

Every rung transition is scrub-interruptible with state carried over. Fire-and-forget is licensed
only for keyboard-triggered overlay enter/exit and the app-zoom class.

### 1.4 The rows—applied behavior at the ladder, and the withholdings

#### A. Momentary controls—button, chip(action), dark-mode-toggle

APPLIED:
- idle: material only. hover: catch-light slides under the pointer (`--mouse-x/y`,
  `--specular-angle`); lift ≤1px-equivalent.
- engage: the press-charge—reciprocal volume-preserving squish (`useLiquidPress`, `--press-t`)
  with the brightness/specular leg on the SAME spring clock; bloom may exceed bounds by 2–4px,
  engagement-only. Grow-on-engage (Q8's stationary twin) stackable: scale ≤φ^¼≈1.128 on `press`.
- release: spring-back with live velocity carried—a rapid double-tap compounds (velocity-continuous
  re-seat), never restarts. settle: light cools ~80ms after scale.
- disabled: no squish on press—the body does not answer; glass flattens one tier. loading: the
  uniform luminance drift; width-morph to spinner banned.
- dark-mode-toggle additionally triggers N11, the theme-flip medium choreography (§2)—the control
  itself stays on this row's script.

WITHHELD: Q1—no travel domain, nothing to overpull. Q9/Q10—a tap has no detents and never scrubs.
Press velocity into the flip spring—CUT (critique A2): a click's pointer velocity is sensor noise;
click ≠ fling. Charge glow on plain press—parked with N10; charging a navigation button teaches
users to fear taps.

#### B. Bistable controls—switch, checkbox, radio-group, toggle-group, chip(filter)

APPLIED:
- The switch thumb is control-tier glass in a container-tier trough. drag: thumb stretches along
  the travel axis with `--atom-drag-v`; the trough tint crossfades under the thumb—state readable
  mid-gesture. Past a well: thumb compresses against the end (micro-overpull ≤2px), never
  translates past; release overshoots once into the well, ζ≈0.6.
- engage(tap): press-charge on the thumb; the flip runs `springPreset("bouncy")` seeded with zero
  velocity—a tap is a slow place.
- checkbox/radio: the mark DRAWS (handmark stroke, ~150ms), never fades in; the box squishes on
  engage; draw velocity inherits the press spring's.
- toggle-group/chip(filter): selection travels as ONE lens body (the G contract at micro scale via
  `useSelectionIndicator`). Q9 here is pure capture—discrete controls never catch-and-pass (a pass
  implies a state that does not exist).
- Grow-on-engage: switch thumb +8% under the finger (fingertip occlusion). disabled: thumb fuses
  into the trough—the two-tier split collapsing is the read.

WITHHELD: Q10—no scrubable surface. Modal variants—tap gestures; growth adds nothing.
Chip drag-to-delete taffy—PARKED (critique B3): the gesture does not exist on disk.

#### C. Continuous controls—slider, number-field (the flagship row)

The named exemplar lives here; the maximum dose in the library IS the proportion speaking.

APPLIED:
- idle: control-tier thumb on container-tier track; fill carries the value tint.
- engage: press-charge + grow-on-engage—the thumb pops out of its shell on `press`, scale
  ≤φ^¼≈1.128 on ALL pointers (critique C7: C's cap beats A's 1.25 touch pop; touch precision is
  the modal variant's job, that is why it exists).
- drag: fill smears behind the thumb (saturating tanh, clamp ~0.7—mass wins); thumb stretches
  along travel; readout counts through intermediates (`animated-digit`).
- mark crossing: the ~170ms weak-well catch—felt as thickening, seen as the mark swelling; marks
  never snap. No mid-range wells on precision-continuous (volume-class) sliders—ends and center
  at most (B-Q9 law).
- overpull past min/max: thumb + fill compress against the end, resistively damped; release
  springs back with one overshoot.
- release: spring seeded with drag velocity—the FILL may overshoot visually, the VALUE clamps;
  the relayed state never lies. settle: smear→0, weight→0.618, light cools last.
- Q10 ◐: the modal variant only (the amended B ledger cell—critique B1). The modal slider is a
  full scrub body; the inline slider never scrubs its own frame.

WITHHELD: number-field modal dial/strip and drag-stop detents—PARKED (critique A10/B4): no
drag-scrub gesture on disk; prove the slider modal first. Value physics on programmatic set:
zero-seed, deterministic.

#### D. Text fields—input, textarea, tags-input, search, labeled-field

The proportion here is restraint: fields are held engagement—calm, legible, never bouncy.

APPLIED:
- hover: rim glint only, no lift (sockets, not bodies).
- engage(focus): the ring blooms from the caret entry point outward ~200ms—the one choreographed
  moment; surface brightens one transparency step; an opacity floor engages under focused text
  (no moving tint under a caret—B-Q5 law).
- label float (labeled-field—the archetype's signature, critique MISSING 3): the label rises from
  placeholder to perch on two clocks—travel and rim brightening desynced at whisper amplitude.
- active(typing): tags-input materializes committed tags via `transient`; native caret blink is
  the only caret life (breathing caret CUT—critique A4).
- invalid: one mass-proportional lateral shake (≤2 cycles) + tone floods the rim—never color
  alone. valid (critique MISSING 5): the FeedbackMark draws—handmark stroke, the positive half of
  the relay, no new vocabulary.
- loading: the uniform drift (bespoke rim-glint CUT—critique A5). disabled: socket flattens.

WITHHELD: Q1—overpull fights text selection. Q7—no gesture integrates velocity here; caret, focus
ring, and text echo are permanently exempt from spring clocks (B-Q2 law). Q8/Q9—no threshold
gestures. Modal for input/textarea—NEVER: enlarging a text surface mid-focus tears keyboard
interaction. search Q10 ◐: the mobile command-sheet growth only (§4).

#### E. List-selection fields—select, combobox, command

APPLIED:
- trigger engage: archetype A's press script; chevron rotation and panel growth share one
  spring—one body.
- open: anchored growth from the trigger; rows enter on the depth-graded ladder—deeper rows travel
  ~20% farther (`--depth-i`, one scalar), arrive later (1/φ overlap). Open energy is PRESET—the
  velocity-seeded unfurl is CUT (critique C1): click velocity is noise, identical intents must
  produce identical motion.
- highlight travel: the highlight is a lens—glides on `snappy`, magnifies the row's leading icon
  ~5%. Under key repeat it travels FAST but always lands exactly on the focused row—the coasting
  keyboard momentum is CUT (critique A1): Enter mid-settle must never commit a row the user did
  not choose. Native focus is truth; the lens is paint.
- selection: the chosen label flies to the trigger (`useElementMorph`—one continuous body); the
  panel collapses behind it on the inverted close order.
- close: content leaves first, medium relaxes after.

WITHHELD: Q1—the list interior is a natively scrolling readable surface; Safari's rubber band owns
it (B-Q1 law; the same law that cut data-table overscroll). Q8/Q9—no threshold or detent gestures.
Q7 ◐ / Q10 ◐: the mobile bottom-sheet variant only, which inherits drawer physics wholesale (§4).

#### F. Overlays—dialog, drawer, popover, dropdown-menu, tooltip

APPLIED:
- open (dialog): three channels, three clocks—medium (scrim blur+dim) leads as a fast-but-softened
  ramp (~120ms; the ≤83ms iOS cliff is a defect we best—N8); paint ~4× faster than geometry
  (stated law: fade = stretch/φ³, the measurement ~1:4 cited as confirmation—critique ruling 12);
  geometry ~500–600ms decelerating from compressed-toward-origin; periphery +100ms. Beat ORDER is
  content-keyed—N2's three named permutations (destructive/form/media), nothing free-form.
- close: inverted—content leaves ~170ms, medium relaxes ~400ms after; the 100–150ms contentless-
  medium beat is kept deliberately; the medium never resets across interrupted cycles.
- drawer: the full scrub body—position height-mapped through the fraction-keyed reveal ladder
  (handle 0–5%, title ghost 10–30%, title solid 30–50%, row N at 40%+10%·N); release seeds the
  spring; peek/half/full detents magnetic both directions with the ~170ms catch crossed fast;
  overpull asymmetric (past top ~1% compression, past bottom deep—forbiddenness scales the
  magnitude); dismissal catchable and reversible at any point. Edge-pull carries the taffy
  pre-commit zone (~8–12px web scale).
- popover/dropdown-menu: E's anchored open at half scale, no scrim.
- tooltip: ONE clock, sub-150ms, fade + 4px rise, no glass bloom, no spring theatrics—the
  zero-dose proof that makes every other dose legible.

WITHHELD: overpull on popover/tooltip/dropdown-menu—no gesture travel domain. Choreography inside
micro-elements—single clock under 24px. A fifth channel—noise, not breath (B-Q6 law).

#### G. Navigational lenses—tabs, dock, pager-dots, carousel, deck, header-ribbon, scroll-progress-rim

APPLIED (the lens contract—one law for every selection lens in the library):
- idle: active item under a lens—brighter capsule, content magnified ~5–8%, idle specular sweep
  ~8s (the one licensed idle light—Safari's good idea; at most one per view).
- engage: press-charge—lens brightens in place, a glow wash crosses the whole bar before any
  travel (the body acknowledges).
- travel: ONE continuous body—stretches to span source and target (~2.5 slots), light leads,
  geometry follows; the source de-materializes under it, re-materializes behind. Sibling labels
  stay ≥AA legible beneath the traveling bloom—the gate, not a taste (N3; besting iOS's ~300ms
  blackout). Jumps ≥2 slots tick each intermediate detent (~170ms micro-catch)—the eye counts the
  travel; a neighbor move is one motion.
- arrival: lands oversized 110–120%, holds ~200ms, cools—overshoot in scale AND light.
- clock proportion: travel ≤500ms (`snappy`) for control-register segments; the luxurious
  1.2–1.4s register is reserved for nav-role surfaces (dock, primary tabs).
- tabs overflow (critique MISSING 2—Safari's second good idea, adopted): the world scrolls so the
  active item centers under a quasi-fixed lens.
- dock: the full hallmark body—grow-to-card, asymmetric overpull with volume compression, the
  ~40px pre-commit taffy zone—all as derivations of the shared primitives, never private code.
- carousel/deck: swipe velocity inherited; ends get the volumetric edge compression (gesture-owned
  chrome only—the P10 narrow survival); pages are strong wells (snap).
- header-ribbon (critique MISSING 6): condenses through the same fraction-keyed ladder, scroll-
  driven—which elements dematerialize at which scroll fractions is specced from the drawer's
  primitive, never a binary snap. scroll-progress-rim: the head glint tracks scroll velocity,
  cools at rest.
- keyboard: arrows re-seat the lens deterministically at rest weight—no goo on keys.

WITHHELD: tabs swipe-past-ends overpull—PARKED (critique B2): no swipe navigation on disk.
Tabs press-and-hold magnified picker—PARKED (critique A7): content tabs, not an OS thumb bar;
collides with text selection; no consumer. Extent-encoding edge stiffness—CUT (critique C2): no
signal to relay, designed-unreadable, session-inconsistent.

#### H. Disclosure containers—accordion, collapsible, expandable-container, card, surface, separator

APPLIED:
- expand: height travel + the fraction-keyed ladder—children fade AND rise ~30–60px as the
  container passes their threshold; coherent at every scrubbed intermediate; the surface never
  reads as one prerendered bitmap.
- accordion handoff: the opening section's growth and the closing section's collapse OVERLAP;
  the separator stretches taffy-like during the handoff only (BOUND—critique A12: an inert line
  has no standalone life).
- header engage: press-charge; chevron spring inherits press velocity.
- card: idle is pure material—two-tier, tinted from beneath (text clamped to full contrast), 1px
  top rim, inner top glow keyed to raised state. Interactive cards run A's press script at ~40%
  amplitude—weight readable through restraint.
- edge-drag expandables: release velocity seeds the settle; heights are weak wells; pull-to-open
  carries the pre-commit stretch.

WITHHELD: Q3—no light motion on static containers, ever (the MARKS law kept structural); the
card→dialog morph's landing glint belongs to the overlay it becomes. surface/separator: material
only.

#### I. Feedback—relaying state the user did not cause; expressiveness is honesty made visible

APPLIED:
- progress: the fill-tip glint tracks fill VELOCITY—the user reads the derivative, the honest
  answer to "is it stuck?" (critique ruling 4 allows the glint as information). Fill motion is
  ζ≥1 ALWAYS—overshoot on progress shows progress that has not happened; the relayed state never
  lies (BOUND—critique A9). No entrance choreography.
- toast: default entrance is A's `transient` center-seed bloom; C's caught-landing + stack ripple
  is the CHALLENGER, decided by the peripheral-distraction test in the prototype pass (critique
  ruling 3); ripple, if it wins, caps at depth 2 (4px·φ⁻ⁿ<1.5px beyond). Flick-dismiss inherits
  gesture velocity; dismiss is scrubbed; exit never overshoots.
- alert: tone floods the rim ~80ms before the body fades in—light leads.
- badge/status-dot: ONE uniform pulse (BOUND—critique A8: mass-proportional differences at
  sub-24px sit below the perceptual floor); single-clock state flips.
- completion-seal: the one theatrical license—handmark draw + a single specular sweep, earned by
  rarity; NEVER scrubbed (replaying a triumph by finger cheapens it).
- skeleton/pulse: the loading vocabulary itself; skeleton never choreographs—honest loading does
  not perform.

WITHHELD: Q1/Q8/Q9—no gesture, no bounds, no wells. Q2 beyond depth-graded stack travel—no weight
theater on honesty surfaces.

#### J. Data display—mostly-idle bodies; suffusion concentrates where data moves or the user reaches in

APPLIED:
- metric/animated values: changes run `animated-digit` smoothing—and inherit energy ONLY along an
  explicit one-hop driver→display link when the USER drives the change (N7, P11's ruling adopted
  verbatim): a slider scrub feeding a metric shares the scrub's velocity channel; amplitude ≤ the
  driver's; at rest indistinguishable from a calm number. Self-updating surfaces stay weight-0—
  data that springs on its own reads cheap.
- data-table sort: rows re-order through real FLIP travel with depth-graded stagger (user-driven,
  Q6 ◐).
- timeline: entries reveal on the scroll-mapped ladder—scrubbed by scroll position, coherent at
  every offset, never time-fired.
- row hover: container-tier brightening one step; press-charge only where rows act.
- instrument-chassis: carries the two-tier contract and the loading breath.

WITHHELD: Q3—no engagement specular on data-display (B law); the sort-header lens-landing and the
velocity-linked bezel glint are both out (bezel glint CUT—critique A3: observers stay calm; a
self-glinting dashboard is self-important noise). Q1—native rubber-band owns readable scroll
(critique C3). Q5—data reads on stable ground; no dynamic tint. avatar/metric modals—PARKED (§4).

#### K. Procedural substrates—the room, not the furniture

APPLIED:
- Substrates relay the PAGE's state, not their own: pointer velocity perturbs the field
  (`usePointerVelocityField`—position→velocity→acceleration + the flick burst; the acceleration
  authority lives here); an opening overlay recruits the substrate into the medium change via the
  co-owned `--medium-t` handshake (critique MISSING 7: written by scrims, read by substrates—
  co-owned by F5 and the overlay archetype so it is not built for one consumer and overfit).
- PRM/battery: still frames, composition preserved. handmark dual-lists: substrate as texture,
  stroke vocabulary serving B's marks and I's seal.

WITHHELD: everything else—a substrate must never read as clickable; its own light economy admits
no second light system; it is the thing seen THROUGH glass, never itself glass.

#### L. Motion primitives—vocabulary, plus one true gesture-holder

APPLIED:
- sortable-list (archetype C's physics on a vertical domain, sharing every primitive): pre-lift
  swell (taffy), lift-on-grab pops the row out of its shell onto a control-tier surface, siblings
  part with depth-graded travel, slot boundaries are wells, drop seeds the landing spring with
  drag velocity—overshoot proportional, drag past list ends overpulls (gesture-owned).
- infinite-scroll/fading-scroll: velocity-linked edge fades (fast scroll deepens the fade)—the
  volumetric compression stays OFF these native-scroll shells (B-Q1 law; only gesture-owned chrome
  compresses).
- typewriter (critique MISSING 4, trace dose): cadence carries attack and decay—ease-in over the
  first characters, settle at the end, a carriage-beat at wrap; a metronome types, a hand writes.
- animated-digit: the value-churn voice of the facility. easing tools: the authoring bench;
  scrub keypoints are wells.

WITHHELD: Q5—edge fades are masks, not material. Substrate-grade light—not theirs.

## 2. The novel-affordance register (every surviving best-iOS27 proposal)

The honesty rule first (critique C10): the liquid lens itself, the reveal ladder, the overpull
law, the three-clock desync—these are ADOPTED from the measured corpus, not invented. The register
below lists only what iOS does not do. Adopted-not-novel, for the record: the lens anatomy, tabs
overflow self-centering, the idle sweep, volumetric edge compression on gesture-owned chrome, the
label float, the success mark-draw, the header-ribbon ladder, typewriter cadence.

- **N1—the shell-pop slider (C-P1, the named exemplar).** Engage pops the thumb out of its shell:
  scale ≤φ^¼≈1.128 on ALL pointers (the C7 bound), shadow deepens a rung, ticks materialize under
  it. The stacked modal variant re-homes the control into a card-scale scrub surface at √φ≈1.272—
  a real ×1.27 precision gain made legible by materializing ticks and a counting readout, the
  finger never detaching. iOS enlarges nothing on slider engagement—this is ours. Bound: one
  popped control at a time; modal only on coarse pointers with container headroom; keyboard gets
  full function, zero pop. Mechanism decided at prototype (§4, the one open mechanism question).
- **N2—read-beat dialogs (C-P4).** The three-clock law kept, beat ORDER keyed to content role:
  destructive leads with the title and holds the action row one beat; form leads with the field,
  focus arriving with it; media leads with the pane. Ratio derived, not copied: fade = stretch/φ³
  (≈4.236; the measured ~1:4 cited as confirmation). Bound: exactly three named permutations;
  total entry inside the panel clock (the read-beat is bought by starting earlier, never ending
  later); DOM focus order and aria-live never re-order with the visuals.
- **N3—the lens additions (C-P5, claim corrected).** The lens is adoption; the novelty is (a) the
  sibling-legibility gate—bloom is a screen-blend layer BELOW the label z-plane, luminance-capped;
  siblings ≥AA throughout the morph—and (b) the momentum tick—jumps ≥2 slots catch each
  intermediate well so travel distance is countable. Tick gated on the convergence test: a 4-slot
  jump must not feel slower than its 500ms budget.
- **N4—strain shimmer (C-P7).** Held past a bound, rim luminance rises with strain depth + a
  sub-1% breath while pinned—"you are past the bound, this far, and I am still listening." Exists
  ONLY while the finger holds; dies with release; luminance under the text-contrast floor. Threads
  the no-idle-specular law exactly.
- **N5—the traveling focus lens (C-P8).** Within bounded containers, keyboard focus stops
  teleporting: a painted follower lens travels between controls, stretching along its axis; Tab
  cadence is its velocity—the acceleration channel finally serves the keyboard. Native focus moves
  instantly and is always truth; the lens catches up ≤200ms; `:focus-visible` still renders under
  forced-colors. No OS does this.
- **N6—anticipation (C-P9).** Before a consequential user-confirmed destructive commit, the
  surface dips −4% against travel (`--ease-cartoon-punch`—the curve class damped springs cannot
  produce; the register is ours alone), then commits. Bounds honored: the dip replaces the first
  ~80ms of commit travel inside the existing budget—dispatch is never gated on it (critique C5);
  once per decision; routine chrome motion (the dock's command collapse) is out (critique C4).
- **N7—one-hop energy inheritance (C-P11, adopted verbatim).** The mostly-reject with the earned
  exception: only an explicit, declared driver→display link inherits the scrub's velocity channel;
  amplitude ≤ driver's; observers stay weight-0 forever.
- **N8—the scrubbed medium onset (defect (b), renamed—critique C9).** The mechanism is an
  opacity-ramped backdrop-filter layer, named as such (no "pre-blurred snapshot" fiction), mapped
  to the first 1/φ of gesture travel—reversible mid-flight, and the close's empty-blur beat kept.
  Standing obligation: profile whether Safari keeps paying for a backdrop-filter at opacity 0.
- **N9—caught-landing toast (C-P6).** Arrival with real weight: a one-beat volume compression at
  contact; the stack beneath absorbs with amplitude ×1/φⁿ, one beat late per rung, capped at
  depth 2. Peripheral vision is motion-sensitive—so A's calm `transient` bloom stays the default
  and this challenger must win the peripheral-distraction test in paint (critique ruling 3).
  Impulses never stack under storm; newest wins.
- **N10—charge glow (C-P2).** A continuous, honest hold meter: glow blooms from the contact point
  on a `gentle` spring, saturating toward commit; drain-on-release relays cancel. PARKED until a
  real hold consumer ships (critique C6)—the named landing partner is a hold-to-confirm button
  variant, which is a user-charterable ask (§6 question 1). The glow is never built before the
  gesture.
- **N11—the theme flip as a medium change (critique MISSING 1—the largest under-suffusal).** The
  dark-mode flip is the page-level medium change and deserves the three-clock treatment: medium
  leads (the world re-tints fast), content paint follows, nothing stretches. `useViewTransition`
  sits on disk unconsumed—this is its consumer. Registry note, argued in place: round zero
  rejected view-transition snapshot morphing for liquid surfaces (snapshots cannot scrub) and
  licensed it only for the app-zoom class; the theme flip is that same class—whole-world,
  non-gesture, fire-and-forget-legal—so the license extends to it explicitly here rather than
  silently. This is the one choreography every consumer of the library triggers daily.

**The four iOS defects, beaten by construction:** (a) sibling illegibility under bloom→N3's gate;
(b) the ≤83ms blur cliff→N8; (c) the Safari lens blink→the one-body law is structural (the
indicator element persists and stretches, it cannot regress under load); (d) late content popping
dark→async content enters through the same ladder as sync, keyed to readiness—lateness changes
WHEN, never HOW.

## 3. The facility, sharpened for pass 2

Hallmark 6—"the MOMENTUM and VELOCITY and ACCELERATION tracking of these curves—ALL of our
components should have such a facility." The matrix above is implementable iff ONE facility
provides the following. This section is F4 ENERGY-FIELD's existential test, and the ride map
(§3.3) is where every other family must show the matrix riding on it.

### 3.1 The provisions (testable, each one a pass-2 acceptance target)

1. **The state pair.** Every animated quantity is (value, velocity), never value alone—C¹ at
   every handoff: gesture→spring (release), spring→spring (interrupt), scrub→spring (catch),
   value→value (chained updates). The kf `SpringProgress` re-seat is the seed; the facility makes
   it mandatory at every seam.
2. **Three sources, one shape.** Pointer gestures (`useDragVelocity`), scroll (`scrollReader`),
   and value churn (`useSpring` velocity) publish the SAME var shape—a consumer never knows which
   source drives. Today only the pointer source is surfaced; scroll and value-spring surfacing is
   pass-2 work.
3. **The scalar roster—six, no more.** `--flex-vel`, `--motion-weight` (exist);
   `--engage-t` (new: the smoothed hover/focus/press envelope—arm question in §3.4);
   `--overpull` (new: signed, bound-anchored); `--impulse` (new: the ONE event channel);
   `--scrub-t` (new: F1's spine, the ONE inheriting scalar). No per-component coefficient
   families; the scheme-motion fence holds.
4. **Acceleration, ruled once (critique ruling 9).** `--impulse` is a decaying RELEASE-burst
   event channel, not a continuous variable. No `--atom-drag-a` exists or ever will; acceleration
   is READ as the weight boost's attack rate and EXPRESSED through the catch/arrest vocabulary
   (the 2-frame hard-arrest). Both prior specs now say exactly this.
5. **The per-role gain table.** A button, a card, and a dock must not smear identically—the
   uniform-gimmick failure is F4's named death. The dose ledger (§1.3) IS the gain table's
   skeleton; the zeros are entries, not omissions.
6. **The write discipline.** Event-scoped rAF only—zero at rest, drag-window-gated, self-parking
   springs; `inherits: false` single-element invalidation everywhere EXCEPT `--scrub-t`, whose
   subtree price is the feature—scoped to the morph root, never body, and Safari paint-profiled
   before the contract is sworn (§3.4).
7. **The keyboard law.** Keyboard/AT input always gets deterministic preset motion with zero
   seeded velocity. Lenses may trail as paint; focus and highlight never coast past truth.
8. **Overshoot is earned.** Springs seeded v=0 land dead; only live velocity buys overshoot; the
   [0,10%] preset fence stays (conditional exemption—§6 question 2).
9. **PRM pins the field to 0.** State still arrives—charge becomes a step, detents become the
   only landings, the scrub still tracks input at a two-state ladder. PRM removes physics, never
   information (§3.5, the single table).

### 3.2 F4's existential test, stated as cells

F4 stands as a family only if field coupling ALONE—no position substrate, no conductor—delivers
the hallmark feel for named cells. Those cells are: A×Q2/Q3 (press-charge + squish depth),
G×Q3-engage (the whole-bar wash as a field threshold), N4 (strain read as rim luminance),
I×Q7 (the fill-tip glint from the value-velocity source), L×Q7 (edge-fade depth from scroll
velocity), K×Q7 (substrate perturbation). If pass 2 cannot make those cells live on the field
contract alone, F4 dissolves into a helper library and must say so. Conversely F4 must confess
its boundary in writing: it cannot express detents, reveal ladders, the lens body, or the
medium—those are F1/F3/F5 territory, composed, not absorbed.
