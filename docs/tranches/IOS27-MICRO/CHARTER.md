## The exhortation (the user's words, cloned exactly; grammar and structure mended, nothing cut)

This video showcases several hallmarks:

1. **The dock-to-card growth (Maps and Find My).** Note how, in the Maps app and in the Find My
   app, the bottom dock grows to expand into a card — and when it grows, it not only grows upward
   to afford the card, but it slightly squishes and stretches at the bottom and at the left and
   right edges — it grows slightly horizontally.

2. **The magnetic overpull.** Mark how the dock can be pulled DOWNWARD even PAST its maximal
   downward position: this makes the component feel alive and REAL, but MAGNETICALLY bounded —
   like a bézier control point.

3. **The eyeglass tabs.** Mark how the eyeglass-tabs facility works in the Find My app — and how
   the same facility works in Safari (though it is markedly BETTER in Find My).

4. **The material.** Mark the glass morphism and the transparency of each page and card, in both
   apps.

5. **The Control Centre pull-down choreography.** Mark the Control Centre pull-down, and the
   choreography between several animations: transforms (stretching and pulling downward), fading
   in and out, and so on — all at different, dynamic points, never naively batched exactly
   together. The fading happens FASTER than the stretching, and so on — to suffuse the breath of
   life. These small details are critical and should be brainstormed upon, though within a sense
   of our aristotelian proportion: how might we BEST these? How might we, with our begotten DAG
   of components in our library, suffuse MORE of these — bounded magnetism, weight, specular
   highlighting, true glass, proper and readable dynamic transparency, choreographed
   multi-dimensional animations — for every component: expressive affordance and interactivity,
   with proper state relayed to the user, for every variant (think button states, but at a more
   abstract level).

6. **The card-expansion choreography and the physics.** Mark how, when the card expands up from
   the dock, the elements animate in, morph, and choreograph in such a multi-dimensional way,
   too: notice the springiness — and mark, too, the MOMENTUM and VELOCITY and ACCELERATION
   tracking of these curves. ALL of our components should have such a facility.

7. **The bounds.** In these prototypes and brainstorming sessions, we bound ourselves to MODERN
   web facilities and design as of 2026, with SAFARI as the common denominator, in a PERFORMANT
   manner. iOS 27 is a custom OS with hyper-optimized glass facilities; we likely cannot beat
   that at every level. But we can approach it — and we certainly can best it in aristotelian
   expressivity.

**HEAR THE BREATH OF LIFE. MARK THE MOMENTUM.**
