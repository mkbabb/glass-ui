## 1. The pass, whole

Pass 1 ran the full loop for the first time: research (8 digests) → specs (5) → prototypes
(4 built + PROBE-NOTES; F2 never built) → adversarial critiques (5) → serialized browser-seat
verification (4 families driven in Chrome 150). The registry's round-zero bet — five complete
formulations of the whole problem with different architectural centers — mostly held: four
families produced falsifiable artifacts and three of them proved their center in paint.

**What pass 1 established.**

- The campaign's central thesis is real in paint, on one engine. Three independent
  architectures reproduced the MARKS choreography in Chrome from measured constants, not
  authored timelines: F1's spine + follower bank (CC open/close/interrupt, the Maps bound
  physics, overpull overshoot 32–33% in the 30–50 band, rAF parked after every settle), F3's
  coupled channels (12/12 battery, depth grading measured at exactly 1.20× vs MARKS ~1.20,
  park mid-scrub, PRM seat in one poll), and F5's layered material (no-blink proven by an
  independent per-rAF joint-presence sampler across three scenarios, the layer contract
  verified computed). F4's modulation plane proved its laws exactly (U-LAW to four decimals,
  22 writes/frame at 2–7µs, PRM pinned) with its one live defect root-caused rather than
  hidden.
- Scrub-everything is achievable: F1 and F3 both caught mid-flight interrupts with state
  carried over — the CC blur-held-featureless beat exists in web paint.
- The park discipline holds everywhere it was tested: every driven prototype parks rAF at
  settle; F4's field is event-window-gated with zero idle writes.
- The corpus read (MARKS) is strong enough to drive gates — and carries defects of its own
  that three families transcribed (see §4, item 2).

**What pass 1 did not establish.**

- A single Safari frame. Every family's G1 is the same gap: the task floor is Safari 2026,
  the registry invariant is Chrome+Safari in paint, and every VERIFIED verdict is Chrome 150
  only. The open Safari items are precisely the load-bearing ones: F1's medium mechanism and
  clip-path residency, F3's blur-rides-opacity claim, F4's abs()/max() transform computing and
  invalidation symmetry, F5's U1/U3/blink/PRM — the whole reason F5 exists.
- Transient-state paint truth for the lens. H3 has zero transient evidence on any engine —
  F5's two travel captures are mislabeled rest states (the ferry confound); F1's H3 row is
  assertion; F3's lens rack is asserted. The one hallmark where we claim to best iOS outright
  is the least evidenced.
- The H6 facility. The task statement names momentum/velocity/acceleration tracking for ALL
  components as a hallmark; no family owns it. F1 name-drops acceleration with no consumer,
  F3 points at X2 §4, F4 — the natural owner — ships the field but no acceleration consumer
  and no verb-delivery surface. Consumer-less substrate is the named failure mode and it is
  live.
- Gate integrity as a discipline. Three families passed on bands that were re-fit to their own
  constants (F1 G2), widened beyond the displayed MARKS figures (F3 G8), or tautological by
  construction (F4 G4, F1's catch cells hard-wired PASS, F5's continuity monitor). The pattern
  is systemic: gates were re-fit to constants, not constants to corpus.

**Seat honesty.** The critique corpus is unusually good — every family's PROBE-NOTES carries a
genuine dishonesties ledger, and most gaps the critics found were named-but-unamended rather
than concealed. The one process failure: F2's prototype seat produced nothing and did not mark
the family spec-only, a charter violation that costs the family its evidence layer entirely.

## 3. Cross-pollination — proven in one family, needed in another

1. **The intent/direction law (F1 ↔ F3).** The same defect surfaced from two directions: F1's

   overshoot crossing (G6). F1's committed-target latch is the cure for F3; F3's law-vocabulary
   declaration surface is the home F1's heuristic needs. Pass 2 writes ONE intent law — latch
   at drive time, projection window and hysteresis stated, idle velocity decay — shared by both
   specs.
2. **Depth grading (F3 → F1, F4).** F3 proved the normalized form in paint (1.20× exact,
   rect-measured). F1's follower bank adopts the same normalized gain; F4's `i % 8` sawtooth
   (G12) is replaced by F3's position-in-viewport read at channel-open.
3. **The rubber-band domain (F1 → F5, F4; confirms F2's loss ledger).** F1's extended domain
   with hyperbolic saturation, μ_down < μ_up, and the margin-depth vs compression-gain
   separation is the one working overpull mechanism across engines. F5's body layer and F4's
   carrier plane consume it; F2's Chrome no-rubber-band loss is the negative proof that the JS
   domain is the only cross-engine answer.
4. **H6 ownership (F1, F3 → F4).** F1 G9 and F3 G11 both name the facility with no owner; F4
   is the only family whose center IS the facility. Ruling: F4 owns H6. F1/F3 expose the

5. **The paint-truth sampler (F5 → F1, F3, F4).** F5's independent per-rAF joint-presence
   sampler (opacity × visibility × rect-area, capsule vs light-child) is the instrument that
   cures F3's self-referential battery (G9) and grounds F1's timing gates paint-side. Pass 2
