## §1 — The verified defects (the set-level gaps; from B2-gestalt, file:line)

### D1 (HIGHEST-LEVERAGE) — the blob carries TWO clashing identities one cell apart.

`W-BLOB2-goo-blob-desktop-light.png` (the resting default) is a muted butter-tan / khaki bead
(OKLCh L≈0.81, hue 78 — yellow-orange). `W-BLOB2-blob-mood-hover-frame{1..5}-desktop-light.png`
(the mood hero, SAME component, SAME page, one section down) is a HOT fluorescent coral-pink ball
(seed `oklch(0.62 0.19 25)` pushed near-neon by the shader's SSS/iridescence). They share NO color
language: the cream default reads SOTA-restrained, on-brand-warm; the mood ball reads like a 2010
lava-lamp toy, the single least-glass-ui-feeling pixel in the whole set. W-BLOB2's gate
(`proof:blob-warm-default`, body-L ≥ 0.62) measures ONLY the resting default; the mood hero's neon
is entirely unmeasured.

### D2 — the blob shadow is a Memphis sticker stamp, not a gel-bead contact shadow.

`GooBlob.vue:229-232` — the wrapper drop-shadow is `drop-shadow(5px 5px 2.5px color-mix(in srgb,
var(--blob-color) 20%, var(--foreground)))`: an 80%-near-black, hard-offset `5px 5px` stamp,
visible in EVERY blob capture (default + all 5 mood frames + dark) as a harsh dark shadow flung
down-right. It makes the "living gel bead" read as a clipart sticker pasted on the page — a flat
decal, not a lit dome sitting in a scene. Out of register with the whole set: constellation, FF,
and aurora all use soft ambient light with no hard offset. The cartoon offset-stamp belongs on
`<Card surface="cartoon">`, not on the SOTA gel bead. A gel bead wants a soft, near-centered,
low-offset ambient contact shadow (small blur-up, near-zero offset, ambient-tinted). Measured by no
gate.

### D3 — the cross-substrate RECESSION knob parity is broken at constellation (3-of-4).

Three of the four live substrates expose an outer-envelope intensity knob so the substrate can
RECEDE behind content (the band's "live substrate behind a glass card" pattern): Aurora
(`opacityCeiling`, `StoryHero` sets 0.4–0.6), FourierField (`intensity`, `StoryHero` threads
`opacityCeiling → :intensity`, the W-FF2 D11 parity), Blob (config-level palette/quality atoms).
**Constellation has NONE** (`Constellation.vue:58-102` — no `opacityCeiling`/`intensity`/`alpha`
prop; it paints at the full `--constellation-alpha`). So in `StoryHero` the aurora and fourier
heroes recede behind the prose but a constellation hero CANNOT — it sits at full strength while its
siblings dim. The four don't share the ONE compositing-envelope contract that would let them be
dropped behind content interchangeably. The W-FF2 "3-substrate parity" claim is really 3-of-4:
fourier was threaded, constellation skipped. (The `--constellation-alpha` token is mode-tuned but
it is NOT the per-instance outer envelope the other three carry.)

### D4 — the unifying accent fractures at the blob (the latent cohesion seam, unclaimed).

The accent that SHOULD bind the set is a warm red: constellation focal `--primary` (warm red-ink),
FourierField `--viz-fourier` = `oklch(0.579 0.201 30.4)` (red-orange), blob mood seed `oklch(0.62
0.19 25)` (red basis). Three of four nominally share a red basis — but the blob breaks it TWICE:
its default leaves the red family for khaki (hue 78), and its mood over-saturates the red into neon
(D1). Bring the blob into the warm-red-but-desaturated register (D1+D2) and all four share ONE
accent identity. The cohesion win is sitting right there, unclaimed.

### D5 (coordination, not new work) — light↔dark set-strength is uneven; each surface is strong in a DIFFERENT mode.

Constellation: both modes stunning (PASS). Blob: both modes hold the cream bead but BOTH carry the
sticker shadow (D2), worse on the dark ground. FF: dark stunning, light near-invisible (the W-FF2
RG3 light-mode floor). Dock: the own-surface DELTA is OWED (the W-DOCK2 RG1 capture). There is no
single "the set looks great in light AND dark, together" statement that holds. W-COHERE does NOT
re-own the FF light floor (W-FF2 RG3) or the dock capture (W-DOCK2 RG1) — it DEPENDS on them and
binds them into the set-level both-mode gate (§6 G4). The new work is D1–D4; D5 is the gate that
holds the set accountable once its dependencies land.

## §2 — Objective (the gestalt, the one-move cohesion gain)

The single highest-leverage move (B2-gestalt's verdict): bring the blob into the set's language.
Then close the recession-envelope parity, then bind the four with a set-level gate.

1. **Blob mood/seed palette → the warm-red, lower-chroma register (D1, D4).** The mood/seed-derived
   palette de-saturates into the same warm-red, lower-OKLCh-chroma register the constellation focal
   (`--primary`) and the FF comet (`--viz-fourier`) already speak, so the blob has ONE identity
   across rest+mood in the same family as its siblings. This is a CHROMA-CEILING on the
   shader-amplified seed path, not a hue re-map of the user's chosen seed (a user who explicitly
   seeds a blue blob still gets blue — the ceiling caps the SSS/iridescence neon amplification, it
   does not override intent). The DEFAULT seed + the demo mood seed move into the register.
2. **Blob wrapper shadow → a soft ambient contact shadow (D2).** Replace the `5px 5px` near-black
   cartoon offset-stamp (`GooBlob.vue:229`) with a soft, near-centered, low-offset, ambient-tinted
   contact shadow (a gel bead sits in the scene; it does not get stamped on the page). The shadow is
   tokenised (`--blob-shadow-*`) so it reads on cream AND ink without a hardcoded `.dark`
   re-declaration (the `--shadow-color`/`--foreground` re-resolution the house shadow family already
   uses).
3. **Constellation outer-envelope recession prop (D3).** Add the per-instance compositing-envelope
   knob constellation lacks (the `opacityCeiling`/`intensity` sibling of aurora + fourier) so a
   `<Constellation>` hero can recede behind content at 0.4–0.6 exactly as its three siblings do —
   the ONE recession contract the set shares. The prop scales the painted alpha at the draw layer
   (over `--constellation-alpha`), NOT a second token; default `1` is byte-identical to HEAD.
4. **The set-level cohesion gate (D1–D5).** A NEW gate that measures the four AS A SET: ONE accent
   family (the blob mood bead's chroma is bounded into the warm-red band the focal + comet share),
   ONE recession contract (all four components expose the envelope knob — a source-witness), ONE
   shadow language (the blob shadow is ambient, not the cartoon stamp), and a both-mode set-strength
   DELTA (the four captured together in light AND dark, each legible). Born-RED at HEAD on D1/D2/D3.

This is a convergence wave: it does not re-build any substrate's mechanism (the refit, the cream
default, the intensity model, the morph are all settled). It pulls the worst-offending surface
(blob) into the constellation-defined optimum and binds the four to one shared contract.

## §5 — Risk ledger

1. **Chroma-ceiling over-flattening (D1).** Capping the mood seed's chroma too hard makes every blob
   read the same dull tan (kills the mood expressivity W-BLOB2's 8-atom set preserves). The ceiling
   is the warm-register C band (the focal + comet sit at C≈0.20) — high enough to read as a saturated
   warm bead, low enough to kill the neon. Locked by the G-ACCENT band (a floor AND a ceiling on the
   mood chroma, not just a ceiling) + the mood DELTA reading as a living warm bead, not a flat slab.
2. **Recession default-OFF byte-identity (D3).** `opacityCeiling = 1` must leave the constellation
   draw byte-identical to HEAD. Locked by a default-path canary (a `opacityCeiling`-absent
   `<Constellation>` produces an identical first-N-frame draw-alpha trace to HEAD).
3. **Shadow dark-arm (D2).** The ambient shadow must re-resolve under `.dark` with no hardcoded
   `.dark` block (the house adaptive-by-construction pattern). Locked by a both-mode π readback of
   the painted shadow (it reads soft+ambient on cream AND ink).
4. **Dependency-order (the whole wave).** W-COHERE edits the blob + constellation SFCs that W-GOD1
   carves and W-CON*/W-BLOB* settle. It runs LAST in the substrate band. Mandate a step-0 re-grep of
   every E1–E3 cite against the carved tree (the stale-worktree-trap discipline). If W-GOD1 split a
   cited file, the recession/shadow/chroma edit lands in the carved sub-module, not the pre-carve
   monolith.
5. **G4 dependency honesty.** The both-mode set DELTA needs the FF light floor (W-FF2 RG3) + the
   dock capture (W-DOCK2 RG1). G4 is born-RED on those arms until they land — the dependency is in
   the gate, not papered over with a prose "assume they land."
