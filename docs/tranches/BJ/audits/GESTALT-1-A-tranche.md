### INC-1 · The feedback cluster: a same-day census drift both specs baked in (the sweep's headline)
**Dissonant.** At `bda718ac` (11:56) the pulse→StatusDot merge reduced `_shared` FeedbackMark.vue
+ feedback.ts to exactly ONE consumer (status-dot; verified by fresh grep this sweep — the only
non-`_shared` hit for `FeedbackMark` is `status-dot/StatusDot.vue`; the only importers of

own scope item (3) — "OPTIONAL de-indirection: inline FeedbackMark.vue into StatusDot.vue" — was
neither taken nor declined with a recorded reason. `feedback-tone.css` is NOT part of this: it is
genuinely shared (alert, toast, ladder.css, dark-arm.css).
**Why it matters.** The tree now enshrines in `_shared/` the exact single-consumer shape the
band's own hygiene fence (COLO W3 Form A clause 1) exists to police — and the fence, scoped to
`src/composables/**`, will not even catch it. The KISS oath prefers the cure that deletes an
abstraction; two waves each followed their band file correctly and jointly produced the opposite.
**Disposition (mechanical cure).** One cut, one owner (a COLO W1 tail or REDUCTION's next touch):
move `FeedbackMark.vue` + `feedback.ts` into `src/components/status-dot/` (inlining FeedbackMark
into StatusDot.vue is the fuller KISS cure; the move alone suffices), leave `feedback-tone.css` in
`_shared/feedback/`, rewrite the ≤4 reference sites (status-dot imports, `index.css` ledger),
prove with the hash-normalized dist identity already precedented by W-COLO-1. If declined, record
the decline in `BAND-REDUCTION.md` W6 §Status so the silence stops being silent.

### INC-2 · The aurora floor "gates the tag" by convention only — release.yml carries no receipt
**Dissonant.** The instrument split (ruled, correct) puts the aurora floor in `scripts/release.sh`
(real GPU, both arms). But `release.yml` — the actual tag-push provenance publish — neither runs
release.sh nor asserts any aurora pi-report receipt exists (verified: zero `pixel-floor`/
`release.sh` references in release.yml). A tag pushed without running release.sh ships an
un-floored aurora silently — the `gate:unwired-gate-non-execution` class, re-created one lane over
from where W2 just cured it.
**Why it matters.** The 7.0.0 lesson is precisely that gate drift surfaces only at tag-push; and
the close battery (`--run release`) is the one hinge the owner runs.
**Disposition (mechanical cure).** Add a release.yml step that FAILS unless the banked aurora
pi-report pair (green + planted) exists at its evidence path and postdates the tagged tree's
aurora-source mtimes — a receipt check that runs fine on ubuntu because it reads files, not GPUs.
Alternative (cheaper, weaker): a TERMINAL-ROUTINGS one-liner ruling `release.sh` the SOLE lawful
tag path, cited from release.yml's header. Owner: GATES band, lands with the P-CLOSE-BATTERY row.
