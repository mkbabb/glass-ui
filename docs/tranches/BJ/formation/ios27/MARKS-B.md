# iOS-27 liquid-glass frame marks — group B (codex:ios27-B, Fable)

Detailed frame-by-frame read of the group-B corpus (June 22-24 + July 10 recordings + late
stills), performed 2026-07-17. Feeds BJ registry **family F** (material/token canon — radius ·
blur · type · the gradient-backdrop-blur experiment) and **family G** (greenfields — blob, dock,
feedback-motion). The goal is a codex to design *against* — to BEST iOS 27, not photocopy it.

All four videos are 1fps JPEG extractions at 1206×2622 (iPhone Pro). Frames cited as
`video/f-00NN`. Videos are keyed by their folder timestamp.

## Cross-corpus synthesis — the idiom registry (design *against*)

### 1. Radius grammar (family F — the radius role-scale)
One opinionated, role-keyed scale, with **constant corner *smoothness* (superellipse)** and radius
scaling by element size:
| role | shape | where |
|---|---|---|
| **sheet / container / module / card** | rounded-rect, continuous-corner, ~R28–R40 | CC modules, Siri cards, Maps/Find My sheets, Spotlight results, widgets |
| **field** | stadium pill | search bars, "Search or Ask", "Search Google" |
| **mode selector** | stadium pill | Focus/Sleep, Top/Newest segments |
| **inline action** | stadium pill | Directions/Contact/5 min |
| **control (toggle / quick-action / +) ** | circle | CC toggles, quick-grid, avatars, "+" |
| **slider** | tall vertical capsule | brightness/volume (bottom-anchored fill + bottom glyph) |
| **app-icon / thumbnail / banner-icon** | rounded-square (~22% radius) | icons, album art, app cards |
**Laws:** a card CONTAINS circles/pills — never the inverse; nesting stays **concentric** (inner
R = outer R − padding); you never mix a sharp corner and a pill in one composition (the exact
radius-canon-collapse the ledger flags in F12/F15/F17/F45/F48). This kills the ledger's
radius chaos with a table, not spot-fixes.

### 2. Gradient / progressive backdrop blur (family F headline — adopt `--glass-halo-*`)
The corpus consistently shows blur+luminance that is **NOT a uniform slab**:
- **Graded scrim** behind a floated element (Spotlight V3/f-0004): darkest/most-blurred near the
  element, easing away — a directional gradient.
- **Radial glow pool** around a floated control against dark (F49/F50): peak at the element,
  falls to black.
- **Detent-graded** sheet material (V1/f-0009 vs f-0011): more transparent partial, more opaque
  full.
**Implication:** the gradient-backdrop-blur experiment should render blur+brightness that
**concentrates near the floated surface and falls off** (radial or directional), plus an overall
**subtler element blur** than glass-ui ships today (the F28/F48 "one heavy uniform blur" defect).
Judge it against the Spotlight/F49 references, not a flat frost.

### 3. Adaptive content-color tint (family F — new material capability)
Liquid glass is a **living frost that takes hue + luminance from its backdrop, positionally**:
Find My sheet green over a green map, blooming warm where a warm avatar sits behind it (V4/f-0011);
CC quick-action circles carry wallpaper-tinted rims (V1/f-0002). glass-ui's material should sample
a positional/dominant tint rather than fixing a neutral grey — this is what makes it read as glass
and not as a grey card.

### 4. Edge caustic / rim light (family F + the liquid-glass signature)
The glass boundary is a **specular caustic**, not a 1px border:
- Concentrates on the **lower rim + corners** (lit from below/within).
- Can carry a subtle **iridescent hue shift**, and on Siri **animates a full hue cycle** along the
  boundary (V2/f-0007→f-0021).
- Lights up **during motion** (CC slider rims in V1/f-0005; Spotlight pill entry rim V3/f-0004).
**Implication:** replace flat rim borders with a lower-weighted specular highlight + optional
iridescent sweep; let it animate on entry/interaction. (This is the tasteful version of the
V-A95/aurora "rim" work — a material property, not a bolted-on ring like F04/F05.)

### 5. Liquid-glass blob (family G — blob greenfield; reference value.js)
A **3D metaball of chrome/liquid-metal** with a mobile pearlescent specular streak + traveling
caustic, morphing **pill ↔ orb ↔ pill** on a spring squash (V2/f-0004/f-0017/f-0019), material per
the METAL FLOW / images-2 stills. Not a flat blob. The value.js metaball is the right substrate;
shade it with a specular highlight + subtle iridescence.

### 6. Sliding selection pill + glass bar (family G — dock greenfield)
Find My's tab bar (V4) is the closest analog to glass-ui's dock: a **stadium glass tray** with a
**brighter glass pill that liquid-slides** to the selected item (the goo-morph/liquid-weight edict
made literal). The home dock is the same tray holding rounded-square icons. For **overflow**, iOS
never clips mid-glyph (the F47 defect) — it uses edge-fade / detents / clustering (the "+3"
overflow pill in V4/f-0009). Dock greenfield: glass tray + liquid selection pill + edge-fade
occlusion + cluster overflow, no interior scroll (F27).

### 7. Bottom-sheet detents + floating glass over live content (structure)
Sheets carry a **grabber pill**, snap to **small/medium/large detents**, scale content, and grade
material opacity with height (V1, V4). Floating controls over live content are **glass pill
clusters** (map-layers+locate stacked; 3D/binoculars circles; weather chip). Overlays either
**slide from an edge** (banners: blur+shadow, rounded-rect, leading rounded-square icon) or
**morph out of the Dynamic Island** (Siri). glass-ui sheets/popovers should adopt detents + grabber
+ material-grading.

### 8. Spring / motion character (family G — feedback-motion)
Observable at 1fps: **staggered entry** (CC density fills top-down, V1/f-0005), **material
responds to motion** (rims light up while translating), **squash morphs** (Siri blob inflate/
deflate), **liquid slide** (tab pill). Exact overshoot isn't measurable from 1fps, but the rule is
clear: motion is never a plain opacity fade — the material *reacts* (rim glow, squash, hue). This
is the liquid-weight edict; glass-ui's feedback components (F20 toast, F21 rim, F22 loop) were
never held to it.

### 9. Segmented capsule progress (family G — feedback-motion; F21/F22 model)
F49/F50's control is a clean discrete-level idiom: **stadium track + a solid pill that grows +
remaining-step dots**. It reads instantly and carries weight (the pill swallows a dot as it grows).
A better model for the broken scroll-progress-rim / loop than the current partial-arc jitter.

### 10. Typography & hierarchy (family F — the F15 type audit)
Consistent scale across surfaces: **hero numeral/heading (bold, ~28–34)** >> **row title
(~17 semibold)** >> **secondary (~15 grey)** >> **caption/tab (~11–13)**. One **accent color** for
interactive/status (blue Directions, green "Now", the accent breadcrumb), grey for secondary,
generous line-height, glyphs primary in dense controls (CC). Section headers are bold + chevron
("Places ›", "Recents ›"). No mono ALL-CAPS jargon captions anywhere in iOS — the opposite of the
meta-caption idiom the ledger kills (family D).

### 11. Aristotelian proportion notes
- CC module: 2×2 circles with gutter ≈ circle radius, even inset inside the card.
- Sliders: capsule height ≈ 3.5× width; glyph at bottom-center of the fill.
- Sheet rows: leading circle ~44px + text, hairline dividers, ~16–20px card padding.
- Concentric nesting: inner control radius = card radius − padding (corners stay parallel).
- Avatars/quick-actions: ~44–56px circles; category circles larger (~64px) with full-saturation
  fills that pop against a muted sheet.

## What "BEST iOS 27" means for glass-ui (not copy — exceed)
1. **Codify the radius table** (§1) and gate it — iOS is *consistent*; glass-ui can be *provably*
   consistent (token + lint gate), which iOS achieves only by convention.
2. **Ship gradient backdrop blur AND adaptive tint together** (§2+§3) — iOS does both; glass-ui
   doing both, tokenized and theme-aware in light+dark, is the differentiator over a static frost.
3. **Rim caustic as a material property** (§4) with an opt-in iridescent sweep — cleaner than
   iOS's occasionally-gaudy Siri rainbow; keep it subtle by default, animate on interaction.
4. **Blob = a real metaball** (§5) shaded like the METAL FLOW stills, not a CSS blur blob.
5. **Dock = glass tray + liquid selection pill + edge-fade overflow** (§6) — fix F47/F27/F04/F05
   in one gesture by adopting the Find-My tab-bar model.
6. **Every transition reacts** (§8) — hold feedback-motion to the liquid-weight edict; no plain
   fades.
