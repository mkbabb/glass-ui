## Phenomena

### 1. Detent springs — position channel

Sheet is a rigid slab translated vertically; nothing else about it animates on a clock (see §4, §5).

Measured trajectories (top, disp px):

Drop mid-zone→collapsed (release, r12B): 995 @3.78 → 1075 @3.87 → 1240 @3.95 → 1400 @4.03 → 1440 @4.12 → 1600 @4.20 → 1712 @4.28 → 1770 @4.37 → 1790 @4.45 → 1795 @4.53. Peak ~1990 disp px/s (~2600 device px/s ≈ 1.0 screen/s); ~500ms deceleration tail; monotone arrival, no bounce below rest.

Flick to full (r12C): 938 @7.07 → 770 @7.23 → 278 @7.40 → 193 @7.48 → 168 @7.57 → 148 @7.65 → 145 @7.82. Peak ~2950 disp px/s (~3870 device ≈ 1.5 screens/s); monotone arrival, no overshoot.

Flick full→medium (r12C + r60): 145 @8.57 → 210 @8.65 → 565 @8.73 → 940 @8.82 → 1075 @8.90 → 1118 @8.95 → 1142 @9.00 → ~1155 @9.05 → 1138 @9.10 → 1135 @9.15. Peak ~4500 disp px/s (~5900 device ≈ 2.25 screens/s); undershoot past the detent by ~15-25px (r60-07..10), recovered in ~100ms.

Reading: edge detents (collapsed, full) land critically damped — no visible overshoot at 60fps; the middle detent lands slightly underdamped (~2% of travel). Detent selection is velocity-biased: the 3.80s release from top≈990 (only ~140px past medium) skipped medium entirely and ran to collapsed because the finger was moving down at release — projected momentum picks the target, not proximity.

Web recreation (rank 1 — carries most of the feel): fixed full-height sheet, `transform: translate3d(0, y, 0)` only; spring integrator with projected-momentum target selection (`target = detentNearest(y + v*τ)`, τ≈0.2s); damping per detent — ζ≈1.0 at travel edges, ζ≈0.85-0.9 at interior detents; peak speeds 1-2.25 screen-heights/s are the reference band.

### 2. Overlay dodge + threshold fades (controls, weather chip)

The 3D/binocular circles and globe/locate capsule ride the sheet: their cluster y = sheetTop − 236 disp (~310 device), held constant through slow drags and fast flicks alike (f4-001 rest gap 235; r12B-04..08 gaps 235-250 during the fast drop). The cluster is clamped at a ceiling of y≈897 disp — exactly the tracked position when the sheet sits at the medium detent. It never rises above that.

- Fade-out (upward): the sheet crosses medium → cluster freezes at the ceiling and fades out in place, opacity-only, no translation. Full @r12A-04 (3.15) → ~30% @r12A-05 (3.23) → gone @r12A-06 (3.32): ~150ms. Repeats identically on the second rise (full @f4-026, gone @f4-027).
- Fade-in (downward) is PREDICTIVE, not positional: on the 3.80s release the ghosts are already reappearing at r12B-03 (3.87) while the sheet top is 1075 — still past-medium territory; on the 8.6s flick they ghost in at r12C-24 (8.82) with the sheet at 940 and still moving ~4500px/s, and are fully opaque at the ceiling by r12C-26 (8.98) — well before the sheet settles. The system keys on gesture direction/projected target, not current position. Fade-in ~150-250ms, at the clamped ceiling, then the cluster unclamps and resumes tracking once sheetTop − 236 > ceiling.
- The weather/AQI chip obeys the same fade rule but never translates (fixed top-left). Ghost states visible at r12A-05, r12B-03, r12C-24.
- Left circles and right capsule move as one rigid cluster — no stagger between sides.

Web recreation (rank 2): `overlayY = max(min(sheetTop - 310, restY), ceilingY)` driven off the same spring state each frame; opacity state machine — OUT on upward mid-crossing, IN on release/flick whose projected target ≤ medium; 150ms linear-ish fades; fade in place, never fade-while-translating. The predictive fade-in is the detail that makes it feel native — waiting for position thresholds reads laggy.

### 3. Scrim + sheet material are position-mapped, not time-animated

Above medium, a dark scrim over the map and the sheet's own tint both deepen as functions of sheet position: absent at medium (r12C-26: map fully legible above the sheet), faint at top≈770 (r12C-05), strong at top≈278 (r12C-07), near-black map sliver at full (f4-033, r12C-12 — "POINTE VIEW" barely legible under the scrim). On the way down the same values reappear at the same heights (r12C-23 top 565 matches the up-leg gradient) — the mapping is scrub-reversible, which rules out clock-based fades. The sheet body runs the same ramp: light warm glass with visible map bleed at low heights (f4-008, f4-024) → medium-dark glass at medium (f4-038) → near-opaque charcoal at full (r12C-08+). Backdrop blur is present at every height; only darkening/saturation ramps.

Web recreation (rank 3): `scrimAlpha = clamp((midTop - sheetTop)/(midTop - fullTop), 0, 1) * maxAlpha` and a parallel sheet-tint interpolation, both derived from the live spring position every frame; constant backdrop-filter blur. No transitions on these channels — pure functions of y.

### 4. Content is a rigid mask reveal — deliberate desync absence

Every internal element (search row at top+73, Places header at top+215, icon row at top+360, labels, Recents rows) keeps constant offset from the sheet top at every sampled instant, including mid-flick (r12B-04/05, r12C-07/08: search-to-Places spacing 138-140 disp throughout). Content is clipped by the rising edge, appearing fully-formed at full opacity (f4-009 icon heads cut mid-glyph; r12B-07 icons half-cut). No per-element fade, slide, stagger, or parallax; no edge squish or stretch of the slab under ~4500px/s flicks.

So the per-channel decomposition for this surface: position is the master channel; scrim/tint/materials are functions of position (§3); the ONLY channels that run on their own clocks are the overlay opacity fades (§2), which intentionally lead (fade-in, predictive) or trail (fade-out at threshold) the master channel by ~150ms. There is no fade-vs-stretch-vs-blur stagger inside the sheet itself.

Web recreation (rank 4): render content once, pinned to the sheet; let overflow clipping do the reveal. Resist adding entrance animations to sheet content — the reference explicitly has none, and the slab's rigidity is what sells the weight.
