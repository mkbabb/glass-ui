## 4. Detent-commit ambient swap (the choreography DESYNC finding)

The sheet has two materials: light glass (pill + mid) and near-opaque dark (full). The swap is not tied to position — it fires as one synchronized beat when the gesture commits to a detent, while the sheet is still mid-flight:

Upward (flick to full):
- f12_088 (7.250, top 490): sheet body still light glass; the Recents group card has already turned dark — the elevated card leads the swap by one frame (~80ms).
- f12_089 (7.333, top 290): entire sheet dark; weather widget gone; 3D/binoculars buttons and globe/locate pill gone; map strip dimmed. Whole swap ≤85ms at 12fps granularity, completed while position still had 190px (~35% of travel) to go.

Downward (dismiss):
- f12_107 (8.833, top 365): sheet still dark, but the weather widget is already back and the 3D button is fading in at its mid-detent position — chrome leads the material on the way down.
- f12_108 (8.917, top 660): sheet fully light glass again. Swap ≤85ms, again mid-flight.

Projection, not position — the drag-1 asymmetry:
- Drag 1 (sustained upward pull): 3D button present at top 730 (f4_013, 3.0); by f12_040 (3.25, top 688) weather widget and all map buttons are gone — the sheet was ~450px from full, but the gesture's projected endpoint was full. On release they fade back (f12_046–047, 3.75–3.83).
- Drag 2 (pull that pauses at mid): at the same heights — and beyond, down to top 600 — widget and buttons stay put (f12_084–088, 6.92–7.25). They only swap when the flick commits.
- Inference (flagged as such): the trigger is the projected detent from position + velocity, with hysteresis holding the current detent during slow creep. All ambient channels — sheet material, weather widget, map buttons, map dim — subscribe to that one boolean and crossfade together in ~100ms.

Web recreation (load: high — a whole-screen luminance flip, the clip's most distinctive beat): keep a single `mode` state on a common ancestor, computed from the gesture projector (same math as the detent chooser), not from y-thresholds; all ambient elements transition opacity/background over 100–120ms linear on mode change. Implementing this as scroll/position thresholds produces the wrong behavior in exactly the drag-1 case above.

- Grabber present at every detent, same width; it is the first morph signal on touch (§1).
- The avatar disk keeps constant size across all detents; only its container context changes.
- Places header appears only once sheet height affords it (f12_033 region) — layout is full-width from birth and clipped, so no text reflow ever occurs during motion.
