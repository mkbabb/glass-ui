## Web-recreation notes, ranked by perceptual load

1. **Paging settle curve** (highest load — every swipe reads through it): exponential approach, distance-to-rest halves every ~70–75ms (τ≈100ms), one-sided for mid-list snaps. CSS `scroll-snap` cannot express this decay; use a JS spring/decay integrator (high damping, no bounce) driving `transform: translateX`. Flick sets initial velocity only; the envelope is invariant.
2. **End-of-list rubber band**: overshoot ~4% of card width (~11pt), single crossing, ~500ms in the overshoot zone, rebound decays like a reversed settle. Needs an overscroll boundary spring — underdamped just enough for one crossing. Skipping this flattens the entire carousel feel.
3. **Ambient card gradients**: 8–10 pt/s blob drift, perceptible at 166ms deltas, never paused by interaction. Compositor-driven (CSS keyframed mesh/`background` layers or a WebGL/canvas loop decoupled from scroll handlers) so gesture work can't stall it.
4. **Glass grades on one shelf**: two materials — near-transparent circles (backdrop passes ~fully, ghost glyphs) vs dense tinted pill (strong tint + edge hue bleed). Distinct `backdrop-filter` strengths plus a tint layer; the pill's tint may sample content color (album art) rather than being fixed.
5. **Marquee**: constant ~50 pt/s, dual-line synced, edge fade masks (`mask-image` linear gradients), >6.5s dwell before onset. Linear `animation-timing-function`, no easing.
6. **Banner exit**: ≤170ms translate-up (~40pt) + fade, ease-in, blur maintained during fade — do not collapse `backdrop-filter` before opacity reaches 0.
7. **Rigid cards under velocity**: explicitly no skew/stretch on carousel cards at any speed — restraint is part of the reference look; reserve squish/goo for the dock choreography, not paging.
