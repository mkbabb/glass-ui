- Icon-color bleed is the dismissal's signature: as card fill fades ahead of blur release, the saturated app icons flare through the thinning glass (w2_05, w4_04) — recreating dismissal without this two-layer lag loses the characteristic color bloom.
- Maps sheet: two material states by detent — translucent grey-green glass at mid (map reads through at ~40%), near-opaque charcoal at full with a separate scrim on the residual map strip. Collapsed bar is the mid material.
- Maps floating controls (3D, binoculars, weather chip, globe/locate pill): darker smoke circles ~60% black over satellite imagery, no rim; they dim/brighten with map tile luminance.
- Home dock: bright warm glass slab (lighter than CC glass) that dims and blurs with the rest of home content under CC — the dock is NOT exempt from the background treatment.

## 7. Web-recreation notes, ranked by perceptual load

1. CC overpull + release spring (highest load): position-locked 1:1 tracking to arbitrary overpull, ~60ms release latency, ~250ms spring home with single 8-10% overshoot. Two-layer parallax (header 1.00, grid 1.04). Pointer/touch tracking + spring (stiffness ~220, damping ~28); never tween a tracked drag.
2. Open/dismiss channel asymmetry: open = blur-leads (80% in 80ms) → fade → position-settles-last; dismiss = text-dies-first → card fade (~230ms) → blur lags one beat (~330ms total) with icon-color bloom. Implement as two distinct choreographies; a reversed entry reads wrong immediately.
3. 4. App zoom both directions: rect interpolation icon-slot↔screen with corner-radius interpolation, icon artwork crossfaded at LARGE scale (~40% of flight), landing-icon 1.4x→1.0 scale settle on close, backdrop blur leading both directions.
5. Glass state events: accent rim-light on displaced/settling toggles, late saturation on entry, icon-color bleed on exit. Cheap to fake with an extra rim gradient + a saturate() ramp keyed to the same progress value.
6. Staggered content population: chrome → body → live data at ~250ms intervals; in-place text swap for late data.
7. Chrome-drops-then-morph on close — trivial, but ordering matters.
