### The five laws

**L1 · INK** — `w = weight · 0.20 · fs^0.75` px, `weight` dimensionless (default 1), **no clamp at either end**:

| rung | 11.50 | 12.97 | 14.63 | 16.50 | 18.608 | 23.67 | 30.10 | 38.29 | 48.70 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| w | 1.25 | 1.37 | 1.50 | 1.64 | 1.79 | 2.15 | 2.57 | 3.08 | **3.69** |
| w/stem | 0.99 | 0.96 | 0.93 | 0.90 | 0.88 | 0.82 | 0.78 | 0.73 | **0.69** |

**Declared a fit**: two free parameters against two painted endpoints in `proto-ladder.png`; the *form* (sublinear, stem-anchored, self-bounding, clampless) is the derived part; at 7 of 9 rungs all three rival laws differ ≤0.40px. The pencil voice is the ratio 0.40×stem, reachable via `weight ≈ 0.6` at display (0.41×) / `≈ 0.64` at the measurement's 67.78 (exact); not the default. Ink at α 1.0 by role — not a space/radius/divider rung; takes no `--ui-scale` step (type keeps its own clamp, PROPORTION §mobile).

**L2 · GESTURE** — `d(u) = S·[4u(1−u) + B·4u(1−u)(1−2u)]`, |B| ≤ 0.4; both modes vanish at the endpoints; `d′` is a quadratic → ≤2 extrema in closed form per seed. S = ±L·[0.006, 0.015] sag-biased 70/30; tilt ±1.2°; lift-off hook ±0.55·W·t² over u ∈ (0.94, 1], monotone in t = (u−0.94)/0.06; lead-in εin ∈ [0.005, 0.020]·span, run-out εout ∈ [0.012, 0.035]·span, **both positive, εout > εin on every seed**. Zero per-sample randomness; N = max(28, span/5).

**L2-RING** — superellipse n = 3, semi-axes **a = 2^(1/3)·(w/2 + 0.06em)**, **b = 2^(1/3)·(h/2 + 0.08em)**, h = cap. k = 2^(1/3) is the exact solution through the padded box corner, where the end letters stand at full cap height; verified clearance +0.1094…+0.0853em from 3–20ch — ≥2.2× the display half-nib (0.0379em) at every length. Vertical run 2b = 1.148em (77/96/109% of the line box at lh 1.50/1.20/1.05 — a ≤3.7px stroke over glyphs, never a fill). Sweep 384–402°, start 118–142°, radial creep +2.5–5.5% monotone (tail closes **outside** the head), wobble `1 + 0.022·sin(θ+φ₁) + 0.014·sin(2θ+φ₂)`. Drawn over the glyphs. Horizontal overshoot `a − w/2 = 0.2599·(w/2) + 0.0756em` is **reserved by `padding-inline` on the inline wrapper** — inline horizontal padding participates in inline layout (neighbours pushed, zero reflow feedback: the Range rects measure the text, not the padded box). **Circle targets a word, not a phrase** — beyond ~8–10ch the gesture itself is wrong; the story says so.

**L3 · PACE** — `T = 140 + 0.55·L_rect` ms, **no floor, no ceiling**; L is the path length of the rect being drawn; a mark emitting three rects runs three clocks, sequential in reading order. Checkbox tick (16px) 148.8 · word underline (216px) 258.8 · full 1224px line 813 — a hand takes that long, honestly.

**L4 · BAND** — one law on two papers:


**L5 · LAYERING** — wrapper isolates; underline/strike/circle over the glyphs in DOM order, no z-index; highlight `z-index:-1` **inside** the isolated context; **no blend mode anywhere** (with the wrapper isolated the backdrop is the empty group and any blend degenerates to the raw source — structural mutual exclusion, closing the chroma-vs-alpha OPEN row permanently). Isolation fixes **paint order**; **geometry fixes extent**. Two duties, two gate arms — because isolation does not clip.

### Motion

Draw = mask-dashoffset over the real `getTotalLength()` (never `pathLength="1"`), the mask a stroked copy of the centreline (mask stroke > max ribbon width), WAAPI with `easing: linear(minJerk 6t⁵−15t⁴+10t³ samples)` — resolves in real Safari 26.4 (capability banked), no rAF. A spring is deliberately wrong for the clock — a stroke cannot un-draw past its own end; LIQUID WEIGHT is carried by the profile. The dash clears outright: solid, whole, at rest. Ink settles 0.85 → 1.0 α over 200ms after geometry completes on `springPreset("gentle")` (0.82, ζ 1.00 — cannot overshoot, by the preset file's own invariant). Stagger: 60ms × the mark's line index within its nearest block ancestor, once per page visit, never on scroll-back. Exit = 150ms fade, never a reverse un-draw; `play()` restarts clean; resize regenerates from the same seed; PRM → finished static state.

**Breath of life, all modalities, ink still at rest:**
- **hover re-ink** (`hover:hover`) and `:focus-visible` of an ancestor interactive: replay from u = 0.35 over 220ms, +4% weight swell, `springPreset("press")` (0.20, ζ 0.80). Geometry never wriggles.
- **tap re-ink** (`hover:none`): `pointerdown` on the marked phrase triggers the same replay — the coarse pointer's native gesture gets the fine pointer's answer.
- **ink-lag under scroll** (all pointers): the mark trails its word by `min(1.5px, 0.06·scrollΔ)` and settles on `springPreset("smooth")` (0.58, ζ 0.80) — ink that has not quite dried. One passive listener, transform-only, bounded well under the nib width, inert at rest. π-SCROLL carries its kill criterion: reads as detachment → deleted, not tuned.
