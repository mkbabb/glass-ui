## §2 · THE CHOREOGRAPHY LAWS — the candidate canon for W-DESIGN-CANON

Fourteen rows, agglomerated from the Fable arm's sixteen and the Opus arm's eleven—duplicates fused, every row proved by ≥2 independent seats, each stated so a gate can check it. **They have no roster home until the owed W-DESIGN-CANON row lands (ADJ-4).**

**LAW 1 — NO TWO CHANNELS SHARE A CLOCK, AND THE ORDER IS FIXED.** Ten confirmations across seven recordings. The rank order: **rank −1** departing ink leaves before any boundary moves · **0** the touched body owns the first frame · **+2** the world starts early on `world` so it finishes last · **+3** container material (tint/density/refraction) is the container's LAST property to arrive · **+6** content resolves by **blur→sharp, not opacity** · **+9..+21** light, whose decay runs longest of all. The two seemingly contradictory readings—"the fading is faster than the stretching" and "geometry leads, content is held"—are both true about different layers: a container's **own** opacity finishes at ~60% of its geometry (real while still in flight—the effervescent read); its **contents** are held near zero and arrive late by blur; a **departing** payload leaves first. Gate: no component runs opacity and transform on one duration; `.glass-reveal`'s opacity leg goes to `calc(clock × 0.6)`.

**LAW 2 — DIRECTIONS ARE NOT REVERSES, AND EXITS EVAPORATE.** Fission fades-then-moves (~60ms opacity lead); fusion moves-then-fades (+150ms label lag) with a blur-resolve channel fission lacks. Expansion 620ms opaque; collapse 250ms translucent. Entries: opacity/focus completes before geometry. Exits: geometry completes before opacity—and the vaporize dialects may let blur lead the fade (stills §1.5). **EXIT = 0.6 × ENTRY** stands (floor 120ms, ceiling 250ms; measured instances 0.40/0.50—ADJ-6); attention objects earn a 4:1 entrance:exit; overshoot is reserved for arrivals and impacts (the landing bloom), never for dismissals or settles-under-finger. Every transition has a forward score and a separate backward score.

**LAW 3 — CONTAINERS OUTRUN CONTENTS, AND CONTENT-LAG IS A SPRING.** The panel lands 300ms before its artwork; the sheet's interior trails its shell by 22pt@400pt/s and 110pt@1570pt/s, recovering after the shell stops—a damped coupling, never a parent transform (`useNestedFlip`'s law, now measured). Chrome arrives first and content catches up—that is what makes an arrival read confident.

**LAW 4 — POSITIONAL OVERSHOOT CEILS AT 4.7%; EVERYTHING LARGER IS A DEFORM.** Six independent measurements (2.5/2.8/3.2/~4/~1/4.7%), nothing above 4.7% in 329 frames. Every larger number is a shape change: banner height ×1.19, lens width 1.35–1.6×, +10%w/−6%h squash-relax—universal, and it belongs in the spring layer as a volume-preserving shape term, not per-component opt-in. Exactly one rebounding row (`panel`), all playfulness in deform.

**LAW 5 — ANSWER BEFORE YOU MOVE.** Every engagement opens with a non-translating answer: the four-beat inhale (700ms before travel), the icon at 1.57× in 42ms, the whole bar's chroma lift one frame before the pill moves. Re-engage runs ~5× faster than cold engage—the material remembers. G1 ACKNOWLEDGE names the channel: never the travel.

**LAW 6 — ANISOTROPY ENCODES INTENT; THE DISCLOSING AXIS IS THE SLOW ONE (ADJ-3).** Nothing is a uniform scale. The axis that merely reaches its bounds runs fast on `dock`; the axis that discloses content runs slow on `panel` and carries the overshoot; acts are sequential and orthogonal. Which axis opens first is the phenomenon's signature.

**LAW 7 — RADIUS IS A ROLE, NEVER A PRODUCT OF SCALE; NESTING IS CONCENTRIC.** Invariant through 6× growth (46→55) and an 8× detent range (30pt); derived where the shape is a capsule (r=h/2 at every instant—the shape never changes category mid-flight); carried as a ratio through identity morphs with a ~+15% mid-flight swell; **outer r = inner r + inset**, twice independently within 1.3pt; children ≈0.6× their parent's radius—a role relation that keeps a nest legible; exactly two radius families. The one measured counter-example (the home tray, tighter than concentric) proves the exception must be a named role, never an accident.

**LAW 8 — THE DOUBLE-DOCK COMPOSITION LAW.** A dock is a STACK of separate glass bodies with load-bearing air—gap bracket 11–20pt, ~0.25–0.33 of the adjacent slab (ADJ-2), gated on legibility through it—each slab its own rim, its own cast, the upper's shadow falling on the lower. The stack is the rest state, born by Y-division, killed by Y-merge, transformed by **member-set diff**: the collapsed form is a first-class composition (controls shed by width, members absorbed, labels vanish whole), never a scaled clone.

**LAW 9 — THE CONTINUOUS-TIMELINE EXPANSION LAW.** A surface grown out of another surface is one object on one timeline, never a crossfade of surfaces: the origin's glass IS the destination's glass (and it **dilutes** when grasped); both text layouts draw simultaneously at two sizes for ~165ms and nothing ever squashes; only the participating element participates—neighbours never scatter, dim, or acknowledge; and its corollary is the orb's—**grow a body out of a fixed anchor hole, never scale a box**.
