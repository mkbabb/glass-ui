## 2. Reconciled verdict

All three critics independently reject the present structure as the target.
Their different emphases are compatible:

1. The topology critic proves duplicate semantic, geometry, time, measurement,
   clip and hit owners. Its one-shell candidate is the mandatory baseline.
2. The motion critic proves that first-open endpoint feedback, whole-root blur,
   center-only scaling, independent crossfade clocks and the stale CTA horizon
   deterministically produce the observed jitter, softness and ownership void.
3. The interaction critic proves that indiscriminate deletion would be unsafe:
   native scrolling, transactional activation, portal identity, focus/inert
   retirement, collision-free linkage and coarse hit ownership are real
   invariants even though their present implementation is incomplete.

The resolution is **not** “keep the machinery and tune it” and is **not**
“delete everything until the DOM is small.” The target is one semantic Dock
shell, one conventional content/native-scroll box, one logical state/activation
authority and one event-local motion generation. Optical material, the
expanded-only aperture and the selection lens may remain separate paint jobs,
but none may become a second hit, geometry, posture or time owner.

## 3. Parsimonious target architecture

```text
optional host/page adjacent frame (persistent placement only; non-interactive)
└── semantic Dock hit/layout root
    ├── non-interactive optical material plane
    ├── stable collapsed semantic controls
    ├── conventional content + native-scroll box
    │   └── selected expanded face / search face / reachable trailing controls
    └── optional selection lens paint carrier outside the body aperture
```

### 3.1 The semantic shell

The real root is the semantic, focus, hit and layout boundary. It owns
orientation, posture facts and the actual interactive region. It is never
scaled, counter-scaled, own-pixel blurred, or used as an animated offscreen
texture. Its descendants do not undo a parent transform.

The current negative-z `.dock-plate` is not a second event surface. Material
paint belongs on the root or one non-interactive pseudo/sibling plane. An
`aria-hidden` optical decoration cannot also be the pointer hit owner. The
current `display:contents` controls wrapper is flattened or becomes the one
conventional content box; it cannot remain a boxless behavior layer.

Persistent leading/trailing content survives only when it contributes real
layout or semantics. It participates in the same reachability and overflow
accounting as the rest of the controls. “Persistent” does not mean outside the
native scroll model.

### 3.2 One state and activation authority

One reducer-like authority owns collapsed/expanded/held/pinned/manual posture,
close eligibility and the current transition generation. Pointer, touch,
hover, focus, overlay membership and scroll domains report facts or intents;
they do not independently finish posture or mutate competing state.

Activation is transactional from the earliest action phase. It compares the
closest surviving interactive lineage, not raw descendant identity. It must:

- keep unsafe morph-time pointerdown-open receivers closed until accepted;
- permit a valid same-control first action at rest and after actionable visual
  stability, while continuing to suppress different-target retarget races;
- retire press keepalive after click, pointerup-without-click, pointercancel,
  lost capture, blur, timeout, replacement, unmount and stale generation;
- preserve keyboard, AT and no-pointer activation without allowing a
  programmatic click to impersonate pointer proof;
- report both inline and block touch displacement so a horizontal swipe cannot
  become a tap, and never strand a newly exposed native scroller behind
  `touch-action:none`.

Direct sibling `inert` mutation is not a required architecture. If it is
removed, the transactional replacement must retain every cross-target and
abandoned-press invariant.

### 3.3 One event-local motion generation

“One timeline” means one director, generation, monotonic progress and terminal
event for each semantic episode—not one global scalar for unrelated events.
Every subordinate carrier in one episode derives from that generation. Outer
posture, face visibility, aperture, opacity and any subordinate material cue do
not run independent schedulers when they are perceived as one action.

The present `--dock-morph-t`, overloaded `--dock-t`, directional
`--dock-expand-t`, live size scale, inverse child scale, reveal blur, crossfade
spring/peak and post-flag natural-layout handoff are rejected as coequal truths.
A direction-normalized value may exist only as a pure derivation. Logical
completion and stable visual geometry are the same terminal event.

Measurement may observe or parameterize a pre-stable frame only if cold-first
operation proves its endpoint before movement. It may not drive a
measurement→transform→remeasurement loop or a settle rebase. Ordinary layout is
the default geometry owner.

### 3.4 Natural origin and the two-dimensional aperture

The Dock must grow from the semantic source of the action. A visible trigger,
selected cell or anchored body supplies its natural origin; an unanchored
episode uses the center. Top, bottom, left, right, center and bounded logical
percentage coordinates remain the complete two-dimensional algebra across
orientation and LTR/RTL. A corner or edge is used only where the mounted
relationship makes it meaningful, never as an incidental transform default.

The earlier `top` compatibility default remains an explicit migration case,
not permission for universal top/corner growth. Any default change is resolved
in the execution contract/API-diff seat; this formation document deliberately
does not freeze a property or type spelling.

One same-generation **expanded-only** aperture derives from the origin and
motion progress. For normalized logical origin `(oi, ob)` and progress `r`:

```text
inline = [oi(1-r), oi + (1-oi)r]
block  = [ob(1-r), ob + (1-ob)r]
```

The collapsed semantic layer is stable, targetable and never clipped, scaled,
blurred, duplicated or made inert by the aperture. The aperture does not hard
switch custom endpoints and does not require child inverse transforms. The
selection lens, when present under existing GF-DOCK W4, remains outside the
body aperture so its bounded protrusion is not clipped; it still shares the
selection/event authority rather than becoming another shell.

### 3.5 Sharp material and compositing

Backdrop blur is an optical material operation. Text, glyphs, focus paint and
interactive own pixels remain native-DPR sharp at rest, onset, mid-flight,
reversal and settle. Computed `filter` on the semantic Dock root is always
`none`; removing root/reveal blur is expected-GREEN.

One optical plane owns background, rim, grain, backdrop filter and aperture
paint in an explicit cascade. Root/plate duplicate clips, negative-z hit paint,
theme-dependent blend accidents and multiple inherited material writers do not
survive by default. Adaptive luminance routes through existing
`R-COMPOSITED-SIGNAL`: one truthful ordered/already-composited source, singular
observer/readback ownership and the existing luma→tint response. This work does
not mint an opacity/attenuation axis.

Breath of Life is therefore legible causal movement in one crisp material
body—not whole-subtree blur, glare, or more layers. Movement of Momentum means
painted position and bounded velocity survive interruption/reversal, source and
destination ownership remain continuously readable, and slow arrivals seat
without a dead tail.

### 3.6 Face, search, CTA and overflow

Faces retain the minimum DOM needed for semantic selection, focus and ARIA
continuity. A dissolve is either a pure channel of the episode progress or is
removed in favor of a discrete accessible handoff. An independent crossfade
spring, registry peak, invisible busy tail and stale maximum height must earn
their return with a user-visible invariant; the current evidence does not.

Search is an owned face of the actual Dock state or a wholly external
composition through a complete public seam. It cannot deep-construct a shadow
state while the rendered Dock is pinned `always-expanded`. The story must mount
the claimed collapsed invocation, first action, reversal, passive-scroll and
PRM behavior.

CTA receipt has exactly one legible owner in every frame. Geometry arrival,
opacity, focus, status and semantic completion share one bounded lifecycle.
The obsolete ~1.92-second horizon, 0.35 target ghost, stale pending state and
reset/restart callbacks are rejected. CTA motion may remain a separate episode
from ordinary Dock posture, but it cannot have a second director during its own
handoff.

Native overflow is a retained semantic/accessibility owner. The true-mobile
Home→End result—Settings selected, focused, revealed and recentered at
`scrollLeft=321.5` in the `290/612` port—is GREEN and non-negotiable. Masks,
fit observers and detents are derived presentation. They may be removed or
changed only while native fine/coarse/keyboard reach, RTL/vertical behavior,
focus paint and intermediate-item access remain GREEN.

## 4. Mobile usability and safe-frame ruling

The present icon-only collapsed shell is not truthful primary navigation. A
mobile Dock must expose the current destination and adjacent action meaning
without hover-only tooltips, retain one named navigation landmark, maintain
logical focus/reading order, and provide reachable ≥44×44 coarse ownership for
every required compact action without overlapping transparent hit regions.
Compact paint may remain visually small; its hit owner may not.

The Glass Dock component converges on the one-shell topology above. A
**persistent mounting composition** may additionally require a host-owned
adjacent frame:

```text
viewport frame
├── min-block-size:0 content/scroll port
└── structurally adjacent Dock region
```

This is not a second Dock engine. It reserves no hit-stealing transparent
expanded footprint and does not animate the page when the Dock changes posture.
It exists only where `R-DOCK-SAFE-FRAME` proves persistent chrome must never
intersect active or focusable content.

No bottom posture is selected. The first actual-tree comparison keeps Atlas's
current vertical rail/crest→sheet behavior in an adjacent side slot. A bottom
row remains owner-authorized-only. Glass exports no frame until Atlas has one
injected ScrollRoot and the producer/consumer family can co-land. Padding,
scroll-margin or a fixed overlay cannot satisfy arbitrary-position
zero-intersection.

## 7. Consolidated detector law

The execution cut must carry one deduplicated detector suite. Every mutation
below must fail independently for its named reason; deletion trials must remain
GREEN without deleting the protected invariant.

### Required born-RED mutations

1. Restore cold-first settle rebase after nominal scalar completion.
2. Put any non-`none` own-pixel filter on the semantic Dock root.
3. Add a second live scheduler or terminal-opacity busy tail to one compound
   posture/face episode.
4. Hard-switch or center-scale a two-dimensional origin/aperture, or add child
   countertransforms.
5. Reverse twice from moving paint and lose position, bounded velocity, origin
   or endpoint identity.
6. Open a pointerdown receiver before an unsafe morph-time transaction accepts
   it.
7. Retarget A→B during motion, swallow a valid sibling/replaced-descendant
   action within A, or use programmatic click as pointer credit.
8. Abandon a press without click/cancel and leak keepalive, origin markers or
   inert state.
9. Collapse/switch/reverse/remove while focus is inside active content and land
   focus disconnected, hidden, inert, unnamed or in a stale generation.
10. Collide normalized/Unicode/duplicate layer IDs, remove the named tablist, or
    permit selection/visible face/ARIA linkage to disagree.
11. Restore compact 32px-only hit ownership: all four corners and four edge
    midpoints of the centered 44px envelope must belong only to the intended
    control.
12. Fire disabled pointer/Enter/Space/as-child/link action or default navigation;
    enabled action fires exactly once.
13. Treat large inline/small block touch movement as a tap, or block immediate
    native panning after collapsed touch expansion.
14. Remove native overflow/recenter, hide an intermediate item, or break
    horizontal/vertical, LTR/RTL, fine/coarse or focus-ring reach.
15. Confuse correct/wrong/stale owner portals across two Docks, or fail
    Escape/outside/focus return for the live generation.
16. Restore the ~1.92s CTA void, leave pending on reset, retain a stale callback,
    or allow zero/two legible action owners.
17. Restore `always-expanded` while claiming collapsed Search first-action
    coverage.
18. Remove the selected host safe frame or `min-block-size:0`, point one reader
    back to `window`, or allow any active/focusable content intersection.
19. Make icon-only/tooltip-only mobile navigation the sole current/destination
    cue, or expose zero/two category-navigation landmarks.
20. Create multiple luma writers, strip ordered source intent, step tint through
    the knee, or reuse an opacity knob as a cure.
21. Reuse `7.0.0`, a workspace link, stale served chunk, mismatched lock or
    substituted tarball.

## 9. Final formation disposition

The structural design has converged sufficiently for a bounded implementation
seat: **one semantic shell, one real native-scroll content box, one logical and
activation owner, one event-local motion generation, one non-interactive
material plane, one natural two-dimensional origin and expanded-only aperture,
and an optional host-owned adjacent frame only for persistent placement.**

Current Glass remains RED because none of those sentences is yet proven by one
immutable source→package→installed→served candidate and the complete
browser/AT/consumer matrix. Formation must not reopen architectural debate
unless new evidence identifies a previously unrepresented user invariant or
contradicts these exact bytes. Execution should now delete compensations,
preserve the enumerated invariants, and stop when a named gate fails—not add a
parallel mechanism.
