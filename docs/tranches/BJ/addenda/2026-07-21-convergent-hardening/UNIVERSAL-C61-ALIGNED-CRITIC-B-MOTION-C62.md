## 2. Binding amendments

### C62-M01 — ASK-27 remains a gate, including for retained renderers

The phrase “one renderer/lifecycle” cannot be read as ambient-motion authority.
Aurora, Blob, Constellation, FourierField, Skeleton, indeterminate Progress,
active StatusDot, Typewriter cursor, and WatercolorDot animate mode must each
prove one of two lawful outcomes: finite/state-bound settlement, or a visible
pause/stop/hide owner for movement or blinking that persists beyond five
seconds. Hidden, offscreen, unmounted, and PRM states must retire the clock and
stale generation; resume must continue from current retained state rather than
remounting or adding another clock.

Home's restoration of the existing Aurora is a receiver correction, not a
waiver. Home, its hero, and the Aurora route together may own one renderer,
context, lifecycle, and pause seam. Static comparisons remain zero-clock.

### C62-M02 — natural origin is a first-changing-frame fact

An endpoint plus prose does not prove natural origin. For every spatial event,
the receipt must retain the pre-event frame and measured source bounds, the
first changed frame, at least two non-degenerate mids, terminal, interruption,
reversal, cancel, and immediate next action. The first changed geometry must be
continuous with the pointer, thumb, selected item, trigger, Dock aperture, or
existing object. An already full-size translucent endpoint, arbitrary-corner
scale, or blur that hides a discontinuity fails even if the settle is correct.

This is load-bearing for Dialog, Command, DropdownMenu, Popover, Select,
Tooltip, Dock, Drawer, Tabs, Slider, Card condensation, Accordion/Collapsible,
Carousel/Deck, HandMark, and CompletionSeal.

### C62-M03 — one geometry authority also means one reveal channel

C61 correctly rejects layout properties as the animation engine, but
“current-value reveal” must not become permission for simultaneous
block-size/height, clip, scale, opacity, and text-metric trajectories. Accordion
and Collapsible share one disclosure substrate, remeasure content, and retarget
from the visible frame without clipping or ghosting text. Card condensation may
snap responsive padding/type metrics, while one compositor-owned shell channel
carries any warranted transition. Overlay content remains sharp for every
frame; scrim and panel clocks stay distinct but generation-coupled.

### C62-M04 — one earned chroma event is concurrent composition law

“One earned chroma event” means one attention-drawing accent event in the
current composition/state, not one reusable color token per component. Semantic
status colors may communicate state, but they may not become competing animated
gold/rainbow pulses. CompletionSeal's earned gold consumes the focal event
during completion. Dock, Tabs, Slider, SortableList, status feedback, and Home's
living field must remain subordinate when they coexist. Mutating in a second
decorative accent or infinite shimmer must fail the composition detector.

### C62-M05 — material proof must separate refraction from paint

Every Glass claim needs the same diagnostic underlay crossing both material and
adjacent non-material regions; light, dark, high-chroma, reduced-transparency,
and forced-color fallbacks; and lossless native DPR 1/2/3 crops in Chromium and
actual Safari. The comparison must show that the backdrop bends while semantic
copy, glyphs, focus rings, and edges remain invariantly sharp. Uniform cream,
opacity alone, CSS-sized JPEGs, mislabeled files, endpoint-only stills, or blur
of the component's own pixels receive no material credit. Surface owns the
canonical tier; nested consumers may use only the lightest sufficient tier.

### C62-M06 — PRM and static stillness need explicit clock-settlement receipts

PRM must render the same useful semantic result, not freeze an onset, suppress
content, or leave a procedural frame unsettled. The clock census must show zero
idle clocks for static families and zero surviving clock after terminal,
cancel, hidden, replacement, or unmount for finite families. Desktop and
390×844 coarse/no-hover mobile must reach the same action and terminal meaning
without fixed-Dock occlusion, clipped controls, or a desktop-only replay path.
This binds CompletionSeal's mobile story repair, HandMark's final ink with
unchanged real text, overlay focus/reveal, and every family row below.
