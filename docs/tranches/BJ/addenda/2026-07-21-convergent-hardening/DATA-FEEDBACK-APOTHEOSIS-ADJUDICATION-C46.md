### C. Breath of Life is semantic responsiveness, not an animation quota

Completion Seal earns a finite semantic construction. Sortable earns spatial
continuity during direct manipulation. Progress, Skeleton, Infinite Scroll, and
Dialog may animate only to explain live status or preserve context. Table,
DataTable, Search, Virtual Section, Metric, and other static readouts receive no
idle glow, shimmer, count-up, smooth-scroll, or decorative motion entitlement.

Natural origin and sharpness remain binding: centered Dialogs may grow from the
center, but their semantic foreground must not blur at an already-actionable
destination. Static data surfaces can satisfy Breath of Life through precise
material response, focus, state truth, and proportion without perpetual clocks.

are unreachable. Closure chooses and proves one honest responsive policy before
adding any transition. Header/cell association, focused-control reveal, state
identity, and valid AT structure survive every projection.
