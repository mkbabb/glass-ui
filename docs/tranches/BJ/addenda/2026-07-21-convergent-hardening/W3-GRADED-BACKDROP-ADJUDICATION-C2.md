## 2. Present truth re-derived from the pin

The current experiment is not box-following. `BACKDROPS = ["scrim", "graded"]` is a public axis.
`DialogContent` forwards it only for centred placement. `ModalOverlay` then removes the normal wash
blur and scrim class, adds `data-backdrop="graded"`, and mounts one full-viewport child. FORM 2 gives
that child fixed viewport-centred x/y ramps based on one `13rem` half-extent and `7rem` bloom, plus
`20px` blur and overlay saturation. No content rectangle reaches the overlay. The child is
`position:absolute; inset:0`.

That yields six statically provable failures:

1. A fixed square core cannot follow short, tall, narrow, and wide Dialog border boxes.
2. The filter element is viewport-sized; a visibility mask does not prove a bounded backing capture,
   blur-kernel allocation, or damage region.
3. The centred `.glass-floating` Dialog retains its independent backdrop filter, so the core ray has
   a halo sample and a body sample.
4. Graded gating removes both whole-field stage dim and fixed immersive sample. Sharp, undimmed far
   content remains visually competitive despite modal inertness.
5. Reduced transparency and forced colors hide the only graded child while the flat selectors remain
   excluded, producing a visual-barrier hole.
6. The tests prove strings, node count, and literal values. They do not prove geometry, pixels,
   stacking, accessibility fallback, browser parity, or cost.

The consumer count is one: the Dialog story can set `DialogContent stage="immersive"` with the
backdrop axis. `CommandDialog` forwards root `DialogProps` and `surface` only; it cannot request stage
or backdrop. Drawer has its own stage contract and never sets the marker. Popover does not consume the
axis. The source comment naming an ENGAGE-AFFORD second consumer is false at the pin.

The independently valid side-sheet edge is a different mechanism accidentally sharing the centre
slot name. A placed glass `DialogContent` mounts a stationary direct child beside the scroll region;
the host filter is disabled; the child owns one 34px×level edge sample and four placement directions.
That survivor is not evidence for the public centred backdrop axis.

## 3. Ranked reconciliation of the two critics

### 3.1 Critical — the owning paint gate cannot ratify ADOPT on absent paint

The W3 band requires the real graded/flat/F49-F50 comparison in current Safari and Chromium. The
design report expressly records zero live engine acceptance and no DesignSync surface. Formation can
reject the landed mechanism and can write research constraints, but it cannot convert an unbuilt
replacement into a public ADOPT ruling when the sole owning judgment gate requires pixels. The
absence of paint is not the only reason for DECLINE; it removes the only route by which H-C could
overcome the mechanism defects below.

### 3.2 Critical — H-C is a new mechanism, not bounded redress

The proposed focus apron requires content registration, border-box measurement, initial and resize
reads, viewport clamping, stale-generation control, an atomic cross-sibling state locus, host-filter
suppression, a new veil composition, nesting policy, and numeric compositor budgets. None exists at
the pin. That is a different overlay system, not a retune of 20px/13rem/7rem. The user's order was to
experiment and judge, not to grant every rejected experiment a second public implementation window.

### 3.3 Critical — W2 ownership and PRM semantics rule out the proposed alias

W2 owns a **private** stage-effect role: fixed 14px at level 1, multiplied by `--glass-level`, blur
only, independent of `--stage-t`; PRM resolves immersive to dim and removes the immersive marker and
fixed sample. W3 cannot simultaneously alias a public `--glass-halo-blur` to that private role and
allow its apron to remain under PRM. Copying 14px creates a second canon; promoting the private role
creates a new public primitive; keeping the apron makes it a distinct W3 role. DECLINE avoids all
three contradictions.

### 3.4 Critical — the x/y product is not rounded-surface-concentric

For `m(x,y)=s_x(x)s_y(y)`, two 0.5 axis ramps yield 0.25 at the diagonal. Equal-alpha contours are
product contours, not signed-distance offsets of a rounded rectangle. Pixels inside the rectangular
hold but outside the rounded Dialog corner remain at full mask. Setting clearance to
`var(--radius-dialog)` changes a distance value, not the topology. A true rounded-rectangle offset
would be another mask mechanism with its own engine proof.

### 3.5 Critical — radius and optical clearance are independent override semantics

`--radius-dialog` is public, not private, but that does not make it a valid owner for apron reach.
Corner silhouette and exterior material clearance are independently tunable concepts. Squaring or
enlarging a Dialog corner must not silently collapse or expand a focus margin. The numerical 16px
coincidence does not create semantic ownership. The halo cohort is also absent from the material
manifest at the pin, so ADOPT would owe a public-ledger decision beyond comments.

### 3.6 High — the global one-sample claim conflicts with nesting

For one settled Dialog, suppressing its host filter while one apron filters beneath it is
mechanically plausible. With nested Dialogs, the inner apron can sample a scene already containing the
outer apron. Two filters then lie on one visual ray unless opening the inner suppresses/freezes the
outer, a policy H-C never defines. The existing stage ownership test proves separately registered
scrims; it does not prove a no-double nested pixel contract.

### 3.7 High — the promised handoff lacks one state locus and a biting detector

`DialogOverlay` and `DialogContent` are portal siblings. H-C names no single owner whose one selector
can switch both the apron and body filter. Reversing two synchronous JavaScript assignments is not a
valid painted-frame mutation because the browser normally coalesces them; forcing a style read would
manufacture a failure. First mount, close/reopen, delayed portal registration, and nesting remain
unruled. The absolute “no zero or double frame” statement is therefore not an acceptance contract.

### 3.8 High — the near/far equation and readability relation are incomplete

If local ink alpha `a_n` composites over a far veil `a_f`, effective local ink is
`a_f + (1-a_f)a_n`. The proposed `a_n <= a_flat` does not bound that total. The Dialog plate then
attenuates the result again. Readability and substrate transmission must be measured on final pixels,
not inferred from child custom properties. The backdrop-root/compositing order also needs engine
pixels, not box geometry.

### 3.9 High — measurement and mobile geometry remain unresolved

`ResizeObserver` reports size, not arbitrary transform/position movement; entry scale/translate,
visual viewport/keyboard movement, and future source motion require other invalidation. Conversely,
animated content size can produce frame-by-frame observer writes. At 390px, a normal Dialog plus two
core+bloom margins cannot complete a 60–85px falloff on every edge. “Clamp to viewport” and “prove
all four falloffs” cannot both be universal requirements.

### 3.10 High — bounded CSS boxes do not establish bounded Safari cost

A smaller element is directionally preferable, but does not prove backdrop capture, kernel outset,
intermediate-surface allocation, promotion, or damage bounds. The design report supplies no numeric
area, frame, memory, or regression threshold. A browser kill switch without a threshold and without
a Safari trace cannot turn RED.

### 3.11 Accepted in principle, but not sufficient for ADOPT

The design critic is correct that a whole-field modal barrier must survive, mask geometry must remain
static, accessibility modes cannot erase modality, the side-sheet sampler must survive, and no new
consumer should be invented. Those principles are carried into row 93 and the strip contract below.
They do not rescue H-C.

### 3.12 Third-pass corrections to the adversarial report

The adversarial disposition is selected, with three corrections:

1. H-D is not “one flat sample” at the modal core. The settled W2 posture has one full-field stage
   scrim sample plus the ordinary Dialog or Drawer surface sample: **two intended visual bodies**.
   Row 93 must measure and state that truth, not claim one filter per pixel.
2. The modal scrim is not a decorative pointer-pass-through layer. It must remain the DialogOverlay /
   DismissableLayer barrier that blocks outside content and supports outside dismissal. The
   **sheet-edge sampler child** is the element that must stay `pointer-events:none`.
3. Missing evidence does not make every future gradient design impossible. It means this public
   experiment cannot be adopted now. A later gradient proposal would be a new design decision with a
   new mechanism and evidence; it is not a deferred W3 implementation arm.
