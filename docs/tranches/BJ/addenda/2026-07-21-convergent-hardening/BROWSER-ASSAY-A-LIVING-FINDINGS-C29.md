## Feedback / Alert — radius-role RED

Warning, and Info Alert plates with visibly tighter corners than the surrounding
Glass card language.

more generous/softer role. A distinct sharp status-box posture is rejected.

Route this through the existing radius-role/material owner. The later board must

- focus, destructive, warning, and informational variants do not alter geometry;

  relation;
- no receiver-side radius override, private selector, or copied Glass class is
  used to obtain the result.

## Motion origin and Dock — owner ruling

All animated elements must grow, reveal, collapse, and reverse from their
semantic centre or another physically/semantically natural anchor. Top-left or
top-right corner growth is RED unless that corner is the actual invocation,
attachment, or content origin and the relationship remains legible throughout
the motion.

The live 390×844 Dock assay is RED beyond ordinary polish:

- the persistent mobile dock is nearly unusable as navigation because it
  presents an icon/control strip without sufficient orientation or destination
  legibility;
- it occludes active specimen/content regions;
- the compact→expanded transition passes through multiple visibly blurred
  intermediary frames;
- geometry jitters rather than carrying continuous momentum;
- the first compact action also changes the nominally independent open specimen,
  so the spatial narrative is not locally causal;
- the transition therefore lacks both BREATH OF LIFE and MOVEMENT OF MOMENTUM.

Required later proof includes rest/onset/mid/settle/reversal frames at native
DPR, invariant or intentionally evolving pivot, swept-bounds continuity, no
unrelated layout displacement, stable raster sharpness, first-action integrity,
same- and cross-target behavior, touch/keyboard/focus parity, and PRM terminal
parity. A short duration, opacity mask, or final-frame-only screenshot cannot
close this finding.
