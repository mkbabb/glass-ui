## Sol rulings for the open design seams

### 1. The corrected nesting law and F12

Concentric subtraction applies when two painted surfaces share a boundary relationship: an inner
plate follows an outer corner across a known inset. A discrete child control floating inside a padded
field/card is not that case. It may keep its own role silhouette when all of the following are true:

- its painted and hit boxes are strictly inset from the container's painted boundary;
- it neither clips nor creates a tangent/corner collision at rest, focus, removal, wrap, or coarse
  touch size;
- the outer surface remains the field/container and the child remains the control/tag; and
- no ancestor overflow silently shears the child's focus ring, remove action, or hit floor.

Thus F12's `--radius-field` TagsInput containing pill `Chip` children is the intended role
composition. W1 corrects the false prose and proves the geometry; it does not flatten the field into
a pill or square the chips. A mutation that makes the outer TagsInput a pill, hand-rolls the chip
radius, removes the required inset/gap, clips the focus/remove affordance, or lets the coarse child
touch the outer corner must RED.

### 2. Command owns no inner radius today

Delete `border-radius` from `.command__input`. The Command panel already owns
`--radius-panel` and clips the composition; the input wrapper owns the divider. The transparent input
has no independent plate or clip and therefore no corner role. If a future change gives that input a
painted plate, that change must choose an explicit field/control role then; it may not inherit the
Avatar/Skeleton media rung by accident.

### 3. Skeleton keeps a default without defeating its public shape seam

Keep `--radius-media` as Skeleton's default, but place the default radius declaration in
`@layer components` while leaving the public class merge intact. Tailwind's utilities layer must then
win for `rounded-full`, `rounded-card`, or another public role utility. Do not add a `shape` prop, an
`!important`, a private selector, or a consumer custom-property shim for this repair.

On the built `/feedback/skeleton` receiver, the default/text specimen resolves through
`--radius-media`, the 48px avatar specimen computes a circle, and the card specimen computes
`--radius-card`. Restoring an unlayered hard owner must fail all applicable computed-style checks.

### 4. `OPEN-1c` — SegmentedTabs resolves through its existing contextual radius

Replace both raw tab-button radii with `var(--bouncy-slider-radius)`:

- horizontal pill and horizontal underline resolve through `--radius-tab` (the control stadium);
- vertical pill and vertical underline inherit the existing parent rebind to `--radius-strip` (the
  bounded column-stack corner); and
- the default-pill button/hit/focus silhouette stays co-geometric with the measured indicator instead
  of introducing a second role decision.

This uses the component's existing orientation-aware substitution seam. Do not hardcode one global
`--radius-control`, force a stadium onto the tall vertical stack, or mint an underline-only radius.
Restoring either `0.3125rem` or `0.25rem`, or breaking horizontal/vertical resolution, must RED the
ordinary gate and the corresponding real receiver.

### 5. F17 — `floating` owns rounded glass; only `bare` is chromeless

Keep the public `floating` variant, but stop it from stripping the `.input-bar` plate. Bind it to the
same complete component-owned glass chrome as the inline field; `bare` remains the sole explicit
parent-owned/chromeless variant. Repoint `.input-bar` itself from the primitive
`--radius-2xl` to the semantic `--radius-control`, so inline and floating single-line search fields
are true control pills across `sm`/`md`/`lg` rather than merely “not square.”

This is deliberately one recipe, not floating-only special chrome. It preserves the public variant
without adding another surface axis: `surface` still chooses glass/veil/opaque, `variant` chooses
component-owned plate versus bare parent ownership. A mutation restoring `floating`'s
`rounded-none`, transparent background, zero padding, or border stripping must RED; a mutation that
also gives `bare` a component-owned plate must RED the distinct bare fixture.
