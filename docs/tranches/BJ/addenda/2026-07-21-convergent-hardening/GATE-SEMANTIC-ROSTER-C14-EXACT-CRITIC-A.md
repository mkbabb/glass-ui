## RED-1 — Chip does not freeze the click trigger surface

C14 correctly repairs every C13 Chip defect it names. At
`GATE-SEMANTIC-ROSTER-C14.json:762-790`, the fixture now supplies hostile
`role=button`, `tabindex=0`, `aria-pressed=true`,
`data-state=caller-state` and a click spy. It proves selectable pressed/on,
action without pressed/state, and a final static root `SPAN` without the listed
host attributes or a descendant Button. The predicate no longer overclaims
universal non-focusability or every activation event; `contenteditable` and
unlisted event semantics are expressly outside the seat.

The click boundary remains under-specified. The predicate says there is no
consumer click listener, and mutation nine says to forward a consumer click
listener into static mode. The mechanism requires only no consumer click
delivery "when triggered." It never says to trigger the static root specifically,
to trigger a named inert child, or to exercise every rendered descendant.

The following product mutation therefore passes a natural exact implementation
of every listed assertion:

```vue
<span data-mode="static">
    <span data-chip-content @click="attrs.onClick"><slot /></span>
</span>
```

The root remains a `SPAN`; it has no role, tabindex, `aria-pressed` or
`data-state`; it contains no Button; and dispatching a click on that root does
not invoke a listener attached to its child. The hostile consumer spy fires when
the child is clicked, however. The listener has been forwarded into static mode,
contradicting both the unqualified predicate and the exact mutation at line 788.

The `nodeIdentityLaw` does not resolve this. It lists the settled host and the
descendant-Button boundary separately, then refers to the click-listener boundary
"above" without assigning the listener exclusively to the host. Even if the
predicate were intended to be host-only, neither the predicate, mechanism nor
mutation says that; a future implementer cannot derive which trigger satisfies
the frozen law without choosing an interpretation.

C15 must make one of two same-seat choices:

- narrow the predicate and mutation to **no hostile consumer click delivery
  from the static host**, and require triggering that captured root `SPAN`; or
- preserve the no-listener-anywhere claim, mount an inert named descendant, and
  require the spy to remain silent after clicks dispatched on the root and every
  rendered descendant (including the named child), with a planted descendant-
  forwarding mutation.

The first option matches the otherwise host-bounded C14 design. It need not add
a seat or promise anything about click handlers authored inside consumer slot
content; the fixture's slot can remain inert so only the hostile component attr
is under test.
