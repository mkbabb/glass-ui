## Scope and evidence law

This packet freezes the already-retained, manually navigated Browser Assay B
frames for the four remaining Feedback stories and all ten Data stories. It
does not substitute one run for the required independent Assay A, and it does
not infer interaction, reduced-motion, Safari, or VoiceOver truth from a rest
frame. The live demo was exercised at desktop `1280×720` / DPR 1 / fine pointer
and true mobile `390×844` / DPR 3 / coarse pointer / no hover. The frames are
formation inputs, not immutable source→pack→install→served proof.

The fixed demo Dock appears over the lower part of many mobile and desktop
frames. That is retained as an exact GF-DOCK receiver witness and is not charged
to the component under assay. Conversely, zero document overflow is not
accepted as proof that a data control, row, column, status, or focus target is
reachable.

- **Progress:** the determinate, arbitrary-domain, RTL, indeterminate, size,
  complete, and error specimens fit the viewport and retain a quiet, readable
  material hierarchy. This rest-only pass proves neither the indeterminate
  clock nor its PRM terminal state, fractional/DPR mark alignment, focus, or
  forced-colors behavior. It stays under `BJ.W-TRACK-DRY` /
  `R-TRACK-PUBLIC-BREAK`; it does not authorize Timeline adoption, a third
  track paint axis, or a new Progress primitive.
- **Skeleton:** text, avatar, and card placeholders remain legible and usefully
  subordinate on both widths. No natural loading replacement, caller shape
  override, shimmer clock, or PRM sequence was exercised. It remains a natural
  receiver for `BJ.W-RADIUS-ROLE` / `R-RADIUS-PUBLIC-BREAK`, especially the
  layered `--radius-media` and caller-override law. Static rest cannot certify
  that cascade.
- **Confirm Dialog:** the composition correctly distinguishes destructive and
  benign intent and uses full-width mobile actions. At click-0, mobile content
  is visibly doubled/blurred while already near destination geometry; by
  400ms it is stable. Desktop likewise begins very near its terminal rectangle.
  The sparse sequence therefore keeps the existing Dialog/overlay and
  `BJ.W-FEEDBACK-MOTION-TUNE` owners RED for natural-origin continuity,
  interruption/reversal, focus entry, destructive default, Escape/outside
  dismissal, busy state, focus return, PRM, and AT naming. It does not justify
  another Dialog engine.
- **Completion Seal:** this is the positive Breath-of-Life reference in this
  cohort. Disc, check, ring, wordmark, and earned-gold variants visibly draw
  through distinct 0/100/400/900ms states and settle into a clear semantic
  result without inventing a new hue. The sequence is not yet replay,
  interruption, reversal, unmount, scroll-clock, or PRM proof. Its independent
  keyframe fold remains within `BJ.W-REDUCE-CROSSREPO-GATED`; `R-TABS-ADORN`
  applies only when the seal is the decorative active Tabs adornment and must
  not globally strip the component's independent label/status contract.

## Three born-RED critic arms

### 1. Motion, material, and Breath of Life

Assume every clock is wrong. Replay Confirm Dialog and Completion Seal through
onset/mid/settle, interruption, cancellation, reversal, rapid re-entry,
unmount, theme, 30/60/120/240Hz, PRM, and native-DPR raster checkpoints.
Indeterminate Progress and any Skeleton loading motion must terminate under PRM
without the global fallback inventing a clock. The detector must RED if Dialog
appears at destination then blurs/snaps, if Completion Seal pops or retains a
stale playing generation, or if a static data surface receives decorative idle
motion. Dock blur/occlusion remains a separately named GF-DOCK result.

### 2. Responsive interaction, reach, and accessibility

Assume every rest frame hides an unreachable state. At desktop and true mobile,
exercise Table/DataTable reflow and all columns; TagsInput add/paste/delete;
Sortable pointer+keyboard reorder; Infinite Scroll loading/error/exhaustion and
announcement coalescing; Timeline scrub/selection; Fuzzy query/ranking/count;
Virtual Section far jump/reverse/focus reveal; Metric optional action; Chassis
state/action order; Confirm Dialog focus/dismiss/return; and Progress controls.
Mutations that restore one-letter status pills, clip required DataTable columns,
lose the focused/selected row, duplicate a live announcement, or let the fixed
Dock own a component target must turn this arm RED. Chromium is detector
coverage; actual Safari/VoiceOver remains mandatory and currently missing.

### 3. Systems, colocation, package, and lifecycle

Assume each green story is source-only or story-shimmed. Reconcile each
component with its colocated composables, styles, tests, exports, story, package
artifact, and real consumers. For DataTable, reorder, same-key replacement,
handler replacement, add/remove, and mutation-restored inline closures must
prove stable row-ref identity and correct old-null/new-element handoff. For
Progress/Skeleton/TagsInput, both public style entries and the installed package
must preserve the adjudicated track/radius/material owners without a copied
consumer rule. For Metric/Chassis/Fuzzy/Virtual Section, owner disposition and
consumer census must precede retention, thinning, migration, or public export.
Any untracked story-only fix, internal selector, duplicated primitive, mutable
`7.0.0`, or source-substitution proof fails closed.
