job is not to defend C65; it is to verify whether C65 still survives all raw

- the underlying defect mechanism, not just the visible symptom;
- owner classification: Glass producer, demo-private, consumer, package,
  browser/AT, or owner-held product decision;

  rejection;

- dependency order and the earliest execution phase from C66;
- whether the finding changes C61/C63/C65 or only reinforces an existing row.

- one passing unit suite as gestalt proof;

- two readings of the same browser run as two independent assays;
- a private consumer selector framed as a Glass producer need;
- elapsed time as convergence or non-convergence evidence;
- a preferred implementation spelling that preserves the frozen semantic law.
