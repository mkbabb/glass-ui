## 2. The two edicts, made falsifiable

**Breath of Life** means the component acknowledges cause, carries state through motion, and settles
with readable material depth. It does **not** require autonomous movement at rest. A static component
can be alive when focus, touch, drag, scroll and commit each receive a proportionate response.

**Movement of Momentum** means velocity, direction and source geometry survive handoffs. Motion is not
merely “springy”: each event has one director and one dominant semantic transformation; every
subordinate carrier names its source/parent and shares the event scalar; interruption re-seeds both
position and bounded velocity from the painted state; and removal may exhale rather than reverse when
reversal would lie. Channel order is event-specific—geometry, content and medium do not have one
universal leader.

The shared rejection set is: teleport, fade-only substitution for a spatial relationship, identical
clocks on every channel, intrinsic bounce with no incoming energy, animated **mask/backdrop-material**
blur radius, unbounded backdrop area, hidden state exposed only by travel, perpetual motion with no
pause, and browser-specific glass appearance. A bounded own-pixel `filter:blur()` decongest path is
adjudicated separately and is not prohibited by that material-sampling rule.

## 3. Sol brainstorm — union candidates

### H1 — Source-continuous window

Music establishes two distinct source pairs, not a filmed three-hop chain: library cover cell ↔ album
window, and mini artwork ↔ full player. Route preview ↔ story header is a third, product-specific pair.
The useful novelty is not “zoom” or image resemblance: each event preserves its own source key and
carrier, with an explicitly allowed early sourceward dissolve for the album-window exit. This
**refines** `V-TIMELINE`, `STORY W7`, and `R-ROUTE-DIRECTOR`; it does not mint another router. Each pair
proves interruption, back navigation where applicable, direct-load truth and PRM parity separately.

### H2 — Dock constellation

The Music dock is not a monolith. A now-playing body and navigation body stack at quiescence, condense
under scroll, then distribute left utility / centre identity / right search without losing group
identity. This **refines** `V-CONST`, `D-POSTURE`, and GF-DOCK W0/W4/W7/W8/W9. Fission is a semantic
layout/posture facility, not the retired spectacle slot: bodies remain separately named, focus order is
stable, and no orientation or width is privileged. GF-DOCK W7 remains PARKED on ASK-14; this vocabulary
does not unpark the fission/inter-body-goo fork. The unresolved Atlas phone posture remains an owner
decision; the exemplar cannot authorize a bottom bar by resemblance.

The supplied July-18 Music clip freshly films trio→stack fusion, not the reverse scroll-collapse;
stack→trio remains prior-corpus support and must retain that evidence grade.

### H3 — Attention lens, not luminous trough

ChatGPT's control uses a diffuse gradient-blur field to recruit attention around engagement. The two
stills show a **discrete four-step effort selector**, not continuous Slider or drag evidence; only the
attention field routes to `X-GRADBLUR`/`V-ALENS`, while the fill-plus-dots geometry stays with an

The halo is secondary focus, never value encoding. It follows hover/focus/touch/drag state, parks when
disengaged, preserves ≥4.5:1 label/value contrast, and uses a bounded masked stack with fixed blur
radii. The broad white fill is not Glass's material target.

The moving recording separately preserves `V-MORPHDOCK`: one composer body and label carrier become

### H4 — Frost is transmitted context

F1 remains the comparative material referent. F4 improves geometry but is too bright, shiny and
trite; F5's tabs-toggle and the comparable slider read like opaque plastic. The cure is not less
brightness alone: the plate needs contextual transmission, soft defocused structure, a quiet warm

MATERIAL W1/W2/W7/W8 and the Optical Bench judge. One component recipe runs on Safari and Chromium;
capability differences may degrade refraction to honest blur, never switch design languages.

### H5 — Contextual growth has separate event arms

Contextual Search and Siri retain a source edge but do not share one order or preset. Search owns a
gesture-tracked medium/recession arm, a separate commit-furniture arm, and a veil→condense→grow results
arm. Siri owns a fired island→panel arm: geometry establishes the body, then text, later data, and
finally rim response. They may share `V-BLACKDOCK`/`X-CHOREO` facilities and a frequency family; a
mutation routing both through one preset/order must fail. Gesture-scrubbed excursion remains
velocity-bought, while a fired panel may carry one bounded intrinsic terminal spring.

### H6 — Popover exhale

The Photos popover charges the pressed source, unfurls nearby with one shared terminal spring, and is
born thin enough for backdrop color to effervesce before settling. Its channels are deliberately
detuned: content is fastest, scrim intermediate, geometry longest. Dismissal is opacity-dominant with a
mild sourceward shrink. This **refines** existing overlay motion and `X-CHOREO`; it is an explicit
counterexample to a blanket ban on opacity exits, not permission for fade-only entry.

### H7 — Listening field and dot relay

Siri's waveform is a surface-scale aurora field rather than a trite bar rack. Gemini's dot matrix

bounded field; state transitions seed from the painted palette; idle is static, ends within five
seconds, or exposes pause/stop/hide. PRM commits state without travel.

### H8 — Perched control to vapor exit

The corner-perched notification control remains a separate body outside the card clip with an honest
44px hit region around a smaller optic. Owner-confirmed G-CLOSE authority still makes
`V-PERCH-PRIMITIVE` the one root close-affordance contract across the named dismissible-surface family;

## 4. Prototype questions

The external lab must test decisions, not imitate screenshots:

1. **Material:** compare a contextual warm-frost arm against bright-shiny and opaque-plastic
   falsifiers using the same substrate. Does the selected state remain legible without becoming a
   white lozenge?
2. **Continuity:** can one named identity move compact→constellation→expanded without a dissolve or
   competing protagonist, and reverse from the painted state?
3. **Momentum:** do geometry, content and atmosphere have distinct but coordinated clocks; does an
   interrupted reversal begin where the eye currently sees the object?
4. **Input:** are hover, keyboard focus, coarse press, drag cancel and commit equivalent in state,
   with touch allowed a stronger but not different material response?
5. **Restraint:** does every automatic sequence finish; does PRM preserve source/destination,
   selection, focus and announcement without travel?

No score invented by the lab can GREEN a row. It may falsify a treatment, establish vocabulary and
produce critic targets; production GREEN still requires the real component and enclosing-route matrix.
The revised lab is explicitly a material/state formation instrument. It does not prove source-pair
identity, velocity continuity, production multi-body conservation, route cancellation or paint budget.
