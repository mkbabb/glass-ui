# Dock Structural Simplification Workflow — C30

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Phase:** formation / audit / prototype only  
**Authority:** owner addition to the convergence criterion

The entire Dock architecture is subject to a dedicated convergence workflow.
Its current layering, compositing, state/context topology, and component
structure are presumptively contrived until each layer proves a necessary job.
The desired result is a dramatically simpler, idiomatic Glass primitive without
losing orientation, fission/fusion, overflow, layered panes, search, disclosure,
or accessible interaction.

## Workflow

1. **Live anatomy assays A and B**
   - horizontal, vertical, compact, open, search, layered, overflow, CTA handoff,
     persistent/mobile, portal, and receiver postures;
   - rest, onset, mid, settle, interruption, reversal, first action, and PRM;
   - native-DPR raster, swept geometry, focus, keyboard, coarse touch, AT,
     collision, and occlusion evidence.
2. **Lossless structural map**
   - public components and slots;
   - composables, contexts, registries, gesture/click-integrity guards;
   - DOM layers, portals, stacking contexts, pseudo-elements, backdrop filters,
     masks, transforms, inverse transforms, and canvas/source sampling;
   - demo-only versus package/runtime responsibility;
   - Atlas/SCI/value/keyframes receiver edges.
3. **Necessity challenge**
   - every layer/state owner must name the invariant it protects and a born-RED
     mutation that fails when removed;
   - duplicate geometry, animation, selection, disclosure, and focus owners are
     RED;
   - component/layer count is not authority. KISS and clean-break laws govern.
4. **Simplification prototypes**
   - test the smallest topology that preserves the surviving invariants;
   - compare DOM/layer/state ownership, not only final pixels;
   - preserve one motion timeline and one spatial origin/aperture model;
   - no receiver shim, private selector, copied Glass effect, or route-local fork.
5. **Three independent failure-assuming Sol x-high challenges**
   - systems/topology and colocation;
   - motion/compositing/raster/performance;
   - interaction/accessibility/consumer/package ownership.
6. **Separate adjudication**
   - agglomerate into existing Dock/GF-DOCK/COLO/A11Y/MOTION owners unless those
     owners are proven insufficient;
   - freeze the dependency-ordered Glass-root execution handoff.

## Current live falsifiers

- mobile icon-only navigation lacks sufficient destination/orientation legibility;
- the persistent dock occludes live story and control regions;
- Dock Sections clips a control at the right edge;
- compact/open specimens change together, undermining local causal ownership;
- CTA handoff spends a long interval with neither source nor destination action
  legible, then completes after roughly two seconds;
- compact/open morphs exhibit blurry and jittering intermediary frames;
- global dock composition interferes with the very overlay/menu/control stories
  it is meant to help inspect.

The workflow is additive to the whole-component apotheosis. It does not authorize
product/source/test/gate/package/lock/repin or acceptance execution.
