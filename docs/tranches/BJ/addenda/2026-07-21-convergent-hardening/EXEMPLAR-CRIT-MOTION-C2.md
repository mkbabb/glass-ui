candidate wants to adopt.

## 6. Interruption, reversal, and real momentum

The 07-17 control source supports three useful controls:

- Find My rapid reverse taps re-seat the traveling lens from its current painted body without a blink;
- CC can be caught mid-dismissal, with the medium held rather than reset;

Those are stronger than “springy.” A gate that checks only rect continuity can still pass a motion
that restarts a cubic-bezier at zero velocity and visibly kinks.

The lab exhibits exactly that weakness:

- one 420ms `cubic-bezier(.22,.86,.2,1.08)` is reused for dock inset/size, identity size/transform,
  cover size/radius, page transform, and effort transform;
- the sequence is three timer-set states 850ms apart;
- there is no pointer displacement, velocity sample, release projection, detent, or catch state;
- an interrupted CSS transition begins a new easing segment; the artifact may preserve current pixels,
  but it does not preserve incoming velocity;
- discrete `display`, grid-area, and position changes create unmeasured snaps around the continuous
  properties.

The lab's small overshoot is therefore decorative easing, not proof of Movement of Momentum. Its
“Play sequence” can establish timing vocabulary only.
