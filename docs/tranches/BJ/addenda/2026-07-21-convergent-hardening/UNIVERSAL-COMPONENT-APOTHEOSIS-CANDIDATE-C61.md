## 3. Normative design and engineering laws

### 3.1 Goal of Glass

Glass is warm scientific paper plus transmissive material over a meaningful
living field. It is not shiny acrylic, a white opacity slab, chrome-specific
behavior, or glass-on-glass ornament. Each story/receiver declares:

1. subject thesis and job;
2. meaningful underlay;
3. one earned chroma event;
4. display/body/data type roles and marker meaning;
5. one orchestrated engagement/hover/scroll moment where useful;
6. the lightest sufficient Glass tier;
7. desktop/mobile order, reach, and minimum safe gutter; and
8. a strong reference plus an explicit void/failure state.

### 3.2 Breath of Life and Movement of Momentum

- Static primitives stay still. Breath is engagement-responsive life, not an
  ambient clock quota.
- One spatial event has one geometry authority and one trajectory.
- Motion begins at the natural source: pointer, thumb, selected item, trigger,
  dock aperture, or the existing object. Arbitrary corner growth is rejected.
- Retargeting reads the current frame. Cancellation is not release and buys no
  velocity destination.
- Overshoot is velocity-earned; exits do not overshoot.
- Independent bodies overlap hard. Goo belongs only to one body/silhouette.
- Glass bends the backdrop. It never blurs semantic foreground, glyph, focus
  ring, or text to impersonate depth.
- PRM renders the same useful terminal semantic state with reduced travel; it
  does not globally pause into an incomplete frame.
- Geometry/layout properties do not become the animation engine when transform,
  clip, or a direct tracked owner can preserve layout and raster truth.

### 3.3 Colocation and idiomatic consumption

- Components own their composables, styles, types, tests, stories, and export
  declarations at the nearest coherent family boundary.
- Contract tests exercise the defining mechanism; Browser tests exercise the
  natural rendered state; package tests install the artifact; consumer tests
  use public props, slots, tokens, and documented compositions.
- External receivers consume Glass outright. Private selectors, copied Glass
  classes/effects, radius/padding/motion skins, and parallel implementations are
  presumptively RED.
- An exception requires a named missing producer contract, exact receiver,
  born-RED proof, owner, and deletion edge. No compatibility alias is added to
  normalize stale private reaches.
- Example fixtures may be colocated with their owning story only outside the
  flat story glob, with rendered/raw identity and route/module/bundle exclusion.
- Alert and Badge import their defining variant leaf, not their own barrel.
  Larger SCCs stay with their existing graph owners.

### 3.4 Accessibility and interaction floor

- Pointer, primary coarse touch, Enter, and Space activate exactly once where
  the semantic control supports them.
- Arrow/Home/End ownership never steals keys from a focused descendant.
- Required coarse actions own at least a non-overlapping 44×44 hit envelope;
  center, four corners, and four edge midpoints resolve to the intended action.
- Focus remains visible, reachable, and logically restored across responsive
  replacement, dynamic removal, portals, fullscreen, and open-unmount.
- Topmost overlay, Escape, outside action, body lock, inertness, focus return,
  and announcement generation share one current owner.
- Semantic name, role, value, selected/pressed/disabled/current state, IDREF,
  reading order, and live announcement are measured outcomes, not inferred from
  source prose.
