## Package-wide subtraction test

The same assay applies to every potentially overfit item in the tranche. A
component or helper must prove all of the following to survive compaction:

1. one distinct, reusable semantic job that is not already owned by a simpler
   public primitive or documented composition;
2. a stable state and interaction boundary rather than demo-specific prose,
   fixture data, layout, or choreography encoded as API;
3. at least one real reusable package need, or an owner-ratified foundational
   role whose value does not depend on a single story;
4. lower total complexity than composition from existing primitives, including
   styles, clocks, exports, tests, documentation, package surface, and consumer
   migration cost;
5. colocated implementation and tests that exercise the same public contract;
   and
6. no parallel renderer, copied Glass effect, private-selector dependency,
   compatibility alias without an extant consumer, or second state authority.

Failure is presumptively a **fold, inline, or remove** disposition. The critic
must name the surviving owner and the atomic deletion/migration boundary; it may
not mint a replacement abstraction merely to preserve current structure.

Potential overfit is not enough to delete an item during formation. Every
additional subtraction candidate needs exact source/export/story/consumer
evidence and independent adjudication so useful low-level primitives are not
mistaken for showcase wrappers.
