## Verdict

**RED.** C32 is credible as a detector packet, and its B1/B3/B6 facts survive
this challenge. The current implementation does not satisfy C30's one motion
timeline, one spatial origin/aperture model, sharp-descendant, native-DPR,
interruption or paint/performance convergence requirements. The source also
contains mechanisms that make three observed failures deterministic rather than
subjective: the first-open endpoint feedback loop, the whole-root filter, and the
CTA's stale 1.92-second horizon.

The minimum viable adjudication is not “retune the spring.” Ownership must be
made singular before tuning has meaning.

## Failure-assuming findings

### M33-1 — whole-root own-pixel blur is a direct native-sharpness and compositing violation

`src/components/dock/styles/morph.css:76-94` applies `filter: blur(...)` to
`.glass-dock[data-morphing]`, not to the optical plate. The affected subtree
includes glyphs, labels, focus paint, the negative-z backdrop plate and every
interactive descendant. `src/components/dock/styles/shape.css:126-160` then
scales that same root and counter-scales the content, with `will-change` on both
root and descendants. This is an offscreen-raster/compositing stack, not a
native-DPR-sharp content path.

The direction is also discontinuous. On collapse, `--dock-expand-t` travels
1→0 (`morph.css:62-64`), so the blur grows toward 1.25px at the tail, then drops
to zero when `data-morphing` is removed. The “front-loaded” description is true
only on expansion. The collapse path deliberately creates a settle sharpness
pop.

This confirms C31's binding correction and the A first-action/settle comparison.
The blur must be definition-absent from the root. Backdrop blur on
`.dock-plate` remains a separate, legitimate material job.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-ROOT-FILTER`:** any non-`none` computed `filter` on
`.glass-dock` during rest/onset/mid/reverse/settle must fail; descendants must
remain 1:1 sharp while plate-only material/tint/aperture may change. The current
baseline already instantiates this mutation.

### M33-2 — the endpoint measurement is causally circular and the settle rebase is structural

`dockMorphMeasure.ts:31-64` refuses capture during `data-morphing`; when mounted
collapsed it knows the collapsed root but derives the unknown expanded endpoint
from the inactive full pane. That pane is absolute/inset under the current
collapsed composition (`layers.css:214-220`), so its box can reflect the current
root rather than the future intrinsic terminal. During the episode,
`layers.css:102-107` reserves whatever `--dock-expanded-px` was derived. At
settle, `useDockMorph.ts:49-57` removes the attribute and inline scalar; natural
class geometry becomes a new authority and the ResizeObserver learns the true
endpoint only afterward.

C32 B1 is the exact visible result: desktop 118→221px and mobile 138→252px when
the morph flag clears even though the scalar was already analytically settled.
This is not spring tuning. It is an endpoint-known-too-late feedback loop and a
settle-time authority transfer.

`useDockMorph.ts:73-86` does contain a useful reversal seed—complement the live
scalar and invert inherited velocity—but continuity of a scalar cannot rescue a
box whose endpoint changes when the episode ends.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-FIRST-OPEN`:** mount collapsed with no previously
captured expanded root, toggle once, and require onset/mid/last-spring/first-rest
rect continuity within a native-device-pixel tolerance. The current baseline
must fail on the witnessed 118→221 / 138→252 rebases. A warm second toggle is not
an acceptable substitute.

### M33-3 — “one aperture” is currently a no-op plate plus a center-only root scale

The claimed plate aperture is not authoritative:

- `dock.css:89-96` aliases `--dock-t` but both collapse inset defaults are `0px`,
  making the plate clip `inset(0)`;
- `shell.css:215-228` leaves root overflow visible;
- `layers.css:78-107` and `shape.css:126-160` therefore create visible extent by
  center-scaling the whole root, reserving the expanded box, and inverse-scaling
  its content.

There is no semantic origin surface. `layers.css:92` hard-codes
`transform-origin: center center`, and the inverse children assume the same
centroid (`shape.css:145-160`). Edge, vertical-edge and bounded custom origins
cannot be represented.

The advertised arbitrary silhouette interpolation is also contradicted by its
implementation. `shape.css:93-110` selects `from` on a generic morphing root and
immediately overrides it with `to` whenever the synchronously flipped class is
`expanded`; no scalar appears in those declarations. Different custom clip
endpoints therefore switch discretely rather than lerp.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-2D-ORIGIN`:** provide unequal compatible
horizontal+vertical clip endpoints and each of centre, leading edge, trailing
edge and bounded custom origins; expand, reverse twice before settle, and require
one continuous aperture with no child inverse transform and no endpoint switch.
The current API cannot express the origin cases and the custom clip path hard
switches.

### M33-4 — “one spring” is a factory, not one live timeline

`useDockSpring.ts` is the sole constructor site, but it is not a singleton motion
owner. `GlassDock.vue:173-187` creates an outer morph handle, and every
`DockCrossfade.vue:110-118` creates another handle. A nested face swap and outer
collapse can therefore run distinct `SpringProgress` instances with separate
start times, settle callbacks, attributes and scalars (`--dock-morph-t` on the
root, per-face `--dock-t` inside the crossfade).

C32 B2 shows the weaker single-episode symptom: both face opacities were already
terminal at first observation while `data-crossfading` remained armed through
+100ms and cleared near +220ms. Equal response/damping does not make separately
started clocks one observable timeline.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-COMPOUND-CLOCK`:** change a face and collapse the
outer Dock in the same task, then reverse the outer Dock and switch to a third
face before either settles. Any second live scheduler, terminal-opacity busy
tail, stale leaving face, or independent settle handoff fails.

### M33-5 — the CTA void is the known stale-horizon defect, and reset does not cancel ownership

The two-second CTA interval is source-determined:

- `useElementMorph.ts:133,355` sets duration to `response * 4 * 1000`; the
  default snappy response is 0.48s, hence about 1.92s;
- `scheme-spring.css:138-158` explicitly records that the old `4×response`
  horizon is the ~1.9s/0.4s mismatch that front-loads motion into the first
  10–16% and leaves a dead-flat tail, while the canonical snappy settle is 0.44s;
- `useDockCtaReceive.ts:203-219` then defines acceptable latency as 0.9–1.2 of
  that stale engine horizon, ratifying the void instead of detecting it.

C32 B3 and the A/B CTA frames are therefore exact agreement between pixels and
source: geometry arrives quickly, source opacity reaches zero, target remains a
0.35 ghost, and ownership waits until roughly 1.95–1.96s.

Cancellation is worse than unproved. `useDockCtaReceive.ts:294-307` cancels and
clears the travelling element but does not call `clearPending()`. After
`setPending() → receive() → reset()`, the source returns while the target can
remain indefinitely `data-cta-pending`, dimmed by `cta-seat.css:47-75`.
`supportsReverse: false` is explicit (`useDockCtaReceive.ts:84,169`). A repeated
`receive()` increments an interruption count but resets observable progress to
zero and can restart the full horizon.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-CTA-CANCEL`:** arm the seat, receive, interrupt at
100ms, reset, then receive again from a moved target. Require exactly one
legible owner in every frame, cleared pending state on reset, monotonic handoff,
bounded canonical latency and no stale callback. The current reset path leaves
the ghost armed.

### M33-6 — raster evidence cannot prove native-DPR sharpness

The selected files use `.png` names but identify as baseline JFIF JPEG data. B
desktop rest is 1280×720 pixels for a 1280×720 CSS viewport at reported DPR 2;
B mobile frames are 390×844 pixels for a 390×844 CSS viewport at reported DPR 3.
The transition strips are 900×300. They preserve CSS-pixel imagery after lossy
compression, not 2560×1440 or 1170×2532 device-pixel raster.

That does not invalidate the qualitative blur and ownership findings. It does
mean neither A nor B can close native-DPR glyph edge sharpness, subpixel jitter,
1-device-pixel rim continuity, or filter-layer resampling. C32 reports browser
DPR identity, not native-DPR raster evidence.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-RASTER`:** compare lossless device-pixel crops of
the same text/glyph/rim at rest, onset, mid, reverse and settle. Root texture
resampling, JPEG/CSS-pixel evidence, or a rim whose phase changes by a device
pixel fails.

### M33-7 — touch expansion disables the native scroll behavior that keyboard proves GREEN

C32 B6 is genuinely GREEN: End selects/focuses Settings and recentres the native
full-layer port (`scrollLeft=321.5`, `clientWidth=290`, `scrollWidth=612`). That
native scroll owner must survive simplification.

Touch parity is not established. `useDockTouchGate.ts:59-82` activates the shared
touch gate after a collapsed tap. `useTouchGate.ts:97-100` then writes
`touchAction = "none"` on the Dock root for the active interval. A subsequent
touch gesture on the newly expanded native overflow descendant is constrained by
that ancestor touch-action policy, so panning is disabled until deactivation; deactivation in
turn drives collapse (`useDockTouchGate.ts:85-88`). Fine-pointer wheel/hover and
keyboard End do not exercise this path.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-COARSE-SCROLL`:** on a collapsed, over-cap
horizontal Dock, tap to expand and immediately pan the native port before the
deactivation delay. The port must scroll without accidental activation,
pass-through, collapse or `touch-action:none`. Preserve the B6 keyboard result in
the same specimen.

### M33-8 — luma adaptation is stepped and its observer ownership is not proven singular

The luma sampler has good bounded work: 32×32 downsample, ≤4Hz, offscreen/hidden
pause, and one sample under PRM. But its “continuous” paint claim is unproved:

- `useGlassBackdropLuminance.ts:255-279` directly replaces luma, hue and strength
  inline values; `@property` typing alone does not animate direct assignments;
- each value change repaints plate background/rim and inherits into descendants;
- each sample reads transformed root geometry (`backdropLuminanceSample.ts:153-176`),
  so the observed settle rebase also changes the sampled region abruptly;
- `GlassDock.vue:92-110` chooses live observer setup only once. A runtime
  `backdropMode` change does not create/dispose the observer;
- per-surface observers test shared ancestry only when they sample or their target
  resolves. No observer watches a shared marker installed later. A later sample
  will stand down its readback, but it does not stop the already-running rAF loop;
  because the early return does not advance `lastSampleAt`, that loop can then
  retry the ancestry check every frame. The N→1 readback may hold while the
  claimed N→1 runtime ownership does not.

No A/B packet supplies sampled luma/hue, writer count, shared-coverage count,
paint cadence, or rest/onset/mid/reverse/settle computed surface receipts.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-LUMA-OWNERSHIP`:** mount N live Docks before and
after a shared route sampler, toggle static↔live, and move a high-contrast field
across the knee while morphing/reversing. Require one readback owner, no 4Hz tint
steps, no stale inline luma on static, and sampled rect continuity through settle.

### M33-9 — “compositor-only / no per-frame layout” is not supported by the declarations

The rAF writer changes an inheriting root custom property every frame
(`useDockMorph.ts:79-84`; `styles/index.css:81-101`). Its consumers include:

- `padding-inline` or `padding-block` (`morph.css:206-257`) — layout-affecting;
- root scale plus descendant inverse scale (`shape.css:126-160`);
- root filter (`morph.css:79-94`);
- plate background and border color (`morph.css:111-185`) — paint;
- radius, opacity, child scale and clipping (`shape.css`, `layers.css`,
  `crossfade.css`).

The root also carries a filtered subtree, a backdrop-filtered negative-z plate,
a blended grain pseudo-element, and multiple `will-change` promotions. Containment
limits damage; it does not turn padding/background/filter into compositor-only
work. No frame packet contains style/layout/paint counts, layer-tree receipts,
GPU texture dimensions, memory, dropped-frame data, or low-end/coarse evidence.

**Born-RED mutation `MUT-C33-PAINT-BUDGET`:** trace first open, warm open,
collapse, reversal, crossfade+collapse and CTA receive on desktop DPR2 and mobile
DPR3. Any per-frame layout from the Dock scalar, full-subtree filter texture,
unbounded layer promotion, repeated backdrop readback, or missed-frame burst
fails. Static final screenshots cannot satisfy this mutation.

## Contradictions that adjudication must resolve

1. **One scalar vs three meanings:** root `--dock-morph-t`, plate/per-face
   `--dock-t`, and directional `--dock-expand-t` coexist; the plate scalar is a
   no-op while the per-face scalar belongs to separate live schedulers.
2. **Aperture vs scale:** prose says the plate clip is the visible extent owner;
   source defaults it to zero inset and uses whole-root scale plus inverse
   descendants.
3. **Native sharpness vs root filter:** prose calls the surface filter
   compositor-safe; it still rasterizes interactive own pixels and visibly
   softens them.
4. **Continuous settle vs endpoint handoff:** the scalar settles before natural
   class geometry takes over and changes the box.
5. **Arbitrary shape lerp vs discrete selector:** no scalar participates in the
   custom clip endpoint selection.
6. **One spring vs one constructor:** a shared factory removes duplicate code,
   not simultaneous timelines.
7. **Canonical 0.44s snappy settle vs 1.92s CTA horizon:** the CTA resurrects the
   exact 4×response mismatch the spring-token source documents as obsolete.
8. **Abort/reset vs ownership reset:** resetting travel leaves the landing seat
   pending.
9. **Native overflow vs touch-action:** keyboard recentering is GREEN, but the
   first touch expansion can disable follow-up native panning.
10. **Continuous luma vs discrete writes:** a typed custom property does not
    smooth direct 4Hz inline replacements without an animation/transition owner.
11. **Compositor-only vs animated padding/paint:** containment is not evidence of
    zero layout or paint.

## Existing GREEN invariants to preserve

- Preserve B6's native overflow scroll owner, End-key selection/focus and
  recentering. A simplified topology must not replace it with transform scrolling.
- Preserve the separation between backdrop sampling and own pixels: one
  `backdrop-filter` on the optical plate is legitimate; the root own-pixel filter
  is not.
- Preserve `useDockMorph.ts:73-86`'s live scalar complement and velocity-sign
  inversion as a useful reversal primitive, but bind it to stable endpoints.
- Preserve inactive/leaving face non-hit-testing and inert ownership while
  eliminating state tails that no longer paint an observable dissolve.
- Preserve PRM's intended snap-to-owned-end-state, source-level blur zeroing and
  no-travel CTA policy. These are source-positive invariants, not Browser GREEN
  until exact PRM frames and computed receipts exist.
- Preserve fixed Dock transform-free centering and do not solve origin by adding
  a translated ancestor that breaks portals/anchors.
