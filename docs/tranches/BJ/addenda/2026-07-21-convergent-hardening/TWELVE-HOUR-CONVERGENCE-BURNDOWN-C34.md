## Percentage law for two-hour reports

Every report gives raw numerator/denominator plus these weighted fields:

1. inventory/source-consumer reconciliation — 15%;
2. Browser Assay A — 25%;
3. Browser Assay B — 25%;
4. three independent failure-assuming challenge coverage — 15%;
5. separate adjudication/agglomeration — 15%;
6. restart-safe compaction/handoff — 5%.

No elapsed-time, file-count, agent-count, passing test, or prose volume earns
progress by itself. Missing-state receipts are honest coverage outcomes but not
GREEN component cells.

## Parsimonious component law

The audit will not spawn a bespoke bureaucracy per component. Components are
frozen in live gallery/category cohorts plus cross-cutting material, motion,
accessibility, colocation/package and consumer-idiomaticity matrices. Every
component still receives its own row in all three independent failure-assuming
passes; a critic may cover many rows in one bounded packet. One shared detector
or one Browser episode cannot impersonate independence.

Priority deep cohorts are:

1. Dock architecture and controls;
2. Tabs/Slider/selection controls;
3. overlays and expandable/configuration surfaces;
4. Aurora/Blob/Constellation/Fourier living substrates;
5. Feedback and iOS-27 motion components;
6. data/display/forms/foundations/compositions;
7. material/cascade/exports/colocation/package and cross-repo idiomaticity.

- run three unchanged-packet Sol x-high passes in parallel:
  systems/ontology/KISS; motion/material/raster/performance; interaction/a11y/
  consumer/package;

- root continues only exact gap closure, not discretionary re-litigation.

### H10–H12 — adjudicate and compact

- independently adjudicate conflicts and duplicate asks;
- freeze the apotheosis, dependency DAG, browser missing-state ledger, owner
  decisions, and execution handoff;
- report exact terminal percentages and any bounded owner-held remainder.

## Stop rules

- A settled issue reopens only when new exact evidence changes mechanism,
  ownership, or acceptance proof.
- Three failed attempts at the same Browser state become one explicit
  missing-state receipt; no retry spiral.
- Cosmetic preference without a spec/receiver/idiomaticity mechanism is routed
  to the later execution portfolio, not expanded into a new audit wave.
- No new tranche row is minted unless all existing owners are explicitly proven
  insufficient.
- SCI/Atlas receives only exact consumer-specific findings; Glass producer work
  remains in this authoritative task.

## Two-hour cadence

Six checkpoints are scheduled in this Glass task. Each reports:

1. completed denominator since the prior checkpoint;
2. exact new RED/GREEN findings;
3. remaining critical path;
4. inventory, Assay A, Assay B, critic, adjudication, compaction and overall
   percentages;
5. any risk to the twelve-hour convergence budget and the corrective cut.
