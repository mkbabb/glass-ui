## Matrix notation and public-reach law

- **D** — direct canonical route story.
- **N** — nested story member on the named live route.
- **I** — internal leaf reached only through its public parent on that route.
- **X** — an exact external consumer exists.
- **M** — the route or branch exists but a required live state/cell is missing.
- **R** — owner-final remove/fold disposition under C45.

`root + /x` means the leaf is reachable from the curated root barrel and its
package subpath. `/x only` means intentional opt-in. `internal` means no public
component export; a public parent owns it. Input and Textarea are `/forms only`.

Every external consumer is governed by the consume-unmodified law: public
props, slots, tokens, and documented compositions only. Private selectors,
copied Glass classes/effects, receiver-local radius/padding/motion skins, or a
parallel renderer are RED unless a bounded, owner-assigned, born-RED missing
producer contract proves the exception and its deletion edge.
