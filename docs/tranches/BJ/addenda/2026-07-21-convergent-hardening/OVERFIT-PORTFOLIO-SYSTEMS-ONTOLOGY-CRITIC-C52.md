## 2. Constitutional test applied

C45 makes the Instrument Chassis removal owner-final and gives every survivor a
six-part burden: distinct semantic job, stable boundary, demonstrated reuse or
foundation role, lower total complexity, implementation/test colocation against
the same contract, and no parallel engine, selector, alias, or state owner. C22
also requires leaf-grain exports, collaborators, consumers, tests/gaps, evidence,
dependencies, and critic/adjudicator state. Source-only tests are not a substitute
for receiver proof.

I therefore failure-assumed each C48 disposition. Adoption count is blast-radius
evidence, not semantic survival evidence. A route screenshot is appearance
evidence, not ownership evidence. Fewer files is not simpler when a coherent
engine is pasted into a consumer. A single visible specimen is not single-engine
ownership when an invisible hero, timer, keyed remount, or global control plane
survives.

Two perceptual invariants constrain subtraction:

- **Breath of Life:** interaction may change a live system without throwing away
  its visual context, canvas identity, or accumulated state.
- **Movement of Momentum:** motion is bounded, interruptible, reduced-motion
  aware, and continuous across semantic mode changes; it is not recreated by
  remount or sustained by a second idle clock.

These invariants do not justify parallel renderers. They require one renderer,
one state/config owner, one clock lineage, and deliberate continuity.

`waves/BAND-STORY.md:38-61` assigns `manifest.ts`, `StoryPage.vue`, and the

`StoryPage.vue:32-34` branches only on hero/page state.

C48 cannot independently bless direct `VizStudio` imports, generic `FamilyTabs`
imports, and route deletions. Reconcile them through the existing owner:

- `VizStudio` becomes the sole registered `studio` variant rather than an ad hoc

- page-root `FamilyTabs` becomes the registered `family` variant;
- in-page `FamilyTabs` remains a section-level primitive using the same active

  newly minted empty engine.

Any direct wrapper path outside the registry is born RED. STORY W1 must precede
or co-land the route/category fold; C48 P1/P2 cannot create a parallel taxonomy.

### F2 — Constellation's “one live specimen” is currently two or more

`manifest.ts:455-463` gives the Constellation route
`background: "constellation"` and `hero: true`. `StoryHero.vue:116-123` mounts a
live `<Constellation>`. The route itself mounts seven more at
`constellation.vue:300,331,372,395,423,441,467`. It also exposes four window
globals at lines 184-273 and polls settlement with `window.setInterval` at line
269.

C48's “one live interactive specimen” is ambiguous: retaining the hero plus one
in-page specimen leaves two renderers and two clock/context lineages. Formation
must choose one exact end state. The least surprising one is a static route hero
(`paper` or `grid`) plus one persistent in-page renderer. Controlled modes mutate
that same instance without `:key`, conditional replacement, global controls, or
a story-owned interval. Static comparisons remain zero-clock.

This is the concrete preservation of Breath and Momentum: visual context and
velocity survive mode change in one canvas; they are not simulated by parallel
canvases or recreated by remount.

### F5 — Tempo should change the next spring, not remount the world

`tempo.vue:44-53` writes a root custom property and removes it on unmount,
destroying any pre-existing inline value/priority. Line 139 keys `GlassDock` by
tempo. Yet `useDockSpring.ts:87-108` creates a fresh `SpringProgress` on each
`playTo` and calls `motionTempo()` at line 100. Springs similarly reads tempo for
each semantic play at `springs.vue:106-113,199-207`.

The fold should therefore:

1. snapshot the prior root value and priority;
2. install one mutable root setting owner;
3. let the next natural spring construction read it;
4. restore the exact prior value/priority on teardown; and
5. remove the keyed remount.

Do not invent a continuous, mid-flight retuning promise unless the public motion
contract is separately changed and proved. That would replace a demo fold with a
new engine requirement. The setting is not a per-frame scalar and need not
violate component-local animation ownership when it has one reversible owner.

`StoryPlayButton.vue` has one receiver, Springs, and its configurable branches
are not exercised there. Inline both call sites with public Button semantics,
but make this the same Springs transaction as the Tempo fold and replace the
stubbed helper with a real receiver test. Two waves editing one story and one
test are churn, not modularity.
