SENSITIVITY LIMIT — 17 of 18 reads bite (measured, and NOT papered over)
SPEEDTEST does not go black under the plant. Probed directly: it is the ONLY preset
whose canvas carries `opacity: 0.26` at read time (every other preset reads opacity 1)
because `runtime.ts` syncPresentationAlpha writes the config `alpha` to
`canvas.style.opacity`. The light page ground reads THROUGH the dead canvas and the
composite mean lands at 140.5.

This is a REAL limitation of the floor, not an artefact of the plant: a genuinely black
substrate on a low-alpha preset would composite the same way. The plant was deliberately
NOT strengthened (forcing opacity to 1 would make the self-test more aggressive than the
defect it simulates, overstating the floor's reach — the floor would then claim coverage
it does not have, which is the exact sin this wave cures).

ROUTED: a black substrate on a low-alpha preset belongs to the per-preset hue/chroma
parity gates (Family G W10/W11), which read chroma collapse rather than a composite
mean. Recorded at the floor assertion in the spec so the next reader cannot mistake
18 labelled reads for 18 bitable ones.

WHY THE READS ARE DISTINCT — the vacuity cure
The authored walk located the picker by three selectors matching NOTHING in the repo
(`data-preset-key`, `.aurora-preset-picker`, `[role="tab"][data-preset]` — 0 hits repo-
wide), so it resolved 0 tiles and pressed ArrowRight against document.body while the
only keydown handler is per-button. All 17 labelled reads re-measured DEFAULT and the
floor's GREEN asserted 17-preset coverage it never obtained. The walk now drives the
demo's real `button[data-preset-tile]` tiles and latches aria-pressed per click. The 18
DISTINCT values above are the witness: a re-measured DEFAULT would repeat one number.

WHY THE METRIC CHANGED — max was measured hollow
The readback region is the canvas BOUNDING BOX and the demo stage draws DOM chrome over
it (nuclei markers, renderer badge, hint caption). Against a MAX floor one lit chrome
pixel carried the whole assertion: a planted all-black aurora read maxChannel 88 and
PASSED. aurora-planted-black.png shows exactly that chrome surviving over a dead canvas.
The MEAN separates real 236.0 from planted 0.4.

THE SELF-TEST'S OWN SELF-TEST — the bite is ATTRIBUTED, not assumed
Crediting ANY failure to the plant cannot distinguish a biting floor from a dead
harness. Each floor now pins the TEXT of its own failure. Proven live: the planted
report was mutated to keep both failures but replace the error text with a harness
