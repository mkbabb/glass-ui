# CHALLENGE 3 — DESIGN-FIDELITY / GESTALT lens (story-page-standard GOLDEN)

Verdict: **SURVIVES — but WOUNDED.** The structural inversion (field-floor + ONE `<DemoFrame variant>` +
φ² stage escape) is sound, deftly-integrable, and the born-RED truth is real (live-verified on HEAD: 0
`.paper-field`, `--field-h`/`--ease-cartoon-punch`/`--motion-weight` all unset, 1 `<h1>`, flat
`StorySection` = `flex flex-col gap-3`). But three of the GOLDEN's headline DESIGN edicts — the cartoon
cel-slam CASCADE, the matrix RIPPLE, and the warm-field "wears the hero's clothes" gestalt — are
**asserted in prose but ABSENT from the de-risked spike** or **mechanically self-contradictory**. The
gestalt bar is not met by what was actually proven. Hardening below makes the spike honest.

---

## TOP REFUTATION (the one that lands hardest)

**The "overlapping cel-slam cascade with the lagging cast" (lens-c's keystone, §1C, §2d, S6, S7) is
mechanically incoherent and UNBUILT in the spike.**

Live-measured the de-risked `spike.html` in Chrome:

- `.demo-cel` `animation-name: cel-slam`, **`animation-delay: 0s`** — there is NO `--i` stagger wired.
- The cel-slam runs on **`animation-timeline: view()`** (scroll-progress driven, confirmed
  `CSS.supports('animation-timeline','view()') === true`).
- **`animation-delay` does NOT apply to a view()-progress timeline** — delay is a *time* offset; a view
  timeline maps the animation to *scroll progress*, not the clock. So the GOLDEN's S7 keystone
  `.story-cel { animation-delay: calc(var(--i) * 0.62 * 120ms) }` (§2d) is a **no-op against the very
  `view()` mechanism it is bolted onto.** The "1/φ overlap step" cannot exist on a view-timeline cel.

This is fatal to the lens-c headline. The §L4 "overlapping action" law requires cel N+1 to begin BEFORE
cel N settles on a shared clock. But each `view()`-driven cel slams **independently when scrolled into its
own `entry 0%→42%` window** — they do NOT overlap as a choreographed cascade; they fire one-at-a-time as
you scroll, each isolated. That is a per-element scroll-reveal, the OPPOSITE of a coordinated
technicolor-cel-sequence. **S6/S7 will green a fundamentally non-cascading motion.** The "page paints
itself like a 1940s cel sequence flipping into place" gestalt (§1C, §7.5) is not what the spike does.

Compounding: the **cartoon CAST** (the lens-c emotional payload — "the shadow visibly CATCHES UP after
the plate lands") is, in the rendered spike, a `::after { box-shadow: oklch(.62 .1 58/0.18) 4px 5px }` at
`z-index:-1`. Measured offset 4×5px at 18% alpha — **imperceptible** in `spike-light.png`; on the cel it
reads as a faint static drop, not a lagging cast that "catches up." The boldest cartoon mechanism the
GOLDEN names is, in paint, invisible.

## REFUTATION 2 — the GLASS is gray; only the FIELD is warm (precept: NO-GRAY applies to the GLASS)

The §3/S2 floor is met by the **field** (raster C 0.09). But the BA.W-NO-GRAY warm-floor is a precept
about the **glass the user actually looks at**, and the spike's cel plate is desaturated:

- light cel: `oklch(0.97 0.02 58 / 0.46)` — **chroma 0.02**
- dark cel:  `oklch(0.32 0.03 58 / 0.5)`  — **chroma 0.03**

C 0.02–0.03 on the plate is **near-neutral**. S3 only checks the *composited transmit-delta over the
field* (C ≥ 0.018) — it samples the field bleeding THROUGH, not the plate's own warmth. A flat gray plate
at 46% alpha over a C-0.09 field will pass S3 purely on the field's bleed-through while the plate itself
is gray. **The "gray glass DIES" claim is only true where the field shows through the gaps; the plate
register is gray-adjacent and the gate cannot tell.** This is exactly the precept the lens exists to
catch.

## REFUTATION 3 — the field is MONOCHROME; it does NOT wear the hero's clothes

The GOLDEN's entire gestalt thesis (§0, §7) is "make every content page wear the `/substrates/aurora`
hero's clothes." Live-compared:

- **Reference `/substrates/aurora`** (screenshot): a genuinely MULTI-NUCLEI, multi-HUE painterly field —
  purple → blue → gold sweeping across the page. Rich, varied, alive.
- **Spike field**: a single `--field-h: 58`, all three stops within `±34°` of h58
  (`calc(--field-h - 10)`, `+16`, `+34`) — a **monochromatic amber wash**. One hue per route.

A single-hue radial/conic gradient is the §3 *floor*, not the hero's *clothes*. Every content page becomes
a flat one-color tint behind glass. That clears the C≥0.045 metric but **misses the gestalt bar the GOLDEN
itself set** (§7: "as alive as the aurora hero"). The metric is satisfied; the gestalt is not — precisely
the failure mode the brief warns against ("the gestalt is the bar, not the metric").

## REFUTATION 4 — the MATRIX "ripple" is asserted, never de-risked

§2a + §7 promise the matrix variant's cells "each slamming on the overlapping stagger (the grid ripples
in)." Live: the spike's `.cell` elements have **`animation-name: none`**. Zero per-cell motion. The
boldest grid mechanism in the taxonomy is unbuilt in the only artefact that claims to de-risk the design.
S6's anti-evasion bite #matrix is therefore unproven by the spike.

## REFUTATION 5 — the φ² stage's protagonist is a DEAD flat fill (no demo, no cartoon-punch, no field)

The φ² escape geometry is real and well-measured (stage 1382 > 1152, stage/prose 1.691 ≈ φ, dead margin
58px — credit where due). But the "protagonist STAGE" in the spike is `oklch(0.9 0.12 58 / 0.4)` — a
**flat solid amber rectangle with a centered text label**, no live component, no field transmitting
through, no meatball, no cartoon shadowing of contents. The GOLDEN's gestalt bar §7.7 ("deftly composes
glass-ui components — docks/viz/cards") is the ENTIRE point of a stage, and the de-risk shows an empty
colored box. A bigger empty box is still empty. The "bigger card" geometry is proven; the "bigger card is
WORTH bleeding to φ²" claim is not.

## REFUTATION 6 — DEPENDS chain is plan-on-plan; nothing is actually consumable yet

The GOLDEN frames the field tokens, `--ease-cartoon-punch`, `--motion-weight`, `--shadow-cartoon`
(redefined), `warmFieldHue` as "CONSUME, never re-mint" upstream DEPENDS. Verified:

- `.paper-field`, `--field-h`, `warmFieldHue`, `--ease-cartoon-punch`, `--motion-weight` = **0 mounts in
  src/ and demo/** (live + grep). They live only in *sibling tranche-dev GOLDENs* (`page-background/`,
  `motion-spring-register/`, `cartoon-shadow/`) that are themselves **plans, not landed code**.
- `--shadow-cartoon` IS shipped (`shadow.css:9`) but as `3px 3px 0 / 8% foreground` — the spike
  **redefines** it to `4px 5px 0 oklch(.62 .1 58 / 0.18)`. That is a **fork of a shipped token**, not a
  consume. "NO LEGACY / DRY / reuse" is violated at the token the cast depends on.

So the spike's "warm-cream floor" is hand-rolled (`--neutral-0: oklch(0.985 0.012 84)`, redefined
`--foreground`, redefined `--shadow-cartoon`), NOT the shipped registers. The de-risk proves a *standalone
recreation*, not the *union* the GOLDEN promises. The build-DAG is a tower of unbuilt GOLDENs; if any
sibling lands different tokens, this chassis's design breaks. **The "byte-untouched library, FOUR net-new
artefacts" claim is only true if four other GOLDENs land first, exactly as specced — unverified.**

## REFUTATION 7 — KISS leak: `bleed: margin-inline: calc(50% - 50cqw)` requires a `cqw` context the spike lacks

§2c bleeds the stage via `margin-inline: calc(50% - 50cqw)`. `cqw` resolves against the nearest
**container-query container**; the GOLDEN never declares `container-type` on the article, and the project
has a documented `container-type` removal scar (MEMORY: "dock-collapse fix = container-type removal"). The
spike sidesteps this by setting `inline-size: var(--story-stage-w)` directly (no `cqw`), so **the spike
does NOT de-risk the §2c bleed recipe it ships** — it de-risks a *different* (simpler, fixed-width) escape.
The shipped recipe is untested and depends on an absent container context.

## REFUTATION 8 (minor) — a11y: φ² bleed reading-order vs visual-order on the stage rail

§5 asserts "tab-order stays reading-order" for the `1fr / 0.382fr` stage grid (canvas LEFT, rail RIGHT).
On mobile the grid stacks canvas-then-rail (fine). On desktop the configurator rail is visually RIGHT but
the GOLDEN gives no `order`/DOM guarantee that the live demo (canvas) precedes its controls in DOM for SR
users; VizStudio's accessible-inspector is cited but the *generalized* DemoStage has no stated contract.
Low severity, but unspecified.

## HARDENING (the concrete changes that answer the refutations)

1. **Cascade vs view-timeline — pick ONE, honestly (kills R1).** Either (a) drop `view()` and drive the
   cel-slam on a real **time-based mount cascade** so `animation-delay: calc(var(--i) * step)` actually
   produces overlapping action (then S7 measures a true overlap), OR (b) keep `view()` and **re-spec S7**
   to assert per-cel scroll-reveal (NOT a cascade) and DELETE the overlapping-action / "cel N+1 before cel
   N settles" claims from §1C/§2d/§7.5. The spike must demonstrate whichever is chosen. The cast lag is
   only meaningful under (a); under (b) it is per-cel and the "catches up across the page" read is gone.
   **De-risk the chosen path with a frame-series capture, not prose.**

2. **Make the CAST perceptible or cut it (kills R1b).** 4×5px @ 18% is invisible. Either push the cast
   offset to a cartoon-register magnitude scaled by `--motion-weight` (and prove it in a paired
   light/dark frame at the settle-lag moment), or remove the lagging-cast claim. A mechanism that doesn't
   show in paint is not a design edict met.

3. **Warm the GLASS, not just the field (kills R2).** Lift the cel-plate chroma to the NO-GRAY floor
   (e.g. `oklch(0.96 0.05 --field-h / α)` light, `oklch(0.34 0.06 --field-h / α)` dark) AND add an S3b
   bite: **raster the plate region where the field does NOT show through and assert C ≥ 0.025 warm** — so a
   gray plate over a warm field cannot pass on bleed-through alone.

4. **Multi-nuclei the field, or stop citing the aurora hero (kills R3).** Either give the per-route field
   ≥2 nuclei at φ-spaced hues (`--field-h` + a complementary stop) so it reads painterly like the
   reference, OR redefine the gestalt bar to "a contained single-hue warm wash" and stop claiming it
   "wears the aurora hero's clothes." Add an S2 bite asserting ≥2 distinct hue clusters if the former.

5. **De-risk the matrix ripple + a real stage demo (kills R4, R5).** The spike must show the matrix cells
   with per-cell stagger animation actually firing, and the stage must host a real glass-ui component (a
   live Button row / a dock) so the φ²-bleed is justified by content, not an empty box. Re-capture.

6. **Pin the DEPENDS as a hard precondition gate (kills R6).** Add a born-RED arm asserting the upstream
   tokens (`.paper-field`, `--field-h`, `warmFieldHue`, `--ease-cartoon-punch`, `--motion-weight`,
   `--shadow-cartoon` final form) EXIST in src/ before this wave greens — so the chassis cannot land
   against unbuilt siblings. Stop redefining `--shadow-cartoon`; consume the shipped token or change it in
   the cartoon-shadow GOLDEN, not here (DRY/NO-LEGACY).

7. **De-risk the SHIPPED bleed recipe (kills R7).** Either declare `container-type: inline-size` on the
   article and de-risk `calc(50% - 50cqw)` in the spike (verifying no container-collapse scar recurs), or
   ship the simpler fixed `inline-size` escape the spike actually proved and delete the `cqw` recipe from
   §2c.

8. **State the stage DOM-order a11y contract (kills R8).** Mandate canvas-before-rail in DOM with visual
   `order` for the desktop right-rail, mirroring the VizStudio accessible-inspector contract explicitly.
