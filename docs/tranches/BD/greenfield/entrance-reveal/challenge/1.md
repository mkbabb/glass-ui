## LANDING REFUTATIONS

### R1 — OVERFIT / HONESTY HOLE in the de-risk spike (the §7e measurements are against the WRONG curve)

The spike header (`golden/liquid-enter-spike.html:21`) says the springs are *"verbatim from
src/styles/tokens/scheme-motion.css"*. They are NOT. The shipping `--spring-snappy` has **48 stops**;
the spike's has **21**. `--spring-bouncy` likewise truncated. The peak values are preserved, but the
**interior settle shape differs** — and §7e's load-bearing finding ("a 0.12 span × a 1.22 ease-peak
yields only ~2% absolute punch; the punch MUST live in explicit STOPS") was measured against this
*shortened* curve, not the real one. A "Chrome-verified" de-risk that silently substitutes a
different easing is not a verification of the shipping recipe. **This is the [[overfitting audit]]
class: the artefact does not match the substrate it claims to derive from.** The §7e correction
(2-stop base vs 3-stop cel) may be right, but it was DERIVED on an unfaithful curve — it must be
re-measured against the real 48-stop `linear()` before it can be called load-bearing.

### R2 — THE CARTOON CAST IS UNVERIFIED ON A REAL GLASS SURFACE (the spike substrate is a lie-by-omission)

The §2c cast is a `z-index:-1; inset:0` child carrying `box-shadow: var(--shadow-cartoon)` = a
**`3px 3px 0px 0px` hard offset with ZERO spread, ZERO blur**. The spike "verified" it on a card with
`background: var(--card)` = **opaque `#fffaf0`**. But the REAL consumer surface, `.glass-card`
(`surfaces.css:19-20`), is `background: color-mix(in oklab, var(--glass-bg-quiet), …)` +
`backdrop-filter` — a **translucent glass plate**. Consequences the golden never paint-tested:
- A zero-spread offset shadow on an `inset:0` child only peeks a **3px sliver** past the parent's
  bottom-right edge; the rest is occluded by the parent box.
- That child is `z-index:-1` — it sits **behind the parent's own translucent background AND its
  backdrop-filter plate**, which washes the already-thin sliver further.
- Net: on a real `.glass-card`/`.glass-floating` the "bold technicolor lagging cast" may be
  **near-invisible** — the cartoon-punch precept (bold layered-offset shadows) is asserted on an
  opaque mock, not demonstrated on the glass it must ride. **The cast is the golden's single most
  audacious move (§3) and it is the LEAST verified.** It also risks clipping: `border-radius:inherit`
  + a child whose shadow must escape the rounded parent — on a parent with `overflow` or a stacking
  context the sliver clips entirely.

### R3 — β (the mount-race) was NOT reproduced; `revealWhenReady()` is speculative complexity (KISS)

I instrumented the exact demo timing: after `open=true`, at the **first** `requestAnimationFrame`,
`.glass-reveal` **was already bound** (`surfaceBoundAtFirstRaf:true`, present across the next 5 rAFs).
β did not fire on this engine. The golden itself hedges ("β can still no-op a *slow* mount") — i.e. it
is a *theoretical* race, not a reproduced one. Yet the cure is heavy: `revealWhenReady()` =
`await nextTick()` + double-rAF + a one-shot `watch(surface,…,{flush:'post'})`. That is three
redundant readiness mechanisms for a defect with **zero captured repro**. Defense-in-depth is
defensible, but KISS says: the load-bearing fix is α (the resolver) + ONE robust arm, not a
belt-braces-and-suspenders trio whose born-RED gate (§7a) only ever exercises the α path. **The §7a
spec cannot go RED on β** (the surface is present), so β's cure ships UNTESTED — a gate that does not
guard what it adds.

### R4 — "PERFECT in Chrome AND Safari" is asserted-by-construction, NOT measured (the binding law's own bar)

The golden's headline and §4 claim cross-engine perfection, and BINDING LAW (design.md / the
hardening plan) demands a **paired-engine π (Chromium + WebKit), NEVER a single-engine green**. Yet
§7e's own closing admits: *"OUTSTANDING (not yet captured): the paired WebKit run + both-modes… the
spike is authored Safari-15-safe **by construction**."* By-construction is not the bar the golden
itself sets. Specific WebKit risks left unmeasured: (a) `filter: blur()` animation on a surface that
ALSO carries a resting `backdrop-filter` glass plate — WebKit composites `filter` + `backdrop-filter`
on the same element differently than Chrome, and a per-frame `filter` re-raster on a backdrop-filtered
box is exactly the WebKit reblur hole the golden cites elsewhere; the golden waves this away for the
*child cast* but the `.liquid-enter` HOST itself blurs while glass-plated. (b) `scale:`/`translate:`
longhands compositing with a centering `transform` — Safari longhand-vs-shorthand interaction on the
same box. **The gate is RED-provable on HEAD but not GREEN-provable cross-engine until the WebKit
capture exists. The golden may not claim cross-engine perfection on a Chrome-only spike.**

### R5 (minor) — the 1/φ stagger overlaps with `--spring-snappy-duration`, not the spring SETTLE horizon

§1 says "the per-beat delay is the spring's **settle horizon** × 1/φ." The recipe (§2b line 187) sets
`--lq-enter-step: calc(var(--spring-snappy-duration) * 0.618)`. `--spring-snappy-duration` is `0.4s`
(`scheme-motion.css:260`) — the WALL clock, which is the snappy spring's full settle. Fine for snappy.
But the cel register runs on `--ease-cartoon-punch` / the bouncy floor whose duration is `0.62s`; the
stagger still keys off `--spring-snappy-duration`. So on the cel path beat N+1 launches at
`0.4s·0.618 = 0.247s` while beat N (a 0.62s animation) is only ~40% through, not 62%. **The "overlap
at 62%" golden-proportion claim breaks the moment the cel register's longer clock is in play** — the
overlap math is hardcoded to the snappy duration. Either key the step off each register's own
duration, or document that 1/φ-overlap is a snappy-register-only guarantee.

## CONCRETE HARDENING (what answers each refutation)

1. **R1:** Re-author the spike's `--spring-snappy`/`--spring-bouncy` as the **full 48-stop** strings
   from `scheme-motion.css` (or `@import` them, not inline-truncate), then RE-RUN `__probe()` and
   re-confirm the §7e 2-stop-vs-3-stop finding against the real curve. Strike "verbatim" until it is.
2. **R2:** Add a spike card (and a born-RED gate frame) whose substrate is a **real `.glass-card`**
   (translucent bg + `backdrop-filter`), capture the cast mid-flight, and PROVE the technicolor shadow
   is visible on glass. If the zero-spread `--shadow-cartoon` washes out, switch the cast to a
   spread/blurred offset OR render it as a SIBLING-behind (not a `z-index:-1` descendant under the
   plate). §7c must assert the cast's painted pixels are non-background on a glass parent, not just
   `castOpacity` on an opaque mock.
3. **R3:** Demote the β cure to ONE mechanism — the `watch(surface,…,{flush:'post'})` one-shot alone
   closes the race deterministically (it fires the instant `surface` binds, no nextTick+rAF needed).
   Drop the double-rAF+nextTick belt unless a captured slow-mount repro justifies it. Add a born-RED
   β arm to §7a (mount with the surface deliberately delayed one tick) so whatever cure ships is
   actually guarded.
4. **R4:** Capture the paired WebKit π (both modes) for BOTH the bloom and the `.liquid-enter`
   entrance BEFORE the gate is called GREEN — and specifically measure the HOST `filter: blur()`
   animating on a `backdrop-filter`-plated `.glass-floating` in WebKit (the resting-glass surface, not
   the spike's plain card). Downgrade every "PERFECT in Safari" to "Safari-15-safe by construction,
   WebKit-π PENDING" until the capture lands.
5. **R5:** Key `--lq-enter-step` off the **register's own** duration
   (`var(--lq-enter-duration, var(--spring-snappy-duration))`), set per-register, so the 1/φ overlap
   holds on the cel/bouncy clock too — or scope the "62% overlap" guarantee to the snappy register in
   §1/§4.

## VERDICT

**SURVIVES — wounded but not refuted.** The spine (α born-RED, flat-fade-no-squish, the asElement
hoist, the glass-reveal→.liquid-enter promotion, the cross-engine-floor-not-fallback inversion) is
live-verified and deftly integrated — a true union, no fork, no legacy. The four landing refutations
(R1 spike overfit, R2 unverified cast on glass, R3 unguarded β cure, R4 Chrome-only "Safari-perfect")
are real and must be closed, but each is a **hardening of the gate/spike**, not a collapse of the
design. R2 is the most serious: the single boldest move (the cartoon cast) is verified on the wrong
substrate. Close R1/R2 (re-measure on the real curve + real glass) and the golden is sound.
