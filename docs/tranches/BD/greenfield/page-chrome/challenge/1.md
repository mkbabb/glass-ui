# CHALLENGE #1 — CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS (page-chrome GOLDEN)

**Verdict: SURVIVES WITH ONE LOAD-BEARING REFUTATION** that must be hardened before build. The
GOLDEN's idea (one `--chrome-t` scalar, one register, one seam, six asks by construction) is genuinely
deft and DRY — but its single most-trumpeted "library-wide" mechanism, **§2e the corner-AA clip co-declared
on every glass tier, is a destructive regression as written**, not a clean fix. Plus a cluster of
correctness/overfit defects below. Default-REFUTED on §2e; the rest are hardenings.

---

## R1 (DECISIVE — destructive, NOT deft) — §2e `clip-path: inset(0 round var(--radius))` on every glass tier KILLS the floating lift + iOS under-shadow library-wide

The GOLDEN sells §2e as "the ONE `src/` paint, paint-only, isolation-safe, inherited by the bar/stamp/toc."
It is none of those things. **`clip-path` clips the element's ENTIRE rendering, including its outer
`box-shadow`** (CSS Masking spec: the clip region is the painting boundary; a drop-shadow lives outside the
border box and is clipped away). Every glass tier in this library paints an OUTER lift shadow:

- `src/styles/glass/ladder.css:49` — `box-shadow: var(--glass-material-rim), var(--glass-shadow-wash)`
- ladder.css §AL-W10 — the floating tier prepends `--glass-under-shadow-vivid` (`0 8px 32px -4px α0.12`)
  + the iOS 0.5px under-shadow hairline; resting → `--glass-under-shadow-default` (`0 4px 16px -2px`).

**Live-proven (Chrome, chrome-devtools-mcp):** two identical boxes, `box-shadow: 0 8px 40px -2px red`,
one with `clip-path: inset(0 round 24px)`. The unclipped box renders the full red lift glow; the clipped
box renders **ZERO outer shadow** — the entire drop-shadow is gone. Screenshot captured. This is not an
edge case — it is the documented behavior, and it is exactly the shadow stack §2e blankets across
`.glass-wash/.glass-quiet/.glass-resting/.glass-floating/.glass-overlay/.glass-card`.

So §2e as written does not "clip the saturate halo to the silhouette" cleanly — it **amputates the
floating/overlay lift + the iOS under-shadow on every Dialog, Sheet, Popover, DropdownMenu, and card in
the library**. That is the opposite of "survival of the fittest, keep what is fit": it deletes a shipped,
load-bearing depth cue (the §3 PERFECTED-glass iOS under-shadow the binding law demands) to fix a corner
halo. It is a regression masquerading as a one-liner, and it violates the binding law's PERFECTED-glass
mandate (the under-shadow IS the iOS-27 reference's defined edge).

**Hardening (mandatory):** do NOT clip the lift away. Two correct routes, pick one:
1. **Clip the HALO source, not the element.** The square saturate fringe is the `backdrop-filter`
   saturate bleed + the `::after` grain layer (ladder.css:437 `.glass-floating::after` already
   `border-radius: inherit; inset: 0`). The grain is already corner-correct via `border-radius: inherit`.
   The real square is the `backdrop-filter` region. Move the clip to a NESTED backdrop element (a
   `::before` that owns ONLY the backdrop-filter, clipped), leaving the host element's box-shadow
   unclipped — OR
2. **Use `overflow: clip` semantics on a backdrop-only child**, never `clip-path` on the shadow-bearing
   host. The GOLDEN must explicitly carve the shadow channel out of the clip, and the gate (C6) must
   assert the floating lift + under-shadow SURVIVE the clip (raster a non-zero shadow band BELOW the
   tier), not merely that "the corner is clean." As written, C6 would pass on a tree that has deleted
   every card shadow — a false-green.

This is the difference between deft union and bolt-on damage, and the GOLDEN currently asserts the wrong
one.

## R2 (overfit / wrong-token) — §2e clips to `var(--radius)` (= 0.625rem global), NOT the tier's real corner

`src/styles/theme/radius.css:16` defines `--radius: 0.625rem` (~10px) as the GLOBAL base. Glass tiers do
NOT render that radius — `.glass-resting` live-resolves `border-radius: 16px` (`/display/buttons`,
measured), `.glass-floating` reads `--radius-card`, the stamp reads `--radius-pill` (999px). §2e writes
`clip-path: inset(0 round var(--radius))` — clipping to **10px** while the box is rounded to **16px / card
/ pill**. Result: the clip RE-SQUARES the corner at a TIGHTER radius than the visible border-radius → a
visible inner notch / corner mismatch on every tier, the exact jaggy-corner artifact §2e claims to fix,
inverted. §2c's comment "each tier reads its OWN corner token" is contradicted by the literal code, which
reads ONE global token. This is an overfit-to-the-spike defect: the spike (golden/spike.html:104,137,145)
hardcodes per-element radii (`--radius-md`, `--radius-card`) and never exercises the `var(--radius)`
generalization — so the spike "de-risk" never tested the actual library-wide token the GOLDEN proposes.

**Hardening:** the clip radius MUST equal each tier's actual `border-radius`. Either co-vary via a single
`--glass-corner` token set per tier (and assert clip-radius ≡ border-radius in C6), or derive from the
computed `border-radius` — never a global `--radius`. The gate must measure clip-radius == visual radius,
not just "clip present."

## R3 (gate-not-born-RED on a sub-arm) — C6 partly born-GREEN; the corner halo is NOT universally RED at HEAD

The GOLDEN claims C6 is born-RED ("clip-count 0, square halo"). Clip-count 0 is true (grep verified: 0
`clip-path` in ladder.css). But C6's GREEN condition is "every glass tier corner rasters CLEAN (no square
saturate halo)" — and on HEAD many tiers ALREADY raster clean corners because the `::after` grain uses
`border-radius: inherit` and the backdrop saturate is mild. A pixel-diff "square halo present" assay at
HEAD will be RED on SOME tiers (the vivid floating/overlay band) but GREEN on others (the quiet/wash band
where the halo is sub-threshold). So C6 is not uniformly born-RED — it is born-RED only where the GOLDEN's
fix is even needed, which means the self-test "remove the clip → C6 RED" will FALSE-PASS the fixed tree
into thinking the clip is load-bearing everywhere when it is load-bearing nowhere on the quiet band (and
DESTRUCTIVE on the vivid band per R1). The born-RED claim is overstated; the gate needs a per-tier
breakdown, not a library-wide boolean.

**Hardening:** C6 must (a) enumerate WHICH tiers show the square halo at HEAD (the born-RED set), (b)
restrict the clip to exactly that set, (c) assert the shadow-survival of R1 on every tier it touches.

## R4 (KISS/DRY drift) — `--chrome-t` is NAMED as the keel but NEVER MINTED; the mechanism is actually three disjoint timelines

The GOLDEN's headline is "ONE `--chrome-t ∈ [0,1]` scalar drives everything." But §2b/§2d and the spike
implement it as THREE separate `animation-timeline` declarations (the shrink, the `::before` bar-in, the
subordinate-fade), each with its OWN `animation-range`, plus a document-timeline entrance — and `--chrome-t`
appears in ZERO lines of CSS (grep: `--chrome-t` defined nowhere in src/ or demo/; not even in the spike).
There is no single scalar; there are 3+ scroll-driven animations that happen to share a range. That is
fine mechanically (it IS the shipped `.story-hero-shrink` substrate, which is genuinely deft reuse), but
the GOLDEN's central "ONE scalar" narrative is aspirational, not implemented — a DRY claim the mechanism
doesn't honor. If "one scalar" is the selling point, mint an actual `@property --chrome-t` registered
custom-property driven by ONE `scroll()` timeline, and have the bar opacity / shrink / fade READ it via
`calc()`. Otherwise drop the "one scalar" framing — it's marketing over a 3-animation reality.

**Hardening:** either mint a real registered `--chrome-t` (the honest single source), or rename the idea
to "one scroll RANGE, three positionally-paired animations" and stop claiming a scalar that doesn't exist.

## R5 (Safari correctness gap, NOT covered) — `@property`-less `--motion-weight` in the slam keyframe + the sticky `::before` z-index trap

Two WebKit-specific risks the §4 ledger asserts "identical" without proof:

(a) §2d's `header-slam` does `scale(calc(1 - .06*var(--motion-weight)), …)` inside a keyframe. If
`--motion-weight` is NOT a registered `@property` with a `<number>` syntax, Safari has historically failed
to interpolate `calc()` with an unregistered custom-prop inside `scale()` mid-keyframe (it resolves at the
0%/100% stops only, or drops the animation). The motion-spring GOLDEN owns `--motion-weight` as a DEPENDS —
but the page-chrome gate must ASSERT it is registered, or the cel-slam silently no-ops in Safari (a
binding-law CARTOON-PUNCH failure, the very audacity the law demands). The §4 "identical Chrome/Safari"
row is unverified for this.

(b) §2b mounts the placard as `.story-hero-shrink::before { z-index: -1 }` on a `position: sticky`
ancestor. A negative-z `::before` on a sticky element that also establishes a stacking context (the
shrink has `transform` via the condense animation → a stacking context) will paint the `::before` BEHIND
the sticky element's own background but can clip/occlude unexpectedly under WebKit's sticky compositing.
The spike uses `position: sticky` + `z-index: -1` + `inset: 0 -1.5rem 0` (golden/spike.html:76,101) and
the GOLDEN's own "layout note" admits the spike over-extended — i.e. the spike did NOT cleanly validate
the real chassis geometry. The §4 claim "verified static in the spike" covers the BLUR being static, not
the z-order being correct in the real full-route-width chassis.

**Hardening:** register `--motion-weight` as `@property <number>` (assert in C9); add a paired-WebKit
born-RED→GREEN raster of the `::before` bar at the REAL chassis width (not the spike's narrow column) to
C3, proving the bar paints behind title+stamp and in front of the body, in Safari, both modes.

## R6 (a11y / PRM hole) — `prefers-reduced-transparency` is asserted as "falls to opaque tier" but the chrome's NEW surfaces aren't wired to it

§4 claims "the bar + toc + stamp fall to the library's opaque tier" under reduced-transparency. But the
NEW surfaces are: `.story-hero-shrink::before` (a fresh `::before`), `.toc-placard`, `.story-header-stamp`
— none of which exist yet, so none are wired into the shipped `prefers-reduced-transparency` fallback
(which lives in a11y-fallback.css / glass-fx.css and targets the `.glass-*` CLASSES). The `::before`
backing and the stamp set `backdrop-filter`/`background` directly, NOT via a `.glass-*` class that the
fallback overrides — so under reduced-transparency they will KEEP their translucent backdrop while the
rest of the library goes opaque. That is an inconsistency + a legibility risk (a translucent bar over a
busy aurora when the user asked for no transparency).

**Hardening:** the bar `::before`, the stamp, and the toc placard must compose the actual `.glass-*`
classes (so they inherit the shipped reduced-transparency + forced-colors fallbacks) OR explicitly add a
`@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)` opaque arm to each. Add a C-row asserting all three new
surfaces go opaque under reduced-transparency.

## R7 (overfit ref) — the stamp glyphs `▸` / `⌘` are arbitrary, semantically wrong, and untested cross-font

§2f hardcodes `▸` for import and `⌘` for route. `⌘` (Place of Interest / Command key) has NO semantic
relation to "demo-only route" — it reads as a macOS command-key, a category error for a path label. It is
also a font-coverage gamble (the stamp is `fira-code`; `⌘` U+2318 is NOT in Fira Code's standard glyph set
→ tofu/fallback box on many setups). This is an overfit decorative choice with no grounding in the iOS-27
reference (which uses NO such glyphs on its breadcrumb).

**Hardening:** drop the glyph-as-kind-marker; encode the kind via the already-present `data-kind`
attribute + a TEXT or color/opacity treatment (the §2f `[data-kind="route"] { opacity:.86 }` already
carries the distinction visually). If a marker is wanted, use a guaranteed-coverage form (a CSS-drawn
chevron/dot, not a Unicode codepoint gambled against Fira Code coverage). Assert glyph-renders (width > 0,
not tofu) if kept.

## WHAT SURVIVES (the fit core — keep it)

- The **register unification** (condensed bar + toc = ONE `--chrome-glass`, mounted twice) is genuinely
  DRY and deft — it reuses `.glass-floating`/`.glass-menu-row`, no fork. KEEP.
- The **chrome/hero SCALE SPLIT** (`--chrome-title-rung` vs the protagonist `heroScale`) is correct and
  born-RED (109.66px live-measured, verified). The split (not a global shrink) is the right call. KEEP.
- The **occlusion-kill** (`::before` warm-glass backing that fades in on the shipped `scroll()` substrate)
  is the right fix for the real bare-text bug (`.story-hero-shrink` background-transparent verified). KEEP,
  modulo R5b z-order proof.
- The **`--chrome-rule` one-token-three-readers** + reuse of `--configurator-divider` (verified to exist,
  dark-adaptive, plain per-mode arms — NO light-dark inset trap) is exemplary DRY. KEEP.
- The **`themed-card` toc re-home** is justified: grep confirms `themed-card` has ZERO backing rule in
  src/ or demo/ — it paints nothing. Re-homing to `.glass-floating` is a clean break, not legacy. KEEP.
- **Punch-on-entrance / settle-on-scroll** is the correct §L4 altitude resolution; the no-overshoot
  scroll-condense is right (a bouncing scroll-nav reads cheap). KEEP.

## BOTTOM LINE

The GOLDEN is a real union, not a bolt-on, and 5 of its 6 mechanisms survive. But **§2e is destructive as
written** (R1 clips away the library-wide floating lift + iOS under-shadow — live-proven in Chrome; R2
clips to the wrong radius), and the born-RED claim for the corner arm is overstated (R3). These are not
polish — they are a library-wide regression shipped under a "one-liner corner fix" banner, which is the
exact anti-pattern the binding law (PERFECTED glass, defined edge, deft integration) forbids. The GOLDEN
SURVIVES only if §2e is re-specced to clip the backdrop/halo source WITHOUT touching the shadow-bearing
host, with a gate that proves shadow-survival. The remaining refutations (R4 honesty, R5 Safari, R6 PRT,
R7 glyph) are mandatory hardenings, not kills.
