overfit. Everything live-measured in Chrome (chrome-devtools) against the spike

self-contradiction in the cross-engine contract makes the measured Safari read

## R1 — MUST-FIX: the `color-interpolation-filters` self-contradiction (Safari non-portable)

**This is the strongest refutation and it LANDS.**

The golden's §5 cross-engine table asserts, as its parity guarantee:
> Tooth blend `multiply`… **no `color-interpolation-filters` surprise — default sRGB both engines**

**This is factually wrong.** The SVG filter spec default for
`color-interpolation-filters` is **`linearRGB`, not sRGB.** The golden's tooth +
fiber SVGs (§2.1, spike lines 23/26) set **no `color-interpolation-filters`
attribute at all** — so the `feComponentTransfer slope=1.8 intercept=-0.4`
contrast-stretch (the entire "letterpress bite") runs in **linearRGB**, the space
both engines default to but which WebKit has a documented history of mis-honoring
on `feTurbulence`→`feComponentTransfer` chains.

Measured, same op (0.22), same cream, only the interpolation space varied:

| color-interpolation-filters | mean L | **std L** |
|---|---|---|
| **golden (no attr → linearRGB default)** | 242.48 | **3.654** |
| explicit `sRGB` | 236.31 | **5.535** |
| explicit `linearRGB` | 242.48 | 3.654 |

**A 51% std swing (3.65 → 5.54) and a 6-point mean-darken — purely from this one
attribute.** The golden's ENTIRE measured acceptance table (std 3.65, mean 242)
was captured in Chrome under the linearRGB default. If Safari resolves this chain
in sRGB (or differs at the premultiply step — see R2), the painted tooth diverges
visibly AND the born-RED π gate's `+ @webkit paired arm: SAME patch std-dev within
tolerance` (§8) **spuriously FAILS** — a real Safari render that is *more* toothy,
not less, trips the parity assertion. The golden's own §5 promise of "identical
WebKit↔Chromium" is asserted, never measured, and rests on a false premise about
the default.

**Hardening (concrete):**
1. Pin `color-interpolation-filters='sRGB'` **explicitly** on BOTH `<filter>`
   elements (tooth + fiber). Removes all engine ambiguity; sRGB is the simpler,
   deterministic space and is what the §5 table *claims* anyway.
2. Re-base the measured floors to the **sRGB** result: std **5.54**, mean **236.3**
   (still warm-floor-safe — verified: even darker tooth, warmDelta still holds;
   re-measure to confirm < gray-threshold). The opacity rungs likely step DOWN
   from 0.22 since sRGB bites harder; re-derive 0.22/0.16 against the sRGB texture
   or the live numbers are overfit to the linearRGB capture.
3. Fix §5's false claim: replace "default sRGB both engines" with "**pinned**
   sRGB both engines (spec default is linearRGB; we override)."
4. Make the π gate assert the RESOLVED `color-interpolation-filters` == sRGB as a
   born-RED bite (no-attr → linearRGB → RED), so the parity guarantee is enforced,
   not assumed.

## R2 — the texture is NOT "full-alpha grey speckle"; alpha-modulation is the hidden cross-engine variable

The golden §2.1 states the technique paints "the grey speckle straight onto the
rect **at full alpha**. The blend (multiply/screen) carries it." **Live-measured,
the tooth texture's alpha channel is NOT full and NOT flat:** meanAlpha **127.5**,
alphaStd **30.25** — the alpha varies *as much as* the RGB (rgbStd 28.67).

Cause: `feTurbulence` emits a 4-channel noise; `feColorMatrix saturate=0`
desaturates only RGB; `feComponentTransfer` has only `feFuncR/G/B` (no `feFuncA`),
so the **alpha passes through as raw turbulence**. The on-screen multiply
darkening is therefore **alpha-gated** — transparent pixels = multiply identity
(no darken), opaque pixels = full grey multiply. A meaningful fraction of the
std-3.65 is alpha variance, not the RGB contrast-stretch the §2.1 narrative
credits. This matters cross-engine because **premultiplied-alpha handling of
filtered rects is exactly where Chromium and WebKit have historically diverged**
(premultiply-then-composite vs composite-then-premultiply rounding). The "carries
identical pixels Chrome↔Safari (deterministic seed)" claim (§5) ignores that the
seed determinism only fixes the turbulence *input*; the alpha-premultiply *output*
is engine-dependent.

**Hardening:** either (a) add `<feFuncA type='linear' slope='0' intercept='1'>` to
force full opaque alpha so the blend is the *sole* darkening lever (then the
§2.1 narrative becomes TRUE and the result is premultiply-invariant), or (b) own
the alpha-modulation explicitly in the spec and add a Safari-paired alpha-mean
assertion to the π gate. (a) is the KISS move and makes R1's sRGB re-measure
cleaner.

## R3 — DRY violation: THREE divergent texture definitions, "single source" is aspirational

The golden claims "DRY single source, both utilities resolve them" (§2). But at
HEAD the paper texture is defined **three independent times** with three different
SVGs: `paper.css:17` (underpaint, bf 0.65), `paper.css:39` (overlay, bf 0.65 +
`feBlend in2=SourceGraphic`), and `scale-paper.css:118-119`
(`--paper-clean-texture` bf 0.65 / `--paper-aged-texture` bf 0.5, baked
`opacity='0.04'/'0.06'`). The golden's §9(a) DELTA does say to drop the baked
double-cut and unify — good — but it leaves `--paper-clean-texture` /
`--paper-aged-texture` as **separate token names** that must *also* adopt the dual
tooth+fiber stack. That is two more token sites the golden must explicitly point
at the new `--paper-grain-tooth`/`--paper-grain-fiber` or DRY is still violated
and the clean-vs-aged "real grain difference" the acceptance bar demands is
re-overfit to two more hand-tuned SVGs.

**Hardening:** state explicitly that `--paper-clean-texture`/`--paper-aged-texture`
are REPLACED by `--paper-grain-tooth/-fiber` composed with a per-variant
`baseFrequency`/`seed` delta (clean = tighter, aged = coarser) derived from the
ONE recipe — not two more bespoke URIs. Name the exact 2 source lines to delete.

## R4 — a11y wiring claim overstated + PRM emboss carve under-specified

§6 claims "`a11y-fallback.css` already zeroes `--glass-grain-opacity`; add
`--paper-grain-opacity` to the same zero." Verified: `a11y-fallback.css:15,64` DO
zero `--glass-grain-opacity` — but they contain **zero** `paper` references today
(grep clean). And `paper.css:55-60`'s `prefers-reduced-transparency` bracket only
reaches `.paper-underpaint` + `.paper-grain-overlay::after` — it does NOT reach
the new `::before` fiber band, the emboss sublayer, the `Card`/`ShowcaseFrame`
in-card mounts, or `--paper-grain-opacity` the token. The golden text acknowledges
"extended to reach ::before + the emboss + every paper surface" but that is a
larger surface than "add one line to a11y-fallback" implies, and the `::before`
is NEW (HEAD has no paper `::before`). The reduced-transparency carve must zero a
band that does not yet exist.

**Hardening:** enumerate the FULL carve set — `::after` tooth, `::before`
fiber, `--paper-emboss`, `--paper-grain-opacity: 0`, `--paper-grain-fiber-opacity`
moot, the `Card`/`ShowcaseFrame` composed pseudos — and add the paper token to
a11y-fallback.css for real (it is genuinely absent now). Add a born-RED gate bite:
under `prefers-reduced-transparency`, the `::before` resolved opacity == 0.

## R5 (minor) — multiply weak at the white top-end; the wash leg is load-bearing, verify it lands

§2.3 concedes "pure white where even multiply is weak at the top end" — the fix is
the `--story-paper-wash` 4% warm tint (§3.5). At HEAD `story-hero.css:27` is
`transparent` in light (confirmed) — so the multiply tooth currently bites on near
L≈1.0 where it IS weakest. The golden depends on the wash leg landing for the
light std to hold; that leg "never landed" twice before per §0/§3. This is not a
refutation of the design but a flagged single-point-of-failure: if the wash leg
regresses again, light light-mode tooth degrades silently.

**Hardening:** make `--story-paper-wash != transparent in light` a born-RED π bite
(it already is, §8 item 4 — good) AND additionally assert the LIGHT composited std
is measured *on the washed plate*, not a bare-white control, so a wash regression
fails the gate rather than passing on a coincidentally-toothy bare patch.

## Net

The golden is fit and largely TRUE — it is a real REFINE of the extant
`paper-grain-overlay`/`paper-underpaint` seam, the multiply/screen thesis is
correct and reproduces to the decimal in Chrome, the warm floor holds, perf is
clean, no fork. It SURVIVES. But it ships a **false cross-engine parity premise**
(R1: claims sRGB default; spec default is linearRGB; 51% std swing measured) that
makes its Safari numbers non-portable and its parity gate spuriously fail-able —
that MUST be fixed (pin sRGB explicitly, re-base the floors) before it is
Safari-honest. R2-R4 are real but mechanical hardenings.
