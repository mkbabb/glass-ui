# Challenge #3 — DESIGN-FIDELITY / GESTALT lens (paper-morphism GOLDEN)

Verdict: **SURVIVES — but WOUNDED.** The headline metric is honest and replicates
independently (light std 3.66 vs claimed 3.65; dark 3.83 vs 3.86; warm floor holds,
no gray). The mechanism (multiply/screen + contrast-stretch grey-speckle) genuinely
clears the JND floor where the shipped overlay/soft-light arms cannot. BUT the golden
trades its own gestalt for its own metric in the one place it warned against ("the
gestalt is the bar, not the metric") — the "coarse anisotropic letterpress tooth" it
sells does not exist at the chosen parameters — and it carries three concrete factual
errors about the extant ecosystem it claims a DEFT UNION with. None is fatal; all need
hardening before this is GOLDEN.

Method: read GOLDEN.md + all 3 brainstorm lenses + the spike HTML; independently
re-measured `golden/spike-proof.png` patches (pngjs, content-free plates) and the tooth
frequency math live in Chrome (chrome-devtools); grepped the real `src/` seams the
golden names.

## R1 (TOP REFUTATION) — the "COARSE ANISOTROPIC TOOTH" is a fiction; bf 0.16 is still FINE speckle, and it is NEITHER coarse NOR anisotropic in the actual render

The golden's entire §0/§1/§7 diagnosis rests on one claim: *"Coarse grain survives
hiDPI; fine grain does not"* (§0.2), and its fix is the *"coarse anisotropic tooth …
carries ~80% of the percept … the headline visibility fix"* at `baseFrequency 0.16 0.21`.

Two independent measurements refute this:

- **It is not coarse.** `bf 0.16` → feature period **6.3 user-px** (≈12.5 device-px at
  DPR 2). The OLD condemned texture was `bf 0.65` → 1.5px. So the "coarse tooth" is only
  ~4× coarser than the speckle it ridicules as "TV static" — and is itself firmly in the
  fine-speckle band. Lens-C — which the golden claims to "take the strongest move from" —
  proposed `bf 0.012–0.018` → a **55–83px** period (~3 cycles/tile), a genuinely coarse
  structural band. The golden **silently abandoned lens-C's coarse band, substituted a
  ~10× finer 0.16, and KEPT lens-C's "coarse tooth survives hiDPI" rhetoric.** The stated
  causal story ("the old grain failed because it was fine; mine works because it's coarse")
  is false: the golden's tooth is also fine. It passes std≥3 because of the multiply/screen
  blend + the slope-1.8 contrast stretch raising AMPLITUDE — not because of coarse STRUCTURE.
  On a 3× panel the 6.3px feature is ~2 device-px and will downsample toward grey by the
  golden's OWN argument.

- **It is not anisotropic.** Measured horizontal/vertical neighbor-difference ratio over
  the grained plates: **0.75 (light) / 0.81 (dark)** — i.e. isotropic, and faintly
  *vertical*-biased, the opposite of the claimed grain direction. The `0.16 0.21` delta is
  a 1.31× frequency ratio: far too small to read as directional cockle. The "grain
  direction / felt / the thing a thumb feels" (§1) is simply not in the pixels. It reads as
  a uniform fine stipple (visible in `spike-proof.png`).

- **The "two bands a golden octave apart so they never beat" is false.** Tooth 0.16 :
  fiber 0.62 ≈ **1 : 3.9**, not the claimed 1:φ⁴ (≈1:6.85). Both bands are fine; there is
  no coarse band bracketing the percept. The Aristotelian-proportion claim (§2.5) is
  decorative, not real.

**Why this matters for the lens:** the acceptance bar is *"tactile pressed paper you could
run a thumb across … the clean-vs-aged panels show a REAL grain difference."* A directionless
6px stipple at 1.5% modulation is not that — it is the JND-floor squint the golden's own
epigraph forbids ("If you have to squint, it FAILS"). The golden satisfied the METRIC (std)
while abandoning the GESTALT (coarse, directional, tactile tooth), which §0/§7 explicitly say
is the bar.

**Hardening:** either (a) actually ship a coarse band — drop the tooth to `bf 0.03–0.05`
(≈20–33px period, survives DPR 3) as the structure-carrier and keep the 0.16 band as the
*mid* detail and 0.62 as fiber (a true THREE-octave bracket), re-measuring std on the coarse
stack; OR (b) deliver real anisotropy with a genuine axis ratio (e.g. `0.05 0.16`, a 3.2×
delta) so the cockle direction reads ≥1.4 in the H/V test; AND (c) strike the "coarse / grain
direction / 80% of percept" language until the measurement supports it. Re-prove on a 3× DPR
emulation, not just the author's 2× panel — the golden's whole hiDPI argument demands it.

## R2 — std 3.66 is the squint FLOOR, not "plainly visible at arm's length"; the acceptance bar and the gate floor are mismatched

The golden sets the gate at `std ≥ 3.0` (the JND *floor*) and the spike lands **3.66 light**
— a 22% margin above *just-barely-noticeable*. JND is by definition the threshold where a
trained observer in a controlled A/B *can* detect a difference — it is the antithesis of the
prose bar "PLAINLY VISIBLE … WITHOUT squinting … at arm's length." A surface at 1.5%
luminance modulation (mean 242, std 3.66) is exactly the "I can tell it's there if I look for
it" register, i.e. the squint. The golden conflates "clears the JND gate" with "satisfies the
no-squint user bar"; these are different thresholds and the spike sits at the lower one.

Lens-C, by contrast, targeted **effective ΔL ≥ 4–5 L units** explicitly *because* JND-floor
is a squint. The golden quietly relaxed that to the bare floor.

**Hardening:** raise the gate to `std ≥ 4.5` (light) to encode the no-squint bar, not the
detection floor, and re-calibrate opacity to hit it (the multiply headroom exists: mean only
moved 247→242, the warm floor has room to ~235 before the legibility concern in §6 bites).
Add a SECOND assertion the metric can't game: a coarse-band power check (dominant spatial
period ≥ ~20px) so a fine-speckle-at-high-amplitude texture cannot pass the way the current
one does.

## R3 — three FACTUAL errors about the extant ecosystem it claims a "DEFT UNION" with (the bolt-on tell)

The golden's KISS/DRY/no-fork claim is undercut by named-but-wrong extant seams — the kind of
error that makes a "union" land as a bolt-on against a misremembered host:

1. **`a11y-fallback.css` does not exist.** §6 asserts *twice*: "`a11y-fallback.css` already
   zeroes `--glass-grain-opacity`; add `--paper-grain-opacity` to the same zero." There is no
   such file in `src/`. The real PRM/reduced-transparency carve lives in **`paper.css:55-59`**
   (`@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce) { .paper-underpaint, .paper-grain-overlay::after { opacity: 0 } }`)
   — and it does NOT touch `--glass-grain-opacity` at all, nor `::before`, nor the wash, nor
   `--paper-grain-opacity` the token. The golden's a11y hardening instruction points at a
   phantom file and a carve that isn't there.

2. **The library-fence GATE asserts the wrong value.** §8 item 5 + §2.3 + §9 assert
   `--glass-grain-opacity == 0.025` as the single fence value. But the token is **0.025 in
   light (`glass-fx.css:17`) and 0.045 in DARK (`dark-arm.css:224`).** A π asserting a flat
   `== 0.025` either false-fails dark mode or (if it only samples light) silently fails to
   fence the dark glass grain — defeating the cardinal "glass stays calm" guarantee it is
   built to protect. The golden never checked the dark token.

3. **The "feBlend muddies to near-black" delete is half-applied at the source.** §2.1/§9(a)
   say drop the `feBlend mode='multiply' in2='SourceGraphic'`. That op IS in
   `paper.css:17,39` — correct. But the *sibling* tokens the golden also folds in
   (`--paper-clean-texture` / `--paper-aged-texture`, `scale-paper.css:118-119`) DON'T have
   the feBlend (they're already clean turbulence with baked `opacity='0.04'/'0.06'`). The
   golden conflates the two texture families; the "drop the double-cut" instruction (§2.4)
   applies only to the scale-paper pair, the "delete feBlend" only to paper.css — stated as
   one move, they're two different files with two different defects.

**Hardening:** correct every extant reference: PRM carve is `paper.css:55-59` (extend it to
`::before` + `--paper-grain-opacity:0` + the wash there, NOT a nonexistent a11y-fallback.css);
the fence gate must assert `0.025 (light) / 0.045 (dark)` per-mode (or assert the dark token
explicitly); and split the texture-cleanup instruction into its two real files. Re-grep the
host before claiming DEFT UNION — a union must be against the actual tree.

## R4 — the born-RED claim for the fence gate (§8 item 5) is unfalsifiable as written / not honestly born-RED

The golden lists "`--glass-grain-opacity` lifted off 0.025 → RED" as a born-RED self-test.
But at HEAD `--glass-grain-opacity` is ALREADY off 0.025 in dark (it's 0.045) — so either the
gate is already RED at HEAD for a reason unrelated to the paper work (a dishonest red), or the
gate only samples light and can never catch a dark-fence regression. A fence gate that is
structurally blind to half the modes it fences is not a real gate.

**Hardening:** the fence assertion must be `light==0.025 AND dark==0.045` (the real frozen
values), born-RED only by a CHANGE to those, decoupled from the paper grain work — so it
proves "I didn't touch glass," which is its job. As written it conflates "fence held" with a
wrong constant.

## R5 — the §3 "paper felt THROUGH glass / colourful field" claim is asserted, never de-risked; the spike proves only opaque plates

The boldest gestalt promise (§3.4, §7) is that the grain reads THROUGH the translucent glass
tint over the §3 colourful field — "paper felt through glass, the duality the system's identity
rests on." The spike (`paper-grain-spike.html`) tests ONLY opaque cream/ink plates with a
`mix-blend-mode: multiply` `::after`. It does NOT test:
- multiply/screen grain composited OVER a `backdrop-filter` glass tile (does the blend isolate
  correctly inside the glass `isolation: isolate` stack, or does it bleed/cancel against the
  backdrop layer?),
- the grain reading through a translucent tint over a SATURATED aurora field (multiply over a
  colored backdrop darkens toward the hue — does the warm floor still hold, or does the grain
  pick up the field's chroma and violate NO-GRAY / NO-tint?),
- Safari at all (the golden's §5 cross-engine table is all reasoning, zero WebKit pixels; the
  multiply-on-backdrop-filter compositing path is exactly where WebKit isolation differs).

So the single most identity-load-bearing application is the LEAST proven. "Both engines perfect"
is a reasoned claim, not a measured one.

**Hardening:** add a second spike: `paper-grain-overlay::after` (multiply/screen) on a real
`.glass-material` tile over a saturated aurora gradient, measured in BOTH Chrome and a WebKit
build (or at minimum Playwright-webkit), asserting (a) std≥floor through the glass, (b) patch
mean-chroma stays in the warm-floor band (no field-chroma pickup), (c) the WebKit std within
tolerance of Chrome. Until then §3 is a hypothesis, and the gate's `+@webkit paired arm` is a
TODO, not a result.

## R6 (minor) — the emboss PUNCH is the only "cartoon FLOW & PUNCH," it's opt-in/default-off, and it's unproven

The binding law demands cartoon-punch FLOW with weight. The golden's entire punch budget is the
opt-in `--paper-emboss` raking-light `::before` (§4.3), default **0** = zero motion everywhere by
default. So the shipped-by-default paper register has NO cartoon energy at all — it's a static
stipple. That's defensible for grain (crawling grain = nausea, §4.1, correct), but it means the
"1940s technicolor PUNCH" claim is carried entirely by an off-by-default flourish that the spike
never exercises (the spike has no pointer, no `--specular-angle`, no emboss). The mask-keyed-to-
`--specular-angle` mechanism is plausible (the vars exist: `glass-specular-track.css:44-52`,
`material.css:104-156`) but unproven, and §4.3's `opacity` calc mixes the fiber band and the
emboss into ONE `::before`, meaning when emboss engages the fiber band's blend (`soft-light`) is
shared with the emboss highlight — a blend-mode collision the golden doesn't address (emboss
wants `screen`/`overlay` for a lit highlight, fiber is `soft-light`).

**Hardening:** prove the emboss in a pointer spike (it's the only PUNCH); and separate the emboss
onto its own sublayer/blend rather than overloading the fiber `::before` — one pseudo can't carry
two blend modes. Confirm the default-off static register still satisfies the "elevate §L4 toward
universal" punch edict via the host card's spring/shadow (§4.2 asserts this but the grain spike
can't show it).

## What SURVIVES (steelman — credit where due)

- The **core mechanism is right and honestly measured.** Independent re-measure of the proof:
  light grained std **3.66** (claim 3.65 ✓), dark **3.83** (claim 3.86 ✓), bare controls **0.00**
  (✓). The overlay/soft-light → multiply/screen + contrast-stretch swap genuinely moves a
  near-white pixel where the shipped arms cannot. The lens-A luminance→alpha matrix critique
  ("zeroing RGB gives meanAlpha 0.1, invisible") is a real, correctly-diagnosed bug.
- **Warm floor holds, no gray:** meanRGB [243,242,240] light, [42,38,35] dark — R>G>B both modes,
  the BA.W-NO-GRAY floor stands. The `saturate 0` grey-speckle modulating only luminance is the
  correct no-tint move.
- **Cross-engine reasoning is sound** for the parts it covers: static data-URI, no
  `backdrop-filter:url`, multiply/screen/soft-light are sRGB Baseline, deterministic seed. The
  isotropic-bf fallback is a real WebKit hedge.
- **PRM static-grain-stays / motion-gated-emboss** is the correct a11y posture (a still texture is
  not a motion hazard; the punch is the enhancement).
- It is genuinely a token/utility-seam union, not a new system — the `paper-grain-overlay` /
  `paper-underpaint` / `--paper-grain-opacity` / `Card :grain` / `ShowcaseFrame :grain` seams all
  exist (verified) and the golden re-points them rather than forking.

The design is FIT at the mechanism layer; it is WEAK at the gestalt layer (R1/R2 — fine isotropic
speckle at the squint floor, sold as coarse directional tooth) and SLOPPY at the union layer
(R3/R4 — three wrong extant references + a half-mode fence gate). Fix R1–R4 and it is golden;
ship as-is and the user will again say "I see grain if I squint, but it's not paper."
