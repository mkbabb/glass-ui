# Challenge 1 — CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS lens

Default: REFUTED until proven. Live-tested in Chrome (chrome-devtools-mcp) against
the spike + the real `/foundations/paper-texture` route, both blend arms
reproduced numerically from the actual data-URIs.

**Verdict: SURVIVES, but with one band that paints nothing and two real defects to harden.**
The central thesis (overlay/soft-light self-cancel on the poles → swap to
multiply/screen) is CORRECT and live-reproduced. The gate is genuinely born-RED.
But the "two-band letterpress" gestalt is, in practice, a ONE-band system, and the
opt-in PUNCH has a pseudo-element collision.

---

## What I verified TRUE (the golden's spine holds)

- **HEAD is genuinely born-RED.** Live on `/foundations/paper-texture`:
  `--paper-grain-opacity: 0.08`, overlay-blend resolved on `.paper-grain-overlay::after`,
  `--story-paper-wash: transparent`, `--paper-grain-tooth` empty. Replicating
  HEAD's overlay-on-cream numerically → **std 0.0** (overlay on L≈0.98 is exact
  identity). The born-RED claim is real, not theatre.
- **The corrected light tooth reproduces.** multiply tooth on cream @0.22 →
  **mean 242.5, std 3.66** (golden claims 3.65). PASS ≥3.
- **The corrected dark tooth reproduces.** screen tooth on warm ink @0.16 →
  **mean 38.1, std 3.86** (golden claims 3.86). PASS ≥3. Chroma spread ~7.5 —
  the warm ink shows through, NOT gray. Warm floor holds.
- **The diagnosis is unanimous and matches the shipped CSS** (`paper.css:43`
  overlay light / `:49` soft-light dark on the OVERLAY utility — exactly the
  self-cancelling path). The seam is a real UNION, not a fork: same token name,
  same `@utility`, internals replaced. No legacy, no parallel system.

## TOP REFUTATION — the FIBER band is DEAD WEIGHT (paints nothing perceptible)

The headline gestalt (§1) is "paper as a TWO-BAND letterpress material (tooth +
fiber)." I measured the fiber band in isolation, on the real poles, from the
golden's own data-URI + its own opacity ladder (`0.22*0.55` light, `0.16*0.55`
dark), `soft-light` as specified:

| fiber `soft-light` | std L |
|---|---|
| on cream (LIGHT mode) | **0.0** |
| on warm ink (DARK mode) | **0.40** |
| on mid-grey (for reference) | 0.63 |

**The fiber contributes std 0.0 in light and 0.40 in dark — far below the JND
floor of 3.0 it itself set.** This is the EXACT failure mode the golden indicts
the shipped design for: soft-light self-cancels on the luminance poles. The
golden diagnosed this for the tooth, swapped the tooth to multiply/screen — then
**reintroduced the identical bug in its own "second band"** and never measured it.
The §8 acceptance table measures only the COMBINED stack ("multiply + soft-light
fiber"), so the fiber's null contribution is hidden inside the tooth's 3.65.

Consequence: the "two-band, golden-octave-apart, never-beat" letterpress story is
marketing — in delivered pixels it is a ONE-band tooth system. The fiber is a
second `::before` pseudo, a second feTurbulence data-URI decode, a second tile
token, and a `--paper-grain-fiber-opacity` token, all for **zero perceptible
signal**. That is a KISS/DRY violation: an entire layer that paints nothing.

This does NOT sink the golden (the tooth alone clears the bar both modes), but it
falsifies the stated gestalt and adds dead complexity.

**Hardening:** EITHER (a) delete the fiber band entirely — the tooth carries the
percept, ship one band, honor KISS; OR (b) if the pulp-mottle second band is
wanted, give the fiber a blend that BITES on the poles too. The honest pair is
the SAME physically-true law: fiber `multiply` (light) / `screen` (dark) at a low
opacity, NOT soft-light. Then re-measure the fiber IN ISOLATION and put that row
in §8's table — the acceptance bar must prove EACH band clears its own floor, not
hide a null band inside the tooth's pass. Recommend (a): one band is fitter.

## REFUTATION 2 — §4.3 emboss and §2 fiber COLLIDE on the same `::before`

§2 puts the FIBER on `::before` (`mix-blend-mode: soft-light`, full coverage).
§4.3 puts the raking-light EMBOSS *also* on `::before`, masked to
`linear-gradient(var(--specular-angle), white, transparent 60%)`. One pseudo
cannot be both. When `--paper-emboss:1` engages, the `-webkit-mask-image` clips
the `::before` to the directional gradient → the FIBER mottle is destroyed
(masked away on the dark side) the instant the emboss turns on. The §4.3 code
block even folds the two opacities into one `calc()` on the same element,
confirming the conflation. You get fiber XOR emboss, never both, and the
transition between them mangles the fiber.

**Hardening:** the emboss needs its OWN third sublayer (a real third pseudo is
impossible — only `::before`/`::after` exist). Options: (i) move the emboss to a
child `<div>`/`<span>` the utility owns (breaks the "pure ::after/::before, no
extra wiring" claim), or (ii) drop the fiber (per Refutation 1) and let `::before`
be the emboss-only layer, masked. (ii) is the clean union: ONE tooth `::after`,
ONE emboss `::before`. This also resolves Refutation 1. Strongly recommend the
two refutations be hardened TOGETHER: tooth=`::after`, emboss=`::before`, no fiber.

## REFUTATION 3 — the acceptance numbers were NEVER measured on the live route

§0 claims live sampling on all four routes, but every PASS number in §8 comes from
`paper-grain-spike.html` (a canvas rasterization in a throwaway file), NOT from
the composited `mix-blend-mode` output of the real route. I confirmed the spike
reproduces — BUT canvas `globalCompositeOperation='multiply'` is sRGB-premultiplied
and is NOT bit-identical to CSS `mix-blend-mode: multiply` against a real stacked
backdrop with `isolation: isolate`, sub-pixel `background-size` re-rastering at
the live DPR, and the wash `::before` underneath. The std is plausibly close, but
"MEASURED · PASS" in §8 overstates: it is "measured on a canvas proxy." The
born-RED π in §8 must read the REAL painted pixels (screenshot/`drawImage` of the
live node), or it will green on the proxy while the route could drift.

**Hardening:** the `proof:paper-morphism` π must rasterize the LIVE route node
(e.g. screen-capture the card bbox, or `html-to-image` the actual `::after`),
not re-derive the std from a standalone canvas. Add a row "measured on
`/foundations/paper-texture` live, both modes" to §8 before claiming PASS.

## REFUTATION 4 — §6 "the σ is sampled on empty plate, NOT under glyphs" is misleading

The `::after` tooth is `inset:0` + `z-index:2` (spike) → it composites OVER the
card's text, not only the empty plate. I measured multiply tooth over dark body
text rgb(42,37,32): mean 36.8 (vs 42 base). It is BENIGN (multiply toward already-
dark is small), so contrast holds — but the §6 framing ("not under glyphs") is
factually wrong: the grain IS painted under glyphs; it is simply harmless there
because the text is dark-on-light. State the true reason.

**Hardening:** reword §6 to "the multiply tooth composites over glyphs too, but
on dark-on-light text the darken is <5 L and never crosses the WCAG floor —
measured." Don't claim it isn't there; claim it is harmless and prove the worst
case (light text on dark glass, where screen LIFTS — measure that the lift
doesn't wash out light glyphs).

## Minor / watch

- **Anisotropic `baseFrequency='0.16 0.21'` Safari fallback is asserted, not
  tested.** The golden hand-waves "if a WebKit two-value delta appears, fall back
  to isotropic 0.18." No Safari run exists (I am Chrome-only here; no WebKit in
  this env). The two-value `baseFrequency` IS in the SVG spec and WebKit honors
  it, but the golden's own escape hatch (`0.18` isotropic) changes the tile period
  vs the φ-stepped 132px claim — verify the fallback STILL clears std 3.0 on
  WebKit before shipping the conditional. The §5 "reads identically" row is
  unproven for the anisotropic case.
- **`--paper-grain-tooth` slope-1.8 intercept-(-0.4) contrast-stretch clips both
  ends** (values <0 and >1 are clamped). That is INTENDED (the bite), and it
  raises std — but it also means the texture is sensitive to `color-interpolation-
  filters`. Default is sRGB both engines (good), but if any ancestor sets
  `color-interpolation-filters: linearRGB`, the stretch shifts. Pin
  `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"` on the `<filter>` explicitly (currently
  absent) for cross-engine determinism — cheap insurance.
- **DRY win is real but the underpaint dark arm changes semantics.** `paper.css:26`
  underpaint dark = `soft-light`; golden moves it to `screen`. That is correct
  (soft-light cancels on ink) and IS the union — just flag it as a behavioral
  break in the wave so consumers relying on the soft underpaint know.

## Net

The golden's SPINE is sound and live-reproduced: the overlay→multiply / soft-light→
screen swap is the right, physically-true, cross-engine-Baseline fix; the gate is
born-RED for real; the warm floor and no-gray hold; it is a genuine UNION on the
landed token seam, no fork, no legacy. It SURVIVES.

But it ships a dead fiber band (std 0.0 light / 0.40 dark — the very self-cancel
it indicts), an emboss that collides with that fiber on a shared `::before`, and
an acceptance table measured on a canvas proxy rather than the live route. Harden
by collapsing to ONE tooth `::after` + ONE emboss `::before` (delete the fiber, or
give it a biting blend and prove it in isolation), re-base the π onto live-route
pixels, and fix the §6 wording. None of these sink it; all of them are KISS/
correctness debts the golden currently hides inside the tooth's pass.
