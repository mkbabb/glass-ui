# CHALLENGE 3 — DESIGN-FIDELITY / GESTALT lens (page-background GOLDEN)

> Verdict: **does NOT survive intact.** The born-RED truth is real (independently re-measured:
> `/forms/select` light page `rgb(251,250,248)` = **C 0.00286 @ L 0.985 H 84.6**; 0 fields, no
> `--field-h`; dark page flat charcoal L 0.146 — all confirmed live). The warm-field IDEA is sound
> and the static visual reads warm-not-gray. But the GOLDEN's load-bearing de-risk claims are FALSE
> on live inspection, and three precept claims (transmit-delta, type-invariant warm-clamp, "adopt an
> existing primitive") do not hold. The design direction survives; the specific GOLDEN as written,
> and especially its "de-risked LIVE on real painted pixels" framing, does not.

All findings below are live-measured on `localhost:5173` via chrome-devtools-mcp, captures in
`./spike-live-light.png` (the spike, vivid rung) and `./spike-live-dark.png` (real `/forms/select`
dark — flat charcoal, born-RED confirmed).

## R1 (TOP / FATAL) — the spike does NOT measure composited painted pixels; its own panel reads RED/TEAL; the §2d "live-measured" numbers are fabricated

The GOLDEN's spine is the §1 promise: *"the numbers below are NOT asserted; they are the spike's
canvas-raster readout"* and §0/§8 *"de-risked LIVE on real painted pixels."* **Both are false.**

- `spike.html sampleField()` does NOT raster the field. It does `getComputedStyle(field).backgroundImage`,
  regex-extracts the **gradient stop strings**, resolves each to rgb via a probe `<span>`, and returns
  an **alpha-weighted mean of the STOP COLORS**. A 6%-radius vivid amber stop and the 120%-coverage
  pale wash count **equally** — the exact opposite of an area-composite. The author's own comment
  (lines 146-152, 156-158) admits a true raster "is impossible cross-origin-free" and that this is an
  "estimate," while the GOLDEN headline sells it as a raster readout.
- I re-ran the spike's verbatim `sampleField()` live. It returns **meanC 0.1164**, and the panel
  prints **"field meanC: 0.1164 … field meanH: 265.3 ⚠ TEAL … VERDICT: RED — pale or cool."** So at
  the default h62 the spike's OWN verdict is **RED and TEAL** (the hue average wraps to 265 = navy).
- The §2d table claims substrates(h62) = **0.073**. The spike outputs **0.1164**. Containers(h70)
  claims 0.077, forms(h48) 0.066 — **none of these match any number the spike actually produces.** The
  §2d "the spike's canvas-raster readout … the binding numbers" is not traceable to the spike at all.
- I attempted a true composite raster (SVG `foreignObject` → canvas → `getImageData`); it **taints the
  canvas** (`oklch()` data-URL = cross-origin) and throws `SecurityError`. This is *why* the author
  fell back to stop-strings — and it means the §3 floor (mean OKLab C ≥ 0.045 over the **composited**
  field) has **never been measured on this mechanism in any browser.** The de-risk is vapor.

**Hardening:** delete the §2d table and the "canvas-raster readout" framing. Re-de-risk with a REAL
composite measurement: a Playwright/CDP **full-page screenshot → `getImageData`** of the field region
(the only path that escapes the taint), area-averaged, not stop-averaged. State the honestly-measured
ground-rung floor; if it caps below 0.045 in a gamut-bound mode, state the achievable number (lens-b's
own discipline, which the GOLDEN cites then violates). Fix the spike's `meanH` to ignore the conic
wraparound so the teal-detector stops false-flagging its own warm field.

## R2 (FATAL) — the transmit-DELTA (G5, gestalt bar #2, the user's actual complaint) is NEVER de-risked; the spike's A/B control is invalid

The user's #1 complaint is **gray glass** — glass that doesn't transmit. The single most important
thing to de-risk is therefore G5: *"the same glass control over the real field differs measurably from
the same control over a flat plate."* The spike claims to do exactly this (`glassA` vs `glassB.flat-plate`,
"the A/B: same control over flat cream"). **It is methodologically broken:**

- `.flat-plate { background: var(--neutral-0) !important; }` changes only **glassB's own surface
  background**. glassB still sits over the **same `.paper-field` fixed plane** as glassA (I confirmed:
  both render over the one `#field` element). `backdrop-filter: blur()` samples what is BEHIND the
  element — which is the warm field for **both** pills. There is no flat-page region anywhere in the
  spike.
- Live confirmation: in `spike-live-light.png` the "field-warm" pill and the "flat-plate control"
  pill are **visually identical** warm-cream capsules. The A/B that is supposed to prove the morphism
  shows zero delta — because it's comparing a thing to itself.

So the one mechanism the whole GOLDEN exists to rescue (glass transmitting a vivid field) is the one
mechanism the spike does **not** test. G5's "composited C ≥ 0.018 warm" is asserted, never observed.

**Hardening:** the A/B must place two real glass controls over two real *backgrounds* — one over the
warm field, one over a sibling element that masks the field with an opaque `--neutral-0` patch behind
the glass (so the blur samples cream). Measure the delta on a screenshot raster. Until that delta is
shown ≥ the floor in BOTH engines, G5 is unproven and the gestalt bar #2 cannot be claimed.

## R3 (STRUCTURAL / "NO LEGACY, DEFT UNION" precept) — the three "adopt an existing primitive" claims are false; `paper-field`, `--glass-key`, `--ease-cartoon-punch` do NOT exist in src

The GOLDEN's entire union framing (§1, §3, §7) is *"ADOPT verbatim, then harden … the library glass
material stays BYTE-UNTOUCHED … the net-new artefacts are exactly THREE … everything else is a
re-point of a shipped seam."* Live grep of `src/`:

- **`@utility paper-field`** — does NOT exist. `src/styles/paper.css` contains only `paper-underpaint`
  and `paper-grain-overlay`. `grep -rn "paper-field" src/styles` = **zero hits.**
- **`--glass-key`** (the "EXISTING" cel key-light that "field + glass rim/cast share") — zero hits in
  `src/styles`.
- **`--ease-cartoon-punch`** (DEPENDED, "not duplicated") — zero hits in `src/` or `demo/`.

All three exist ONLY inside **sibling greenfield SPECS that have also never been built**
(glass-material GOLDEN, cartoon-shadow/motion-spring greenfields). So this GOLDEN does not "adopt a
shipped primitive and harden it" — it **builds paper-field net-new** while *describing* it as an
augment, and it takes a hard **build-order dependency on two other unbuilt greenfields** (glass-material
for `--glass-key`, cartoon-punch for the easing). The "three net-new artefacts" count is wrong: it's
the whole `paper-field` utility + the key-light token + the easing token + the script + the prop. The
"byte-untouched / re-point a shipped seam" claim is contradicted by its own §2b ("AUGMENT `@utility
paper-field`" — you cannot augment a thing that does not exist).

**Hardening:** state honestly that `paper-field` is **net-new** (or land glass-material FIRST and make
this wave a true augment with a hard `depends:` edge). Provide a real fallback for `--glass-key` and
`--ease-cartoon-punch` that does NOT assume the sibling greenfields ship (the spike already hardcodes
`-58deg` and a literal cubic-bezier — so admit the dependency is unmet and these are local defaults,
not "depended-from-a-sibling"). Re-count the net-new surface honestly.

## R4 (PRECEPT — the teal-purge "type invariant") — the warm-clamp is a TS convention, not a paint guarantee; an inline `--field-h:210` paints teal

§1.1 sells the headline safety property: *"a cool field is unrepresentable … the §E purge as an
invariant, not a gate-hope."* Live test: `clampWarm` is a **JS function in the button handler only**.
`--field-h` reaches paint as a CSS custom property. I set `document.documentElement.style
.setProperty('--field-h', 210)` (exactly what §2e's "chassis writes `--field-h` inline per route"
would do if a category map had a bad value) → computed `--field-h` = **210**, unclamped, and the
field paints teal. CSS `@property { syntax: "<number>" }` **cannot** range-clamp; there is no paint-layer
guard. The "unrepresentable" claim is false — it's the same runtime convention the GOLDEN claims to
have eliminated. (G3 catches a teal *render* at gate-time, but that is a gate-hope, precisely the thing
§1.1 says it replaced.)

**Hardening:** either (a) clamp in the CSS calc itself —
`--field-h: clamp(25, var(--field-h-raw), 95)` so paint is hard-bounded regardless of writer — or (b)
downgrade the prose: it is a *registry convention enforced by a runtime assert + the gate*, not a type
invariant. Don't claim a guarantee CSS can't deliver.

## R5 (DE-RISK VALIDITY) — the spike measured the VIVID rung (1.0); the universal GROUND rung (0.6) was never de-risked, and the GOLDEN can't agree what it is

The field mounts on all 118 routes at the **ground** rung; `<Aurora field>`/hero lifts to vivid. The
floor must be cleared at GROUND, universally. But:

- §1 + the spike comment say ground intensity = **0.6**. §2b CSS says ground default = **0.85**. The
  GOLDEN contradicts itself on its single most load-bearing scalar.
- The spike **loads at `--field-intensity: 1` (VIVID)**. Every §2d number and the light screenshot I
  judged were taken at the hero rung. At ground 0.6 every stop alpha drops ~40% (0.95→0.57, 0.50 wash
  →0.30), so the **universal** field is materially paler than anything "measured." §2 already concedes
  the glass-material stops landed *below* 0.045 in light; the calibration fix was tuned and shown only
  at vivid. The rung that actually ships everywhere is the one never tested against the floor.

**Hardening:** pick ONE ground intensity, re-measure the §3 floor at THAT rung (composite raster, R1),
in both modes, both engines. If ground 0.6 can't clear 0.045 through an AA-legible stack, either raise
the ground default (and re-check prose AA, §L5/G8) or state the honest ground floor. The vivid-rung
numbers are irrelevant to G2's universal claim.

## R6 (CROSS-ENGINE — Safari, unverified) — §4's "byte-comparable" claim is asserted, never run; the one true-raster path taints in WebKit too

§4 is all reasoning, no WebKit capture (§4 itself admits "must be re-run in Safari … before
build-close" — so it is NOT de-risked now, contradicting §0's "de-risked LIVE both modes"). Concrete
WebKit risks the prose waves past:

- **oklch gamut-mapping differs Chrome↔WebKit.** The whole calibration sits *at the gamut edge*
  (C 0.145/0.155 at L0.85-0.87). WebKit's gamut-map can desaturate edge-of-gamut oklch differently than
  Chrome's, so the floor that *barely* clears in Chrome may not in Safari — the §3 floor is per-engine
  and unmeasured in one engine.
- **sRGB-vs-oklab interpolation.** §4 claims default interpolation is sRGB and "byte-comparable." But
  gradients between two oklch stops interpolate in **oklab by spec default** in both engines unless
  `in srgb` is stated — and the spike states no interpolation space. The claim "no `in oklab` hint
  WebKit handles differently" is backwards; the default IS oklab. Interpolation parity is unverified.
- The true composite-raster path (foreignObject) **taints in WebKit as well**, so the binding π that
  §6 mandates ("getImageData of the field region") must come from a Playwright **WebKit screenshot**,
  not in-page canvas — the gate spec doesn't say how it escapes the taint in WebKit, and the de-risk
  never proved it can.

**Hardening:** run the spike in a real WebKit (Playwright `webkit`), capture both modes, measure the
composite floor per-engine. Pin the gradient interpolation explicitly (`in oklch` or `in srgb`) and
re-measure both engines so the mesh is actually byte-comparable. Demote §0's "de-risked LIVE both
modes/engines" to "Chrome-only, WebKit pending" until the WebKit capture exists.

## R7 (GESTALT / CARTOON-PUNCH) — the drift is de-risked nowhere; "liquid-weight" is one slow alternate-transform, and the punch easing is a fallback literal, not the real spring

Binding law demands cartoon FLOW & PUNCH (anticipation, overshoot, follow-through, real weight) on ALL
motion. The field drift (§2c) is a single `translate/scale/rotate` `alternate` over **42s** with a
`cubic-bezier(.5,-0.2,.4,1.2)` **fallback literal** (the real `--ease-cartoon-punch` doesn't exist,
R3). At 42s `alternate`, the bezier's overshoot is imperceptible — a 42-second easing curve reads as
linear to the eye; there is no felt anticipation→follow-through, just a slow imperceptible pan. None of
the §8 gestalt-bar #5 "drift swings with inertia, never linear" is de-risked — the spike's drift is
visually static in a screenshot and the GOLDEN provides no motion capture. "Liquid-weight universal" is
asserted, not shown. (Also: a heavy fullscreen `::before` with `background: inherit` + `will-change:
transform` re-rasterizing five gradients + a conic is not as "free" as §1 claims — conic gradients are
notably expensive to composite-animate; perf is unverified.)

**Hardening:** capture the drift as a GIF/trace and judge the punch live; shorten the period or stage
the keyframes so anticipation/overshoot are *perceptible* (a 42s linear-reading pan is not cartoon-
punch). Land `--ease-cartoon-punch` (depends-edge) instead of a baked literal, or own the literal as
the real curve. Profile the conic `::before` animation for compositor cost before claiming "free."

## R8 (OVERFIT / GOLDEN-PROPORTION) — the per-route hues and stop geometry are hardcoded magic numbers with no √φ / golden-ratio derivation

Binding law: Aristotelian golden-ratio proportion *in all things*. The `FIELD_SCRIPT` (62/48/70/38/
82/58/66/52/74/62) and all stop positions (`24% 20%`, `78% 30%`, `80% 88%`, radii `100% 84%`, `95%
78%`…) are **bare magic numbers** with zero φ/√φ derivation and no rationale beyond "looks warm." Two
categories collide (foundations 62 = substrates 62), so the "no two routes share a default" claim
(§1) is already false in the registry. This is an overfit constant table, not a proportioned system.

**Hardening:** derive the stop geometry from a golden-ratio grid (key-mass at the φ point, fill-bounce
at 1−1/φ, etc.) so the proportion law is satisfied and auditable; de-collide foundations/substrates;
justify the hue spacing or derive it from an evenly-warm partition of [25,95].

## What SURVIVES

- The **born-RED truth is real and independently re-measured** (light C 0.00286; dark charcoal L 0.146;
  0 fields; no `--field-h`). G1/G3/G4 are genuinely born-RED structurally. The gate is not a paper one.
- The **core idea** — one warm `--field-h`, two renderers, mount on the existing `<PaperBackdrop>` at
  `AppShell.vue:251`, Aurora as amplifier not a fork — is genuinely DRY and the right union shape.
- The **static warm visual reads warm, not gray** in light (the amber/peach field is real), clearing
  gestalt bar #1/#6 *as a still image at the vivid rung*.
- The reconcile seams it points at (`CATEGORY_DEFAULT_BG` @ `manifest.ts:181`, `heroAuroraConfig`,
  `useGlassBackdropLuminance`, `useIntersectionPause`) **do exist** — the integration targets are real.

## Net

The design DIRECTION survives; the GOLDEN AS WRITTEN does not. Its "de-risked LIVE on real painted
pixels" is the central false claim (R1): the spike measures stop-strings not composites, outputs RED/
TEAL at its own default, the §2d numbers match nothing it produces, the transmit-delta (the user's
actual complaint) is tested by an invalid self-comparison (R2), and it was all measured at the wrong
(vivid) rung (R5). Fix R1+R2+R5 (real composite raster of the GROUND rung, a valid flat-page A/B, both
engines) before this can be called de-risked.
