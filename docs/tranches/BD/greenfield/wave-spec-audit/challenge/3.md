## TOP REFUTATION — the "ONE globally-divergent gestalt violation" (§4) is undercounted ~4×

§4 is the GOLDEN's *only* concrete, falsifiable design-fidelity verdict: "THE ONE GLOBALLY-DIVERGENT
GESTALT VIOLATION — TEAL/NAVY… **5 categories carry COOL `sectionHue`**" (substrates 3, forms 2,
containers 9, navigation 11, scenes 4), and it declares this "**the sole globally-divergent row**."

Cross-checked against disk reality:
- The 11 heroes assigned in `demo/stories/category-hero.ts` use indices `{7,3,2,5,9,11,6,1,8,12,4}`.
- Their OKLCH hue angles (`src/styles/tokens/color-radius.css §251-263`):
  `1 purple=305.9 · 2 indigo=265.5 · 3 teal=222.8 · 4 forest=171.1 · 5 amber=69.6 · 6 tomato=30.4
  · 7 violet=317.5 · 8 ruby=8.4 · 9 slate=239.6 · 11 ocean=208.0 · 12 periwinkle=291.9`.
- The GOLDEN's **own** warm band is `H∈[25,95]`. Membership: **only amber(69.6) and tomato(30.4)
  are IN band. NINE of eleven heroes are OUT.**

So by its own clamp definition the divergence is **9 heroes, not 5** — the GOLDEN silently drops
violet(317.5, the root category), purple(305.9, ledger), periwinkle(291.9, constellation), and
ruby(8.4, status — below 25). It calls this "the sole globally-divergent row." It is not sole and
it is not five. The consequence is structural, not cosmetic: the proposed `BD.W-SECTION-HUE-WARM-FENCE`
born-RED proof only RED-checks `222.8/239.6/208.0/171.1` (§4) — so even if the fence ships and goes
GREEN, **violet/purple/periwinkle/ruby remain unclamped**, and the gestalt "NEVER gray, warm-cream,
warm floor" edict is still violated on the root/ledger/status/constellation categories. The headline
design fix is scoped to ~half the actual problem.

(Caveat I held myself to: violet/purple are arguably *intentional library-identity jewel-tones* —
`dark-arm.css §87` literally calls `--section-color-7`/violet "a LIBRARY-identity chroma." That is a
legitimate design position. But then the GOLDEN cannot ALSO claim a flat `H∈[25,95]` warm clamp as
the gestalt law without explicitly carving the identity hues out. It does neither — it asserts a
warm clamp AND undercounts the violators. Pick one and make it explicit.)

## REFUTATION 2 — PASS 2 ("the consistency gate") cannot detect any gestalt violation it names

The binding law lists the violations to hunt: *gray glass · naive ellipsoid · tight-springy · non-golden
proportion · missing cartoon-punch*. PASS 2 is sold as the machine-checkable rendering of that gate
("renders the consistency gate as a glance," §4; "a matrix cell," §0).

But PASS 2 is `re.test(a.text)` — **presence of a keyword in prose**. The GLASS axis is
`/\.glass-capsule|--glass-(?:bg-floor|tint|key)|warm.?floor/i`. A wave earns a GREEN GLASS cell by
containing the *string* "warm floor" — including in a sentence that says "we deliberately do NOT use
the warm floor," or one that forks its own gray capsule while name-dropping the canonical token. The
gate measures **vocabulary, not adherence.** It is structurally incapable of distinguishing CONSUME
from FORK, or of detecting gray glass, a naive ellipsoid, or a tight/springy curve — none of those
have a positive keyword the author would volunteer.

Corollary: PASS 2 has **zero negative-precept axes.** The entire negative-precept detection in the
GOLDEN is ONE literal in PASS 3 (`linear-gradient(-58deg)`), which fires **0**. So the GOLDEN's
machine reports "design consistency = green-where-owed / 167/259 cells" while having checked *nothing*
about gray-glass, ellipsoid-vs-metaball, spring stiffness, or √φ proportion. The "computed fraction,
not a vibe" (§6) IS a vibe — it is a word-cloud coverage score.

## REFUTATION 3 — every load-bearing CROSS-ENGINE / metaball design gate is prose, not machine-checked

§3a is the boldest design-fidelity material: "real blob↔meatball metaball merge, never a naive
ellipsoid," the waist gate `max_t S.ratio ≤ 0.45 ∧ hasLocalMinimum`, the tier ladder `|S−G| ≤ 0.10`,
the OWED Safari-26 capture "RED gate that BLOCKS GREEN." I grepped `audit.mjs` for every one of
these: `ellipsoid · smin · metaball · barbell · waist · S.ratio · 0.45 · hasLocalMin · Safari-capture
· getImageData · screenshot · 0.02 · chroma` → **all absent.** PASS 3's only Safari logic is
`hasArm = /§L7|Safari|WebKit|@supports.../.test(a.text)` — it checks the amendment *says the word*
"Safari," not that a frame was captured. So the GOLDEN's claim that convergence is "immune to the
fake-gate fraud… the structural twin of the paint-arm chroma π" (§0, §6) is exactly the fraud it
legislates against: a gate that passes on a string match over prose. The waist/parity/Safari gates are
asserted as RED-blocks-GREEN but **live only in the markdown**, unreachable by `audit.mjs`.

## REFUTATION 4 — the warm-floor "CODE not prose" promise is unfalsifiable by this mechanism

§3 / §4.1: "glass warm-FLOOR = CODE not prose — the real non-zero decl… painted-pixel gate." The
foundation gate greps src/ for `glass-capsule` → **0 hits, born-RED.** Good for tranche-DEV — but it
proves *absence*, never that the eventual decl actually clears chroma 0.02 in BOTH modes. There is no
hook in `audit.mjs` that will ever verify the floor's correctness; the painted-pixel arm is named but
not wired. So "CODE not prose" is, in the deliverable, still prose — the audit can go 2-consecutive-
clean with a gray floor shipped, because nothing reads a rendered pixel.

## HARDENING (concrete, in priority order)

1. **Re-derive §4 from the ramp, in code.** Replace the hand-typed "5 cool" list with a generated
   tally: read `category-hero.ts` indices → join to `color-radius.css` OKLCH angles → flag every hero
   with `H∉[25,95] ∧ C≥<accent-floor>`. Today that yields **9**, not 5. Either (a) the
   `BD.W-SECTION-HUE-WARM-FENCE` born-RED proof RED-checks all 9 angles (add 317.5/305.9/291.9/8.4),
   or (b) the GOLDEN explicitly carves the jewel-identity hues (violet/purple) out of the clamp with a
   one-line rationale and the fence covers only the remaining 7. State which. No silent 5.

2. **Add a real PASS-2 negative-precept lint band** (anti-keyword + structural), so the gate can fail:
   `gray-glass` = a glass amendment that declares a chroma-0 / pure-neutral floor OR omits any warm
   token; `naive-ellipsoid` = a morph amendment using `border-radius:50%`/`ellipse(` for the merge
   without `smin`/metaball/`feColorMatrix` goo; `tight-springy` = a motion amendment naming a spring
   with `stiffness > N` / `damping`-less / `cubic-bezier` instead of the `--ease-cartoon-punch`
   `linear()` anticipation leg; `non-golden` = a radius/proportion token off the √φ ladder. A GREEN
   today must come from *evidence the precept is honored*, not from the author having typed its name.

3. **Wire the §3a gates or stop calling them gates.** Either land the waist/parity/Safari-capture as
   artefact-checks `audit.mjs` reads (a committed frame-series JSON the linter asserts on, with the
   `S.ratio≤0.45 ∧ hasLocalMinimum ∧ |S−G|≤0.10` thresholds parsed), or demote them in the GOLDEN from
   "RED gate that BLOCKS GREEN" to "prose acceptance bar deferred to implementation." The current
   framing claims machine immunity the mechanism does not deliver.

4. **Make the foundation gate a correctness gate at build, not just an absence gate.** When the
   warm-floor decl lands, the gate must flip from "0 hits = RED" to an out-of-page `screenshot →

   Until that arm exists, drop the "CODE not prose / painted-pixel gate" claim from §3/§4.1; it is a
   promise the deliverable cannot keep.

5. **Stop reporting `green/total` as a convergence number.** 167/259 over presence-keywords is a
   coverage-of-vocabulary score, not a design-consistency fraction. Either gate it behind the negative
   lints (so a cell can be RED on a real violation) or label it "vocabulary coverage" and remove it
   from the convergence proof.
