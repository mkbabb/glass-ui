# CHALLENGE #1 — CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS

> Lens: idiom · deft-integration (union vs bolt-on/fork/legacy) · KISS/DRY · gate-born-RED ·
> overfit/hardcoded · cross-engine · precept compliance. Default: REFUTED.
> Verified against HEAD source + the golden's own spike captures (2026-06-24).

VERDICT: **DOES NOT SURVIVE CLEAN.** The gestalt (glass-is-a-relationship; field + edge + transmit)
is correct and the integration is genuinely deft in *shape*. But the golden ships a **falsified
calibration** (its own spike fails the headline bar by 2.3×), **mis-describes two of three legs as
missing when HEAD already implements them**, and **the born-RED gate is prose, not code**. These are
landing-blocking, not cosmetic.

---

## R1 (TOP REFUTATION) — THE SPIKE FALSIFIES THE HEADLINE BAR. The golden is born-RED against ITSELF.

§3/§5/§10 set the load-bearing acceptance number: **composited C ≥ 0.018 warm** ("the field bleeds
through"; acceptance criterion #2; gate arm F2). The golden's OWN spike, the artefact cited as
"live-verified, both modes" (§7), prints in its readback (read from the committed captures):

| capture | plate OVER FIELD | golden bar | plate OVER FLAT ("the gray read") |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spike-light-tuned.png` | **C 0.0077** H 78.2° | ≥ 0.018 | C 0.0013 |
| `spike-dark-tuned.png`  | **C 0.0051** H 69.6° | ≥ 0.018 | C 0.0018 |

The field read (0.0077 / 0.0051) **misses the bar by 2.3×–3.5×**. Worse, in **dark** the
field-vs-flat delta is 0.0051 vs 0.0018 — both far below 0.018; the "transmissive lift" the spike
claims to prove is ~0.003 of chroma, sub-perceptual, and on the wrong side of the golden's own line.
The spike does not de-risk the boldest mechanism — it **demonstrates the mechanism does not clear the
bar the spec writes around it.** §7 hand-waves this ("the exact bar is a CALIBRATION the live gate
tunes against the real painted composite") — but that is an admission the headline number is unproven
and possibly unreachable with these field-α/plate-α values. A born-RED gate at 0.018 over a mechanism
that tops out at 0.008 is a gate that **stays red after the work lands** — the source-green/visually-
broken hole the golden claims to close, re-opened in the opposite direction (gate-stuck-RED).

Note also the spike's readback is itself a **fiction**: it does NOT sample the painted field
(`spike.html:202-206` hardcodes `fieldSample = {252,244,232}` / `fieldDark = {32,27,22}` and comments
"the live composited field is sampled by the real gate; here we read the plate fill over a
representative warm-field sample"). So even the 0.0077 is an *optimistic synthetic*, not a real
composite. The real painted composite is plausibly lower still.

**HARDENING:** Either (a) re-derive the bar from a REAL painted-composite spike (sample the actual
`.paper-field` pixel behind the plate, not a hardcoded triple) and set the gate to the value the
mechanism *actually* reaches at acceptable α — likely ~0.010–0.012, not 0.018; OR (b) raise field
chroma / lower plate-α until a real composite clears 0.018, and re-capture. The spec MUST NOT ship a
gate number its own de-risk artefact fails. Pick the number from paint, then write the gate.

## R2 — TWO OF THE THREE "MISSING/HELD" LEGS ALREADY EXIST IN HEAD. The delta-assay is wrong.

The golden's entire framing is "leg (a) landed; legs (b)+(c) HELD/MISSING." Two sub-claims are false
against HEAD:

1. **§4(A) premise — "The HEAD rim is `inset 0 1px 0 hsl(0 0% 100%/0.30)` — a flat omnidirectional
   halo."** FALSE. `src/styles/glass/rim.css:70-83` (BC.W-BLACK-BAR D2) already made the rim
   **DIRECTIONAL**: a bright `--glass-rim-top` catch-light + a grounding `--glass-rim-bottom`
   under-shadow, accent-composed. The golden describes a rim two refactors stale. The "refine →
   directional" move is partially **re-inventing a landed seam** — exactly the fork the union law
   forbids. The genuinely-new part (conic key-coherence) is real, but the golden over-claims its delta
   and risks a parallel rim path next to the shipped directional one.

2. **§5 premise — `useGlassBackdropLuminance` is "luminance ONLY; the hue term is wired only on the
   dock."** FALSE. `src/composables/glass/useGlassBackdropLuminance.ts:112-148` (BE.W-AMBIENT-TINT) is
   a **general per-sample 12-bucket chroma×alpha OKLCh hue histogram** that ALREADY imports
   `srgbToOKLab, rawOklabToOklch` (line 49) and ALREADY returns `ambientHue` for every sample, with a
   `gray-room-tints-nothing` null identity. The golden's headline §5 add — "a `srgbToOKLab →
   rawOklabToOklch` dominant-hue read off the SAME sampled background" — **is already shipped**. The
   real gap is narrow (the dock is the only *consumer* that writes the hue to `--glass-ambient-hue`;
   the generalization is wiring existing output to more tiers), not the composable surgery §5 implies.

**HARDENING:** Re-write §4(A) to state the rim is ALREADY directional (rim-top/rim-bottom) and the
move is purely "make the existing directional rim KEY-COHERENT via one conic keyed off `--glass-key`,
re-using `--glass-rim-top/-bottom` as the lit/shade stops" — NOT a new rim. Re-write §5 to "the
ambient-hue histogram already exists and is general; the only new work is wiring its `ambientHue`
output to `--glass-ambient-hue` for non-dock tiers over the field" — delete the false "luminance ONLY"
claim. This shrinks the golden's true footprint (good — more deft) but the spec must say so honestly,
or an executor builds a duplicate.

## R3 — THE BORN-RED GATE IS PROSE, NOT CODE. F1–F4 do not exist; the spec has 5 tests, none touch the field.

§8 claims "Born-RED today on all four arms" and presents F1–F4 + a born-RED F2 sketch. But
`tests-visual/no-gray.spec.ts` (HEAD) contains **5 tests, arms (a)–(f), and ZERO references to
`paper-field`, `composite-over-real-field`, `defined-edge`, or `no-flat-glass`** (grep: 0 hits for
`paper-field`/`ancestor`/`no-flat`). The gate is **unwritten**. "Born-RED" is only meaningful if the
assertion EXISTS and FAILS today; an unwritten test is not born-RED, it is born-ABSENT.

Two of the proposed arms are also under-specified to the point of being non-executable:
- **F4 ("a `.glass-*` with no `.paper-field` ancestor is a FAIL")** — `.paper-field` is `position:fixed`
  at the document root (a sibling, NOT an ancestor) per §3 and the spike (`spike.html:155` the field is
  a body-direct sibling of `.stage`). So **no glass element will EVER have a `.paper-field` ancestor** —
  F4 as written fails 100% of elements forever, including post-fix. The arm tests the wrong DOM relation.
- **F2's sketch** queries `.glass-floating, .glass-card` and does `elementsFromPoint(...).find(paper-field)`
  — but a `z-index:-1; pointer-events:none` fixed field at document root is reliably returned by
  `elementsFromPoint` only if nothing opaque is stacked between; over a real page with an opaque
  `--neutral-0` body background the field may be occluded in the hit-stack. Unverified.

**HARDENING:** Actually WRITE the four arms in the spec and run them red BEFORE claiming born-RED;
attach the red run output. Fix F4 to test **sibling/backdrop presence** (e.g. `document.querySelector
('.paper-field')` exists AND composites behind the plate's bounding box), not `closest('.paper-field')`.
Verify `elementsFromPoint` actually returns the field through the body background, or sample the field
color by reading the fixed layer directly + compositing in JS rather than relying on the hit-test.

## R4 — KISS/PRECEPT: the field is `--neutral-0`-decoupled, but the §3 stops are GAMUT-RISKY and the
##      ambient-hue is DOUBLE-COUNTED with the field; transmit may over-warm or no-op.

- The golden mounts BOTH (b) a chromatic field AND (transmit) an ambient-hue lift that "lifts the plate
  TOWARD the field hue." But the ambient-hue sample reads the **field it sits over** — so the plate is
  warmed by (2) its own warm fill + (b) the field showing through the blur + (transmit) an ambient mix
  *of that same field*. That is the field's warmth counted up to **three** times into one plate. The
  0.018 bar may be reachable only because of this triple-count — which then risks **over-rotation** past
  the `WARM_HUE_HI` ceiling (the gate's own H-bound, 88°) on saturated section-accents. No analysis of
  the combined H under a non-default accent is given (§3 says the field derives from "ONE accent hue";
  a violet/teal section-accent + a warm ambient mix is unanalyzed — could land outside [45,88] and the
  gate's own hue arm fails).
- §3 dark field stops `oklch(0.34 0.07 58)` etc. — the spike's ACTUAL dark stops are `oklch(0.30 0.040
  58)` (lower C). The GOLDEN.md §3 values (C 0.05–0.075) are **higher than the spike that "verified"
  them** (C 0.040–0.045). So §3's "spike-calibrated values (live-verified)" are NOT the values the spike
  shipped. Another calibration mismatch between spec and de-risk.

**HARDENING:** State the warm-budget allocation explicitly: which leg owns how much ΔC, and cap the
**combined** plate-over-field-plus-ambient H to stay in [45,88] for ALL section-accents (clamp the
ambient mix, or skip ambient when the field is already at-bar). Reconcile §3's stop chroma to the
spike's actual values (or re-spike at the §3 values and re-capture). Add a non-default-accent gate case.

## R5 — CROSS-ENGINE: `mask-composite` + `@property` floors are asserted, not paired-π-verified; the
##      conic rim animation (§Motion "rim catch smears with pointer velocity") is the WebKit risk, not the static rim.

§6 claims "verified live in Chrome" and "Safari-safe by construction" but presents **no WebKit
capture** — the spike comment says "Verified live in Chrome AND Safari" yet only Chrome PNGs exist in
`golden/`. The static `mask-composite: exclude` border ring is indeed Safari-old-safe. The real
cross-engine risk the golden under-weights:
- **Animating an `@property <angle>` driving a `conic-gradient` from `calc(var(--glass-key)+90deg)`**
  per the MORPH-MORE-ON-MOVE law (§Motion: "the rim catch smears along the lit edge with pointer
  velocity"). Animated registered-custom-property conic gradients are a known WebKit repaint/jank
  surface (not compositor-promotable — a conic regenerated per frame is a main-thread paint). This is
  the opposite of the "compositor-only" claim the motion section makes. Unproven in WebKit; likely the
  first thing to drop frames on Safari.
- `cos()`/`sin()` in `box-shadow` offset (§4B) — CSS Values 4 trig; Safari support is recent (16.4+).
  Fine as a floor, but `@supports` not specified for the trig path; an older WebKit gets `box-shadow:
  0 0 ...` (no offset) = a centered cast = **no cel coherence** = the iOS-7 sticker the golden forbids,
  silently. No fallback named.

**HARDENING:** Attach a REAL WebKit paired-π capture (the §6 claim is currently a "live-verified"
inflation — the cardinal lesson). Move the velocity-smear rim to a **transform/opacity** channel, not an
animated conic (regenerating a conic per frame is not compositor-only — pick ONE static keyed conic +
animate a transformed catch-light overlay instead). Add `@supports (top: cos(1deg))` around the trig
cast with a directional-but-static `box-shadow` fallback so the cel coherence degrades to a fixed
offset, never to a centered halo.

## R6 (minor) — OVERFIT / HARDCODED: the field is bolted to the DEMO chassis only; the library glass
##      primitive STILL composites to gray in any consumer that does not mount `.paper-field`.

§3 is explicit: "It is a DEMO-CHASSIS contract (band-C scope), not a library-API burden. The library
glass primitives are UNCHANGED." Read adversarially: the golden's thesis is *glass-is-a-relationship,
no-glass-without-a-field, enforced by construction* — but it enforces that ONLY in glass-ui's own demo
chassis. A real consumer (slides, a downstream app) that renders a `<Card glass-resting>` over a flat
neutral page gets **exactly the gray read the golden diagnoses** — because the fix lives in
`AppShell.vue`, not in the material. The "enforced by construction" claim is true only inside this
repo's storybook. That is a demo-overfit: the gate greens on the demo while the shipped material is
unchanged and still gray-prone in the wild. (Per MEMORY overfitting-audit + presets-in-consumers this
is arguably acceptable — the field is a *preset* a consumer mounts — but then the golden must STOP
claiming "enforced by construction / a flat plate becomes impossible" as a material property; it is a
consumer responsibility, and the spec conflates the two.)

**HARDENING:** Either ship `.paper-field` as a documented, exported library utility a consumer is
REQUIRED to mount under any glass surface (with a console.warn in dev when a `.glass-*` paints with no
field behind it — the F4 precept as a runtime dev-assert, which would ALSO be the honest F4) — or
down-rank the "enforced by construction / impossible flat plate" language to "the demo chassis mounts
the field; consumers mount their own per presets-in-consumers." Do not claim a structural guarantee the
library does not provide.

## WHAT SURVIVES (fit, keep)

- The gestalt — **glass is a relationship (field + edge + transmit), not a color** — is correct and is
  the right root-cause for the live gray read. Keep it; it is the spec's spine.
- The **decoupling of the field from `--neutral-0`** (§1 leg b, lens-b's insight) is genuinely deft and
  DRY — solid chrome stays calm-neutral, glass transmits a separate fixed warm layer. This resolves the
  KEEP-NEUTRAL-vs-warm-page tension correctly without a token re-edit. Strong.
- The **`--glass-key` cel coherence** (one vector → rim + cast agree) is the right idiom upgrade and is
  CSS-native; the static form is cross-engine-safe. Keep the static keyed rim/cast (drop only the
  animated-conic velocity-smear per R5).
- Composing `paper-underpaint` + `auroraFallbackGround` static-mesh precedent is a real union, not a
  bolt-on — the idiom reuse is correct.

## NET

Survives as a DIRECTION; fails as a SHIPPABLE SPEC. The headline 0.018 bar is falsified by the golden's
own spike (R1), two legs are mis-scoped as missing (R2), the born-RED gate is unwritten and one arm
tests the wrong DOM relation (R3), the warm budget is triple-counted with no combined-hue analysis
(R4), the cross-engine claim is unverified and the motion path is not actually compositor-only (R5).
All five are concrete, source/capture-grounded, and landing-blocking.
