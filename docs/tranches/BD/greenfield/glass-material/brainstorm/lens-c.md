## 1 — THE CORE IDEA: glass is a TECHNICOLOR CEL — a lit lens over a painted field, edged in ink

A 1940s technicolor cel is three load-bearing strata, in order of read:
1. **a saturated PAINTED FIELD** (the gouache background — never a flat void; it is the *reason*
   the cel pops),
2. **a CHARACTER PLATE** lit by a *fixed* key-light (the warm body color + a bright specular
   catch on the lit edge),
3. **a hard INK OUTLINE + an offset CAST SHADOW** that detaches the character from the field and
   gives it cartoon weight.

Map that, exactly, onto the glass material — and the §3 three-fold fix falls out as the three
cel strata:

| cel stratum | glass §3 leg | the mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **saturated painted field** | §3.b COLORFUL FIELD (mandatory) | the warm-cream *paper underpaint* + a calm living tint-wash behind EVERY glass demo |
| **warm-lit character plate** | §3.a WARM glass (LANDED) + the key-light | the warm `--card` plate + a *fixed-key specular RIM-LIGHT* (the iOS-26 SOTA rim, angle-varying) |
| **ink outline + offset cast** | §3.c DEFINED EDGE | the cartoon-ink rim hairline + the §Shadows moving layered-offset cast |

**The thesis in one line:** *the glass was made warm; now make it a CEL — paint the field behind
it, light its edge with a fixed key, and ink-and-cast it so it lifts off the field with cartoon
weight.* Warmth was necessary; the **CEL composite** is what makes warmth READ as luminous
liquid glass instead of a pale wafer. This is the §3 mandate executed through the technicolor
lens, and it unions cleanly with the landed chroma work (I touch ZERO of the `--card` chroma
legs — I add the field, the key-light rim, and the ink-cast that the chroma legs were always
meant to sit inside).

## 2 — THE SINGLE BOLDEST MOVE: `--glass-key` — ONE fixed cartoon key-light vector, drives BOTH the specular rim AND the cast shadow, in lockstep, on EVERY tier

This is the move that makes it a CEL and not just warm glass, and it is the move that is *most
alive*: **promulgate a single global key-light direction token `--glass-key` (an angle, default
the cartoon top-left `-58deg` — the §Shadows fixed cel light source) and derive, from that ONE
vector, BOTH:**

- **(A) the specular RIM-LIGHT** — the iOS-26 angle-varying edge highlight. The rim's bright arc
  concentrates on the edge facing the key (top-left) and fades to the warm under-shadow on the
  opposite edge. Today `--glass-rim-top` is a flat `inset 0 1px 0 white/0.30` — a uniform halo.
  The bold move makes it **directional**: a `conic-gradient` (or a two-stop `linear-gradient`
  border-image) keyed off `--glass-key`, brightest on the lit edge (white/0.55 — a real catch),
  warm-amber on the shadowed edge. *This is the defined-edge §3.c floor AND the iOS-26 rim SOTA
  in one device.* The edge POPS because it is a lit edge, not a flat ring.

- **(B) the cartoon CAST SHADOW** — the §Shadows moving layered-offset cast, offset *opposite*
  `--glass-key` (down-right), warm-tinted (never neutral-gray — a gray cast on warm glass is the
  same no-gray sin one layer down). Today the drop shadow is a per-tier `--glass-shadow-*` blur.
  The bold move *re-bases* it on `--glass-key` so the cast and the rim share ONE light source —
  and the cast TRAVELS opposite motion (the §Shadows "moving cast", `transform` on a `::after`
  caster, compositor-cheap), so a card that springs in throws its shadow with cartoon weight.

**Why this is THE boldest move and not a flourish:** it is *coherent cel lighting*. A flat rim +
an independent drop-shadow is iOS-7 sticker. A rim and a cast *that agree on one key-light* is a
1940s cel — the brain reads it as a real lit object lifted off a painted field. It directly
delivers §3.c (defined edge) and the iOS-26 rim SOTA *simultaneously*, on the EXISTING six-layer
composite (it re-tunes layers 3 *edge rim* + 4 *catch-light* + 5 *drop shadow* to share one
vector — zero new layers, zero new compositing path). And it is the most ALIVE because the key is
*fixed* while objects MOVE: every spring, drag, press, and morph slides its lit edge and its cast
against the fixed light — overlapping action and follow-through fall out for free.

`--glass-key` is ONE token. It threads the existing rim/highlight/shadow tokens. It is the
cartoon-technicolor lens's keystone and the §3-defined-edge keystone, unified.

## 3 — THE FULL CEL SPEC (visual · motion · interaction · mechanism)

### 3.1 — Stratum 1: the PAINTED FIELD (§3.b, mandatory, the demo-chassis union)

The §3 mandate is *a colorful field behind EVERY glass demo*. The forms/foundation routes have a
flat page. The fix is NOT to tint the glass more (that re-fights the landed chroma work) — it is
to **paint the field**, two-tier:

- **Tier 1 — warm the PAGE itself (apply H1, now mandatory).** `--neutral-0` light
  `hsl(40 30% 98%)` → `hsl(34 44% 98%)` (OKLab H 84.6→~74, C 0.0029→~0.0045 — warm-cream, NOT
  yellow-green). Lockstep `color-radius.css` literal + `light-dark.css` arg + WIDEN (never
  delete) the `proof:no-gray` KEEP-NEUTRAL byte-assert. This stops the cool page from dragging
  the thin plates back toward gray (the trigger plate recovers from C 0.0089 above the floor).
  Dark `--neutral-0` is already warm (H 68.6) — keep.
- **Tier 2 — a CALM LIVING TINT-WASH behind every demo region (the chassis seam).** A
  compositor-only, PRM-static, *very low contrast* warm-amber → faint-section-hue radial/conic
  wash painted on the demo-page substrate behind the glass (the `auroraFallbackGround` static-mesh
  generalized — already shipping, see IOS27-REFERENCE T11 `W-LIVING-ARTWORK`). NOT a loud
  aurora; a *gouache underpaint* — enough chromatic variation that the glass blur has SOMETHING
  to bend, so the transmissive read finally reads. This is the load-bearing §3.b device and it is
  a DEMO-CHASSIS amendment (every storybook page gets the field by default), not a per-component
  hack. **The boldest-move rim then has a vivid field to catch — the cel snaps into focus.**

### 3.2 — Stratum 2: the WARM-LIT PLATE (§3.a LANDED + the key-light rim §2.A)

- The warm `--card` plate is **DONE** (triumvirate FIX-A/C) — untouched.
- ADD the directional `--glass-key` rim-light (§2.A). The rim is the §3.c defined edge AND the
  iOS-26 angle-varying specular. Per-tier intensity scales with the tier's existing
  `--glass-level` (wash = faint lit edge, overlay/dock = a bold catch). On the near-white page
  the lit edge (white/0.55 at the key) is what finally lifts the card off the field — the held
  H2 rim-α bump, *generalized* into a directional key-light instead of a flat α bump.
- **Saturate companion (FIX-D) is LANDED** (content 1.4, floating/overlay 1.6, deep 1.8) — with
  the painted field now behind (§3.1), the saturate finally has chroma to pull THROUGH the glass
  (the Maps-card transmissive read). FIX-D + §3.1 are the two halves of transmission; both now
  present.

### 3.3 — Stratum 3: the INK + CAST (§3.c defined edge + cartoon weight)

- **The cartoon CAST** (§2.B): re-base `--glass-shadow-*` on `--glass-key` (offset opposite the
  key, warm-tinted). Opt-in *bold* register for the hero/loud surfaces via the existing
  `.shadow-cartoon-{sm,md,lg}` / `<Card surface="cartoon">` (design.md §Shadows) — the default
  glass surface keeps the calm six-layer cast; the loud surfaces (hero card, dock, dialog) get
  the layered-offset technicolor pop. The cast is WARM, never neutral-gray (no-gray, one layer
  down).
- **The moving cast** (design.md §Shadows, elevate toward universal per the hardening plan): as a
  glass surface translates/presses/morphs, the cast slides opposite the motion scaled by
  `--motion-weight` (`transform` on a `::after` caster — NEVER animated `box-shadow`; §L7
  compositor floor). The lit rim brightens on the leading edge as it moves. This is the
  overlapping-action + follow-through that makes the glass feel like a weighted cel, not a sticker.

### 3.4 — MOTION (liquid-weight universal — the material's own animation)

The glass-material fix is mostly static tokens, but the cel comes ALIVE on motion, and it must
not regress the shipped liquid motion:

- **Entrance — the cel "pop-on" (squash & stretch, anticipation, overshoot).** Glass surfaces
  enter via the shipped `.glass-reveal` (scale + fade + `filter: blur→0`) on `--spring-bouncy`,
  AUGMENTED toward the graceful squish (≈0.88 vol-preserving, IOS27-REFERENCE T10
  `W-LIQUID-ENTRANCE-GENERAL`): a real anticipation dip then a ~22% overshoot via
  `--ease-cartoon-punch` for the LOUD surfaces (design.md §Easing — the loud register), the calm
  `--spring-snappy` for the default. The lit rim and cast track the squash: at peak stretch the
  cast detaches (the object lifts off its shadow), on settle it snaps back. This is the §L4
  anticipation/follow-through made universal, riding the cartoon-punch curve that already ships.
- **Press — the cel "press-into-field".** `--scale-press` (0.96) squish; the cast DEEPENS (object
  lifts) then the rim catch slides as the plate tilts toward the key. Shipped
  `useSpringPress`/`useLiquidFlex` reciprocal squish — composed, not re-forked.
- **Hover — the key catches.** On hover the lit-edge rim intensity lifts (the catch-light
  "concentrates" — design.md §L1 layer 4), a compositor-only `opacity`/`filter` cross-fade, PRM
  → static. The MORPH-MORE-ON-MOVE law: the faster the pointer, the more the rim catch smears
  along the lit edge (compose `usePointerVelocityField`, already shipping for the dock).
- **NO snapping, NO linear fade, NO hard swap** — every channel rides a spring + the cartoon
  punch curve, fade-coupled-to-transform, compositor-only.

### 3.5 — INTERACTION & the per-tier ladder (preserve separation)

The cel strata apply UNIFORMLY across the 7-tier ladder so monotone tier-separation holds
(design.md §Glass-Surfaces): the rim/cast intensity scale with the tier's `--glass-level` (wash =
whisper, chassis/overlay/dock = a bold lit edge + a deep cast). The deep tier keeps its
`saturate(1.8)` ceiling (the calm-vs-deep fence). NO tier reads gray; NO tier reads flat — every
tier reads as a *lit cel at its weight*.

## 5 — CROSS-ENGINE (Chrome + Safari, §L7) + A11Y / PRM carve

- **Cross-engine (§L7).** Every cel device is on the cross-engine base: `--glass-key` is a plain
  angle; the rim is a `conic/linear-gradient` border (Safari-native); the cast is `box-shadow` /
  `::after` `transform` (Safari-native); `backdrop-filter: blur() saturate()` is the warmth term
  (Safari since 9/14). NO `backdrop-filter: url(#…)` (the §L7 WebKit drop). The Chrome-only
  `.glass-lens` SVG refraction is the §7 progressive-enhancement *bonus* — NEVER load-bearing on
  the warm/edge read (the warm read survives in WebKit on the gradient-rim + cast + saturate
  alone). Acceptance is a PAIRED-engine π (Chromium AND WebKit) — the cardinal §L7 bar.
- **A11Y.** AA re-ratified after F1 (chroma-first, L holds — light 16.3:1 / dark 12.2:1 carry).
  The directional rim is decorative — text contrast rides the warm plate, unaffected. `prefers-
  contrast: more` → the rim/cast opacity floors UP (the inked edge is a legibility ASSET, per
  design.md §Shadows) — high-contrast users get a *more* defined edge, never less.
- **PRM.** `prefers-reduced-motion` → the moving cast goes STATIC (no travel, no punch), the rim
  catch stops smearing, the `.glass-reveal`/squash collapses to a fade — all shipped carves, the
  cel degrades to a *still cel* (a lit edge + a static cast — still a defined edge, just not
  moving). `prefers-reduced-transparency` → `--glass-level: 0` opaque escape; the now-warm
  `--card` is the solid fill (warm-cream, not gray), the rim/cast survive as the legibility edge.
  The painted field (F2) is PRM-static by construction (parked-when-hidden, no advection).
