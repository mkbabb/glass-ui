## REFUTATION 1 (TOP — DEFT INTEGRATION / KISS) — the re-platform DETONATES the `/motion-core` carve-out

**This is the one that lands.** `useScrollProgress`, `useScrollTrigger`, and `useScrollChrome` ALL
ship on the engine-free `/motion-core` barrel (`src/composables/motion/core/index.ts` exports all
three). That barrel's header is an explicit hard invariant:

> "AP.W3 R0G-7 — the engine-free core of the motion family. These leaves import `vue` ONLY (verified
> leaf by leaf); none statically reaches `@mkbabb/keyframes.js` or `@vueuse/core`."

The carve-out exists for a measured reason: the old `/motion` barrel "walked all twelve leaves as
one SCC, so a cheap consumer dragged the ~125 KB keyframes engine onto its eager graph through a
path that touches ZERO keyframes." The `scroll-system` demo page even advertises its subpath in the
header text I read live: **"`@mkbabb/glass-ui/motion-core`."**

The GOLDEN §1a/§2a plans to **"internal re-platform `useScrollProgress` onto `SmoothProgress`"** and
thread `SpringProgress`/`SmoothProgress` into `useScrollChrome` + `useScrollTrigger`. Every one of
those is a **static `import { SmoothProgress } from "@mkbabb/keyframes.js"`** — which **breaks the
keyframes-free invariant for THREE leaves simultaneously**, re-collapsing the exact SCC AP.W3 spent
a wave carving apart, and dragging 125 KB onto the dock-search consumer (`density.css:84`) and the
ToC tracker that reach these on `/motion-core` precisely *because* they're cheap.

The GOLDEN half-sees this — §2a says "threading kf physics moves it (or a thin physics shim) onto
`/motion`" and "the `/motion-core` floor degrades to the existing instant snap when the physics shim
is absent." **That is the refutation, not the answer:**
1. The "thin physics shim" **does not exist** and is never specified. Hand-waving a non-existent
   artefact is not KISS; it is the bolt-on tell.
2. "Floor degrades to instant snap" **directly contradicts the GOLDEN's own §3 edict** — "the JS
   spine is the floor for every FELT axis on both engines… Safari is never the degraded path for
   anything the user feels (byte-identical to Chrome)." The chrome collapse IS a felt axis (the
   GOLDEN itself tags it FELT in the §3 table). If `/motion-core` consumers get instant-snap and
   `/motion` consumers get the spring, then liquid weight is **NOT** default/identical — it forks on
   the import subpath. That is the precise "default-to-broken / parallel-fork" anti-pattern the
   GOLDEN claims to eliminate.

**HARDENING (REQUIRED):** Pick ONE and state it loudly, don't bury it:
- **(a) Relocate, own the break.** Move the now-physics-bearing `useScrollProgress` / `useScrollTrigger`
  / `useScrollChrome` to `/motion`, DELETE them from the `/motion-core` core barrel, and bump the
  dock-search + ToC consumers to `/motion` with a CHANGELOG/MIGRATION entry (the AP.W3 "NO back-compat
  alias, consumers rename per call site" precedent). This is a real wave cost the DELTA-ASSAY §5 must
  list as a PRUNE/RELOCATE row — it currently shows ZERO prune, which is now false.
- **(b) Keep `/motion-core` keyframes-free; physics is a peer-injected dependency.** `useScrollProgress`
  stays import-free; the SMOOTHER is passed in (`useScrollProgress({ smoother })`) and only the
  `/motion` `useScrollScene` wires a real `SmoothProgress`. Engine-free callers get the honest 1:1
  clamp (a documented floor), `/motion` callers get weight. This preserves the carve-out AND is DRY
  (one injected primitive), but the GOLDEN must concede the engine-free floor is **not** weighted and
  drop the "identical both engines on every felt axis" overclaim for the `/motion-core` chrome path.

Either is fine. Silently re-importing keyframes into three `/motion-core` leaves is not.

## REFUTATION 2 (CORRECTNESS / PORT-FIDELITY) — the spike `createScrollReader` is a re-inlined FORK, not the shipped one

The GOLDEN repeatedly says the production wave composes "the SHIPPED `createScrollReader` (the ONE
coalesced listener — reused)." But the de-risk spike (`golden/pin-spike.html:125-138`) **re-inlines
its own `createScrollReader(source, onTick)`** with a DIFFERENT shape than the real one: the spike's
reader exposes `{ position, extent, schedule }`; the shipped `scrollReader.ts` exposes
`{ target, position, extent, schedule, stop }` and resolves window/document sources. So "the
mechanism is proven on the shipped core" is **not** what the spike actually proved — it proved a
look-alike.

Worse, the spike's progress math is its OWN: `rawProgress()` reads `pinEl.getBoundingClientRect()`
and computes `-rect.top / (rect.height - source.clientHeight)`. The shipped reader gives you
`position()/extent()` (scrollTop / scrollHeight−clientHeight) — a *whole-document* ratio, NOT a
*per-pin-slice* rect ratio. Porting the spike onto the real reader is therefore **not a mechanical
lift**: the production `useScrollPin` must add per-element rect measurement (a `getBoundingClientRect`
per tick) that the shipped reader deliberately does not do. That is new measurement code, and on a
`position: sticky` stage inside a scroller, `rect.top` is read off the *pin container* (correct) —
but the GOLDEN's §1c also claims `createScrollReader` "resolves `main.demo-main-scroller`
automatically." **It does not** — I read `scrollReader.ts`: it resolves the *passed* source, else
window/document. There is no auto-discovery of `main.demo-main-scroller` anywhere in `src/`
(grep returns only a comment in `base-misc.css`). The caller must pass the scroller.

**HARDENING:** (1) Change every "shipped `createScrollReader`, reused" to the honest "the spike
inlined the reader IDIOM; the production `useScrollPin` composes the real `scrollReader.ts` and adds
a per-tick rect read for the pin-slice ratio." (2) Delete the false "resolves
`main.demo-main-scroller` automatically" — specify the explicit `source` the consumer passes (the
demo passes `main.demo-main-scroller`; the composable resolves nothing magic). (3) Re-run the spike
gate over the REAL `scrollReader.ts` before claiming "proven on the shipped core."

## REFUTATION 3 (KISS / OVERFIT) — the `damping` heuristic is an un-principled magic-number map, divorced from the `--scroll-scrub` token

The GOLDEN's whole liquid-weight story rests on ONE golden token: `--scroll-scrub: 0.62 (1/φ)`,
"the ONE knob; PRM → 0." But the spike maps scrub→damping with
`damping = 0.06 + (1 - scrub) * 0.14` (`pin-spike.html:144`) — two hardcoded magic constants
(`0.06`, `0.14`) with a comment admitting "scrub 0.5 → damping ~0.13 (a perceptible glide)." There
is no derivation tying `1/φ` to `0.06`/`0.14`; the "golden-proportioned scalar" is laundered through
an arbitrary affine map, so the Aristotelian-proportion claim evaporates at the only place it touches
real physics. Worse, `--scroll-scrub-pin: 0.50` is asserted "still 1/φ-family" — 0.50 is **not**
1/φ (=0.618) nor 1/φ² (=0.382); it is just 0.5. That is an overfit "golden" label on a round number.

**HARDENING:** Either (a) derive the damping from `--scroll-scrub` by a *stated* physical mapping
(e.g. scrub is the normalized lag time → damping = f(scrub) with f written once and justified), and
drop the two free constants; or (b) feed `--scroll-scrub` to `SmoothProgress`'s damping *directly* in
the units SmoothProgress already uses, with no affine fudge. And fix `--scroll-scrub-pin`: make it an
actual 1/φ-family value (0.382 if "heavier", since lower scrub = less lag = lighter — note the spike's
own comment says lower damping = heavier, so the polarity needs an audit too) or drop the "1/φ-family"
claim and call it what it is.

## REFUTATION 4 (CORRECTNESS / GATE-RIGOR) — R-SNAP overshoot is asserted but UN-spiked, and the chrome path is the one that just lost its engine

R-SNAP (§6.4) demands `collapseT` "overshoots then settles via spring (≥1 frame past the endpoint
then back)." `SpringProgress` *can* overshoot (it has `zeta`/`omegaD`) — but the GOLDEN's §2a/§4 also
say the chrome PRM/engine-free path must "degrade to the existing instant snap," and §1d says thread
the snap "through a `SpringProgress` snap so the bar settles with a spring." `SpringProgress.snap()`
is the *settle-instantly* method (it's in the proto). So the gate wants overshoot, the PRM arm wants
`.snap()`, and the `/motion-core` arm wants instant — three behaviors, and **none of them was spiked**
(the spike only proved the PIN, explicitly: "de-risks the load-bearing JS `--pin-t` mechanism only").
R-SNAP is an *assumed* GREEN with no captured artefact, which violates the Live-verify-capture law the
GOLDEN itself invokes in §6.

**HARDENING:** Add a chrome-snap micro-spike (`SpringProgress` with ζ<1, capture `collapseT`
crossing the endpoint and returning across ≥3 frames) before calling R-SNAP designable, OR demote the
overshoot to a NICE-TO-HAVE and let the snap be a critically-damped settle (ζ=1, no overshoot) so the
gate matches a behavior that's actually proven. Pick the curve, then prove it.

## REFUTATION 5 (CROSS-ENGINE / SAFARI) — the Safari floor is reasoned, NOT measured; R-SAFARI is an emulation, not WebKit

§3 + §6.6 claim the `--pin-t` writer is "byte-identical Chrome/Safari" and R-SAFARI runs "under a
forced no-`timeline-scope` / WebKit emulation." But every live capture in the spike + my own probe
ran in **Chromium**. A JS custom-prop writer is very likely fine on WebKit (it's transform/opacity +
`setProperty` — no `backdrop-filter:url`, the §MEATBALL carve is honored, I confirm no scroll-axis
filter), but "very likely" is not "captured," and the GOLDEN's *own* law is paired Chromium+WebKit
DELTA artefacts. The `linear()` cascade easing also rides a Safari<17.2 cubic-bezier fallback that is
asserted (`@supports (animation-timing-function: linear(0,1))`) but never shown rendering the spring
shape on a real old-WebKit. "Default-to-broken-on-Safari is eliminated by construction" is a
construction *argument*, not the WebKit screenshot the binding law requires.

**HARDENING:** The acceptance bar MUST include a real WebKit run (Playwright `webkit` channel) of
R-PIN + R-WEIGHT + the cascade `linear()`/fallback, captured as the paired-engine DELTA — not a
Chromium emulation of "no timeline-scope." If WebKit can't be driven in this harness, say so and mark
R-SAFARI CONDITIONAL rather than implying it's proven.
