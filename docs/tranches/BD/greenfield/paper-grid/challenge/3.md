## TOP REFUTATION (LANDS) — the "warm-DIVERGENT technicolor" palette collapses to muddy brown-orange

The GOLDEN acceptance bar §5.3 + §1 promise a *DIVERGENT* ramp: "rose-umber trough → ember →
amber → warm-wheat crest," hue ∈ [20,90], a 1940s-technicolor PUNCH. The live in-frame readback of
the spike's own RIPPLE preset (`warmLo=[0.42,0.16,0.10]`, `warmHi=[0.99,0.86,0.55]`,
`light=[0.55,0.70,0.45]`) tells a different story:

```
facePaintFrac 0.692   warmHueFrac 1.000   tealNavyFrac 0.000     (warm-purge holds — good)
meanLuma 166.3        sdLuma 33.0          → CV ≈ 0.20
topHueBins (10°): 30→125960  20→47512  40→27137  10→94   (bins 50–90: ZERO)
```

Two design failures fall out of this:

1. **The crest never reaches wheat/amber.** EVERY painted pixel lands in hue bins 10–40 (rose →
   orange → brown). The promised "amber → warm-wheat crest" (hue 60–90) is **statistically absent
   — zero pixels in bins 50–90.** The Lambert `shade` only ever lerps `warmLo→warmHi`, but `shade`
   never gets near 1.0 in practice (the slope term `faceRelief*∇H` is small for most cells, so
   `shade≈0.5`, parking the ink at mid-ramp = orange-brown). The ramp is divergent in the *config*
   but monochromatic-brown in the *paint*. The screenshot confirms it: a muddy tan sheet, not the
   vivid technicolor register the edict demands. This is the gray-glass disease's cousin —
   "brown-glass" — and it fails the §5.3 "VIVID + warm" bar AS A USER.

2. **The lit contrast is far weaker than the GOLDEN advertises.** The GOLDEN §7 quotes
   `sdLuma=41.3 / meanLuma=51.8` → CV≈0.80. My live in-frame read is `sdLuma=33 / meanLuma=166.3`
   → CV≈0.20 — a **4× weaker lit gradient relative to brightness.** The GOLDEN's quoted readback
   must have sampled a darker crop or an earlier preset; the actual full-frame paint is a bright,
   washed-out, low-contrast tan. The "unmistakable rippling sheet … crest-facing faces brighten to
   warm-wheat and troughs fall into rose-umber" claim is not what paints. The ripple READS as a
   faint sheen on a brown sheet, not a technicolor crest.

**HARDENING (blocking):** the ramp must be re-authored as a genuine MULTI-STOP warm-divergent ramp
keyed on `shade` AND `h` (height), not a 2-point `mix(lo,hi)` that parks at mid. Concretely: (a)
key the crest highlight on `h` directly (`faceInk = ramp3(rose-umber, ember-amber, warm-wheat;
mix(shade, h))`) so the traveling crest — not just the local slope — drives the wheat highlight;
(b) re-tune `faceRelief`/`lightDir` so `shade` actually traverses [0.15,0.95] across a cell (the
spike's `shade≈0.5` clustering is the muddiness root); (c) add a born-RED gate clause **G3b — crest
hue REACH**: over the brightest face-decile, `hue ≥ 55` for `≥ 0.3` of pixels (the wheat crest
must PAINT), and a **G1b — lit-CV** floor (`sdLuma/meanLuma ≥ 0.35`) so a washed-out tan reds.
EYE-VERIFY both modes against IOS27-REFERENCE before claiming "vivid."

## R2 (LANDS, design) — the cell-warp reads as static "crinkled foil," not a TRAVELING liquid ripple

The screenshot + the motion are honest about ONE thing: cells DO twist about their own centers
(the diagnosis is right, this is NOT a line-bow). But the GESTALT is wrong. With `twistMax=0.62`,
`shearMax=0.26`, and a `TWIST_FLOOR=0.62` that forces a *minimum* twist on EVERY cell regardless of
crest, the whole field reads as a **uniformly crinkled crumpled-foil texture** — a high-frequency
static crinkle everywhere — rather than a coherent traveling crest sweeping over an otherwise-CALM
sheet. The edict is LIQUID-WEIGHT: inertia/bounce/squish, "ONE coherent traveling front, never a
global pulse," "morph MORE on move." A floor-twist that crinkles cells far from the crest is the
opposite — it is a global static crumple that swamps the traveling read. There is no calm paper for
the crest to ripple ACROSS; the whole sheet is already maximally crumpled.

**HARDENING:** gate the twist by the SAME `travelingEnvelope` so cells AWAY from the crest relax to
near-flat (calm paper) and only the crest band crinkles+inflates — that is what makes the front
read as a traveling RIPPLE rather than static foil. The `TWIST_FLOOR=0.62` is the enemy of the
traveling read; it must be crest-gated (floor → 0 off-crest) or dropped. Add a born-RED **G2b —
off-crest calm**: the cell-twist magnitude in the trough band is `< 0.3×` the crest band
(otherwise it is a global crumple, not a front). The spike's `cellTwist` already multiplies `th` by
`env`, but `directedTwist` re-imposes `max(TWIST_FLOOR, |s|)` — so the floor leaks back the static
crumple. That interaction is the bug.

**HARDENING:** commit the in-frame readback as a real `scripts/`-runnable artifact (a

genuinely reuses `cellTwist`/`waveSwell`/`cursorSwirl`/`heightField` — confirmed at
`levelField.ts:19`), so this is not a fork. But the "inherits for free" framing is a DRY
overstatement that should be cut so a builder doesn't expect concentric to grow a face.

**HARDENING:** drop the "concentric inherits the face for free" line; state plainly that
`cellHeight`/`faceRelief`/`facePlateau` are paper-grid-consumed leaf fns that concentric MAY later
reuse, not an automatic inheritance. Keep the shared-leaf placement (it is the correct DRY home).
