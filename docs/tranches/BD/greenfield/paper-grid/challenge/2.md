VERDICT: **SURVIVES as the core DIRECTION (render the lit face — the diagnosis is correct and the

## R1 — TOP, BLOCKING. The §3 "colorful field behind glass + defined edge" is a PHANTOM dependency. The lit-ripple READ in the spike is the BACKGROUND field, not the face-shade — and the real page has no field at all.

**Live evidence (the real substrate, `isSpike:false`, http://localhost:5173/substrates/paper-grid):**
the canvas's ENTIRE ancestor chain is `background-color: rgba(0,0,0,0)` / `background-image:
none` for 8 levels up. The nearest painted element is `.configurator.glass-floating` at
`oklab(0.793 0.005 0.012 / .84)` → chroma ≈ **0.0128**, FAR below the §3 0.045 floor. There is NO
colorful field, NO defined edge — gray-cream glass over black `BODY{rgb(0,0,0)}`. This is the
IDENTICAL "gray-glass over nothing" root the orchestrator already live-confirmed born-RED for
aurora (0.0350), fourier (0.012), concentric (flat) and dot-matrix.

**Why this guts the golden, not just decorates it:** the §3 field is a BINDING precept
(GREENFIELD-HARDENING-PLAN §1: "a COLORFUL FIELD behind glass + a defined edge"), and the golden
leans on it TWICE — §1 ("the page reads through the gutters"), §2f, gate G3 ("the stage field
behind measures chroma ≥ 0.045"). But §2f routes that field into **`BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND`, which
is NOT an implemented asset** — it appears ONLY in plan/doc files; `docs/.../greenfield/
page-background/` does not exist; no `src/` or `demo/` warm-mesh primitive exists. Every Band-A
viz "FOLDs/ROUTEs into" this same unbuilt wave. So the golden's §3 compliance rests entirely on a
phantom shared dependency that is itself born-RED and unscheduled relative to this wave.

**The spike CONCEALS this** by hard-coding an inline CSS conic `#field` (`paper-sheet-spike.html`
L28-36) as a "stand-in." Live readback of the spike full-frame (screenshot captured) proves the
hazard: the dominant luminance structure in the spike is the BACKGROUND conic (pink hotspot
upper-right, yellow lower) bleeding through `faceAlpha:0.62`, NOT a coherent lit ridge sweeping
the faces. Remove the conic (the real-page reality) and the "rippling lit sheet" read
substantially collapses to a flat mid-tan terrazzo. The spike de-risked the WRONG composite: it
proved "face + a vivid field behind it" reads vivid — it did NOT isolate that the face-SLOPE-SHADE
alone carries the traveling-wave read.

**HARDENING (blocking):**
1. The golden CANNOT depend on a phantom. Either (a) `BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` is hard-sequenced as a
   STRICT predecessor of this wave with its own born-RED stage-paint gate (the dot-matrix/fourier
   amendments' shared warm-mesh CSS primitive — NOT `auroraFallbackGround`, a teal-navy
   violation), and the paper-grid wave is BLOCKED on it; or (b) paper-grid's substrate route owns
   a local warm-mesh stage paint as a net-new ~6-line CSS primitive (honest, not "route into" a
   thing that isn't there).
2. Re-shoot the spike WITH `#field` set to flat warm-cream (the real-page condition) and prove the
   face-slope-shade ALONE produces a coherent traveling-luminance band — `sdLuma` over the crest
   must survive with the field GONE. If it doesn't, G1's `sdLuma` floor is being met by the field,
   not the face, and the gate is a false-pass.
3. Gate G3 must measure the face-shade chroma/luminance-spread over a NEUTRALIZED background, so a
   missing/flat field REDS the gate instead of a vivid conic masking it.

## R2 — BLOCKING. The face is sampled at the WRONG cell center: `cc = floor(g)+0.5` is taken from the POST-twist coordinate, so the lit face does NOT spatially register with the twist it is supposed to "ride."

`cellTwist` (verified `waveField.wgsl.ts:39-53`) returns `cc + rot`, where `rot` is the cell-local
offset rotated by `theta` AND skewed by `shear` (`rot.x += shear*rot.y`). With the shipped preset
(`twistMax 0.62`, `shearMax 0.26`) `rot` can exceed ±0.5 — i.e. the twisted coordinate crosses the
ORIGINAL cell boundary. The golden then computes `cc = floor(g)+0.5` on the ALREADY-TWISTED `g`
(spike L151, GOLDEN §2c L115). That `floor` lands on a DIFFERENT cell than the one `cellTwist`
used to gate the twist. Consequence: `cellHeight(cc,…)` and `faceRelief(cc,…)` are evaluated at a
center that no longer matches the twist's gating crest. The "the crest that twists a cell is the
crest that lifts and lights it" claim (§0 reconciliation #3, the entire ONE-basis thesis) is
geometrically FALSE at exactly the high-twist crest where it matters most — the faces light up
SHIFTED from the cells that twist, reading as incoherent shimmer rather than a registered ridge.

This is also visible in the live spike screenshot: at the twist-dense bands the faces look like a
cracked mosaic with light/dark assigned somewhat randomly to tiles, not a clean lit ridge tracking
the twist.

**HARDENING (blocking):** compute the cell center from the ORIGINAL pre-twist coordinate (carry
`ccPre = (floor(g_pre/cs)+0.5)*cs` out of `cellTwist`, or recompute it before the twist call), and
evaluate `cellHeight`/`faceRelief`/`facePlateau`'s GATING height at `ccPre` so the height, the
twist, and the shade provably share ONE pivot. Add a gate clause: the bright-face centroid must
coincide (within ½ cell) with the max-twist cell centroid over the crest band — a shifted pair
REDS. (Note: `facePlateau` coverage still uses the warped `g` for the inset shape; only the HEIGHT
pivot must be the pre-twist center.)

## R3 — Safari/WebKit parity is asserted "by construction" but the spike NEVER ran on WebKit, and three concrete WebKit-divergence vectors are unaddressed.

The §3 claim "PERFECT in Chrome AND Safari, paired-engine π" is backed by a Chrome-only spike
(`golden/shots/pg-golden-light.png`, Chrome). No WebKit capture exists. The "by construction"
argument is sound for the big risks (no `backdrop-filter:url`, no goo filter, central-difference
not `dFdx`-on-height, sRGB output) — those genuinely de-risk WebKit. But:

- **(a) `fwidth`/`dpdx`/`dpdy` on the warped `g`.** `facePlateau` AA uses `aa = fwidth(g)` (spike
  L126/L159) and `gridCoverage` uses `dpdx(g)/dpdy(g)` (`paper-grid.wgsl.ts:174`). After the twist
  `g` has high spatial-frequency content; WebKit's Metal/ANGLE derivative quality at cell seams
  differs from Chrome's. The golden waves this away as "the Golus AA already works on both" — but
  the golden ADDS a SECOND derivative consumer (`facePlateau`) at the inset edge, a new surface for
  the seam-shimmer the Golus path was tuned to avoid. Needs a WebKit-captured paired-π on the FACE
  edge specifically, not an inherited assertion.
- **(b) `premultipliedAlpha:true` + `blendFunc(ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA)` over a transparent
  canvas.** The spike (L183, L193) and the shipped shader (`vec4f(col*a,a)`) rely on the browser
  compositor honoring premultiplied output. WebKit's canvas compositing of premultiplied-alpha over
  a CSS background has historically diverged from Chrome at low alpha (the `0.45+0.55*h` faceA
  fades to near-zero behind the front). A faint-alpha face over the field is exactly where WebKit
  banding/halo shows. Needs the paired capture at low `faceA`, not just the crest.
- **(c) `linearToSrgb`/OETF parity.** The golden routes face ink through the shared `linearToSrgb`
  (good) but the spike's preset feeds RAW linear-ish triples (`warmLo:[0.42,0.16,0.10]` etc., L208)
  and does NOT call `linearToSrgb` on `faceInk` before output (spike L164/L174 — no OETF on the
  face path, unlike the shipped line path L184 which DOES `linearToSrgb(u.line.rgb)`). So the spike
  proves the face read in a DIFFERENT color space than the production path will use. The vivid hues
  the spike screenshot shows are not the hues the shipped OETF path will produce.

**HARDENING:** (a) capture a real WebKit (Playwright webkit or Safari Tech Preview) paired-π over
the FACE inset edge + the low-alpha face-behind-the-front, ΔE bar, born from the actual shader not
the spike. (b) The spike must transcribe the production OETF on the face path (`linearToSrgb` on
`faceInk`) so the de-risk color space matches production; re-verify `warmHueFrac`/`tealNavyFrac`
AFTER the OETF (a linear triple that is warm can shift hue through the transfer). (c) Add the
`@supports`/PRM compositor floors explicitly — the golden names them for the field but not for the
face premultiply path.

## R4 — The interactive cursor height-bump is a NET-NEW mechanism mis-billed as "KEEP + extend," and it ships a WCAG-2.1.1 keyboard gap.

§1 ("extends `cursorSwirl` with a local height-bump about the cursor so a finger-press visibly
DENTS the sheet") and §4 ("`cursorSwirl` KEEP + extend with a local height-bump"). Verified at HEAD:
`cursorSwirl` (`waveField.ts:147`) is a pure 2-D swirl of the sampling coordinate — it has NO height
output and NO scalar return; a "local height-bump" is a genuinely NEW function/term, not an
extension of an existing one. Billing it as KEEP+extend understates the new surface (a new Gaussian
height contribution that must round-trip JS↔WGSL↔GLSL for the numeric-parity gate, a new uniform,
a new cursor-radius interaction with the squash). This is a DRY/honesty refutation: name it as the
RE-INVENT it is so its parity + a11y cost is gated, not hidden under "extend."

**a11y:** the only a11y attribute on the paper-grid DOM is `aria-hidden="true"`
(`PaperGrid.vue:110`) — there is NO keyboard path. A cursor-only "press to dent the sheet" is a
pointer-exclusive interaction. The golden's a11y carve (§3) covers PRM and `prefers-reduced-
transparency` but is SILENT on WCAG-2.1.1 keyboard-equivalent for the new cursor-dent (the SAME gap
the fourier-loom amendment had to fold in as a keyboard shape-cycle). If the dent is a genuine
interaction it needs a keyboard equivalent; if it's pure decoration it should stay `aria-hidden`
and the golden should SAY the dent is non-interactive decoration (then it can't be a "finger-press"
affordance).

**HARDENING:** (1) re-census the cursor height-bump as RE-INVENT with its own parity gate clause.
(2) Decide: decorative (stays aria-hidden, no keyboard, drop "press" affordance language) OR
interactive (add a WCAG-2.1.1 keyboard-equivalent + a gate clause). Default to decorative — KISS,
the substrate is `aria-hidden` ambient art.

## R5 — Cartoon-punch / squash precept under-met: the spike reads as terrazzo, not paper; the "squash inflation" produces irregular tile sizes that read as a cracked mosaic, and the divergent warm ramp is washed out.

Live spike full-frame (screenshot): the faces read as a flat warm CRAZY-PAVING / terrazzo, not a
sheet of paper rippling. The "volume-preserving squash" (`inset = baseInset*(1-squashK*h)`,
squashK 0.42) makes crest tiles physically larger and trough tiles smaller — but because the GATING
height is mis-registered (R2) and the inset is bounded small, the visible result is irregular tile
SIZES with chaotic gutter widths, which the eye reads as a fractured mosaic, not as a coherent
inflate→deflate of a continuous surface. The "1940s technicolor" rose-umber→wheat divergence is
NOT visible — every face is mid-tan; the only saturated region is where the background conic shows
(R1). So precepts "PAPER morphism visible," "cartoon squash & stretch with real WEIGHT," and "vivid
warm divergent" are NOT met by the de-risk artifact — they're asserted in prose.

This is partly downstream of R1 (no real field) and R2 (mis-registered pivot), but also a
calibration failure: `faceRelief 2.6` slope-gain against a Gaussian envelope of `waveSigma 0.42`
yields a gentle gradient that the warm-divergent ramp barely separates; the squash inset range
(`baseInset 0.14`, squashK 0.42 → inset swings 0.14→0.08) is too small to read as inflation against
the twist-driven size noise.

**HARDENING:** after fixing R2's pivot, re-tune so the LIT band is the dominant luminance structure
on a NEUTRAL field (R1.2): widen the slope-shade contrast (key-light/ambient split), raise the
squash range so a crest face visibly INFLATES relative to its trough neighbour, and verify the
warm-divergent ramp produces ≥2 distinct hue pools across the crest (the fourier "≥2 warm pools"
precedent) — not a monochrome tan wash. The G1 `sdLuma` and the (new) warm-pool-count gate must be
met by the FACE, field-neutralized.

## Minor / non-blocking

- **G2 (traveling-front) self-test bite is weak.** "stationary pulse → G2 RED" via cross-correlation
  peak shift is right, but the spike never demonstrated the front actually translates in painted
  pixels over N frames (only single-frame readback). Cross-correlate ≥8 frames in the gate, and
  add the static-hold control (a stationary lit band must RED G2) — the dot-matrix G2-trap lesson
  (a swept single-frame can false-pass).
- **`cellHeight` vs `heightField` near-dup.** `heightField` (`waveField.ts:205`) is an FBM octave
  sum; `cellHeight` is `travelingEnvelope*amp`. Distinct, so not a literal dup — but the census
  row "`heightField`/`waveSwell`/`waveFlow` reuse register where fit; `cellHeight` surfaces the
  envelope" is hand-wavy. State plainly: `cellHeight` is the surfaced twist-envelope (NOT the FBM
  heightField); they are different fns and concentric uses the FBM one. Avoid implying reuse that
  isn't.
- **Overfit check (clean):** the preset constants live in the spike/preset only, the `src/` default
  is `faceAlpha:0` (byte-identical), line-refs check out. No hardcoded ref smell in `src/`. Good —
  presets-in-consumers honored. The only overfit risk is the spike's hard-coded `#field` conic
  masquerading as the §3 field (folded into R1).
- **PRM "one flat static LIT frame":** with `amp=0` the envelope→0 so height/shade/squash→0, but
  `faceAlpha` is still 0.62 → you get a flat UNIFORMLY-shaded face grid (shade = 0.5+dot at h=0
  grad=0 → constant), which is fine and legible. Confirm the PRM frame isn't a flat GRAY plate
  (constant shade × warm ramp midpoint must land warm, not gray) — add a PRM warm-floor clause.

## Bottom line

The DIAGNOSIS (faces never fill → render the lit face) and the Safari-safe-BY-CONSTRUCTION spine
(no url-filter, no goo, central-difference not dFdx-on-height) are FIT and survive — this is a real
refinement, not a fork, deftly landing in the shared `waveField` leaf. But as WRITTEN the golden is
not buildable: (R1) its §3 vividness depends on a phantom `BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` and the spike's
inline conic concealed that the FACE alone doesn't carry the ripple; (R2) the face is sampled at
the wrong post-twist cell center so the lit band doesn't register with the twist — the one-basis
thesis is geometrically false at the crest. Fix those two and re-de-risk on a NEUTRAL field with a
WebKit-captured paired-π, and it becomes the lit rippling sheet it claims. Survives as direction,
REFUTED as written.
