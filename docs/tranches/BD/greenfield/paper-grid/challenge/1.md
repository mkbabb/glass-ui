# Challenge #1 — CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS lens

Verdict: **SURVIVES — with two real refutations that must be hardened (one correctness,
one integration). Neither sinks the spine; both are concrete and land.**

The GOLDEN diagnosis is right and the spine is genuinely deft (one shared leaf, `faceAlpha:0`
byte-default, no new pass/basis/component, central-difference not `dFdx`, premultiplied-over-
transparent so the page reads through). I tried hard to break the spine and could not. But the
spec ships two latent defects that the spike *paints over* rather than solves, plus a forward-
reference dependency dressed as a present primitive.

---

## R1 (TOP, CORRECTNESS) — the face samples the height of the WRONG cell at the crest

The whole legibility claim rests on "the crest that twists a cell is the crest that lifts and
lights it … provably ONE envelope" (§0, §2a). But the math the spike and §2c actually run does
NOT hold that invariant where it matters.

- `cellTwist` (`waveField.ts:106` / spike L104) computes its driving envelope at the **pre-twist**
  cell center: `cc = (floor(g/cs)+0.5)*cs`, then twists `g` about that `cc`.
- The GOLDEN face layer (§2c, spike L151) then recomputes the cell center on the **already-twisted**
  `g`: `cc = floor(g)+0.5`, and reads `cellHeight`/`faceRelief`/`facePlateau` at THAT.

These are two different cell centers. For a calm cell the twist is ~0 and they coincide — but in
the **crest band**, the exact place the ripple is supposed to read, the twist is maximal
(`directedTwist` is FLOORED to ≥0.62·twistMax — `TWIST_FLOOR=0.62`, deliberately decisive). A
floored twist of ~0.4–0.6 rad rotates the local coordinate enough that `floor(twisted_g)` lands in
a NEIGHBOUR cell along the boundary. There the face is lit by a *different* crest cell's height
than the one that actually twisted it. The "ONE envelope" parity gate (G4) will still pass — it
round-trips each fn in isolation — yet the composed visual reads the wrong cell's slope at the
crest. Result: a slope-shade that desyncs from the twist precisely where the design promises they
fuse, reading as shimmer/seam at cell edges in the crest band, not a coherent lit ridge. This is
the squash too: `inset = baseInset·(1−squashK·h)` uses the twisted-cell `h`, so the inflation can
inflate the wrong cell's face. The spike's static screenshot hides it (one frame, faces look
filled); a frame-series at the crest boundary would expose the edge shimmer.

This also quietly violates KISS/DRY: there are now TWO notions of "the cell" (pre-twist driver
vs post-twist sampler) when the spec asserts one.

### Hardening (R1)
Sample height/relief/face on the **pre-twist** cell center — the SAME `cc` the twist rode — not on
`floor(twisted_g)`. Concretely: have `cellTwist` (or a tiny shared `cellOf(g,cs)`) RETURN the
`cc`/`env` it used, and feed THAT into `cellHeight`/`faceRelief`/`facePlateau`. Then "one envelope"
is true by construction (single `cc`, single `env`), DRY is restored (one cell notion), and the
parity gate G4 actually witnesses the composed invariant. Add a born-RED gate bite to §6: at the
crest band, the face-shade cell-id must equal the twist-driver cell-id (a desync reds). The plateau
coverage can still use `fract(twisted_g)` for the *inset shape* inside the warped quad — only the
height/slope SOURCE must move to the driver `cc`.

## R2 (INTEGRATION) — §2f routes into a wave that DOES NOT EXIST on disk

§2f / §3 / the census all say the colorful field is "not this viz's job — route into the SHARED
`BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` warm-mesh." Grep of `docs/tranches/BD/waves/` shows NO such wave file —
only `BD.W-PAGE-HEADER-FOLD` and `BD.W-PAGE-OFFTOKEN-SWEEP`. `BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` appears ONLY in
planning prose (`GREENFIELD-HARDENING-PLAN.md`, the SYNTHESIS docs), never as an executable wave.
So acceptance-bar #3 ("over a colorful field with a defined edge") and gate G3's "the stage field
behind measures chroma ≥ 0.045" depend on a primitive that is unbuilt. The spike fakes it with an
inline `radial-gradient` `#field` div (spike L30–36) — which is exactly the "hardcoded ref / overfit
stand-in" this lens is told to hunt. The GOLDEN presents a forward-reference dependency as a present
shared primitive ("ROUTE INTO (shared, not a sibling)") — but you cannot route into nothing, and the
fallback temptation is `auroraFallbackGround`, which §2f itself forbids (teal-navy purge violation).

Note: `paperGrid` already ships its OWN `fieldAlpha` (`constants.ts:89`, `paperGrid.ts:247`) — the
existing field-behind knob. The GOLDEN never reconciles its NEW `faceAlpha` translucency-over-field
story with the EXISTING `fieldAlpha`; two field-blend scalars now coexist with no stated relation
(another latent DRY seam).

### Hardening (R2)
Make the dependency explicit and gated: (a) state `BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` as a HARD PREREQ wave —
G3 is conditionally-RED until that wave lands, and the paper-grid amendment must NOT merge its G3
clause before it; OR (b) scope G3 down to what paper-grid OWNS now — assert warm-hue + no-teal/navy
ON THE FACES (which the viz fully controls), and move the "colorful field behind" clause to the
page-background wave's gate, cross-linked. Either way, delete the implication that the field is a
present primitive. Separately: reconcile `faceAlpha` vs `fieldAlpha` explicitly — state whether the
face composites OVER the existing field-tint (and the §3 "translucent face so the field reads
through" is the EXISTING `fieldAlpha` path) or replaces it. One field-blend scalar, documented.

## R3 (KISS/minor) — the squash brightness double-counts the crest

`faceA = face · faceAlpha · (0.45 + 0.55·h)` (spike L163) brightens face ALPHA by height, AND the
slope-shade already brightens face INK at the crest, AND the squash already inflates the face at the
crest. Three crest-coupled terms (alpha, ink, inset) stacked. That is more than the "ripple is LIGHT
not line-bending" KISS thesis needs and risks an over-punchy crest that blooms past the bounded-squash
legibility promise. Not a correctness break — a calibration/parsimony smell. Harden: drop the `h`-term
on `faceA` (let the SHADE carry the brightness, the SQUASH carry the inflation — one channel each),
or justify the triple-coupling against the CV<0.15 fence with a measured frame.

## What I could NOT break (survives clean)

- **Born-RED is real.** HEAD's JS oracle returns `{ line, alpha: line·fieldAlpha }`
  (`paperGrid.ts:247`) — literally zero face term; the shader twin is the `vec4f(col*a,a)` line-only
  paint. `facePaintFrac≈0`, `sdLuma≈0` at HEAD → G1 reds honestly. The all-zero-readback precondition
  (§6/§7) is a genuine, correctly-flagged trap, not a hand-wave.
- **Safari-safe by construction holds.** No `backdrop-filter:url`, no goo filter (N/A — not a metaball
  viz), central-difference height (no `dFdx` on H), `fwidth` only on the already-cross-engine Golus AA,
  sRGB OETF output. The WebKit gaps this lens hunts (color-interp, filter:url, derivative-of-derivative)
  do not apply.
- **byte-identical default holds.** `faceAlpha:0` → face evaporates → HEAD-identical; `fieldAlpha`
  precedent shows the opt-in-knob pattern is already idiomatic here.
- **PRM holds.** `amp=0` (`usePaperGrid.ts:98`) collapses the envelope → height/shade/squash all
  evaporate → one flat static lit frame. Real, inherited, not bolted on.
- **No fork.** Three pure fns into the EXISTING shared leaf; `cellTwist` refactored (not duplicated)
  to call `cellHeight`; KEEP/REFINE/RE-INVENT census is honest. This is a union.

## Net

The spine is fit and deftly integrated — keep it. R1 is a true correctness defect (the face lights
the wrong cell at the crest, contradicting the headline "ONE envelope" claim) and MUST be hardened
before the gate can witness what the prose promises. R2 is a forward-reference dressed as a present
primitive (and an unreconciled `faceAlpha`/`fieldAlpha` DRY seam). R3 is a parsimony nit. With R1+R2
hardened, the golden survives this lens intact.
