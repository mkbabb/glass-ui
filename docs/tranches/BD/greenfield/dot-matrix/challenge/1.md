# Challenge #1 — CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS lens on the DotMatrix GOLDEN

**Verdict: SURVIVES (battered).** The headline mechanism (the velocity-warped
directional lens + comet-tail) is real, source-grounded, and live-validated in the
spike — but four claims are FALSE or fragile as written, and two of them touch the
deftness crux. The golden survives only because every defect has a concrete hardening
that does NOT require re-architecting the headline. Default-REFUTED was the start; the
spec earns survival on the mechanism, loses points on its integration story.

Live-verified against HEAD: `dot-matrix.{glsl,wgsl}.ts`, `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts`,
`useDotMatrix.ts`, `usePointerVelocityField.ts`, `constants.ts`, the W-DOT-UNIFY +
W-DOTFLOW-REBUILD union waves, and the throwaway spike driven by a synthetic sweep in
Chrome (`spike-live.png` in this dir).

## R1 — [DEFT-INTEGRATION, the heaviest] The "DRY-shared `presence` helper between dot-matrix and W-DOTFLOW-REBUILD `mode=field`" is a RENDER-LAYER fiction the codebase already rejected

GOLDEN §2.5 + §7 commit to "extract ONE shared `presence = edgeFalloff·(1−centerClear)`
GLSL helper consumed by BOTH this plane vignette AND `W-DOTFLOW-REBUILD`'s
`mode="field"` vignette (the no-second-leaf fence)" and "ONE shared `dotTwinkle(phase,
rate)` snippet for the dot suite."

But the already-developed **`BD.W-DOT-UNIFY` F1** (re-grepped against HEAD) proves the
three dot vizzes use **genuinely distinct render mechanisms** with no shared fragment
layer:
- **dot-flow-field** — instanced billboard over a `@compute` advection (WGSL) / a
  **fullscreen-fragment cell-lattice** (the WebGL2 fallback — a *different mechanism
  from its own primary*).
- **dot-matrix** — instanced billboard **STATIC phyllotaxis**; the displacement +
  `facingOpacity` live in the **VERTEX** stage (`dot-matrix.glsl.ts:69-114`).
- goo-dot — a fullscreen-fragment quantizer.

So the GOLDEN's `presence`/`dotTwinkle` "shared GLSL helper" would have to be consumed
across a **vertex-stage phyllotaxis displacement** (dot-matrix, where the vignette
multiplies `facingOpacity` in `vs_main`) AND a **fragment-stage cell quantizer**
(dot-flow's GLSL `mode=field`). That cross-stage, cross-mechanism share does not exist
and the unify wave explicitly states the genuinely-DRY layer is the **shell +
configurator**, NOT the render layer ("a 'ONE … rasterizer common to all three' does
not exist"). The GOLDEN's no-second-leaf fence is therefore a render-DRY the codebase
forbids — exactly the "invented shared primitive" overscope F1 was written to kill.

**Hardening:** DROP the cross-viz GLSL-helper share from the spec. Keep the vignette +
twinkle as **dot-matrix-LOCAL vertex-stage snippets** (they are ~3 lines each). If a
shared idiom is wanted, share it as a **GLSL string constant in the `procedural-color`
seam** consumed *textually* (each backend pastes the same source), NOT as a
cross-mechanism "one leaf" — and explicitly DEFER any dot-flow consumption to
W-DOT-UNIFY's shell layer, never claim it as this wave's DRY win.

## R2 — [PRESETS-IN-CONSUMERS / identity fence] G4/G6 lift the LIBRARY-DEFAULT palette + opacity in `src/`, fighting the warm-identity fence; the read-at-rest fix belongs in a demo preset

GOLDEN §2.6 raises `WARM_IDENTITY_PALETTE` `C:0.03→0.055` (and a 2nd stop) AND §2.4
raises `baseOpacity 0.5→0.66` — **in `src/constants.ts`, the library default** — under a
"correctness floor, not a loudness choice" rationalization.

W-DOT-UNIFY **D2** (re-confirmed against HEAD) is explicit that the near-invisibility is
a **DEMO-DEFAULT bug, not a src redevelopment**: the warm-cream src token IS the library
identity, the contrasting register **already exists** demo-local
(`FLOW_PRESET_MONO_REFERENCE` / `NEAR_BLACK_GROUND`), and the fix is **W-VIZ-PRESENCE
(~3 lines/story)**, NOT a src token edit. The identity is fenced by `proof:viz-dotflow`
**F5**, which **REDs a non-warm literal in `src/`**. The user-memory law is the same:
*presets-in-consumers — named themed presets live in consumers; the library's own
default tokens evolve in `src/` ONLY as the lib's identity changes.* A read-at-rest +
chroma lift is a demo-stage legibility choice, not a library-identity evolution.

The golden even names presets-in-consumers in §2.6 — then edits `src/` anyway. That is
the contradiction. (Note: `C:0.055/0.085, h∈[60,76]` does not trip F5's *teal/navy*
literal, so it would not RED — but it still violates the *spirit* of the fence and the
D2 "this wave does NOT touch the src default register" boundary.)

**Hardening:** MOVE both the chroma lift and the `baseOpacity` raise to the **demo
preset** (the §2.6 "demo loud" ladder + a `baseOpacity` field in that preset). Leave
`WARM_IDENTITY_PALETTE` and the `0.5` default **byte-for-byte**. G4/G6 then assert the
floor **on the demo page** (the contrasting stage), exactly as `proof:dotflow-rebuild`
R1/R4 already do — read-at-rest is a DEMO-stage gate, never a src-token mutation. This
also removes the §2.7 tension (demo supplies BOTH the ground AND the loud palette — one
preset, one place).

## R3 — [BORN-RED INTEGRITY + GATE FRAGILITY] G2 (comet-tail wake) is a razor-thin, velocity-sensitive metric that reads the WRONG sign at rest/decel — a flaky gate, not a clean born-RED

Live spike (synthetic horizontal sweep, `spike-live.png`):
- moderate-velocity sweep (vmag≤0.027): **avgWake +0.011, peakWake +0.017** — positive
  (trails behind), correct sign, but **~5–10× smaller than the GOLDEN's claimed
  "+0.05 avg, +0.11 at peak."** The GOLDEN's headline numbers assume a faster flick than
  a controlled gate sweep produces.
- at rest-but-active (vmag 0.001, active 1.0): **wakeBehind −0.043 (AHEAD)** — the HUD
  itself shows the sign INVERTS on the decelerating tail (the anticipation-lead
  dominates when `vmag→0`).

So the G2 born-RED/born-GREEN margin is a thin band centered near zero whose sign
**depends on the exact instantaneous velocity at the sampled frame.** A gate that drives
a sweep and reads `> 0` at one captured frame can land in the negative decel tail and
**false-RED a correct implementation** (or, if it samples mid-stroke, barely clear). This
is precisely the calibration trap §8 documents — but the FIX (low threshold + "scales
with velocity") makes G2 a **weak discriminator** (a tiny positive number that a noisy
symmetric pool could also produce on a biased sample).

**Hardening:** (a) Gate G2 on the **velocity-CONDITIONED** centroid: require the
−vdir-projected centroid offset to exceed a floor **ONLY in frames where vmag > a
stated minimum** (e.g. drive the sweep at a *fixed, high* synthetic velocity and assert
the mean over the high-vmag window), and SEPARATELY assert it is **≈0 at rest** — the
two-sided clause makes it a real discriminator, not a one-frame sign check. (b) Add the
**luminance-tail** assertion as the PRIMARY G2 (trailing dots glow brighter ∝
`behind·vmag`) since the spike already proves displacement alone under-reads; the glow
asymmetry is larger and more robust than the centroid shift. (c) State the **minimum
synthetic vmag** the gate drives, in the gate spec, so the +0.11 claim is reproducible
(my sweep could not reach it).

## R4 — [PRECEPT: golden-proportion / "not sphere with z=0"] the plane register, in its OWN prototype, reads as a hard-edged center-DENSE disc — not a content-framing backdrop

GOLDEN §2.5 promises a "content-deferential vignette (clear center)" that "FRAMES the
content" and is "unambiguously distinct + useful (not 'sphere with z=0')." But the spike
screenshot (`spike-live.png`) shows a **tight phyllotaxis sunflower DISC floating in the
viewport, densest at its CENTER**, over a dark gradient. The `presence` term brightens
edges, but the phyllotaxis lattice is itself center-dense (`r = sqrt((i+0.5)/N)` packs
the inner radius), so the net read is a **circle**, not a viewport-spanning calm-center
backdrop. The "clear center where a glass card sits" duality is NOT demonstrated — the
center is the brightest, densest region.

This matters because the GOLDEN's whole "2D-PLANE background register" differentiation
(§3, T17 surpass-target) rests on the field reading as a **backdrop that recedes behind
content**. The buildable artifact shows the opposite.

**Hardening:** The plane lattice must be **VIEWPORT-FILLING and edge-weighted**, not a
unit disc scaled by `planeScale`. Either (a) tile a rectangular phyllotaxis/jittered
grid that covers the full frame with the `presence` vignette doing the center-clear
(true T17 "dense edges → clear center"), or (b) invert the radial density so the disc is
edge-dense. As written, `base2d = unitPos.xy * planeScale` with a sunflower disc CANNOT
clear its own center. Add a gate clause: **center-region lit-fraction < edge-region
lit-fraction at rest** (G5 already names "edge-density > center-density" — but the disc
geometry contradicts it; G5 would itself be born-RED *and stay RED* on this geometry).

## R5 — [BORN-RED INTEGRITY] G3 (the well MAGNIFIES) is NOT cleanly born-RED — the shipped shader already swells dot SIZE near the cursor

GOLDEN §6 G3: "*Born-RED:* the current taper swells brightness but the lens-magnify
gain is absent." FALSE. The shipped plane branch already has
`sizeTaper = 0.8 + 0.9 * nearness * pAct` (`dot-matrix.glsl.ts:81`) — dots within the
well already grow to **1.7× SIZE**, not just brightness. So a naive "footprint near
cursor > rest footprint" assertion is **already GREEN on HEAD**. G3's discriminator must
be the *additional* `LENS_MAGNIFY 1.6` gain over the existing 0.9, which the GOLDEN does
not state as the threshold.

**Hardening:** Re-state G3 as "near-cursor footprint exceeds the **current HEAD**
near-cursor footprint by ≥ a stated ratio" (i.e. anchor born-RED to the *delta* over the
existing swell, not over rest), OR cut G3 and fold magnify-read into G1 (the ellipse
already implies localized swell). As written G3 is not born-RED and would pass on HEAD.

## R6 — [KISS / coupling] §2.7 "reuse the shipped `auroraFallbackGround`" under-specifies a real coupling — it takes an `AuroraConfig`, not a free CSS string

`auroraFallbackGround(config: AuroraConfig, options)` returns
`{ backgroundImage, backgroundColor, metrics }` derived from an **Aurora palette +
nuclei field**. The GOLDEN treats it as "reuse the shipped CSS warm-mesh ground" as if
it were a free `<div>` style, but to call it the demo must **construct/own an
AuroraConfig** (palette + nuclei) tuned to the dot field's warm-amber→rose. That is a
real demo-side dependency on the Aurora component's config surface — fine, but it is NOT
the zero-cost "reuse" the spec implies, and it imports an Aurora coupling into the
dot-matrix demo chassis.

**Hardening:** Name the **explicit `AuroraConfig` preset** the demo passes (palette
stops + nuclei), in `demo/stories/substrates/presets.ts`, and gate G7 against THAT
ground's `metrics.mean`/quadrants (the function already returns them — use them as the
born-GREEN target rather than re-measuring). If the Aurora coupling is unwanted, use a
plain hand-authored `radial-gradient` stack (the spike already does exactly this in CSS,
lines 20-27 — that CSS, not `auroraFallbackGround`, is what was actually validated). The
spike's ground is a hand-rolled gradient, NOT `auroraFallbackGround` — so the "shipped
primitive reuse" is unverified; the verified artifact uses raw CSS.

## R7 — [minor / precision] byte-layout text is imprecise

GOLDEN §2.1: "DOT_RENDER_UNIFORM_BYTES grows by one vec4 → 208 + 4*16." The current base
is `192 + MAX_DOT_STOPS*16 = 256` (`uniformBridgeWGPU.ts:67`); after inserting `u7` the
palette offset moves 192→208 and the total becomes `208 + 4*16 = 272`. The "208" is the
new palette *offset*, not the total — the prose conflates them. Non-load-bearing (the
table is generated from one declaration so the edit is still single-source-correct), but
fix the comment so a reader does not size the buffer at 208.

## What SURVIVES intact (the fit core — do not re-invent)

- The **u7 single-lane** addition (one uniform-table edit → std140≡WGSL by
  construction) is the correct deft choice over lens-b's branch-dependent `uU2.z/.w`
  repurpose. Source-verified: `uU2.z/.w` ARE the sphere DOF taper
  (`uniformBridgeWGPU.ts:191-192`), so lens-b's path WOULD break the sphere lens. The
  GOLDEN's reconciliation is right.
- The **velocity/acceleration vectors are genuinely thrown away today**: `useDotMatrix`
  reads `pointer.speed.value` (L126) but never `pointer.velocity.value` /
  `pointer.acceleration.value`. Consuming them is a clean no-new-rAF, no-new-measurement
  reclaim. Correct.
- The **directional-ellipse warp (G1) reads strongly + correctly** live (ecc 1.38–1.42,
  elongates along travel, ≈1.0 at rest). The headline mechanism is sound.
- **Born-RED is honest for G1, G4, G5(geometry-permitting), G7** — the shipped plane
  branch is `toCursor*well` symmetric (ecc≈1), `baseOpacity 0.5`, `background:transparent`.
- The **no-dup fence with goo-dot-matrix / dot-flow-field** holds (distinct mechanisms;
  the GOLDEN correctly does NOT add a 2nd GL context or an SDF merge).
- **Cross-engine §4** is sound: born-GPU billboards, no `backdrop-filter:url`, no SVG
  goo — the WebKit fence genuinely does not bite the dots; the ground is compositor-only
  CSS. The MEATBALLING law correctly does not apply.
- **PRM/a11y §5** is coherent (tick(0) freeze → symmetric calm well, frozen twinkle,
  deterministic mid-engage capture) and rides the shipped `usePointerVelocityField`
  PRM discipline (verified L222-227).
