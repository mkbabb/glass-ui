## REFUTATION 1 (BINDING-PRECEPT — the strongest) — cartoon-shadow DEFERRED ⇒ the headline viz ships WITHOUT the mandated cartoon register

The binding law (design.md + GREENFIELD-HARDENING-PLAN §1) names CARTOON ANIMATION
+ SHADOWING — the 1940s technicolor register, **bold layered-offset shadows** + the
flow-and-punch principles — as a **UNIVERSAL** edict to elevate toward universal,
not an opt-in. The GOLDEN §1 table puts the **only** cartoon-shadow element (the
"layered-offset SHADOW on the gathered lozenge") in **"DEFERRED to a stretch-gate
… ship the lens first, add the shadow only if the spike proves it clean."** The
§8 spike then de-risks the ellipse/wake — and **never touches the shadow at all**.

As a user judging the gestalt: the dot field gathers, magnifies, brightens, and
trails a luminance wake — but it has **no cast shadow, no layered-offset depth, no
1940s technicolor stamp**. That is precisely the "missing cartoon-punch" failure
this lens hunts. "Squash & stretch with WEIGHT" is present (the ellipse); the
SHADOWING half of the same edict is punted indefinitely behind a gate the spike
declined to run. A deferred precept on the suite's flagship gravity viz is a
precept *omission*, dressed as survival-of-the-fittest.

Hardening: **Promote the layered-offset cast shadow into the headline wave, not a
stretch-gate.** It need not be a second full stamp-pass (the muddiness fear is
legitimate): render it as a cheap **per-instance offset duplicate billboard** in
the SAME instanced draw — a darkened, +offset, lower-α copy keyed off `nearness ·
pAct` (offset along a fixed light vector laddered on √φ, magnitude ∝ the magnify
swell). Zero new pass, zero new buffer, born-RED on the current shadowless field
(a G11 clause: gathered-dot luminance has a measurable dark offset-lobe under the
lens). If the spike must prove it clean, the spike must RUN it — add the
offset-billboard term to spike.html and capture it, or drop the "spike proves it"
claim.

## REFUTATION 3 (born-RED gate WILL flicker) — G2 is positive only on AVERAGE; rest/decel frames read the WRONG sign, and the "verified" PNG captured exactly that

The §8 spike's own metrics expose the trap the spec under-specifies. Live readback:
`minWakeBehind = −0.018`; **11% of moving frames are negative** (centroid ahead).
Worse, the committed proof artefact `spike-verified.png` HUD reads **`vmag 0.0000
… G2 wake centroid-behind −0.043`** — the "verified" screenshot froze on a
zero-velocity frame where the wake is the WRONG SIGN. So the headline G2 evidence
is a still that *fails* G2. The spec's gate text — "assert the centroid-behind
projection **> 0 during a sweep**" (§6 G2, §8) — is under-specified: a per-frame
or single-capture assertion is **born-FLAKY** (any decel/settle frame trips it),
and a naive grader could even capture a passing-RED that's actually the symmetric
pool at a fluke phase.

Hardening: pin G2 to a **velocity-gated sweep AVERAGE**: sample only frames with
`vmag > τ` (the spike's 0.002 floor), require **mean(centroid-behind) > 0 AND the
positive-frame fraction ≥ 0.8 AND a monotone velocity-scaling** (≈0 at rest,
rising with vmag). Forbid single-frame G2 capture and forbid any capture at
`vmag≈0`. Re-shoot `spike-verified.png` ON a moving frame (the HUD must show
`vmag>0` and G2>0), or the proof artefact is a self-refutation.

## REFUTATION 4 (integration trap — KISS) — §2.1's byte-layout prose is internally wrong; "u7 off 112" collides with the live `spin0`

§2.1: "`u7 : vec4<f32> off 112`". Source-verified `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts` L60–67:
**off 112 is `spin0`** (the mat3 col0); the layout is `…u6@96, spin0@112,
spin1@128, spin2@144, ints@160, bg@176, pal@192, total = 192+4*16 = 256`. There is
no free 112. The spec's own next line ("the spin columns + ints + bg + palette
shift down by 16 bytes") contradicts the "off 112" header — u7 cannot be at 112
*and* push spin0 down from 112. The "DOT_RENDER_UNIFORM_BYTES grows → 208 + 4*16"
is the only correct part (new base 192→208, total 272), but the offset prose is a
copy-from-memory error that, taken literally, overwrites the spin matrix and bricks
the sphere register. A reader implementing §2.1 verbatim corrupts the buffer.

Hardening: rewrite §2.1 against the real table — **u7 inserts AFTER u6 (new off
112), shifting spin0→128, spin1→144, spin2→160, ints→176, bg→192, pal→208; total
= 208 + 4*16 = 272; U_OFF words shift by +4.** State it as the table diff, not
prose, since the table is the single source of truth.

## REFUTATION 5 (a11y/PRM determinism) — the "frozen twinkle" and the "deterministic mid-engage capture" contradict

§2.4: PRM → the per-dot `sin(uTime·rate + phase)` "FREEZES (reads the parked
time)." §5/§9: pin the PRM capture to a "deterministic mid-engage phase." But a sin
read at whatever wall-clock `uTime` the loop happened to park at is **not
deterministic across runs** — two PRM captures park at different `uTime`, so the
twinkle pattern (hence the lit-pixel fraction G4 reads) differs run-to-run, making
G9 non-reproducible. The goo-dot lesson cited ("never freeze at t=0 max-separated")
is about the *warp* phase, not the *twinkle* clock; the twinkle has the same
determinism hole one layer down.

Hardening: under PRM/capture, **pin `uTime` to a fixed constant** (e.g. the √φ-rung
mid-engage phase) for BOTH the warp settle AND the twinkle `sin`, so the static
frame is bit-identical across runs. G9 asserts a fixed lit-pixel fraction, not a
floor that drifts with the park instant.
