## REFUTATIONS

### R1 (TOP, design-fidelity, LIVE-PROVEN) — the reveal bloom delivers ~3.5% punch, not the advertised 22%; the cartoon register is quantitatively ABSENT

The GOLDEN §G6 sells the signature move as "anticipate-dip → **~22% overshoot** →
settle … squishes **up past full**, settles with real weight." This lens' whole bar is
CARTOON-PUNCH. I drove the spike's reveal live and sampled the **computed** transform
mid-flight:

```
peakScaleX = 1.0347   →  overshoot = 3.47%   (claimed: ~22%)
minScaleX  = 0.8281
peakVolProduct (X·Y at peak) = 1.06          (claimed: volume-preserving ≈ 1)
```

**Root cause — the math is structurally wrong.** The `--ease-cartoon-punch` `linear()`
does reach `1.22` at ~58% progress, but CSS easing >1 only *extrapolates between the two
bracketing keyframe values*. The overshoot segment runs `18% scale(0.80,0.86)` →
`100% scale(1,1)`, a keyframe delta of only `0.20` in X. At ease-output 1.22 the
interpolated scale = `0.80 + 1.22·(1.00−0.80) = 1.044` — and the live peak is even
lower (`1.0347`). **The 22% ease value is multiplied by a 0.20 keyframe delta and
collapses to ~3.5%.** A 3.5% scale pop is sub-perceptual; it is the silent mechanical
pop the §G6 prose explicitly promises to abolish, dressed in a cartoon-punch ease that
never gets to act. The one moment the invisible substrate is perceptible — the moment
this entire lens exists to judge — has **no FLOW & PUNCH**. This is a §L4-elevation
failure and an iOS-27-register failure.

### R2 (overfit / rigged gate) — the spike's "volume-preserving" gate is hardcoded `true` and disproved by its own numbers

`index.html:226` ships `ck("D2 … X·Y ≈ 1 …", true, "…0.671 … 0.688 …")`. The assertion
is the literal boolean `true` (it can never fail), and the detail string reports the
products as **0.671 and 0.688** — i.e. NOT ≈1, ≈0.68. A "volume-preserving" check that
is hardcoded green while its own evidence says volume drops 32% is an overfit gate, not
a proof. The born-RED gate clause F ("X·Y≈1") inherits this — it would pass on a curve
that visibly loses a third of its volume at the anticipate beat. The squish does not
read as liquid weight; it reads as a flat shrink-then-grow.

### R3 (KISS/DRY contradiction — a latent THIRD ResizeObserver / parallel path) — UNRESOLVED in the golden

§0 says **KEEP** "the per-backend `ResizeObserver` (built INSIDE `buildContext`)". §G2
says **move the `ResizeObserver` UP into the agnostic leaf, wired at `arm()`**. Grep
confirms TWO live per-backend ROs today: `useWebGLCanvas.ts:180` +
`useWebGPUCanvas.ts:337`, each `new ResizeObserver(() => frameHooks?.resize())`. The
golden's PRUNE list (§7) deletes the consumer measure-blocks and the per-consumer
`useIntersectionPause` — but says NOTHING about the two backend ROs. So G2 as written
either (a) leaves them in place → a leaf RO + two backend ROs = **two observers firing
`resize` on every box change, the double-observe the BINDING LAW forbids as a parallel
path**, or (b) silently requires deleting them → then the §0 "KEEP byte-for-byte" claim
is false and the wave touches `buildContext` in both backends (more churn than "a
one-line consumer shrink per viz"). The golden must pick one and say so. As written it
is a bolt-on risk, not the clean union it advertises.

### R4 (overfit "verbatim" claim) — `sizeBacking` is NOT aurora's verbatim logic; it is a behavior-changing enhancement smuggled in as a lift

§G1: "The body is aurora's verbatim logic promoted, not re-invented." It is not. Aurora
(`runtime.ts:305`) walks **exactly one** level: `parentRect = canvas.parentElement?…`.
The golden's `sizeBacking` (GOLDEN.md:107, spike:90) `while`-walks **all** ancestors.
That is a strictly different algorithm with a new failure surface the spike's own C2
test depends on (the `display:contents` trap needs the multi-level walk; aurora's
one-level sizer would NOT pass C2). Calling an enhancement "verbatim" hides that the
multi-ancestor walk is unproven against the real consumers and can over-recover a
WRONG box: if the immediate parent is a zero-height flex/grid track but a grandparent is
full-viewport, the walk grabs the grandparent's box and sizes the backing to the whole
viewport — a silent over-allocation (memory + fill cost) that the one-level aurora sizer
never had. No clause bounds the walk (e.g. stop at the nearest positioned/sized
ancestor). This is the precept "no overfit/hardcoded ref" applied to a smuggled
algorithm change.

### R5 (spike does not prove what the GOLDEN needs in Chrome, and proves NOTHING in Safari)

- The README itself admits the keystone: **"`clientWidth` is NOT reliably 0 under a
  content-visibility skip (Chromium reported 952 here)."** So B1's headline ("survives
  the skip") is moot in the case the golden actually fears — the only place the legacy
  sizer demonstrably breaks is the synthetic `display:contents` C2 trap, which **no real
  consumer canvas uses** (canvases have a box). The live concentric `300×150` stuck-canvas
  I measured is the real defect — but the spike does not reproduce *that* path (a real
  GPU canvas under a real `content-visibility:auto` reveal); it reproduces a contrived
  `display:contents` case instead. The proof and the defect are not the same canvas.
- The BINDING LAW requires "PERFECT in CHROME *and* SAFARI" and the gate clause F demands
  a **PAIRED-engine (chromium+webkit) frame-series**. The spike ran on **Chrome only**
  (`spike-result.png` = "Chrome, Metal-3 host"). Zero WebKit capture exists. The §L7-safe
  channel-set argument (transform/opacity/filter only) is sound *in principle*, but
  WebKit `filter: blur()` on a live GL/WGSL canvas during a compositor transform is
  exactly the kind of thing that triggers a WebKit raster-on-every-frame fallback — and
  that is unmeasured. The reveal's Safari parity is asserted, not shown.

### R6 (a11y / PRM — a real gap in the reveal's PRM carve) 

§G6/§4 say PRM collapses to "instant `opacity` fade, zero transform" via
`--motion-weight: 0`. But the spike's PRM arm (`index.html:52-55`) swaps the animation to
`substrate-fade` via a **media-query class override** — it does NOT consume
`--motion-weight` at all in the transform path; the `0%/18%` transform keyframes simply
don't run because the whole animation is replaced. That happens to be correct here, but
the GOLDEN's stated mechanism ("the §L5 cascade via `--motion-weight: 0`") and the spike's
actual mechanism (a separate `@media` keyframe swap) **diverge** — the wave that "ships
`sizeBacking` body verbatim from the spike" would also inherit a PRM path that doesn't
match its own spec. More: there is no `prefers-reduced-transparency` handling in the
reveal at all, and §4 punts it to "a consumer SHOULD fall to its static mesh" — a
SHOULD, not a substrate guarantee, so the floor leaks back to the consumers the whole
golden is trying to de-leak.

### R7 (gestalt — the reveal is the WRONG cartoon principle for a substrate FIELD)

Even if R1's overshoot were fixed: a uniform `scale()` squish-grow of the *entire canvas
rect* is the cartoon vocabulary of a discrete OBJECT (a chip, a card) entering — not of a
*colorful FIELD behind glass* (§3) arriving. A field's cartoon-punch register is a
*bloom/wash from within* (luminance/saturation/parallax swell, the aurora drift catching
up), not a box-zoom. Scaling the canvas rect also momentarily reveals the stage's
gradient at the rect edges (the `scale(0.86)` start leaves a 14% gutter of bare
`.stage` background) — on a real hero that is a flash of un-fielded warm-cream at the
glass edge during entrance. The §3 mandate is "a colorful field + a DEFINED EDGE"; a
shrinking canvas momentarily breaks the defined edge. The signature move imports the
wrong gestalt for the thing it animates.

## HARDENING (concrete, the answer to each refutation)

1. **R1+R7 — re-spec G6 as a FIELD bloom, not a box-zoom, and make the punch real.**
   Move the overshoot into the KEYFRAME values, not the ease: e.g.
   `0% scale(0.94) opacity:0` → `64% scale(1.05) opacity:1` → `100% scale(1)` on a
   settle-ease, so the *keyframe* carries the +5% and the ease only smooths it — OR
   better, drop rect-`scale()` entirely and bloom the FIELD: a one-shot
   `--substrate-reveal-t` 0→1 the shader reads to ramp luminance/saturation/drift-speed
   (the field "catches up"), plus a CSS `opacity` + own-`filter: blur()` settle. Keep the
   canvas rect at `scale(1)` so the defined edge (R7) never breaks. Calibrate the punch by
   live measurement, not by the ease label — the acceptance is the *measured* peak, e.g.
   "measured peak field-luminance overshoot ≥ 12% then settle," sampled from the compositor.
2. **R2 — DELETE the hardcoded D2.** Compute X·Y from the keyframes and assert it against a
   real band (`0.95 ≤ X·Y ≤ 1.05` at every sampled beat), born-RED on today's 0.68 curve.
   The clause-F gate must measure volume, not assert it.
3. **R3 — make G2 explicit:** state that the leaf RO REPLACES both backend ROs; ADD
   "delete `useWebGLCanvas.ts:180` + `useWebGPUCanvas.ts:337` RO" to the PRUNE list; amend
   §0's "KEEP the per-backend RO" to "KEEP minus the RO (it rises to the leaf)." One
   observer, no double-fire.
4. **R4 — bound the ancestor walk.** Stop at the nearest ancestor with a non-zero
   *content-box* that is positioned or has an explicit size; cap the walk depth; never
   size to `documentElement`. Drop the word "verbatim" — call it "aurora's sizer,
   generalized to a bounded ancestor walk," and add a gate clause: a zero-height-parent /
   sized-grandparent fixture must NOT over-recover the viewport.
5. **R5 — make the spike prove the REAL path + add WebKit.** Reproduce the live concentric
   `300×150`-under-`content-visibility:auto` defect on a real GL canvas (not
   `display:contents`), show G1 cures it, and capture the reveal on BOTH chromium AND
   webkit (the clause-F paired-engine series is already specced — the spike must actually
   run it, with a blur-on-GL-canvas frame-time check on WebKit).
6. **R6 — unify the PRM path with its spec.** Either drive the reveal transform from
   `--motion-weight` so PRM=0 zeroes it through the *same* keyframes (matching §G6 prose),
   or change the prose to admit the `@media` keyframe-swap mechanism. Promote
   `prefers-reduced-transparency` to a substrate-level guarantee (park + static frame),
   not a consumer SHOULD.

## VERDICT

**SURVIVES — as a SIZING reform. Does NOT survive as the cartoon-punch reform it
advertises.** The sizing spine (G1–G5) is real, grounded, and live-corroborated (I
measured a fresh stuck `300×150` on concentric). But the signature G6 reveal — the one
deliverable this DESIGN-FIDELITY lens exists to judge — delivers a **live-measured 3.5%
pop against an advertised 22%**, rides a **hardcoded volume-preservation gate disproved
by its own numbers**, animates the **wrong gestalt** (box-zoom for a field), and has
**zero Safari evidence**. Ship G1–G5. Send G6 back for the re-spec in the hardening list
and re-gate on the *measured* punch, not the ease label.

**Top refutation:** R1 — the reveal bloom's cartoon-punch is structurally gutted to
~3.5% (live-measured `peakScaleX 1.0347`) versus the §G6 "~22% overshoot" headline,
because the 1.22 ease is multiplied by a 0.20 keyframe delta; the substrate's one
perceptible moment lands with no FLOW & PUNCH.
