# CHALLENGE #1 — CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS

**Verdict: REFUTED (survives the architecture, FAILS the live-verification claim).**

The GOLDEN's *structural* idea (buttons own no material; compose the tabs `.glass-capsule`)
is fit, DRY, and deftly integrable — that survives clean. But the GOLDEN's headline proof —
"the boldest mechanism is **live-verified** by a throwaway spike, GREEN both modes" — is FALSE
as shipped. The spike's gate is a hardcoded-field arithmetic theatre that measures the backdrop,
not the button, and the spike de-risks a fill mechanism that is NOT the one the tabs GOLDEN ships.
The §8 born-RED gate inherits the same defect verbatim. A green here would survive even a pure-gray
button. That is exactly the "computed-not-measured / hardcoded-hsl" failure §8 forbids in its own words.

---

## TOP REFUTATION (load-bearing) — the spike gate's `restChroma` measures the FIELD, not the button. The "live-verified GREEN" is a false positive.

`golden/spike.html` `fillChroma()` (lines 173-184) and `gate.json` claim `restChroma: 0.0892`
(light) / `0.0739` (dark), both `>> 0.02` → "warm, not gray, GREEN both modes." **Reproduced live
and the number is a lie of construction:**

1. `const bg = getComputedStyle(el).backgroundColor` resolves to **`oklab(0.992 0.00176 0.0042 / 0.59)`**
   (live readback on the Vite-served spike — confirmed).
2. `bg.match(/[\d.]+/g)` then parses `[r,g,b,alpha] = [0.992, 0.00176, 0.0042, 0.59]` — it grabs the
   **oklab L/a/b coordinates and treats them as sRGB 0-255 channels.** So the "button color" the gate
   composites is `(0.992, 0.00176, 0.0042)` ≈ pure near-black at ~0/255.
3. It then composites that over a **hardcoded purple field `(120,60,180)`** at `alpha 0.59`:
   `cr,cg,cb = (49.8, 24.6, 73.8)` → `oklab chroma = 0.0892`. **I reproduced 0.0892 exactly.**

The reported chroma is the **hardcoded purple field's** chroma, scaled by `(1 - 0.59)`. The button's
actual warm fill contributes ~nothing (it was parsed as near-zero). **The gate passes 0.02 even if the
button is pure CSS `gray`** — swap the fill to `#808080` and `0.0892` is unchanged, because the chroma
is sourced from the constant `(120,60,180)` literal, not the paint. This is precisely the
"hardcoded-hsl spike" / "computed-not-measured" the GOLDEN's §8 prose explicitly bans ("no arithmetic,
no stop-string, no computed-not-measured … never a hardcoded-hsl spike, sample the painted chroma over
the LIVE field"). The spike does NOT sample painted pixels; it does sRGB arithmetic on a hardcoded
backdrop. **The boldest-mechanism de-risk is unproven.** When I read the actual resolved fill on the
live spike and composited it honestly, the chroma falls to **~0.017 — BELOW the gate's own 0.02 bar.**

### Hardening
Replace `fillChroma()` with a REAL painted-pixel read: `html2canvas`/`drawWindow` or `getImageMap`
the rendered capsule region over the live field, sample the center pixel(s), convert *that sRGB* to
oklab. No `getComputedStyle().backgroundColor`, no hardcoded `(120,60,180)`. The Playwright §8 gate
must `page.screenshot({clip})` the button bbox and read the center pixel — never read a token/computed
value. Add a detector bite the GOLDEN already names but does NOT actually have: a `gray-pinned` control
button must drive arm-1 RED; today it would pass because the field supplies the chroma.

## REFUTATION 2 — the spike de-risks a fill mechanism the tabs GOLDEN does NOT ship. §9's "EXACT shipping mechanism" claim is false.

§9: "mirrors the EXACT shipping mechanism: the warm-floor `.glass-capsule` fill … the warm-admit
`color-mix(in oklab, …, --glass-tint-source <floor>)`."

- **Spike (line 16, 66):** invents `--glass-capsule-floor: 18%` (light) / `26%` (dark) and fills via
  `color-mix(in oklab, var(--glass-bg-floating), var(--glass-tint-source) var(--glass-capsule-floor))`.
- **Tabs GOLDEN (the actual upstream this consumes), GOLDEN.md:117 + tabs/GOLDEN.md:28,141,489:**
  the capsule fills from **`--glass-bg-floating-tinted`** (the W55 `surfaces.css:283` adaptive seam) —
  NOT a new `--glass-capsule-floor` var, NOT a button-authored floor.

`--glass-capsule-floor` exists in NO src file and in NO tabs GOLDEN. It is a THIRD invented recipe —
exactly the "button-local warm-floor `color-mix` (a THIRD diverging recipe)" that the GOLDEN's own
lens-b reconcile (§1) and §8 detector bite ("a button-local warm-floor color-mix … → arm 3 RED")
declare a DRY violation. The spike proves a mechanism that, by the GOLDEN's own law, must NOT ship.
Worse: the real seam `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` is `light-dark()`/`@media`-driven and depends on
`--glass-tint-strength` (live `0%` at rest per §0) — so the spike's hand-set `18%/26%` floor proves
NOTHING about whether the real W55-seam capsule clears 0.02 at rest with `tint-strength: 0%`. The
load-bearing question (does the SHIPPING seam go warm at rest?) is untouched by the spike.

### Hardening
The spike MUST fill the capsule from `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` (the real W55 token), with
`--glass-tint-strength: 0%` as live, and reproduce the seam's actual `light-dark()`/`@media` arms —
then measure painted chroma. If the real seam does NOT clear 0.02 at rest with `tint-strength: 0%`,
the GOLDEN's "fixed for free by the tabs capsule" thesis collapses and the tabs GOLDEN itself must
introduce the warm floor on the seam (a real upstream dependency to name, not assume). Delete every
reference to `--glass-capsule-floor`; it is a phantom.

## REFUTATION 3 — three §8 gate arms are NOT born-RED measurements; they are static-constant asserts (the gate is not actually painted-pixel).

The `window.__gate()` (spike lines 185-210), which the §8 gate mirrors:

- **arm 4 (hover lift):** `const hoverScale = 1.015, hoverSpecular = 0.14;` — **literal constants**,
  not a read of the painted `:hover` scale. The gate asserts `1.015 > 1` — a tautology. A `scale:1`-pinned
  button would still report `1.015` and PASS. §8's detector bite ("a `scale:1`-pinned control → arm 4
  RED") is unenforceable as written.
- **arm 6 (anticipation/overshoot):** `anticipates = /-0\.0\d/.test(curve)` and `overshoots = /1\.[12]\d/.test(curve)`
  are **regex over the token TEXT** (`--ease-cartoon-punch`), not a sample of the painted center-Y
  dipping below origin. This is the exact "stop-string" match §8 bans. A `linear()` whose negative arm
  is at `-0.09` but never actually moves the rendered element would pass.
- **arm "punchPeakArea":** `1.09 * (1 - 0.04)` — pure arithmetic on the JS `maxStretch` literal, not a
  measured painted bbox area. The area "fence" is asserted, never measured.

So 3 of the headline gate arms are computed-from-constants. The GOLDEN's §8 preamble ("judge painted
pixels — no arithmetic, no stop-string, no computed-not-measured") is contradicted by its own §9 reference
implementation. The gate is born-GREEN-by-construction for these arms, not born-RED.

### Hardening
Arm 4: drive a real `:hover` and read `getBoundingClientRect()` width delta (or screenshot-diff the
bbox) — a painted scale > 1. Arm 6: `pointerdown` then sample the element's painted center-Y across
rAF frames; assert it moves OPPOSITE (dips) before depressing, by ≥1px — a measured pre-dip, not a
regex. Punch area: measure the painted bbox W·H ratio at peak frame, assert ≤1.14. Until these read
paint, §8 is not a born-RED gate and must not be claimed as one.

## REFUTATION 4 — the `--glass-blur-btn` blur is DROPPED in the spike's "exact mechanism," masking a real cross-engine question.

`.btn-glass` ships `backdrop-filter: var(--glass-blur-btn)` (surfaces.css:188) and `.glass-deep`
re-points it to `--glass-blur-deep` (surfaces.css:225). The spike hardcodes
`--glass-blur-btn: blur(13px) saturate(1.7) brightness(1.02)` (line 20) and applies it on `.glass-capsule`
directly. But the GOLDEN's class string is `glass-capsule glass-capsule-hover btn-glass glass-deep` —
i.e., in the real build BOTH `.glass-capsule` (if it also sets a backdrop) AND `.btn-glass` set
`backdrop-filter` on the SAME element. `backdrop-filter` does not stack; the last-cascaded wins. The
GOLDEN never resolves which class owns the backdrop, and the spike can't surface the conflict because it
collapsed both into one hardcoded value. On WebKit the `saturate(1.7)` over a desaturating field is the
exact gray-risk the no-gray law warns of — and the spike's hardcoded `saturate(1.7)` was never validated
against the seam's real `saturate` (the tabs capsule blur may differ).

### Hardening
The GOLDEN §2a must state explicitly which class owns `backdrop-filter` (recommend: `.glass-capsule`
owns the blur, drop `.btn-glass` from the class string OR scope `.btn-glass`'s backdrop to
`:where(.btn-glass):not(.glass-capsule)`). Resolve the double-`backdrop-filter` cascade in writing. The
spike must read `--glass-blur-btn`/`--glass-blur-deep` from the real tokens, not a literal, and verify
the WebKit `saturate()` path does not desaturate to gray over the worst-case field (a real screenshot,
not arithmetic).

## REFUTATION 5 — DockIconButton selected-fill fold: the `::before` material fork is real, but the GOLDEN under-specifies the JOIN and risks a parallel fork.

Live `/dock` readback: `.dock-icon-button` rest is `rgba(0,0,0,0)` (confirmed transparent — lens-a's
hairline rest is correct). The selected fill is `--dock-control-active-bg` painted on `::before`
(DockIconButton.vue:42, material.css fork — confirmed). The GOLDEN says "re-points its
`--dock-control-active-bg` `::before` fill onto `.glass-capsule`." But `.glass-capsule` is a *class with
a `background`*, not a *token*. You cannot "re-point a `::before` fill onto a class." Either (a) the
`::before` keeps its own `background` and you re-point the TOKEN `--dock-control-active-bg` to the
capsule's resolved fill (a token alias — but then it's not "the same class," it's a copy = a fork that
drifts), or (b) you move the capsule onto the `::before` selector (real composition — but `.glass-capsule`
sets `border-radius`/`box-shadow`/`backdrop-filter` that may fight the `::before` geometry). The GOLDEN
does not say which. This is exactly the bolt-on-vs-union ambiguity the challenge targets.

### Hardening
Specify the mechanism: make `.dock-icon-button[data-active]::before` literally read the capsule's fill
**token** the tabs GOLDEN must EXPOSE (e.g. `--glass-capsule-fill`), not re-derive it — one token, one
source, both consume. Verify arm-7 ("dock SELECTED resolves the SAME computed `background-color` as a
selected tab + a glass button") by reading all three computed fills live and asserting byte-equality;
today they are three different code paths (`::before` token vs `.glass-capsule` class vs CVA string)
and equality is asserted, not measured.

## REFUTATION 6 — `gold-audacious` accent-in-gleam (arm 9) has no spike coverage of the WebKit `plus-lighter` path; the gold may not read.

The spike's gold (line 104) sets `--glass-accent: var(--color-gold)` and the gleam uses
`mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter` (line 88). `plus-lighter` is the one blend mode with documented WebKit
fragility when the layer has sub-1 alpha or sits over a backdrop-filtered element (it can clamp/no-op on
older Safari, falling back to `normal`). The gate's arm 9 ("gold reads in the gleam, accent-channel
luminance non-zero at the grazing edge") is in the §8 table but is NOT in the spike's `__gate()` at all —
there is zero readback for it. The boldest *visible* gold claim is entirely unverified, on the exact
blend mode most likely to differ Chrome↔Safari.

### Hardening
Add a gold-gleam readback to the spike that samples the painted grazing-edge pixel and asserts a gold
a-channel lift in oklab — in BOTH a Blink and a WebKit run (paired-engine, as §6 demands). If
`plus-lighter` no-ops on WebKit, the gold needs a `@supports`-gated or `screen`-blend fallback. Until a
WebKit screenshot shows the gold, arm 9 is RED-in-name-only.

## What SURVIVES (fit — keep, refine)

- **The structural thesis** (buttons own no material; compose `.glass-capsule` + `.glass-capsule-hover`;
  one recipe for buttons + tabs + dock-tabs) is genuinely DRY/KISS and a true union. KEEP.
- **§0's defect diagnosis** is largely source-accurate: the shipping `default` is
  `glass-wash btn-glass glass-deep` with `hover:bg-* scale:1` (verified index.ts:54) — surface-only
  hover, no lift. The gray-at-rest is the `glass-wash` fill's warm-admit gap. Correct.
- **PRM/a11y carve** (§7) is sound and reuses the existing `--glass-level: 0` opaque escape + `.focus-ring`
  — no bespoke arm. KEEP.
- **No `backdrop-filter: url()`, no SVG goo, no meatball on buttons** (§6) is correct — buttons are
  lozenge glass; the metaball law is N/A. KEEP.
- **`.btn-punch` as ONE opt-in decoration** parallel to `surface=`/`liquid=` is the right altitude
  (one new class, no new material/component/spring). KEEP — but its gate must measure paint (Ref 3).

## Bottom line

The DESIGN survives; the PROOF does not. The GOLDEN must not ship the sentence "live-verified … GREEN
both modes" — it is a hardcoded-field arithmetic gate that would pass a gray button (Ref 1), de-risks a
phantom `--glass-capsule-floor` mechanism that won't ship (Ref 2), and asserts 3 motion arms from
constants instead of paint (Ref 3). Re-spike against the REAL `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` seam at
`tint-strength: 0%`, read painted pixels (screenshot-clip, not `getComputedStyle`), run paired-engine,
and prove the gold gleam on WebKit. Then the boldest mechanism is actually de-risked.
