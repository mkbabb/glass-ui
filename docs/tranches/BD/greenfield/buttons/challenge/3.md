## R1 (TOP, LANDS) — the spike's "live-verified gray fix" is a FIELD-BLEED ARTEFACT, not a warm-admit proof. The gate does not bite gray.

The single load-bearing claim of the GOLDEN (§9, the boldest-mechanism de-risk) is that
the warm-floor `.glass-capsule` fill reads `restChroma 0.0892 light / 0.0739 dark` over
the vibrant field — "GREEN both modes, never gray." **This number does not measure the
glass warmth. It measures the purple field bleeding through the fill's alpha.**

Proof (Node, exact spike math):
- The warm source the spike + the real codebase use is `--card` = `hsl(30 85% 96%)`
  (light) / `hsl(26 22% 17%)` (dark). At L=96% an HSL-85%-sat colour collapses in oklab:
  **true oklab chroma of `hsl(30 85% 96%)` = 0.0148 — BELOW the 0.02 gate floor.** The
  warm fill itself is near-gray. (This is exactly why §0 measures the rest fill at 0.0138.)
- The spike's `fillChroma()` does NOT sample a painted pixel of the fill. It reads
  `getComputedStyle(el).backgroundColor` (a translucent colour, alpha ≈ 0.5), then
  **composites it over a hardcoded purple `(120,60,180)`** and returns the chroma of THAT
  composite. Result: 0.087 — the purple field, not the glass.
- Swap the warm `--card` for a pure-neutral gray fill at the same alpha over the same
  purple and the gate returns the SAME ~0.087 → PASS. **The detector self-test bite
  "a `--glass-tint-strength`-pinned-0 control → arm 1/2 RED" (§8, §9) is FALSE.** At any
  floor, over a colourful field (which §3 *mandates* exists), the bleed dominates and the
  arm passes whether the glass is warm or gray.

This is the precise inflation the user's own lessons name (`feedback_live_verify_capture`,
`feedback_live_pi_oklab_paint_arm`): "live-verified" is claimed from arithmetic over a
computed string, not a captured painted-pixel DELTA. The GOLDEN even *cites* its §8 law
("judge painted pixels — no arithmetic, no stop-string, no computed-not-measured") and
then ships a §9 spike that is **all three**: arithmetic (`punchPeakArea = 1.09*0.96`),
stop-string (`anticipates = /-0\.0\d/.test(curve)`, `overshoots = /1\.[12]\d/.test(curve)`),
and computed-not-measured (`hoverScale = 1.015` and `hoverSpecular = 0.14` are HARDCODED
CONSTANTS returned by `__gate`, never read from paint). `spike-light.png` / `spike-dark.png`
exist but no pixel from them feeds the verdict — they are decorative.

**Why it matters to the user (gestalt):** the user rejected the buttons as "flat translucent
gray pills." A gate that cannot distinguish warm-cream glass from gray glass over the field
will pass a still-gray build. The user will re-reject the exact same defect, and the gate
will still say GREEN. The "boldest mechanism de-risked before any src/ change" is undriven.

**HARDENING (R1):** the spike + the real π MUST sample a **painted pixel of the capsule
interior over the field MINUS the same pixel of the bare field behind it** — the warm-admit
DELTA, isolating the glass contribution from the bleed. Concretely: render the capsule, read
the centre pixel `P_glass`; read the field pixel at the same coords with the capsule removed
`P_field`; the gate asserts `chroma(P_glass) − chroma(P_field) ≥ Δ` AND that the residual hue
is warm (oklab `a>0, b>0`, the warm quadrant), not merely that the composite is colourful.
Replace the three hardcoded/regex arms with real readback: `hoverScale` from a measured
`getBoundingClientRect` ratio on `:hover`, the punch area from the measured X·Y of the
mid-flight transform, the anticipation from a sampled centre-Y curve, never `.test()` on the
token TEXT. Capture the PNG delta as the artefact. Until then §9 is REMOVED, not "GREEN."

## R2 (LANDS) — the consumed `.glass-capsule` (as the upstream tabs GOLDEN actually mints it) is STILL GRAY at rest. The dependency does not deliver the warm floor the buttons assume.

The buttons GOLDEN's whole thesis is "own NO material — CONSUME the tabs GOLDEN's
`.glass-capsule`, the gray dies ONCE upstream." But read what tabs GOLDEN §2b *actually
mints*:

```css
.glass-capsule { background: var(--glass-bg-floating-tinted); … }
```

and `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` (`surfaces.css:283`) `= color-mix(in oklab,
--glass-bg-floating, --glass-tint-source × --glass-tint-strength)`. At the `:root` default
`--glass-tint-strength: 0%` (verified the live default), this resolves to **pure
`--glass-bg-floating`** — the `--card`-derived near-gray, chroma 0.0138. **The tabs GOLDEN
mints NO non-zero floor anywhere** — grep of `tabs/GOLDEN.md` for `--glass-tint-strength:`
or a `floor:` declaration returns ZERO CSS decls, only PROSE ("the warm admit-floor lifts it
back," line 130) with no mechanism behind it. The buttons GOLDEN therefore consumes a capsule
that is gray-by-default, then *claims* the gray is fixed because it consumed the capsule. The
fix is asserted in two docs and implemented in neither.

Meanwhile the spike *invents a different mechanism that neither doc ships*:
`--glass-capsule-floor: 18%` mixing toward a hand-authored `--glass-tint-source:
hsl(30 85% 96%)` — an independent warm floor decoupled from `--glass-tint-strength`. So the
"de-risk" validates a third recipe (the very button-local-warm-floor `color-mix` that §8's
detector calls a "THIRD recipe → arm 3 RED"), while the actual build path (`var(--glass-bg-
floating-tinted)` at 0%) is never exercised.

**HARDENING (R2):** decide the ONE warm-floor mechanism and put it in the upstream tabs
GOLDEN's `.glass-capsule` CSS, not in prose and not only in a throwaway spike. Either (a)
the capsule fill becomes `color-mix(in oklab, --glass-bg-floating-tinted, --glass-tint-source
<floor>)` with a *named, non-zero* `--glass-capsule-floor` minted at `:root` (then the spike's
mechanism IS the shipped one and R1's delta-gate bites it), or (b) raise the rest
`--glass-tint-strength` floor globally (a larger blast radius — must re-gate every existing
consumer). Pick one, write the decl, and make the buttons GOLDEN's "consume" point at a
capsule that is provably warm at `tint-strength: 0%`. Add the cross-check: BA.W-NO-GRAY's
`meanChroma` arm must run on the capsule at the rest default with NO field behind it (a black
backdrop) so the floor — not the bleed — is what passes.

## R3 (LANDS, soft) — the warm source is HIGH-L LOW-CHROMA; even a correctly-admitted floor may not clear a *perceptual* warm read. The 0.02 threshold is itself too weak.

Even granting R2 a real floor, mixing 18% toward `hsl(30 85% 96%)` (chroma 0.0148) barely
moves the needle — the *source* is near-gray, so admitting more of it admits more pale-cream,
not more chroma. The user's edict is "perfected warm-cream glass, a COLOURFUL field behind a
DEFINED edge" — the warmth must be *perceptible against* the field, not a 0.02 arithmetic
floor that `hsl(30 85% 96%)` itself nearly fails. A 0.02 bar that the bare warm source
under-clears is not a "never gray" guarantee; it is a rounding error.

**HARDENING (R3):** the warm-admit target must be a HIGHER-chroma cream than `--card`'s
near-white plate — a dedicated `--glass-warm-floor-tint` at, say, `oklch(0.92 0.04 70)`
(perceptibly cream, chroma ~0.04) so the admitted floor delivers a *visible* warmth, and
the gate bar rises to a perceptual delta (R1's `P_glass − P_field ≥ 0.025` in the warm
quadrant) rather than a 0.02 absolute that the source nearly fails on its own.

## R5 (LANDS, motion gestalt) — the calm-default hover is a 1.5% scale + 0.14 specular: that is the TIGHT/SPRINGY-adjacent timidity the user explicitly rejects, dressed as "liquid-weight universal."

The user's bar: "LIQUID-WEIGHT UNIVERSAL — inertia/weight/bounce/squish on ALL motion;
morph MORE on move; NEVER tight/springy." The GOLDEN's universal hover is `scale: 1.015` on
a 160ms BEZIER (`--ease-standard`, `--duration-fast`) with no spring, no overshoot, no
squish — a 1.5% lift on a plain cubic-bezier. That is the *definition* of a tight, weightless
micro-transition. The cartoon-punch (weight/anticipation/overshoot/squash) is gated OUT of the
default and the entire dock ("a dock of punching icons would be manic" → calm). So the surface
the user touches 95% of the time — the default button, every dock icon — carries NONE of the
liquid-weight register. The GOLDEN hands the user back a 1.5% bezier nudge and calls it the
fix for "better hover states." Gestalt: still timid, still flat-feeling on hover, no perceived
mass. The cartoon soul lives only in an opt-in `.btn-punch` two hero CTAs use.

**HARDENING (R5):** elevate the DEFAULT hover/press toward the liquid-weight register, not
just the opt-in tier: (a) the default hover lift rides the spring clock with a *hair* of
overshoot (the `--spring-snappy` settle, not a dead bezier) and `--motion-weight` co-scales it
so it reads as mass rising, not a CSS scale tick; (b) the default press squish amplitude lifts
off the timid 1.04 `maxStretch` toward a perceptible (still-fenced ≤1.14 area) deform so the
press has WEIGHT for everyone; (c) keep `.btn-punch` as the LOUD tier but make the default
*itself* liquid-weighted, not bezier-flat. "Universal" must mean the default, not the opt-in.

## R7 (SOFT, cross-engine) — the Safari claim is sound BY CONSTRUCTION but UNTESTED on WebKit; one real WebKit risk (plus-lighter over backdrop-filter) is hand-waved.

§6 argues every channel is compositor-only + Safari-native (no `backdrop-filter:url`, static
`color-mix`, `plus-lighter` `::before`, `box-shadow` rim). The reasoning is correct and the
no-url law is honoured (verified: no `backdrop-filter:url` on any button). BUT the spike ran
**Chrome only** (`gate.json`: "verified: Chrome, file:// spike.html") — zero WebKit readback —
and the GOLDEN markets it as "Chrome==Safari by construction." `mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter`
on a `::before` *over a `backdrop-filter` element* has known WebKit compositing-order quirks
(the gleam can composite against the un-blurred backing on Safari, not the blurred plate). The
"by construction" claim is exactly the cardinal-lesson WebKit hand-wave the user keeps flagging.

**HARDENING (R7):** the paired-engine π (§8 arm 11 promises chromium+webkit) MUST actually run
on WebKit before any GREEN — specifically the `plus-lighter` gleam composite over the
`backdrop-filter` plate, and the `--glass-specular` `@property` interpolation (WebKit
`@property` support is newer than Blink's). Capture a webkit screenshot delta, not a "by
construction" assertion.
