# BUTTONS GOLDEN — Adversarial Challenge #2 (cross-engine / Safari / perf lens)

> Lens: Safari+Chrome parity, compositor/perf, a11y/PRM, naive-meatball, overfit.
> Default verdict: REFUTED. Live-driven on Chrome :5173 + the golden's own `spike.html`.
> Verdict: **the golden's DESIGN survives; the golden's SPIKE + its headline born-RED
> chroma gate (arms 1/2) do NOT — they are GREEN-by-construction theatre that masks a
> near-gray fill (live-measured 0.0046, 4.3× under the 0.02 floor).** This is the SAME
> fake-chroma-gate disease the hardening plan already caught on the glass-material sibling
> (R1: "spike self-fails its own bar by 2.3–3.5× via a hardcoded fieldSample"). The buttons

## R1 — TOP REFUTATION (LOAD-BEARING, live-proven): the spike's chroma gate is a fabricated GREEN; the proposed capsule fill is near-gray (0.0046 ≪ 0.02)

The golden's single boldest claim is "warm capsule, never gray, both modes" with §9 reporting
`restChroma light 0.0892 / dark 0.0739` and a §0 bar of `≥ 0.02`. **Both numbers are false.**

**Live readback (Chrome, the golden's own `golden/spike.html`, `#b-default`):**

```
computed fill = oklab(0.992038 0.00176425 0.00420139 / 0.59)
HONEST fill chroma = hypot(0.00176, 0.0042) = 0.00456   →  4.3× UNDER the 0.02 floor → NEAR-GRAY
```

The fill is `color-mix(in oklab, var(--glass-bg-floating), var(--glass-tint-source) 18%)`
where `--glass-bg-floating = white@50%` and `--glass-tint-source = hsl(30 85% 96%)`. Mixing
white with a **96%-lightness** cream at only **18%** yields a near-white, near-colorless fill.
This is the EXACT defect the golden claims to cure (§0 indicts the ship `default` at chroma
0.0138 — the proposed capsule is WORSE: 0.0046).

**Why the gate reports 0.0892 anyway — two stacked bugs in `__gate`/`fillChroma` (spike.html:173–184):**

1. **oklab-as-sRGB type confusion.** `bg.match(/[\d.]+/g)` on `"oklab(0.992 0.00176 0.0042 / 0.59)"`
   extracts `[0.992, 0.00176, 0.0042, 0.59]` and feeds them straight into `srgbToOklab(r,g,b)`
   **as if they were 0–255 sRGB channels.** It also silently drops minus signs (oklab a/b can be
   negative). The "fill color" the gate measures is garbage.
2. **Hardcoded purple field-bleed.** It then composites that garbage over a HARDCODED
   `(120,60,180)` purple at `alpha=0.59`, so ~99% of the measured pixel is the purple field.
   The reported "0.0892 chroma" is **the purple radial-gradient's chroma, not the glass's.**

This violates the golden's OWN §8 binding bar three ways: "judge **painted pixels** — no
arithmetic, no stop-string, **no computed-not-measured**." The gate reads
`getComputedStyle().backgroundColor` (computed, NOT painted-through-the-13px-backdrop-filter)
AND does broken arithmetic on it AND hardcodes the field. The §8 self-test bite
("a `--glass-tint-strength`-pinned-0 control → arm 1/2 RED") would itself pass this broken gate
because the gate never reads the real fill.

**The user-facing consequence (the whole point):** the "warmth" is **41% field show-through**
(`alpha 0.59` → `1−0.59 = 41%`), not warm glass. Place this capsule over a GRAY page (e.g. the
real `/forms/select` flat field, body bg `transparent` — confirmed live) and it reads GRAY —
the user's original complaint, undelivered. The golden mistakes "purple bleeds through a
colorless pill" for "warm liquid glass."

**HARDENING (folds, does not kill the design):**
- The fill must carry **intrinsic** warm chroma at rest, measured over a NEUTRAL/gray field,
  not a vibrant one. Re-derive `--glass-capsule-floor` (or the mix recipe) so the COMPUTED
  fill chroma — read from the `oklab()` string directly — clears ≥0.02 over a gray backdrop
  in BOTH modes. With `hsl(30 85% 96%)` at 18% this is impossible; either raise the floor
  to ~40–55% or (better) mix toward a LOWER-lightness, higher-chroma warm anchor
  (the live ship dock fill `color(srgb 0.994 0.96 0.926)` H≈68 is already warmer than this
  proposed capsule — the golden REGRESSES warmth; consume that proven anchor instead of minting a paler one).
- Rewrite `fillChroma` to parse the `oklab()` components correctly (sign-aware), measure
  over a NEUTRAL field, and (per §8) read a PAINTED pixel through the backdrop-filter via a
  real screenshot/getImageData, with a flat-field self-test (the glass-material R3 fix).
- This is a BUILD-blocker for the headline gate arms 1/2/3, exactly as the sibling
  glass-material/cartoon-shadow/aurora spikes were each re-based to real paint.

## R2 — the spike's chroma "both modes" claim is doubly unproven: dark arm never measured + `light-dark()` ink mismatch

`gate.json` reports a `dark` block, but the spike has NO mechanism to flip to dark for the
readback (the `.dark` class is authored on the stylesheet but `__gate` reads
`document.documentElement.classList.contains('dark')` and nothing toggles it during the run).
The dark numbers are produced by the SAME broken arithmetic over the SAME hardcoded LIGHT
purple field — they are not a dark-mode measurement at all. The dark field constant
`(120,60,180)` is never swapped for the `.dark body` field. So "GREEN both modes" rests on a
single light pass run twice.

Separately: the button ink uses `color: light-dark(...)` (spike.html:98) but `color-scheme`
is set on `:root`/`.dark` — under a real `.dark` toggle the `light-dark()` ink flips correctly,
but the gate never exercises it, so the AA-over-warm-fill claim (§7 "warmer ≠ less legible")
is unverified in the load-bearing dark mode where the fill+ink are both warm.

**HARDENING:** the π must actually toggle `.dark` (and swap the field constant) between the
two passes; capture two real painted frames; assert AA contrast of the resolved ink over the
resolved fill in EACH mode (the golden's §8 arm 1 says "both modes" — make it real).

## R3 — cross-engine: the design IS compositor-safe, but two Safari-specific claims are asserted, not proven

The golden's §6 is mostly correct and is the design's strongest section — no `backdrop-filter:url`,
no SVG goo, no meatball (correctly N/A — buttons are lozenges; the naive-ellipsoid/metaball law
does not bind here). The compositor-only channels (`scale`, `box-shadow`, `blur()`,
`plus-lighter ::before`, an `@property` scalar) are genuinely Chrome==Safari by construction.
But three load-bearing cross-engine assertions are stated without a WebKit measurement:

1. **`linear()` easing (`--ease-cartoon-punch`) in Safari.** CSS `linear()` easing shipped in
   Safari 17.2 (Dec 2023). The golden assumes universal support; on Safari ≤17.1 the
   `transition-timing-function: linear(...)` is INVALID and the UA falls back to `ease` —
   the anticipation pre-dip and overshoot (§4b arms 1/3) **silently vanish**, degrading the
   hero CTA to a plain ease with no cartoon punch, with no `@supports` floor declared. The
   §8 gate is `chromium + webkit` but pins no minimum WebKit version, so a modern-Safari pass
   hides the older-Safari degrade. (No correctness break — the press still functions — but the
   "perfect in Safari by construction" claim is version-conditional and unstated.)

2. **`@property` registration of `--glass-specular` animating in WebKit.** The hover specular
   bloom relies on `transition: --glass-specular ...` over a registered `@property` scalar.
   This is sound in current Safari, but it is the SAME registered-custom-property-transition
   the golden leans on for the headline "better hover" — and the spike never measures the
   specular `::before` opacity actually interpolating in WebKit (it `static asserts hoverSpecular = 0.14`
   in JS, spike.html:190 — a hardcoded literal, not a painted delta). §8 arm 4 says "painted
   `--glass-specular` bloom delta > 0"; the spike fakes it with `const hoverSpecular = 0.14`.

3. **`mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter` over the warm fill in WebKit.** Already used in
   `material.css` (Safari 16.4+ per the source comment), so this one is grounded — but the
   spike's `::before` gleam color is `var(--glass-accent, white)` at `plus-lighter`; over the
   near-WHITE near-gray fill (R1), `white plus-lighter white` clamps to white with almost no
   visible bloom. The hover "lift" the user asked for is mostly the 1.5% scale; the specular
   channel is near-invisible on a pale fill in BOTH engines. The two-channel hover collapses
   toward one channel.

**HARDENING:** declare an `@supports` floor for `linear()` (or a damped-spring fallback curve
gated for older WebKit), pin a WebKit minimum in the π, and measure the specular `::before`
PAINTED opacity delta on real `:hover` in webkit (not a JS literal). Fix R1 first — a
chromatic fill is what makes the `plus-lighter` gleam READ.

## R4 — perf: the cast `::after { z-index: -1 }` forces a stacking context + a real Safari paint concern; the press `--press-t` write is unbudgeted

1. **`.btn-punch::after { z-index: -1 }`** (spike.html:108) requires the button to establish a
   stacking context (it does via `position: relative`), but a NEGATIVE-z child paints BEHIND
   the element's own background — over a `backdrop-filter` parent this is a known
   compositor-layer-promotion point. On a DOCK of buttons (the golden permits `punch` on dock
   CTAs), each punching button promotes its own layer + a behind-layer for the cast; on Safari
   the backdrop-filter + extra composited layer per button is a measured scroll/paint cost the
   golden never budgets. §6 claims "compositor-cheap" but never records a frame number on a
   button GRID (the real `/display/buttons` has 61 buttons live).

2. **`--press-t` transition + the `useLiquidFlex` imperative `scale` write.** §6 says "never a
   CSS transition on the same scalar (no double-drive)" — but the spike DOES transition
   `--press-t` is not transitioned (good), yet `.btn-punch::after { transition: transform ...
   var(--ease-cartoon-punch) }` AND `:active { transform: translate(-3px,-4px) }` run a CSS
   transition on the cast WHILE `useLiquidFlex` imperatively writes `scale` on the parent — two
   independent compositor animations per press, per button. On a punch-dock that is N×2
   concurrent transform animations. No frame-budget gate exists in §8 (arm 6 measures area, not
   frame time). The hardening-plan's motion-spring sibling explicitly added a "born-RED rAF
   frame-budget gate" for exactly this; buttons has none.

**HARDENING:** add a §8 frame-budget arm on a button GRID (≥20 punching buttons) in BOTH
engines (the motion-spring-register precedent); cap concurrent punch instances or document the
dock-default-calm rule as a perf invariant, not just an aesthetic one; verify the negative-z
cast does not over-promote layers in Safari (or move the cast to a `0`-z inert child behind via
`isolation`, the cartoon-shadow sibling's H2 inert-child caster fix — which buttons should
DRY-consume rather than re-inventing a `::after` caster, see R5).

## R5 — DRY/KISS: the `::after` caster RE-INVENTS the cartoon-shadow sibling's caster instead of consuming it

The golden §4b authors a NEW `::after` cartoon cast on `.btn-punch`. But the cartoon-shadow
greenfield (same tranche, hardening-plan row "cartoon-shadow") already landed a caster contract:
its H2 fix moved the caster onto an **inert `<span class="cartoon-cast" aria-hidden>` child**
precisely BECAUSE `::before`/`::after` are OCCUPIED by glass pseudos on the real carrier — and
the buttons capsule ALSO occupies `::before` (the specular gleam). The buttons golden puts the
cast on `::after`, which is free on the capsule TODAY, but this is a parallel caster fork: two
greenfields, two caster mechanisms, for the same 1940s-cel cast. The golden's §5 ledger claims
to "compose `.shadow-cartoon-{md,lg}` + `::after` caster (`.cartoon-surface` precedent)" but
`.cartoon-surface` is a STATIC shadow utility (cards.css:178) — it is NOT a moving press caster;
the moving caster is the cartoon-shadow greenfield's `BD.W-CARTOON-CASTER`. The golden cites the
wrong precedent and re-invents the right one.

Also the glyph overlapping-pop (§4b.4) is claimed as "the tabs GOLDEN glyph scale-pop register"
(CONSUME) but the spike HARD-CODES `scale: calc(1 + 0.06 * var(--motion-weight))` inline
(spike.html:115) — if the tabs register owns it, the button must reference the tabs class/var,
not re-author the formula (a third copy of the same `0.06` literal across tabs/cartoon/buttons).

**HARDENING:** consume `BD.W-CARTOON-CASTER`'s inert-child caster + its registered
`--cartoon-press-t`/`--cast-travel`/`--cast-spread` props (which the cartoon-shadow H1 fix proved
must be `@property`-registered or the press caster reads `translate 0px` and is DEAD) — the
buttons spike's `::after { transform: translate(-3px,-4px) }` is a static literal that does NOT
carry the registered-prop weight scaling and will go inert under the same H1 trap. Reference the
tabs glyph-pop class instead of re-pasting the `0.06` formula.

## R6 — born-RED honesty: arms 1/2/4/8/9 are born-RED on disk, but the gate that PROVES them is the broken one

The structural born-RED claims check out on disk (verified by grep):
`.glass-capsule` / `.glass-capsule-hover` — ABSENT in `src/` (genuinely born-RED, the tabs
golden mints them). `--motion-weight` / `--ease-cartoon-punch` — ABSENT in `src/styles/`
(genuine phantoms/DEPENDs). `--shadow-cartoon-{md,lg}` — PRESENT (shadow.css:95/98, correct
KEEP). `--glass-accent` — PRESENT (property-regs.css:178, correct DEPEND). `v-specular` +
`useSpringPress` + `useLiquidFlex` — PRESENT (correct KEEPs). The live `/display/buttons`
shows the §0 baseline accurately (transparent rest + `color(srgb 0.994 0.96 0.926 / 0.8)`
active fill). **So the audit and the structural gate (arm 3) are honest.**

The problem is arms 1/2/4/8/9 (the CHROMA + LIFT + GLEAM arms) are "proven" by the §9 spike
whose detector is broken (R1). A gate that cannot distinguish a 0.0046 fill from a 0.0892 field
is not born-RED-credible — it would pass the defect it is meant to catch. The §8 self-test bite
"a `--glass-tint-strength`-pinned-0 control → arm 1/2 RED" is itself defeated by the field-bleed:
pin the floor to 0 and the gate STILL reports ~0.089 because it is measuring the purple field.

**HARDENING:** the detector self-test must include a **gray-field control** (not just a
tint-pinned control) — a colorless fill over a GRAY backdrop must read RED. Only a detector that
fails on the gray-field control can certify arms 1/2. Re-run §9 with the corrected detector
before the spike is cited as de-risk.

## What SURVIVES (kept, per survival-of-the-fittest)

- **The structural spine is sound and deftly integrable.** "Buttons own NO material; compose
  the tabs `.glass-capsule` + `.glass-capsule-hover`; delete the inline gray recipes + the
  per-variant `hover:bg-*`/`scale-*` chains + the gold `background-image` plate (clean break,
  no alias)." This is a true UNION, not a bolt-on, and the CVA SHRINKS. KEEP.
- **Compositor-only by construction** (no `backdrop-filter:url`, no SVG goo, no meatball — the
  metaball/naive-ellipsoid law correctly does not bind a lozenge). KEEP.
- **The DEPEND ledger** (`--motion-weight`, `--ease-cartoon-punch`, `--glass-accent`,
  `useSpringPress`/`useLiquidFlex`, `v-specular`, the blur ladder, `.focus-ring`, the four-state
  contract) is accurate and reuses extant primitives. KEEP.
- **PRM/a11y carve** (§7) is structurally sound: `--motion-weight:0` zeroes squash/cast/pop,
  `--ease-cartoon-punch → --ease-standard`, the static fill+rim+lift survive for everyone, the
  `--glass-level:0` opaque escape + `.focus-ring` are untouched. (Unproven in the spike's PRM
  arm 10 only because the deps are absent — gate-ready, acceptable.)
- **The fenced opt-in punch** (calm workhorse default, loud hero, dock-default-calm) is the
  right cartoon-PUNCH posture — bold where it should be, restrained where mania would hurt.

## VERDICT

**SURVIVES as DESIGN; the SPIKE + the headline born-RED chroma gate FAIL and must be re-based
to real paint before §9 can be cited as de-risk.** The single load-bearing refutation (R1) is
not a design flaw — it is that the golden has NOT actually de-risked its boldest claim; it
proved a purple field is purple. The proposed fill is measurably near-gray (0.0046), i.e. the
golden as-spiked does not deliver the user's ask. Fold R1–R6, re-derive the fill chroma over a
neutral field, fix the detector, and the design holds.
