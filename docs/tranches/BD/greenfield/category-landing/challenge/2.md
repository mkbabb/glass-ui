## REFUTATIONS (specific, live-grounded)

### R1 — TOP REFUTATION: the spike SELF-FAILS the golden's own B4 — the marquee `control` specimen occupies **5%** of its stage (the "large + empty" defect, un-killed)

Live-measured on `golden/spike.html`: the Inputs (`control`) specimen rect vs its stage =
**5% occupancy** (stage `304×188`, control cluster ~scale(.82) of a 3-element row). B4 demands
"window occupies ≥38% of card height with NON-EMPTY content." The spike's screenshot shows it
plainly in BOTH modes: a vast pink/brown void with a tiny Submit+slider+switch cluster floating
dead-center. **This is the exact "useless large cards / vast plate around an empty box" disease
the golden claims to abolish (§0 RED, B4 GREEN-when), reproduced by the golden's own de-risk.**
The `aspect-ratio:var(--phi)` window GROWS with the lead card's `span-2` width, but the specimen
is a fixed-size cluster — so the wider the card, the emptier the stage. φ-proportioning the
WINDOW does not proportion the CONTENT; B4 is unmet by construction for `control`/`metric`/
`surface` (all fixed-size clusters), only `field` (inset:0) fills.

**HARDENING:** the specimen must FILL its stage, not float in it. (a) `field` already does
(`inset:0`). (b) `control`/`surface`/`metric` must scale-to-fit: wrap the cluster in a
`container-type:size` stage and size the specimen in `cqmin` units (or a `transform:scale()`
keyed off `--stage-cqw`) so it occupies ≥45% at every card width; the lead `control` becomes a
3-row STACK (already promised in M3 — but the spike's lead is `field`, so the stack was never
de-risked). (c) ADD a B4 sub-arm that rasters the specimen's painted bbox / stage bbox ≥ 0.38
AND asserts it holds at the WIDE lead width (the span-2 column), not just the 1-col default —
the current B4 only checks "card height bounded," never the wide-card emptiness that §0 flagged.

### R2 — the boldest move (the goo-merge neck) DOES NOT READ in the painted pixels

The spike screenshot (both modes) shows the lead Aurora card with NO visible metaball waist
between the chip and the stage — only a faint orange smudge clinging to the chip's lower edge.
Live DOM: 2 blobs (`b1` 40×40 at ~(36,36), `b2` 64×46 at ~(52,84)), `filter:url(#goo)`,
`stdDeviation:7`, threshold `20/-9`. The blobs are ~50px apart center-to-center but the stage
they're supposed to bridge sits at `z:1` ABOVE the bridge (`z:0`) — so the neck is OCCLUDED by
the opaque-ish warm stage it's meant to connect TO. The "specimen pours out of the chip" gag is
asserted (§2.1, "BUILT, live-verified") but is invisible in the actual capture. **"Live-verified
both modes" is inflated — the pixel does not show the claimed metaball waist** (the cardinal-
lesson live-verify-inflation, MEMORY).

Two cross-engine sub-faults compound it:
- **R2a — the spike's `#goo` filter has NO explicit region** (`gooHasRegion:false`). The shipped
  `fission-bridge`/`GlassGooFilter` EXPLICITLY declares a generous `x=-50% width=200%` region
  precisely because "the neck + flying pieces never clip." The spike hand-rolls a bare `#goo`
  with the default `-10%/-10%/120%/120%` region — in WebKit the default region + `feComposite
  atop` is exactly where Safari clips goo necks. So the spike is NOT "the shipped pattern," it's
  a NEW filter that drops the region the shipped one needs. This is the overfit/non-DRY tell.
- **R2b — the goo-bridge (`filter:url(#goo)`) is a CHILD of the `.card` that carries
  `backdrop-filter:blur(10px)`** (`bridgeInsideBackdropCard:true`). Nesting an SVG `filter`
  inside a `backdrop-filter` host is a documented WebKit compositing-order hazard (the
  filter rasters against a backdrop that is itself being sampled) — the dock-goo-tear class the
  golden's own §2.1 cites. The spike never proved this in WebKit (it was only "verified live in
  Chrome," §7).

**HARDENING:** (a) make the goo-bridge a sibling of the card OR a layer with its OWN stacking
context that does NOT sit inside the `backdrop-filter` host — lift it to `z` ABOVE the stage so
the neck is visible, or render chip+stage as the goo SourceGraphic so the neck is BETWEEN them,
not behind. (b) port the SHIPPED `GlassGooFilter` mount (with its `x=-50% width=200%` region +
`sRGB`) verbatim — do NOT hand-roll a bare `#goo` (DRY; the golden's own §4 says reuse
`#glass-goo`/`fission-bridge`). (c) the §5 B11 "goo waist reads a metaball at the split midpoint"
must raster the ACTUAL painted neck pixel between chip and stage with an alpha-bridge Δ, on a
**WebKit** capture — the spike's Chrome-only readback does not discharge B8/B11 for Safari.

### R3 — the lead `field` specimen is INDISTINGUISHABLE from the field behind it (B3 fraud-adjacent)

Live: the lead `spec-field` stops are oklch H `[78,42,92]` (warm), the page/window field stops
are H `[48,72]` (warm) — they OVERLAP. The screenshot shows the lead Aurora as ONE
undifferentiated warm amber→terracotta smear, visually identical in character to the warm field
on every other card and the page background. B3 demands the `field` window raster "polychrome
aurora, hue-spread Δ≥25°" — the spike's 50° spread is all WITHIN the warm fence, so it reads as
MORE FIELD, not as "the Aurora component." **The warm-fence was enforced so hard the
representative specimen lost the very identity it exists to show.** A user lands on /substrates
and cannot tell the Aurora card's "live demo" from the page's decorative background — the widget
metaphor (LENS-A "show, never symbolize") collapses back into "warm wash everywhere."

**HARDENING:** the `field` specimen must use Aurora's ACTUAL `paletteToCssGradient` first-frame
for the substrate's real palette (which legitimately spans cooler stops — Aurora is not
warm-only), bounded + edge-defined, so it READS as aurora while the WINDOW field stays warm.
The warm-fence governs the card CHASSIS + the bounded window FLOOR, NOT the specimen's own
palette (a real component preview shows the component's real colors). B3 must raster the
specimen's hue-histogram DISTINCT from the field-floor histogram (Δ in dominant hue or a
multi-modal spread), not merely "Δ≥25° somewhere" (which the all-warm smear passes while
looking like field). Otherwise B3 is a fraud-bite that the spike itself sneaks past.

### R4 — FOUR of FIVE DEPEND waves do not exist on disk; the golden builds on UNBUILT dependencies

Live grep of `src/` + the live `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on
`:5173/substrates`: `--phi`, `--glass-key`, `--card-field-h`, `--field-h` ALL return **UNSET**.
The DEPEND waves `BD.W-SECTION-HUE-WARM-FENCE`, `BD.W-PAGE-FIELD`, `BD.W-FIELD-SCRIPT`,
`BD.W-GLASS-KEY-EDGE` have **no file on disk** (only `BD.W-CONCENTRIC-RADIUS` exists). The tokens
`warmFieldHue`/`paper-field`/`warmHeroHue` appear ONLY in plan/brainstorm prose, never in `src/`.
They are BOOKED as amendments in `GREENFIELD-HARDENING-PLAN.md` (W-GLASS-FIELD / W-GLASS-KEY-EDGE
folded into glass-material's WAVE-AMENDMENT) — but as PLANNED, NOT LANDED. The golden's §4
"CONSUME / DEPEND (NO edit here)" assumes these are shipped; they are not. **If this wave builds
before its four dependencies land, every `var(--card-field-h)`/`var(--phi)`/`var(--glass-key)`
falls to its fallback** — and the fallbacks (`var(--card-field-h, 62)`, `var(--phi, 1.618)`) are
HARDCODED LITERALS in the spike, meaning the "warm fence by construction" + "teal impossible"
guarantee silently degrades to a per-card magic-number `62` the moment the fence wave is late.
The teal chip (`H≈220`, live) is owned by the unbuilt fence — until it lands, B2 cannot pass on
the real page no matter what this wave does.

**HARDENING:** make the dependency ORDER a hard gate in the wave's preamble: assert
`getComputedStyle` resolves `--card-field-h`/`--phi`/`--glass-key`/`paper-field` to NON-fallback
values (probe a sentinel) as a born-RED precondition; FAIL the build if any DEPEND wave is
absent (don't silently eat the literal fallback). Sequence `BD.W-BENTO-SPECIMEN` strictly AFTER
the warm-fence + field-script + key-edge waves in the tranche DAG, and state that explicitly in
§4. The literal `62`/`1.618` fallbacks in the production CSS are LEGACY-by-stealth (a magic
number standing in for an absent token) — forbid them; the token must resolve or the gate is RED.

### R5 — the universal warm-fence is violated by the spike's own `metric` delta chip (overfit escape)

Live: the `metric` specimen's delta chip uses oklch H **145** (green), outside the warm
`[25,95]` fence the golden asserts for "ALL things." B2 (`H∈[180,270]` teal-ban) literally
passes 145, so the gate does NOT catch it — but the golden's narrative ("every hue warm-clamped
by construction," §6) is false in its own de-risk. A success-delta being green is arguably
defensible (green=positive is a semantic, not decorative, color) — but the golden never carves
that exception, so it's an unexamined leak that proves the "warm by construction" claim is
aspirational, not enforced.

**HARDENING:** EITHER (a) explicitly carve a semantic-color exception (delta up=success-green,
down=danger-red) in §3 and exclude semantic chips from the warm-fence raster, OR (b) warm the
delta to the success token within the fence. Don't leave it as an un-named hue-145 that the
gate's own teal-ban can't see — name the exception or kill it.

### R6 — perf: `content-visibility:auto` parking + 5 distinct `backdrop-filter` cards is asserted, never measured; WebKit `backdrop-filter` cost is real

§1 M4 claims `content-visibility:auto` + `contain-intrinsic-size` parks below-fold cards and the
previews "cost nothing." But each card carries `backdrop-filter:blur(10px) saturate(1.4)` (live
on the spike) — up to 11 simultaneous backdrop-filter layers on a landing. In WebKit,
`backdrop-filter` is NOT free: each is a separate offscreen blur pass, and `content-visibility`
does NOT skip a backdrop-filter that intersects the viewport. The golden measured `canvasCount`
(GL budget) but NEVER measured the backdrop-filter blur-pass count or a paint/raster cost. The
"zero cost" claim is GL-only; the blur budget is unaudited.

**HARDENING:** add a perf arm to §5 — measure paint+composite time (chrome-devtools
performance trace) on the real 11-card landing AND a WebKit raster; cap simultaneous
`backdrop-filter` layers (the card plate can be a `background` warm-glass without a live blur
when below-fold, or share ONE blur via a parent). Assert the landing holds 60fps on the
cel-slam cascade with all cards' blur active. "Costs nothing" must be a measured number, not a
GL-context count.

### R7 — `aspect-ratio:var(--phi)` window + a fixed-height card body can DOUBLE-BOUND and overflow (layout)

M3 sets the window `aspect-ratio:var(--phi)` AND M3 bounds card `max-inline-size` on the φ-ladder
AND the lead card is `span-2`. An `aspect-ratio` window inside a flex-column card whose width is
driven by the grid track will compute a window HEIGHT = width/φ; on the span-2 lead at ≥1280 that
height can exceed the old `7rem`/`112px` bound by 3-4× (the spike lead window is ~`188px` tall on
a narrow card; on a 643px-wide lead it's ~`397px`). Combined with the head + blurb, the lead card
grows TALL — re-introducing the "vast card" §0 flagged, now vertically. The φ-window solves the
empty-WIDTH but can create an empty-HEIGHT on the marquee unless the specimen fills it (see R1).

**HARDENING:** clamp the window with `aspect-ratio:var(--phi)` AND a `max-block-size` on the
φ-ladder so the lead window can't run away vertically; or give the lead a DIFFERENT aspect
(the marquee earns height by SHOWING the 3-row stack, R1). Gate B4 must assert the card's TOTAL
height stays bounded at the span-2 width, not just the window:text ratio.

## Anti-evasion (did the golden pre-empt these?)

- B12 lists ≥8 bites incl. "a stop-string-average reader (the fraud bite)" and "a goo filter on
  an ANCESTOR of the glass (B8)" — GOOD, those are real. But it does NOT bite: (i) a specimen
  that floats at <38% occupancy in a wide window (R1 — B4 checks ratio+bounded, not wide-width
  occupancy); (ii) a `field` specimen whose hue-histogram MATCHES the field floor (R3 — B3's
  "Δ≥25° somewhere" passes an all-warm smear); (iii) a goo-bridge nested INSIDE the
  backdrop-filter host (R2b — B8 bites an ANCESTOR filter, not a DESCENDANT-of-backdrop filter);
  (iv) an absent DEPEND wave eaten by a literal fallback (R4 — no bite asserts tokens resolve).
- The gate is genuinely born-RED (R-floor confirmed live) — that part is NOT evasion.

## Bottom line

The IDEA survives: specimen-stage-over-warm-field, the seam re-point, the frozen-CSS one-GL
budget, both-mode warm. But the golden's "BUILT + live-verified, de-risked" SPIKE is the weak
point — it self-fails B4 (5% occupancy), its boldest move (goo neck) is invisible in the paint,
its lead `field` specimen is indistinguishable from the field, it hand-rolls a region-less goo
filter instead of the shipped one, it leaks a green chip past its own warm-fence, and it leans on
FOUR tokens that don't exist on the live HEAD. "Verified live in Chrome" never touched WebKit.
Harden R1–R4 (occupancy-fill, goo readability+shipped-filter+WebKit raster, specimen-distinct-
from-field, dependency-order gate) before any build.
