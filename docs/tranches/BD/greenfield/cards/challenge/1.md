## REFUTATION 1 (TOP, LANDS) — the spike's "follow-through" / "shadow lags a frame" has NO mechanism; `--cast-dist` transition is a triple-dead no-op

§5 sells the cel WEIGHT as: "The shadow LAGS the lift by a frame (follow-through)" and "the offset cast
SHRINKS … then on release the spring overshoots and the cast springs back open." The spike claims to
de-risk exactly this. **It does not.** Three independent reasons the cast does not animate at all:

1. **`transition: --cast-dist 0.42s …` (keyed-cel.html:110) is inert** — `--cast-dist` is a plain
   custom property with NO `@property --cast-dist { syntax:"<length>" … }` registration (grep: zero
   `@property` in the file). Unregistered custom properties have computed-value-type `*`/CSSUnparsedValue
   and **cannot interpolate** — the transition snaps. Same trap in BOTH engines.
2. **The hover/active `box-shadow` is set with HARDCODED literals, not `var(--cast-dist)`** —
   `:hover` writes `… 16px … 16px …` (line 165) and `:active` writes `… 4px … 4px …` (line 182) as
   explicit pixel literals. So even if `--cast-dist` DID interpolate, the painted shadow never reads it
   on the state arms. The resting `--cast-dist:10px` (used in the resting `box-shadow` calc) is
   overridden wholesale by the explicit hover/active shadow. The variable is decorative.
3. **The shadow DOES transition (via `transition: box-shadow 0.42s`), but with the SAME 0.42s curve as
   the transform** — there is NO lag/lead delay, no spring-overshoot, no follow-through. A
   `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)` ease-out is not a spring; it cannot overshoot. So the "spring overshoots
   and the cast springs back open" is unrepresented. The "cel WEIGHT the bare scale lacks" is, in the
   spike, the bare scale plus a co-timed shadow swap — i.e. exactly the "sticker slide + shadow swap"
   the golden indicts at §5 ("a sticker slide, no weight, no arc").

**Why it matters to my lens (correctness + KISS):** the boldest-move payoff (the squash↔cast coupling
with follow-through) is the one thing the spike exists to prove, and it proves the *static* keyed look,
not the *motion*. The §0 paint-arm `card-pi.mjs` only samples K2/K3/K5 (static color/edge/direction) — it
NEVER samples K6 (the frame-series squash↔cast coupling). So K6 is asserted, never spiked, and the spike's
own mechanism for it is dead. A reviewer who runs the spike and tabs hover/press sees a co-timed ease, not
a weighted cel.

**HARDENING:** (a) register `@property --cast-dist { syntax:"<length>"; inherits:false; initial-value:10px }`
(Safari ≥16.4, already a stated floor) AND drive the hover/active `box-shadow` THROUGH `var(--cast-dist)`
(delete the hardcoded `16px`/`4px` literals) so the cast distance actually interpolates; (b) put the cast on
a `linear()`-sampled spring with a real overshoot (the house `--spring-smooth`/`--ease-cartoon-punch`
`linear()` stack, the same one §6 already mandates) and a small +1-frame delay vs the transform so
"follow-through" is mechanically true; (c) extend `card-pi.mjs`/the executed gate with the K6
frame-series readback (sample the resolved `box-shadow` offset at press-down vs rest and assert it SHRANK,
+ assert `scaleX·scaleY≈1`), so K6 is gated, not narrated. Without (a)+(b) the spike's §5 claims are
aspirational text, and K6 is born-RED with no proven cure.

## REFUTATION 2 (LANDS) — W-CARD-MATERIAL-FLOOR is NOT a "SHARED re-point, not a card-local fork"; the tinted rung the card needs is NOT on the resting ladder, and lifting `--glass-tint-strength` off 0% is an unbounded cross-consumer blast

§4/§8 W-CARD-MATERIAL-FLOOR: "the card's resting/quiet rungs compose the W55-tinted rung at a small
non-zero warm-floor `--glass-tint-strength` (a SHARED ladder re-point off the `0%` no-op default)."
Grep on HEAD (surfaces.css:280-310) shows the tinted rungs are **scoped**, not on a shared ladder:
- `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` lives under `:where(.btn-glass, .segmented-indicator)`.
- `--glass-bg-quiet-tinted` + `--glass-bg-resting-tinted` live under `.btn-glass` ONLY.
- `--veil-bg` (cards.css:229) composes the seam, and cards.css:126 explicitly says the selection rule
  "NEVER writes `--glass-tint-source`/`--glass-tint-strength`".

So a `<Card surface="glass" tier="resting">` reads `--glass-bg-resting` (the UN-tinted rung), NOT
`-resting-tinted` — there is no card-enrolled tinted rung to re-point. Two ways to land the floor, both
violate a golden claim:
- **(a) mint `--glass-bg-resting-tinted` on the card/glass-resting ladder** = a NEW card-scoped rung →
  the "not a card-local fork" claim breaks (it IS a new scoped rung the card alone reads).
- **(b) raise the GLOBAL `--glass-tint-strength` off its `0%` default** = every consumer that reads the
  seam re-tints in lockstep: `drawer.css:66/91`, `menu.css:40`, `cards.css:229` (veil), `jubilance.css:52`,
  `btn-glass`, `segmented-indicator`. The "ZERO-delta at the 0% default" guarantee those rungs are written
  against (surfaces.css:280 comment) is exactly what (b) destroys. That is a tranche-wide warm-shift with
  no enumerated blast-radius and no re-ratification of AA on the affected surfaces — a KISS/DRY violation
  masquerading as a one-token re-point.

**Why it matters:** the golden's load-bearing honesty section (§0 "no-invented-lever fence") is precisely
that levers must exist on disk where claimed. Here the lever (a card-enrolled tinted resting rung) does
NOT exist, and the two routes to create it each contradict an explicit golden promise ("not a fork" /
"shared re-point off the 0% no-op"). The material floor — the leg that makes the card warm on a dead
ground, the thing that closes "far too gray" at the material — rests on a rung that isn't wired.

**HARDENING:** state the EXACT mechanism: mint a `--glass-bg-resting-tinted`/`-quiet-tinted` on the SAME
`:where()` seam used by buttons/tabs, ADD the card's resting/quiet surface selectors to that `:where()`
list (so it is genuinely shared, one rule, DRY), and keep `--glass-tint-strength` at a **card-local
non-zero** set via a `--glass-tint-strength` override scoped to the card surface (NOT the global default),
so buttons/tabs/drawers stay at their 0% floor unless THEY opt in. Enumerate the blast radius explicitly
(the 6 seam-readers above) and assert each is unchanged in a born-RED bite. Otherwise the "shared re-point"
phrasing is a euphemism for either a fork or a global re-tint.

## REFUTATION 3 (LANDS, smaller) — the `::after` catch-light is `mix-blend-mode: screen` and sits OVER the card text (no z-clip), a legibility + correctness gap the gate never samples

keyed-cel.html:143-153: `.cel::after` is the inner catch-light with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, `inset:0`,
NO `z-index` and NO `overflow`/clip relationship to content. The `h2`/`p` are lifted to `z-index:1`
(lines 160-161) to escape it — but `::after` paints on the element's own stacking context AFTER content,
and `screen` lightens whatever is beneath the pseudo within that context. On a real `<Card>` with denser
slotted content (list-rows, chips, a search-pill — the §7 composite), a top-left screen wash over text is
a contrast risk the §10 AA carve does NOT cover (it only re-ratifies body text vs the FIELD, not vs the
catch-light overlay). The pointer-tracked `vSpecular` lens (§3.2) compounds this — two screen-blend
overlays over content. The gate (`card-pi.mjs` K2/K3/K5 + the narrated K-set) never samples a
text-over-catch-light contrast pixel.

**Why it matters:** the golden asserts "AA holds" as a frozen given ("the plate L is unmoved, leg a
frozen"), but the catch-light is an ADDITIVE screen layer the leg-a-frozen argument doesn't cover. Over the
§7 dense composite this is a real WCAG 1.4.3 exposure, both modes (the dark `::after` is weaker but the
light one is 22%-white screen at the top-left where a card title typically sits).

**HARDENING:** constrain the catch-light to NOT overlap the text column — either clip it to a top band
(`radial-gradient` already fades by 46%, but assert the text safe-area), or move it BELOW content in the
paint order (it is a *material* highlight, it should read as glass, not wash the prose), or add a K-bite:
sample the composited text-pixel contrast WITH the catch-light + specular live, both modes, over the §7
dense card, and assert ≥4.5:1. Add this to §10's carve explicitly.
