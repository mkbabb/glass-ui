## REFUTATIONS (real, but none break the gestalt)

### R1 (TOP) — Safari parity is REASONED, never CAPTURED. The cardinal §L7 bar is unmet by the spike.
The golden's headline cross-engine claim (§9, K9 "paired-engine π") and the whole "meatballing perfect
in Chrome AND Safari" binding law rest on a Chromium-only spike. I live-tested `keyed-cel.html` in
**Chromium only** — `mask-composite: exclude` and `-webkit-mask-composite: xor` both report supported,
but **WebKit's conic-gradient-border-via-mask-composite has historically mis-composited the corner
miter** and `oklch()` conic stops can band differently in Safari's sRGB-interpolated paint. The golden
asserts "Safari-native" for the keyed conic rim but provides **zero captured WebKit pixel**. Per the
team's own [live-verify capture] lesson, "live-verified" requires a captured DELTA artefact, not a
reasoned claim. The spike's `_spike-light.png`/`_spike-dark.png` are Chromium captures. **The single
most-cited cross-engine mechanism (the keyed conic rim) is un-captured on the engine that matters most.**

### R2 — The press SQUASH is sub-perceptual (1.8%); the "cartoon PUNCH / real WEIGHT" edict is underdelivered.
§5 + the spike use `--squash: 1.018` (press) and `--lift-s: 1.015` (hover) — a 1.5–1.8% deformation.
The binding law demands "exaggeration, squash & stretch with real WEIGHT/INERTIA" and "elevate
design.md §L4 weak/medium principles toward universal." A 1.8% squash on a 300px card is **invisible
to a user** — it reads as a static sticker, exactly the iOS-7 register the golden swears off. The
golden even concedes "the card is a larger surface than a button so the shrink is shallower (0.02)."
That self-justification is the gray-glass evasion in motion form: a card that squashes 1.8% is NOT a
1940s technicolor cel with PUNCH; it is a tasteful, timid lift. The cartoon-PUNCH bar is not met by a
2% scale — the PUNCH must live in the CAST amplitude + the shadow-gap travel, not the body scale, and
§5 leaves the loud amplitude opt-in (`surface="cartoon"` only), so the DEFAULT card has no punch.

### R3 — The "no-invented-lever fence — verified by grep on HEAD" is itself NOT verified: stale paths + line numbers.
§0's on-disk table claims grep-verification but cites `surfaces.css:286` (real: `glass/surfaces.css:19`),
`cards.css:229` (that line is the `--veil-bg`, not the cited tint seam), `Card.vue:354`/`:301`/`:227`/
`:250`/`:342` (Card.vue is at `ui/card/Card.vue`, the cited line numbers do not land on the claimed
tokens), `shadow.css:9` for the card's cast (the card uses `--shadow-cartoon-md` at `:95`, NOT `:9`).
A fence whose own citations are wrong cannot be trusted as a fence. The LEVERS exist — I re-grepped and
found every one — but the golden's *proof of existence* is broken, which is the exact "lies about a
lever that ships" failure §0 claims to prevent. The honesty section is dishonest about its own coordinates.

### R4 — Title/§0 mislabels its own axes: `resting/cartoon/veil/opaque/deep` are NOT all "surfaces."
The GOLDEN title and the task framing call these "surface variants (resting/cartoon)." On disk
`CardSurface = "glass" | "cartoon" | "veil"`; `resting`/`opaque`/`deep` are `CardTier` rungs, and
`glass` (the actual default surface) is never named in the headline. A spec that miscategorizes its own
component's API in the title invites a consumer to write `surface="deep"` (invalid) or `tier="cartoon"`
(invalid). KISS demands the spec speak the shipped axis names exactly. Minor, but it is an overfit-to-
narrative slip where the prose bends the API to fit the "five surface variants" story.

### R5 — The catch-light `mix-blend-mode: screen` is in the spike but ABSENT from the golden's §3 layer-4 spec.
The spike's `::after` catch-light uses `mix-blend-mode: screen` (keyed-cel.html:153) — load-bearing for
the catch reading as light, not a white film. §3 layer-4 ("inner catch-light, re-aim toward the key")
never names `screen`. `mix-blend-mode: screen` over a busy field can **lift text contrast unpredictably**
(an a11y/legibility risk §10 never brackets) and Safari composites `screen` over `backdrop-filter`
differently. The boldest-move's third agreeing layer has an undocumented compositing dependency.

### R6 — K7/K7-weight has no born-RED PROOF the eased lanes paint differently; "no new keyframe family" is asserted, not shown.
K7 asserts the 4 lanes "ease with the spring/punch curve (not `linear`)" and depends on
`--ease-cartoon-punch`/`--motion-weight` (MISSING on disk). There is NO spike for the scroll-shrink
weight — the golden de-risks only the static cel (§12 is the keyed-cel, not the header). So the single
"refine, do not re-invent" claim of §6 (the liquid-weight eased shrink) is **entirely un-prototyped**.
The `linear()`-sampled-spring claim is plausible but unproven; a `linear()` stack on a scroll-DRIVEN
timeline can read as STEPPED (the discrete samples) rather than weighted at low scroll velocity.

### R7 — `proof:no-gray` is a DEVICE-FREE SOURCE gate; "extend it, no new gate family" understates the work.
§11 says "Extend `proof:no-gray` to the card route — no new gate family." But `scripts/proof-no-gray.mjs`
is explicitly device-free SOURCE parsing (its own header: "device-free; the π arm … NEVER this gate
alone"). The card gate samples a PAINTED composited pixel via `getImageData` over a REAL field in TWO
engines — that is a π/visual-spec mechanism (`tests-visual/no-gray.spec.ts` territory), categorically a
DIFFERENT gate than the source gate. Calling it "no new gate family" hides a real build cost and risks a
greenfield executor wiring the painted assertion into the device-free script (where it cannot run).
