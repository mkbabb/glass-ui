## REFUTATION 1 (the strongest — LANDS as a proof gap): mask-composite is the load-bearing rim mechanism and it is UNPROVEN on WebKit

**The claim (§3.1, §7-cross-engine, K9):** the defined-edge floor is a `conic-gradient` border via
`mask-composite: exclude` / `-webkit-mask-composite: xor`, asserted "Chrome AND Safari native,"
"Safari-native," and gated by paired-engine π.

**What I measured:** on the `keyed-cel.html` spike in Chromium the `::before` rim resolves
`mask-composite: exclude` and paints. **But the entire de-risk ran on Chromium only.** There is no
WebKit paint in the golden's evidence (the two PNGs in `golden/` are Chromium screenshots; no
`_spike-safari.png`, no paired readout). This is exactly the cardinal §L7 bar the golden cites — and
it is unmet for its single boldest visual mechanism.

**Why this is a real bite, not pedantry:** `-webkit-mask-composite` does NOT share `mask-composite`'s
keyword set. `mask-composite: exclude` (CSS Masking L1) ≠ `-webkit-mask-composite: xor` (legacy
WebKit) — they are different properties with different value vocabularies, and Safari's support for
the STANDARD `mask-composite` has historically lagged behind the prefixed `-webkit-mask-composite`.
The two-arm fallback in the spike is correct in spirit, but: (a) it is unverified that BOTH arms
resolve to the same painted ring on WebKit; (b) the `from calc(var(--glass-key-angle) - 90deg)`
conic with a CSS-var angle inside `calc()` inside `conic-gradient(from ...)` is a parse path that
Safari has historically been fragile on; (c) the cut-out-border idiom (two mask layers, one
`content-box`, composited) is notorious for off-by-a-subpixel seams on WebKit's mask rasterizer at
non-integer DPR. None of this is tested. "Safari-native" is asserted, not shown.

**Hardening:** the gate K9 must produce a WebKit paint artefact for the conic rim specifically — a
`_spike-safari-light.png` + `_spike-safari-dark.png` from a real WebKit engine (Playwright `webkit`
channel or a physical Safari), plus a paired-π readback that the lit-edge-vs-host ΔL ≥ 3:1 holds on
BOTH engines. If `mask-composite: exclude` does NOT paint on the target WebKit, the rim must fall to
the **already-shipped** two-stop `--glass-rim-top`/`-bottom` (`rim.css:79`, omnidirectional but
real) as the WebKit floor, with the conic as a `@supports (mask-composite: exclude)` progressive
enhancement — NOT a hard dependency. The golden currently treats the conic as THE rim; it must be
the enhancement, with the shipped two-stop as the cross-engine floor. (DRY bonus: this re-uses an
existing primitive instead of betting the edge on an untested WebKit path.)

## REFUTATION 2 (LANDS — spike misrepresents its own motion mechanism): the `--cast-dist` transition is dead code

**The claim (§5, spike header):** the press/hover squash is "COUPLED to the keyed cast" — the
shadow-gap closes/opens via a transitioned `--cast-dist`, sold as the cel-weight the bare scale
lacks.

**What I measured on the spike:** the `.cel` rule transitions `--cast-dist 0.42s` — but (a)
`--cast-dist` is NEVER registered with `@property` (confirmed: `hasAtPropertyCastDist: false`), so
an unregistered custom property animates **DISCRETELY** (no interpolation) in EVERY engine, Chrome
included; and (b) the `:hover` and `:active` rules **re-declare `box-shadow` with literal `16px` /
`4px`**, so the box-shadow that actually paints never reads the transitioned `--cast-dist` at all.
The `--cast-dist` transition is dead code. The gap-close is a hard CSS-transition on the literal
`box-shadow` offset, which IS smooth — but it is NOT the "coupled cast-dist drive" the prose sells,
and `box-shadow` offset is a **paint-layer** animation, not compositor.

**Why it matters for MY lens (perf):** §5 and §9 promise "compositor-only throughout" and the §L7
paint-cost fence. A transitioned `box-shadow` offset is a per-frame **repaint** of the shadow (not
GPU-composited like transform/opacity). On a card GRID, N cards each repainting a `box-shadow` on
hover is exactly the paint-storm the fence is supposed to forbid. The golden hand-waves this as
"gated to discrete state-flips," but a CSS `transition` on box-shadow over 0.42s IS a continuous
per-frame repaint for that 420ms, on as many cards as are mid-transition.

**Hardening:** (1) register `--cast-dist` (and any animated custom prop) with `@property
{ syntax: '<length>' }` so it actually interpolates, OR drop the dead transition and own the
box-shadow transition honestly. (2) For the grid case, the moving cast must ride the **separate
`cartoon-cast` child layer** the golden itself names in §3.3/§8 (a transform/opacity layer), NOT a
transitioned `box-shadow` on the card — the spike does the wrong one. Make the spike demonstrate the
child-layer cast so the de-risk matches the shipped mechanism. (3) K6 must assert the cast animation
is on a compositor layer (transform), not a `box-shadow` repaint, and add a paint-count budget to
the grid case (e.g. DevTools paint-flashing ≤ the card under the pointer, not the whole grid).

## REFUTATION 3 (LANDS — the no-invented-lever fence is itself broken): wrong file paths in the load-bearing citation table

**The claim (§0 on-disk reality check, §4, §8):** the golden cites `surfaces.css:286`,
`surfaces.css:302`, `cards.css:229` as the proof that the `--glass-tint-*` warm-admit seam and the
tinted rungs ship. The whole §0 "no-invented-lever fence … verified by grep on HEAD" rests on these
paths being real.

**What I found on HEAD:** there is **no `src/styles/surfaces.css`**. The file is
`src/styles/glass/surfaces.css` (the tinted rungs are at `glass/surfaces.css:283`/`:302`, close on
line but WRONG path). The `--glass-tint-source`/`-strength` defaults live in `glass/ladder.css` /
`glass/surface-axis.css` / `drawer.css`, not the cited `surfaces.css:286`. `--glass-bg-sheet` is
`tokens/glass.css:290` (correct). The PRIMITIVES exist — the floor lands — but the citation table
that the golden brandishes as its anti-lying fence contains paths that a grep on the literal cited
string returns ZERO for. A fence that mis-cites is a fence that didn't actually run the grep.

**Why it matters:** the golden makes a virtue of "the honest status MUST be recorded so this golden
never lies about a lever that ships." A reader who greps `surfaces.css:286` to verify gets nothing
and loses trust in the whole table — including the genuinely-correct rows. The fence's value is its
checkability; a broken path defeats it.

**Hardening:** re-run the grep against the literal paths and fix every citation to its real
`src/styles/glass/...` path with the verified line. Specifically: `surfaces.css:286,302` →
`glass/surfaces.css:283,302`; the `--glass-tint-*` default-`0%` claim should cite
`glass/ladder.css:385` + `glass/surface-axis.css:83` (the real default sites). Add a CI grep that
fails if any `§0`-cited path+line does not contain the cited token.

## REFUTATION 5 (LANDS soft — perf, MY lens): the card-local field underlayer's cost is undercounted for the GRID case

**The claim (§2.2, §9):** the card-local field is "a SINGLE static gradient (one paint,
compositor-cached, zero JS, `content-visibility`-friendly)" and "a card GRID does NOT mount N GL
contexts."

**The bite:** the GL claim is true (it's CSS, good). But the golden conflates "no GL" with "cheap."
A `:has()`-gated, per-card `radial-gradient` underlayer in a 50-card grid is **50 distinct
gradient-paint layers**, each on a `background-image` longhand BELOW `.paper-grid` and BELOW two
pseudos — i.e. each card host now has grid + field + specular(::before) + grain(::after) +
backdrop-filter blur stacked. That is a deep per-card layer stack, and the `:has(.paper-field)`
no-double-paint guard is itself a **live selector** the engine re-evaluates on DOM mutation. On
WebKit specifically, `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` over a busy local gradient in a long scrolling
grid is a known scroll-jank source (each card re-samples the blurred backdrop on scroll). §9 asserts
`content-visibility`-friendly but a card with `backdrop-filter` is NOT trivially `content-visibility:
auto` (the blur samples outside the element's own box). No live grid-scroll π is shown.

**Hardening:** (1) the field underlayer should be ONE field on the **nearest scroll container /
grid wrapper**, not per-card — the `:has()` gate already knows when an ancestor supplies a field, so
the grid wrapper mounting ONE field satisfies every child by construction (the common case should be
ancestor-supplied, per-card is the rare orphan fallback). Make per-card the explicit exception, not
the default the prose implies. (2) Add a grid-scroll perf gate: a 50-card scroll trace on BOTH
engines with a frame-budget assert (no long-frame > 16ms during fling), since the blur-resample is
the real cost, not GL. (3) Confirm `:has()` is acceptable on the WebKit floor (it is, Safari 15.4+,
but the golden never states the floor for the GUARD itself).

## WHERE THE GOLDEN HOLDS AGAINST MY LENS (the genuine survivals)

- **No naive ellipsoid / no meatball in the card path.** The golden explicitly bars goo/`filter:url`/
  `feDisplacementMap` from any card (§7, §9) and live scan confirms 0 on the route. The meatball-
  attack lens finds NOTHING to bite — cards are correctly the most Safari-safe surface in the band,
  and any fission/goo rides a SIBLING layer (§9). This is the right call and it is enforced.
- **The `light-dark()` inset-shadow trap is honored** (§3, §9): plain per-mode `box-shadow` arms, no
  `light-dark()` wrapping an inset fragment. The spike's per-mode `.dark` arm confirms it. This is
  the exact recorded trap and the golden dodges it correctly.
- **`color-mix(in oklab)` / `oklch()` / `conic-gradient` / `animation-timeline: scroll()` are all
  real cross-engine baselines** (Safari 15.4+/16.4+/26). The transmissive read, the ambient lift, the
  scroll-shrink are on solid WebKit floors.
- **No new component, no `surface="lens"` fork, no second field/edge/scroll engine** (§8) — the KISS/
  DRY/union discipline holds; every delta is a re-point/re-host of a shipped mechanism. The DELTA-
  ASSAY reconciles the 3 named waves without dup. This is deft integration, not a bolt-on.
- **A11y/PRM carve is thorough** (§10): the material floor doubling as the reduce-transparency anchor
  is a genuinely good move (warmth is structural, so a11y states can't gray it). PRM drops the
  scroll-lanes (the `@supports`-under-`no-preference` contract is correct and live-confirmed in
  `CardHeader.vue`).
- **The gate is genuinely born-RED** and the anti-evasion K10 (≥5 bites) is real — `card-pi.mjs`
  fails HEAD and the distinguishing measure (painted composite, not token) is the right one.
