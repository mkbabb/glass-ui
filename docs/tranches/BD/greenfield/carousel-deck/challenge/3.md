## REFUTATION 2 — THE SQUASH-REFRACTION IS A FAKE GAG (no differential deform; the "squeeze through the neck" does not read) ⛔ LANDS

§2.3 is the golden's headline DISTORTION and C3's "most-missed word": "the outgoing slide squashes
toward the seam and the entering slide stretches OUT of it." Live readback of the slide-pair
transforms at the peak waist:

```
slide 0 (outgoing): sx=0.861  sy=1.161   tx=0
slide 1 (incoming): sx=0.861  sy=1.161   tx=196   ← IDENTICAL transform to the outgoing
slide 2 (the real entering neighbor): sx=1 sy=1 op=0  ← untouched, invisible
```

Both slides get the **same** `scaleX 0.861 / scaleY 1.161`. There is no "outgoing squashes IN,
incoming stretches OUT" — they both just vertically stretch in unison. The spike code (line 230–237)
applies one `sx/sy` to every `i===from||i===to`, with the directional `dx` term zeroed
(`* 0` on line 233). So the "object squeezed THROUGH a gap" 1940s gag the prose sells does not
exist: nothing translates through the seam, nothing differentially deforms. It's a synchronized
vertical squash-in-place. As a user there is no refraction, no lensing, no "content bends with the
morph" — just two cards getting taller together while a blob necks behind them.

Worse: the slides **never translate during travel** (tx stays 0/196/392 the whole window), then
`placeSlides()` snaps them at settle. So the carousel motion is squash-in-place → **jump-cut**. The
gestalt-row promise ("the entering card BUDS out of the leaver... the content visibly SQUASHES
through") is not what renders. The distortion is the weakest, not the boldest, part.

**Hardening:** the C3 born-RED must assert a SIGNED differential — `sign(outgoing.skew or seam-ward
translate) == -sign(incoming)` AND a real cross-seam translation, not just `|sx·sy−1|<0.05` (which a
synchronized squash trivially passes — the cardinal "the gate passes for the wrong reason" trap).
Rebuild the spike so the outgoing slide's transform-origin is the seam and it compresses toward it
while the incoming emerges from it with opposite sign, and the active translates across. Until the
spike shows a TRUE differential refraction, C3 is unproven and the gate as written (vol-preserving
only) is a born-GREEN-by-construction fake.
