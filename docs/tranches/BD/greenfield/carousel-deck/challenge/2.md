## REFUTATIONS (specific, ranked)

### R1 [TOP] — "Chrome=Safari" is the headline, yet there is NO Safari capture anywhere; the de-risk is single-engine
- `golden/` contains ONLY `delta-midtravel-chrome.png`. `find … -iname '*safari*' -o '*webkit*'
  -o '*metal*'` across BOTH `carousel-deck/` AND the depended-on `goo-morph/` golden dirs → **zero
  hits.** The barbell foundation (`goo-morph/golden/`) is likewise Chrome-only (`peak-waist.png`,
  `delta-current-carousel-peak.png`).
- The GOLDEN §2.3 / §7 / §5 rest the WHOLE distortion bar on "transform-only, Safari-native,
  identical Chrome/Safari" and §5 demands a **paired Chromium + Safari-Metal `@webkit` π**. But the
  GOLDEN-stage proof that the design team actually produced is ONE engine. "Safari-native" is
  *reasoned* (transform-only ⇒ no `backdrop-filter:url`, no var-driven blur) — which is the right
  reasoning — but it is NOT *paint-verified*. For a tranche whose memory explicitly logs the
  "live-π false-FAIL" and "Safari `:global()` drop" recurrences, a one-engine de-risk on the
  cross-engine headline is the exact gap the lens exists to catch.
- Latent WebKit risk the Chrome capture cannot exclude: (a) `@property --goo-t` *transition*
  interpolation timing differs subtly on WebKit (supported since 16.4 but the dwell `linear()` land
  has shown engine drift); (b) the `clip-path: path()` concave throat — WebKit rasterizes `path()`
  clip at a different AA than Chrome, so the `waistRatio ≤ 0.45` rendered-alpha threshold may land
  on opposite sides of the gate per engine; (c) the `feColorMatrix … 18 -7` threshold over an
  `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation=11` produces engine-divergent edge alpha (the classic metaball
  fringe split). NONE of these are excluded by a Chrome-only PNG.

### R2 — the prototype's "squash-refraction" does NOT implement the directional deform the prose sells
- GOLDEN §2.3: "the two slides squash toward the seam and the **entering slide stretches OUT of it**
  … the slide being SQUEEZED THROUGH the neck." That asymmetry (outgoing pinch-in vs incoming
  stretch-out) is the whole "object pushed through a gap" gag.
- `squash-refraction.html:230-236` gives **BOTH** the from-slide and to-slide the **identical**
  `sx = 1−0.22·squashAmt`, `sy = 1/sx`. No directional opposition. It is a symmetric in-place pinch,
  not a through-the-neck refraction.
- Worse, line 233 computes a directional `dx` (with the travel offset) and then **never uses it** —
  line 236 transforms with `baseDx` (the static rest centre). So the deforming pair never even
  translates during travel; the cards sit still and scale in place. The "squeezed THROUGH" reading
  the screenshot is supposed to show cannot occur from this code.

### R3 — the prototype's born-RED π is SYNTHETIC arithmetic — it violates the GOLDEN's OWN cardinal fence
- GOLDEN §5 / §7 fence (twice, bolded): "**The born-RED is a REAL rendered readback, NEVER
  arithmetic … NEVER `neckGirth·const` arithmetic.**"
- `squash-refraction.html:282-308` does NOT rasterize the goo layer. It reads the painted
  transforms, then **reconstructs a synthetic alpha field arithmetically** (`fA = 1−(dA/r)²`,
  `fB`, `fNeck = neckGirth·0.9`, `profile.push(fA+fB+fNeck…)`) and runs `hasLocalMinimum`/`waistRatio`
  over that *computed* curve. The HTML comment claims "rendered transforms — NOT neckGirth·const"
  while line 299 literally is `neckGirth·0.9`. This is the precise anti-pattern the GOLDEN forbids,
  shipped in the GOLDEN's own proof artefact. The metaball topology is never read off pixels.
- The squash arm is measured on the WRONG element: line 312 reads `slides[active]` (the resting
  centre slide), not the deforming `from`/`to` pair — so `volPreserved` reads `1.000×1.000` at rest
  = trivially-true, and the distortion gate cannot actually FAIL. The C3 "RED on HEAD" cannot be
  demonstrated by this harness.

### R4 — the GOLDEN is ~90% a *dependency wrapper*; almost nothing here is independently provable
- The silhouette (barbell), the engine 3-ref re-projection, the concave throat, the dwell-follows-
  neck, the 3-consumer migration are ALL `DEPEND BD.W-GOO-BARBELL-NECK` (§2.1, §6). The §3 deck
  field + §2.4 inertia ALL `DEPEND BD.W-MOTION-WEIGHT / BD.W-CARTOON-PUNCH` (tokens confirmed
  absent). Strip the deps and the NEW surface area is: `<GooBridge>` + `useGooTransition` (the
  Layer-2 shell de-dup) + the squash coupling + the deck-field preset. The de-dup is real and
  net-negative LOC — genuinely fit — but the GOLDEN's most *exciting* claims (barbell, distortion
  punch, no-gray) are all someone else's wave. This is fine for a UNION, but it means this GOLDEN's
  own acceptance bar is **un-runnable until two sibling waves land** — and the prototype tries to
  paper over that by re-proving the barbell here (R2/R3 show it fails to).

### R5 — the mount-only-during-travel perf carve has an unaddressed Safari seam
- §2.5(1): `<GooBridge>` is `display:none` at rest, `display:block` on travel-start. Toggling
  `display` forces a fresh **filter-region raster + a layerization** of a `filter:url()` subtree on
  the FIRST travel frame. On WebKit, mounting a `filter:url()` layer mid-gesture is exactly where
  the first-frame jank lives (the filter graph compiles on first paint). The GOLDEN measures the
  *idle* win (correct: a standing `will-change` filter is a GPU cost on the 7-fps route) but never
  measures the *travel-onset* cost on WebKit. On the already-7-fps carousel route this risks a
  dropped first frame precisely when the anticipation pre-dip must read. `content-visibility`/
  pre-warm or an `opacity:0` + `contain:strict` (not `display:none`) alternative is unconsidered.

### R6 — §3 "no-gray deck field" floor is set at the razor's edge; the worm field already sits *at* the boundary
- Live `/motion/deck`: the `.deck-goo-plate` field resolves `oklab(0.9808 0.0042 0.0101)` → **C ≈
  0.0109**, only 0.0009 above the C ≥ 0.010 gate. The GOLDEN's own near-gray finding for the slide
  PANEL is `oklab(0.793 0.005 0.012)` (C ≈ 0.013). Both modes must clear C ≥ 0.010 — but `--card`
  in light is `hsl(30 85% 96%)` (very high L ⇒ collapsing oklch chroma), so any consumer that builds
  the field from `color-mix(currentColor, white 20%)` off `--card` (which the deck worm DOES, line
  262-266) sits one rounding-error from gray. The fix (adopt the carousel's saffron radial field)
  is correct, but the C ≥ 0.010 gate is too tight to be a robust floor — a stricter C ≥ 0.015 (or
  a hue-locked min-chroma utility) is needed or it will false-pass on a near-gray that still reads
  taupe to the eye.
