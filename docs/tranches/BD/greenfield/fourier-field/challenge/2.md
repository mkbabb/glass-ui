# CHALLENGE #2 — CROSS-ENGINE / SAFARI / PERF / A11Y lens on the FourierField GOLDEN

> Adversarial pass. Default REFUTED. Trust nothing; every claim verified at HEAD + live-tested
> on the spike (Chrome) and `/substrates/fourier-field` (both modes). Verdict at the end.

The GOLDEN's **diagnosis is sound and the gates are genuinely born-RED** — I confirmed D1
(dead cream/charcoal ground), D3 (pastel/hairline chain near-invisible in light), and the
absent `--ff-head-xy`/`.viz-warm-field`/draw at HEAD all live on the real route. The design
*direction* survives. My refutations land on **execution-precision, cross-engine symmetry, the
self-certifying spike gate, the light-mode §2a weakness, and the a11y/keyboard hole** — several
are real and require hardening before this is buildable as written.

---

## R1 (LANDS, top refutation) — the §2a "zero new CPU math" claim is FALSE on the WGPU/Chrome twin; the seam is engine-ASYMMETRIC

GOLDEN §2a/§4 says the `--ff-head-xy` write is free because "the CPU has it via
`partialSumAt(getSpectrum(), headT)` — the shipped math … ZERO new GL." **Verified false for the
WGPU path:**

- `useFourierField.ts` `onFrame(timeSec)` advances ONLY the scalar `headT`. It **never calls
  `partialSumAt`** (grep: zero call-sites in the composable). The head XY is computed where the
  curve is drawn:
  - **GL twin** (`fourierFieldGLSetup.ts:165,176`): `partialSumAt(spectrum, headT, …)` runs
    CPU-side every frame — so on the GL/Safari path the head XY is genuinely already in hand.
  - **WGPU twin** (`fourierFieldWGPUSetup.ts:240,252`): only `headT = getHeadT()` (a scalar) is
    uploaded as a uniform; the head XY is computed **inside the compute shader**
    (`fourier-field.compute.wgsl.ts:88`). There is **no CPU readback** (grep: no `mapAsync`/
    `getMappedRange`/readback anywhere in the fourier composables).
- **Consequence:** on Chrome (the default WGPU path) writing `--ff-head-xy` requires a NEW
  per-frame CPU `partialSumAt` evaluation that duplicates the GPU's work (the math runs twice:
  GPU for the visual, CPU for the seam) — this is net-new compute, not "zero new," and the
  GOLDEN's headline perf claim ("ZERO new GL, ZERO re-layout, one setProperty") omits the CPU
  re-eval entirely.
- **Hardening:** state the seam plainly as a NEW CPU evaluator shared by both twins — add a
  `headXY()` derive in `useFourierField` (one `partialSumAt(getSpectrum(), headT, harmonicN)`/
  frame, cheap: N≤64 cos/sin) and have BOTH setups consume it (kills the GL/WGPU asymmetry by
  construction). Update §2a/§4 to own the cost honestly; drop "zero new."

## R2 (LANDS) — the cited write-site `onFrame` has no host element; the §4 call-site is wrong

GOLDEN §4: "In `useFourierField.ts`'s `onFrame` (`:100`): … `hostEl.style.setProperty('--ff-head-xy', …)`."
**`onFrame(timeSec)` has no `hostEl` in scope** — the composable's only DOM handles are the
`canvasRef` and a derived `pointerHost` (the canvas parent) used solely for pointer listeners.
The styleable wrapper (`.fourier-field` `hostRef`) lives in the **SFC** (`FourierField.vue:193`),
not the composable. The seam must be an SFC-level effect (a `watch`/`onFrame` callback exposed
to the SFC writing `hostRef`), not a `setProperty` inside `onFrame`. Minor but it's an
integration-precision miss in a doc that swears "every symbol grep-/source-confirmed at HEAD."
Also: the precedent cite `useGlassBackdropLuminance.ts:442` is at the right file (442/448/453 DO
`el.style.setProperty`), but that observer owns its OWN target `el` — the fourier seam does not
yet have that wiring; it's owed, not "verbatim."

**Hardening:** re-home the seam to the SFC (expose `headXY` from the composable, write
`hostRef.value.style.setProperty` in an SFC frame hook or `watchEffect`). Correct §4's call-site.

## R3 (LANDS) — the spike's G2 "near>far" gate is SELF-CERTIFYING; it proves nothing about the painted field

The spike (`phosphor-bloom.html`) reports "G2 bloom is LOCAL (near 1.00 > far 0.00): PASS" — but
its `fieldLumaAt()` **does not read painted pixels**. Its own comment admits: *"can't read
painted pixels without canvas; instead report the seam distance proxy."* It returns
`1 - hypot(ux-hx, uy-hy)/0.5` — i.e. luma = closeness to the bloom centre. `near` is sampled AT
the bloom centre (distance 0 → **always 1.00**) and `far` at the opposite corner (distance >0.5
→ **always 0.00**). **The gate cannot fail by construction** — it is a born-GREEN tautology
dressed as a born-GREEN proof, the exact anti-pattern §8 forbids ("never a … name-presence").
A spike that "de-risks the boldest mechanism" must actually measure whether the `screen`-blend
bloom is *perceptible against the field* — which it never does.

**Hardening:** make the spike read real pixels — render the `.viz-warm-field` + bloom into an
offscreen `<canvas>` via `drawImage`/`html-to-image`, sample OKLab L near vs far, assert a real
ΔL ≥ floor. Or drop the proxy and rely on the `src/` π's screenshot-pixel readback. Until then
the spike de-risks nothing about visibility — only that the var changes (which was never in
doubt).

## R4 (LANDS) — §2a is strong in DARK, WEAK in LIGHT; the `screen` blend has almost nothing to add over a near-white cream field

Live-tested both modes on the spike:
- **Dark:** the bloom reads clearly (a hot-amber glow on an ember field) — §2a genuinely
  de-risked here, warm floor holds (NOT gray). Good.
- **Light:** the bloom is **barely visible** (first screenshot — a faint smudge bottom-left).
  `mix-blend-mode: screen` over a `--ff-base oklch(0.95 …)` near-white cream adds ≈nothing
  (screen of a bright base is ~identity). This is the **inverse of the D3 light-mode invisibility
  the GOLDEN claims to cure** — the SURPASS mechanism is itself faint in the exact mode the
  diagnosis flags as weakest. G2's "near>far" will pass numerically (the var is local) while the
  eye sees no bloom — passing the letter, failing the spirit.

**Hardening:** the light-mode bloom must use a blend/strategy that darkens-toward-warm or
multiplies a warm pool (e.g. `mix-blend-mode: multiply` with a warm-amber that *removes* blue,
or a 2nd `plus-darker`/`color-burn` layer) so the comet's wake reads as a deepening amber pool
over cream, not an additive no-op. G2 must assert a per-mode **perceptual** ΔL (light: the wake
is *darker/warmer*; dark: *brighter*) — one blend mode cannot serve both. Note `plus-darker` is
WebKit-only and `color-burn` differs in gamut handling — pick a Safari-verified pair.

## R5 (LANDS) — `BD.W-FOURIER-INTERACT` (the AFFIRMED, load-bearing D2/G5 anchor) does NOT EXIST as a wave; `useVizKeyboard`/`FOURIER_KEYMAP`/`useFourierStroke` are doc-only

The GOLDEN leans the entire headline interaction (D2, G5, §3c, the egg-purge, the keymap) on
"`BD.W-FOURIER-INTERACT` — AFFIRMED verbatim." **There is no such wave file** in
`docs/tranches/BD/waves/` (grep: `FOURIER-INTERACT` appears only in planning docs — VIZ-DAG,
DEFERRED-CENSUS `D89 … BUILD … BD.W-FOURIER-INTERACT (Band 13)`). It is a **deferred/unwritten
BUILD item**, not an affirmed spec. Likewise `useVizKeyboard`, `FOURIER_KEYMAP`, and
`useFourierStroke` appear ONLY in docs — **none exist in `src/`**. So §3c "composes DIRECTLY at
the SFC … `useVizKeyboard`" and §4 "`useFourierStroke` … AFFIRM" cite primitives that are
themselves unbuilt. The golden's "deftly integrable, reuse extant primitives" claim is partly
fictional for the D2 arm — D2 is net-new build resting on net-new build.

**Hardening:** either (a) write `BD.W-FOURIER-INTERACT` first and make the golden depend on a
real wave, or (b) inline the draw/keymap spec into THIS golden and stop citing a phantom
affirmation. Mark `useVizKeyboard`/`useFourierStroke` as NEW (they are), not as extant reuse.
G5's "rides `BD.W-FOURIER-INTERACT`'s π" must point at a π that exists.

## R6 (LANDS, a11y) — the DRAW gesture (the headline payoff) is pointer-ONLY over an `aria-hidden` canvas; no keyboard/SR path is specified

`FourierField.vue:197` — the canvas is `aria-hidden="true"`. The draw-your-own-path interaction
(D2, the gestalt's promised "re-thread it BY HAND") is `pointerdown→move→up` only. A keyboard or
screen-reader user **cannot draw a path** and the canvas is hidden from AT entirely. §5's a11y
section covers PRM/contrast/reduced-transparency but is **silent on how the DRAW is operable
without a pointer** — a WCAG 2.1.1 (keyboard) gap on the headline interaction. The curated
ℱ/heart/star gallery is the only AT-reachable path, but the golden frames *drawing* as the
payoff.

**Hardening:** §5 must add the keyboard story — the `FOURIER_KEYMAP` (curated-shape cycling +
N-scrub) IS the keyboard-operable equivalent of the draw; state it as the AT fallback explicitly
(draw = pointer enhancement; keymap = the accessible baseline), label the gallery shapes, and
give the host an accessible name/role so the studio is reachable. Don't ship draw as the sole
path to "re-thread."

## R7 (PARTIAL) — G1's "hue spread in [20,70]" can pass on gradient STOPS while the eye sees a monochrome wash

Live: both modes read as essentially **one hue** — light = uniform coral/salmon, dark =
uniform red-umber (the conic hue variety is washed out by the `screen` bloom + low chroma). G1
asserts an OKLab-hue *spread* ∈ [20,70] sampled at ≥3 points — but a field whose stops span
28→34 technically "spreads" while reading flat. The §3 finding was "a COLOURFUL field," not "a
warm monochrome." The gate risks passing a tint that still reads dead.

**Hardening:** raise G1 from a hue-*range* check to a hue-*variance* + per-point chroma floor
that demands ≥2 visibly distinct warm pools (e.g. an amber pool AND a rose pool, ΔL and Δhue
between the two sampled pools above a perceptual floor), so "warm-cream field with amber pools +
a coral rope" is enforced, not a single coral haze. Light mode especially needs a real second
pool — the spike's light field is monochrome today.

## R8 (MINOR) — line-cites drift; "byte-frozen + grep-confirmed at HEAD" is overstated

`render.wgsl` cites (`:151` `*0.7`, `:178` `dHead`, `:180` `haloR`) check out. But §2a's
"`useFourierField.ts:100` onFrame already knows the head model position" is false (R1), the
`:442` precedent is right-file/wrong-mechanism (R2), and `computeFourierFit` is in
`uniformBridgeWGPU.ts:92` (cited correctly) but is consumed inside the GL/WGPU setups, not
reachable from the SFC seam without re-plumbing the fit center/scale out. The "every symbol
grep-confirmed" framing is ~85% true — enough drift to mislead the builder on the one genuinely
novel seam.

**Hardening:** re-verify the §2a seam citations against the actual `onFrame` signature and
export the cached `FourierFit` (center/scale) from the composable so the SFC seam can map
model→[0,1]² without re-deriving the fit.

## WHAT SURVIVES (fit, keep)

- The §2b squash head is cross-engine-SOUND by construction: `curveSamples[0]` (head) and
  `curveSamples[1]` (next) are both in the render storage buffer on BOTH twins, so the tangent
  `T = normalize(s0 - s1)` and the volume-preserving ellipse SDF derive identically in
  `render.wgsl` and `glsl` — real twin parity, no readback needed. KEEP.
- The warm field is genuinely Safari-safe: plain `radial`/`conic` gradients + a `@property`
  angle drift, sRGB interp, NO `filter:url`, NO `backdrop-filter` on the field (the one
  `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)` is on the glass-hint plate, a plain blur — Safari-fine). No
  meatball/metaball register here, so the naive-ellipsoid precept doesn't bite. KEEP.
- The math leaf byte-freeze + the presets-in-consumers lift discipline are correct and the gates
  G6 (math diff) is real. The dark-mode §2a de-risk is genuine (bloom perceptible, warm floor
  holds). KEEP.

---

## VERDICT

**SURVIVES — WOUNDED.** The gestalt, the diagnosis, the born-RED gates, the §2b parity, and the
Safari-safety of the field all hold. But the golden is **not buildable as written**: the §2a
seam is engine-asymmetric and not "zero new" (R1), cited at a call-site that can't host it (R2),
the spike that "de-risks" it proves nothing (R3), the mechanism is faint in light mode (R4), the
D2 headline rests on a phantom "affirmed" wave + three unbuilt primitives (R5), and the draw
payoff has no keyboard/AT path (R6). These are hardening-shaped, not fatal — fix R1–R6 and it
ships. Top refutation: **R1** (the §2a engine-asymmetry + the false zero-cost claim).
