baseline. A gate that asserts "the painted comet width exceeds the HEAD 3px/0.92 hairline" will

reviewer can find the 0.92. A born-RED gate MUST cite the REAL pre-lift value or it is the exact
"name-presence / doc-keyword" theater `BD.W-FOURIER-INTERACT §The GATE` was created to kill.

Worse: lifting `trailFloor` from 0.34→0.45 is a SMALLER delta than the golden implies (it reads

driven more by `trailWidth: 3` + the additive composite washing out over a LIGHT ground than by
the floor — which strengthens R3 below (the field, not the floor, is the real D3 fix).

**HARDENING:** G3 must cite the REAL HEAD baseline (`trailWidth:3`, `trailFloor:0.34/0.36`,
`peak*0.7` scaffold) and assert the painted-stroke-width + peak-luminance DELTA over THAT measured

## REFUTATION R2 (a binding-law gestalt gap) — the §3a field is GENERIC, not "the same math that redraws the ℱ handmark"; the gestalt is asserted but not MECHANIZED

The golden's gestalt thesis (§1) is "the HARMONIC LOOM that lights its own field" — the field is

`.viz-warm-field` primitive. The spike (`phosphor-bloom.html`) proves this: its field is two
static radial blobs + a slow conic spin — handsome, but it has **zero fourier identity**. Strip the
comet and it is indistinguishable from any other warm card.

This is a gestalt-fidelity refutation, not a perf one. The user's bar (IOS27-REFERENCE T11) is a
**living vibrant backdrop that breathes with MEANING** — the Music field bleeds the *album hue*,
the Weather sky lights from the *sun*. The golden borrows the "lit by content" half (§2a, good)
but the AMBIENT half is a decorative gradient with no harmonic structure. For "the same math that
redraws the ℱ handmark," the field should carry a faint ECHO of the harmonic structure even at
rest — e.g. the phosphor trail's decay path IS the partial-sum curve's wake (the spike already
half-does this with `--ff-trail-xy`), or the conic-drift frequency is φ-locked to the spectrum's
fundamental. As written, the §2a bloom is the ONLY thing tying the field to the viz, and under PRM

**HARDENING:** (a) the PRM static field must STILL read as fourier-specific — seat the frozen-T
phosphor trail as a visible woven wake along the frozen partial-sum curve (not just a single
frozen bloom dot), so the "loom wove this" reading survives reduced-motion. (b) Add a gate clause

woven wake), not just a single near>far hotspot — otherwise the gestalt is motion-only and fails

"nothing arbitrary" Aristotelian precept the golden itself invokes).

## REFUTATION R3 (a Safari/compositor gap the golden DISMISSES too fast) — `mix-blend-mode: screen` on the phosphor layers is the real WebKit risk, not `filter:url`

The golden's §5 Safari section is smug: "Fourier has NO meatball/blob register — the metaball law
applies to dock/blob, not here." True, but it then declares the warm field "engine-identical by
construction (pure CSS)" and moves on. **The spike reveals the actual cross-engine hazard the
golden never names:** the phosphor bloom + trail layers use `mix-blend-mode: screen`
(`phosphor-bloom.html:110`) to add warm light onto the field, AND a `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)`
on the glass-hint (`:138`). Two real WebKit concerns:

1. **`mix-blend-mode` interaction with `backdrop-filter` and `isolation: isolate`** is a known
   Safari compositor-correctness minefield — isolated stacking contexts can clip or mis-composite
   blend layers, and `screen` over a `conic-gradient` with `@property` animation has historically
   triggered WebKit repaint/banding bugs. The golden asserts "sRGB, Safari-safe" but `screen` blend
   + animated `@property <angle>` conic is NOT a path it has live-verified on WebKit (the spike is
   "open in Chrome AND Safari" per its header comment, but no captured WebKit artifact exists in
   `golden/`). "Safari-safe by construction" is a claim, not a capture — the binding law demands a
   captured WebKit delta, not a reasoned one (`feedback_live_verify_capture`).

2. **The bloom reads `--hx`/`--hy` via `calc(var(--hx) * 100%)` inside a `radial-gradient`
   position.** Animating a gradient POSITION per-frame (even via a CSS var, not the property
   itself) forces a gradient RE-RASTER each frame on some engines — the golden claims "compositor-
   cheap, one setProperty/frame" but a moving radial-gradient center is a PAINT, not a
   composite-transform. On a 60fps head sweep this is a full-layer repaint every frame, which on a
   large stage in Safari (no GPU-rasterized gradients in all cases) can drop frames. The golden's
   "ZERO re-layout, one setProperty/frame" is true for LAYOUT but FALSE for PAINT.

**HARDENING:** (a) capture a REAL WebKit artifact of the phosphor bloom + screen-blend + conic
drift before claiming Safari-safe (the `golden/` dir has zero WebKit captures — add one or drop
the claim). (b) Replace `mix-blend-mode: screen` with a `plus-lighter`/additive that is
compositor-promotable, OR pre-promote the phosphor layer to its own GPU layer (`will-change`,
`translateZ`) and verify it does not break the `isolation: isolate` stacking the defined-edge
vignette needs. (c) Re-classify §2a honestly as a per-frame PAINT (a bounded one), gate a frame-
budget clause (G7b: the phosphor sweep holds 60fps on the webkit project at the stage's real px),
and consider clamping the bloom to a fixed-size sprite TRANSLATED to `--ff-head-xy` (a true
composite-transform) rather than a re-rastered gradient position — the genuinely compositor-cheap
form the golden CLAIMS but the spike does not implement.

## REFUTATION R4 (tight/springy + missing-punch, the cartoon precept) — the §3d "liquid-weight" is asserted but the shipped clock is a TIGHT exponential decay, and the squash is the ONLY punch

Two cartoon-register refutations:

**4a — the momentum is `Math.pow(0.92, dt*60)`, a stiff exponential, not the gooey overshoot the
law demands.** The golden §3d says "KEEP + extend the same settle" of the shipped flick momentum
as "the inertia register." But I read it (`useFourierField.ts:129-141`): the flick injects
`pointer.burst * 4.0` then decays by `momentum *= Math.pow(0.92, dt*60)`. That is a pure
exponential settle — it has inertia but NO overshoot, NO bounce, NO squish. The binding law is
explicit: "NEVER tight/springy; morph MORE on move." An exponential-to-zero decay is precisely the
"tight" settle the law forbids — it glides to rest without the liquid over-travel-and-back the
user keeps flagging. The golden uncritically blesses it as "the iOS fling settle" and proposes to
COPY it to the keyboard scrub (§3d: "a `,`/`.` press injects a small momentum impulse"). Copying a
non-compliant settle to a second site PROPAGATES the defect.

**4b — the squash bead is the ONLY new punch, and it is DEMO-gated to `k≈0` by default.** The
golden's cartoon-punch inventory is: squash head (§2b, default `k≈0` → OFF in src), cel-shadow
(§3b, DEMO-preset-gated → OFF in src), assembly squish (§3c, interaction-only). So in the LIBRARY
DEFAULT — the identity the user judges first — there is ZERO cartoon punch: a round bead, no ink,
no squash. The "1940s technicolor PUNCH" lives entirely in a consumer preset. Presets-in-consumers
is correct for the VIVID lift, but the binding law elevates cartoon principles toward UNIVERSAL
("elevate design.md §L4 weak/medium principles toward universal"). A library default with literally
zero squash/anticipation/follow-through on its flagship math viz under-serves the precept. The
golden hides behind "byte-frozen src" — but byte-frozen is a KEEP-fence for the MATH, not a license
for a punch-free default register.

**HARDENING:** (a) re-spec the clock momentum as a real spring settle with bounded overshoot (the
`--ease-cartoon-punch` / `SpringProgress` register the dock uses, ζ<1 for a hair of give), NOT
`pow(0.92)` — and gate it (a momentum-impulse readback that the head OVERSHOOTS its rest phase then
settles, not monotonic decay). Do NOT copy the exponential to the keyboard scrub; compose the
spring leaf. (b) Give the SRC DEFAULT a small non-zero squash (`k` small-but-visible) and a
minimal cel-offset so the library identity carries cartoon-weight at rest — the VIVID preset turns
it to 11, but zero is not the floor the precept allows. Re-word §2b/§3b: the default is "subtle
punch," not "byte-frozen round bead." (c) G4 must assert the DEFAULT (not just the vivid preset)
paints anisotropy > 1 at speed, or the gate green-lights a punch-free ship.
