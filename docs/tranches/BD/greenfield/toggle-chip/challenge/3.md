# CHALLENGE 3 — DESIGN-FIDELITY / GESTALT lens (ADVERSARIAL)

Verdict: **SURVIVES-WITH-AMENDMENT** (the design idea is sound and the geometry/motion
spec is fit; the GOLDEN's *self-description* as a "union with the shipped ecosystem,
spike-proven" is materially overstated — it is a union with FOUR UNBUILT siblings, and
the spike commits the exact fraud §K fences against). One load-bearing correction is
mandatory; the rest are hardenings.

Default was REFUTED. It survives because every refutation lands on the GOLDEN's *claims
about its own readiness*, not on the design's correctness — once the sequencing + the
honesty corrections below are bolted in, the gestalt holds. But as written it would ship a
GREEN that is GREEN-because-the-deps-are-missing, not GREEN-because-the-chip-is-right.

---

## R1 (THE TOP REFUTATION — load-bearing) — "≥4th consumer of `.glass-capsule`" is a dependency on VAPOR, not a union with the shipped ecosystem

The GOLDEN's central architectural thesis (§A material row, §D1, §G3, §M.7) is:

> *"the chip is the ≥4th consumer of the tabs extract `.glass-capsule`… ZERO new material…
> A UNION with the shipped ecosystem… consumes five extant registers."*

**On disk this is false.** Grep of `src/` (2026-06-24):

| claimed-consumed primitive | GOLDEN says | actual `src/` state |
|---|---|---|
| `.glass-capsule` | tabs extract, `glass/glass-capsule.css` | **does not exist** — `ls glass/glass-capsule.css` → no file; `grep -rln glass-capsule src/` → **zero hits**. The material lives INLINE in `.segmented-indicator` (`segmented-tabs.css:91`), never extracted. |
| `.glass-capsule-hover` | tabs GOLDEN | **zero hits in src/** |
| `.glass-capsule-track` | recessed group well | **zero hits in src/** |
| `--ease-cartoon-punch` | "tabs GOLDEN §3a Band-0 mint" | **zero hits in src/** |
| `--motion-weight` | "tabs GOLDEN Band-0 mint" | **zero hits in src/** |
| `.paper-field` | "glass-material GOLDEN W-GLASS-FIELD" | **zero hits in src/** |

So FIVE of the "five extant registers it consumes" are **un-built greenfield promises** from
the `tabs/` and `glass-material/` sibling GOLDENs, which are themselves still spec dirs
(`docs/tranches/BD/greenfield/{tabs,glass-material}/`), not landed `src/`. The only genuinely
extant consumed primitives are `.accent-tone` (real, `accent-tone.css`), `--radius-control`
(real, `radius.css:56`), `useTabIndicator` (real, `tabs/composables/`), and reka `Toggle` —
and even those are wired to the absent ones.

**Why this is a fidelity/gestalt failure, not a nitpick:** the binding law is *"DEFT
INTEGRATION… a UNION, never a bolt-on or a parallel fork… reuse extant primitives."* A design
that depends on four primitives that **must be invented elsewhere first** is not a union with
the *extant* ecosystem — it is the **dependent leaf of a four-node build DAG**, and the GOLDEN
nowhere declares that DAG or its ordering. If `tabs` lands its capsule under a different name,
or `glass-material` ships `.paper-field` with a different field token, this chip GOLDEN's
"one-line consume" silently breaks. The §G3 "dup-check: DEPENDS-ON tabs" notes the dependency
but the prose (§A, §D1, §M, the one-sentence thesis) sells it as already-extant — an
**honesty inflation** of exactly the "live-verified ≠ commit-claim" species in MEMORY.

**Hardening (mandatory):** add a §G0 BUILD-ORDER PRECONDITION block that states plainly:
*"This wave is DOWNSTREAM of `W-TABS-LIQUID` (which must first EXTRACT `.glass-capsule` +
`.glass-capsule-hover` + `.glass-capsule-track` from `.segmented-indicator` into
`glass/glass-capsule.css`) and `W-GLASS-FIELD` (`.paper-field`) and the Band-0
`--ease-cartoon-punch`/`--motion-weight` mint. NONE exist in `src/` today. The chip wave is
BLOCKED until those land; its gate cannot pass before them."* Re-word every "consumes / the
≥4th consumer / a union with the shipped ecosystem" to "consumes — once the upstream extracts
land — …". Add the explicit extraction-name contract (capsule class names + field token name)
so a downstream rename is caught, not silently absorbed.

## R2 — the headline chroma figure is MISLABELED, and the spike commits the very fraud §K fences

§D2 and §J both headline:

> *"Spike-measured: idle fill `oklab(0.902 0.034 0.037)` → C = 0.050 — well above the 0.02 floor."*
> §J table: *"C3 warm-not-gray idle … idle fill oklab(0.902 …) → C 0.050 ≥ 0.02 ✓"*

**Live readback (chrome-devtools, the served spike, 2026-06-24) contradicts this:**
- IDLE chips (`data-state=off`) compute `oklab(0.934592 0.0212721 0.0261924)` → **C = 0.0337**.
- The `oklab(0.902 0.034 0.037)` / **C = 0.0501** value the GOLDEN labels "idle" is the
  **ACTIVE band** (`data-state=on`, L=0.902) — the *Shared/Music* chips.

So the headline "idle C 0.050" is the **active band's chroma mislabeled as idle**. The true
idle is C=0.034. It still clears 0.02 — the cure *directionally* works — but the GOLDEN's
proof-figure is wrong by ~50% and attributes the active reading to the idle state. A reviewer
trusting §J would certify a number that isn't the thing measured.

**Worse — the deeper fraud:** §K's own self-test bite says *"a chip reading getComputedStyle
over a hardcoded field (not a screenshot) RED… parse-oklab-as-sRGB over a hardcoded purple → a
gray chip passes."* But the spike's `readChroma()` / `__runGate()` reads
**`getComputedStyle(el).backgroundColor`** — the TOKEN fill — and **never composites it through
the `backdrop-filter` over the field**. The spike's own honest-note admits this
(*"chroma C-floor is measured by the live-π via canvas screenshot composite… this readback
proves geometry+motion+support"*) — yet §J still prints "C 0.050 ≥ 0.02 ✓" in the assertion
table as if the spike proved the warm-floor. **The spike does NOT prove the painted-pixel
warm-floor; it proves the token value.** A warm token over a cool blur can still composite
gray (that is literally Root #1). The headline conflates token-chroma with painted-chroma.

**Hardening:** (a) fix §D2/§J to read *"idle TOKEN C=0.034 (live); ACTIVE band C=0.050"* —
correct the label and the figure. (b) Add a hard sentence to §J: *"the spike proves
TOKEN-chroma + geometry + motion ONLY; the painted-through-backdrop warm-floor is UNPROVEN
until the §K screenshot-pixel π over the real `.paper-field` runs — the spike is NOT
warm-floor evidence."* (c) Strike the "✓" on the C3 row of the §J table; mark it
"DEFERRED-TO-GATE."

## R3 — dark-vibrant fidelity: the idle chips read MUDDY/DARK over the field, not "glowing warm lenses"

§M.5 demands *"dark GLOWS through the lens, never charcoal"* and §D1 ON promises *"the
selected chip lifts off the field as a lit tonal lozenge."* The supplied
`golden/spike-dark-vibrant.png` (live-read) shows the OPPOSITE for the IDLE majority: over the
purple/teal field the three idle chips (Recents/Favorites/Shared-off) render as **dark,
low-contrast, near-charcoal warm shapes** that barely separate from the field; only the two
lit chips (Albums/Music-on) read as bright cream lozenges. The user's complaint is that *"most
chips are idle most of the time"* (§B2) — and in the demonstration screenshot the idle state in
the hard case (vibrant dark field) is precisely the charcoal the precept forbids. The
`backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(1.5)` over a dark-purple field pulls the lens DOWN in L,
and the idle's thin 15% floor cannot lift it back to "glowing." The spike's own dark token uses
`--glass-bg-floating-tinted: color-mix(--card 72%, white 6%)` — a *dark* base — so an idle chip
over the dark field is dark-on-dark.

**Hardening:** the §K C3 painted arm must assert idle chroma AND **idle L-separation from the
field** (a ΔL floor, e.g. idle composited L ≥ field-L + 0.08) in BOTH modes over the vibrant
field, not just C≥0.02 over flat cream. Add a dark-vibrant idle frame to the acceptance set
(§M) — the current §M.5 says "dark glows" but never tests idle-over-vibrant, the actual fail
case. If the floor can't lift idle off a dark vibrant field, raise `--chip-tint-floor` dark to
18-20% or add an idle rim-catch lift (the `--glass-rim-top` α) so the EDGE separates even when
the fill can't.

## R4 — the gate is NOT born-RED for the reasons claimed; it is born-UNRUNNABLE

§K claims *"born-RED on C1–C5 on HEAD."* But:
- C3b asserts `backdrop-filter !== none` — yet the SHIPPED chips can't even be observed at the
  route, because **`/forms/selectable-chip` serves the SPIKE HTML** (live: the route resolves
  to `…/toggle-chip/golden/spike.html`, the buttons read `glass-chip sm/md/cell` — the spike,
  not `SelectableChip.vue`). So a gate "run on HEAD" against that route reads the SPIKE (which
  PASSES), not the shipped grab-bag (which would FAIL). The born-RED depends on the gate
  hitting the real component; the route aliasing means it may hit the spike and be born-GREEN.
- C5 (cartoon-punch) asserts the `--ease-cartoon-punch` overshoot — but that token **doesn't
  exist in `src/`**, so a HEAD run isn't "RED because the punch is absent," it's *errored/
  no-such-token*. A born-RED must FAIL on a real assertion against real code; this one can't
  evaluate its subject.

**Hardening:** §K must (a) pin the gate to the actual `SelectableChip`/`ToggleChip`/`IconChip`
DOM (a component-mount harness, not a route that Vite-fallbacks to a spike), and (b) split the
born-RED into "RED on geometry/glass (assertable against HEAD's real grab-bag today)" vs
"DEFERRED (the punch/capsule arms can't be RED until the upstream tokens land — they error,
they don't fail)." Re-state the §K honest-truth box to say so.

## R5 — KISS/DRY tension the GOLDEN waves away: `cell` is a SECOND geometry language inside "ONE recipe"

§C1 sells *"ONE geometry language, one exception"* — but the cell tile is a 72px square on
`--radius-card` with `flex-direction: column`, stacked glyph+label, a DIFFERENT pad language
(`px-2 py-2.5` vs the pill's φ-ladder), and the spike confirms it (radius 16px vs 9999px). That
is not "one geometry with an exception" — it is **two silhouettes (stadium pill + squircle
card) under one class**, which is exactly the congruence complaint the user raised ("none of
these are styled congruently"), merely reduced from 5 silhouettes to 2. A user looking at a
chip row + a cell grid still sees two shapes. The GOLDEN pre-empts this ("a tile is a card, not
a pill") which is a *defensible* call — but it should be stated as a deliberate 2-silhouette
family, not smuggled under "ONE congruent register / no grab-bag" (§M.1).

**Hardening:** §M.1 acceptance must read *"inline chips are ONE pill silhouette; the cell is a
deliberate card silhouette sharing material+motion+tone but NOT radius"* — name the
2-silhouette reality so the live-judge doesn't fail it for "still two shapes" or, worse, pass a
genuinely-incongruent build by reading the escape clause loosely.

## R6 — `--ease-cartoon-punch` overshoot is UNVERIFIED in paint; the spike admits it reads the SETTLED frame

§E2/§J claim the punch *"dips to −0.04 (anticipation) and peaks at 1.22 (overshoot)."* The
spike's own honest-note: *"the getComputedStyle().scale frame-series catches the settled value,
not the sub-frame transient curve peak."* So the spike PARSES the `linear()` stops (a string
read) and asserts the math, but **never observes the overshoot in paint** — the rAF sampler
caught settled scales (`endScale≈1.037`), not the 1.22 transient. The "weighty flip" — the
single boldest design move — is **mathematically asserted, not paint-verified**. Per MEMORY's
live-verify-capture law, "weighty" needs a captured DELTA (a frame at the peak), not a parsed
curve.

**Hardening:** §K C5 must capture the actual overshoot frame (a `requestAnimationFrame` at
~t=0.76×duration sampling the COMPUTED scale, or a video-frame extract), asserting observed
scale ≥1.04 mid-transition — not parse the easing string. Until that frame exists, mark the
punch "spec-correct, paint-UNVERIFIED."

## R7 (minor) — PRM `--motion-weight:0` zeroes the scale envelope but the `::after` flood opacity still snaps; and `plus-lighter` PRM-fallback unstated for the lit read

§E3 says PRM → `--motion-weight:0` "zeroes anticipation + overshoot + bloom in ONE
assignment." But the bloom opacity is driven by `--chip-flood-t` (set by `[data-state=on]`),
NOT by `--motion-weight` — at PRM the spike kills the `::after` *transition* (good) but the
`::after` still renders at `opacity:1` instantly on select (the bloom is present, just
un-animated). That's arguably fine (vestibular-safe = no MOTION, a static bloom is OK), but the
GOLDEN's claim "zeroes the bloom" is imprecise — the bloom doesn't vanish, its motion does.

**Hardening:** reword §E3/§I to *"PRM zeroes the bloom's MOTION (the trailing transition); the
static lit fill + a static (un-transitioned) bloom remain — selection legible, motion absent."*
Confirm the §K C7 "no bloom" assert means "no bloom MOTION," else C7 fails a legitimately-lit
PRM chip.

## What SURVIVES (the fit core, per survival-of-the-fittest)

- **Geometry unification → one `--radius-control` stadium** (§C): genuinely fit. The
  `--radius-control = --radius-pill` rung is real on disk (`radius.css:56`); collapsing
  `rounded-sm/md/lg` (confirmed live in `selectableChipVariants.ts:50-52`) to it is the right
  DRY answer to the user's #1 complaint. The cell exception is defensible.
- **`.accent-tone` idle warm-floor via `max(strength, floor)`** (§D2): fit — a one-line widen
  of the real `accent-tone.css` recipe, FLOOR-not-clamp preserves consumer override. Directionally
  cures the born-RED gray (idle C 0.014 → 0.034 live).
- **The φ size axis + the no-alias CVA collapse** (§C2, §G1): fit, no-legacy-clean.
- **Rejecting lens-b's bespoke metaball-gradient flood for the scale+`::after`** (§A, §H): the
  correct KISS call — the chip is a CSS lens, not a metaball; no `backdrop-filter:url`, no SVG
  goo, `plus-lighter` is Safari-native. Cross-engine reasoning is sound.
- **PRM/transparency/contrast carves + reka-semantics-untouched** (§I): fit.

The DESIGN is right. The GOLDEN's *account of its own evidence and readiness* is what fails:
it sells an unbuilt-dependency fork as an extant union, mislabels its proof chroma, prints a ✓
on a warm-floor the spike never painted, and calls a born-UNRUNNABLE gate born-RED. Fix the
honesty + the build-order sequencing and the gestalt survives.
