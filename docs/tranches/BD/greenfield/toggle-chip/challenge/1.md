not yet built**. If those waves slip or diverge, the chip golden has nothing to
compose and silently becomes a *fork* (it would have to inline `.glass-capsule`

does this: `spike.html` **inlines** `.glass-chip` with its own `background-color`,
`backdrop-filter`, `::after` — it does NOT `@import` a `.glass-capsule` (because
none exists), so the spike does not actually prove the *union*; it proves a
*standalone re-implementation* of the capsule.

**This is a deft-integration finding:** the union is **aspirational across an
unbuilt dependency chain**, presented as **already-real**. KISS/DRY hold *only if*
tabs §2 + motion-spring-register land first and land *exactly* as specced.
