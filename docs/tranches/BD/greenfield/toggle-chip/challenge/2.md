## R1 (FATAL — the centerpiece never paints). The `::after` commit-flood is dead by `@property inherits:false`.

The GOLDEN's single boldest move is "the chip that PUNCHES when you pick it" — and the visible
half of that punch is the §E3 `::after` accent-bloom ("the iOS-27 selected-chip 'ink-flood'").
It is keyed `opacity: var(--chip-flood-t, 0)` and `--chip-flood-t` is declared
`@property --chip-flood-t { syntax:"<number>"; inherits: false; initial-value: 0 }` (§E1, spike
line 23).

**Live readback on an ON chip in the GOLDEN's own spike:**

```
elFloodT (on the .glass-chip element) : "1"
afterFloodT (on its ::after pseudo)   : "0"     ← initial-value, NOT inherited
afterOpacity                          : "0"     ← the bloom is fully transparent, ON or OFF
```

A registered custom property with `inherits: false` resolves to its **initial-value on the
pseudo-element**, because the pseudo is a *separate box* that does not inherit the
non-inheriting property from its originating element. `[data-state="on"]{ --chip-flood-t:1 }`
sets it on the ELEMENT; the `::after` reads `0` forever. **The commit-flood — the §E3
"EFFECTS-trails-SPATIAL" ink-flood the entire motion chapter is built around — NEVER FIRES, in
Chrome OR Safari.** This is not a Safari gap; it is a spec bug that ships gray-on-arrival in
every engine. The GOLDEN §J table claims this mechanism "proven sound" — it is proven BROKEN by
its own artefact.

**Hardening:** declare `@property --chip-flood-t { inherits: true }` (the `::after` then
inherits the element's `1`), OR drive the `::after` opacity from a `[data-state="on"]` selector
on the element scoping the pseudo (`.glass-chip[data-state="on"]::after { opacity:1 }`) with the
trailing transition-delay for the EFFECTS-trails-SPATIAL ordering. The spike + the shipping
`glass-chip.css` + the §K gate MUST add a born-RED arm that reads `::after` opacity > 0 on the
ON chip (the current C5b only checks frame-ordering of a flood that never starts).

## R2 (FATAL — even fixed, it is occluded). The `::after` at `z-index:-1` paints BEHIND an OPAQUE fill.

`.glass-chip::after` is `z-index: -1` (spike line 145) and `mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter`. The
chip's own `background-color` is **opaque**:

```
chip bg (idle)  : oklab(0.90182 0.0342663 0.0365815)   ← NO alpha → fully opaque
chipBgLikelyOpaque : true
```

`--accent-fill`/`--accent-band` are `color-mix(in oklab, var(--card), var(--tone) …)` — a mix of
two opaque colours is opaque. A `plus-lighter` additive bloom at `z-index:-1` sits **behind** an
opaque fill and is **occluded** — `plus-lighter` is a no-op against an opaque layer in front of
it. So R1 and R2 are independent: fixing the inheritance (R1) still yields ZERO visible bloom
because the z-order buries it under the fill. The "additive over the warm fill, never a separate
opaque plate" claim in §D4 is contradicted by the spike's own z-index.

**Hardening:** the flood `::after` must sit ABOVE the fill (`z-index:0`/auto inside the
`isolation:isolate` chip, painted after the background), with the chip fill itself
semi-transparent so the bloom reads. This is the select-forms inset idiom the GOLDEN cites
(§D4) — but the spike implements the opposite (opaque fill + behind-flood). Re-spike with the
real layering before claiming "de-risked."

## R3 (FATAL to "GLASSY"). The lens is an OPAQUE rect — `backdrop-filter` is a visual no-op; nothing transmits.

The whole §D thesis is "the chip stops being a flat tinted rectangle and becomes a lifted warm
LENS… transmits a field." A lens requires a **translucent** body so the blurred field shows
through. The spike's body is `background-color: var(--accent-fill)` — **opaque** (R2). With an
opaque fill, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(1.5)` samples the field and then paints the
opaque fill ON TOP of it — the blur is **invisible**. Over the flat warm-cream page
(`rgb(253,245,236)`), a blurred uniform cream is identical to unblurred cream anyway, so the
"§3 colourful field + defined edge" relationship is absent in the worst-case (flat) route the
gate is supposed to cover. The chip is exactly the "solid `color-mix` fill, nothing transmits,
composites gray" failure §B2 Root#1 diagnoses — the spike re-commits it.

**Hardening:** the chip fill must be the `.glass-capsule` semi-transparent
`--glass-bg-floating-tinted` (alpha < 1), with `--accent-fill` tinting that translucent body
(not replacing it with an opaque `background-color`). The spike must paint over a REAL
`.paper-field` (not `background: var(--card)` flat) so the lens transmission is actually
exercised — the §K gate's C3 "no `.paper-field` ancestor FAILS" arm is correct, but the spike
itself never mounts one, so the spike proves nothing about transmission.
