## TOP REFUTATION (A) — "warm" is not "COLORFUL": the field is MONOCHROME amber, violating the §3 "colorful field" precept
The binding law (design.md:17, IOS27-REFERENCE T7 HEADLINE) is explicit that the §3 root-cause is
glass needs a **COLORFUL FIELD behind it** — and T7's headline reference is glass that **BLEEDS
purple/teal/varied HUE** as vibrant cards pass. "Colorful" in the iOS-27 canon means *chromatic
variety*, not merely *not-gray*.

The golden collapses the ENTIRE field to a single warm hue family: `warmFrac = 1.000`,
`r≥g≥b` for every painted pixel, a 4-stop ramp all in `hue ∈ [40,90]`. The live spike confirms
it — the dark panel especially reads as a uniform copper/orange wash with near-zero hue
diversity (basin→ridge is a *value* ramp, not a *hue* journey). It is vivid in CHROMA but
MONOCHROME in HUE.

The golden's gate (§5 clause 2, "warmFrac > 0.40, hue ∉ [180,270]") actively *rewards* this
collapse: a 100%-single-hue field passes the warm gate trivially. The gate cannot distinguish a
rich warm-divergent field (the §3.1 spec's own "cream→wheat→amber→ember" PLUS the §198 preset's
"sunset-coral→magenta-ember") from a flat amber wash. **The golden satisfies "never teal" by
over-correcting into "never anything but amber" — and calls a monochrome a colorful field.**

This is a gestalt failure as a user: a real USGS hypsometric map IS multi-hue (greens→tans→
browns→white). The golden cites "USGS survey" as the north star but ships a single-family ramp
that no published hypsometric tint actually uses. It is legible but not *audacious* and not
*colorful* — it reads as a sepia etching, not a living technicolor map.

### Hardening A
- Widen the default `WARM_IDENTITY_PALETTE` to a true warm-DIVERGENT hypsometric tint that still
  honors no-teal/no-navy: e.g. deep-plum basin → ember → amber → wheat → warm-gold ridge, hues
  spanning [340..90] (wrapping through red, NEVER into [180,270]). Multi-family warm, not one.
- Add a gate clause: **hue-SPREAD floor** — the painted hue histogram must occupy ≥ N distinct
  warm bins (chroma-weighted), not just "all warm." A single-bin amber wash must RED. Without
  this clause, clause 2 green-lights the very monochrome the precept forbids.

## REFUTATION (B) — the cartoon ANTICIPATION punch is shipped at ZERO; the spring CANNOT substitute (design.md self-contradiction)
design.md:309 is categorical: anticipation is "a thing no damped spring can express, since a
single damped spring approaches its target monotonically from one side." The cartoon register
(`--ease-cartoon-punch`) exists precisely BECAUSE springs cannot anticipate — a real ~4% pre-dip
below origin + a ~22% overshoot.

The golden (§1 last two rows) **REDUCES the anticipation pre-dip to "the kept spring envelope"**
and **DEFERS the cel cast-shadow to a default-LOW/zero hook** (`castLen: 0`). It then claims "the
kept spring + velocity heave already give anticipation→overshoot→follow-through." **By design.md's
own line 309 this is false** — the kept spring (overshoot 1.06, monotone approach) has NO
anticipation leg. The golden sheds the one mechanism that delivers cartoon anticipation and
asserts a substitute the precept says cannot exist.

Net: the golden ships the LAW's signature cartoon-punch (anticipation + the moving cast-shadow,
the §Shadows "moving cast" + §L4-toward-universal directive) at amplitude ~0. The binding law
"elevate design.md §L4 weak/medium principles toward UNIVERSAL" is not met — it is *demoted* to a
dormant hook. KISS is a fine instinct, but the precept here is explicitly anti-KISS-at-the-cost-
of-punch ("loud by design"). For a procedural viz that is meant to MATCH-or-BETTER the reference,
shipping the punch at zero is a fidelity miss, not a restraint win.

### Hardening B
- Ship the cursor-heave on the **`--ease-cartoon-punch` envelope** (the pre-dip→overshoot
  `linear()` shape), not the monotone spring: when the well engages, the terrain DIPS ~4% before
  bulging (anticipation), overshoots ~22%, settles. This is the field-domain expression of the
  one curve a spring provably cannot give.
- Ship `castLen` at a real non-zero default (the moving cel cast-shadow on index lines is the
  §Shadows "moving cast" signature — the golden itself calls it "the most audacious"); gate it
  cross-engine rather than defaulting it off. A default-LOW hook nobody enables is dead code
  (overfit/KISS violation in the OTHER direction).
- Add a gate clause that the heave envelope crosses BELOW the rest state before overshooting
  (anticipation present), not merely "compresses spacing." Clause 7 today only checks squash on
  arrival, which the monotone spring passes — it cannot detect the missing anticipation.

## REFUTATION (C) — "NO struct resize, NO new bind group" is structurally STRAINED: ~10 new float scalars do not fit ~4 float spare lanes
§0 and §4 promise the tunables "ride the spare `u0.w` / `norm.zw` / `line.w` lanes — NO new bind
group, NO struct resize." I counted the live `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts` layout:

- **Spare FLOAT lanes:** `u0.w`, `norm.z`, `norm.w`, `line.w` = **4 floats**. (The `ints` lane has
  3 spare slots but they are **Int32** — useless for `toneGain`/`lightDir`/`shadeAmp` floats.)
- **New scalars the golden adds (§4 constants row):** `toneGain`, `shadeAmp`, `lightDir.x`,
  `lightDir.y`, `indexEvery`, `indexMul`, `inkDarken`, `castLen`, `velocityHeave`, plus a `uMode`
  lane = **~10 floats**.

10 floats do not fit 4 float spares. The golden's own §4 even says "add a `uMode` lane" — adding
a lane IS a struct change. The "NO resize" / "zero new fork" claim is therefore not honest as
written; the build WILL resize the struct (or pack `lightDir`/`indexEvery` into the int lanes,
which fights the float math). This is not fatal — a struct resize is cheap and legitimate — but
the golden SELLS itself as "tunables on spare lanes, no resize," and that specific load-bearing
KISS/DRY claim is false. An honest golden must either (a) drop scalars to fit 4 floats, or
(b) admit the std140 struct grows by one+ vec4 and update the layout table.

### Hardening C
- Re-spec §4: explicitly add the needed `vec4` lane(s) to the layout table in
  `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts` (e.g. a `relief: vec4 (toneGain, shadeAmp, indexEvery, indexMul)` + a
  `relief2: vec4 (lightDir.xy, inkDarken, castLen)` + fold `velocityHeave`/`uMode` into remaining
  spares). Bump `CONCENTRIC_UNIFORM_BYTES`. Stop claiming "no resize" — claim "one declaration,
  std140-safe, +1–2 vec4."
- The gate's L4/L6 must assert the WGSL struct and the JS offset table stay ONE declaration after
  the resize (the existing single-source-layout invariant) — that is the real KISS guarantee,
  not "no resize."

## REFUTATION (D) — the committed PIXEL-PROOF artefact (`spike-shot.png`) is a BORN-DEAD witness (all-zeros)
[live-verify-capture] requires the proof be a *captured DELTA artefact*. The committed

I confirmed the spike is actually FINE by live re-render (`:5173` gives the real 0.491/0.421), so
the mechanism is de-risked — but the *committed proof file* is a born-dead witness. Per the
cardinal-lesson inflation note, a golden that cites a screenshot whose embedded numbers say
"grayFrac 1.000, fully gray" as its "never-gray PIXEL-PROOF" has shipped an artefact that
disproves its own claim. Anyone auditing the PNG (not re-running the spike) reads a FAILURE.

### Hardening D
- Re-capture `spike-shot.png` AFTER the probe settles (move `probe()` behind 2+ rAF + a longer
  delay, or gate on first non-zero readback) so the committed artefact's embedded numbers match
  the cited 0.49/0.42. A proof file whose own readout is all-zeros is not a witness.

## Lesser notes (not refutations, but harden these)
- **§3.5 proportion is post-hoc.** "13 levels / index 5 = Fibonacci 5/13" and "ramp spacing
  golden-related" read as numbers retrofitted to φ after the fact (13 levels is the EXISTING
  default, not chosen for φ). Aristotelian proportion is a *bias not a tyranny* (design.md:17) so
  this is allowed — but the golden should not dress an inherited constant as a φ derivation.
  Either DERIVE the ramp stops from √φ spacing for real, or drop the proportion-theater.
- **Opaque-substrate vs "field BEHIND glass."** The golden makes concentric fully opaque and says
  it now "CONTRIBUTES a field." Good — but verify it is still composited UNDER the page's glass
  demos (not painting over them). The §3.1 note about routing `BD.W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` is right;
  the gate clause 8 ("live warm ground behind") covers it. Keep that clause load-bearing.
- **Dark-mode hue is the weakest panel.** In the live spike the dark "luminous etch on ember"
  reads as near-uniform orange-on-black with the LEAST hue range of the two — exactly where
  Refutation A bites hardest. The per-mode dark arm (`fill*vec3(0.85,0.62,0.42)`) crushes the
  ramp toward one orange. Dark mode needs MORE hue spread, not less.
- **Cross-engine claim is sound.** `tanh`/`floor`/`fract`/`select↔step`/`mix`/`normalize` are
  genuinely identical-semantics primitives, no `backdrop-filter:url`, no Houdini, sRGB OETF +
  OKLab-in-linear. The Safari WebGL2 GLSL twin runs today. No cross-engine refutation lands.
- **a11y/PRM carve is genuinely BETTER than HEAD** — the opaque static fill + hillshade + index
  hierarchy make a legible printed relief under PRM, vs HEAD's frozen faint-line scaffold. No
  refutation here.

## Bottom line
The golden's diagnosis and opaque-fill spine SURVIVE — they are honest and pixel-proven. But as a
USER judging design fidelity: it ships a MONOCHROME amber field while citing "colorful field" and
"USGS survey" (A), it ships the cartoon ANTICIPATION punch at amplitude zero while the precept
says the spring it kept provably cannot anticipate (B), it oversells a "no struct resize" KISS
claim the lane math contradicts (C), and its committed pixel-proof artefact reads all-zeros (D).
Harden A + B before this is golden; C + D are honesty fixes.
