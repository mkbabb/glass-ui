## R3 (LANDS) — the live dark-mode render shows a HARD-EDGED CURSOR BOX, an unaddressed motion defect
Live `/substrates/concentric` dark mode (screenshotted) shows a visible orange **parallelogram/box
artifact** at the cursor well — the `cursorWell` Gaussian is rendering with a hard quad edge, not a
smooth bulge. The GOLDEN §3.4 "cursor HEAVE" assumes it is scaling a clean Gaussian well; it never
diagnoses this boxy artifact. Scaling well depth+radius by velocity (§3.4) will SCALE THE ARTIFACT —
a fast sweep heaves a bigger hard-edged box. That violates liquid-weight (the heave must be soft,
weighted, no hard edges) and cartoon squash-&-stretch (a box is not a bulge).

**HARDENING:** before §3.4 velocity-heave lands, root-cause the cursor quad edge (likely a clamped
domain or a missing smoothstep falloff on the Gaussian in `sampleHeight`'s cursor term, or the well
radius exceeding the sampled domain). Add a §5 gate clause: the cursor-heave region must be C1-smooth
(no luminance step discontinuity at the well boundary above a floor). Without this, §3.4 ships a
hard-edged box that morphs MORE on move — the opposite of the precept.

## R4 (LANDS, partial) — the contour DASHING is a DPR/`fwidth` failure the opaque fill MASKS but does NOT fix
Both live modes show the contours **dashing/dotted** at sub-pixel — the GOLDEN names this ("lines dash
at sub-pixel DPR", §0.1) and claims the opaque fill "kills the dashing root cause" because "the fill
carries the field even where the line aliases" (§2). That is sleight-of-hand: the fill HIDES the gap
between dashes; it does NOT make the line continuous. The dash is `band/aaW` with `aaW=fwidth(fN)`
blowing up where `|∇fN|` is large (steep ground / packed contours — exactly where §3.4's cursor-heave
PACKS them tighter). So the very interaction the GOLDEN celebrates (index lines pack around the well)
is where `fwidth` saturates and the line *fragments worst* — and a fragmenting index line reads as
noise, not the map grammar §1 promises. The opaque fill makes the field non-gray, but the index
hierarchy (R's load-bearing "reads as a MAP" claim) degrades precisely under the headline interaction.

**HARDENING:** clamp/floor `aaW` against a DPR-aware minimum (or feed `fwidth` a screen-space-stable
derivative), so the index line stays continuous at high `|∇H|`. Add a §5 π clause: under a synthetic
cursor heave, the index-line continuity (max gap along an iso) stays below a floor — i.e. the heave
does NOT dash the line. The GOLDEN's claim #7 (cursor heave → spacing compression) must be paired with
"...without fragmenting the line."

## R5 (LANDS, cross-engine) — `tanh` in WebGL2/WebKit ES 3.00 is real but `select`↔`step` parity is asserted, not proven
§2/§4.1 lean on `tanh`/`select`(WGSL)↔`step`(GLSL) being "identical semantics." Two cross-engine traps
the GOLDEN waves past:
(a) `tanh` IS in GLSL ES 3.00 (WebKit OK), but `tanh(H*1.6)` saturates to ±1 fast; with the §3.5
Aristotelian claim that "13 levels / index every 5" maps to the ramp, a saturating `tanh` COMPRESSES
the level spacing at the ramp ends — the opposite of §1's "fill the ramp ends" intent, and it
compresses DIFFERENTLY than a clamp would, so the JS-oracle↔WGSL↔GLSL ΔE (§5 L6) must include the
`tanh` saturation tails, not just mid-range. (b) `select(a,b,cond)` vs `step()`-driven `mix` are
*numerically* equal only if the boolean is exact; the spike already hit this — `spike.html:92-93`
writes `step(0.5, 1.0-abs(fract(lvl/5.0)-0.0))` then THROWS IT AWAY and replaces it with a ternary
`(mod(lvl,5.0)<0.5)?1.0:0.0` on the next line, because the `step` form was wrong. That means the
GOLDEN's own proven spike does NOT use the `select`↔`step` form §4.1 claims is the cross-engine
primitive — it uses `mod`+ternary. `mod()` of a float `lvl` near integer boundaries is a classic
WGSL↔GLSL divergence point (rounding of `floor` feeding `mod`).

**HARDENING:** §5 L6 must sample `isIndex` at the level-boundary lattice points (lvl = 4.999, 5.0,
5.001) across JS↔WGSL↔GLSL, not just generic `(p,t)` — `isIndex` is the one term whose cross-engine
divergence flips a line from minor to index. And §4.1 must name the ACTUAL form (`floor`+`mod`+select),
not the `step` form the spike discarded. State the index test as boundary-exact, born-RED on a one-ULP
`floor` drift.
