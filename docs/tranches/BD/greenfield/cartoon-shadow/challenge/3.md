# Cartoon-shadow GOLDEN — ADVERSARIAL CHALLENGE #3 (DESIGN-FIDELITY / GESTALT)

Lens: attack design fidelity to the edicts AS A USER. Default REFUTED. Live-tested the
`golden/spike.html` in Chrome (light + dark) via chrome-devtools-mcp and read the LANDED src/ state.

**Verdict: SURVIVES with REAL DAMAGE.** The mechanism (own warm ink, `::before` caster,
compositor-only transform, reused composables, born-RED G2) is sound and well-scoped. But the
GESTALT does NOT clear the bar — the rendered stamp is a muted taupe/near-black smudge, NOT a 1940s
technicolor cel, and in DARK mode it is nearly invisible. Plus the §0 verdict mis-states the landed
geometry, and the squash beat reads as "shrink + flee," not "punch down." These are hardenings, not
re-inventions — so it survives, but it is NOT shippable as written.

---

## R1 (TOP REFUTATION) — DARK-MODE CEL IS NEAR-INVISIBLE: the ink is DARKER than the card

Live-measured the spike in dark mode (chrome-devtools, screenshot captured):

- `--cartoon-ink` (dark) = `oklch(0.22 0.07 70)` → **L = 0.22**.
- `--card` (dark) = `rgb(33 30 28)` → **L ≈ 0.27**.

The cel ink is DARKER than the card it sits behind by only ~5% L, at 46% alpha. A dark ink on a
near-black ground has near-zero figure-ground separation. **The dark screenshot shows exactly this:**
the offset plate is a barely-perceptible brown sliver at the bottom-right — the SAME "faint smudge"
the GOLDEN's §0 indicts in light mode, reborn in dark. The bar is "a BOLD warm DARK ink, both modes,
opacity floored UP for presence on a dark ground" (§2 / §7-DARK). It is NOT met.

The §2 premise is internally contradictory: "a cel cast is INK — it stays a warm DARK ink in BOTH
modes (never flips to white)." But a 1940s cel cast is dark because the CEL (the paper) is LIGHT.
On a near-BLACK card, a dark cast is physically invisible — there is no light ground for the dark
ink to read against. The dark-mode register cannot be "dark ink" dogmatically; it must be a
**lighter-than-card warm ink** (the cast reads as a lit edge, an "emboss" register) to have any
figure-ground at all. The GOLDEN bans the white flip as "the iOS-7 sticker glow," but it conflates
a full white glow with a *warm light-toned offset* — the latter is the only thing that reads on a
near-black card. The G1 dark gate (`L ≤ 0.40`) actively ENFORCES the invisible-ink defect — it
passes the broken render.

**HARDENING (R1):** Re-spec the dark ink as a warm MID-tone offset that clears a contrast floor
*against the card*, not an absolute-dark dogma. Replace the `oklch(0.22 0.07 70)` pin + the
`L ≤ 0.40` gate with: `--cartoon-ink` (dark) = a warm ink whose L sits a measured ΔL ≥ 0.12 AWAY
from `--card`'s L (lighter on a dark card, darker on a light card — same as every other
adaptive-edge token). Change G1-dark from "ASSERT L ≤ 0.40" to "ASSERT |L_ink − L_card| ≥ 0.12 AND
chroma ≥ 0.05" — a figure-ground gate, which is what "presence on a dark ground" actually means.
This is still a warm chromatic cel (never a neutral glow), just one that is VISIBLE.

## R2 — LIGHT-MODE GESTALT FALLS SHORT: a muted taupe, not "1940s technicolor"

Live light-mode spike: ink-lead resolves to `oklab(0.216 0.039 0.058 / 0.32)` — a dark warm-brown
at 32% over cream `hsl(40 36% 96%)`. The rendered stamp (screenshot) reads as a **soft taupe drop
shadow**, not a bold saturated cel line. Two fidelity gaps:

1. **Chroma 0.07 is barely above the gray floor, not "technicolor."** The bar (BINDING LAW) is the
   "1940s technicolor register: bold layered-offset shadows." Technicolor cel ink is SATURATED
   (deep sepia/umber with real chroma, or an accent line). `max(c, 0.07)` clears the anti-gray gate
   (C ≥ 0.05) but lands a desaturated umber a hair off neutral. It passes G1 yet still reads muted —
   the gate is calibrated to the wrong bar (non-gray ≠ technicolor-bold).
2. **The contact rung carries blur** (`7px 7px 1px`, `11px 11px 2px`). A real cel ink is HARD-edged
   throughout. The 1-2px contact blur is precisely what softens the read toward "drop shadow." §3
   calls it "one soft contact rung," but a soft rung dilutes the cel identity the whole register
   exists to assert.

**HARDENING (R2):** (a) Raise the chroma floor to `max(c, 0.11)` AND drop the lead L toward
0.14-0.16 so the ink reads as a deliberate deep-umber cel line, not a taupe smudge; verify live that
the CARTOON swatch reads visibly *saturated*, not just *non-gray*. (b) Make the G1 ink-chroma gate
`C ≥ 0.09` (technicolor-bold), not `≥ 0.05` (merely-non-gray). (c) Make the contact rung 0-blur too
(or delete it) — the cel is hard-edged; if a contact anchor is wanted, use a 4th hard 0-blur plane
at higher offset, not a blurred one. The whole point is "hard cel edge," and 3 of 9 planes currently
blur.

## R3 — THE PRESS BEAT READS AS "SHRINK + FLEE," not "SQUASH DOWN onto the cast"

Live press state (spike): host `scale: 0.96` AND host `translate: -3px -3px` (up-left) SIMULTANEOUS.
A cartoon squash is a compression DOWN ONTO the ground — the object flattens toward its cast. But
the body simultaneously SHRINKS (0.96 uniform) AND FLIES up-left (-3px -3px). Uniform 0.96 scale is
not a squash — squash & stretch (§L4 #2, the BINDING LAW) is NON-uniform (widen X as you compress Y).
A uniform shrink reads as the card receding, and the up-left translate reads as it fleeing the
cursor. The net read is "the card got smaller and ran away," not "the card punched down and the
shadow shot out." The §1 narrative ("the object squashes DOWN onto it... PUNCHES... the plate
spreads 1.18×") is not what the geometry produces.

Also: on press the GOLDEN keeps the body lifting UP-LEFT while the cast travels DOWN-RIGHT — so the
gap OPENS on press. But "squash onto the cast" means the gap should CLOSE on press (object meets
ground) and OPEN on the rebound/overshoot. The direction of the gap animation is inverted relative
to the stated metaphor.

**HARDENING (R3):** (a) Make the squash NON-uniform: `scale: 1.04 0.94` (widen X, compress Y) so it
reads as real squash & stretch, not a shrink. (b) Resolve the gap-direction: on press the body
should DROP toward the cast (gap narrows → anticipation), then on release OVERSHOOT up (gap punches
open → the lift), with the caster's 1.15× lag closing it late. Re-storyboard §1/§4b so the
press/release gap choreography matches the "squashes DOWN onto it, then punches off" narrative the
GOLDEN itself writes. (c) Add a G3 arm: ASSERT the press-state body scale is NON-uniform
(scaleX ≠ scaleY) — a uniform scale = FAIL (it's a shrink, not a squash).

## R4 — §0 LIVE-VERDICT TABLE MIS-STATES THE LANDED GEOMETRY (overfit to a stale readback)

The §0 table claims the landed `--shadow-cartoon-md` cast is "down-RIGHT ... 1-2px blur ...
`color(srgb 0.11 0.098 0.09 / 0.12)`" and §3 frames the fix as "cast DOWN-RIGHT (coherent with the
upper-left key)." Live `getComputedStyle` on `/foundations/shadows` (real demo, NOT the spike):

```
--shadow-cartoon-md: -4px 3px 1px color-mix(..., light-dark(hsl(24 10% 10%), hsl(30 14% 90%)) 12%, ...),
                      0   4px 1px color-mix(..., ... 12%, ...),
                     -4px 4px 2px color-mix(..., ... 8%, ...)
--shadow-color: light-dark(hsl(24 10% 10%), hsl(30 14% 90%))
```

The landed cast is **down-LEFT** (`-4px 3px`), three planes, mixed 1/1/2px blur. So:
- The §0 "down-RIGHT" description is **factually wrong** — the landed is down-LEFT. The GOLDEN's
  headline framing ("change the cast to down-RIGHT to match the key") is built on a misread of where
  the cast currently is.
- The landed `--shadow-color` is now `light-dark(...)`, which means in DARK mode the EXISTING cartoon
  shadow flips `--foreground` to near-CREAM `hsl(30 14% 90%)` → a near-WHITE cast. THAT is the actual
  live dark defect (a white sticker-glow), and the §0 table never names it — it audits a stale
  pre-`light-dark()` srgb state. The single most important live defect (the white-flip in dark via
  `light-dark()` on `--shadow-color`) is MISSING from the verdict the whole GOLDEN is built to fix.

**HARDENING (R4):** Re-measure §0 against the CURRENT `light-dark()`-wrapped `--shadow-color` and
correct the geometry to down-LEFT. Add the real headline defect: "in dark, the landed cast rides
`--foreground` flipped to near-cream → a near-WHITE offset (the iOS-7 sticker glow the GOLDEN itself
warns against) — present TODAY in src/, caused by `light-dark()` on `--shadow-color`." Decide and
state the key-light direction ONCE (the house specular is upper-left → cast down-right is correct
intent) and note this is a CHANGE from the landed down-left, not a description of it.

## R5 — `light-dark()` INSET-SHADOW TRAP is live on `--shadow-color`, unaddressed by the GOLDEN

The recorded house lesson: "light-dark() inset shadow fragments compute the WHOLE box-shadow to
none." The landed `--shadow-color` is `light-dark(...)` and feeds `--shadow-cartoon-*` AND
`--shadow-modal` AND `--glass-material-rim`. The GOLDEN's §3 re-points `--shadow-modal`'s cartoon
plane onto `--cartoon-ink-lead` and the aliases onto `--cartoon-ink-lead`. If ANY consumer of those
families also carries an `inset` rung in the same `box-shadow` list, the `light-dark()` arm voids the
entire shadow. The GOLDEN decouples the CARTOON ink from `--shadow-color` (good — that's R-correct),
but it leaves `--shadow-modal` / `--glass-material-rim` half-migrated onto `--cartoon-ink-lead` while
those tokens may still mix with `light-dark()`-derived neighbors. The §9 doc edits and §10 wave plan
never sweep the `light-dark()`-on-`--shadow-color` hazard the decoupling creates a seam against.

**HARDENING (R5):** As part of `BD.W-CARTOON-CEL-INK`, explicitly audit every box-shadow that lists a
`--cartoon-ink-*`-derived plane ALONGSIDE any `inset` rung, and confirm none use `light-dark()` in
the same list. Prefer the plain per-mode `.dark` arm the GOLDEN already adopts for `--cartoon-ink`
(it does this right) — and EXTEND that discipline to `--shadow-modal`/`--shadow-color` so the family
is coherently plain-arm, retiring the `light-dark()` on `--shadow-color` (no-backwards-compat: it's
a trap, cut it).

## R6 — DARK INK DROPS THE `oklch(from)` ADAPTIVE CONSTRUCTION (the §0 best-of-three claim is broken)

§0/§1 sell the ink as "C's own warm-ink token BUILT VIA B's `oklch(from --foreground …)` construction
(adaptive hue-extract, no hardcoded literal)." But §2's DARK arm throws that away:
`--cartoon-ink: oklch(0.22 0.07 70)` — a **hardcoded literal** (the exact "no hardcoded ref" the
brief flags as overfit). So the "adaptive construction" only exists in light mode; dark mode is a
magic-number pin. That is two different mechanisms for one token — a DRY violation and a
half-adaptive design. If `--foreground` is retuned by a consumer, light-mode ink follows; dark-mode
ink silently does not.

**HARDENING (R6):** Build the dark ink from `oklch(from var(--foreground) …)` TOO, with an
L-target relative to `--card` (per R1), so ONE construction serves both modes and a consumer's
`--foreground` retune carries through in both. If a literal floor is needed for a non-`oklch(from)`
engine, that belongs in the `@supports` fallback (§3 already has one) — not in the primary dark arm.

## R7 — `prefers-reduced-transparency` and the "opaque ink survives" claim is half-true

§6 says PRT "does NOT touch the cast (opaque ink ... survives as a bonus legibility anchor)." But the
cast is NOT opaque — it's `color-mix(... 32%, transparent)` (and 18% on the contact rung). It is a
TRANSLUCENT ink. Under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, a 32%-alpha offset is exactly the kind
of low-contrast translucency the media query exists to firm up. Claiming it's "opaque ink" is wrong
on its face — the whole register is alpha-composited.

**HARDENING (R7):** Either (a) under PRT floor the three strengths UP toward fully-opaque
(re-mix at 70/55/40% or solid) so the "legibility anchor" claim is actually true, or (b) drop the
"opaque ink survives" framing and treat PRT like `prefers-contrast: more` (floor strengths up). Don't
assert opacity the tokens don't have.

## R8 — DEFT-INTEGRATION / WIRING GAPS (minor, but real bolt-on risk)

- §4c says `<Card surface="cartoon" pressable>` passes `pressVar: "--cartoon-press-t"`, but the live
  `Card.vue` hardcodes `pressVar: "--card-press-t"` (confirmed, line 230). There is no surface-keyed
  pressVar switch today. The GOLDEN needs `Card.vue` to choose the pressVar BY SURFACE — that's a real
  (small) SFC edit the §10 wave plan understates as "wire `useLiquidPress`" (it already IS wired, to
  the wrong var for cartoon). State it as: "parameterize Card's existing `pressVar` by `surface`
  (cartoon → `--cartoon-press-t`)."
- The landed `.cartoon-surface` hover uses `--spring-smooth` (cards.css:186); the GOLDEN swaps the
  transition to `--ease-cartoon-punch`. Fine — but the existing `:hover` is `:not(:disabled)` while
  the GOLDEN's is `:hover:not(:active):not(:disabled)`. The added `:not(:active)` is correct, but the
  wave plan must note it REPLACES the landed hover rule (no double-declaration), else two hover arms
  fight. KISS: one hover rule.

**HARDENING (R8):** §10 `BD.W-CARTOON-PUNCH` lists the exact `Card.vue` diff (surface-keyed pressVar)
and the exact `.cartoon-surface` hover-rule REPLACEMENT (not addition), so it's a true union, not an
overlay.

## What SURVIVES (fit, keep)

- The `::before`-caster / transform-only / compositor / no-backdrop-filter cross-engine story is
  genuinely the safest register in the tranche — the live spike composites identically, no WebKit
  transmissive path touched. **Strong.**
- ZERO new SFC, ZERO new composable, reuse of `useLiquidPress` + `usePointerVelocityField` — DRY,
  deft, real union onto landed carriers. **Strong.**
- The own `--cartoon-ink` decoupled from `--shadow-color` is the right architectural move
  (retuning the cast no longer disturbs every elevation shadow). **Keep.**
- G2 (motion tokens) is genuinely born-RED — `--motion-weight` / `--ease-cartoon-punch` measured
  EMPTY on the live `:root`. **Confirmed born-RED.**
- `oklab` mix over `srgb`, the `@property` length registration, the PRM `--motion-weight:0`
  one-assignment collapse — all correct. **Keep.**

## Born-RED audit

- **G2 — VALID born-RED.** `--motion-weight` + `--ease-cartoon-punch` measured EMPTY on live `:root`.
- **G1 — born-RED but MIS-CALIBRATED.** The C≥0.05 / α≥0.28 / blur==0 arms fail today, but the
  thresholds encode "merely non-gray," not "technicolor-bold" (R2), and the dark `L≤0.40` arm
  ENFORCES the invisible-ink defect (R1). Re-calibrate before it certifies a muted/invisible render.
- **G3 — born-RED (no caster today) but INCOMPLETE.** Add the non-uniform-squash assert (R3); the
  monotonic-settle / overshoot / no-per-frame-box-shadow arms are good.

## Bottom line

SURVIVES as a mechanism and a scope; FAILS the gestalt bar as currently tuned. The two
ship-blockers: **dark-mode invisibility (R1)** and **muted-not-technicolor light ink + blurred
contact rung (R2)**. R3 (shrink-not-squash) and R4 (mis-stated §0 geometry + the missed live
white-flip defect) are correctness fixes the wave needs before born-RED can certify anything. None
require re-invention — refine the ink L/C, fix the squash to be non-uniform, re-measure §0 against
the live `light-dark()` state, and re-calibrate the gates to the real bar.
