## TOP REFUTATION (the one that lands) — the Tier-S waist PASS is a CHERRY-PICKED frame; at the merge a user actually watches, the waist BALLOONS to 0.558 and FAILS M2

§11's headline — `Tier-S waist/body = 0.144, hasLocalMin=true, |S−G|=0.017` — is real *for ONE frame*:

**Two falsifications fall out of one sweep:**

1. **M2 FAILS at the frame the eye fixates on.** The merge midpoint (`t=0.5`, two masses fusing into
   one — the iOS-27 teardrop money-shot) renders Tier-S `waist/body = 0.558`: the neck is *fatter than

   visually-dominant moment of the default tier is a BULGE. The π dodges this by redefining "neck peak"
   as the thinnest *bridging-just-formed* frame (`t=0.2`) — a definition that conveniently excludes the
   frame where the metaball actually has to hold its waist.

2. **M4 "tiers agree ±0.10" is FALSE across the morph.** It holds ONLY at the cherry-picked frame.
   At `t=0.30` Tier-S=0.192 vs Tier-G=0.539 — `|S−G| = 0.347`, **3.5× over the gate**. The two
   "projections of one field" diverge by up to 0.35 over the sweep; they are demonstrably NOT "one
   field, two renderers" frame-for-frame. The clip-path-blur-threshold and the analytic `sminCircular`

**Why this is a fidelity refutation, not a metric nit:** Tier-S is what ships on the dock/carousel

metaball waist, never a slab." Measured across the actual morph, the default tier holds a waist only at
a hairline-bridge instant and balloons to a 0.558 bulge through the merge. The metaball gestalt of the
default tier is **unproven at the moment it matters**, and the spike hides that by reporting one frame.

### Hardening (top)

frame — `assert(maxOver(t∈[0.2,0.8], S.ratio) ≤ 0.45)` and `assert(maxOver(t, |S−G|) ≤ 0.10)`. Then
**re-tune the structural throat + filter slope/bias so the RENDERED Tier-S waist holds ≤0.45 through
the merge midpoint, not just at bridge-formation.** Concretely the `throat = R·0.22·close` + `sigma =
R·0.07` + `slope 18 / bias −6.5` triple re-fattens past 0.45 as `close→1`; the throat must shrink (or
the bias deepen) as the bodies converge so the pinch survives the blur at mid-merge. Until the SWEEP
(not a single frame) shows Tier-S ≤0.45 end-to-end, M2/M4 stay born-RED against the spike too.

## SECONDARY REFUTATIONS

### R2 — the §3 "COLORFUL FIELD reads THROUGH the transmissive weld" precept is NOT realized; the spike paints OPAQUE cream and reports a hardcoded literal as "never gray"
The binding law (design.md §3, the GOLDEN §5) is explicit: glass is **transmissive (~0.55–0.80 α) so
the colorful field reads THROUGH the weld** — that is the whole "PERFECTED glass, NEVER gray" thesis.
The measured spike does the opposite, twice:
- `drawBodies()` fills the "colourful field behind glass" as **`rgba(0,0,0,0)`** (line 86) — there is
  no field behind the glass at all; the only color is the panel's CSS background gradient, which the
  opaque blobs sit *on top of*, not *in front of transmissively*.
- `blurThreshold()` then **hardcodes** `out[i*4+0]=249; out[i*4+1]=238; out[i*4+2]=218` with
  `α = av·255` clamped solid (line 132). I sampled a body-center pixel live: **`[249,238,218,255]` —
  fully OPAQUE.** The π's "warm-cream rgb(249,238,218), never gray" PASS is reading back the literal it
  just wrote, not a measured transmissive composite. It is a tautology of the same family as the one
  the prior round killed — a hardcoded constant asserted as a measured property.

So the single most load-bearing material precept — *six-layer transmissive composite over a colorful
field* — has **zero realization** in the only artifact, and the gate that "proves it never goes gray"
is reading a paint constant. A user looking at this spike sees flat opaque cream lozenges on a CSS
gradient, not liquid glass with a field refracting through the neck.

**Hardening R2:** the spike must paint a real colorful field layer, composite the bodies at the §5 α
(0.55–0.80) so the field reads THROUGH, and the π must sample a pixel **over the field-behind-glass**
and assert (a) the field's hue is visible through the body (chroma > 0 from the field, not the cream),
AND (b) the composite still lands C≥0.010, H∈[45,85] (warm, never gray) — measured from the *composite*,
not a written literal. Same for the body in `.dark`. Without this, §5's "warm six-layer survives every
tier" is asserted, never shown.
