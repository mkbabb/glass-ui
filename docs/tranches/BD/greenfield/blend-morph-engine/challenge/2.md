## TOP REFUTATION (R1-bis) — the NEW spike kills the hardcoded tautology but installs a CHERRY-PICK tautology in its place

§11 retires `field-weld.html` (the `S = G·0.74 + 0.40·0.26` hardcode) and replaces it with
`field-weld-measured.html`, claiming each tier is "rendered to ITS OWN pixel buffer and the waist
read from those pixels (never a formula)." That claim is *literally* true — and it still does not
prove M4, because the headline number is produced by **cherry-picking the one frame where the tiers
happen to coincide.**

**Measured LIVE this session** (ran `window.__renderAt(t)` across a fine sweep on the spike's own
canvases, both tiers measured from their own pixels):

| t | Tier-S waist/body | Tier-G waist/body | \|S−G\| | both connected? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.18 | 0.130 | 0.000 | — | **S yes, G NO** (topology mismatch) |
| **0.20** | **0.144** | **0.161** | **0.017** | yes ← **the ONLY frame the π reports** |
| 0.22 | 0.155 | 0.340 | 0.185 | yes |
| 0.26 | 0.169 | 0.495 | 0.326 | yes |
| 0.30 | 0.192 | 0.539 | **0.347** | yes ← **WORST disagreement** |
| 0.38 | 0.227 | 0.437 | 0.210 | yes |
| 0.42 | 0.293 | 0.248 | 0.046 | yes |
| 0.50 | 0.558 | 0.510 | 0.048 | yes |

`runPi()` (line 237) sweeps `i=20..50`, picks `peakT` = the frame with the **minimum Tier-S ratio**,
then reports `|peak.S.ratio − peak.G.ratio|` at THAT frame. Because S is at its tightest there, and G
happens to also be near its tight end at t=0.20, the printed `|S−G| = 0.017` PASSES. But the gate's
own claim — "Tier C/S geometrically *approximate the same smin* Tier G evaluates" — is a statement
about the WHOLE morph, and across the morph **the two fields disagree by up to 0.347 (20× the
reported figure).** The π samples one lucky frame and generalizes. This is the prior R1 defect
re-dressed: the agreement is manufactured by the measurement procedure, not exhibited by the field.

**Two corroborating tells from the same live data:**

1. **Tier-G's waist is non-monotonic garbage:** 0.000→0.161→0.340→0.495→**0.539**→0.437→0.248 as the
   bodies *approach*. A real metaball waist should NARROW monotonically toward overlap; G's *widens*
   then narrows because its per-frame `k = 1.25·gap·close` is mis-scaled (k grows as the gap shrinks
   only via `close`, so mid-approach k over-fattens the blend and the measured "waist" balloons to
   0.539 — wider than a body). The Tier-G reference is itself unphysical, so "agreement with it" is
   meaningless even where it occurs.

2. **The tiers disagree on TOPOLOGY:** at t=0.18 Tier-S is connected (waist 0.130) while Tier-G is
   still disconnected (0). The §11 "wells→pinches connected-component sweep" — the load-bearing
   topology-free-field claim that kills the crossfade — fires at DIFFERENT frames on the two tiers.
   They are not "one field, three projections"; they are three different fields with three different
   bridge-onset times.

**Root cause (read from source):** Tier-S and Tier-G do NOT render the same geometry.
`drawBodies(ctx, g, W, H, withNeck=true)` paints two circles **PLUS an explicit hand-drawn
`paintHourglass()` polygon** (a literal concave-waisted pentagon bridge, lines 108-119) for Tier-C
and Tier-S. `paintTierG()` (lines 157-168) draws **only two `sdCircle` masses fused by
`sminCircular`** — NO bridge polygon. So Tier-S's waist is authored by a hand-drawn hourglass and
then blurred; Tier-G's waist is the actual metaball. They are structurally different fields. The
prior round's R1 said "Tier-S is derived from a formula"; this round it's "Tier-S is derived from a
*different hand-drawn shape* than the field it claims to approximate." The metaball is still not the
thing producing the Tier-S waist — a polygon is. **M4's "two renderers of ONE smin field" remains
unproven; §11's "VERIFIED LIVE / the tautology, KILLED" banner is again not honest.**

### Hardening R1-bis
Two concrete, landable fixes:
- **(a) Render BOTH tiers from the SAME field.** Delete `paintHourglass`. Tier-S must be the two
  cream circles blurred+thresholded with NO hand-drawn bridge — if the blur-threshold alone cannot
  hold a ≤0.45 concave waist at the neck peak, *that is the finding* (and it sharpens R4 below: the
  SVG-goo default may genuinely need help the polygon was secretly supplying). Tier-G must use the
  identical two-circle geometry. Only then is "one field, two projections" a measured claim.
- **(b) Replace the M4 gate with a WHOLE-MORPH agreement bound, not a single-frame peak.** Assert
  `max_t |S(t)−G(t)| ≤ 0.10` over every frame where both tiers are connected — not `|S−G|` at the
  cherry-picked min-S frame. With the current spike that bound is **0.347 → RED**, which is the
  honest born-RED state. Fix Tier-G's per-frame `k` so its waist is monotonic before claiming
  agreement. Until both land, M4 must read SKIPPED/RED, not PASS.

to bridge?* The §2 claim "the clip-path hourglass IS the waist, purely on the compositor" may be
true — but if so, the waist is the **clip-path geometry**, not the metaball, and Tier-S is Tier-C
with a cosmetic blur, not a "true metaball merge." Either reading needs the polygon-free measurement
to settle it. Right now the precept-compliance evidence is the polygon doing the work the metaball
was supposed to do.

### Hardening R4
Run the polygon-free Tier-S measurement (R1-bis-a). If the blur-threshold alone holds the waist:
great, delete the polygon and the precept is met by the real mechanism. If it does NOT: say so in
§2/§3 — the honest statement becomes "the cross-engine metaball waist is carried by the **clip-path
hourglass GEOMETRY** (Tier-C structural concavity); the SVG blur only SOFTENS it" — which is actually
a *stronger* cross-engine story (no filter needed for the waist) but retires the "Tier-S is the
