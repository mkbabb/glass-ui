# CHALLENGE #3 — DESIGN-FIDELITY / GESTALT lens (the SELECT GOLDEN)

**Verdict: DOES NOT SURVIVE CLEAN.** The GOLDEN is structurally honest and DRY, but
its single central claim — *"the menu reads WARM GLASS, never gray, because the floor
makes it structurally impossible the way the six-layer recipe makes a flat plate
impossible"* — is **FALSE on painted pixels**. I drove the spike in Chrome, sampled
the real composited pixels (the cardinal rule), and isolated the floor from the field.
The un-gray guarantee collapses to "field-only," the field gives the plate ZERO
internal structure, and the warm-accent "hot lozenge" is ~3× too weak on chroma and
fails luminance outright (it is DARKER than the plate, not a hot pill). The motion
"anticipation" the precept sells is a sub-perceptual 0.3% dip. These are gestalt
failures against the binding edicts, not nitpicks.

Tested: `golden/spike.html` live in Chrome (chrome-devtools-mcp), real open, both
modes, painted-pixel `getImageData` over the menu region, field-on AND field-killed
(the true born-RED flat condition), reveal frame-series. Disk claims grepped.

## TOP REFUTATION (the one that lands hardest)

**§2a's warm admit-FLOOR does NOT un-gray the plate. Painted-pixel proof:**

| condition | menu rows composited C | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| field ON (`.paper-field` + `::before` painting) | **C = 0.0206** (razor-thin over 0.02) | passes — but see below |
| field KILLED (the GOLDEN's own §0 born-RED flat page, rgb 251,250,248) | **C = 0.0029** | **GRAY — the floor alone is dead** |
| page bg behind (field on) | C = 0.0239 | **the page is MORE chromatic than the menu** |

The §0 born-RED page pixel is `rgb(251,250,248) · C 0.0029`. With the field removed,
the menu plate over that flat page measures **C = 0.0029 — byte-identical to the page,
exactly the born-RED gray.** The `--overlay-tint-floor` (10% in the spike, 6%/8% in
the GOLDEN) mixed into `--glass-bg-floating` (`oklch(0.95 0.008 58 / 0.80)`) at 0.80α
**cannot reach 0.02** — the 80% near-neutral fill + 20% flat bleed-through dominate the
color-mix. The §2 prose ("lifts the menu plate to warm-cream **regardless of
backdrop**", "the floor (2a) **holds** when the field is faint", token comment `/*
lifts composited C ≥ 0.02 over flat */`) is contradicted by its own spike's painted
pixels. **The warm read is 100% field-dependent.** The two-layer "FLOOR × FIELD"
guarantee is really "FIELD only" — and `--field-h` / `.paper-field` **grep ZERO in
`src/`** (unlanded sibling GOLDEN), so the menu is GRAY today and the un-gray is gated
on three unproven conditionals (the field lands AND the route mounts it AND the portal
`data-field-palette` re-emit actually feeds the backdrop). G2/G3/G4 are PASS-on-paper,
RED-on-pixels.

This is a §3-precept violation: the edict is "a COLORFUL FIELD behind glass + a DEFINED
EDGE, the glass BENDS it." The spike's field does not transmit THROUGH the plate — it
leaks as a halo OUTSIDE the box (`inset: -20%`), and the plate's internal **L-variance
= 0.00001 (dead flat).** G2's own sub-clause "spatial L-variance > floor (the field
gives real structure)" **FAILS**: there is no bent structure, just a uniform cream wash
with an orange glow spilling around the edges. The glass isn't transmissive; it's a
flat cream card next to a glow.

## THE OTHER REFUTATIONS

### R2 — the warm-accent "HOT LOZENGE" is not hot, and is DARKER not lighter (§5, G9)
Painted pixels, selected "Terracotta" row vs the plate:
- getComputedStyle composite: **ΔC = +0.020, ΔL = −0.028** (the spike's own probe).
- screenshot getImageData: selected strip C 0.0264 vs body 0.0206 → **ΔC = +0.0058, ΔL = −0.0066.**

G9 demands **ΔC ≥ 0.02 AND ΔL ≥ 0.05.** The painted ΔC is ~3× too weak; ΔL is
**NEGATIVE** both ways — the "hot lozenge" is a hair DARKER than the plate, not the
brighter splash the precept ("the ONE warm-accent hot lozenge", iOS hot-pill) demands.
A user sees a barely-there peach smudge, not a hot pill. The §5 leg mixes
`--glass-accent 14-16%` into the *already-tinted* `--menu-row-bg`, which floors the
contrast. The "cartoon-punch" warmth event is invisible — the exact "no warm-accent
event" born-RED the GOLDEN claims to fix (line 24: ΔL 0.016) is barely moved (still
ΔL ≈ 0.007–-0.028).

### R3 — the "anticipation / welling" recoil is sub-perceptual 0.3% (§3, G5, cartoon precept)
The punch curve's anticipation values are `-0.014 6%, -0.032 12%`. Applied to the
scale leg interpolating 0.913→1.0, the max recoil is `0.913 + 0.087×(−0.032) = 0.910`
— a **0.3% scale dip below closed.** The overshoot peaks at 1.009 (**0.9%**). Frame
series confirmed: start 0.913, max 1.009, min ≈ 0.9126 (no measurable pre-dip below
the closed start). The binding edict is **"anticipation, EXAGGERATION, squash & stretch
with REAL WEIGHT/INERTIA" — the 1940s technicolor register.** A 0.3% recoil + 0.9%
overshoot is a polite damped spring, not a cartoon wind-up-and-punch. G5 ("dips below
closed") would technically pass on the 0.3% hairline while the user feels NOTHING — a
gate that green-lights an invisible move is a metric, not the gestalt. The precept says
"the gestalt is the bar, not the metric."

### R4 — the POUR's per-row index has NO real mechanism in reka SelectItem (§4, G8)
§4 drives `--menu-row-index` "set by the viewport (or a nth-child `--i` fallback)."
**Neither exists.** `SelectItem.vue` (read on disk) renders via reka with `cn(menuItem
Variants(...))` — it sets NO `--i` and NO `--menu-row-index`; `menuItemVariants` (read)
has no index axis. The spike fakes it with hand-authored `style="--i:0..5"` inline on
static divs. In the real shipped Select the rows are reka-portalled options with no
ordinal custom prop, so the windowed read `(--menu-pour-t − --menu-row-index×0.06)`
collapses to index 0 for ALL rows → **every row pours simultaneously = the slab the
born-RED already is.** The "zero-JS nth-child `--i` floor" is asserted but never
shown working (CSS can't set a `<number>` custom prop from `:nth-child` without a
per-index rule list — i.e. N hardcoded rules, which is the overfit the GOLDEN forbids).
G8 ("rows stagger top-to-bottom") cannot go green without new per-row index plumbing
the integration map (§6) does not budget. This is a hand-wave at the single boldest
move.

### R5 — KISS violation: a 4th competing warm-source stack with no measured budget
The plate warmth now stacks: (a) `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` floor, (b) the
`.glass-field-portal::before` 3-stop gradient, (c) the page `.paper-field` behind the
body, (d) the `--menu-row-bg` row tint, (e) the `--glass-accent` chip — FIVE warm
admits color-mixing over each other, none measured in composite. The painted result is
the tell: the menu (C 0.0206) is LESS chromatic than the page (C 0.0239) despite five
warm layers — they are not additive through the glass, they wash to a uniform cream.
The §3 "defined edge + bent field" is lost in the wash. This is the opposite of KISS:
more sources, less legible structure. One *correct* transmissive recipe (raise
`--glass-bg-floating` chroma at the floating tier, or drop the plate α so the field
actually transmits) would beat five stacked near-neutral mixes.

### R6 — born-RED honesty caveat: G2/G3/G4 are NOT born-RED on the floor; they're born-RED on an UNLANDED dependency
The GOLDEN's born-RED table (§0) is honest about HEAD. But §9's GREEN-when column for
G2/G3/G4 ("floor + field land") conflates two things: the floor (lands in this GOLDEN's
waves) and the field (an unbuilt sibling). My flat-condition test proves the floor's
contribution to G2 is **C 0.0029 → 0.0029 (zero lift)**. So W-OVERLAY-WARM-FLOOR
(self-contained, "depends-on: —") will land GREEN on its own gate ONLY if that gate
secretly relies on the field being present. If proof:select-forms mounts the field, the
floor wave passes on the field's back (cross-contaminated gate). If it doesn't, the
floor wave's G2 stays RED forever. The wave dependency graph (§10) is mis-drawn:
W-OVERLAY-WARM-FLOOR's G2/G4 **depend on W-SELECT-PORTAL-FIELD**, not "—".

### R7 — `@property --enter-scale { inherits:false }` + class-toggle thrash desyncs the reveal
During repeated open/close in one session the closed `scale` read **"1" instead of
0.913** (the squish silently vanished — verified, then confirmed it self-heals only on
full reload). A real Select opens/closes many times per session without reload. If the
registered `--enter-scale` (or `--menu-pour-t`) latches stale under rapid toggles, the
welling squish drops to a flat pop intermittently — a robustness gap the gate (one
fresh-paint open) would never catch. Worth a multi-open frame-series in
proof:select-forms, not a single open.

## CONCRETE HARDENING (what answers each refutation)

1. **(TOP) Make the floor actually reach 0.02 over flat, OR stop claiming it does.**
   Either: raise the FLOATING-TIER plate chroma at source — lift `--glass-bg-floating`'s
   own oklch chroma (0.008 → ~0.018) so the floating band is born warm-cream before any
   mix (the band that floats over unknown surfaces self-warms by construction), and DROP
   the plate α from 0.80 → ~0.72 so the field behind actually TRANSMITS through (giving
   real L-variance). Re-measure: menu-over-flat must hit C ≥ 0.02 with the FIELD KILLED.
   If it can't, rewrite §2/§8.4/G2 to state honestly "warm requires the field; the floor
   is the faint-field amplifier, not a flat-page guarantee," and make W-OVERLAY-WARM-FLOOR's
   gate **depend on W-SELECT-PORTAL-FIELD** (fix the §10 graph).

2. **Give the field real transmission, not a halo.** The `::before` at `inset:-20%`
   glows AROUND the box. Pull it INSIDE (`inset:0`, clipped) and rely on the plate α
   drop so the blur bends it through. Gate the L-variance arm of G2 on the menu INTERIOR,
   not the halo — a uniform plate (var 0.00001) must FAIL.

3. **Make the accent a real hot lozenge.** Mix `--glass-accent` into a NEUTRAL base, not
   the already-tinted `--menu-row-bg`, and ADD luminance (lighten in light mode) so
   ΔL ≥ +0.05 AND ΔC ≥ 0.02 on painted pixels. Re-measure the selected strip over the
   real plate; the current −0.007 ΔL must flip positive.

4. **Land the punch with real weight.** The `-0.032` anticipation against a 0.913→1.0
   span is 0.3%. Either deepen the closed squish (enter-scale → ~0.80 so the recoil +
   bloom have travel to be FELT) or amplify the curve's negative lobe (e.g. `-0.10`)
   AND the overshoot (peak ~1.06). G5/G6 must assert a PERCEPTIBLE dip (≥2% below closed)
   and overshoot (≥4% above 1.0), not a hairline.

5. **Give the POUR a real per-row index.** Budget the plumbing the boldest move needs:
   a tiny `v-for`-index `:style="{'--menu-row-index': i}"` on SelectItem (and the 12
   sibling item SFCs) OR a documented IntersectionObserver/viewport projection — and
   PROVE it staggers in proof:select-forms. The "nth-child `--i` zero-JS floor" must be
   shown emitting distinct indices or dropped as vapor. Without it §4 is a slab.

6. **Collapse the 5-source warm wash to ONE transmissive recipe** (folds into #1/#2):
   fewer admits, more structure. Measure the composite, not each source.

7. **Add a multi-open reveal frame-series** to proof:select-forms (open/close ×5, no
   reload) to catch the `@property` stale-latch.
