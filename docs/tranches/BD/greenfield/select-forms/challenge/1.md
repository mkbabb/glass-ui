Lens: idiom · deft integration (union vs bolt-on) · KISS/DRY · gate-born-RED · overfit ·
cross-engine. Default REFUTED. Live-tested the spike (`golden/spike.html`) in Chrome,
both modes, instrumented composited-pixel + frame-series + the punch math. Grepped every

VERDICT: **SURVIVES — but two refutations LAND and force concrete hardenings before build.**

## R1 (TOP — LANDS) — the "FLOOR is the guarantee that holds over a flat page" is FALSE; measured floor-only composite is C 0.009–0.013, BELOW the 0.02 warm-floor

§2/§2a's central architecture claim: *"Floor = the guarantee; field = the song"* — the
warm admit-FLOOR (2a) "lifts the menu plate to warm-cream regardless of backdrop" and
"holds when the field is faint." The spike DISPROVES this with its own pixels.

Measured (spike, menu OPEN, `--overlay-tint-floor: 10%` — note: HIGHER than the GOLDEN's
spec'd 6%/8%), the menu FILL composited over the flat page (`getComputedStyle` fill ×
alpha over `body` bg, the only honest read of the floor's standalone contribution):

| mode | composited fill-over-flat-page | warm-floor target (G2) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| light | **C 0.0091** · H 68 | C ≥ 0.02 | **FAILS — < half the floor** |
| dark  | **C 0.0126** · H 54 | C ≥ 0.02 | **FAILS — below the floor** |

The plate ONLY reads warm (and it does — both screenshots confirm genuinely warm peach /
warm-brown, never gray) because the `.paper-field` + `.menu::before` field paints BEHIND
the translucent fill. That is the FIELD (2b) carrying the warmth, not the FLOOR (2a). And
the real src floor (6%/8%) would composite EVEN LOWER than the spike's 10%.

Why it matters: the GOLDEN sells the floor as flat-page insurance — *"the floor is the
flat-page insurance" (§2c), "holds even over a flat page" (§2a header).* It does not. If
`BD.W-PAGE-FIELD` is absent/faint behind the body plate, or `prefers-reduced-transparency`
escapes to the opaque tier (§7 claims "the floor stays so the opaque escape reads warm"),
the menu falls toward C 0.009 = **reads gray** — exactly the §3 disease the GOLDEN claims
to have made "structurally impossible." The guarantee/song framing is INVERTED: the field
is the guarantee, the floor is a whisper.

HARDENING: either (a) raise `--overlay-tint-floor` until the floor-ALONE composite clears
C ≥ 0.02 over a flat page in BOTH modes (the spike must re-measure the FILL-ONLY composite,
field disabled, and pass — make that an explicit born-RED bite in G12), OR (b) re-cast §2's
honest claim: the FIELD is the un-gray guarantee, the floor is the secondary lift, and add
a hard gate that the `prefers-reduced-transparency` opaque-tier menu (no field transmission
through an opaque fill) still measures C ≥ 0.02 — today it cannot, because the opaque fill
buries the field. The opaque-escape warmth needs its OWN solid warm fill token, not "the
floor + field stay."

## R2 (LANDS) — the cartoon-punch "welling bloom" is mathematically SUB-PERCEPTUAL: 1.4% overshoot, 0.28% recoil

§3b/§8.1 promise *"a hair of recoil (anticipation), a bloom past settle"* — the
LIQUID-WEIGHT-UNIVERSAL + CARTOON-PUNCH binding law (real squash & stretch with WEIGHT).
The punch `linear()` peaks at 1.16 and dips to −0.032 in NORMALIZED curve space — but it
drives `scale` interpolating from enter-scale 0.913 → 1.0, a range of only **0.0865**. So:

- overshoot peak scale = 0.913 + 1.16·0.0865 = **1.0138 → a 1.4% pop**
- anticipation recoil = 0.913 + (−0.032)·0.0865 = 0.9107 → **0.28% below the closed plate**

Live frame-series (Chrome, rAF) confirmed: minScale 0.9129 vs startScale 0.9135 — a 0.0006
dip; maxScale never observably exceeded 1.0 in sampled frames. A 1.4% overshoot on a 280px
menu = ~4px of bloom over ~120ms; a 0.28% recoil = ~0.8px. This is NOT the 1940s-technicolor
welling/squash-stretch with WEIGHT the binding law demands — it is a near-damped pop dressed
in a punch curve. The curve is fit; the AMPLITUDE is starved because a punch fraction of an
8.6% range cannot read.

Worse for the gate: **G5 ("scale dips below closed before launch") asserts the punch shows a
pre-dip a `--motion-weight:0` control lacks** — but a 0.28% / 0.0006-absolute dip is inside
frame-quantization + sub-pixel-rounding noise. The gate cannot reliably distinguish the
"anticipation" from measurement jitter; it risks passing on noise (false-green) or flapping.

HARDENING: deepen the enter-squish so the punch has range to read — e.g. enter-scale
`calc(1 - 0.30·--motion-weight)` (≈0.81 @ 0.618) gives overshoot ≈1.03 (3%) and recoil
≈0.79 (a visible ~2% pre-dip), or express the overshoot as an ABSOLUTE target (peak scale
1.04–1.06) decoupled from the enter range. Then re-state G5/G6 thresholds in ABSOLUTE scale
terms (pre-dip ≥ 1% below closed; overshoot ≥ 2% above 1.0) so the gate measures a
perceptible event, not noise. Verify the deepened squish in the spike frame-series.

## R3 (PARTIAL — hardening) — the POUR's `--menu-row-index` has NO defined source in reka-rendered rows; the spike hand-hardcodes `--i`

§4 drives the cascade off `--menu-row-index` *"set by the viewport (or a nth-child --i
fallback for the zero-JS floor)."* The spike hardcodes `style="--i:0..5"` inline on every
row — there is NO `nth-child` fallback present, and reka renders `SelectItem` rows through a
roving virtual list where the GOLDEN never specifies WHO writes `--menu-row-index`. The 13
SFCs that share `.glass-menu-row` (§5) get the accent leg for free, but the POUR leg needs a
per-row index none of them emit. "Set by the viewport" is hand-waved — there is no viewport
mechanism named, and an `nth-child(n) { --menu-row-index: ... }` block is not authored
anywhere (spike or GOLDEN §4 code). Also: spike stagger step is `0.10`, GOLDEN §4 says
`0.06` — an un-reconciled divergence (the spike's own cascade is slower/different than spec).

HARDENING: author the literal `nth-child` index ladder in `menu.css` as the zero-JS floor
(`.glass-menu-row:nth-child(1){--menu-row-index:0} …` up to a reasonable N, then clamp), so
the pour works with ZERO consumer wiring — that is the actual DRY/KISS close, not "the
viewport sets it." Reconcile the stagger step (0.06 vs 0.10) and prove the total cascade ≤
the reveal clock at the real row counts. State explicitly that beyond N rows the cascade
clamps to simultaneous (graceful, not broken).

## R4 (PARTIAL — gate robustness) — the born-RED is GENUINE, but G3 (the A/B delta) is the only arm that proves the field; protect it

The born-RED is honest and reproduced: `--overlay-tint-floor`, `--ease-cartoon-punch`,
`--motion-weight`, `--menu-pour-t`, `.glass-field-portal` ALL grep ZERO in `src/` (verified

passes chroma but FAILS the variance + A/B arms"). That makes **G3 (the A/B delta — same
menu over real field vs flat plate, ΔC ≥ 0.015) the load-bearing anti-fraud arm.** It must
NOT be softened, and the "spatial L-variance > floor" sub-clause of G2 must have a concrete
numeric floor (currently "> floor" is undefined).

HARDENING: pin G3's ΔC ≥ 0.015 as NON-NEGOTIABLE and give G2's L-variance an explicit
threshold (e.g. stdev of menu-region L ≥ 0.01 across the painted region). Add the
floor-only-fails bite from R1 to G12.
