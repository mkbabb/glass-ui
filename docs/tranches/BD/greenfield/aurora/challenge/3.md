## TOP REFUTATION (gestalt) — the metal proof artefact reads as PASTEL TERRAZZO, not metal

`golden/spike-metal-fixed.png` is the GOLDEN's own "de-risked, gate-GREEN" proof that
metal "genuinely FOLDS, reads as fine warm folded metal." **It does not.** Rendered, it is
a pale pink-and-cream high-frequency SPECKLE — a grainy terrazzo / confetti wash. There is:
- NO coherent specular streak running along a tangent (the "brushed sheet" read);
- NO catch-light RIDGE with a legible crest→valley sheen gradient;
- NO polished body — the technicolor dynamic range the spec promises ("deep warm-shadow
  valleys, near-white crests, reads POLISHED not brushed-gray") is invisible. The image is
  uniformly mid-pale.

This is the **metric-vs-gestalt trap** my lens exists to catch. The gate clause 2 measures
`localContrast` (high-freq OKLab-L texture) ≥ 0.020 AND ≥ 1.5× smooth. The spike clears it
(0.0267 vs 0.0011, "25×"). But **localContrast rewards speckle.** A field of random
salt-and-pepper noise maxes that metric while looking nothing like metal. The 25× number
proves the BRDF adds HIGH-FREQUENCY TEXTURE — it does NOT prove the texture is *coherent,
streaked, ridged metal*. The user asked for "pure liquid-metal" + "gradient-metallic-
sparkle" (the iOS-27 register: a flowing molten sheet). The artefact delivers grain, not
sheen. As a user looking at this behind glass, I see noise, not chrome.

Root cause in the math: `METAL_HEIGHT_SCALE=2.3` on a Sobel of the nuclei field produces a
NORMAL that flips sign every pixel-pair (the field's iso-bands are dense + noisy), so `N·H`
crest and the `valley` term both alias into pixel-grain rather than resolving folds at the
SCALE a metal sheet folds at (centimetre ridges, not pixel speckle). The streak's
`METAL_SHININESS_ANISO=28` is sharp enough that the highlight is sub-pixel-thin — it never
coalesces into a visible rake. The coherence gate (`A` floor 0.12) doesn't help because the
nuclei field is high-coherence almost everywhere, so the gate is open everywhere.

### Hardening
1. **Add a SECOND gate clause that measures COHERENCE, not just contrast.** Compute the
   directional autocorrelation / orientation-coherence of the high-freq L texture (the
   metal's streaks must be CO-ALIGNED over a neighbourhood, not isotropic speckle). A
   salt-and-pepper field FAILS this even though its localContrast is high. This makes the
   gate measure "reads as brushed metal," not "has texture." Born-RED holds (smooth fails;
   isotropic-speckle metal ALSO fails — which is the current artefact's actual failure).
2. **Low-pass the height field before the normal.** Drive `N` from a *smoothed* gradient
   (a 2–3px blur of `grad`, or a lower-octave of the nuclei field) so folds resolve at a
   sheet scale, not pixel scale. Cut `METAL_HEIGHT_SCALE` and re-tune so the crest→valley
   swing is VISIBLE as a gradient, not grain.
3. **Widen the streak** (`METAL_SHININESS_ANISO` down) so the rake is a visible band, and
   add a soft inner falloff so the catch-light reads as a *moving highlight* not a hard
   line — the liquid-weight register.
4. **Re-shoot the proof and EYE-VERIFY both modes in the live demo, not just the metric.**
   The spike PNG is the cardinal-lesson inflation (a captured artefact that the metric
   blesses but the eye refutes). The acceptance bar MUST include a human/visual sign-off
   that the metal reads as metal, per the live-verify-capture memory rule.

## REFUTATION 2 (taxonomy coherence / KISS) — "orthogonal medium × finish" is self-contradicted by the slot assignment

The spec's organizing claim (§1, §1): *four ORTHOGONAL axes — `medium` (substance) ×
`finish` (operator) × `source` × `vividness`* — where `finish:"metal"` is "an operator
RELIGHTING the rendered field, NOT a new shader pass," composable over any medium. But the
wave amendment (§10) and the union map (§9) place metal at **`uMedium == 8/9`**.

`uMedium` IS the medium axis. Both dispatch ladders (`aurora.frag.ts:400-406`,
`aurora-mediums.wgsl.ts:301-311`) are a SINGLE `if/else-if` chain on `uMedium` that
**replaces `col`**. A value at `uMedium==8` is mutually exclusive with `uMedium==0`
(smooth). So `finish:"metal"` at slot 8 **cannot compose with `medium:"smooth"`** — picking
metal means you have LEFT the medium ladder. The axes are NOT orthogonal; metal is a
ninth/tenth MEDIUM wearing a "finish" costume.

This matters three ways:
- **It breaks the user-facing mental model.** The configurator will present `medium` and
  `finish` as independent dropdowns, but `finish:metal` will silently override whatever
  `medium` is set — a `:pressed`-style silent no-op (the glass-ui-binding-verification
  lesson, in shader form). A user picks `medium:vangogh, finish:metal` and gets... metal,
  vangogh discarded. That's a configurator lie.
- **It contradicts "metal RELIGHTS the rendered field."** If metal were truly a finish
  operator it would run AFTER `applyMedium(col)` over whatever the medium produced — a
  van-Gogh-painted field RELIT as metal. At `uMedium==8` it instead REPLACES the medium
  body; there is no van-Gogh field underneath to relight. The §2b code confirms this: it
  rebuilds `base` from `chroma`, ignoring any prior medium pass.
- **kuwahara was ALSO re-classed a "finish" in §1** but is `uMedium==7` — same category
  error, just inherited.

### Hardening — pick ONE, honestly:
- **(A) Metal is a MEDIUM, name it so.** Drop the "orthogonal finish operator" framing.
  Metal/metal-gradient are `medium:"metal"` / `medium:"metal-gradient"` (uMedium 8/9), peers
  of smooth/oil/vangogh. The configurator shows them in the single medium dropdown. This is
  the HONEST KISS shape and matches the slots. `vividness`/`source` stay genuinely
  orthogonal (they ARE post/pre stages). The taxonomy becomes `medium × source × vividness`
  — three axes, all real. **This is the fittest fix.**
- **(B) Metal is a REAL finish.** Then it does NOT live in `uMedium`; it is a separate
  `uFinish` int lane (a free pad slot) and a post-`applyMedium` operator, genuinely
  composable. More flexible, but more wiring, and §2b would need rewriting to relight the
  medium's output rather than rebuild from chroma. Heavier; only worth it if "vangogh-relit-
  as-metal" is actually desired.

Either way the current spec asserts (A)'s slots with (B)'s framing. That is incoherent and
must be resolved before the wave, or the configurator ships a lie.

## REFUTATION 3 (liquid-weight / cartoon-punch) — the metal is correct-but-COLD; no anticipation, no squash, no follow-through MORE-on-move

The motion spec (§5) gives the catch-light an inertial cursor lerp (0.22/frame) — good,
that's liquid-weight on the LIGHT. But the metal SURFACE itself has zero cartoon register:
- **No anticipation / overshoot.** The rake "follows on the cursor lerp" — that's *damping*,
  not the bold technicolor punch the binding law demands (anticipation, exaggeration,
  follow-through, overshoot with real weight). A liquid-metal sheet should BULGE toward the
  cursor (a specular swell that overshoots and settles), not just slide a highlight.
- **No squash & stretch / morph-MORE-on-move.** The edict: "morph MORE on move." The metal's
  ridges are static field iso-bands that drift at the ambient `nucleiDrift` rate regardless
  of interaction. On a fast cursor sweep the sheet should momentarily STRETCH along the
  sweep (anisotropic smear) and settle — the liquid-weight-universal rule. Absent.
- **The valley term is a floor (`mix(0.22,1,facing)`), not a deep technicolor trough.**
  lens-c's "deep warm-shadow valleys" was the cartoon-DR ask; a 0.22 floor is a gentle
  dimming, not the 1940s-technicolor near-black-warm trough. On the artefact the valleys are
  pale-cream, not deep. This is the "missing cartoon-punch" failure literally.

### Hardening
- Drive a small specular SWELL / displacement toward the cursor with overshoot+settle (a
  spring with bounce, NOT critically damped) — the anticipation+follow-through register.
- On cursor velocity, transiently lower `METAL_SHININESS_ANISO` / stretch the streak along
  the motion vector (squash&stretch), recovering on a weighted ease — morph-more-on-move.
- Deepen `METAL_VALLEY_FLOOR` toward a warm near-black (respecting BA.W-NO-GRAY: warm, never
  gray/cold) so the technicolor DR is real, and add a gate clause asserting the valley→crest
  L-swing ≥ a technicolor-DR bar (a third born-RED arm — the gentle current floor fails it).

## REFUTATION 4 (a11y / PRM completeness) — the idle-rake breaks the "one static frame" PRM contract subtly

§7 says PRM seats "one lit static frame, rake frozen to idle." But §2b's idle light is a
FIXED `vec3(0.55,0.55,Z)` upper-right rake — fine, static. EXCEPT the metal's ridges ride
the nuclei field, and §5 says "metal flows with the drift." Under PRM the drift is frozen
(verified: the substrate master-tempo zeroes it), so the ridges freeze too — consistent.
**But the catch-light's idle rake + the static ridges can still produce a high-contrast
busy field** that, while static, is visually NOISY for a reduced-motion user (who often also
wants reduced visual complexity). The PRM carve addresses MOTION but not the metal's static
busy-ness. Minor, but: the spec claims PRM is fully inherited "NO new lifecycle" — true for
motion, but a reduced-motion metal frame should arguably also REDUCE the speckle intensity
(tie metal contrast to a calm floor under PRM), not just freeze it.

### Hardening
Under `reducedMotion`, additionally damp `METAL_HEIGHT_SCALE` / streak intensity toward a
calmer static read (one scalar on the existing master-tempo path — no new matchMedia). Cheap,
and it makes the PRM frame genuinely restful, not merely frozen-busy.

path, and prove clause-1 (vividness floor) passes on the BLURRED image output in both modes —
not just on the palette default. Add the flower to the born-RED gate's image arm. Until an
asset clears the floor measured, the blurred-image §3 claim is unproven.
