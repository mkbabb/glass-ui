VERDICT: **SURVIVES with material hardening required.** The diagnosis is real and live-confirmed (the HEAD

## TOP REFUTATION (the one that lands hardest)

**The "deft union, one new vec4 lane + reuse the existing ramp/Aurora/flowAmt" story is partly FICTION against HEAD.
Two of the four moves are net-new plumbing the GOLDEN frames as "promote what's already there," and Move 4a (aurora
ground) is a second pipeline + second bind group + second draw + the entire Aurora uniform pack — a bolt-on, not a
string-splice. The KISS/DRY claim is inflated.**

### R1 — `flowAmt` is NOT an "existing lane to promote." It is DEAD config with ZERO shader plumbing. (deft-integration / overfit-claim)
GOLDEN §2 Move 4b: *"`uFlowAmt` is the lane the schema ALREADY declares (`config.flowAmt`, today only Register-B
reads it — promote it into the Register-A read)."* Grepped HEAD:
- `goo-dot.wgsl.ts`: **no** `flowAmt`/`uFlowAmt`/`flow` token anywhere.
- `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts`: **no** `flowAmt` packed; the buffer is exactly 4 lanes (s8–s11, 64 bytes); there is no
  Register-B uniform write at all.
- `constants.ts:75` declares `flowAmt` and `cols` (Register-B), but **no Register-B shader/pipeline exists in the
  shipped component** — there is one fragment dot-stamp pass, full stop.
So "promote the existing lane" is false: Move 4b must ADD a uniform lane + bridge offset + pack write + struct field
from scratch, in BOTH twins. Not a promotion — net-new plumbing. The GOLDEN's KISS framing ("zero new field eval,
one new vec4 lane") hides a second new lane PLUS the lattice-squash uniform PLUS the flow uniform, i.e. it is closer
to *two* new vec4 lanes (s12+s13, which §6 actually admits) — so §2's headline contradicts §6's own mechanism.

### R2 — Move 4a (aurora ground) is a BOLT-ON, not "the same idiom as the goo-blob field splice." (deft-integration / KISS)
GOLDEN §4/§6: *"Reuses `src/components/custom/aurora/shaders/` via splice+compose (the same idiom the hybrid uses
for the goo-blob field)."* Grepped HEAD — this is wrong on three counts:
1. **The path does not exist.** Aurora shaders live at `src/components/custom/aurora/constants/shaders/`, not
   `aurora/shaders/`. The cite is to a directory that isn't there.
2. **It is NOT the same idiom.** The goo-blob splice is a *string slice of WGSL helper fns* concatenated into the
   SAME single pass (one pipeline, one draw). The Aurora ground requires a *second render pipeline*, a *second bind
   group*, a *second draw*, and the Aurora's own per-frame uniform packing (`aurora/composables/uniformBridge*.ts`).
   That is a compose-two-pipelines bolt-on, categorically heavier than a helper-fn splice.
3. **Aurora is not a "warm mesh."** `aurora/constants/shaders/` is a 798-line oil-painting / Van Gogh-medium engine
   (brush.glsl, composition.glsl, mediums.glsl, vangogh-medium.glsl, tonemap.glsl, oil-modes.glsl, flow.glsl). The
   GOLDEN treats it as a cheap clear-load amber quad. Splicing "just the warm field" out of that engine is itself a
   re-author, and risks exactly the re-fork the GOLDEN forbids elsewhere.

### R3 — `auroraFallbackGround` is NOT "one quad, near-free" — it is a CPU 2D-canvas data:URI raster for a different surface. (KISS / overfit)
GOLDEN §4: *"degrades to the static `auroraFallbackGround` (one quad, near-free)."* HEAD's
`aurora/composables/auroraFallbackGround.ts` header is explicit: it is the **software-raster / headless capture
substrate** ground — a CPU-side `sampleAuroraField` mirror painted to a low-res 2D `<canvas>`, exported ONCE as a
`data:` URI and CSS-upscaled, built so a *contrast-certify harness* reads the right floor. It is not a GL quad and
not in the live WebGL2 draw path. Wiring it as the "WebGL2 tail degrade" is a category error: you cannot blend a
`data:`-URI CSS background as pass-1 of the GL context. The degrade path the GOLDEN promises does not exist as
described and must be re-specced.

## SECONDARY REFUTATIONS

### R4 — LIGHT-mode base-lattice legibility (the D2 it claims to cure) is MARGINAL live. (precept: no-gray / vivid both modes)
In the spike's LIGHT card, the vivid core reads, but the **presence-floor base lattice (`presenceFloor:0.12`) over
flat cream `oklch(0.96)` is near-invisible** — faint grey-cream specks. The GOLDEN's G3 born-RED gate checks
*core/neck/rim* ΔL≥0.18 and C_core≥0.13, which the vivid core clears — but it does NOT gate the **base-lattice-vs-
cream** contrast, which is the actual D2 failure mode in the §3 light-host case. A design can pass G3 while the
"field fills the card" promise stays invisible on light because the floor dots vanish into cream. The gate is
under-specified relative to its own headline claim.

### R5 — the cartoon shadow does not visibly land in the spike (precept: cartoon-punch / 1940s layered-offset)
GOLDEN §2/§3 Move 3 promises *"each dot stamps a second, larger, darker, offset disc UNDER the bright dot (the
1940s layered-offset shadowing → the field lifts off the ground with weight)."* In the live spike the dots read
FLAT — no perceptible lift, no layered offset, no weight. The spike's `shadowRgb=vec3(0.10,0.06,0.03)` at `shA*0.35`
under premultiplied dots is too weak to read, and the offset (`vec2(1.0,1.6)`) is sub-pixel at this cell pitch. The
"cartoon punch / squash-stretch with real WEIGHT" precept is asserted but NOT demonstrated. Born-RED G4 checks "each
dot carries a layered-offset shadow" — but a shadow that exists in the buffer yet is imperceptible passes the gate
while failing the precept.

### R6 — the neck-ridge (the BOLDEST move) is NOT witnessed in the live spike. (gate honesty / boldest-move proof)
The spike's HUD asserts "necks in@~0.06, out@~0.34" but the rendered field shows essentially ONE blob's halftone;
the 2→1→2 connected-component cycle and the "fattest/brightest dots fill the waist a beat before fusion" climax are
not visually evident at the captured frame. The boldest mechanism — the entire reason the design claims to beat
prior dot-metaballs — is the LEAST de-risked thing in the prototype. The spike de-risks Moves 1+3 (which were never
in doubt) and hand-waves Move 2 (the actual hard one). G2's born-RED is plausible but the spike does not yet PROVE
the GREEN side is reachable with these constants (`weldSwell*4.0`, `smoothstep(0.55,1.05,gradMag)`).

### R7 — cite drift in the D-table (minor, but "trust nothing"). (overfit/hardcoded refs)
- D4 cites `goo-dot.wgsl.ts:131` as "a linear `smoothstep(fieldFloor,1,fCell)` ramp." Line 131 IS a `smoothstep`,
  not linear — the prose calls it "linear ramp" then quotes the smoothstep. The defect (neck reads as thinner dots)
  is real, but the "linear" wording is imprecise; the actual issue is *radius-monotonic-in-fCell*, gradient-blind.
- The §6 byte-table is correct (64→96, s12/s13 on binding1, field struct byte-identical). ✓ — credit where due.

### R8 — Safari/WebKit risk is reasoned but ONE real gap is glossed. (cross-engine)
The GOLDEN correctly routes this as the §L7 viz path (in-shader smin, no backdrop-filter:url, fwidth fenced to
fs_main, sRGB-in-shader). The spike confirms the read-out math runs on WebGL2/ANGLE. BUT: the two-pass aurora
compose (R2) introduces a **load:"load" over a premultiplied-alpha context** dependency, and WebKit's premultiplied
canvas compositing + the ANGLE-Metal blend-state for a second pass is exactly where cross-engine ΔE creeps in. The
GOLDEN's parity net (G5) covers the dot read but does NOT call out the *two-pass blend-order* as a parity risk — the
single most Safari-fragile new surface the design adds. PRM-freeze of TWO passes to one composite is also asserted
but not de-risked.

## CONCRETE HARDENING (answers each refutation)

1. **(R1)** Strike the "promote the existing lane" language. Spec Move 4b honestly: ADD `uFlowAmt` + `uLatticeSquash`
   to a NEW binding1 lane (s13), with bridge offset + pack write + WGSL/GLSL struct field, in lockstep both twins.
   Delete the dead `config.flowAmt`/`cols`/Register-B vocabulary from constants.ts (no-legacy clean break) OR wire
   Register-B for real — do not cite a phantom reader.
2. **(R2/R3)** Re-spec the ground as a FIRST-CLASS second pipeline owned by the goo-dot viz: a tiny dedicated
   warm-amber gradient/mesh fragment (≤ ~30 lines, its OWN minimal shader, NOT the Van Gogh engine), drawn pass-1
   clear-load, dot-stamp pass-2 load-blend, both in the viz's `setup` draw closure. Call it what it is — a
   second-pass compose, deft but NOT a "splice." For the WebGL2 tail, the degrade is "pass-1 paints the flat warm
   gradient quad," NOT `auroraFallbackGround` (which is a CPU data:URI for the capture substrate). Fix both cites.
3. **(R4)** Add a born-RED sub-gate G3b: **base-lattice-vs-ground** ΔL ≥ ~0.12 in BOTH the light-cream host AND the
   near-dark preset (sample the floor dots, not just the core). On light hosts, drive the floor dots toward the
   AMBER/coral stops (not the L0.96 core stop) so the resting lattice carries chroma against cream. This is the
   actual D2 cure; today's G3 only proves the core pops.
4. **(R5)** Either make the cartoon shadow PERCEPTIBLE (raise `shA`, scale the offset to ≥1px at the budget DPR,
   couple offset to velocity for real) and re-capture proof, OR cut it from this wave and defer to the
   cartoon-shadow greenfield. A gate that passes on an imperceptible buffer term is dishonest; tie G4's shadow
   clause to a measured luminance delta under the dot, not mere presence.
5. **(R6)** Make the spike (or the first build gate) actually WITNESS the neck-ridge: capture the merge cycle frames
   and show the connected-component 2→1→2 AND a frame where the waist dots' radius > the two core radii. The
   boldest move must be the MOST-proven, not the least. If the constants don't produce a visible ridge, tune them
   before committing the design.
6. **(R7)** Reword D4 from "linear ramp" to "radius monotone-in-fCell, gradient-blind" — accurate to line 131.
7. **(R8)** Add a G5 parity clause for the **two-pass blend order** on WGSL-Metal × GLSL-ANGLE (and the WebKit
   premultiplied second-pass composite), and a PRM clause proving BOTH passes freeze to ONE held composite. This is
   the design's most Safari-fragile new surface and must be gated explicitly.

---

## BOTTOM LINE
The thesis (dead speck → presence-floored technicolor metaball field, no fork, born-RED genuine) SURVIVES and the
live HEAD evidence is damning enough to justify the wave. But the GOLDEN inflates its deftness: `flowAmt` is dead
not "promotable," the aurora ground is a second-pipeline bolt-on not a "splice" (and its cited path + fallback are
both wrong), and the two visually-load-bearing precepts it leans on — light-host lattice legibility and cartoon
shadow weight — are marginal-to-invisible in its OWN prototype. Harden the seven points (especially R1–R3 deftness
honesty + R4 the real D2 cure + R6 proving the boldest move) and it is buildable. Ship as-written and the "surgical
union" will land as a partial bolt-on with an invisible base lattice on light.
