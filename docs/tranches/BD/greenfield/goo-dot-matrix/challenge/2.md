## R1 (THE STRONGEST) — the BOLDEST move fails its OWN de-risk: the neck is NOT the brightest/fattest dots

The golden leads with the neck-ridge (§3): *"the weld is the FATTEST, BRIGHTEST dots in the field …
the visual climax … the bridge between merging bodies the brightest, fattest dots."* This is the
single load-bearing inversion — the whole "makes a dot grid read as a real metaball" thesis.

**Live result (golden's own `spike.html`, WebGL2 — the Safari surface, the exact mechanism + the
exact swell=0.18 / spec=0.30 the golden specifies):** across the entire merge cycle the **two cores
stay the brightest dots in every frame**; the waist bridge, when it forms (sep≈0.16), is a band of
*dimmer* coral dots between two brighter hot-yellow cores. At no captured sep does the neck out-bright
or out-fat the cores. The "visual climax" never arrives — the climax is the two cores, the neck is a
**connecting isthmus that reads dimmer than what it connects.** The boldest move, in its author's own
faithful proxy, does NOT do the one thing it claims.

Mechanism cause (verified in the spike + the §2 MOVE-2 snippet): `weld = (1-smoothstep(uWeldLo,
uWeldHi, gradMag)) * core`. The weld is gated on `* core` (the body ramp) AND specular is only
`+ uWeldSpecular * weld`. At the waist `core` is *lower* than at a body center (the SDF is shallower-
negative there), so `core` suppresses the weld term exactly where the golden wants it loudest, and the
+0.30 specular lift is swamped by the cores' full `(1-brightFloor)*core ≈ 0.58` body brightness. The
neck swell is +18% radius — perceptually invisible against the cores' size. The math actively fights
the thesis.

**HARDENING:** decouple the weld read from `core`. Gate the weld on a **rim/iso band** (`fCell` near
the silhouette-cross value, where two bodies' membranes meet) NOT on `core`; and make the specular a
**multiplicative HDR pop** (`bright *= 1 + uWeldSpecular*weld`, not additive) so the waist can exceed
the core ceiling, plus a **larger swell** (≥1.35×, not 1.18×). Add a born-RED gate clause that is a
DIRECT inequality: at the peak-merge frame, `max dotR in the NECK band > max dotR in EITHER core band`
AND `max luminance in the NECK band > max luminance in either core band` — the golden's G2 only asks
"waist radius > the two core radii" but the live spike would FAIL even that (the cores stay fattest),
which means **G2 as written is born-GREEN-impossible on this mechanism** — it can never pass, OR (worse)
it passes on a technicality while the percept fails. Make G2 a luminance inequality too, and tune the
mechanism until the spike actually shows a bright waist BEFORE declaring 55% convergence.

## R2 — MOVE 4a (aurora ground, two-pass, one context) is under-costed vs the VERIFIED single-pass substrate

The golden asserts (§2 MOVE-4a, §6) the dots float over a live aurora *"in the SAME GL context … a
two-PASS frame … pass 1: the warm aurora mesh, load `clear`; pass 2: the dot-stamp, load `load`"* and
calls it *"a SPLICE+compose, the SAME idiom the hybrid already uses."* **This is materially false to
the on-disk reality.** Verified on HEAD:
- `useGooDotMatrix.ts` `frame()` issues exactly ONE pass: `encoder.beginRenderPass({... loadOp:"clear"})`
  → `pass.setPipeline(res.pipeline)` → `pass.draw(3,1,0,0)` → `pass.end()`. The GL path issues ONE
  `uploadBlobUniforms` draw. There is **no second pass, no second pipeline, no aurora bind anywhere.**
- `gooDotSetup.ts` builds ONE pipeline, ONE bindGroupLayout (binding0 field + binding1 dot), ONE bind
  group. There is no aurora pipeline, no aurora binding, no aurora program.
- The "SAME idiom" is a SPLICE (string-concatenating the field WGSL into the dot shader) — that is NOT
  a second render pass. The golden conflates *shader-source splicing* with *adding a render pass +
  pipeline + bind group + a whole second shader program with its own uniforms*. The hybrid has NEVER
  done a two-pass compose; it is single-draw by construction.

So MOVE 4a is not "the same idiom" — it is a **new render architecture**: a second compiled pipeline,
an aurora uniform buffer + bind group (the claimed "third binding" must actually be a second bind group
or a re-layout, since binding0/1 are taken), a `load:"load"` blend pass, AND the GL twin must replicate
it as a second program with a framebuffer-preserving draw. On the WebGL2 (Safari) path, blending a
second program over the first into the SAME default framebuffer with correct premultiplied-alpha order
is a real correctness hazard the golden waves past. This is the largest "deftly integrable" overclaim.

**HARDENING:** either (a) DROP MOVE 4a's "live aurora in the same context" to the **CSS/static
`auroraFallbackGround` behind the canvas** as the DEFAULT (a sibling DOM layer, zero GL change — the
demonstrably cheaper §3 fix that still gives a colorful ground + defined edge), and reserve the
in-context live-aurora pass as an EXPLICIT, separately-gated stretch with its own paint-proof; or
(b) keep the two-pass but re-spec §6 honestly as **a new pipeline + bind group + GL second-program +
premultiplied-blend-order proof in BOTH twins**, costed as a real architectural add (not "~3 lines"),
with a born-RED clause that the GL path's second-program blend matches the WGPU `load` blend ΔE on the
parity-metal net. Do not call it the hybrid's existing idiom — it is not.

## R3 — the presence floor is a GHOST lattice, not a living field; G1 is born-GREEN-trivial

Live (spike, both cards): at `presenceFloor=0.12` the base lattice away from the blobs is **barely
perceptible** — on the LIGHT cream card the corner dots are near-invisible ghost-grey specks; on the
DARK card a dim grid you must hunt for. The percept is *"floating clumps in a near-void,"* not the
golden's *"card alive corner-to-corner, never dead ground."* The D1 fix technically paints dots
everywhere but does NOT deliver the claimed living field.

The gate G1 (*"edge-presence-vs-center ratio is FINITE and > 0"*) is satisfied by a SINGLE lit pixel
anywhere — it is **born-GREEN-trivial the instant presenceFloor > 0.** It does not measure whether the
field reads as ALIVE; it measures whether the discard was removed. A gate that any non-zero floor
passes is not a born-RED bar for the actual goal (a vivid, legible base field). The self-test "a
re-introduced step() gate REDS G1" is the ONLY thing it catches.

**HARDENING:** raise the gate to a perceptual floor: the base-lattice (non-body) region must clear a
**minimum mean luminance contrast vs the card ground** (e.g. base-dot ΔL vs `--card` ≥ 0.06 on the
LIGHT card — the D2 metric applied to the FLOOR, not just the body) AND a minimum lit-area fraction
(e.g. ≥ 60% of cells carry a dot with α ≥ a perceptual threshold, not α > 0.002). Bump the default
`presenceFloor` toward ~0.20–0.25 and RE-TEST the spike for a legible base field on flat cream — 0.12
is demonstrably too low. The §4 "demo default turns ground ON" hides this: over a colorful aurora the
ghost lattice is masked, so the *transparent/light-host* default (the library's own composable
default) ships the weakest, least-tested case.

## R4 — the GLSL ⟷ WGSL "byte-lockstep twin" claim is overstated; the lane-extend lands DIFFERENTLY in each

The golden repeatedly leans on *"every move lands in BOTH twins identically … byte-lockstep … the
GLSL twin IS the Safari surface."* Good instinct (Safari = WebGL2 overwhelmingly, correct). But the
twins are NOT structurally parallel today, and the golden's `s12/s13` vec4 lane-extend (§6) does not
apply symmetrically:
- WGSL packs uniforms into **vec4 lanes on a binding1 struct** (`s8..s11`, `packGooDotUniforms`,
  std140). Adding `s12 (uPresenceFloor,uWeldLo,uWeldHi,uTime)` + `s13` means a 64→96-byte struct,
  new `OFF` words, new shader struct fields — fine, mechanical.
- The GLSL twin uses **individually-named `uniform float` decls** (`uDotPresenceFloor`, etc.) set via
  `gl.uniform1f(dU.*)` in `useGooDotMatrix.ts`'s GL frame — NO vec4 packing, NO offset table. So the
  "ONE vec4 lane" framing is WGSL-only; the GLSL side is N new scalar uniforms + N new `getUniformLocation`
  + N new `dU.*` sets. The golden's §6 "mirror the offsets in OFF + packGooDotUniforms + the GL dU.* set"
  glosses that these are two DIFFERENT mechanisms, and the GL twin already drifts (it has
  `uDotPointerRadius`/`uDotCursor`/`uBodyRadius`/`uPulsePhase` names with NO counterpart in the WGSL
  lane names). A "byte-lockstep" gate over differently-shaped uniform plumbing is a misnomer.

This matters for the parity gate (G5): `proof:viz-hybrid` keeps *constants* lockstep, but the new
`uTime`-driven twinkle + flow advection + velocity-coupled shadow offset introduce **time-dependent,
hash-dependent** terms. `hash21` and `sin(uTime*…)` can diverge between Metal (WGSL) and ANGLE (GLSL)
at the bit level — and the golden's parity net is the `W-VIZ-PARITY-METAL` ΔE bar, which `BD.W-VIZ-
PARITY-METAL` itself notes is **still a device-free structural proxy at HEAD** (deltaE.mean=0, byte-
identical PNGs) — the LIVE Metal×ANGLE readback is OWED, not done. So G5 ("read identically on Metal ×
ANGLE") leans on a gate that does not yet have real numbers.

**HARDENING:** (a) Re-word §6/§7: the lane-extend is a vec4 pack on WGSL **and** N named scalars on
GLSL — state both, and add a unit that asserts the GLSL `dU.*` set count == the WGSL lane-field count
so a dropped uniform reds. (b) For the time/hash terms, derive `twinkle`/`flow`/shadow-offset from a
**shared deterministic helper already in the spliced field** (the goo-blob `breath`/`hash21` the field
already exports) rather than a fresh `sin(uTime*0.6+hash21(cell))` authored per-twin — DRY *and* it
inherits whatever parity the field already proved. (c) Make G5 BLOCK on the real `viz-parity-metal.spec.ts`
live readback (the §3 owed item), not the proxy — single-engine + proxy is explicitly NOT acceptance,
and right now the net has no live number.

## R5 — PRM "ONE static frame mid-merge, FILLED + legible" is unverified for the FILLED claim, and the twinkle/shadow PRM-freeze is asserted not shown

The golden claims (§5, §7, G5) PRM paints *"ONE static composite (aurora + dots frozen mid-merge, the
field FILLED + legible — strictly better than today's frozen speck)."* Two gaps:
- The substrate's PRM path (`useGooDotMatrix` → `createGpuSubstrate` one-frame freeze) calls
  `frame(0)` / `renderAt(0)`. At `t=0` the merge phase is `sep = 0.20 + 0.14*cos(0) = 0.34` —
  **maximally SEPARATED, not "mid-merge."** "Frozen mid-merge" requires choosing a `t` that lands on a
  merge, which the substrate's `tick(0)` does NOT guarantee. The golden asserts a flattering frozen
  frame it has not pinned. The frozen frame could be the two-separate-clumps state (the LEAST compelling).
- The `twinkle = 0.85 + 0.15*sin(uTime*…)` and the velocity-coupled cartoon-shadow offset must be
  PROVEN to collapse to a static value under PRM. The shadow offset `* (1 + uFlowAmt*2)` couples to
  flow; if `uFlowAmt` isn't zeroed under PRM the shadow could still imply motion in a static frame.

**HARDENING:** pin the PRM freeze `t` to a deterministic **mid-merge** phase (compute the `t` where
sep is minimal and freeze THERE) so the static composite shows the filled, necked, vivid state — the
"strictly better than the speck" claim is only true if the frozen frame is the GOOD one. Add a born-RED
PRM clause: the frozen frame's connected-component count == 1 (merged) AND field-fill ≥ the G1 floor,
i.e. the freeze captures the best state, not `cos(0)`. Assert `uTime`/`uFlowAmt`/twinkle-phase are
zeroed (constant) in the PRM uniform pack so no term encodes residual motion.

## R6 — overfit / hardcoded refs + a DRY snag in the cartoon shadow

- **R6a (overfit):** the technicolor re-grade (§2 MOVE-3) hardcodes 3 OKLCh stops *and* §3 hardcodes
  the merge geometry (`bodyRadius`/`orbitRadius`/`smoothK`) to a √φ demo fill. The neck-ridge thresholds
  (`uWeldLo=0.55`, `uWeldHi=1.05` in the spike) are tuned to the spike's specific 2-blob `k=0.18`
  field — the production field is the full goo-blob `sceneDistG` with FBM-warped membrane + satellites,
  whose gradient magnitude distribution differs. The weld thresholds will NOT transfer; they are
  overfit to the spike's clean analytic 2-blob. **Harden:** derive `uWeldLo/Hi` from the field's actual
  gradient statistics (or normalize `gradMag` by `bodyR`), and gate G2 on the REAL satellite-bearing
  field, not a 2-blob proxy — the spike's clean merge is not the production merge.
- **R6b (DRY/cost):** the cartoon shadow (§2 MOVE-3) stamps a *second, larger, offset disc per dot* —
  the golden bills it "one extra smoothstep … compositor-cheap." In the spike it is a second `length()`
  + `smoothstep` + a premultiplied composite per fragment, ON TOP of the field eval. That roughly
  DOUBLES the per-fragment dot work across the WHOLE filled field (now that the field FILLS, every cell
  pays it — the old discard skipped most cells). On the WebGL2 (Safari) budget-DPR tail the golden flags
  as the watch-cost, a full-field doubled per-dot composite is the real perf risk, not the aurora pass.
  **Harden:** gate the shadow to dots above a presence threshold (skip the ghost-floor dots), and add a
  perf clause to the gate (frame-time at budget DPR on the WebGL2 path, the actual Safari surface),
  since "compositor-cheap" is asserted, not measured.
- **R6c:** the §3 spike's color path mixes OKLCh stops via `mix()` in **OKLCh (cylindrical) space**
  (`ramp()` lerps L/C/h directly) — hue lerp across stops (85→45→62) can swing through off-axis hues.
  The golden claims "sRGB color-interp … no reliance on color-interpolation-filters" (correct re: no
  SVG filter) but the in-shader hue interpolation in cylindrical OKLCh is its own cross-engine risk if
  `cos/sin(h)` differ Metal vs ANGLE. Prefer lerping in OKLab (rectangular) — the field already has
  `oklchToOklab`; lerp the oklab vec3, the same DRY-the-field-helper move as R4b.

## Verdict

**SURVIVES** the cross-engine/perf/a11y lens — no fatal (fork / forbidden-filter / naive-ellipsoid)
refutation lands, and the diagnosis + reuse discipline are genuinely fit. But it is **WOUNDED**: the
boldest move (R1) does not render as its headline claims in the author's own de-risk spike; MOVE 4a
(R2) is an architectural overclaim mis-sold as the existing idiom; and three gates (G1, G2, G5) are
born-RED-too-weak or lean on an owed live-parity number. The 55% convergence is OPTIMISTIC: the
neck-ridge mechanism needs re-derivation (decouple from `core`, multiplicative pop, bigger swell) and
re-de-risking before the boldest claim is true.
