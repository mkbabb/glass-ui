The golden's Move 4 + §4 + §6-bar-4 repeatedly justify retiring the Canvas2D egg as fixing
"a thin **grey** Canvas2D line", "clever math, **cold paint**", "the **one cold spot**", "the
ℱ-egg's old **grey** Canvas line (the one cold spot) is RETIRED to the warm accent", and the
acceptance bar reads "Born-FAIL on HEAD (**cold grey Canvas line**)."

**This is false.** `demo/eggs/FRedrawOverlay.vue:56-58` already inks in
`var(--viz-fourier, #d4344a)` — a warm crimson — sampled live via `getComputedStyle`. The egg
is NOT grey and NOT a no-gray violation today. The born-FAIL premise for bar 4 ("cold grey
Canvas line") is fabricated; an honest gate would born-RED on something REAL (Canvas2D ≠ the
SVG HandMark family / no cartoon weight / no gold seal / not warm-cream-card-composited), not on
a grey-ness that does not exist. As written, the binding-π bar 4 is a **false born-RED** — it
will "pass" by fixing a defect that was never there, and a reviewer trusting the spec ships a
mis-keyed gate. This is exactly the W-GATE-TRUTH-AUDIT false-green class the spike was praised
for catching — and the golden re-introduces it one section later.

**HARDENING:** Strike every "grey/cold-paint" claim about the ℱ-egg from Move 4, §4, and §6.

`<HandMark>` SVG (assert the overlay mounts a `.hm__svg`); (b) HEAD has no cartoon-weight

the warm-cream card. The egg's existing warmth is a point IN FAVOR of the union (it already
shares the `--viz-fourier` accent), not a defect — say so.

the already-accepted highlighter**, not just boil. The golden frames a shared mutation of a
shipped, audited brush as pure-upside DRY while fencing it as "ONLY addPressure" — those two
statements are in tension. A1/A2/A3 gate boil; **nothing in §8 guards the highlighter against
regression**, so the highlighter's accepted width-profile can silently drift and no gate REDs.

**HARDENING:** Add a gate arm A8 (born-GREEN, must-stay): the highlighter's emitted hull
