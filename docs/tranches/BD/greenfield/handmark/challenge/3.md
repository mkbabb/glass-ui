## Refutations that LAND (hardenings required)

### R1 (TOP) — The de-risk is NARROWER than the load-bearing design claim: curvature-coupled pressure + the hull width-emit are UN-SPIKED.

The GOLDEN's strongest DESIGN claim (§1, §2 Move 1, §5) is that **curvature-coupled pressure is "the single move that turns a wiggly line into a pen line"** — i.e. the gestalt fix is NOT the value-noise centerline alone, it is the value-noise centerline *rendered as a variable-width pressured hull whose width tracks curvature*. A3 (the gate) keys on WIDTH variation reaching the renderer.

But `golden/boil-spike.mjs` de-risks ONLY the 1-D centerline y-series (autocorr/spacingCV/ampCV). Grep for `hull|getStroke|width|pressure|thinning|swell|curvature` in the spike returns **nothing**. The boldest gestalt promise — "pen line, not wiggly line," via the hull body + the curvature→pressure walk — is verified by ZERO spike. §9 even concedes the cartoon/ℱ moves are "verified live at implementation, not spiked here," but it does NOT concede that the curvature-pressure half of MOVE 1 (the correctness core) is equally un-spiked. The spike's "SPIKE PASS ✓ — de-risks the GOLDEN" banner over-reaches: it de-risks the centerline, not the rendered pressured stroke that the acceptance bar (#1: "irregular ... WIDTH visible") actually judges.

**Why it matters as DESIGN:** value-noise on a constant-width thin stroke would still read as a wiggly line (just a non-periodic one). The thing that makes it read as a *pressured hand pen-line* is the hull swell — and the curvature-coupling is asserted, not demonstrated, with no constants pinned (unlike the centerline's `OCTAVES=4`/`F0=1.9`/`AMP_FRAC=0.05`). There is a real risk that `getStroke` over a high-frequency value-noise centerline (NOISE_F0=1.9 × φ^3 ≈ 8 cyc) with `streamline=0.55-roughness*0.1` SMOOTHS the wobble back out (perfect-freehand's streamline is a low-pass), partially undoing the very non-periodicity the centerline just bought — a known pf interaction the spike never measured.

**Hardening:** extend the spike (or a second spike) to render the value-noise centerline through the REAL `getStroke`+curvature-`addPressure` path and MEASURE (a) the emitted hull width-profile CV ≥ a floor, AND (b) that `getStroke`'s streamline does NOT re-periodicize the outline (re-run A1's autocorr on the HULL OUTLINE points, not the centerline — assert maxPeak ≤ 0.6 *post-hull*). Pin the curvature→pressure constants (curvature window, pressure clamp, gain) the same way the noise constants are pinned. Until that exists, §9's "de-risked" is true only of half of Move 1.

### R2 — The A7 fence is self-contradicting: re-authoring shared `addPressure` REGRESSES the shipped highlighter, and the gate cannot catch it.

§3's edit table mutates `ink.ts`'s `addPressure` swell to a curvature-coupled profile and §1 declares it "Shared by every hull brush (DRY) — crayon/marker/highlighter inherit it." But the **highlighter ships in production today** routing through this exact `addPressure` with its tuned symmetric `0.5+0.35·sin(π·t)` swell (`ink.ts:56`) — that swell IS the highlighter's painted character (a wide slab that fattens mid-word). §7 promises "KEEP ... the highlighter"; §0/Move 2 lists only boil/crayon/marker as the brushes that change. Re-authoring the SHARED `addPressure` body silently alters the highlighter's width-profile — a regression to an unlisted, shipped brush.

The A7 self-test bite (g) hashes `ink.ts`-**logic** for the no-rebuild fence, but a curvature-coupling rewrite of `addPressure` IS an `ink.ts`-logic change — so either (a) A7 RREDs the golden's own Move-1 edit (the fence is too tight and the golden violates it), or (b) A7 is silently widened to exempt `addPressure` and then it can no longer catch a highlighter paint regression. The golden never resolves this. The DRY framing hides a shared-mutation hazard.

**Hardening:** either (i) gate the curvature-coupling behind a NEW brush field (e.g. `pressureMode:'swell'|'curvature'`, defaulting `'swell'` so highlighter is byte-stable) — costs the field-count discipline a 13th-ish enum but keeps the fence honest; OR (ii) keep one `addPressure` but add a binding-π arm that PAINTS the highlighter both pre/post and asserts its width-profile is unchanged within ε (curvature-coupling must be a no-op for the highlighter's low-curvature flat slab — prove it). State explicitly in §3 that the highlighter's emit is part of the A7 fence's MEASURED surface, not just its hash.

### R3 — The "demo cards are opaque flat plates, paper-grain OCCLUDED, marks float / gray" defect (§6 Move 6) is OVER-STATED — live the card is already warm-cream `rgb(253,245,236)` with `.paper-grain-overlay`.

Live readback of the demo card: `class="paper-grain-overlay rounded-card border bg-card p-8"`, computed `background-color: rgb(253, 245, 236)` — a warm cream, carrying the paper-grain overlay utility. This is NOT "opaque `bg-card` ... the paper grain ... OCCLUDED ... marks float on a flat plate" (§6) and it is emphatically NOT gray. The BA.W-NO-GRAY warm floor is ALREADY satisfied on this demo. Move 6's framing manufactures a gray/occlusion defect that the live page does not exhibit; the real Move 6 is a *refinement* ("translucent glass over a MORE visible field, bump `--paper-grain-opacity` above the 0.025 floor"), not a correction of a gray defect. Selling a refinement as a defect-fix risks the §3-colorful-field acceptance bar grading a phantom.

**Hardening:** re-word Move 6 / acceptance bar #5 to the truth: the field is ALREADY warm-cream-with-grain; the refinement is (a) lift `--paper-grain-opacity` from the sub-perceptual 0.025 floor to a *visible* tint and (b) optionally a translucent glass card so the grain reads as a field BEHIND glass (the §3 "colorful field behind glass + a defined edge" edict). Drop the "occluded / flat plate / float" language; it does not survive a live look. Keep the born-RED bar as "grain opacity is sub-perceptual (≤0.025)" — that is measurable and honest — not "the card is gray/opaque."

### R4 — `boil` cannot be both `natural`-routed AND `ribbon:'hull'` without a stated reconciliation; the two mechanisms are described as if orthogonal but they compose in an unspecified order.

At HEAD, `boil`'s underline is special-cased: `normalizeProps` auto-engages `natural=true` (brush.ts:129-134 comment), so `shapeGeom(...natural=true)` routes through `naturalUnderlinePoints` to PRODUCE the centerline — and then `ink()` renders that centerline per `ribbon`. The golden flips `boil`'s `ribbon` to `'hull'`. Fine in principle — but Move 1 ALSO says the new `naturalUnderlinePoints` does "endpoint-only taper" and the hull `taper:{start:14,end:22}` ALSO tapers the ends. Two end-tapers compose (the geometry endpoint-settle AND the pf width run-out). The golden never says the centerline taper and the hull taper are coordinated; double-tapering can pinch the ends to a hairline-of-a-hairline, killing the visible "ends settle to baseline" anchor. Also: `boil` only auto-`natural` on the UNDERLINE shape — for `boil` on `path` (the ℱ-showpiece uses `brush="boil" shape="path"`), `natural` does NOT engage, so the ℱ gets a *plain hull over the raw DFT centerline*, NOT the value-noise voice. The showpiece's "the re-authored warm pressured boil hand-line writes the ℱ" (Move 4) is therefore only the hull body, not the value-noise morphology — the centerline is the DFT walk, un-noised. That is fine (you don't want noise on a glyph trace) but it contradicts the prose that says the ℱ is drawn "in the re-authored boil voice (Move 1)."

**Hardening:** §3 must state the compose order: value-noise centerline → curvature-pressure → hull, with ONE coordinated end-taper (either the geometry settles the ends and the hull taper is ~0, or vice-versa — not both). And §Move 4 must correct the claim: the ℱ inherits boil's HULL+pressure+warm color+cartoon, NOT the value-noise displacement (which is underline-only). Otherwise the showpiece prose oversells what `brush="boil" shape="path"` actually emits.

### R5 — PRM/a11y for the cartoon arms is asserted but the NEW DOM (nib-bead `<circle>`, cel-cast `<path>`) is not enumerated in HEAD's PRM block, and the bead's `offset-path` fallback hides it without confirming the stroke is unharmed.

§4 says PRM zeroes `--motion-weight`, aliases punch→standard, "no bead, no cast travel, no squash." HEAD's `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` block (HandMark.vue:337) only carves the two existing draw mechanisms (`dashoffset`/`clip-path`). The golden adds NET-NEW persistent DOM (the leading-nib `<circle>`, the cel-cast `<path>` copy) — under PRM these must be either removed or rendered in their settled state (bead off-end gone, cast at zero offset). The golden says "no bead" under PRM but a `<circle>` that is merely not animated still PAINTS at `offset-distance:0` (the stroke START) — a stray dot at the line's left end. The `@supports not (offset-path)` fallback "hides the bead" — good — but that means in Safari <16 / unsupported the cartoon register loses its overlapping-action beat silently with no design note on whether the cast alone carries the punch. The cartoon-punch gestalt degrades unevenly across engines and the golden doesn't grade that.

**Hardening:** §4 must enumerate the new nodes in the PRM carve explicitly: under PRM the nib `<circle>` is `display:none` (not just un-animated — a static dot is a defect), the cel-cast renders at its settled offset (or `display:none` if the static mark already reads). And state the engineless-`offset-path` degraded gestalt: bead hidden, cast `transform` still carries the weight, so the punch survives without the bead (confirm live in a WebKit-16-floor probe at implementation). `prefers-contrast:more` floors cast opacity UP — confirm that doesn't make the cast read as a double-strike (a contrast-mode legibility regression).

## Bottom line

The golden is fit and largely honest — the structural KEEP is true, the boil-fix centerline mechanism is genuinely spiked and born-RED→GREEN, the demo/token born-RED claims check out live. It SURVIVES. But: the de-risk covers only half of the correctness core (R1 — curvature-pressure + the hull's streamline-vs-noise interaction are un-spiked), the DRY `addPressure` rewrite quietly endangers the shipped highlighter and the fence can't catch it (R2), the "gray/occluded field" defect is over-stated against a live warm-cream card (R3), the boil natural×hull compose order + the ℱ-voice claim are under-specified (R4), and the new cartoon DOM's PRM/cross-engine carve is asserted not enumerated (R5). Fold all five before build.
