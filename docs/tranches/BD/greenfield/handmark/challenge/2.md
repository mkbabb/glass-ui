## R1 (LANDING — the spike's GREEN is SEED-OVERFIT; the gate is NOT actually de-risked)

The §9 spike prints `GOLDEN value-noise: A1 maxPeak=0.597 (all 4 seeds 0.49–0.60) → GREEN` over seeds `[3, 17, 42, 101]`. I re-ran the EXACT recipe over **400 seeds**:

```
A1 ceil=0.6 over 400 seeds: BREACHES(>0.6)=131/400  maxPeak=0.801  p95=0.740
```

**33% of seeds BREACH the 0.6 autocorr ceiling. maxPeak hits 0.801 — within noise of the HEAD sinusoid's 0.85 that A1 is supposed to RED.** The four seeds in the spike are a lucky draw. The §8 gate spec says A1 asserts `peak ≤ 0.6 for EVERY seed over ≥3 seeds` and the §9 verdict claims the mechanism is "de-risked." Both are false: pick `[1, 5, 9]` and the gate flips RED on a correct implementation. This is precisely the W-GATE-TRUTH-AUDIT *false-green* class the spec congratulates itself for catching on A2 — except here it is a **false-de-risk** on A1, the load-bearing teeth.

Worse, segment-count is a hidden knob the spike fixed at 14:

```
segments=6:  maxPeak=0.687   segments=8: 0.726   segments=10: 0.735
segments=14: 0.773           segments=20: 0.868   (all >0.6, over only 50 seeds)
```

The real renderer does NOT sample at the gate's `segments`; `naturalUnderlinePoints` segment count is geometry-driven by span. The gate measures one sampling, the paint uses another, and the autocorr peak is sampling-sensitive. **The π and the gate will disagree.**

**HARDENING:** (a) the constants must be RE-TUNED against ≥256 seeds with a margin, not 4 — either raise `NOISE_OCTAVES` to 5–6 (more incommensurate octaves crush the autocorr peak) or LOWER `NOISE_F0` so fewer humps fall on a near-period; target maxPeak ≤ 0.50 at p100 over 256 seeds so the 0.6 ceiling has real headroom. (b) The gate must compute autocorr at a FIXED resampled resolution (e.g. resample to 64 evenly-spaced samples) decoupled from the geometry segment count, and the π must read the SAME resampling, or the gate/paint split (cardinal lesson) lands on the wrong side. (c) Re-run the spike over ≥256 seeds in `golden/boil-spike.mjs` and print p100 maxPeak before claiming "de-risked."

## R2 (the curvature-coupled pressure — the spec's headline "pen line not wiggly line" move — is UNSPIKED, and it FIGHTS perfect-freehand's streamline low-pass)

§Move-1 sells curvature-coupled pressure as "the single move that turns a wiggly line into a pen line" — and §9 admits it is NOT in the spike ("verified live at implementation, not spiked here"). Two concrete cross-engine/render refutations:

1. **`getStroke` streamlines the input before it ever sees the pressure.** `ink.ts:121` passes `streamline: Math.max(0.1, 0.55 - roughness*0.1)`. In `freehand.ts:85`, `streamline=0.55 ⇒ t = 0.15 + (1-0.55)*0.85 ≈ 0.53`, a running lerp toward the previous point — a LOW-PASS. The golden's k=3 noise octave runs at `F0·φ³ ≈ 1.9·4.24 ≈ 8 humps` over the span; that is exactly the high-frequency wander that defeats periodicity, and it is exactly what a 0.53 streamline lerp DAMPS. The gate's A1 measures the **raw centerline** autocorr (the spike never runs one point through `getStroke`); the painted hull is the **smoothed** centerline. So the gate can pass on irregularity the paint quietly erases — the boil could measure-GREEN and still paint as a smooth squiggle. Nobody tested the post-`getStroke` autocorr.

2. **Curvature-coupling is numerically fragile on a value-noise centerline.** "high local curvature → low pressure → thin" computed by finite-difference second-derivative on a noisy point-set AMPLIFIES the high-octave noise (differentiation is a high-pass); without smoothing it produces a jittery pressure array that `getStroke` then thins erratically — frayed, not "pen." This interacts with R2.1: you cannot both streamline-smooth (to look like a pen) and curvature-couple off the un-smoothed curve (to vary width) consistently. The order of operations is unspecified.

**HARDENING:** spike the FULL path — feed the value-noise centerline through `addPressure`(curvature) → `getStroke` → measure autocorr + width-CV ON THE EMITTED OUTLINE, both at `streamline=0.1` and `0.55`. Decide explicitly: compute curvature on a pre-smoothed copy (one Catmull pass) then apply pressure, OR drop streamline to ~0.2 for boil and accept the angularity. Pin the order in the spec. Add a π arm that measures the PAINTED hull width-profile CV, not just presence (A3 as written only checks "non-constant" — a 2-sample variance passes it).

## R3 (`offset-path` nib-bead — net-new mechanism, ZERO existing uses, Safari motion-path quirks under-specified)

`grep offset-path src/` → **zero hits**. The leading-nib bead (Move 3) is a NET-NEW compositor mechanism, not a "shipped primitive" the way the spec frames it ("the SAME leading-dot idea the ℱ-egg already proves" — the egg uses Canvas2D `ctx`, NOT `offset-path`; that is not the same primitive at all). Cross-engine reality the golden hand-waves:

- **Safari `offset-path: path()` was buggy through 16.x** — `offset-distance` animations on a `path()` with `offset-rotate` had subpixel jitter and, in some 16.x builds, ignored the path's coordinate origin (bead flies off-anchor). The golden's `@supports not (offset-path: path(""))` fallback only fires when the property is *absent* — Safari 16 HAS the property but renders it wrong, so `@supports` reports true and the broken path runs. Feature-presence ≠ feature-correctness. This is the classic WebKit trap.
- **`offset-path` + SVG coordinate space.** The bead is a `<circle>` inside the SVG; `offset-path: path(<d>)` uses the d-string's user units, but `offset-distance` is resolved against the element's own coordinate system — inside a `viewBox`-scaled SVG with a non-1 transform, the path units and the bead's local units can diverge between Chrome and Safari. Untested in BOTH engines = unverified by construction.

**HARDENING:** (a) version-gate, not presence-gate — `@supports` cannot detect WebKit's broken-but-present `offset-path`; either drive the bead with a JS rAF that sets `cx`/`cy` by sampling the path with `SVGGeometryElement.getPointAtLength()` (works identically both engines, already the platform's idiom) OR accept the bead is Chrome-only and make its ABSENCE the default, not a degraded fallback. (b) Live-verify the bead in real WebKit (Playwright `webkit` channel or a Safari Tech Preview capture) BEFORE the spec claims "ships Safari 16+ / graceful." The golden asserts cross-engine parity it never tested.

## R4 (the cel-cast "lag" + the ℱ SVG re-skin — compositor claims unverified; `box-shadow` fence respected but a NEW per-frame SVG churn introduced)

- **The cel-cast** is "a second offset `<path>` copy translated by `--shadow-cartoon`." The golden correctly avoids animated `box-shadow` (§L7 fence — good, that survives). BUT "on draw it LAGS the stroke (the cast offset travels opposite the motion, scaled by `--motion-weight`)" means a per-frame-updated `transform` on a `<path>` whose `d` is ALSO changing per frame (the dashoffset reveal). Two animated properties on an SVG path (`d`/`stroke-dashoffset` + `transform`) — `transform` is compositor, but `stroke-dashoffset` is NOT a compositor property in WebKit (it triggers paint each frame). The golden's blanket "compositor-only / Safari-safe by construction" (§4) is FALSE for the dashoffset reveal it depends on — dashoffset animation repaints the stroke layer every frame in both engines, and the cel-cast doubles that paint cost (two full-glyph paths repainting). For the ℱ-showpiece (128-point glyph, term-by-term growth) that is a non-trivial per-frame paint on a full-screen overlay.

- **The ℱ re-skin emits "a static `d` per frame from `positionsAt`"** (§4) — i.e. the path `d` attribute is REWRITTEN every rAF. Setting `d` on an SVG path is a full path re-parse + re-tessellation + repaint each frame, in BOTH engines — this is exactly the kind of per-frame churn the Canvas2D version AVOIDED (Canvas redraws are cheaper than SVG path re-layout for a growing polyline). RETIRING Canvas2D "for KISS" may be a PERF REGRESSION on the one artefact that runs full-screen. The spec asserts the SVG render "inherits PRM + warm-cream + cartoon for free" but never measures the frame cost of per-frame `d` rewrites on a 128-segment glyph + the doubled cel-cast.

**HARDENING:** (a) stop calling the family "compositor-only" — be honest that the dashoffset/clip reveal repaints; scope the claim to "no backdrop-filter, no per-frame filter re-raster" (which IS true and IS the real Safari win). (b) Spike the ℱ-showpiece frame cost: measure `performance.now()` per frame for the SVG `d`-rewrite + cel-cast vs the retired Canvas at the real 128-term glyph; if the SVG path churn drops frames, KEEP Canvas2D for the egg (KISS does not mean "one renderer" when the renderers have different perf envelopes — a full-screen growing polyline is Canvas's job). (c) PRM is the easy out (static), but the non-PRM 60fps path is the one that must be measured, and it is not.

## R6 (the token dependency is a HARD build blocker dressed as "graceful fallback")

§Move-3 + §3 confirm (I re-grepped): `--ease-cartoon-punch` and `--motion-weight` are **absent from `src/styles/`** (only `--spring-*`, `--shadow-cartoon-*`, `@utility cartoon-surface` exist). The golden says `weight>0` "falls back to the existing bezier (graceful, no broken paint)." But the ENTIRE Move-3 cartoon register (anticipation dip, punch overshoot, cast-travel scaling, velocity-coupling) is keyed off these two tokens. With the fallback, `weight>0` degrades to *exactly today's flat bezier* — meaning the ℱ-showpiece (`weight:0.7`) and the seal lose ALL cartoon punch (anticipation/overshoot/cast/bead) until a DIFFERENT wave (the §L4 cartoon-register mint) lands first. So acceptance bar #3 ("the cartoon draw-on PERFORMS: anticipation dip → punch overshoot → …") is **un-satisfiable in this wave alone** — it is born-RED forever until the dependency lands, and the golden ships a hard ordering constraint it labels "honest" but doesn't enforce as a gate precondition.

**HARDENING:** make the §L4 token mint a HARD upstream gate of this wave (cannot land Move-3/4/5 cartoon arms until `--ease-cartoon-punch` + `--motion-weight` exist), and split the spec: Moves 1-2 + the boil/crayon/marker hull fix ship NOW (token-independent, the real correctness core); Moves 3-5 (cartoon/showpiece/seal-punch) ship in the wave that mints the tokens. Do not let acceptance bar #3 gate a wave whose dependency is unmet.

## R7 (smaller landings)

- **KISS/DRY tension in Move 5:** "reconcile the draw VOCABULARY but KEEP `CompletionSeal.vue`'s `role="status"` shell" — fine — but the seal now must consume the cartoon register + a `seal` brush preset row in `brush.ts` while staying a separate SFC. That is two components sharing a draw clock and a token set but not a code path = the "parallel-fork-by-convention" risk the binding law warns against. Either the seal's draw genuinely routes through `<HandMark>`'s draw machinery (true union) or it is documentation-only union (a bolt-on with a shared README). The golden's "document as the gold sub-case" leans toward the latter.
- **Overfit ref:** `boil` taper `{start:14, end:22}` is justified as `√φ` (22/14 = 1.571 ≈ √φ=1.272 — NO, 22/14 = 1.571 ≈ φ-ish but NOT √φ; √φ·14 = 17.8, not 22). The §5 proportion claim "the run-out is longer than the lead by √φ" is arithmetically wrong: 22/14 ≈ 1.57, which is closer to φ (1.618) than √φ (1.272). Either the constant or the ratio label is incorrect — an unfalsified golden-ratio decoration.
- **A2/A3 gate teeth:** A3 ("width variation present") as specified can pass on a 2-element non-constant pressure array; it does not assert the width-CV exceeds a floor. A constant-but-tapered hull (start/end taper only, flat middle) passes A3 while reading as a flat ruler with rounded ends. Tighten A3 to a body-width-CV floor measured between the taper zones.
- **No meatball — correctly out of scope (this SURVIVES).** §4's "no metaball, no goo, a hand mark is not a metaball surface" is the right call and forecloses a bolt-on. No naive-ellipsoid risk here. Credit where due.

## VERDICT

**SURVIVES: partial / NO as written.** The architectural read is fit (KEEP the continuum, re-author the boil — correct), and the no-meatball scoping is clean. But the §9 "de-risk status: SPIKED + PASSING" headline is **refuted**: the GREEN is seed-cherry-picked (R1 — 33% of seeds breach the gate; maxPeak 0.801 ≈ HEAD's 0.85), the headline "pen line" pressure move is unspiked and fights the streamline low-pass (R2), and three of the bold cross-engine mechanisms (`offset-path` bead, cel-cast/SVG-`d` churn, ℱ re-skin) claim Chrome+Safari parity they never tested in WebKit (R3, R4). The "graceful fallback" on the missing tokens silently voids acceptance bar #3 (R6), and the live page is gray under the cards (R5). **Top refutation: R1** — the spike does not de-risk the gate; it overfits 4 seeds and the constants must be re-tuned (or octaves raised) against ≥256 seeds with the post-`getStroke` autocorr measured, before §9 may claim "PASSING."
