## 2. The LOAD-BEARING delta the golden got WRONG (the gate-keying correction)

The golden's §8 gate and §9 "de-risk status: SPIKED + PASSING" rest entirely on **A1 = autocorrelation periodicity peak ≤ 0.6 as the discriminator**. Challenge #2 (R1) and Challenge #3 (R1) both flagged this as seed-overfit. **This assay CONFIRMS it and finds it is worse than a cherry-pick — the autocorr metric is structurally wrong.** Three measured facts (scripts in `scratchpad/`, re-running the golden's own `golden/boil-spike.mjs` value-noise body):

1. **At the gate's `segments=14`, 73% of 400 seeds BREACH the 0.6 ceiling** (maxPeak 0.883 — ABOVE the HEAD sinusoid's 0.85 that A1 is meant to RED). The §9 banner "all 4 seeds 0.49–0.60 → GREEN" is a 4-seed lucky draw. `[1,5,9]` flips a correct implementation RED.
2. **Segment count is a dominant hidden knob the gate and the paint disagree on.** Same constants: segs=6 → 2% breach, segs=9 → 29%, segs=14 → 73%, segs=20 → 91%. The real `naturalUnderlinePoints` segment count is span-driven, NOT the gate's fixed 14 → the gate measures one sampling, the paint another (the cardinal-lesson split).
3. **Resampling to a fixed high resolution makes it WORSE, not better** (the "decouple sampling" hardening challenge-2 proposed FAILS): at RES=64 the NEW value-noise autocorr peak is **0.76–0.97** — HIGHER than the OLD sinusoid (0.79–0.92). A smooth low-frequency value-noise is locally smooth, so neighboring samples self-correlate strongly. Autocorr peak cannot separate value-noise from a sinusoid at any resolution.

**The discriminator that DOES hold (measured, 400 seeds, fixed RES=64):**
- **Inter-extremum SPACING-CV** (the irregular hump spacing): NEW median **0.526** (p05 0.120) vs OLD median **0.143** (p95 0.339). Clean separation. The mean-over-≥3-seeds the gate spec uses is reliably ≥0.40 for NEW and ≤0.20 for OLD. **This is the teeth.**
- `peakBinShare` (dominant-period spectral concentration) does NOT separate (NEW median 0.728 > OLD 0.669) — value-noise concentrates in low bins like a tone. Useless.
- Spectral flatness ~0 for both — useless.

**UNION decision:** re-key A1 to **spacing-CV mean over ≥3 seeds ≥ `SPACING_CV_FLOOR` (0.30, headroom below the measured NEW 0.53 and above the OLD 0.20)** as the BORN-RED discriminator; demote autocorr-peak to a CORROBORATING signal with a relaxed ceiling (≤0.80, only ever a sanity rail) OR drop it entirely. Re-run the spike over **≥256 seeds at the real span-driven segment count**, print p100 spacing-CV, before re-claiming "de-risked." This is exactly the W-GATE-TRUTH-AUDIT false-green class — the golden caught it on A2 (amplitude-CV) and then re-introduced it on A1.
