## VERDICT: SURVIVES — but the BOLDEST move (envelop goo-tear) is the one part NOT actually de-risked, and three secondary claims are inflated or live-falsified.

The GOLDEN's **spine** (surface-TYPE-blind `toSurface`, geometry-only topologies, the de-overfit
census, the born-RED gate) is sound and the diagnosis is grep-true — the overfit it targets is
real and live-visible (HEAD `/dock/liquid-playground` literally renders "Search→sheet / Split→islands
/ Now Playing" buttons + "Maps Places" + "Apple Music" prose; `examples/{AppleMusic,AppSwitcher,…}.vue`
clone Apple apps; `liquid-playground.vue:109-110` hardcodes "Winston-Salem"/"Costco Wholesale"). The
gate is genuinely born-RED. **But the single move the GOLDEN stakes its boldness on — the `envelop`
goo-tear of the dock's OWN boundary as a first-class morph target hosting ARBITRARY content — is
de-risked with the exact technique §4 forbids, and does not generalize on the shipped substrate.**

## TOP REFUTATION (the one that lands hardest)

**The de-risking spike animates real `width`/`height` per-frame — the precise `proof:no-layout-animation`
technique the GOLDEN's own §4 fence forbids — so the boldest move is NOT actually de-risked
compositor-only.**

- GOLDEN §4: *"`useElementMorph` writes ONLY `transform`/`opacity`/`filter` … never
  width/height/top/left (the `proof:no-layout-animation` fence)."*
- `eruption-spike.html:96-99` drives the dock body's `width: calc(220px + 360px * var(--dock-portal-t))`,
  `height: calc(64px + 392px * …)`, `border-radius: calc(999px - 967px * …)` — i.e. **animated layout
  geometry per frame.** Live-measured in Chrome: as `--dock-portal-t` ramps 0→1, the dock's real
  `getBoundingClientRect().width` walks **220 → 404 → 579px** and height **64 → 264 → 455px** every
  frame. That is layout animation, full stop. The spike's own line 92-95 comment admits it:
  *"we animate the dock plate size here to PROVE the boundary-grows claim; in glass-ui this is the
  FLIP'd compositor morph."*
- The hand-wave "in glass-ui this is the FLIP'd compositor morph" is doing ALL the work and is
  unproven. The **shipped** envelop substrate (`dock-core/morph-spike.html`) does NOT animate
  width/height — it uses `scale: var(--sx) var(--sy)` on a fixed natural-width box with content laid
  out ONCE and clipped (lines 61-95). The spike diverges from the shipped technique it claims to
  compose, and from its own fence, to make the demo work. **The boldest move is de-risked on a
  forbidden mechanism; the legal mechanism is not shown to produce the same read.**

## SECONDARY REFUTATIONS (each lands)

### R2 — The `envelop` topology does not generalize to ARBITRARY content on the shipped scale-morph substrate (the central architectural gap)

The shipped `--dock-morph-t` envelop (`morph-spike.html`) is a **center-origin `scale` morph of a
box whose content is measured ONCE at natural width, then clipped/revealed** (lines 85-95: *"the
content row — laid out ONCE at natural width behind the clip. NEVER measured."*). That works because
the dock's own icon row is a known, fixed, library-owned layout.

The GOLDEN promises `envelop` hosts **ARBITRARY consumer content** ("a `<Card>`, a `<Drawer>`, a
`<Popover>`, an `<Aurora>` configurator, another `<GlassDock>`"). A `scale`-from-pill morph of
arbitrary, unmeasured consumer content means either (a) the content is **scale-distorted** during the
morph (28px titling squashed/stretched mid-flight — a quality defect the iOS-27 reference never shows),
or (b) the content must be pre-laid-out at final size and clipped — but you cannot "measure once at
natural width" a consumer surface of unknown intrinsic size the way you can the dock's own row.
**The `bloom` topology (separate sibling, FLIP from the trigger rect — `useLiquidReveal` as-is)
generalizes cleanly; the `envelop` topology — the headline boldest move — is the one that does NOT,
precisely because "the dock's own border-box becomes the surface" couples arbitrary content to the
dock's fixed-row scale machinery.** GOLDEN §5 lists envelop as CONSUMING `--dock-morph-t` "box-INVIOLATE";
it never reconciles measure-once-fixed-row with arbitrary-unmeasured-content.

### R3 — `filter:url(#goo)` on an ancestor group BREAKS `backdrop-filter` on glass descendants in WebKit — the spike masks it with near-solid glass

- The spike puts the goo on `.goo { filter: url(#golden-goo) }` (line 81) and the dock + erupting
  surface — **both `backdrop-filter` glass elements** (lines 102, 121) — are DESCENDANTS of that
  filtered group (verified live: `surface.parentElement.id === 'dock'`, goo filter on the `.goo`
  ancestor). In WebKit, a `filter` on an ancestor establishes a stacking/isolation context that
  changes what `backdrop-filter` descendants sample — the descendant blurs the FILTERED group, not
  the page behind, so the live-behind colourful-field read (§3 the precept the GOLDEN binds itself to)
  is corrupted on Safari.
- The spike hides this because its glass is the near-solid token at **0.62 alpha** — so the broken
  backdrop barely shows. The GOLDEN's actual spec is the **six-layer TRANSMISSIVE composite reading
  the field THROUGH** — exactly the case the ancestor-filter trap corrupts.
- The **shipped** fission goo knows this: `fission-bridge.css:148` uses `isolation: isolate` and
  scopes the blur "to the pieces, not the page behind", and gates the filter OFF at rest (line 145
  `filter: none`). The GOLDEN's "the whole `.goo` group carries the tear membrane" is a step BACKWARD
  from the shipped, Safari-correct scoping. **Hardening: never put the goo on an ancestor of the
  backdrop-filter glass; the neck must be a SIBLING filament element (the shipped fission pattern),
  not a parent of the glass bodies.**

### R4 — "Real metaball merge, NO naive ellipsoids" is overstated: the spike's `feComposite operator="atop"` is the standard 2-box trick and renders CONCAVE notches, not a convex neck

- Live screenshot at settle shows the erupted card meeting the plate with **two visible concave
  indentation notches** at the card's lower corners — the threshold filter pinches the negative space
  between two rounded rects INWARD. That is the well-known naive-merge artifact, not the
  "blob↔meatball merge" the binding law demands. There is no thinning/snapping NECK in the static
  composite — the surface just sits embedded in the plate top.
- The filter (`feColorMatrix 0 0 0 20 -9` → `feComposite atop SourceGraphic`, line 178-181) brings the
  SourceGraphic back sharp atop the threshold, so the metaball only affects the alpha-silhouette edge
  — it is exactly the "two boxes, blur the alpha, threshold, re-composite sharp" recipe. The GOLDEN
  asserts the neck "stretches and THINS … snaps" (§2.2 beat 2-3) but the spike's static composite
  shows no neck waist between the two bodies — they are adjacent, not bridged by a thinning filament.
  **The dynamic neck the spec describes is asserted, not demonstrated by the de-risking artifact.**

### R5 — Warm-cream glass FAILS the live paint even with the correct token (the live-π gap the binding law exists to catch)

- The `--glass-dock` token IS warm-cream (verified `color(srgb 0.944 0.903 0.865 / 0.62)`). Yet the
  live-rendered settled membrane reads **gray-brown / muddy taupe** — see the screenshot. Why: at 0.62
  alpha over a multi-hue colourful field, the field's complementary hues (orange + blue + green)
  average toward a desaturated neutral UNDER the warm scrim, and the goo blur+threshold further
  desaturates the merge zone.
- This is the BA.W-NO-GRAY trap landing IN PRACTICE: **a warm TOKEN does not guarantee a warm
  rendered MEMBRANE once the glass is transmissive over a multi-hue field.** The GOLDEN's "the
  membrane is warm-cream … never gray (live-measured)" is asserted at the token layer; the de-risking
  spike falsifies it at the PAINT layer. Per the live-π-per-band lesson, the gate's §7.4 readback MUST
  sample the RENDERED membrane chroma over the live field (not the token), and the warm floor needs a
  per-mode opaque warm UNDER-layer beneath the transmissive scrim so the field tints but never
  dominates to gray. The spike proves this is non-optional.

### R6 — The H5 "four bloom engines, four rAF loops" census is inflated → the headline DRY claim oversells

- Grep at HEAD: `useLiquidReveal` (4 rAF refs), `useBloomUp` (4), `useDockCtaReceive` (4) — but
  **`useLiquidMorph` has ZERO `requestAnimationFrame`**; it drives via `SpringProgress` from kf and
  **already composes `useLiquidReveal`** (imports it, line 56). So §2.1's "`useLiquidMorph`'s
  duplicate FLIP loop is DELETED" deletes a loop that does not exist; the real picture is THREE rAF
  leaves over a shared kf substrate, one of which (`useLiquidMorph`) is already a composition of
  another. The unification is still directionally right (collapse the shared kf usage behind ONE
  `useElementMorph`), but the H5 gate as written ("a 2nd/3rd bloom runner anywhere REDs") will mis-fire:
  `SpringProgress` instances are not "bloom runners", and the census must count kf `ElementMorph`/
  `SpringProgress` *instantiation sites*, not the leaf composables. **Hardening: re-state H5 as "≤1
  FLIP `ElementMorph` instantiation in the dock morph path; `SpringProgress` scalar-drivers are
  exempt," and fix the §0/§2.1 census to "three rAF leaves, one already a composition."**

### R7 — PRM/`@supports` floor is asserted in the spike but the FALLBACK still erupts via the forbidden width/height

- The spike's PRM floor (lines 154-157) sets `goo: none` + `transition: opacity` — but the dock body's
  `width`/`height` calc still binds to `--dock-portal-t`, which under PRM is seated instantly (line 266),
  so the layout-jump is instant rather than animated. Acceptable for PRM. BUT the `@supports
  (filter:url())`-absent fallback the GOLDEN §4 promises ("degrades to a clean scale-bloom, still
  erupts") is NOT exercised in the spike at all, and on the `envelop` path "a clean scale-bloom" of the
  width/height-driven body is undefined. The cross-engine degrade arm is unproven. **Hardening: the spike
  must add an `@supports not (filter:url(#x))` arm and prove the envelop still reads as a scale-bloom
  WITHOUT layout animation.**

### R8 — a11y: the spike's trigger is the SAME element as the expanded surface's container (focus-trap + restore ambiguity)

- `eruption-spike.html`: `#dock` is `role="button"` AND contains `#surface` as a child. On open the
  same element is both the trigger (`aria-expanded`) and the dialog container — there is no separate
  focusable region to move focus INTO, no focus-trap boundary, no restore target distinct from the
  expanded body. The GOLDEN §4 promises `<DockExpand>` does focus-move-in + restore + trap, but the
  de-risking artifact models the trigger-IS-container topology that makes a clean focus contract hard
  (where does focus restore to when the trigger is swallowed by the expanded surface?). This is fine in
  prose but the spike should not have modeled the conflated topology; `<DockExpand>` must keep the
  trigger a SIBLING of the surface, not its parent. **Hardening: spike + `<DockExpand>` must separate
  `[data-trigger]` from `[data-surface]` as siblings so focus-restore has a stable target.**

  and never inspects the surface).
- The **de-overfit census** (§6) — every hit verified at HEAD: `DockSplitContext = "search"|"media"|"nav"`
  (`useDockFission.ts:56`), `splitContext` on the public shell (`useDockShellProps.ts:198`),
  `if(ctx==="nav")`/`if(ctx==="media")dy*=0.25` (`GlassDock.vue:408,413`), the DEAD `"search"` silhouette

- The **`bloom` topology** (separate sibling FLIP from trigger rect = `useLiquidReveal` kept as-is) —
  the fittest, generalizes cleanly, Safari-safe (regular `filter` on own pixels, no ancestor-goo trap).
- The **compositor-only FENCE itself** (the §4 list) is correct as a SPEC — the spike just violates it.
- The **Safari goo primitives exist + are correct in the shipped code**: `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"`
  (DockGooFilter, fission-bridge), regular `filter:url()` not `backdrop-filter:url()` (bug 245510),
  `isolation:isolate` scoping. The spec's Safari fence is real where it leans on the SHIPPED fission goo.

## CONCRETE HARDENING (what makes the golden survive intact)

1. **Re-do the de-risking spike compositor-only.** Drive the envelop with `scale`/`transform` on a
   fixed-final-size clipped body (the SHIPPED `morph-spike.html` technique), NOT `width`/`height` calc.
   If the warm-cream read + neck cannot be achieved that way, the `envelop` boldest move must be
   downgraded or the generalization narrowed (see #2).
2. **Resolve envelop-vs-arbitrary-content.** Either (a) restrict `envelop` to LIBRARY-shaped surfaces
   that can be measured-once (and route arbitrary consumer content through `bloom`), or (b) prove a
   "reveal-at-final-size + scale-the-frame-only, content opacity-staggered" model that does not
   scale-distort arbitrary content. Make this explicit in §1.3 / §2.2.
3. **Move the goo OFF the glass ancestor.** The neck filament must be a SIBLING element with
   `isolation:isolate` (shipped fission pattern), filter gated OFF at rest, never a parent of the
   backdrop-filter glass bodies — else WebKit corrupts the transmissive field read.
4. **Add a RENDERED-membrane-chroma assertion to §7.4 π** (sample the painted pixel over the live
   field, not the token) + an opaque per-mode warm UNDER-layer so the field tints but never grays the
   membrane. The spike falsified the token-only warm claim.
5. **Fix the H5 census + gate wording:** three rAF leaves (one already composing another), not four;
   count kf `ElementMorph` FLIP instantiations, exempt `SpringProgress` scalar drivers.
6. **Prove the `@supports not(filter:url())` + PRM degrade on the envelop path** (scale-bloom, no layout
   animation), and separate trigger from surface as siblings for a clean focus-restore/trap contract.

## Bottom line

The spine, the de-overfit, and the born-RED gate are FIT and survive. The boldest move (envelop
goo-tear) survives only as an INTENT — its de-risking artifact uses the forbidden layout animation,
its generalization to arbitrary content is unreconciled, its goo sits on the wrong DOM tier for Safari,
and its warm-cream claim is live-falsified at the paint layer. None of these kill the design; all are
fixable by leaning HARDER on the shipped fission/morph patterns the spike drifted away from. SURVIVES,
conditioned on hardening #1-#4 landing in the wave spec + a re-cut spike.
