## TOP REFUTATION (the strongest, lands hardest)

**The "warm-cream NEVER gray" floor FAILS in the GOLDEN's own prototype, live.**
The token is correct — `--glass-dock: color(srgb 0.944 0.903 0.865 / 0.62)` (live-measured,
warm: R>G>B). But the RENDERED composite reads **muddy gray-brown / taupe**, both at the
"Open hub" pill (over the vivid red/blue/green field) and at the full-open envelope. The
giant open dock body is a desaturated taupe slab — exactly the BA.W-NO-GRAY violation the
GOLDEN claims to honor.

Root cause is structural, not a token typo, and it indicts the boldest move itself: the
`envelop`/goo path runs the membrane through `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="9"` +
the alpha-threshold `feColorMatrix` (`0 0 0 20 -9`) goo filter. That graph **blurs and
desaturates the colourful field behind the glass into a flat mud** before the warm scrim
composites over it. The §3 edict ("a COLOURFUL FIELD reads THROUGH the transmissive scrim")
and the goo-tear mechanism are **in direct tension**: the very filter that makes the neck a
metaball is the filter that greys the field. The spec asserts both will hold; the prototype
proves they don't coexist as-built. The π asserts "(e) warm-cream never gray" but the only
artifact that runs the π's mechanism renders gray.

## THE OTHER THREE LANDED REFUTATIONS

### R2 — The squish is a FLAT UNIFORM scale, not volume-preserving (born-RED on the GOLDEN's OWN π assert (a))
Measured the erupting surface transform across the travel: `scaleX === scaleY` for the entire
flight (sampled `sx=sy=0.55, vol=0.303` at rest; the spec's own CSS is
`--sx = 0.55 + 0.45·t (+0.06·burst)`, `--sy = 0.55 + 0.45·t (−0.06·burst)`). The X/Y
differential (`±0.06·burst`) is non-zero ONLY in a ~0.07-wide window around t≈0.65. For
~90% of the travel **X·Y sweeps 0.30 → 1.0 uniformly** — a flat zoom, the precise thing the
GOLDEN's π §7.4 born-REDs: `assert(Math.abs(mid.scaleX*mid.scaleY - 1) < 0.12)` evaluated at
`frames[0.4·n]` gives `|0.30·0.30 - 1| = 0.91 ≫ 0.12` → **the GOLDEN's own π would RED its own
prototype.** "T10 squish, X·Y≈1, real deformation not a flat 0.95" is asserted in §3 but is
not what the mechanism does. The squash&stretch is a 70ms garnish on a uniform shrink, not a
volume-preserving deformation through the arc.

### R3 — Non-golden proportion at full-open (Aristotelian edict violated by the headline geometry)
At t=1 the envelope measures **580×456 (ratio 1.27, near-square)** hosting a **300×208**
surface. The content occupies ~22% of the envelope footprint; the rest is empty taupe slab.
The edict demands φ (1.618) proportion "in ALL things… card-width… nothing arbitrary," yet the
spike's `220px + 360px·t` width vs `64px + 392px·t` height are arbitrary literals that land at
1.27 — neither φ nor 1/φ — and the surface-to-envelope ratio is unmotivated. The "dock GROWS to
CONTAIN the surface, one continuous body" read fails: it looks like a small card pasted on a big
blank plate, not a body that is mostly its payload.

### R4 — The metaball neck doesn't read as a thinned WAIST; the bodies just OVERLAP
The whole audacity rests on "the skin TEARS into a goo neck that stretches and THINS… real
blob↔meatball merge, NO naive ellipsoids." Measured mid-flight: at t=0.4 the surface bottom
(651) sits 113px BELOW the dock top (537) — i.e. the surface **overlaps INTO** the dock body by
113px rather than being bridged by a stretched gap the goo filter fills. The metaball filter
only has a thin waist to act on in a narrow t-band; for most of the travel the two glass bodies
are coincident, so the "tear → neck → snap" you'd read is mostly two overlapping rounded rects
with a blurred seam — the very "fade-disconnect / naive ellipsoid" failure mode the π §7.4
`neckWaistRatio(frames) < 0.5` is meant to catch. Whether a true thinned waist ever reads (vs.
an overlap with a soft edge) is UNPROVEN by the artifact; the spec asserts it.

## SECONDARY (real, smaller)

- **DRY/KISS leak in `<DockExpand>` a11y.** §5 lists "a11y → focus-move + aria on `<DockExpand>`"
  as *new code*. But `src/components/ui/focus-scope/FocusScope.vue` (focus-trap) and
  `src/components/ui/drawer/Drawer.vue` (scrim + dismiss + escape + aria overlay) already ship.
  The GOLDEN's "focus-trap, Escape-to-close, live-behind scrim, aria-expanded/controls/haspopup"
  re-implements an extant overlay-a11y substrate. A UNION mandate that re-forks focus-trap/scrim
  is a bolt-on by its own law. The amendment must say `<DockExpand>` COMPOSES `FocusScope` +
  the extant overlay dismiss/scrim, not re-author them.
- **`receive` topology is asserted, never de-risked.** The spike proves only `envelop`. `receive`
  (external→control) and `fission` are claimed to fall out of the one runner "reversed" with zero
  new code, but the artifact exercises neither — and a CTA flying INTO a control while the dock is
  the morph target is the case most likely to break the "one in-flight rAF" fence (two morphs:
  the incoming element + the control's acknowledge squish).
- **`@property --glass-ambient-strength` "Baseline / Safari-safe" overclaims.** Registered custom
  property *animation* via `@property` is Baseline-recent; older WebKit interps it as a discrete
  swap (no smooth interp), so the "4th field channel" hue ramp degrades to a jump on exactly the
  Safari versions the hard-gate targets. Needs an explicit `@supports (transition: --x)` floor or
  it silently no-ops the ambient warm-lift on WebKit — and the spec sells it as a smooth interp.

## CONCRETE HARDENINGS (what makes the GOLDEN survive CLEAN)

1. **Kill the gray-field-through-goo coupling.** The goo `feColorMatrix`/blur must NOT be the
   compositing layer the field reads through. Split the membrane: render the warm-cream
   transmissive scrim (with the live field reading through it, sRGB, saturate≥1.3) on its OWN
   layer; apply the goo `filter:url()` ONLY to the neck/tear silhouette mask, never to the body
   that carries the backdrop. Add a born-RED π arm: sample the open-envelope center pixel's oklab
   CHROMA over the field — `assert(chroma > C_floor)` and `assert(not grey-by-L)`, BOTH modes.
   (The live-π-oklab paint-arm already parses `oklch()`; reuse it.) The current spike would RED
   this — make it RED, then fix the layer split until it's GREEN. That is the missing de-risk.
2. **Make the squish genuinely volume-preserving across the WHOLE arc**, not a uniform 0.55→1
   zoom with a 70ms differential. Drive `sx`/`sy` from `useLiquidFlex`'s tanh volume-preserving
   law for the entire travel (X·Y≈1 throughout, capped ≤1.08), so the π's `|sx·sy−1|<0.12` at
   t≈0.4 actually passes. Re-author the spike to this and re-measure; the artifact must pass its
   own assert (a) before "de-risked" is true.
3. **Derive every envelope/neck dimension from φ off the trigger rect** (the spec already says
   this in §3 prose — the SPIKE must embody it): settled envelope ratio = φ, surface:envelope
   footprint ≥ 1/φ (payload dominates, not empty slab), neck waist = trigger-width/φ². Replace the
   `220+360t / 64+392t` literals. Add a π arm asserting the settled ratio ≈ φ ± 5%.
4. **Prove the metaball WAIST, not an overlap.** The neck must read as a *thinned bridge with a
   gap*, not two coincident bodies. Re-stage so the surface pulls AWAY (a real gap opens) and the
   goo filter bridges it; assert `neckWaistRatio < 0.5` AND `gap > 0` at the tear midpoint in BOTH
   engines. An overlap (gap < 0) is a born-RED fade-disconnect.
5. **`<DockExpand>` COMPOSES `FocusScope` + the extant overlay scrim/dismiss** — amend §5 to list
   them as CONSUMED primitives (new code = ref-wiring + aria only). Add the overfit/DRY-audit
   assertion: no second focus-trap implementation in the dock band.
6. **De-risk `receive` + `fission` under the one-runner fence too** (a second artifact or two more
   spike modes), and gate `--glass-ambient-strength` behind `@supports (transition: --x)` with a
   discrete-swap floor for older WebKit.
