via chrome-devtools + on-disk grep of every named seam. The GOLDEN's born-RED is largely

## VERDICT: SURVIVES (conditional) — the spine is real, the design payoff is mortgaged to unbuilt waves

The `--stage-t` spine, the DRY panel/trigger unification, and the born-RED are sound and
live-verified. But three of the eight acceptance criteria (G1 warm field, G2 field-delta,

## R1 (TOP — near-fatal mislabel) — the see-through peek/half FLOOR is DEPEND-on-UNBUILT, and the freeze does NOTHING below t=0.85

**Claim under attack (§10 RECONCILE):** *"`BE.W-SHEET-TRANSLUCENT` (shipped, the 0.74 see-through
crown) → DEPEND — it owns the t<0.85 peek/half FLOOR."* And G3: *"translucent ≤0.8 at peek/half
(page bleeds)."*

**Live + on-disk truth:**
- `docs/tranches/BE/waves/BE.W-SHEET-TRANSLUCENT.md` line 2: **`Status: SPEC (tranche-dev; NOT
  executed)`**. It is NOT shipped. `--glass-opacity-sheet` / `--glass-bg-sheet` are ABSENT from
  src (its own born-RED: "the token is ABSENT").
- Live drawer (`/containers/drawer`, real open, snap=0.4): sheet `bg = oklab(0.9739 … / 0.95)`,
  `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` — and I forced `--glass-drawer-t` across `{0, 0.25, 0.4, 0.7,
  1.0}`: **α stays 0.95 and blur stays 20px at EVERY value.** The slab is opaque-looking at peek.
- The GOLDEN's own freeze (§2a): `--sheet-freeze = clamp(0, (stage-t − 0.85)/0.15, 1)`. For all
  t < 0.85 this is **exactly 0** → the fill stays the source `--glass-bg-overlay` (live α 0.95).
  **So this GOLDEN supplies NO transmissivity at peek/half.** The see-through floor is entirely
  the unbuilt BE wave's job.

**Why it's a design-fidelity failure:** G3 PASS requires "page bleeds at peek/half." With the
named dependency unbuilt and the freeze inert below 0.85, peek/half composite at α 0.95 — the
iOS-27 see-through bottom-sheet (the literal guiding-light cue: "the album grid reads behind the
now-playing sheet") **does not happen.** The headline "heavy slab landing on a recessed stage"
lands, but the *transmissive crown that makes it iOS-27* is mortgaged. The GOLDEN mislabels a
SPEC wave as "shipped," hiding a hard prerequisite behind a DEPEND.

**Hardening:** Reclassify BE.W-SHEET-TRANSLUCENT from "DEPEND (shipped)" to a **hard inbound
PREREQUISITE that lands FIRST** (move it into §10's "Band-0/sibling PREREQUISITES land FIRST"
list, alongside `--field-h`). Add to G3 an explicit precondition assertion: *peek/half α reads
`--glass-bg-sheet` (~0.74), NOT `--glass-bg-overlay` (0.95)* — born-RED bite if the sheet still
resolves the 0.95 rung. Alternatively, if STAGE-COUPLE must stand alone, extend the freeze to a
**two-segment lerp**: a transmissive floor (0.95→~0.74) over t∈[0,0.85] AND the solidify
(0.74→1.0) over [0.85,1] — so the GOLDEN itself owns the crown and does not depend on the unbuilt
wave for its own headline criterion.

## R3 — the cartoon PUNCH (the 1940s-technicolor edict) is opt-in, DEPEND-on-unbuilt, and absent from the frequent band

**Hardening:** make the FREQUENT band carry a *scaled-down* weight (`--motion-weight` near

only the *loud* squish for the takeover band — a continuum, not a calm/punch binary. And gate the
punch tier's prerequisite (`--ease-cartoon-punch`) as a hard land-FIRST, not a soft DEPEND, so the
acceptance bar can't go green on the calm floor while claiming cartoon-punch.

## R6 — KISS/altitude: `filter: saturate()` on the page-root (coupling C) risks the very ancestor-filter trap the GOLDEN polices elsewhere

§2c: `[data-stage-wrapper] { filter: saturate(calc(1 − 0.18 * --stage-t)); }`. §7 correctly bans
ancestor `filter` over a glass panel ("would clobber the sheet's backdrop-filter"). The wrapper is
the PAGE root; the scrim+sheet are siblings (z 40/50 vs the wrapper). That's fine *only if* the
portal truly renders OUTSIDE the wrapper. reka portals to `body` by default, so this holds — but
the GOLDEN never asserts the portal-root is a SIBLING of `[data-stage-wrapper]`, not a descendant.
If a consumer scopes the portal container inside their app root (a common reka `to=` pattern), the
`saturate()` filter becomes an ancestor of the glass panel → the Safari backdrop-tear the GOLDEN
claims to avoid "by construction." The construction is unstated.

**Hardening:** add an explicit fence: *the `[data-stage-wrapper]` filter is valid ONLY when the
overlay portal target is a DOM SIBLING (body-level), never a descendant; assert
`!wrapper.contains(portalRoot)` in the cross-engine gate (G11).* Or drop `filter` from the wrapper
and desaturate via a sibling tint overlay, eliminating the trap entirely.

## R7 — `@property --stage-t inherits:true` + multi-open: the stale-latch the GOLDEN names but does not mechanize

G12 lists "the multi-open frame-series (open/close ×5, no reload — the `@property --stage-t`

just a test that will fail.
