## REFUTATION 1 (LANDS) — coupling (C) is an ancestor `transform`+`filter`+`clip` on the
## glass panel's stacking world, and the golden's own §7 fence forbids exactly that

**The contradiction.** §2c sets on `[data-stage-wrapper]`: `scale`, `filter: saturate()`,
`overflow: clip`, `border-radius`, `transform-origin`. §7's MEATBALLING/§L7 fence states in
its own words: *"NO filter on an ancestor of the glass panel"* and the staging transform must
be *"`transform` ONLY, NO filter on an ancestor of the glass panel."* The golden waves this
away with *"the scrim's z-stack sits BELOW the panel"* — but that reasons about backdrop-filter
OVERLAP, not about **containing-block / stacking capture**.

**Why the wave-away is wrong, live-proven.** On the live route I measured the portal ancestry:
the drawer sheet's parent chain is `DIV#reka-dialog-content.glass-drawer → <body>`. reka portals
the overlay to `document.body`, **OUTSIDE `#app`**. So today the panel is NOT a descendant of
`#app` and an `#app` filter would NOT clobber it — *that is the only reason (C) is even safe*.
But the golden's spike (`golden/spike.html`) puts the SHEET and the popover as **siblings of**
`[data-stage-wrapper]`, not descendants — i.e. the spike quietly side-steps the very topology
the real app uses. The spike does not de-risk the real wiring; it de-risks a strawman where the
overlay was never inside the staged wrapper to begin with.

**The real break.** The moment a consumer wraps their app root (which §2c REQUIRES — "the
demo/consumer shell wraps their app root") and the staged wrapper gains `filter: saturate()`
**and** `overflow: clip` **and** `scale:`, three things happen that the golden does not address:
1. `filter` on the wrapper establishes a **containing block for `position: fixed`** (CSS
   Transforms/Filters spec). Any `position: fixed` overlay that the consumer renders INSIDE the
   wrapper (Tooltip, HoverCard, inline Popover that is NOT portaled to body, the dock that lives
   in-shell) gets re-parented to the wrapper's box and **mis-positions / clips** under
   `overflow: clip`. This is a Chrome AND Safari behavior (identical), and Safari historically
   has had extra `filter` containing-block quirks.
2. `scale:` on the wrapper mints a new stacking context; a portaled-to-body overlay is FINE, but
   the dock + any in-shell `z-` layering now composite against a transformed parent — sub-pixel
   seams + the known "transformed ancestor blurs descendant text" Safari rasterization (the
   wrapper carries `filter` so it is force-rasterized, and `scale<1` resamples it → the receded
   page text is softened in WebKit specifically).
3. `filter: saturate()` on the wrapper **force-promotes the entire page to a single
   compositor layer every frame the spring runs** — on a content-heavy `/containers` page this
   is the exact "filter on a huge subtree" jank the §L7 fence exists to prevent. The golden calls
   (C) "compositor-cheap; one-shot per open" but a `filter` animation on a full-viewport subtree
   is NOT cheap in Safari (WebKit rasterizes the filtered layer at the receding scale each frame).

**HARDENING.** (a) Forbid `filter` on `[data-stage-wrapper]` entirely — move the desaturate to
an overlaid scrim pseudo (a `mix-blend` or a flat warm veil on the scrim element, which already
sits above the page and below the panel) so the page subtree is NEVER force-rasterized and NEVER
becomes a fixed-containing-block. (b) Specify that `[data-stage-wrapper]` must be an ANCESTOR of
NOTHING that is `position: fixed` and in-shell — OR explicitly require all overlays (incl. the
dock + in-shell tooltips) to be portaled to body, and add a gate bite that FAILS if any
`position:fixed` overlay is a descendant of the staged wrapper. (c) The spike MUST be re-authored
so the sheet/popover are DESCENDANTS of `[data-stage-wrapper]` (the real-app topology when the
wrapper is the body-or-app root) — otherwise it de-risks nothing. Without this, (C) is a Safari
text-blur + fixed-mispositioning regression, not a free depth cue.

## REFUTATION 2 (LANDS) — the headline does NOT "stand alone"; §3's panel resolves to a
## TRANSPARENT background on HEAD because its sole DEPEND token is scoped elsewhere

§3's `@utility overlay-panel` sets `background: var(--glass-bg-floating-tinted)`. I grepped +
live-probed: `--glass-bg-floating-tinted` is declared ONLY under `:where(.btn-glass,
.segmented-indicator)` (`glass/surfaces.css:282`) and is **EMPTY at `:root` and at the panel
scope** (live `getComputedStyle(:root)` returned `""`). So if `BD.W-OVERLAY-PANEL` lands BEFORE
the select-forms "widen the `:where()`" DEPEND, every overlay panel composes
`background: <empty>` → the initial value `transparent` → a **see-through-to-page panel with no
fill**, a worse regression than HEAD (which composes the real `--glass-bg-overlay` ladder).

The golden asserts (§10) the headline "is **not blocked** on the mints — the stage couple is the
headline build that stands alone." That is true for the STAGE couple (it rides
`--glass-drawer-t`/`--glass-level`, which DO exist). But it is FALSE for §3's panel and §5's
trigger: `.overlay-trigger` reads `--glass-edge-floor` (live: EMPTY) and `.glass-capsule-hover`
(grep: **does not exist in src at all** — zero hits), and §4b reads `--ease-cartoon-punch` +
`--motion-weight` (live: EMPTY). Three of the four moves are hard-blocked on un-landed siblings,
and one of them (§3 panel fill) doesn't degrade gracefully — it degrades to *transparent*.

**HARDENING.** (a) §3 panel must `background: var(--glass-bg-floating-tinted, var(--glass-bg-overlay))`
— a fallback to the SHIPPED ladder so the panel is never transparent if the widen hasn't landed
(this is the same graceful-fallback discipline §7 already applies to `@property --stage-t`). (b)
The wave table (§10) must mark `BD.W-OVERLAY-PANEL`, `BD.W-OVERLAY-TRIGGER`, and §4b as
HARD-BLOCKED on their Band-0/select-forms prerequisites (not "small / self-contained") and the
born-RED gate must assert the fallback path renders a NON-transparent warm panel when the DEPEND
is absent — otherwise a mid-tranche ordering slip ships a transparent menu. (c) Correct the §6/§10
claim "stands alone" → "the STAGE COUPLE stands alone; PANEL/TRIGGER/PUNCH ride siblings."

## ATTACKS THAT DID NOT LAND (the golden held — record them so the next lens does not re-plough)

1. **`@property --stage-t` Safari interpolation / stale-latch.** Probed: `CSS.supports('--stage-t','0')`
   = true; Safari 16.4+ registers it; the golden already carves "absent → snaps to endpoints
   (graceful)" and G12 bites the multi-open stale-latch. The interp is on a `<number>` (the cheap
   case). HELD. (Minor note: the gate should assert the registered-property does NOT leak its
   inherited value to a sibling route via the portal subtree — but G12's open/close×5 covers it.)

2. **Surface FREEZE clobbering the sheet's own backdrop-filter.** The freeze is an element-level
   `color-mix` + a per-element `backdrop-filter` radius decay — NOT an ancestor filter. Verified
   against `drawer.css:101` `[data-surface=opaque]` which already composes the solid plate AT THE
   ELEMENT for exactly the substitution-vs-inheritance reason. Dropping blur→0 at full is a real
   Safari WIN (no wasted backdrop sample behind an opaque plate). HELD — this is the cleanest part.

3. **Scrim is gray/flat-black.** Live scrim = `srgb 0.11 0.098 0.09` = warm near-black (R>G>B),
   reads `--overlay-scrim-ink: hsl(24 10% 10%)`. NOT gray. HELD.

4. **a11y regression.** The golden adds ZERO a11y surface (reka owns trap/escape/roving, FROZEN
   byte-untouched) and G9 asserts anti-regression. The `stage=` enum change is a paint/attr knob,
   not an aria change. HELD.

5. **PRM correctness.** `--motion-weight: 0` zeroes anticipation/overshoot/squish; `--stage-t`
   still FLIPS (structure) but `useDrawerSnap.respectReducedMotion` snaps in one frame; opaque-at-
   full survives (structure, not motion). This is correct reasoning — staging IS structure. HELD.
   (Caveat folded into Ref-1: under PRM the wrapper still gets `scale<1` + `filter` STATICALLY, so
   the Safari static-rasterization text-soften persists even with motion off — another reason to
   kill the wrapper `filter` per Ref-1.)

6. **Meatball / naive-ellipsoid.** N/A by construction — the overlay family carries ZERO goo;
   glass plates only. No metaball merge to break. The §7 fence correctly scopes the only
   ancestor-filter risk to the freeze (which is element-level, not ancestor). HELD.

7. **`light-dark()` inset-shadow trap.** The golden explicitly mandates PLAIN per-mode pairs for
   `--overlay-tint-floor` (6%/8%) and G12 bites a `light-dark()` floor. Correct per the documented
   trap. HELD.

8. **KISS/DRY.** ONE `--stage-t`, ONE `@utility overlay-panel`, ONE `.overlay-trigger` modeled on
   the SHIPPED `.dock-trigger` (verified live: `dock-controls/triggers.css` exists + unifies dock
   triggers). `shouldScaleBackground` DELETED (no alias) — verified it is currently a dead prop
   (`Drawer.vue:45` declared, `src` grep 0 `scale(`). The de-fork is real. HELD.

## SECONDARY HARDENINGS (do not change the verdict, but the gate should add them)

- **G11 cross-engine must paint the (C) wrapper case specifically** — assert no `position:fixed`
  overlay is a descendant of `[data-stage-wrapper]`, and assert WebKit text-edge sharpness on the
  receded page (the filter-rasterization soften) is within tolerance, since that is the Safari-
  specific failure mode the current gate does not probe.
- **`.glass-capsule-hover` does not exist in src (grep 0)** — §5 and §0 cite it as a "tabs GOLDEN
  DEPEND," but the prerequisite list must name it as UN-MINTED (not "ready"), else `.overlay-trigger`
  composes a non-existent class and the ready-state is silently absent (the stale-binding class of
  bug — a no-op that vue-tsc + units miss, only paint catches).
- **Coupling (D) one-shot fence is asserted but not MEASURED.** G11 says "no per-frame re-blur"
  but the only `immersive` consumer (Command) ramps `backdrop-filter` radius on `--stage-t` —
  which, if `--stage-t` is driven by the snap-spring PER FRAME, IS a per-frame backdrop re-blur on
  the scrim. The golden says "one-shot over the open window" for the modal flip, but for the
  drawer-driven case `--stage-t` moves every frame. The gate must assert that `immersive` is
  gated to the MODAL-FLIP path only (0→1 one-shot), NEVER the drawer's per-frame scalar — else
  Safari pays a full-viewport backdrop re-blur every animation frame (the §L7 cardinal Safari sin).

## BOTTOM LINE

SURVIVES. The `--stage-t` spine, the element-level freeze, the warm scrim, the a11y/PRM carve,
the de-fork of `shouldScaleBackground`, and the honest born-RED are all FIT and live-verified.
But the golden must (1) **kill the `filter` on `[data-stage-wrapper]` and forbid fixed-overlay
descendants** (Ref-1, the real Safari+Chrome break), (2) **add a `--glass-bg-overlay` fallback to
the §3 panel fill + correct the "stands alone" overclaim** (Ref-2, the transparent-panel ordering
trap), and (3) gate the `immersive` blur to the one-shot modal flip only (the per-frame Safari
backdrop-re-blur sin). With those three, it is shippable; without #1 and #2 it regresses Safari
text + can ship a transparent menu.
