# Adversarial Challenge #1 — GooBlob GOLDEN (CORRECTNESS / DEFT-INTEGRATION / KISS)

Lens: correctness, deft-integration (union vs bolt-on/fork/legacy), KISS/DRY, born-RED
truth, overfit/hardcoded refs, Chrome+Safari fidelity, precept compliance. Default REFUTED.

Verdict: **SURVIVES with a material hardening required.** The DESIGN (a colony that splits +
a droplet that lenses, both as a deft union over the existing engine, no fork, no 9th atom)
is correct and idiomatic — every source-truth claim grepped on HEAD holds. But the de-risking
SPIKE over-claims, and Move B is declared BUILD on an unmeasured budget the wave itself gates.
Two refutations LAND; neither kills the design, both demand concrete spec hardening.

## R1 (TOP — LANDS) — the spike's "lean-safe" PASS is an artifact of an under-modeled field; the real metric has ~0.009 of headroom, the spike measures none of the load

The spike (`fission-topology-spike.mjs`) reports `max lean centroid 0.0127 (ceiling 0.10) →
LEAN-SAFE` and concludes "the headline mechanism is de-risked." This is the load-bearing
de-risk for the born-RED Gate 1 / Move A. It does NOT hold:

1. **The spike omits every term that drives the REAL lean.** `grep -E "pert|wobble|pointer|
   flick|arousal|mood"` over the spike = **0 matches.** The spike's orbit is a clean static
   ellipse (`orbitPos(angle, r, ecc)` → `cos·rx, sin·ry`). The REAL `orbitPos`
   (`useBlobSatellites.ts:105-118`) adds `wobble` (two detuned sines), `pertX/pertYAmp`
   perturbation, mood `orbitSpeedScale/wobbleScale`, AND the renderer applies a pointer-flick
   lean on top. The `proof:blob-render` ceiling is measured against ALL of that.

2. **The real metric is already at the edge.** `blob-render.spec.ts:158-169`:
   `CENTROID_SHIFT_MAX = 0.1` with the comment "calm lean ≈0.075, +satellite phase noise
   **peaks ≈0.091** when a satellite sits on the leaned side at flick-time (a boundary FLAKE
   right on the prior 0.09 ceiling, confirmed across repeated runs). 0.10 admits the…". So the
   SHIPPED creature already lives at 0.091 against a 0.10 ceiling — **~0.009 of headroom.**
   The spike measures a static field at 0.0127 and declares a 0.087 margin that DOES NOT EXIST
   in the engine the gate runs against.

3. **The spike's geometry is LOUDER than the spec, hiding the problem further.** GOLDEN §2
   Move A.1 says `orbitRadius 0.17 → ~0.30`. But the spike's fission excursion reaches
   `FISSION_APEX = 0.40` (line 158), not 0.30 — a satellite pulled to 0.40·POS_SCALE off-center
   is MORE mass on the leaned side than 0.30, yet the spike still reports 0.0127 because it has
   no wobble/pointer/mood stack underneath. The de-risked geometry is both off-spec (0.40≠0.30)
   AND measured on a field missing the terms that put the real metric near its ceiling.

**Why it still survives:** the TOPOLOGY claim (HEAD always-1 born-RED, GOLDEN 2↔1↔2) is sound
and is the spike's real contribution — a static smin field IS sufficient to prove connectivity
changes, and that result is robust to the omitted terms (wobble/pert only jitter the neck, they
don't reconnect a snapped bead). What is UNPROVEN is the lean co-assert, which is exactly the
AZ.W-BLOB-STUDIO D2 trap the GOLDEN §2 Move A.4 claims to "sidestep entirely." The claim that
geometry-driven necking is lean-safe is asserted, not de-risked.

**Hardening:** the spike MUST integrate the real lean stack before claiming lean-safe — port
the `wobble + pertX/pertY + a synthetic pointer-flick toward the fissioning satellite's apex
side` into the centroid sweep, sweep at the SPEC orbit 0.30 (not 0.40), and report the peak with
the satellite-on-leaned-side phase aligned to flick-time (the documented 0.091 worst case). If
peak ≥ 0.10, Move A.4's "lean-safe at rest" is false and the apex/cadence must be re-tuned
DOWN against the real worst case, OR the gate's lean co-assert must run on a PRM-frozen/pointer-
idle frame only (and the spec must say so). Either way, "de-risked" is premature until the lean
is measured on the real field, not a static ellipse.

## R2 (LANDS) — Move B is declared "DE-PARK → BUILD" on a frame budget that is NOT measured; the wave it cites gates it CONDITIONAL and the spike explicitly skips it

GOLDEN §6 says `BD.W-GOOBLOB-SQUIRCLE-REFRACT` arm 2 (the `uBackdrop` lens) is
"**DE-PARK → BUILD (Move B)**". But the wave's own ledger (`BD.W-GOOBLOB-SQUIRCLE-REFRACT.md`
§3a, §"Decisions") says arm 2 is **CONDITIONAL on the frame budget**: "IF it clears the budget…
IF the budget bites: arm 1 ships, arm 2 re-stamps HELD with the recorded budget number." Its Q3
gate requires "a recorded frame-budget-clears assertion (the refraction ships ONLY with a
recorded budget number under the live ceiling)."

GOLDEN §9 admits "Move B's lens is lower-risk… the spike de-risks the headline [Move A]" — i.e.
the budget that gates Move B is **never measured by the spike or the GOLDEN.** The wave's
language is "re-test rather than re-stamp HELD" — re-test, an OPEN action, not a cleared gate.
The GOLDEN converts a conditional ("BUILD iff budget clears") into an unconditional ("BUILD")
without producing the number. The Snell refraction adds a `sampler2D`/`texture_2d` fetch +
`refract()` + a ≤1px 3-IOR chromatic dispersion (3 extra samples at the rim) per fragment over
the full metaball field — that IS a per-fragment cost increase, contradicting §2 Move B's "adds
NO new per-pixel loop" (true for the shadow march, false for the sample/dispersion math).

**Why it survives:** the design of Move B is correct and idiomatic (portable WebGL2 sampler, no
`backdrop-filter:url`, glass-ui-produced texture not a DOM read, twin-byte-faithful) — the gap
is process, not architecture. The lens is buildable; the spec just can't assert it WILL build
without the budget.

**Hardening:** the GOLDEN must either (a) restate Move B as CONDITIONAL-on-measured-budget
matching the wave (BUILD iff the recorded `/substrates/blob` frame-budget number clears the live
ceiling WITH the sampler + dispersion present; else squircle-only ships and the lens terminal-
HOLDs), or (b) produce the budget measurement as a second spike (a GPU paint on the real route
with the sampler armed) before claiming BUILD. The §2 Move B "no new per-pixel loop" claim must
be corrected to "no new MARCH loop; +1 backdrop sample +2 dispersion samples at the rim, budget-
gated." Until a number exists, Move B is HELD-pending, not BUILD.

## Net

The DESIGN survives the correctness/integration/KISS lens intact — it is a genuine deft union,
born-RED is real, every ref checks out, no legacy. What FAILS is the EVIDENCE: the spike
over-claims "lean-safe / de-risked" on a field stripped of the very terms that put the real
metric at 0.091/0.10 (R1), and Move B is upgraded from CONDITIONAL to BUILD without the budget
number its own wave demands (R2). Both are honesty/rigor gaps in the de-risk, not architectural
breaks. Harden the spike to the real lean stack at the spec orbit, and restate Move B as
budget-gated (or measure it). With those two, the golden is shippable.
