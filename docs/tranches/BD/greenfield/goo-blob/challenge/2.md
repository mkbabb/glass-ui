## R1 (LOAD-BEARING) — Move A fights the EXISTING `orbitWiden` bridge; the spike doesn't model it, so the gate is NOT validated where it matters

The GOLDEN's whole headline is "re-base orbit `0.17→0.30` so a satellite SNAPS off through
a thinning neck." But the repo ALREADY has a mechanism whose entire job is to PREVENT that
snap at exactly orbit 0.30 — `orbitWiden` (BA.W-GOO-REDRESS, `uploadBlobUniforms.ts:246-259`
+ the WGPU twin `uniformBridgeWGPU.ts:129-144`):

```
const worstOrbitDist = cGeo.orbitRadius * 1.2 * (1 + cGeo.eccentricity);
const bridgeGap = Math.max(0, worstOrbitDist - satR - bodyRadius);
const orbitWiden = Math.min(1.25, 1 + bridgeGap / nominalBand);  // WIDENS smin to keep the neck
gl.uniform1f(U.uSmoothK, nominalBand * orbitWiden * POS_SCALE);
```

The in-source comment's worked example is the GOLDEN's exact proposed geometry:
> "the studio Calm bead: orbit 0.30, near-edge ≈ 0.274 BEYOND body 0.22 → gap ≈ 0.054 …
> the band only needs a gentle worst-case lift … so the smin neck persists across the WHOLE
> orbit envelope, not only the nominal frame."

Computed on HEAD at orbit 0.30 / ecc 0.05 / body 0.22 / satR 0.082 / band 0.05:
`worstOrbitDist=0.378`, `bridgeGap=0.076`, uncapped widen `2.52`, **capped 1.25**. So the
SHIPPED renderer answers orbit-0.30 by inflating the smin band 25% to GLUE the neck — the
precise opposite of "the gap exceeds the smin reach so it SNAPS."

The two are in direct mechanical conflict, and the GOLDEN never names `orbitWiden` once. The
DELTA-ASSAY (§6) reconciles against waves but not against this live uniform packer. Move A
cannot just "retire `ORBIT_RANDOM_BASE`" — it must ALSO retire/scope `orbitWiden`, and that
retirement re-opens the R8-07 "instantaneous fully-detached disc with no gooey neck" failure
that BA.W-GOO-REDRESS was created to cure. The GOLDEN's "single-fissioner + bounded-apex" is
asserted as a "cheaper guard" but is never shown to subsume R8-07 across the per-satellite
×0.8–1.2 random `baseR` envelope that `orbitWiden` covers.

**Why the spike does NOT catch this:** the spike (`fission-topology-spike.mjs:135`) hardcodes
`const k = SMOOTH_K * POS_SCALE` — a FIXED band, no `orbitWiden`, no `bridgeGap`. It models a
world where the band never widens. So it "VALIDATES" a 2→1→2 split that the real shader would
GLUE shut at the same geometry (or, with `orbitWiden` retired, would snap as a hard-edged disc
without the gooey neck the acceptance bar §7.1 demands "~⅓ satellite-diameter waist"). The
born-RED/born-GREEN claim is proven against a CPU strawman, not the shipping field. **The gate
is overfit to a model that omits the one mechanism it collides with.**

**Hardening:** (a) the spike MUST import/replicate `orbitWiden` from `uploadBlobUniforms.ts`
and re-run both scenarios — show HEAD-with-widen is still born-1 and GOLDEN-with-widen still
produces a CLEAN-NECKED 2→1→2 (a thinning waist, not a hard disc). (b) The GOLDEN MUST name
`orbitWiden` in the DELTA-ASSAY and specify its disposition: either phase-scope it (widen only
during `merging`, drop to nominal during `fissioning` so the neck CAN snap) or retire it and
PROVE the single-fissioner guard re-prevents R8-07 across the random `baseR` span. Without
this, Move A is not "deftly integrable" — it is a bolt-on that silently contradicts a live,
gated, dual-backend uniform.

## R2 (STRONG) — Move B's `uBackdrop` is NOT compositor-only and re-introduces a Safari color-management gap the GOLDEN waves away

The GOLDEN repeatedly claims Move B is "compositor-only … no `backdrop-filter:url`, no Metal
artifact … Safari's WebGL2 runs it identically." Two of those are unearned:

1. **It is NOT compositor-only.** It is a per-fragment `refract()` + a `texture_2d`/`sampler2D`
   sample + chromatic dispersion (R/G/B at three IORs) inside the metaball fragment. That is
   GPU shader work, not the CSS compositor. Calling it "compositor-only" (§title framing, §4)
   conflates "no DOM `backdrop-filter`" with "compositor-only." Fine as shader work — but then
   the PERF claim "adds NO new per-pixel loop" is only true for the shadow march; the dome
   pixels now pay a dependent texture fetch + 3× dispersion taps + a `refract`. On the
   oversized 160% canvas this is real fill cost the GOLDEN never budgets. No fps/frame-time
   number, no `uMaxReach` bounding-discard interaction analysis (the dispersion fringe expands
   the painted reach — does the existing `maxReach` pad clip it? unproven).

2. **The Safari color gap is asserted, not closed.** "Safari's WebGL2 runs it identically" is
   exactly the kind of claim the MEMORY ledger flags (`feedback_live_pi_oklab_paint_arm`,
   `BD.W-VIZ-PARITY-METAL`). WebKit's WebGL2 texture upload + `texImage2D` colorspace handling
   (the `unpackColorSpace`/`drawingBufferColorSpace` defaults differ from Chrome/ANGLE), and
   Metal's fp16 default precision on intermediate `refract`/dispersion math, are precisely
   where a backdrop SAMPLE drifts. The GOLDEN leans on "OKLab-aware shared `/color` leaf
   matrices" for the sample but the BACKDROP TEXTURE itself (the Aurora FBO or the baked
   gradient strip) carries its own colorspace tag that Safari and ANGLE upload differently.
   The paired-engine ΔE is named but the threshold is the OLD `BD.W-VIZ-PARITY-METAL` one
   tuned for the self-lit dome — a transmissive backdrop-bleed has a NEW, larger drift surface
   (the displaced sample crosses hue gradients, amplifying any per-engine sRGB/linear mismatch).

**Hardening:** (a) re-label Move B as "GPU-shader, no-DOM-backdrop" not "compositor-only," and
add an explicit fill-cost budget (dependent-fetch + 3 dispersion taps × dome-pixel count) with
a measured frame-time delta on the live route, both backends. (b) Pin the backdrop texture's
colorspace explicitly (`texImage2D` with a known internal format + `linearToSrgb` parity on
the SAMPLE not just the core) and add a DEDICATED paired-engine ΔE gate for the bleed-through
ring with a threshold re-derived for transmissive drift — do NOT reuse the self-lit dome
threshold. (c) Prove the dispersion fringe stays inside `uMaxReach` or widen the pad.

## R3 (STRONG) — the born-RED gate's "DEFAULT creature splits" premise re-bases a SHIPPED, gated default, which is a backwards-compat/scope break disguised as a greenfield edit

Gate 1 asserts the split must happen on `variant:"blob"` DEFAULT with "no knob dial." To make
that born-GREEN, the GOLDEN re-bases `BLOB_CONFIG_DEFAULTS.geometry.orbitRadius 0.17→0.30`.
But 0.17 is not arbitrary — it is the value `proof:blob-render` / `proof:blob-studio` /
`proof:blob-page` (the four-side containment + calm-lean ceiling) are all gated against, and
the in-source comments (`uploadBlobUniforms.ts:231-235`) explicitly cite "orbit 0.17, near-edge
≈ 0.132 INSIDE body 0.22 → gap 0, factor 1.0, the band BYTE-IDENTICAL so the proof:blob-render
body lean does not move." Re-basing the default to 0.30:
- moves the gated body lean (the comment's whole invariant),
- changes the painted footprint (`maxReach` grows — four-side containment must be re-solved,
  the GOLDEN asserts it "clears the edge" via `0.30*0.625=0.1875` but that IGNORES the +smin
  band +FBM +pulse terms summed in the real `maxReach` at line 298-304; at orbit 0.30 the
  WIDENED band alone adds materially),
- and is a behavioral break for every EXISTING consumer of `<GooBlob variant="blob">` (the
  slides decks, the dock, AV backgrounds) who now gets a splitting colony instead of a calm
  merged accent. The README §273 literally instructs "Keep it calm by default … hold
  `orbitRadius` small." The GOLDEN inverts the shipped contract.

"No backwards compat / clean break" (MEMORY) licenses retiring legacy *tokens* — it does NOT
license silently changing what every live consumer renders without an opt-out atom. The
calmness was a deliberate, gated property. The GOLDEN should make fission an OPT-IN register
(a new `lifecycle:"colony"` or a `fissionAmp` atom defaulting to the calm floor) so the
DEFAULT stays gate-faithful and calm — otherwise Gate 1's "no knob dial" is not a born-RED
seam, it is a regression dressed as a feature.

**Hardening:** make the default calm (fission floor ≈ 0), gate the SPLIT on an explicit atom
(or the `material=mercury`/`colony` register), and re-point Gate 1 at that register — born-RED
because the register doesn't exist yet, not because you mutated the shipped default. Re-solve
and RE-RECORD `proof:blob-render`/`-studio`/`-page` for whatever the new default is, with the
FULL `maxReach` terms, not the `0.30*0.625` back-of-envelope.

## R4 (MEDIUM) — a11y/PRM: the cel-ink `drop-shadow()` chain + `prefers-reduced-transparency` carve are under-specified and risk the gray-glass / perf floors

- The "third bold offset `drop-shadow()` rung following the metaball silhouette" (§3) is a CSS
  filter on the canvas element. A `drop-shadow()` chain over a per-frame-changing WebGL canvas
  forces the compositor to re-rasterize the shadow EVERY frame from the alpha — on Safari this
  is a known jank source (filter on an animating canvas defeats layer caching). The GOLDEN
  says "PRM → static cast" but says nothing about the NON-PRM per-frame cost of a live
  `drop-shadow()` tracking a 60fps-mutating silhouette. This can dwarf the shader savings.
- `prefers-reduced-transparency` → "does not touch the opaque cel-ink … the lens transmission
  is decorative." But at `material=mercury` the BODY goes transmissive (§Move B.3 "at
  material=1 the body goes transmissive"). Under reduced-transparency a transmissive body that
  drops its backdrop bleed falls back to… what? If it falls back to the self-lit core that's
  fine, but if the bleed was carrying the warm color, dropping it risks the body reading GRAY
  (the BA.W-NO-GRAY floor). Unspecified — and gray-glass is the cardinal precept violation.

**Hardening:** (a) specify the cel-ink cast as a layer-cached approximation (e.g. a blurred
duplicate at the silhouette's CONVEX bound, or a CSS `filter` only on a static low-freq proxy)
OR drop it on non-PRM if it can't hold frame-time; measure it. (b) State the explicit
reduced-transparency fallback for `material=mercury` and PROVE it stays ≥0.62 warm-L (the
warm-cream floor), not gray.

## R5 (MEDIUM) — the spike's smin model diverges from the shader, weakening "born-RED by construction"

`fission-topology-spike.mjs` claims to mirror `sminCircularG`, but: (a) it omits `orbitWiden`
(R1); (b) it uses `ECC=0.05` and `SAT_R=0.082` but a FIXED 3-satellite ring with hand-authored
`FISSION_APEX=0.40`/`FISSION_PERI=0.18` excursion (lines 158-160) that does NOT come from the
real `useBlobSatellites` phase machine (orbiting→merging→absorbed→emerging, lines 220-285) —
the real machine necks via `0.08/dist` center-ward targets, NOT a `sin(phase)` radius
modulation. So the spike validates a kinematic that the GOLDEN has NOT yet implemented in the
phase machine. "Byte-identical on both backends BY CONSTRUCTION" (§Move A) is true of
`resolveFrame`, but the spike proves the WRONG kinematic — it proves a hypothetical sine
excursion splits, not that the proposed `emerging→fissioning` repurpose splits. The two could
behave differently (the `0.08/dist` neck-in target is a center-ward pull, the opposite of a
bud-OUT).

**Hardening:** rewrite the spike to drive the ACTUAL phase machine (import or faithfully port
`useBlobSatellites` orbiting/merging/emerging with the proposed `fissioning` beat) so the
born-RED/GREEN delta is over the real kinematic + the real `orbitWiden` band — then the
"by construction" claim is earned.

## Verdict

**SURVIVES: NO.** The design's central thesis is sound (a colony that splits + a lens that
drinks the warm field is the right read, and the lens/split orthogonality is genuinely elegant),
but it does NOT survive intact: R1 is a true refutation — Move A mechanically contradicts the
live, dual-backend, gated `orbitWiden` bridge whose in-source calibration example IS the
GOLDEN's proposed geometry, and the validating spike omits that mechanism, so the born-RED gate
is proven against a strawman. R2 and R3 compound it (unearned "compositor-only"/Safari-parity
claims; a shipped-default re-base that breaks the calm contract and the gated lean). The
hardenings are concrete and the design is recoverable, but as written the spike's "VALIDATES"
is not trustworthy.
