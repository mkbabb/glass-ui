## REFUTATIONS (the gestalt gaps that DON'T kill it but must harden)

### R1 (top) — "ONE fissioning satellite per cycle" risks reading as a TWITCH, not a colony — the cartoon-punch is under-specified at the apex

The headline is "a living **colony** of warm mercury that necks, SPLITS, snaps." But Move A §3
bounds it to **at most ONE** fissioning satellite per cycle, on a SLOW φ-detuned clock (§3:
"the fission clock is SLOW"), with the other satellites in the bonded breath. A slow,
once-per-long-cycle, single-bead pinch over a 6–16s phase duration (`phaseDuration: 6000 +
rng()*10000`, useBlobSatellites.ts:57) reads as a **rare twitch on an otherwise-static lump**,
NOT a "living colony" and NOT the 1940s-technicolor cartoon-PUNCH the binding law demands
(anticipation→exaggeration→snap→follow-through as a LEGIBLE beat). The spike's own cadence
(`0.5+0.5*sin(phase)` over 48 frames) is a smooth sine — there is no **snap velocity profile**,
no anticipation squash modeled, no exaggeration overshoot. A pinch that eases out on a pure
sine is BUTTER, which §7.4 explicitly forbids ("never butter"). The gestalt risk: the user
sees a lump that occasionally grows a slow nub and reabsorbs it — fidelity-FAIL against
"PERFECT meatballing" read as a user.

**Hardening:** the pinch-off MUST carry an authored cartoon velocity curve, not a sine. (i)
Specify the apex beat as **anticipation (≥1 frame of inward squash toward the bud point) →
fast snap (the neck thins past smin reach in a SHORT window, not gradually) → overshoot →
damped recoil ring** — and make this a NAMED easing in `easing.ts` (DRY — reuse, the file
exists), not an ad-hoc sine. (ii) Add a SECOND acceptance criterion to Gate 1: not just
"2→1→2 topology EXISTS" but "the neck-width time-derivative at the snap frame exceeds a
threshold" (the SNAP is fast, the merge-in can be slow) — a born-RED *velocity* assert, so a
slow butter-morph that technically reaches 2 components still REDS. (iii) Reconsider the
single-fissioner cap: allow the slow bonded-breath satellites to also visibly **neck to a
waist** (not pinch) so the silhouette is perpetually alive at MULTIPLE necks — the "colony"
read — with only the apex one fully snapping. The spike already shows lean stays at 0.0127
with one fissioner at apex 0.40; there is ample lean headroom (ceiling 0.10) to let the
others breathe louder.

### R2 — the MERCURY material read has no live evidence it clears "wet, not clay"; the squircle+lens is specced but the WET MENISCUS / travelling catch-light is hand-waved

Move B promises "catches a travelling crown specular, brightens the necking waist (wet
meniscus)." But the live HEAD surface is competent matte-clay (the golden admits this:
"static Blinn-Phong lobe… no travelling catch-light, no neck-bright meniscus" — WEAK). The
golden's Move B §2 details the squircle dome-Z + Snell displacement + composite math
RIGOROUSLY, but the **wet** signature — the travelling specular and the neck-bright meniscus —
gets one clause each with NO mechanism. The lens (refracted backdrop) is a TRANSMISSION read;
wetness is a SPECULAR/Fresnel-meniscus read. They are different physics. A transmissive lens
over a flat warm strip can still read as a **frosted/clay lens** (matte transmission) if the
crown specular and the waist-meniscus aren't authored. The gestalt bar (§7.2) explicitly
requires "the crown catches a travelling specular, the necking waist glows wet" — that is
UNDER-MECHANIZED relative to the lens, which is the spec's gravity well.

**Hardening:** lift the wet read to first-class mechanism parity with the lens. (i) The
travelling crown specular: a `uLightDir`-independent moving highlight = a small phase-driven
offset on the Blinn-Phong half-vector OR a screen-space anisotropic streak that tracks
`morphT`/time — specify which, in both backends. (ii) The neck meniscus: the smin neck region
(where the field gradient magnitude is LOW — the flat waist) should BRIGHTEN — a
`smoothstep(neck-gradient)`-gated highlight add, riding the already-computed `fieldGrad`
(metaball.frag.ts:352), no new derivative. (iii) Add to Gate 2 a wet-witness: sample the crown
ring across two frames and assert the specular CENTROID MOVES (travelling), and sample the
neck waist and assert it is BRIGHTER than the body interior — so a static matte lens REDS even
if transmission passes.

### R3 — §3's "COLORFUL FIELD behind glass" is unmet by the baked warm-cream radial strip; the lens may have nothing worth lensing

Binding law §3: glass morphism is "a COLORFUL FIELD behind glass + a defined edge." Move B §1
offers two backdrops: the route's `<Aurora>` FBO "when present," OR — when absent — "a cheap
baked warm-cream radial-gradient strip (the warm `paletteStops` ramp)." I verified live: the
`/substrates/blob` stage has NO aurora behind the blob (the field behind the lump is a flat
muted warm-taupe, the canvas's own clear). So on the SHIPPED default route the lens samples a
**warm-cream gradient lensing a warm-cream droplet** — cream-through-cream. The refraction
displacement of a near-uniform low-chroma ramp produces a near-INVISIBLE lens (ΔE tiny), which
both (a) makes the §7.2 "warm field bends and pools at the rim" un-seeable to a user, and (b)
makes Gate 2's "backdrop hue bleeds through displaced, measurably ≠ self-lit core" marginal —
a cream strip's local hue barely differs from the cream core, so the born-RED bleed-ΔE and the
GREEN bleed-ΔE could be within noise. The "colourful field" precept is NOT satisfied by a
warm-cream-on-warm-cream gradient.

**Hardening:** (i) Make the default backdrop a **colourful field with real chroma variation
across the dome footprint** — not a flat warm ramp but the warm `paletteStops` spread into a
genuinely varied field (multi-stop, with the cool/warm chroma spread the §3 "defined edge"
needs), OR mandate the aurora-present hero as the GATE configuration (judge the lens where
there IS a colourful field to bend). (ii) Gate 2 must run over a backdrop with a KNOWN chroma
GRADIENT under the rim so the Snell displacement produces a measurable hue-SHIFT (the in-rim
hue tracks a backdrop position OFFSET, not just the backdrop's flat hue) — assert the
displaced sample differs from the UN-displaced sample at the same pixel, isolating the lens
from a mere alpha-composite. (iii) Add the §3 "defined edge" assert: the lens must have a
crisp rim caustic (the rim-concentrated squircle curvature), not a soft fade — a rim
gradient-magnitude floor.
