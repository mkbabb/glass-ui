# CHALLENGE 2 — CROSS-ENGINE / SAFARI / PERF lens vs the TIMELINE GOLDEN

Verdict: **DOES NOT SURVIVE INTACT.** The GOLDEN *design prose* is sound and
deftly-integrating; but the shipped **prototype** (`golden/timeline-golden.html`)
and the **born-RED gate** (`golden/timeline-pi.mjs`) contradict the design's own
binding Safari/compositor laws in five concrete, reproducible ways. These are not
nitpicks — three of them mean the prototype "de-risks" a mechanism that, as
authored, **does not work in Safari** (R-1), **does not form the metaball it
advertises** (R-2), and **rides the exact layout-jank path §6 forbids** (R-3),
while the gate is **blind to all three** (R-4). The design survives; the
de-risking artefact does not, so the "proven" claim is false until hardened.

Measured live on `file://…/golden/timeline-golden.html` (chrome-devtools-mcp) +
read against `src/styles/dock/fission-bridge.css` (the shipped goo pattern).

---

## R-1 (TOP REFUTATION) — `filter:url()` + `backdrop-filter` ON THE SAME ELEMENT: the head's glass DIES in Safari (and is wrong in Chrome)

**Live-measured** with goo ON, on `.scrub-head`:
```
head_filter:          url("#tl-goo")
head_backdropFilter:  blur(14px) saturate(1.6)
head_BOTH_filter_and_backdrop: true   ← both on the SAME element
```

The shipped `DockGooFilter` pattern (`fission-bridge.css` ll.103-155) is
**scrupulously** built to NEVER do this: the goo `filter:url(#dock-fission-goo)`
is applied to the **`.dock-fission-bridge` layer, which carries NO
`backdrop-filter`** — the neck/island alpha is goo-merged on a layer separate
from any backdrop sampling. The whole memory-line "backdrop-filter:url trap" and
the §7 binding ("the goo-neck is `filter:url()` … NEVER `backdrop-filter:url`")
is honoured by the shipped engine precisely *because* the two never coexist on
one element.

The GOLDEN prototype breaks that discipline: `.scrub-head` carries
`backdrop-filter: var(--glass-blur-floating)` (html l.124) AND, when `:goo` is
on, `filter: url(#tl-goo)` (l.139). Stacking a regular `filter` and a
`backdrop-filter` on the same box:
- **Safari/WebKit**: `filter` establishes a new stacking/clip context that
  interacts badly with `backdrop-filter` sampling — WebKit frequently drops the
  backdrop sample or clips it to the filter region, so the head loses its glass
  transmission (it reads as a flat blurred blob, the gaussian eats the rim+specular).
  This is the *opposite* of the "warm transmissive lozenge forward of the rail."
- **Chrome**: the `feGaussianBlur` in `#tl-goo` blurs the head's *own* painted
  result (including its backdrop-filtered glass), softening the keyed rim and the
  `vSpecular` catch into mush — the very "defined lit shape" R3 demands.

**The design text is innocent** (§4/§7 keep them separate in spirit), but the
artefact that is supposed to *prove* Safari-safety demonstrates the trap. A
reviewer running this in Safari sees a broken head and (wrongly) blames the
concept.

**HARDENING:** mirror the shipped fission topology EXACTLY. The goo filter must
live on a **dedicated neck+head sandwich layer that carries NO backdrop-filter**
— an opaque-alpha sandwich (the shipped `DockGooFilter` "isolation: isolate" +
no-backdrop bridge). The glass (`backdrop-filter`) head and the goo-merge layer
are **two different elements**; the glass head paints crisp, the goo layer
(aria-hidden, solid-alpha shapes) merges underneath. Gate addition: a born-RED
assert that **no element in the timeline path carries both `filter:url()` and
`backdrop-filter` simultaneously** (a static DOM scan, both modes, both engines).

## R-2 — the advertised head↔fill GOO-NECK CANNOT FORM: two sibling elements each get their OWN per-element filter

**Live-measured:**
```
fill_share_parent_with_head: true   (both siblings under #scrub)
.scrub.is-goo .scrub-fill, .scrub.is-goo .scrub-head { filter: url(#tl-goo); }  (html l.139)
```

The `:goo` rule puts `filter:url(#tl-goo)` on the **head** AND on the **fill** as
**two separate sibling elements**. A CSS `filter` is applied **per element**, to
that element's own rendering, in that element's own filter region. Two distinct
elements each independently filtered **do not share a filter pass** — the
`feGaussianBlur`+`feColorMatrix` threshold runs inside the head's box and,
separately, inside the fill's box. **They never see each other's alpha**, so the
metaball "neck thins to a waist between head and fill, snaps, bubbles back"
(§4 OPT-IN goo-neck) **physically cannot happen** with this markup.

This is exactly why the shipped `DockGooFilter` wraps the **neck AND the island
as co-children of ONE filtered host** (`.dock-fission-bridge` — the necks and the
flying island are *inside* the single filtered element). The GOLDEN prototype
fails to reproduce that — so the boldest "audacious" mechanism it claims to
de-risk is, as authored, a no-op (each shape just gets its corners rounded by its
own blur+threshold; no inter-shape merge). §11's "Proves: … Safari-safe goo" is
unearned.

**HARDENING:** the head and the neck filament and the fill-leading-edge that
participate in the merge must be **co-children of ONE `filter:url()` host** (the
shipped `DockGooFilter` topology — neck+head as solid-alpha shapes in one
filtered, backdrop-filter-free wrapper). The glass head can ride *above* that
merge layer as a separate crisp element. Update §4 to make the single-host
requirement explicit, and have the prototype demonstrate a real waist (the π must
sample the alpha bridge at the midpoint, as the shipped fission π already does —
not merely toggle a class).

## R-3 — the head position rides `left:%` and the fill rides `width:%` EVERY FRAME: the precise layout-jank path §6 forbids (Safari double-hit)

**Source-confirmed** (`timeline-golden.html` ll.329-331, in `frame()` every RAF):
```js
head.style.left  = (p*100)+'%';   // position via LEFT — a layout property
fill.style.width = (f*100)+'%';   // fill via WIDTH — a layout property
```
plus `.scrub-fill { will-change: width; }` (l.113) is **permanent**, never gated.

GOLDEN §6/§0 binding, verbatim: *"compositor `transform`/`clip` for the scrub
(NEVER `left`/`width` for the head — Safari composites transform, not left; the
fill width is the one non-compositor channel, **gated to `will-change` only
during `[data-scrubbing]`, dropped at rest**)."* And §4: position tracks a spring
"NOT a `style.left` teleport."

The prototype does the forbidden thing on **both** channels, **every frame,
unconditionally**:
- head position is `left:%` (layout/paint), not a `translateX`. The transform on
  the head holds **only the scale** (`translate(-50%,-50%) scale(sx,sy)`), so the
  position is genuinely off the compositor — the worst case on WebKit, which the
  GOLDEN explicitly calls out.
- fill `width:%` runs every frame with a **permanent** `will-change: width` (a
  standing compositor-layer cost even at rest — the opposite of the §6 "dropped
  at rest" gate).

On a real scrub-drag this is a per-frame relayout of the rail's children in
Safari — the stiff-jank the whole RE-INVENT was meant to cure. The design says
the right thing; the artefact ships the anti-pattern.

**HARDENING:** head position = `transform: translateX(var(--t))` (compositor),
keeping scale in the SAME transform (`translateX() scale()`); fill = a
`transform: scaleX()` on a pinned full-width layer **or** a `clip-path`
inset animation, with `will-change` set on `[data-scrubbing]` and **removed on
pointerup**. Re-author `frame()` to write a single `--t`/`--fill-t` custom prop
the transform reads, never `style.left`/`style.width`.

## R-4 — the BORN-RED GATE is BLIND to R-1/R-2/R-3: R4 reads only the scale matrix, so a non-compositor, no-goo, double-filter implementation passes GREEN

**Source-confirmed** (`timeline-pi.mjs` ll.117-120):
```js
const cs = getComputedStyle(head).transform;   // matrix(a,b,c,d,e,f)
const m  = cs.match(/matrix\(([^)]+)\)/);
sx = Math.hypot(v[0],v[1]); sy = Math.hypot(v[2],v[3]);
```
R4 asserts only `sx≠sy` (squish) and `sx·sy≈1` (vol-preserving). It **never
asserts** (a) that position is on `transform` (it would pass a `left:%`
implementation — indeed the prototype's own `e`/`f` translate components are
ignored; the head could teleport via `left` and R4 is happy); (b) that no element
carries `filter`+`backdrop-filter` together; (c) that the goo actually merges
(it only checks `seg gap > 0`, never samples a neck waist). So the gate is
**not actually born-RED against the failure modes that matter for this lens** —
it is born-RED only on the chroma/squish axes. A broken-in-Safari build is
GREEN by this π.

Worse, R1's composite sampler `sampleComposite()` **averages every pixel in the
rail's bbox** (mjs ll.66-68). For the scrubber, the bbox is dominated by the
blue `--scrub-fill` (`oklch(0.72 0.17 250)`, hue ≈ 250) and the head; the warm
rail is a thin sliver behind them. Averaging blue fill + warm rail yields a hue
that is **neither** — R1's "H ∈ [40,90] warm" assert can FALSE-FAIL on a
correctly-warm rail simply because the blue fill dominates the average, or
FALSE-PASS by luck. The sampler must mask to the **unfilled rail region**, not
the whole bbox. (This is the live-π-paint-arm lesson: sample the right pixels.)

**HARDENING:** add gate arms — R4b: assert the **translate** components of the
head matrix change across the frame-series (position is on `transform`, not
`left`); R4c: static scan that no `.timeline-*` element computes both
`filter !== none` (url) and `backdrop-filter !== none`; R4d (goo opt-in): sample
the alpha at the head↔fill midpoint and assert a connected bridge mid-flight then
a snap (the shipped fission π's waist assay). Fix R1 to sample the **rail-only**
strip (exclude the fill/head rects from the average), both engines.

## R-5 — NAIVE-ELLIPSOID / single-blob risk + the goo `feColorMatrix` is the dock's, not re-derived for a 26px bead

The §7 law: *"NO naive ellipsoid, a real blob↔meatball merge."* The prototype's
`#tl-goo` is `stdDeviation="5"` + `matrix … 19 -8` — a **copy of the dock
fission throat tuned for ~40px capsules over a wide bridge**. On a **26px** head
+ a thin fill edge, `stdDeviation=5` is ~20% of the head diameter: the threshold
either (a) swallows the small head into a rounded blob (a **naive ellipsoid** —
the exact precept violation) or (b) never reaches the fill to neck at all (R-2
compounds). The goo throat is geometry-relative and was **not re-derived for the
timeline's √φ bead scale**; it is an overfit lift of the dock constant.

Secondary on the same axis: the **head diameter is hardcoded `26px`** (html
l.121), OFF the √φ ladder the GOLDEN §5 demands ("head diameter … derive from `h`
by √φ steps"). Live: `h=18`, `h·√φ=22.89` (the scrub channel), yet `head=26` —
neither `18·√φ` nor `18·φ` (29.1). And **26 > 22.89**, so the head **bulges
proud of its own channel** — it does NOT "seat as a bead inside the strip"
(§5's "channel-height : head-diameter ≈ φ"). The proportion law is asserted in
prose and violated in the artefact. The √φ ladder is real for `seg`/`cont`/`rivet`
(measured `14.148 = 18/√φ` exactly), but the headline bead is the one element
left arbitrary.

**HARDENING:** derive the head diameter on the √φ ladder so it seats φ-inside the
scrub channel (`--timeline-head: calc(var(--timeline-h) / something)` such that
channel:head ≈ φ → head ≈ channel/φ ≈ 14px, or a deliberate proud-bead ratio
*chosen on the ladder*, not 26 by feel). Re-derive the goo `stdDeviation` +
threshold as a function of head diameter (geometry-relative throat), and gate that
the merged result is non-circular at the waist (no naive-ellipsoid) at the bead
scale — a paired-engine π, not the dock's constant reused.

## What SURVIVES (kept, per survival-of-the-fittest)

- The **core thesis** (one warm-glass `.timeline-rail`, three postures; field
  transmitted not painted; gray→warm via `--glass-bg-resting` over a field) is
  correct, DRY, and a true union — no parallel fork. The `geometry.ts` keep-verbatim
  and the a11y/PRM keep-verbatim are right.
- The **√φ ladder for rail/seg/cont/rivet** computes EXACTLY (`14.148 = 18/√φ`,
  `22.89 = 18·√φ`, ratio 1.272 measured) — fit, except the head (R-5).
- **R1/R2/R3/R5 of the design gate** are conceptually born-RED on the shipped
  routes (the gray-flat composite, field-count 0, the divergent substrates) —
  those bites are honest. The defect is in the **prototype + π implementation**,
  not the design's diagnosis.
- The **segmented gap** (2px, measured) and the **tinted-GLASS cel** (translucent,
  field bleeds up) correctly avoid the opaque `chart-*` precept violation.

## NET

The GOLDEN **design** survives this lens; the GOLDEN **de-risking prototype + π
gate do NOT** — they contradict the design's own Safari/compositor binding law
in three load-bearing ways (R-1 double-filter, R-2 no-merge, R-3 layout-jank)
and the gate is blind to all three (R-4), with an overfit goo + off-ladder head
(R-5). Because §10/§11 stake the acceptance on "the prototype de-risks BOTH so
the default is proven and the opt-in is real," and the prototype proves neither
in Safari, the "proven" claim FAILS until the five hardenings land. Re-author the
prototype to the shipped `DockGooFilter` topology (separate backdrop-free goo
host, single filtered wrapper for the merge, transform-only position, gated
will-change), re-derive the head on the √φ ladder + geometry-relative throat, and
add the R4b/R4c/R4d/rail-masked-R1 gate arms — then it survives.
