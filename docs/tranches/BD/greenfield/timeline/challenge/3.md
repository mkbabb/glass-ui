## REFUTATIONS (the scars)

### R-1 (LOAD-BEARING) — the de-risk prototype animates `left`, the exact channel §6 forbids
The GOLDEN §6 / §11 sells `golden/timeline-golden.html` as the proof that the liquid scrub is
"Safari-perfect, compositor-only": *"compositor `transform`/`clip` for the scrub (NEVER `left`/`width`
for the head — Safari composites transform, not left)."* The prototype does the OPPOSITE. Live
readback of `#head` mid-run:
```
head_inline_left : "30%"          ← JS sets head.style.left = (p*100)+'%' EVERY rAF frame
head_transform   : matrix(1,0,0,1,-13,-13)   ← only the -50% centering + squish; NOT the travel
```
The travel rides `left` (a layout/paint channel), the transform carries only the static center
offset and the squish scale. So the ONE artifact whose job is to prove the Safari-safe compositor
path proves the forbidden path instead. The spec's own §6 sentence is the indictment. **The
"de-risked" headline interaction is un-de-risked** — and on WebKit, per-frame `left` on a
`backdrop-filter` element is exactly where the jank lives.

**Hardening:** rebuild the prototype's position channel on `transform: translateX()` (the squish
multiplies into the same matrix: `translateX(p·trackW) translate(-50%,-50%) scale(sx,sy)`), drop
the per-frame `left` write entirely, and add a one-line assert to `timeline-pi.mjs` that the head's
computed `left` is CONSTANT across the drag frame-series (born-RED on the current prototype too).
The production wave must bind the same: travel = `translateX`, fill width = the lone gated layout
channel (`will-change:width` only under `[data-scrubbing]`, already specced — keep that).

### R-2 (LOAD-BEARING) — the gooed head is ALSO a backdrop-filter element; the real WebKit trap is unaddressed
§6 defends the goo as *"the shipped `DockGooFilter` `filter:url()` on the head+neck layer (an
opaque-alpha sandwich) — NEVER `backdrop-filter:url`."* True but evasive. Live readback:
```
head_backdrop : "blur(14px) saturate(1.6)"   ← the head HAS backdrop-filter
.scrub.is-goo .scrub-head { filter: url(#tl-goo); }   ← AND filter:url on the SAME element
```
Stacking an SVG `filter:url()` over an element that itself samples the backdrop (`backdrop-filter`)
is a distinct, well-known WebKit fragility (the filter graph + backdrop-sample interaction;
self-filtering a backdrop element commonly drops or mis-composites the backdrop on Safari). The
spec only inoculates against `backdrop-filter:url` — a strawman — and never against
`filter:url` ⊗ `backdrop-filter` on one node. The opt-in goo is sold as "real, proven" but its
load-bearing Safari risk was never tested (the webkit gate arm is an unrun sketch — see R-4).

**Hardening:** when `:goo` is on, the goo must be applied to a SEPARATE opaque sibling layer
(a solid-fill head proxy + neck on its own stacking context) — NOT the backdrop-filter head — so
`filter:url` never wraps a backdrop-sampler. The transmissive glass head and the goo metaball
become two coordinated layers (the proxy carries the silhouette, the glass head carries the
transmission). Gate it with a born-RED webkit-engine assert that the backdrop is still sampled
under the gooed head (composite C over the field with goo ON ≈ goo OFF).

### R-3 — the "ONE shared substrate" thesis is FAKED in the segmented variant (the one that needs it most)
The central gestalt claim (§0, §2, acceptance #1) is *"the three variants become the SAME capsule."*
The prototype's segmented rail is **explicitly nulled**:
```html
<div class="timeline-rail seg" id="seg"
     style="background:transparent;backdrop-filter:none;box-shadow:none;border:none">
```
i.e. `.timeline-rail` paints NOTHING for segmented; each cel re-mints its own
`backdrop-filter + border + box-shadow` glass. So segmented is NOT "the shared capsule with a
different interior" — it is N independent capsules with NO shared rail, the same parallel-fork
shape the GOLDEN claims to retire. The shared-substrate congruence is proven for scrubber +
continuous, ASSERTED-but-contradicted for segmented. R5's "same computed bg-token family" assertion
will read three rails where one has `background: transparent` — either R5 is gamed or the thesis is
half-true. As a USER, the three still don't obviously read as ONE primitive: the segmented row reads
as floating pills with no lane behind them (screenshot: the cels sit on the card, not in a rail).

**Hardening:** decide the segmented model explicitly and gate it: EITHER (a) the rail paints the
warm lane and the cels are translucent insets that let the lane show in the gaps (true shared
substrate — the gap reveals the RAIL, not the card), OR (b) drop the "shared capsule" language for
segmented and reframe the shared identity as "the shared MATERIAL recipe (token family), three
chassis." (a) is the stronger gestalt and matches the iOS battery/storage bar (a tinted lane behind
the segments). The prototype must show the lane, not null it.

### R-4 — "born-RED, both engines, paired-π" is a SKETCH that was never run; the webkit arm is unproven
§9/§10/§11 promise a "PAIRED-engine π (chromium AND webkit)" and "captured DELTA frames." On disk:
no `*.png`, no `*.json`, no run artifact in `golden/`. `timeline-pi.mjs` is self-labeled a SKETCH.
playwright 1.60 is installed but webkit was never exercised. Per the live-verify-capture lesson, a
"both-engines born-RED" claim without a captured paired artifact is the cardinal inflation. Worse —
the two mechanisms most likely to FAIL on webkit (R-1 `left` jank, R-2 `filter⊗backdrop`) are
precisely the ones the unrun webkit arm would have caught. The gate is honestly born-RED on
**chromium** (I confirmed the gray composite + paperFieldCount:0 live); on **webkit** it is
born-NOTHING.

**Hardening:** RUN `timeline-pi.mjs --target=:5173/data/timeline` AND `--target=file://…golden.html`
on BOTH chromium and webkit, commit the captured frames + the OKLab/scale JSON into `golden/`, and
make the wave's R-gate cite the webkit numbers. No "both engines" prose survives without the webkit
artifact on disk.

### R-5 — "dark GLOWS, never gray" is asserted by token-swap but not measured in dark mode
§2/acceptance #2 promise the dark rail "GLOWS, never the invisible 6%-on-black." But dark `--card`
= `hsl(26 22% 17%)` (a near-black brown); `--glass-bg-resting` = `65% of that + 35% transparent`.
Over a field that the prototype itself DIMS (`.dark .paper-field { filter: saturate(0.9)
brightness(0.7) }`), a 65%-dark-brown scrim does not self-evidently clear the C ≥ 0.018 warm floor —
the warmth is borrowed entirely from the transmitted field, which is dimmed. The light-mode composite
is plausibly warm; the dark-mode "glow" is the unproven half and R1's dark arm was never captured
(R-4). The 1940s-technicolor / cartoon-punch register also reads WEAK in dark: the `--shadow-cartoon-sm`
is a warm-brown cast that vanishes on a dark field — the "bold layered-offset 1940s pop" is a
light-mode-only flourish as authored.

**Hardening:** capture the dark-mode composite OKLab over the real (un-dimmed-by-the-spike) field on
both engines; if C falls below floor, lift the dark rail toward a warm-luminous floor (the
W-DARK-MATERIAL luminosity-lift the IOS27-REFERENCE T7 already ships) rather than relying on a dimmed
field. Re-key the cartoon shadow for dark (a luminous/edge cast, not a brown drop) so the punch
survives both modes — the precept is "both modes," and the cartoon register is currently mode-gray.

### R-6 (minor) — proportion is √φ-on-paper but the head:channel ratio reads OFF, and the cels read pastel-flat
The √φ ladder is real and DRY (one base `h`, derived heights/rivet/gap). But as a USER on the
screenshot: the scrubber head (26px) over a 23px-ish scrub channel does NOT read φ ("a bead seated
in the strip"); it reads as a thumb fatter than its lane (head ≳ channel). And the segmented cels at
`38% tint` over a bright field read as near-white pastel washes, not "distinct lozenges" — the
differentiation the GOLDEN leans on (§3) is perceptually weak; you cannot tell phase-status apart at
a glance. The √φ math is satisfied; the GESTALT it is supposed to produce is not.

**Hardening:** the head must read SMALLER than the channel by φ (a bead IN the lane), not larger —
re-anchor head-diameter to `channel/√φ`, not `h·√φ` colliding with the touch target (keep the 44px
target as an invisible `::before` halo, decouple from the visible bead size). Raise the cel tint
floor (or add a keyed inner edge per cel) so the segments read as defined chips over the warm lane,
not pastel ghosts. Judge §10 acceptance #1/#6 on a fresh capture, not the ladder math.

## Does it SURVIVE?

**Yes — the design is RIGHT and the diagnosis is honest.** The thesis (one warm-glass rail, three
postures; warm over a colorful field; liquid four-beat scrub on shipped engines; DRY token-swap; no
fork) is sound, the born-RED is live-real, and the union/freeze discipline is correct. It is NOT
refuted at the architectural level.

But two scars are LOAD-BEARING and the wave must not land without closing them: **(R-1)** the
de-risk prototype proves the forbidden `left`-travel instead of the mandated compositor `transform`,
and **(R-2)** the opt-in goo stacks `filter:url` on a `backdrop-filter` head — the genuine WebKit
trap — untested, behind a `backdrop-filter:url`-strawman defense. Both are invisible until a webkit
run that **(R-4)** was never done. Plus **(R-3)** the shared-substrate gestalt is faked in segmented
and **(R-5/R-6)** the dark-mode warmth + cartoon punch + head/cel proportion are asserted, not seen.

Top refutation: **R-1** — the proof-of-Safari-compositor-scrub animates `left` per frame, the exact
channel §6 forbids; the headline interaction is un-de-risked on the engine the whole §6/§7 fence
exists to protect.
