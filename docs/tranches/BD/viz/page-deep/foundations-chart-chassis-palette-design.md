# foundations/chart-chassis-palette — FRONTEND-DESIGN critique (Pass-E, design lens)

**Page:** `demo/stories/foundations/chart-chassis-palette.vue` · live `http://localhost:5173/foundations/chart-chassis-palette`
**Lens:** the frontend-design skill bar (distinctive, production-grade, AVOID generic-AI) APPLIED to glass-ui's own language — DESIGN.md §L1–L5, design-idioms / motion-canon / affordance-map, the dock system.
**Captures (this dir, 1440×806, both modes):** `_cap-chart-chassis-light.png`, `_cap-chart-chassis-dark-top.png`, `_cap-chart-chassis-dark.png`.

This is the **chart-palette + chassis-tier token page** — it documents the speedtest-shaped semantic chart aliases (`--chart-{ping,download,upload,jitter}`) and the four chassis-tier composite tokens (`--glass-bg-dock`, `--glass-bg-chassis`, `--glass-curvature-overlay`, `--glass-specular`). The subject is **two color/material registers that are load-bearing on the system's most distinctive surfaces** (the InstrumentChassis, the GlassDock, the data-viz palette). The bar is therefore high: a page that documents the chassis material ought to BE a chassis-grade artifact. It currently reads as a **token-export spreadsheet** — accurate, legible, but generic-AI-template flat, leveraging **zero live glass surfaces** and carrying **two structurally-dead "specular" swatches** that misrepresent the very tokens they exist to show.

## What is already RIGHT (do not regress)

- **The √φ page title is present + audacious.** "Chart & Chassis Palette" resolves a measured **86px** `text-display-4` `<h1>` in Plus Jakarta Sans (the brand face) — correct as the page title (the typography-forward ladder, used at the head).
- **The semantic-alias framing is genuinely useful + correct.** The `--chart-*` → `--viz-*` basis mapping (ping→fourier, download→chebyshev, upload→legendre, jitter→bessel) is the right abstraction to document, and the chart hues resolve as distinct, saturated, on-brand OKLCH colors (`oklch(.484 .163 265.5)` blue, `oklch(.579 .201 30.4)` red, etc.). The four chart swatches are the page's one legible, on-doctrine moment.
- **The live `<InstrumentChassis>` is the right instinct.** The "composed surface" section mounts a real `<InstrumentChassis phase="ready">` — the page does compose ONE genuine library component (the only one in the body), and showing the chassis tokens *composed* alongside the raw ladder is the correct documentary move.
- **The path label is the correct convention for its kind.** The chip reads `/foundations/chart-chassis-palette` (the demo-route form) — correct, because this is a demo-only foundations page documenting tokens, with no single exported component. Do NOT "fix" it to a `@mkbabb` subpath; it is already standardized.
- **The monochrome page chrome is on-doctrine.** Mono-caption labels, muted blurbs, no spurious color events on the chrome — the one-color-event proportion is respected on the frame. Do not tint the labels.

## TOP design problems (ranked, opinionated, concrete)

### 1. STRUCTURAL BUG — two of the four "specular" swatches are DEAD by construction (the page misrepresents its own tokens)
The single most damning defect, and it is a correctness bug, not a taste call. The "chassis-tier opacities + specular" ladder paints each token as a `background:` on a `size-12` tile via `TokenLadder`. But the resolved values are:
- `--glass-curvature-overlay` → `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% -20%, hsl(40 30% 96% / 0.012), transparent 38%)` — a **0.012-alpha** radial gradient. As a 48px tile background it is **perceptually invisible** (1.2% white over the card). The swatch reads as an empty bordered square.
- `--glass-specular` → `inset 0 1.5px 0 0 hsl(0 0% 100% / 0.30)` — this is a **`box-shadow` value**. Assigning a `box-shadow` literal to the `background` property is **invalid CSS; it paints nothing.** The swatch is a transparent empty square.

So the section that exists to prove "the specular tokens drive the curved highlight band that reads as 'glass under angled light'" demonstrates the OPPOSITE: two dead tiles. This is the worst failure a token-documentation page can have — it lies about the tokens. A `box-shadow` and a near-zero radial-gradient are **not swatch-able as flat backgrounds**; they only read **composed on a real surface under angled light**.
**Fix:** these two tokens must NOT ride the flat-background `TokenLadder` — they are *composite* effects, not fills. Show them ONLY composed: render two identical glass tiles side-by-side, one with the specular/curvature layer applied and one without, so the catch-light reads as the delta (the §L1 layer-4/curvature "edge that catches the room light" demonstrated, not asserted). The flat opacity tokens (`--glass-bg-dock`, `--glass-bg-chassis`) can stay as fills; the specular/curvature ones become a before/after composed pair.

### 2. THE GLASS DEMOS OVER NOTHING — no aurora field, so the §L1 lensing is inert (the headline mandate)
Verified: `document.querySelectorAll('canvas').length === 0`. The page background is `story-hero` over the static paper wash — **no colorful field.** The body sits in ONE real `glass-resting` plate (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px) saturate(1.05)`), but **there is nothing behind it to lens.** DESIGN.md §L1 is explicit: "Liquid Glass *bends and concentrates* light … surfaces are lensing layers, not blur swatches." A blur over a flat sheet is the iOS-7-flat read the precept condemns. In **dark mode it is the canonical W-DARK-MATERIAL failure re-introduced at the composition layer** (`_cap-chart-chassis-dark-top.png`): the body card reads as a **flat charcoal slab on a pure-black void**, the exact "charcoal slab on a dead void" the dark-material work was built to kill. A page documenting the *chassis material* — the most refraction-dependent surface in the system — shows that material over the one backdrop that gives it nothing to refract.
**Fix:** declare a **colorful `<Aurora>`** as the page background (the manifest `background` row → a live field, ONE GL context per route per the budget). Thematically exact here: the aurora can seed from the `--chart-*`/`--viz-*` basis hues this very page documents, so the field IS the palette. Then the body plate, the chassis, and every sub-card lens a real chromatic field — the six-layer composite demonstrated, not asserted.

### 3. EACH SUB-SECTION IS NOT ITS OWN GLASSY CARD — and the cards that exist are OPAQUE PAPER, not glass (W-STORY-PAGE-STANDARD)
Verified: the five sub-sections are **bare transparent `<section class="flex flex-col gap-3">`** (`backdrop-filter:none`, `background:transparent`), separated only by faint `story-sections--delimited` hairlines. Worse, the `<ShowcaseFrame pad="lg">` inside each one resolves to `rounded-card border shadow-cartoon bg-card` (`bg: rgb(251,248,244)`, `backdrop-filter:none`) — an **opaque Memphis-paper card, not a glass plate.** So the page's only "cards" are flat paper rectangles with a cartoon shadow; nothing on the page lenses, and the sub-sections aren't cards at all. The user mandate is explicit: "each sub-section in its OWN glassy card; the main card area BIGGER." Unmet on both counts — there is ONE outer plate and five undifferentiated transparent stacks, the inner specimens hosted on opaque paper.
**Fix:** promote each sub-section to its OWN glass specimen card (`<DemoSpecimen>` / a `glass-quiet`/`glass-resting` inner plate), each lensing the aurora from move #2. Respect **glass-cannot-sample-glass (§L1):** the inner cards and the outer card must share the ONE composition container — so either the outer drops its own filter and the children carry it, or the inner specimens are `glass-quiet`/`veil` rungs composed inside the single outer container. Then enlarge the focal artifact (the "main card BIGGER" ask): the **chart-alias ladder + the live chassis** are the two protagonists — give them the dominant bento span; demote the two "token-color tile" repetition sections (see #6).

### 4. ANIMATION AFFORDANCE IS NEAR-ZERO — the page is inert against the iOS-27 bar (motion-canon P1–P6, §L1–L3)
The mandate is "HIGH animation affordance for EVERY component." Measured:
- **Swatches are dead** — `cursor:auto`, `transition:all` (the catch-all smell motion-canon P1 flags), no hover, no press, no entrance. Every chart hue is a `<div :style="{background}">` rectangle that cannot be clicked, copied, or lit.
- **The live `<InstrumentChassis>` is mounted at `phase="ready"` and never advances** — the page documents a phase-bus instrument and shows it in ONE static phase. The chassis is the system's premier kinetic surface (ping→download→upload→jitter→complete, each with its `--chart-{phase}` ink + the `--phase-tint-amount` warmth ramp), and the page leaves it frozen. This is the deepest missed opportunity: the page literally documents the phase-tint tokens while showing the chassis that consumes them inert.
- **Only `.scroll-cascade` is wired** (the section entrance) — good, but it animates whole bare sections, not designed cards, and it's the only motion present.
**Fix:** (a) route every swatch through `<ColorSwatch>` — four-state + `--scale-press` 0.96 squish (§L3) + click-to-copy for free; (b) **animate the chassis** — a `<SegmentedTabs>` or `<StoryPlayButton>` that cycles `phase` through the full bus (ping→download→upload→jitter→complete), so the page demonstrates the phase-ink + warmth-ramp tokens LIVE, the chassis breathing through its register — this single move satisfies the "deftly uses glass-ui components" + "high animation affordance" + "contextual-switching" mandates at once; (c) give the chart swatches a spring-clocked stagger-in on the `--spring-snappy` clock (P3 coupled opacity), the ladder assembling row-by-row.

### 5. ZERO LIVE COLOR/INTERACTION COMPONENTS — the dock + tabs + color-swatch mandate is absent
Verified in the body: `colorSwatch:0, tabs:0`. The page about the **chart color palette** hand-rolls dead `<div>` color rectangles while omitting `<ColorSwatch>` (`@mkbabb/glass-ui/color-swatch`, the library's own designed color-input primitive — the chip + hex affordance that exists precisely to replace raw color slabs). The dock APIs (contextual-switching/animating — the explicit headline) are entirely absent from the page's own composition; the only docks present are the demo-shell nav chrome.
**Fix:** (a) every chart-alias + chassis-opacity swatch becomes a `<ColorSwatch>` with the hex/OKLCH readout (the deadrectangle → alive, click-to-copy, hover-lit chip); (b) a `<DockStack mode="facets">` whose facet chips ARE the two token families — **Chart palette** / **Chassis tiers** — each carrying its `--glass-accent` context hue (BE.W-DOCK-RAIL-REALIZE + BB.W-GLASS-ACCENT), contextually switching which specimen card is in focus (the contextual-switching dock mandate made literal); (c) `<SegmentedTabs>` over the chassis to drive the phase bus (move #4b) — three real library components doing real work on a page that today composes only the demo-chassis scaffold + one frozen instrument.

### 6. CONTENT REDUNDANCY + DEAD COPY — two sections say the same thing; one section is a changelog
- **Sections 1 and 2 are the same data twice.** "chart palette aliases" (a `TokenLadder` of the four `--chart-*` tiles) and "chart palette · token-color tiles" (a flex row of the same four `--chart-*` tiles) show the **identical four hues** — one stacked, one inline. There is no reason to render the palette twice; the second section adds nothing the first doesn't, and it dilutes the page's focus.
- **The "resolved drift" section is a CHANGELOG in the UI.** Its visible body — *"Pre-V the storybook referenced `--viz-topology` and `--viz-recursion` — neither token exists in the canon. This page replaces those references…"* — is **internal tranche-history bookkeeping leaking into the rendered page.** A user reading the color docs does not need to know what a prior version referenced. This is the antithesis of the "no meta / no version-history in artifacts" discipline. It must be DELETED from the UI (it belongs in a code comment or the git log, nowhere on screen).
**Fix:** merge sections 1+2 into ONE chart-palette specimen card; **delete the "resolved drift" section entirely** from the rendered page; cut the blurbs to one code-voiced line each (a token page speaks in swatches, not paragraphs).

### 7. SPACING/RHYTHM + DISTINCTIVENESS — token-export flat, not bespoke
At 1440px the head consumes a tall band (86px `<h1>` + blurb + the first label) before any specimen. The ladders use uniform `gap-8`/`size-12` with no √φ relationship (BB.W-CARD-PAD's sqrt-φ block-over-inline ladder is unused on the inner content). The chart swatches are `48px` flat rounded squares in a thin column — the **generic design-token-export aesthetic** (indistinguishable from any Figma/Storybook palette dump). On a page documenting the system's two most material-rich registers, the presentation is its least distinctive. The viz-basis tiles on `foundations/colors` (the `ℱ/T/P/A/G` display-glyphs, hue-inked, accent-barred) prove this corpus can be bespoke; nothing here rises to that bar.
**Fix:** apply the BB.W-CARD-PAD sqrt-φ padding ladder to the specimen cards; make the chart swatches **larger glass chips over the aurora**, each tinting the field behind it via `--glass-accent`, labeled with the alias + the resolved OKLCH coords in the mono-caption register; borrow the colors-page display-glyph treatment for the four chart hues (a focal letter/figure per channel) so the palette reads as a designed spectrum, not a token row.

## The top design moves to make this page EXCEPTIONAL

1. **Fix the dead-swatch bug (#1):** never paint `--glass-specular` (a `box-shadow`) or `--glass-curvature-overlay` (a 0.012-α gradient) as a flat tile background — show them as a composed before/after glass pair where the catch-light reads as the delta (§L1 layer 4 demonstrated). This is a correctness fix, not a polish pass.
2. **Declare a colorful `<Aurora>` page background, seeded from the `--chart-*`/`--viz-*` basis hues** the page documents — so the field IS the palette and every glass surface (body plate, chassis, sub-cards) lenses a real chromatic field (§L1). Kills the dark-mode charcoal-slab-on-void read. ONE GL context per route.
3. **Promote each sub-section to its own GLASS specimen card** (not the current opaque `bg-card` paper), sharing the ONE composition container (glass-cannot-sample-glass, §L1), with the chart-alias ladder + the live chassis as the dominant bento spans (the "each card; main area BIGGER" ask), on the √φ padding ladder (BB.W-CARD-PAD).
4. **Make the live `<InstrumentChassis>` BREATHE through its phase bus** — a `<SegmentedTabs>` / `<StoryPlayButton>` cycling ping→download→upload→jitter→complete, so the `--chart-{phase}` ink + `--phase-tint-amount` warmth ramp this page documents are demonstrated LIVE on the surface that consumes them. The single highest-leverage move: it lands the animation + component + contextual-switching mandates together.
5. **Render every swatch as `<ColorSwatch>` + add a `<DockStack mode="facets">` family switcher** (Chart palette / Chassis tiers, per-facet `--glass-accent` hues) — the dead `<div>` rectangles become alive four-state click-to-copy chips, and the dock provides the contextual-switching index the mandate asks for.
6. **Cut the redundancy + dead copy:** merge the two identical chart-palette sections into one; **delete the "resolved drift" changelog section** from the UI; one-line code-voiced blurbs throughout.

## Verdict (5 lines)

1. CORRECTNESS BUG (headline): two of the four "specular" swatches are dead by construction — `--glass-specular` is a `box-shadow` painted as a `background` (paints nothing) and `--glass-curvature-overlay` is a 0.012-α gradient (invisible at swatch scale); the section meant to prove the catch-light demonstrates the opposite, misrepresenting its own tokens.
2. The headline mandate fails: NO aurora (`canvas.length===0`), so the body's real `glass-resting` plate lenses nothing — §L1 lensing is inert, and in dark mode the page reads as the canonical W-DARK-MATERIAL "charcoal slab on a dead void," re-introduced at the composition layer.
3. The "each sub-section its own glassy card / main area BIGGER" ask is unmet AND inverted: the sub-sections are bare transparent `<section>`s, and the only cards present are OPAQUE `bg-card` paper rectangles (`backdrop-filter:none`), not glass.
4. Near-zero animation affordance (swatches `cursor:auto`/`transition:all`, the live chassis frozen at one phase, zero `<ColorSwatch>`/tabs/dock) — the page documents the phase-tint tokens while leaving the chassis that consumes them inert; plus content debt (two identical chart-palette sections + a changelog "resolved drift" section leaking into the UI).
5. Net: an accurate but generic token-export spreadsheet carrying a real correctness bug — CONVERGENCE ~30%; needs the dead-swatch composed-pair fix + an aurora field seeded from the chart hues + per-section glass cards + a phase-cycling live chassis + `<ColorSwatch>`/dock/tabs composition + the redundancy/changelog cuts before it earns its place as a chassis-grade artifact.
