The COMPONENT is small, clean, idiomatic, Safari-safe, PRM-safe, compositor-only — a single-source DOM-split leaf with a real `≥2`-consumer bar (no orphan gate, unlike its typewriter sibling). The PAGE is the single most UNDER-DESIGNED surface in the motion band: a page ABOUT kinetic typography whose own kinetics are a bare 6px opacity fade, three static `<SplitChars>` instances naked in ONE flat `glass-wash` card over a near-invisible monochrome constellation, a 53px violet word standing in for what should be the audacious-ladder protagonist of the whole band. All three lenses converge HARD and WITHOUT CONFLICT on the same defects, and every one folds onto the systemic Band-17 chassis the dock + substrates + typewriter GESTALTs already named (`W-STORY-PAGE-STANDARD` · `W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` · `W-LIQUID-ENTRANCE-GENERAL`) — plus ONE genuinely-leaf MODIFY (the `replay()` seam + the `--char-total` dead-custom). **ZERO net-new waves.** **No orphan gate** — split-chars is consumer-load-bearing (speedtest survey-deck + the slides consume-back; the BC.W-SPLIT-CHARS ≥2-bar), so unlike typewriter the redesign is UNCONDITIONAL.

## The one cross-lens tension (resolved)

The demo + design lenses each propose a heavy bespoke redesign (three glassy sub-cards, a `<SegmentedTabs>`/dock mode-switcher, violet aurora, an audacious-ladder hero, a stagger-direction gallery). The component lens says the LEAF stays surface-free (a kinetic word sits ON a plate, it is not one) and the glass belongs on the DEMO's card wrapper, not on the spans. **No conflict** — they describe different altitudes: the LEAF gets the `replay()`/`--char-total`/squish-entrance MODIFY; the PAGE gets the chassis-fold glassification. Both agree the six-layer composite never binds to the text leaf. The only thing the component lens guards against (a bespoke per-page wave) is exactly what the synthesis refuses — every page item FOLDS onto the systemic chassis the other 11 motion pages inherit.

### One real BUG (live-confirmed, separable)
**Grapheme emoji renders as tofu.** `text="a 👨‍👩‍👧 b"` (`split-chars.vue:54`) shows a placeholder box live (system font lacks the ZWJ family glyph). The grapheme-safety POINT is real (the cluster IS kept as one `.char`, total `3`) but the chosen emoji doesn't VISIBLY render it — it reads as a bug. Swap for a reliably-rendered multi-scalar grapheme (a flag, or a combining-mark `é` sequence, or a widely-supported single emoji) so "ONE glyph, never torn" is VISIBLE. Folds to the grapheme sub-card content (`W-STORY-PAGE-STANDARD`).

### Conflicts resolved
- **Redesign-now (demo/design) vs leaf-stays-surface-free (component):** no conflict — different altitudes. The glass goes on the demo's card wrapper; the leaf gets `replay()`/`--char-total`/squish. The component lens explicitly scopes the glassy-card asks to the demo. (Contrast typewriter: split-chars has NO orphan gate, so the redesign is unconditional — no sequencing tension.)
- **"Make it kinetic" (design) vs "compositor-only PASS" (component):** agree — the LEAF is correctly compositor-safe TODAY; the F2 upgrade keeps it compositor-only (the squish is `scale`, the blur is `filter`, both compositor channels) — fidelity up, no perf regression. The Safari `filter`-arm is the only new cost, enrolled in the wave π.
