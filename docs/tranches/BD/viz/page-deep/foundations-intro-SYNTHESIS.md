## 1 · One-line verdict

A world-class typographic + substrate FOUNDATION (the 177px `text-display-mega` D0 hero + a real colorful both-mode aurora + true six-layer glass on the 11 bento cards) undercut by THREE structural defects the front door cannot ship with: the HERO TEXT IS NOT GLASS (`closest('.glass-*') === false` — raw ink on a mesh gradient, the generic-AI tell on the page that preaches "glass, paper, golden ratio"), animation affordance is near-zero (dead-glyph thumbs, no hero entrance, no card press-squish), and the dock-API mandate is entirely absent. The page is the storybook's thesis statement and currently demonstrates ~3 components where it should be the system's SAMPLER. Convergence is **demo-side and systemic** — it needs the front-door rebuild + the chassis waves, NOT a component rewrite.

## 2 · Reconciliation — where the 3 lenses AGREE (high-confidence, binding)

All three converge on the same defect spine; this is the load-bearing set:

- **A. The HERO IS NOT GLASS (THE #1 DEFECT — design §1, demo §3, demo bug #2).** The hero eyebrow + `text-display-mega` h1 + blurb float on a TRANSPARENT `.story-hero-bleed-content` (verified live: `backgroundColor: rgba(0,0,0,0)`, `backdropFilter: none`). `StoryHero.vue:323-325` COMMENTS "the content floats on a thin readability plate" — **there is no plate at render** (the element carries the `--glass-backdrop: light` bucket var but paints no glass surface). The page that says "Glass, paper, and the golden ratio" demonstrates raw type on a gradient. It is legible only by the bright rose-indigo-amber luminance luck (the "golden" word already grazes the peach band in light mode — AA at risk on a darker palette). **Unanimous, highest impact.**
- **B. Animation affordance is near-zero (design §2, demo §1, component §1/F-side).** The D0 hero has `animationName: none` (rides only the generic `gl-cascade-build`); the 11 category thumbs are DEAD static lucide glyphs (`.intro-cat-thumb`, `animationName: none`, a `transition: all` catch-all smell); the cards animate only a 1px hover-translate with NO §L3 press-squish (they are fully-interactive `<RouterLink>`s). The user bar "HIGH animation for EVERY element" is unmet on the system's front door. **NOTE the clean side:** the IconChip's OWN entrance (per-spring `icon-chip-reveal` + bloom, coupled opacity, PRM-correct) and the Aurora's lazy-arm drift ARE exemplary (component §1) — the gap is the DEMO composition, not the components.
- **C. The dock-API mandate is entirely absent (design §4, demo §1/§2).** The only dock on the page is the incidental demo-shell collapsed sidebar — chrome, not composition. The front door is the ideal stage for the dock as a HERO primitive: a `<DockStack mode="facets">` whose facet chips ARE the 11 categories, each carrying its `--glass-accent` context hue (BE.W-DOCK-RAIL-REALIZE + BB.W-GLASS-ACCENT), contextually switching/navigating. **The user's named headline, unleveraged.**
- **D. The SectionPreviewCard `#preview` live-specimen slot is built and WIRED TO GLYPHS ONLY (demo §1, design §2/§3).** The card's own header comment (`SectionPreviewCard.vue:6-16`) promises live specimens (a mini `<Button>` row, a glass-card silhouette, a frozen aurora still); the page passes a frozen lucide glyph to every one. The richest affordance on the card is unused — this is the seam that makes the front door a sampler.
- **E. The hero eats the fold (design §3, demo §4).** At 1440×900 the 177px h1 (745px tall × 3 lines, measured) seated at top 223 consumes the entire first viewport; the category grid (top ≈1356px — the page's actual UTILITY) needs a ~600-1180px scroll. A front-door index where you cannot see one category without scrolling fails its job — and the user EXPLICITLY asked "main card area BIGGER," which here means the GRID deserves the fold, not the hero monologue. **Bug #1 (title clips above-fold mid-entrance, top -957) is the same root cause** (the 745px title + entrance translate exceed the scroller top).
- **F. Path-label non-standard (all 3, user-named).** No `/foundations/intro` route chip renders (the bespoke `:hero-title="false"` bypasses StoryPage's standardized chip chassis); the card chips are package-import labels on a subset. Standardize per the import-label header above.

## 3 · Conflicts resolved

- **Card-suffusion: UNDER-spent vs adequate.** Design §5 calls the grid "timid / generic admin-template" (one chip-sized color event per card on the page that pitches the 13-stop ramp); demo/component treat the IconChip POP as correct. **Resolved: BOTH are right — the IconChip is correct AND the suffusion is under-spent.** The fix keeps the IconChip as-is and adds ONE more channel *within proportion*: the `--glass-accent` per-instance chromatic RIM (BB.W-GLASS-ACCENT) keyed to each card's `--section-color-N`, so the rim+catch-light carries the category hue (still one color event — the AA-safe whisper, never a plate fill). The grid becomes a warm spectrum of glass tiles. Design §5 is the binding direction.
- **Hero glass tier: `resting` vs `glass-deep`.** Design §1 offers `glass-resting` baseline OR the opt-in `.glass-deep` (BB.W-DEEP-GLASS) for the maximal iOS-27 refractive read. **Resolved: `.glass-deep` + `paper-grain-overlay`** — the front-door HERO is the one route that earns the deep refractive register (the budget's one-deep-per-route is spent on the thesis moment), with the W55 `--glass-tint-*` bright-bucket so the warm-ink display type clears AA over the busy field. The grid cards stay `glass-resting` (already correct).
- **Hero entrance: SplitChars vs gravity-rise.** Design §2/move-2 offers both. **Resolved: `<SplitChars>` per-glyph gravity-stagger on `--ease-out`** (BC.W-SPLIT-CHARS, on the root barrel, no-overshoot per motion-canon P2 — the audacious title arrives with gravity, never bounces). It satisfies the "HIGH animation" bar AND demos a shipped library primitive on the front door (a SERIES move). The W-HIERARCHY2 gravity-rise is the fallback if SplitChars over-animates the 177px peak.
- **Title size: drop the peak vs keep it.** Demo §4 recommends dropping to `text-display-hero`/`-5`; design §3 says "177 is right for impact, wrong for density at this viewport." **Resolved: KEEP the 177px peak (it is the single best move on the page — do not regress) but CONTAIN the hero plate to a `max-block-size` so ≥1 grid row peeks above the fold** (the iOS-27 large-title-that-invites-scroll). Tighten the blurb to one line (design §7). Re-balance the fold by containing the plate, not shrinking the type.
