## 0. What the page IS right now (the honest read)

Three `StorySection`s ("9-rung tint scale" / "tier aliases" / "dark-mode parity") inside ONE `StoryHero` `resting` card over the inherited `foundations→paper` static wash. Each of the first two sections is an `auto-fill minmax(8rem)` grid of `16/9` checkerboard cells, each painting a `--surface-tint-N` translucent fill over the checker, plus a Fira-Code token label + a mono-caption hint. The THIRD section is a bare `label`+`blurb` with NO visual at all — it tells you to "toggle the theme" and watch the swatches above, which is the page asking the reader to do its demonstration for it.

The checker idea is genuinely the right call for the problem the comment names (a `color-mix(…transparent)` over a same-tone plate is imperceptible — BA.W-STAGE scope 7 fixed that). But the execution is the canonical "token spec-sheet": monochrome grey-on-grey, two clone grids, one dead descriptive section, and after the `.scroll-cascade` entrance the page is FROZEN. The capture is the proof — at swatch scale the warm→cool gradation the blurb promises (`warm-cream → cooler`) is nearly invisible; rungs 4 through 12 read as the same neutral checker. The page is ABOUT a perceptual gradation it cannot make you perceive.

## 1. VISUAL HIERARCHY — the eye lands on the title, then onto a flat grey field

- **The display ladder is used ONCE and abandoned.** "Surface Tints" rides the audacious chrome `<h1>` (good — the W-PAGE-CHASSIS depth-keyed rung). Below it the page collapses to ONE body rung: three `text-mono-caption` eyebrows over flat checker grids and `text-mono-caption` labels. There is NO second focal moment. DESIGN.md's typography-forward identity (the √φ `text-display-mega`/`-hero`/`-audacious` tail) is INERT on a page literally about a 9-step PROGRESSION — a sequence is the most natural thing to render as one giant typographic specimen (a hero word whose backplate sweeps `tint-4 → tint-25` so you SEE the ramp at display scale).
- **Two clone grids = no hierarchy.** "9-rung tint scale" (the raw numeric ladder) and "tier aliases" (the role-named ladder) render IDENTICALLY — same cell size, same grid, same caption rung. But these are NOT peers: Scale is the primitive vocabulary, aliases are the APPLIED vocabulary. A world-class page makes Scale the quiet reference strip and the aliases the LOUD applied showcase (a real `<Card tier="quiet">` / `floating` / `modal` wearing its tint), never two grey clones stacked.
- **The 16/9 checker cell shows alpha but kills the gradation.** At ≈139×78 the checker pattern dominates each cell; the actual tint fill is a faint wash you have to squint at, and the warm→cool hue walk (the page's headline claim) is sub-threshold across the low rungs. The thing the page exists to teach — you can SEE rung 4 differ from rung 25 in alpha AND hue — only half-works (alpha reads at the high end; hue never reads).
- **The third section is a hierarchy hole.** "dark-mode parity" is a heading + blurb with no specimen — it points UPWARD ("the swatches above shift"). A section that renders nothing is dead weight; parity should be a side-by-side light|dark split cell, not a sentence.

## 2. AFFORDANCE — zero interactive cues; the token-tour's primary affordance is missing

- Every cell is a dead `<div>`. No hover, no click-to-copy, no cursor change, no focus ring. A token-tour page's PRIMARY affordance is **click-to-copy** the token (`--surface-tint-12` → clipboard) with the affordance-map.md FOCUS-RING + a tooltip confirm. None of the five affordance primitives (HOVER-LIFT · GLEAM-TRACK · PRESS-SQUISH · DRAG-MORPH · FOCUS-RING) are present — the page is entirely outside the closed affordance vocabulary the library ships.
- **The aliases name real surfaces but show abstract checkers.** "→ tint-quiet / floating / modal" maps to `<Card tier>`, popovers, dialogs — yet the reader sees three identical grey squares. The affordance to LEARN (hover the `modal` alias → it blooms into a real `glass-overlay` tile so you see the tint AS the surface it names) is the whole game, unplayed.

## 3. ANIMATION AFFORDANCE — fails the iOS-27 bar hard

- The page has exactly ONE animation: the `.scroll-cascade` entrance. After that it is frozen. The user's bar is "HIGH animation affordance for EVERY component." Here: zero hover, zero press, zero state, zero ambient life.
- **This is the most damning gap given the subject.** A tint is an ALPHA, and alpha is the single most animatable property over a moving backdrop — the demo writes itself: a live aurora behind the grid, and each tint cell is a glass tile whose `--surface-tint` alpha BREATHES the backdrop through it; a hero specimen whose `--surface-tint-strength` sweeps `4% → 25%` on a `--spring-smooth` clock (`@property`-typed interpolation per DESIGN.md §L2) so you watch the plate densify in real time. Instead the alpha is static against a static checker.
- No `useLiquidFlex` squish, no W-LIQUIDHOVER specular catch-light (the cells are opaque checker `<div>`s, so they cannot even RECEIVE the gleam), no press register. The `--spring-snappy`/`--spring-bouncy` vocabulary the library SHIPS is unused on a page that is about a continuous register.

## 4. POLISH + DISTINCTIVENESS — reads generic-AI-template

- Grey checker squares with mono labels on a cream plate. Strip the `ℱ` dock and this is ANY design-system opacity page from a Tailwind starter — the OPPOSITE of bespoke. The DESIGN.md six-layer Liquid Glass composite (backdrop blur+saturate · surface tint · edge rim · inner catch-light · drop shadow · grain) is NOWHERE; the cells are flat-shaded plastic, the exact "iOS-7-flat, not iOS-26-liquid" failure DESIGN.md §L1 names.
- **GLASS is not demonstrated on a glass-library's own surface-tint page** — and surface tint is LAYER 2 OF THE SIX-LAYER COMPOSITE. This is the page that should show the tint AS what it is: the admit-through layer of a real glass plate, not a flat fill over a checker simulation of a backdrop. The user's explicit bar — "glass demos over COLORFUL aurora backgrounds" — is violated twice over: there is no aurora (the row inherits `paper`), and the cells are opaque, so even with an aurora nothing would transmit. The checker is a SIMULATED backdrop; a live aurora is the real one, and the tint over real light is the whole point.
- The single flat outer card is the "everything in one undelimited slab" anti-pattern. The user explicitly asks: **each sub-section in its OWN glassy card**. Currently all three sections share one `resting` plate separated by the chassis hairline.

## 5. iOS-27 / PAPER / GLASS NORTH-STAR FIDELITY

- **The page conflates `--surface-tint-*` with the glass composite — and never shows either as glass.** Per CLAUDE.md the `--surface-tint-*` family is the IN-SRGB brand-overlay ladder (chassis hairlines, chip backplates), DELIBERATELY distinct from the IN-OKLAB `--glass-tint-*` legibility axis. A world-class page MAKES that distinction visible: show `--surface-tint-N` as the hairline/chip it actually is (a `border-[var(--surface-tint-12)]` rim on a real card, a chip backplate) AND contrast it against the glass-tint admit-through on a live plate — so the reader learns the two axes the library keeps disjoint. Right now it shows neither in situ; it shows an abstract fill.
- **Glass tier ladder unused.** DESIGN.md promulgates 7 tiers; this page uses one (`resting`, the outer card). The tier aliases (`quiet`/`floating`/`modal`) are the natural place to render each ON its real tier so tint AND tier read together.
- **Dock APIs entirely unleveraged.** The user names this specifically: "leverage the dock APIs (contextual switching/animating)." The perfect use here: a `<DockStack mode="facets">` / `<DockLayerGroup>` switching the showcase CONTEXT — Numeric ramp ↔ Tier aliases ↔ Light|Dark parity ↔ "tint as hairline vs tint as plate" — with the dock's morph/contextual-switch (W-DOCK-MORPH-FAMILY) animating between views. The dead third "dark-mode parity" section becomes a live dock facet that side-by-sides the modes. Zero dock composition exists beyond the global nav dock.

## 6. SPACING / RHYTHM (golden-ratio) + COLOR (suffusion proportion)

- The `gap-4` uniform grid is 1:1 — no √φ block rhythm (W-CARD-PAD ships a sqrt-φ padding ladder the page ignores). The main card area is NOT bigger as the user asks: the content under-fills its own plate, with generous dead margin and the third section trailing into emptiness above the dock.
- COLOR: the suffusion proportion (AZ.W-SUFFUSE, ONE deliberate color event per surface) is ABSENT — the page is fully monochrome grey. A foundations page is allowed its ONE event. Here the correct event is NOT a tint chip — it's the COLORFUL aurora behind glass tint cells, so the color is the LIGHT the tint admits, not a decoration. That single move turns the page's monochrome liability into its headline.

## TOP DESIGN MOVES (concrete, prioritized)

1. **Put a COLORFUL aurora behind the page and make every tint cell GLASS over it.** Change the manifest row from inherited `paper` to `aurora` (rose-indigo-amber). Re-render each rung as a real `glass-resting` tile carrying `--surface-tint-N` as its actual admit-through layer (the six-layer composite, DESIGN.md §L1) over the LIVE field — replace the simulated checker with the real backdrop the tint exists to modulate. Now the warm→cool hue walk AND the alpha ramp read, because there is real color behind them to bend. (§3, §4, §5)
2. **Each sub-section in its OWN glassy card; make the canvas BIGGER.** Split "scale" / "aliases" / "parity" into three distinct `<Card tier="floating">` glass plates (the user's explicit ask), each filling more width; retire the single flat slab + the empty third section. Differentiate weight: Numeric ramp = quiet reference strip, aliases = loud applied showcase (each alias ON its real tier — quiet/floating/overlay), parity = a live light|dark split. (§1, §4)
3. **Animate the ALPHA — the page's reason to exist.** Add a hero specimen whose `--surface-tint-strength` (or the rung) sweeps `4% → 25%` on a `--spring-smooth` clock (`@property`-typed interpolation) so you WATCH the plate densify over the aurora; per-cell hover springs the alpha up one rung with a W-LIQUIDHOVER specular gleam + `useLiquidFlex` squish. This is the iOS-27 moment the page is missing. (§3)
4. **Leverage the dock APIs for contextual switching.** Compose a `<DockStack mode="facets">` / `<DockLayerGroup>` to swap the showcase context — Numeric ramp ↔ Tier aliases ↔ Light|Dark parity ↔ "tint as hairline vs tint as plate" — the dock morph animating the transition (W-DOCK-MORPH-FAMILY). The dead "dark-mode parity" prose becomes a live facet. Turns the page into a deft series of glass-ui components per the user's bar. (§2, §5)
5. **Teach the two-axis distinction the library keeps disjoint.** Render `--surface-tint-N` AS its real uses (a `border-[var(--surface-tint-12)]` hairline on a card, a chip backplate) and CONTRAST it against the in-oklab `--glass-tint-*` admit-through on a live plate — so the page teaches the deliberate in-srgb-overlay vs in-oklab-glass fence (AW.W26) instead of an abstract fill that conflates them. Promote ONE giant hero specimen to `text-display` scale (the audacious √φ tail the page abandons). (§1, §5)
6. **Click-to-copy affordance + standardize the import-path label + tighten language.** Make every cell click-to-copy its token (`--surface-tint-12` → clipboard) with the affordance-map FOCUS-RING + tooltip confirm — the token-tour's primary affordance. Keep the Fira-Code `/foundations/surface-tints` chip as the ONE canonical route-identity label (standardize across the deep-dive set). Tighten the blurbs: the first blurb is a paragraph re-explaining the checker rationale to the READER (that rationale belongs in a source comment, not on-screen) — cut to one line and push role prose into hover. The third section's "toggle the theme… the swatches above shift" instruction is the page outsourcing its own demo; replace with a live parity cell. (§2, §6)
