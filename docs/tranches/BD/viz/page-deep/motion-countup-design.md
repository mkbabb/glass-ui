# motion/countup — FRONTEND-DESIGN critique (Pass-E, design lens)

Page: `demo/stories/motion/countup.vue` · live `http://localhost:5173/motion/countup`
Captures: `_cap-countup-light.png` · `_cap-countup-dark.png` · `_cap-countup-run.png`
North star: DESIGN.md (§L1 six-layer composite · §L2 spring physics · the 7 tiers · glass-cannot-sample-glass) · motion-canon.md (P1–P7) · design-idioms.md · PROCEDURAL-SUITE.md · the dock APIs.

Verdict in one line: the thinnest demo in the motion band — a single 81-line SFC that prints a paragraph, three buttons, and a 3-stat grid, then fires a count-up ONCE on click and goes inert; it documents a kinetic-typography primitive on a page with almost no kinetics, over a dead constellation, in the WRONG font.

---

## 1. VISUAL HIERARCHY — the eye lands on the title, then on a wall of prose, then nowhere

- **The figures are not the protagonist.** This is a *count-up* page — the entire point is a big number tweening up. The figures should be the loudest thing on the screen. Instead they render at `text-display` ≈ **41.9px** (verified `getComputedStyle`), the SAME rung as nothing else competes with, but DWARFED by the 96px chrome `<h1>` "Count-up" above and buried under a 3-line prose paragraph that occupies the top third of the card. The eye lands on the masthead, is forced through a dense vocabulary blurb, and only THEN reaches the numbers. The numeric hero arrives third. DESIGN.md is TYPOGRAPHY-FORWARD and the metric/number tiers (`text-display-mega` 177px / `text-display-audacious` 352px) have their fast.com-peg home EXACTLY here — "a metric/number value" — and they never appear. The most animated, most interesting value on the page is rendered at a mid-ladder rung beneath a commit-message paragraph.
- **The blurb is the visual mass.** Lines 25–34 are a 3-sentence `text-prose` paragraph that is physically larger than the stat grid it precedes. On a page whose job is to SHOW a number animate, the dominant ink is prose ABOUT the number. The blurb should be one human sentence (see §7), demoted below or beside the figures, never the lead.
- **Flat section weight — there are no sections at all.** The whole page is a single `<section class="flex flex-col gap-6">` (line 24): prose, then a button row, then a grid. No `StorySection` `heading` rung, no sub-structure. So there is no secondary tier organizing "what this is" vs "play it" vs "the result." It reads as one undifferentiated stack (the AZ.W-HIERARCHY caption-vs-heading flatness, here taken to its limit — there is not even a caption).

## 2. STRUCTURE — one big card, three slabs inside it (the user's explicit ask, unmet)

The user's binding directive: *each sub-section in its OWN glassy card; the main card BIGGER.* This page does neither.

- **Everything lives in ONE `StoryPage` glass card.** The prose + buttons + grid all flow inside the single chassis card. There is no per-sub-section card — there is barely a sub-section. The three figures, the controls, and the explainer are one column. Each natural unit (the controls cluster · each counting figure) should be its OWN `glass-resting`/`glass-quiet` sub-card, and the *figure* cards specifically should be ENLARGED to be the protagonists, not three small equal-weight pills in a `grid-cols-3`.
- **The figure cards read as OPAQUE GRAY SLABS in light mode (BG-2, the anti-precept).** Verified: each card IS a true `.glass-card` — `oklab(... / 0.6)` tint, `backdrop-filter: blur(8px) saturate(1.05)` — six-layer-adjacent. But in the LIGHT capture they render as flat warm-gray rectangles, because the constellation field behind them is near-white with a few sparse dots: **there is nothing to refract.** Per DESIGN.md §L1, a glass surface over a flat substrate "reads iOS-7-flat, not iOS-26-liquid" — the blur is imperceptible when the backdrop has no structure. The dark capture proves the point: over the near-black field the SAME cards read markedly more like glass (edge rim + tint visible). The fix is not the card — it is the FIELD (§3).
- **The main card is not bigger — it is mostly air.** The single chassis card is wide but its content (one paragraph + a row + a short grid) fills maybe half its height; the bottom third is empty. The user wants the *demo stage* bigger, not the empty container. Promote the figures into a large, breathing stage; let the card earn its size with content, not whitespace.

## 3. BACKGROUND — a dead constellation, not the colorful aurora the ask names

- The live background is `constellation-canvas` (verified) — sparse grey dots, **near-invisible in light, faint in dark.** The user's ask is explicit: *glass demos over COLORFUL aurora backgrounds.* The motion-family violet identity (`--motion-accent` = the demo-local `--viz-legendre` twin) is already the page's one color event (the four accent bars over the figures) — it is the natural aurora SEED. A violet-tuned `<Aurora>` (offscreen-paused, one-GL-per-route, the W-STAGE / DockStage pattern) would make the glass figure-cards POP as real liquid glass AND unify the page's color story into one designed field. As shipped, the constellation reads as "a background was placed here," not "this glass lives over a designed surface." This is the single highest-leverage *visual* upgrade — it fixes §2's gray-slab problem at the root by giving the glass something to bend.

## 4. ANIMATION AFFORDANCE — a kinetic-typography page that animates ONCE, then dies (the deepest failure)

Per the brief: *every element ALIVE at the iOS-27 bar — entrance, hover, press, state.* This is the cruelest gap on this page, because the page's WHOLE SUBJECT is animation. Audit:

- **The count-up itself fires once on click, then the page is frozen.** `runActive` tweens 0→target one time. There is no auto-play on entrance (the figures sit at "0" until the user finds and clicks Run), no replay-on-scroll-into-view, no stagger between the three figures beyond the hand-set `data-countup-delay` (0/200/400 — fine, but it only happens once). A count-up demo should COUNT when you arrive: ride `useIntersectionPause`/a view() trigger so the numbers animate on first paint, the way every real count-up in the wild does. As shipped, a visitor who never clicks Run sees three zeros and a paragraph — the demo does not demo itself.
- **The figures have no coupled motion (motion-canon P3 violated).** The number tweens its `textContent` and NOTHING else moves — no scale-pop on settle, no brightness/specular leg coupled to the tween, no `--*-press-t`-style drive, no overshoot READ on the card (the `easeOutBack` overshoots the *value* but the card is geometrically static). motion-canon P3 (fade coupled to transform) and §L3 (every settle has a physical read) want the figure to PULSE/scale-settle as it lands. The accent bar above each figure is a static `h-1 w-8` pill — it should sweep/fill/glow as the number arrives.
- **The accent bars are dead decoration.** Lines 48/57/67: `h-1 w-8 rounded-pill bg-[var(--motion-accent)]` — a static violet tick. On a motion page this is the obvious place for a `border-progress`-style fill, a draw-on, or a glow that tracks the count — a 0→100% sweep synced to the tween would make the bar a live progress read of the count itself. It is currently the least alive element on a page about being alive.
- **The buttons carry the library press floor and nothing else.** Run/Settle/Cancel (lines 37–39) are stock `<Button>`s — they have the four-state contract (the only true affordances on the page) but no entrance stagger, no liquid-reveal, no contextual relationship to the figures. "Cancel" as a bare `ghost` button reads as tertiary noise; there is no visual cue that Run is the primary action (it IS `variant="default"`, but against gray slabs it does not lead).
- **Entrance:** the page rides `.scroll-build` from the chassis (the only free motion). The grid, the buttons, the figures do NOT `.scroll-cascade` stagger in — they appear as a block. On the motion band's OWN demo, that is a missed open.
- **The dock APIs (contextual switching/animating) the user named are entirely absent.** The page never composes a dock, never uses dock-morph, never makes the count a dock-contextual surface. The canonical move: a `<DockStack mode="facets">` or `<DockLayerGroup>` that switches the count-up DATASET (requests · latency · savings) with a morph — pick a metric facet from a dock rail, the figures re-tween to the new dataset with a contextual crossfade. That is the user's "deftly uses a series of glass-ui components (docks/cards/buttons)" ask, and it is the natural fit for a multi-metric count-up.

## 5. POLISH + DISTINCTIVENESS — a generic-AI stat-grid template

- The composition is the textbook generic-AI dashboard: title → paragraph → button row → 3-col stat grid. Nothing about it says "this is THE kinetic-typography primitive." A world-class count-up page would let the NUMBER author the layout — one giant `text-display-audacious` hero figure that counts on arrival as the page's protagonist, with the secondary stats as smaller satellites, the accent bars as live progress fills, the dataset switchable from a dock. As shipped it is three identical pills in a row — interchangeable with any SaaS landing page's "trusted by" strip.
- **The figure font is WRONG — a silent cascade bug.** Verified: the figures carry `class="text-display fira-code tabular-nums"` but `getComputedStyle().fontFamily` resolves to **"Plus Jakarta Sans"** — the `text-display` utility's font-family wins the cascade over `.fira-code`, so the intended Fira-Code monospace tabular face NEVER applies. The consequence is real and visible on a count-up specifically: in a proportional sans, digit glyphs have UNEQUAL widths, so the number's box JITTERS left-right as it tweens through values (1→12→128→1280). `tabular-nums` on a non-mono Plus Jakarta does give tabular figures IF the font ships them, but the SFC's clear intent (fira-code mono) is silently dropped. For a count-up this is the one typographic detail that MUST be right; it is broken. Either fix the cascade so `.fira-code` wins, or commit to a tabular-figure sans — but the stable-digit-width contract is non-negotiable for a counting figure.
- **Color suffusion is correct but timid.** The violet accent bars honor the one-color-event rule (the value+unit stay ink — good, matches the SFC comment lines 42–44). But violet appears only as four static 32px ticks; it never becomes the aurora seed, a gradient, or a live fill. It is an accent dab, not the page's identity.

## 6. SPACING / RHYTHM

- `gap-6`/`gap-3`/`p-6` are on the √φ-adjacent scale — fine. The card padding is the BB.W-CARD-PAD ladder (chassis-owned), correct. The one deliberate-geometry miss: `grid-cols-3` is an even 1:1:1 split on a golden-ratio library — for a count-up where one figure could be the hero, a `[φ_1fr_1fr]` or a single-hero-plus-satellites layout would be more intentional than three equal cells. And the empty bottom third of the card (§2) is dead rhythm — the stage should breathe with content, not air.

## 7. COPY — a commit message on the rendered surface (the "tighten language" ask)

- The blurb (lines 25–34) is internal vocabulary: *"The tween rides the keyframes `NumericAnimation` engine — the engine owns the rAF loop, the composable owns the DOM write and the teardown."* A visitor does not care who owns the rAF loop. This is documentation for a maintainer, rendered as the page's dominant text. → **"Numbers that count up. Walk any `[data-countup]` figure to its target."** The ownership/teardown detail belongs in a code comment, off the rendered surface.
- The manifest blurb (`Walk [data-countup] figures and tween textContent on the keyframes NumericAnimation engine.`) has the same jargon density in the chrome subtitle. → "Editorial count-up — figures that tween to their target on the spring engine."
- **The import-path label is correct and standardized — KEEP.** The chrome chip reads `@mkbabb/glass-ui/motion` (verified, manifest line 314) — the canonical subpath form, no `/motion/countup` local-label drift. This page is on the right convention.

## TOP DESIGN MOVES (ranked, concrete)

1. **Auto-play the count on arrival + replay on scroll-in.** A count-up demo MUST count when you reach it. Wire the run to a `view()`/`useIntersectionPause` trigger so the figures animate on first paint and re-run on re-entry — Run becomes "replay," not "the only way to see anything." (The deepest fix: the demo currently does not demo itself.)
2. **Fix the figure font — `.fira-code` must win, digits must not jitter.** The `text-display` family clobbers `.fira-code`; the count-up's box jitters mid-tween in proportional sans. Make the mono tabular face actually apply (raise `.fira-code` specificity / author a `text-display-mono` rung), or lock a guaranteed tabular-figure face. Non-negotiable for a counting figure.
3. **Make the hero number AUDACIOUS.** Promote ONE figure (or the lead metric) to `text-display-mega`/`-audacious` (the metric tier's home) as the page protagonist, the other stats as smaller satellites. Give the page a large numeric anchor that re-counts as you interact. (DESIGN.md typography-forward; the fast.com peg.)
4. **Live field, violet aurora.** Swap the dead constellation for a `--motion-accent`-seeded `<Aurora>` (offscreen-paused, one-GL-per-route). This single move fixes the §2 gray-slab problem at the root — the glass figure-cards get something to bend and finally read as iOS-26 liquid, not iOS-7-flat (DESIGN.md §L1).
5. **Each unit → its OWN glassy sub-card; the figures bigger.** Wrap the controls cluster and the figure stage in distinct `glass-resting` sub-cards (the W-STORY-PAGE-STANDARD specimen/interaction taxonomy); enlarge the figure stage so the numbers are the protagonist, not three equal pills. Fills the empty card with content, meets the user's "own card / bigger stage" ask.
6. **Couple physical motion to the count (motion-canon P3 + §L3).** As each figure lands, scale-settle/brightness-pulse it on a `--spring-snappy` clock; turn the dead accent bar into a 0→100% live fill/draw-on synced to the tween — the bar becomes a progress read of the count, the figure SETTLES like it has mass.
7. **Bind the dock APIs.** Make the dataset switchable from a `<DockStack mode="facets">`/`<DockLayerGroup>` rail — pick a metric facet, the figures re-tween to the new dataset with a contextual morph/crossfade (the user's contextual-switching ask; the natural fit for a multi-metric count-up).
8. **Animate every control.** `.scroll-cascade` stagger the buttons + figures in; give Run a liquid-reveal / press-squish lead so the primary action reads; demote/retire "Cancel" as a bare ghost (it reads as noise).
9. **Tighten copy.** Replace the rAF-ownership blurb with one human sentence each (chrome + body); the engine-ownership detail moves to a code comment, off the surface.
10. **Add real section structure.** Even at this small scale, give the controls and the figure stage a `heading`/`subheading` rung so the page reads as composed sections, not one flat gap-stack.
