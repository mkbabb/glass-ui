## 0. What the page IS right now (the honest read)

Three `StorySection`s inside ONE flat `StoryPage` plate over the inherited `paper` wash:

1. **"three scrim weights"** — an `auto-fill minmax(11rem)` grid of three cells. Each cell is a 16/9 `.scrim-stage` (a static `radial+linear` `--section-color` gradient) with a `.scrim-overlay` painting one of `--overlay-scrim-{subtle,,strong}` over it, plus a Fira-Code token label + mono hint.
2. **"motion + lift offsets"** — a `<ShowcaseFrame pad="lg">` wrapping a `<TokenLadder>` of seven numeric rungs (`--motion-slide-*`, `--lift-*`, `--popover-offset`), each rendered as a `size-3 rounded-full bg-primary` dot and a label. A pure numeric table.
3. **"ModalOverlay primitive"** — a bare `label`+`blurb` with **NO visual at all**. It tells you `<ModalOverlay>` backs the dialog/sheet/confirm scrims and to "compose `<ModalOverlay tier='strong'/>`." A section that renders nothing.

The `.scrim-stage` idea (BA.W-STAGE scope 7) is the right instinct for the comment's problem — a translucent dim over a same-tone `bg-card` plate is imperceptible, so paint it over a vivid field. But the execution betrays it: the "vivid" field at 204×115 reads in the capture as a muddy dark thumbnail, the three weights are nearly indistinguishable at that scale (subtle vs default vs strong is a ~10% alpha delta over a 115px-tall swatch — sub-threshold), and the gradient is a STATIC `<div>`, not the live aurora the dim is supposed to act on. After the `.scroll-cascade` entrance the entire page is FROZEN. This is the canonical "headless-green / muddy-in-render" spec-sheet the BA gestalt bar exists to kill — and on a SCRIM page it is doubly damning, because a scrim's entire identity is a transition, not a swatch.

## 1. VISUAL HIERARCHY — the eye lands on the title, then falls off a cliff into a grey table

- **The display ladder is used ONCE and abandoned.** "Overlays & Scrims" rides the 86px audacious chrome `<h1>` (correct — the W-PAGE-CHASSIS depth-keyed rung, with the W-DISPLAY-TRACKING −1.5% tracking reading on it). Below it the page collapses to ONE body rung: three `text-mono-caption` eyebrows over thumbnails and labels. There is no second focal moment, no `text-display-mega`/`-hero` specimen, no kinetic-typography beat. DESIGN.md's typography-forward identity is INERT on a page whose subject — the takeover surface — is the most natural thing in the system to render as one giant typographic specimen arriving through a scrim.
- **The three sections are mis-weighted into uniformity.** Scrim weights (the headline — a visual, optical concept), motion offsets (a numeric reference table), and the ModalOverlay note (pure prose) render at the SAME eyebrow weight with the SAME left margin. But they are NOT peers: the scrim ladder is the LOUD showcase, the offsets are a quiet reference strip, the ModalOverlay note is a one-line API footnote. A world-class page makes the scrim moment fill the canvas and demotes the rest; here all three are flat siblings, so the eye reads "table, table, sentence" and leaves.
- **The dead third section is a hierarchy hole at the bottom of the page.** "ModalOverlay primitive" renders nothing and sits in dead space above the dock (`scrollHeight === viewportH`). A section that draws zero pixels is negative information — it tells the reader the page ran out of things to show.
- **The numeric offsets get MORE visual real estate than the scrims.** The `<ShowcaseFrame pad="lg">` ladder is the largest framed object on the page, yet it teaches the least theatrical thing (a slide distance is a number; a scrim is an event). The hierarchy is inverted: the abstract table is loud, the optical phenomenon is a thumbnail.

## 2. AFFORDANCE — zero interactive cues anywhere; the page's PRIMARY affordance (trigger → overlay) is entirely absent

- Every cell is a dead `<div>`. No hover, no click, no cursor change, no focus ring. The page about OVERLAYS contains not a single thing you can OPEN. The most obvious affordance in the universe here — a `<Button>` that triggers a `<Dialog>`/`<Sheet>`/confirm-flow so you WATCH the real `<ModalOverlay>` scrim dim the page and the surface bloom in (`.glass-reveal` / `useLiquidReveal`, DESIGN.md §L1's six-layer surface arriving) — is nowhere. The page describes the ModalOverlay primitive in prose and never instantiates it.
- **None of the five closed affordance primitives are present** (affordance-map.md: HOVER-LIFT · GLEAM-TRACK · PRESS-SQUISH · DRAG-MORPH · FOCUS-RING). The token labels should be click-to-copy with the `.focus-ring` (`--focus-ring-shadow`) + a tooltip confirm — the token-tour's primary affordance — and they are inert `<code>`. There is not one focusable, hoverable, or pressable content element on the entire page.
- **The TokenLadder dots are the wrong affordance entirely.** A `--motion-slide-md` rung is a DISTANCE — its only honest representation is a thing that slides that distance. A static `bg-primary` dot communicates nothing; a hover that slides a real chip by exactly `--motion-slide-md` (and a `--lift-md` cell that lifts on hover by exactly that lift) would make the table self-demonstrating. The numbers are shown; the motion they encode is not.

## 3. ANIMATION AFFORDANCE — total failure of the iOS-27 bar, and uniquely damning on THIS page

- The page has exactly ONE animation: the `.scroll-cascade` entrance. After that, frozen. The user's bar is "HIGH animation affordance for EVERY component." Here: zero hover, zero press, zero state, zero ambient life, and — fatally — zero of the transition the page is literally documenting.
- **A scrim is a verb, not a noun.** `--overlay-scrim` exists for ONE moment: the ~200ms when a modal opens and the backdrop dims from 0 to its weight while the surface scales-from-squished and fades in (the `dialog.glass-top-layer::backdrop` dim + `.glass-reveal` bloom, motion-canon P2 enter-bouncy + P3 fade-coupled-to-transform). The page renders the scrim as a STATIC FILL — the END STATE with the event surgically removed. It is a film still of a door, captioned "this is how doors open." The demo writes itself: a trigger that fires the real dim-and-bloom on the `--spring-snappy`/`--spring-bouncy` clock, with a "replay" `<StoryPlayButton>` so you can watch each weight's dim land repeatedly.
- **The TokenLadder is a static table of MOTION tokens.** `--motion-slide-*` and `--lift-*` are animation distances rendered as still dots — the most self-defeating possible presentation. Per motion-canon P1 (spring-iff-spatial), each rung should animate its own value on a `--spring-smooth` clock: the slide rungs slide-in a chip by their distance, the lift rungs hover-lift a card by their offset, the popover-offset rung pops a real `<Popover>` at exactly that gap.
- **No `useLiquidFlex` squish, no W-LIQUIDHOVER specular gleam, no `useLiquidReveal` bloom** — on the page that is the natural home of `useLiquidReveal` (the surface-rect bloom is THE overlay-entrance primitive). The library SHIPS the exact mechanism this page exists to teach and the page uses none of it.

## 4. POLISH + DISTINCTIVENESS — reads generic-AI-template; the muddy thumbnails are an active liability

- Three muddy gradient thumbnails + a dot table + a sentence, on a flat cream slab. Strip the `ℱ` dock and this is any design-system "overlays" page from a Tailwind starter — the OPPOSITE of bespoke. The DESIGN.md six-layer Liquid Glass composite (backdrop blur+saturate · surface tint · edge rim · inner catch-light · drop shadow · grain) appears NOWHERE on the content; the only real glass on the page is the nav dock the user didn't author.
- **The "vivid reference stage" is not vivid in render.** At 204×115 the multi-hue `--section-color` gradient composites to a dark muddy rectangle, and the three scrim weights over it read as the-same-dark, slightly-darker, dark. The contrast field the comment promises (FD-FS-1) does not deliver at this scale — the swatches are too small for the gradation to read, which is the exact failure the comment claims to fix. A LIVE `<Aurora>` at full section width, with the three weights as bands or a single sweeping scrim, would make each weight's dim unmistakable because there would be real, MOVING color being darkened.
- **The single flat outer card is the "everything in one undelimited slab" anti-pattern** the user explicitly names against. The three sections share one `StoryPage` plate separated by chassis hairlines. The user's bar: each sub-section in its OWN glassy card. None are.
- **The page demonstrates a glass-library's modal SCRIM with zero glass on screen.** The scrim is layer-0 of the overlay composite; the surface that arrives over it is the six-layer glass plate. Showing the scrim without ever showing a real glass surface arriving through it is teaching the shadow and hiding the object.

## 5. iOS-27 / PAPER / GLASS NORTH-STAR FIDELITY

- **`<ModalOverlay>` is described, never instantiated.** Per DESIGN.md §L1 and the §A5-1 modal-scrim note, the scrim's correct expression is `dialog.glass-top-layer::backdrop` reading `color-mix(in srgb, var(--background) N%, transparent)`. The page hand-paints `bg-[var(--overlay-scrim-*)]` over a static `<div>` instead of mounting the real primitive and triggering it. A scrim page that never opens a modal is a glass page that never composites glass.
- **The glass tier ladder is unused.** DESIGN.md promulgates 7 tiers; the content uses none. The natural showcase: a real `<Dialog>` (overlay tier), `<Sheet>` (overlay), `<Popover>` (floating), each arriving over its real scrim weight, so the reader learns scrim-weight AND tier together — the subtle scrim under a popover, the default under a dialog, the strong under a destructive confirm.
- **Dock APIs entirely unleveraged** — the user names this specifically. A scrim/overlay page is the perfect `<DockLayerGroup>`/`<DockStack mode="facets">` host: switch the live showcase context — Scrim weights (live dim) ↔ Overlay tiers (real surfaces arriving) ↔ Motion offsets (self-demonstrating) ↔ "ModalOverlay in situ" — with the dock's W-DOCK-MORPH-FAMILY morph animating the transition. The dead third section becomes a live dock facet that mounts the real `<ModalOverlay tier>` and triggers it. Zero dock composition exists beyond the global nav.
- **Paper morphism is also absent.** This page inherits the `paper` wash but never USES the paper register (no `paper-grain-overlay`, no math-paper rail, no blueprint grid). A page can lead with EITHER pole; here it leads with neither — it is a flat cream void.

## 6. SPACING / RHYTHM (golden-ratio) + COLOR (suffusion proportion)

- **No √φ rhythm.** The `gap-4`/`gap-1rem` grids are uniform 1:1; W-CARD-PAD ships a sqrt-φ block-over-inline padding ladder the page ignores. The 11rem `minmax` produces a sparse three-up row with generous dead margin and the canvas under-filling its plate — the user's "main card area BIGGER" ask is violated; the content is SMALLER than its container, not bigger.
- **The page is one viewport tall and trails into emptiness.** `scrollHeight === viewportH (806)` with the dead third section at the bottom — the worst rhythm signal: the page ends before it fills, and ends on a sentence.
- **COLOR suffusion is mis-proportioned.** AZ.W-SUFFUSE: ONE deliberate color event per surface. Here the color event is THREE muddy gradient thumbnails (a decorative `--section-color` field used as a swatch backing, not as the page's coherent event) while the rest is monochrome. The CORRECT single event for an overlay page is the LIVE aurora behind glass surfaces, so the color is the LIGHT the scrim dims and the surface admits — the phenomenon, not a decoration. That one move converts the page's muddy-thumbnail liability into its headline.

## TOP DESIGN MOVES (concrete, prioritized)

1. **Mount the REAL `<ModalOverlay>` and TRIGGER it — make the scrim a verb.** Put a LIVE `<Aurora>` (rose-indigo-amber) behind the page (manifest row `paper → aurora`), and replace the three static thumbnails with three real triggers: a `<Button>` opening a `<Popover>` (subtle scrim), a `<Dialog>` (default scrim), and a destructive `<ConfirmDialog>` (strong scrim) — so the reader WATCHES the dim land from 0→weight and the six-layer surface bloom in over the live color (motion-canon P2/P3, `.glass-reveal`/`useLiquidReveal`, DESIGN.md §L1). Add a `<StoryPlayButton>` "replay" so each dim-and-bloom can be re-fired. This is the iOS-27 moment the page is built to teach and currently omits. (§2, §3, §5)
2. **Each sub-section in its OWN glassy card over the live aurora; make the canvas BIGGER.** Split scrim-weights / overlay-tiers / motion-offsets into three distinct `<Card tier="floating">` glass plates (the user's explicit ask), each filling more width, each transmitting the aurora through its own backdrop-filter so the surface-tint and grain READ. Retire the single flat slab and the empty third section. (§1, §4, §6)
3. **Make the TokenLadder self-demonstrating — animate every motion token's own value.** Replace the `bg-primary` dots: each `--motion-slide-*` rung slides a chip in by exactly its distance on a `--spring-smooth` clock; each `--lift-*` rung hover-lifts a real card by its offset; `--popover-offset` pops a real `<Popover>` at that gap (motion-canon P1 spring-iff-spatial, `@property`-typed interpolation per DESIGN.md §L2). A motion-token table that doesn't move is the page's sharpest self-contradiction. (§2, §3)
4. **Leverage the dock APIs for contextual switching.** Compose a `<DockStack mode="facets">` / `<DockLayerGroup>` to swap the live showcase — Scrim weights ↔ Overlay tiers ↔ Motion offsets ↔ "ModalOverlay in situ" — with W-DOCK-MORPH-FAMILY morphing the transition. The dead "ModalOverlay primitive" prose becomes a live facet that mounts and fires the real primitive. Turns the page into a deft series of glass-ui components per the user's bar. (§2, §5)
5. **Reclaim the typography-forward identity + add the click-to-copy affordance.** Promote ONE giant hero specimen — a `text-display-hero` word arriving THROUGH the default scrim as the page's kinetic-typography beat (the audacious √φ tail the page abandons after the h1). Make every token label click-to-copy with the affordance-map `.focus-ring` + tooltip confirm. (§1, §2)
6. **Standardize the import-path label + tighten language.** Keep the Fira-Code `/foundations/overlays-scrims` route chip as the ONE canonical route-identity label (this page already does — hold it as the deep-dive-set convention). Tighten the blurbs: the first blurb re-explains the checker/stage rationale to the READER (it belongs in the source comment, already there — cut to one line); the ModalOverlay blurb is API prose that should be a hover/tooltip on the live primitive, not a standalone dead section. (§1, §6)
