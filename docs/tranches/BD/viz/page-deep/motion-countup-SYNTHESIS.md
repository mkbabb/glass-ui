## Verdict in one breath

The src core is GOOD-and-KEEP — a value.js-free, single-engine, tracked-teardown DOM-tween with the ≥2-consumer bar MET (`countup.vue` + `data/metric-cell.vue`). But it has THREE real animation-idiom gaps the page's whole subject (kinetic typography) makes acute: **no auto-play-on-enter** (a count-up that does not count when you reach it), a **hand-rolled PRM path** (no engine policy, no live re-monitor), and a **monkey-patched `anim.stop`** for the `setTimeout` delay. The DEMO is the inverse and deeper failure: the thinnest page in the motion band — one flat `<section>` with a prose blurb, three text buttons, and a 3-cell hand-rolled `glass-card` grid (it re-implements the shipped `<MetricCell>`), over a near-monochrome constellation, with the figure font silently broken (`.fira-code` clobbered by `text-display` → digits jitter mid-tween). All three lenses converge HARD: the page meets ZERO of the user's BD bars (own glassy cards / bigger main / aurora field / dock APIs / tabs). EIGHT findings fold onto the systemic Band-16 chassis (`W-PAGE-BACKGROUND` · `W-PAGE-CHASSIS` · the standardized-page+entrance chassis) the dock + aurora GESTALTs already named; the src gaps are the ONE genuinely net-new home (a Band-16 component sub-wave). The page needs SEVERAL more loops — it converges only after the chassis lands and re-audits.

### Conflicts resolved

- **No real conflicts** — the three lenses agree on every defect. Demo adds the component-census (zero docks/tabs, the hand-rolled `<MetricCell>`) + the structure FAIL; design adds the font-cascade bug (the load-bearing one this page surfaces uniquely) + the type-ladder framing + the live-fill accent-bar move; component adds the src root-cause trio (GAP A/B/C), the perf framing (the off-screen text-write storm), and confirms the colorful-backdrop + glass-absence are DEMO-side (the src core is sound).
- **One framing reconcile (the entrance trigger — demo opt-in vs src facility).** The design/demo lenses want the page to auto-play; the component lens scopes it as an opt-in `playOnEnter`/`view()` entrance on the COMPOSABLE. **Resolution:** these are the SAME move at two layers — the src grows an opt-in `playOnEnter` IntersectionObserver gate (which ALSO parks the off-screen rAF text-write, closing the perf defect for free — GAP A + perf are one fix), and the demo CONSUMES it. The IO that gates play IS the IO that parks off-screen; one seam, two wins.
- **Component "refinement, not rewrite" vs demo/design "big refactor":** not a conflict — different layers. The src (`useCountup.ts`) needs the 3-GAP refinement (a real net-new sub-wave); the demo PAGE is where the other 9 findings live, near-zero `src/` paint, all folding onto the systemic chassis.
- **The font bug ownership (Rank 2):** it is a DEMO-side cascade defect (`.fira-code` vs `text-display` on the SFC's figure spans), but the clean fix is a LIBRARY rung — author a `text-display-mono` rung (or raise `.fira-code` specificity) so the mono-tabular intent actually applies. Routed to the chassis as a small src/styles touch (the digit-stability contract is the load-bearing reason — non-negotiable for a counting figure), NOT a per-page `!important` patch.
