## Ranked changes (by impact)

1. **Fix the semantic-tone emission BUG (`bg-success`/`bg-warning`/`bg-info` paint nothing).** The single highest-impact change — the page's loudest, most colorful section currently renders invisible, making the protagonist status-primitive page look broken. Closes: own-cards · bigger-area-used · glass-over-colorful-field · the dead-paper-void. All three lenses rank this #1 or #2.

Plus a `<SegmentedTabs variant="pill">` to switch tone presets — a SERIES of glass-ui components, a tour not a table. Demo arm.

5. **Lift the section-tone fill OFF the inline `:style`/`text-white` pastes onto a real `tone` CVA axis (DESIGN.md §846's deferred axis — BD is the 2nd consumer).** Add a `tone` axis to `badgeVariants` painting the section/viz fill, drop the hardcoded `text-white` for a `--*-foreground`/contrast-color flip — one CVA axis, no per-demo paste.
