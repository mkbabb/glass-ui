### Net-new (component report only — the enrichments the page audits could not see)
The component report's full-corpus read surfaced three component-level findings the demo/design page-audits could not reach, all small and all foldable onto the one existing wave:
1. **The continuous-boil clock has NO offscreen-park.** `useLineBoil` re-perturbs + re-serializes the centerline per shared-RAF tick with no IntersectionObserver/`content-visibility`/`document.hidden` gate. Not live-broken (Pass-D was Chrome) but the one un-verified Safari risk. The π `tests-visual/handmark.spec.ts` ran Chromium only.

Plus one fold-note: the draw-easing literal `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` (`HandMark.vue:87`) should read `var(--ease-out-expo)` (token-first; byte-identical now).
