## RANKED changes (impact-ordered; reconciled across all 3 reports)

### R1 [P1 · HEADLINE] Glass demos over NOTHING — dead flat paper wash, the morphism cannot read
Unanimous #1 design gap (demo §3, design §4, component §6). Background resolves `CATEGORY_DEFAULT_BG.foundations = "paper"` (flat cream grain, `canvas.length === 0`). Shadows specifically **cannot be evaluated without something behind them**, and the page documents shadow — layer 5 of the six-layer Liquid Glass composite — in total isolation from the five layers it co-renders with. Worse, the `xs`/`sm` cream swatches on the near-same cream wash read almost shadowless (the SFC's own `.shadow-stage` comment admits the invisibility problem — the RIGHT fix is a contrasting/colorful stage, not a flat grey backing).
**Resolution (the colors/radii/typography binding precedent — TWO paths):**
- **(a) GL-FREE default, in-scope NOW:** the body specimen FILLS move off opaque `bg-card` onto `tier="field"` / a transmissive glass tier so the existing designed paper-grain wash reads THROUGH them; the BC deep/lens/accent band reads over the wash. M8 stays GREEN (no GL on a static-wash route). The specimen-KEEP fence holds: the BORDER + the demoed `shadow-*` cast stay bare/un-masked; only the incidental fill glassifies.
- **(b) live-aurora upgrade, decision-gated:** the SHARED `BD.W-FOUNDATIONS-AURORA-FIELD` wave (≥2 consumers — typography/colors/radii name the identical ask; shadows JOINS the consumer set). This is the off-default orchestrator scope-call + an M8 carve. **Shadows is arguably the STRONGEST aurora candidate of the foundations set** — a cast shadow needs maximal backdrop contrast to read, and a colorful field is both on-brand AND functionally necessary. Recommend shadows be a NAMED consumer if the orchestrator funds the field.

### R2 [P1 · STRUCTURE] Each sub-section is NOT its own glassy card; main area too small
Unanimous, user-named verbatim (demo §4, design §7-move-1). Both `<StorySection>` blocks (Elevation, Cartoon-lift) flow inside the SINGLE StoryHero `<Card>`, separated only by the chassis hairline. The 11-cell `lg:grid-cols-6` grid leaves large dead margins on a 1440 viewport (low-density spec sheet in a generous frame).
**Resolution:** promote each StorySection to its OWN glass specimen card lensing the R1 field, main area widened (`--story-page-max-inline` for this token-tour route), BB.W-CARD-PAD √φ ladder on the inner cards. **Critical constraint (glass-cannot-sample-glass, DESIGN.md):** inner specimen cards + outer plate share ONE composition container (inner cards are `glass-quiet`/`veil` rungs inside a single outer container, NOT two stacked `backdrop-filter` plates).

### R3 [P1 · COMPONENTS] Zero glass-ui components — the page omits the surfaces that CARRY shadows
Unanimous, the deepest irony (demo §2, design §4): a shadows page hand-rolls raw `<div class="bg-card border">` rectangles + a raw `<button class="glass-card">` while the system ships the exact surfaces shadows ground in real use. `dock:0 tabs:0 button:0 card:0`. A shadows page should be a PARADE of glass-ui surfaces at different elevations.
**Resolution, in impact order:**
- (a) re-cast the elevation specimens as **real glass tiers** (`deep`/`floating`/`resting` rungs) so each shows shadow AS layer-5 of the six-layer composite over the field — the elevation tour becomes a glass-tier tour;
- (b) a **`<DockStack mode="facets">`** context switcher (Glass tiers / Cartoon-paper / Modal+overlay) — each facet animating the specimen set via the `useDockOrientationMorph`/crossfade vocabulary, per-facet `--glass-accent` context hues (BE.W-DOCK-RAIL-REALIZE + BB.W-GLASS-ACCENT). **This single move satisfies the dock contextual-switching/animating headline** AND makes the page navigable;
- (c) compose REAL surfaces at their elevation: a `<Card surface="cartoon">` (the signature mechanic, promoted to HERO — R5), a `shadow-elevated` `<GlassPanel>`, a `shadow-modal` `<Dialog>` opened over the field (a modal casting its `shadow-modal` is the most legible elevation demo possible);
- (d) `<SegmentedTabs variant="pill">` if a second axis is wanted (e.g. light/dark perception-correction toggle). **Conflict resolved:** the component report's KEEP-fence (bare elevation swatches) and the design report's "make them glass" are NOT in conflict — the swatch's BORDER+SHADOW geometry stays bare (M12-2), the FILL glassifies (R1a), and the COMPONENT parade (b/c) is the NEW composition beside the kept swatch grid.

### R4 [P1 · ANIMATION] One entrance, one hover — ~10% of the iOS-27 bar
Unanimous (demo §1, design §3, component §1). Entrance `.scroll-cascade` ✓; hover only the lone cartoon button; press ✗; continuous/state ✗. 10 of 11 specimens are inert non-interactive divs. The four-state contract (`affordance-map.md`) is absent from 10/11.
**Resolution:**
- (a) the R3(a) glass-tier specimens deliver four-state for free (hover lifts onto the hover-shadow on `--spring-smooth`, press seats via `useSpringPress`/`useLiquidPress` — **W-PRESS-UNIFY gets its hungry consumer**, focus-ring on keyboard);
- (b) **the signature living-motion moment — a continuous raking key-light** (design §3, design-move-3): animate a `--light-angle` custom prop (compositor-only, PRM-static per motion-canon P5/P6) that slowly rakes a key-light across all specimens so the cast shadows shift in real time. The single most convincing + most distinctive + most on-iOS-27 way to communicate elevation; nothing like it exists on any generic shadow page. Zero layout cost (drives `box-shadow` offset / `filter: drop-shadow` direction);
- (c) **promote the cartoon mechanic to HERO with audacious type** — pull the cartoon card large/central, set a `text-display-audacious` word (`DEPTH` or `cartoon`) cast in the very `shadow-cartoon` token it documents (type-as-specimen, the typography.vue treatment). Activates the √φ ladder past the hero and makes "the signature surface affordance" actually READ as signature.

### R5 [P1 · COMPONENT-SOURCE · DEAD TOKENS] `--shadow-soft` / `--shadow-elevated` are dead + non-adaptive
**Shadows-specific, not on the sibling pages** (component §5 DEFECT A). `tokens/shadow.css:11-12` + dark-arm `:169-170` author them as RAW `rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`/`rgba(0,0,0,0.12)` — the ONLY two rungs that do NOT ride `--shadow-color`/`--foreground` (a COLD black cast over the warm-cream identity, the W-NO-GRAY spirit violated) AND they force the manual `.dark` re-declaration every adaptive rung avoids. Worse: **grep proves ZERO `src/components/` consumers** — pure shelf-ware shown in the spec sheet (the dead-token class `proof:no-dual-path`/W-DEAD-SWEEP target).
**Resolution:** RETIRE both rungs (clean break, no alias — no-legacy) — the demo grid drops the two dead cells. IF a real consumer is genuinely intended, the fallback is AUGMENT (re-author as `color-mix(in srgb, var(--shadow-color) N%, transparent)` to join the adaptive family + drop the dark-arm re-decl); default verdict is RETIRE (no consumer → no token).

### R6 [P2 · COMPONENT-SOURCE] `cartoon-surface` four-state gap + generic clock
**Shadows-specific** (component §1 + §5 DEFECT B). The `cartoon-surface` `@utility` is rest+hover only — no `:active` press register (a paper-sticker that lifts on hover but never presses reads 2-of-4 against the affordance bar), AND the `translate` leg pairs `--spring-smooth` with the GENERIC `--duration-normal` not its matched `--spring-smooth-duration` settle clock — the EXACT re-timed-tail class the §"per-spring DURATION clock" condemns (`cartoon-surface` predates the W-GLASS-CAL Unit 3 adoption).
**Resolution:** re-point the `translate` transition to `--spring-smooth-duration` + add the `:active` press register (compose the `.glass-press`/`--*-press-t` floor). Gated by `proof:shadow-contract` + `proof:no-layout-animation` staying GREEN.

### R7 [P2 · COMPONENT-SOURCE] Alias round-trip + demo-local hover desync
**Shadows-specific** (component §5 DEFECT C + the demo §7 desync). (i) The `--cartoon-shadow`/`--soft-shadow`/`--elevated-shadow`/`--modal-shadow` reversed-name mirror block (`shadow.css:17-21`) is a self-referential round-trip (`--shadow-cartoon` → `--cartoon-shadow` → `--shadow-cartoon`) that exists only as naming residue. (ii) `shadows.vue:66-67` hand-rolls `transition-transform` only while ALSO swapping `hover:shadow-cartoon-hover` — so the shadow SNAPS while the translate glides (desync), bypassing `cartoon-surface` which transitions both legs.
**Resolution:** (i) collapse the reversed-alias mirror to the canonical `--shadow-*` names, re-point the `@theme` bridge to read canonical directly (gated by `proof:shadow-contract` GREEN). (ii) fold the demo-local hover card onto `cartoon-surface` (which transitions both legs correctly) — kills the desync + teaches the right idiom.

### R8 [P3 · LANGUAGE] Tighten visible copy (user-named)
