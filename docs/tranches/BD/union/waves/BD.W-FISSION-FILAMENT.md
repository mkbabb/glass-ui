## The mechanism (the body-anchor RE-ANCHOR; EXTENDS the byte-tested goo, NO second spring)

### 1. The neck is BODY-ANCHORED — it spans the GAP between the piece and the dock body (the `fission-bridge.css` re-anchor)

The neck stops being a confined `inset: 0` pinch on the piece and becomes a gel filament anchored at the DOCK BODY origin, spanning to the FLYING piece. The geometry is the orchestrator's (it knows the piece's live `(dx, dy) × reach` translate); the paint is the bridge CSS. The mechanism:

- **The orchestrator WRITES the span geometry (`useDockFission.ts`, the per-piece write loop `:333-349`).** Beside the existing `--split-dx`/`--split-dy`/`--neck-t` writes, the orchestrator writes the piece's LIVE gap vector — the displacement from the dock body anchor to the piece center, in dock-local px (`--neck-span-x`/`--neck-span-y` = `dx × reach × piece-progress`, the SAME term the piece's `translate` reads `:274-276`, so the neck span and the piece translate are ONE source — no second geometry). The body anchor is the dock origin (the `.glass-dock` root center-bottom, the constellation body the pieces split FROM). The neck is the band BETWEEN the anchor and the piece, NOT the piece's own box.
- **The neck `::before` is RE-ANCHORED to span the gap (`fission-bridge.css`).** The `.dock-fission-piece::before` re-points from `inset: 0` (the confined box) to a filament that ORIGINATES at the dock body anchor and EXTENDS toward the piece along the `--neck-span-x`/`--neck-span-y` vector, its LENGTH = the live gap magnitude, its width necking via the SAME `--neck-inset` clip-path pinch (`:315-319` — kept, it now pinches a SPANNING band, not a confined box). The filament is a `transform: translate` + `rotate` (oriented along the span vector) + `scaleX` (the span length) on a reserved unit-footprint (the W-DOCK-MORPH-FAMILY reserved-footprint discipline — one layout solve, the live length is `transform`, NEVER an animated `width`/`inline-size`). So the neck STRETCHES across the widening gap as `--dock-split-t` rises (the gap grows → the span scales → the filament stretches), THINS to a tense filament (the `--neck-inset` pinch), then SNAPS (the opacity drops past `--neck-break`, `:322-324`). The goo `<filter>` merges the body, the filament, and the piece into ONE metaball mass while the throat is fat; as the throat thins past the threshold the goo can no longer bridge them and they read DISCRETE — the metaball break, structurally.

### 2. The gap-sized blur reads `--dock-goo-spacing` (the merge-threshold the blur scales against)

The goo `feGaussianBlur` radius (the throat softness) is no longer FIXED — it reads the `--dock-goo-spacing` merge-threshold (BD.W-DOCK-GOO-SPACING, the GATE-SPLIT — GOO-SPACING lands the token FIRST). The blur is a `f(gap, --dock-goo-spacing)`: while the live gap is WITHIN `--dock-goo-spacing` the blur is generous (the throat blends — the pieces read as ONE liquid mass); as the gap APPROACHES the threshold the blur tightens (the throat necks to a tense filament); BEYOND the threshold the goo cannot bridge (the filament SNAPS, the pieces read discrete). So `--dock-goo-spacing` is the distance that gates blend-vs-discrete (the SwiftUI `GlassEffectContainer(spacing:)` analogue), and the gap-sized blur is the VISIBLE realization of that threshold. The blur write is a `--dock-goo-blur` length (`= f(gap / --dock-goo-spacing)`) the orchestrator writes per frame; the `<filter>` `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation` reads it (an SVG `<feGaussianBlur>` `stdDeviation` CAN be a CSS-`var()`-driven attribute via the in-document SVG mount — NOT a data-URI `feDisplacementMap scale` which cannot, the W-LENSING CSS limit; the goo blur is the mountable case).

### 3. The neckHold dwell + the n>2 case (the tense hold-then-snap across the gap)

The signature's `neckHold` (DATA, `useDockFission.ts:78`) now governs a REAL dwell across the gap: the spanning filament HOLDS its tense thin throat (the `--neck-inset` near-max, the goo still barely bridging) for the `neckHold` band of `--dock-split-t`, then SNAPS (the opacity drop + the `useLiquidFlex` recoil overshoot, `:281` — kept). The `search` RADIAL burst (n>2, `DOCK_SPLIT_SIGNATURES.search`) now reads as N filaments each spanning from the ONE dock body to its flying piece — the body is the shared anchor, each neck a spanning throat, so the burst reads as ONE liquid mass budding off N pieces, not N independent discs. The dwell phase-shifts per rank (`:320-328`, kept — the N necks break in SEQUENCE, never simultaneously).

Compositor-only: the span is `transform: translate`/`rotate`/`scaleX` on a reserved footprint + `clip-path`/`opacity` (the `proof:no-layout-animation` floor — NEVER an animated `width`/`inline-size`; the `--dock-goo-blur` is a `filter` `stdDeviation`, a paint property, not layout). Safari-safe: the goo is the REGULAR `filter: url(#dock-fission-goo)` (sRGB, WebKit-supported — bug 245510 is `backdrop-filter: url()`, which this never uses); the in-document SVG `<feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="var(--dock-goo-blur)">` reads the var (WebKit reads SVG-attr `var()`).
