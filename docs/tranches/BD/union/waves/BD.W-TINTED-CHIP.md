## The defect / the ask

glass-ui has a per-instance RIM chromatic axis (`--glass-accent` tints the rim + the `::before` specular CORE) but NO per-instance PLATE-FILL tint that PAINTS — there is no way to make a glass chip's BODY read as colored glass (a green chip, a violet facet) without re-declaring the whole `--glass-bg-*` composed token. iOS-27's facet chips + tinted pills (the Maps facet-chip, the now-playing pill plate) tint the PLATE BACKGROUND, not just the rim — and they do it over the CLEAR surface (the backdrop bleeds genuinely through the colored glass).

**The axis TOKENS are shipped but DEAD — no plate reads them.** The BE WF-1 foundation commit landed the `@property --glass-fill-tint`/`--glass-fill-strength` REGISTRATIONS early (`glass.css:376-392`, rode `proof:glass-foundation` A2 — the regs + the no-op floor + the DISTINCT-from-`--glass-accent` fence). But NO plate `background` reads the axis (`grep 'glass-fill-tint' src/styles/glass/ladder.css` → ZERO hits) — the registered axis is a dead scalar (a registered-but-unconsumed token). This wave wires the CONSUME (the plate body mixes the fill on top of the W55 legibility composite), mints `<GlassChip :tone>` (the first-class colored-glass chip), and threads the `surface="clear"` arm so the colored glass reads its body hue WHILE the backdrop bleeds through.

The iOS-27 betters-claim: per-instance colored GLASS (the body tints, the rim+core+legibility all hold, the backdrop reads through on the clear surface) — the data-keyed facet/album/series chip Apple paints, where shadcn paints an opaque colored pill.
