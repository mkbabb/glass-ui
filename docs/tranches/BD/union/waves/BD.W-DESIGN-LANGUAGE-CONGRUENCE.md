| (2) PAPER | `paper.css` (`paper-grain-overlay`/`paper-underpaint`) · `scale-paper.css` blueprint-grid · `.paper-ink-mark` 2px ink hairline · the `math-paper.vue` gold-standard |

| (5) GOLDEN-RATIO (√φ) | `Card.vue` card-pad ladder (`inline × 1.272` / `÷1.618` / `÷2.618`) · `--type-*` √φ scale · `--space-phi-*` cadence · `--configurator-section-*`/`-preset-row-*` hierarchy |

Pillars (1)/(3) are load-bearing in every V wave; pillars (2)/(4)/(5) appear ONLY in D8's own prose sentence (`VIZ-BAND-PLAN.md:37`) and are wired into ZERO wave mechanism, surface design, or gate. The six incongruences (D8 §2):

- **INC-3 (GOLDEN-RATIO, totally silent — the cleanest miss).** Not one V-surface reads the √φ card-pad ladder or names a golden token; the richest geometry surface (the card-sheet, a CARD that expands) is golden-ratio-blind, defaulting to ad-hoc `p-N`.
