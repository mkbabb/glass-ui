## The defect / the ask + the EXACT HEAD state (verified on disk)

The user, live on the 118-page storybook: **"I do not see any paper grain or grit anywhere."** D8: *"PAPER MORPHISM is ABSENT — no paper grain/grit anywhere."*

The audit ROOT CAUSE (live-confirmed, both batches): paper morphism is **SUB-PERCEPTUAL, not absent** — the mechanism ships, three compounding strength/occlusion failures bury it:

1. **The grain is INVISIBLE.** The full-bleed paper background div mounts `.story-bg-paper.paper-grain-overlay` (StoryHero.vue:312-321), and its `::after` carries the real SVG fractal-noise texture (`paper.css:29-45`, `paper-grain-overlay`). But the `::after` opacity reads `var(--glass-grain-opacity)` = **`0.025` (2.5%)** with `mix-blend-mode: overlay` (light) over the cream page (`glass-fx.css:17`) → effectively invisible. (`foundations.md:92`, `display.md:58`.)
2. **The light wash is TRANSPARENT.** `.story-bg-paper { background-color: var(--story-paper-wash) }` and `--story-paper-wash: transparent` in LIGHT mode (`story-hero.css:27`/`:363`). Only the `.dark` arm lifts it to `color-mix(in srgb, var(--foreground) 7%, transparent)` (`story-hero.css:57`). So in LIGHT there is NO base tint under the ~invisible grain — a flat cream page (`display.md:59`).
3. **The opaque card OCCLUDES it.** The full-bleed grain sits BEHIND the content card, which is `glass-resting` (`--card` at α 0.664 + `blur(10px)`, ~66% opaque cream — `display.md:61`/`foundations.md:99-101`). Even the faint grain is hidden under the article column.

**The deeper finding (`foundations.md:104-113`):** paper is a BACKGROUND wash, never a MATERIAL on the specimen surfaces. Only 2 of 13 foundations pages surface grain on actual content (`paper-glass.vue` tier tiles + `paper-texture.vue` PaperBackdrop). **`typography.vue` — the print-/type-specimen page, THE paper HOME — wears ZERO grain** (`foundations.md:17`/`:113`): its frames are `tier="field"`/`tier="quiet"` with NO `grain` prop set, despite `ShowcaseFrame` ALREADY SHIPPING a `grain?: boolean` prop (`ShowcaseFrame.vue:48-49`/`:109`).

**The CARDINAL FENCE — this is the DEMO paper register, NOT the library glass-tier grain.** `--glass-grain-opacity` (`glass-fx.css:17`) is read by the LIBRARY glass tiers' grain `::after` (`ladder.css:450` — the `.glass-material::after` decorative grain on every glass surface) at the perceptual floor BY DESIGN (the texture-not-reading role, the P.W1.B calibration). Lifting `--glass-grain-opacity` directly would stipple every glass plate library-wide (a regression). The fix MUST be a DEMO-LOCAL paper rung — the demo's OWN paper material identity (presets-in-consumers: the library's identity is its calm glass grain; the demo's paper-HOME specimen pages WANT a readable grit). The library token is BYTE-UNTOUCHED.
