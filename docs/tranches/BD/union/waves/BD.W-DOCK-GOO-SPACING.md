## The defect / the ask

SEED-DRAFT Band-4 (`W-DOCK-GOO-SPACING [NEW]` — "the `--dock-goo-spacing` merge-threshold token — GlassEffectContainer analogue") + the SEED's "What the videos ADDED": *"The --dock-goo-spacing merge-threshold + the small-vs-large adaptivity flip (Apple HIG)."* Two Apple Liquid-Glass facts the dock band names but never tokenizes:

1. **The merge-threshold (SwiftUI `GlassEffectContainer(spacing:)`).** The BE/BF research pins it verbatim (`docs/tranches/BE/audit/pass-1-findings.json:1289` — "The goo-merge bar = SwiftUI `GlassEffectContainer(spacing:)` — two glass shapes within `spacing` visually blend/morph"). Apple's `GlassEffectContainer(spacing: 20|30|40)` is ONE knob: two glass elements WITHIN `spacing` points goo-blend (the bridge engages, they read as one liquid mass); BEYOND it they read DISCRETE. glass-ui ships the goo-merge MECHANISM (`fission-bridge.css` — the `--dock-split-t` neck + the gap-sized blur, BF.W-FISSION-FILAMENT) but has **no merge-threshold token**: the bridge engages off `[data-fissioning]` (a binary attr) and the gap-blur peaks off `--dock-split-t`, but nothing governs WHEN two sibling glass elements should blend vs read apart — the distance knob the constellation gutter (BD.W-DOCK-CONSTELLATION) and the sub-dock re-seat (BD.W-DOCK-SUBDOCK) both need to reason about "within threshold → goo, beyond → discrete."

2. **The small-vs-large adaptivity flip (Apple HIG).** Apple's HIG: SMALL glass elements (buttons, chips, the satellites) FLIP their content light↔dark by the backdrop luminance (a small element over a bright backdrop goes dark content, over dark goes light — it adapts hard); LARGE panels (the pill, a card, a sheet) LIFT-without-flip (they darken/lift the plate for legibility but keep their content register — a large panel does not invert). glass-ui ships the `useGlassBackdropLuminance` observer (`--glass-backdrop: light|dark` bucket, AZ.W-ADAPTIVE-AUTO) + the bright-bucket darken (ladder.css `@container style(--glass-backdrop: light)`), but the SAME darken applies to every glass surface regardless of SIZE — a small satellite and a large pill adapt identically. The size-aware FLIP (small=flip, large=lift) is unexpressed.

## The mechanism

ONE token + ONE size-aware adaptivity arm — both token-first, both reading the SHIPPED seams. KISS+DRY: `--dock-goo-spacing` is the single merge-threshold knob (the GlassEffectContainer analogue) every goo-merge consumer reads; the adaptivity flip is a size-gated branch on the EXISTING bright-bucket seam (no new observer, no new compositing path).

1. **The `--dock-goo-spacing` merge-threshold token (the ONE knob).** Mint `--dock-goo-spacing` in `src/styles/dock/density.css` `:root` (beside the dock geometry tokens — the merge-threshold rides the `--dock-scale` thread like `--dock-control-safe-inset`): the distance (a length, default `1.875rem` ≈ 30px — the Apple `spacing: 30` mid-register) WITHIN which two sibling dock-glass elements goo-blend, BEYOND which they read discrete. It is the analogue of `GlassEffectContainer(spacing:)`. The token GOVERNS the goo-merge in `fission-bridge.css`: the bridge's `--seam-give` / the gap-blur peak (BF.W-FISSION-FILAMENT's `--dock-goo-blur`) read the threshold so the goo engages while the inter-piece distance is WITHIN `--dock-goo-spacing` and the alphas decouple (the bridge fades, the pieces read discrete) once the live gap exceeds it. ONE knob, the whole goo-merge: a consumer setting `--dock-goo-spacing: 40px` makes glass elements blend at a larger distance (a more eager merge — the Apple `spacing: 40`); `20px` is the tight discrete register. The constellation gutter (BD.W-DOCK-CONSTELLATION) + the sub-dock re-seat gutter (BD.W-DOCK-SUBDOCK) READ it — a satellite within the threshold goo-blends toward the pill, the re-seated accessory seats BEYOND it (so it reads discrete). The merge is the EXISTING `fission-bridge.css` mechanism; this wave gives it the threshold knob it lacked.

2. **The small-vs-large adaptivity flip (the size-gated arm on the bright-bucket seam).** A `--dock-element-scale` data signal (`small | large`, or a numeric size proxy) drives the EXISTING bright-bucket darken (`ladder.css` `@container style(--glass-backdrop: light)`) DIFFERENTLY by size — NOT a second observer, NOT a second compositing seam:
   - **SMALL elements** (the satellites, dock-control chips — `[data-glass-adapt="small"]`): the bright bucket FLIPS the content register hard — over a bright backdrop the small element goes DARK content (the foreground ink anchors dark via the existing `contrast-color(var(--card))` `@supports` refine, AZ — the small element inverts), over a dark backdrop it goes light. A small glass element ADAPTS hard (the Apple small-element flip).
   - **LARGE panels** (the pill, a card, a sheet — `[data-glass-adapt="large"]` or the default): the bright bucket LIFTS the plate (the existing 20% AA darken toward `--glass-tint-ink`) WITHOUT a hard content flip — the large panel keeps its content register and darkens its plate for legibility (the Apple large-panel lift-without-flip). This is the SHIPPED behaviour (the bright-bucket darken), now NAMED as the large arm.
   The flip reads the SHIPPED `--glass-backdrop` bucket (the `useGlassBackdropLuminance` observer's write — no new sample, no new loop) + a size data-attr the constellation/control sets (the satellites are `small`, the pill is `large`). It is a paint-property branch (the foreground register), compositor-irrelevant — no animated property, just a size-gated `--foreground`/content re-point on the existing tint seam.

The merge-threshold is a `var()`-read length (no animation — it governs WHEN the goo engages, the goo MOTION is `fission-bridge.css`'s on `--dock-split-t`). The adaptivity flip is a paint-register branch. `proof:no-layout-animation` holds (neither arm animates a layout property). Safari-safe: `--dock-goo-spacing` is a plain length token (universal); the goo it governs is the regular `filter: url()` (sRGB, WebKit-safe — the threshold gates the existing WebKit-correct goo). The adaptivity flip reads the SHIPPED `@container style()` bright bucket (style queries are Safari-baseline; a gap engine drops the unknown at-rule and keeps the zero-delta glass, the existing graceful degrade) + the `contrast-color()` `@supports` refine (already `@supports`-gated, the Safari floor is the bucket darken). No new SVG filter, no `backdrop-filter: url()`.
