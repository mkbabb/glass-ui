## The mechanism

ONE native slow-glide register wired onto the SHIPPED `<DockStack>` `<FadingScroll>` rail port — composing the native `.smooth-scroll` substrate, never a momentum loop, never a second rail component. The fluid scroll is the native CSS register + the shipped fan port, nothing forked.

1. **Wire the native slow-glide onto the `<DockStack>` `<FadingScroll>` fan port.** The rail's `<FadingScroll>` scroll port (the `.dock-stack-fan`, `DockStack.vue:200`) gains the continuous slow-glide: it composes the shipped `.smooth-scroll` native register (`scroll-behavior: smooth`, `scroll-choreography.css`) so a programmatic/keyboard/wheel scroll through the rail's members GLIDES smoothly to its target instead of jerking. The glide is the native `scroll-behavior` — NOT a rAF momentum lib (the no-Lenis/GSAP/Locomotive fence, binding; a rAF momentum loop on the rail is the exact thing the native-first identity REFUSES). The rail-LOCAL register is a `--dock-rail-scroll-glide` opt-in token on `.dock-stack-fan` so the rail's glide is tunable without forcing it on every consumer's scroller (presets-in-consumers — the library ships the named discipline, the rail opts its OWN port in, the `.scroll-gutter-stable` precedent).

2. **The continuous-position glide, not a snap (the R11 regression fix).** The slow-glide is a CONTINUOUS-position scroll (the rail glides through intermediate positions to its target) — NOT a `scroll-snap` step (which would jerk member-to-member, the R11 regression). A `scroll-snap-type` on the rail port is FORBIDDEN here (it re-introduces the step jerk the fluid scroll kills); the glide is the continuous `scroll-behavior: smooth` interpolation. The `<FadingScroll>` edge feather (the shipped dual-path-single-writer mask) rides the glide unchanged — the feather is a legibility cue, the glide is the motion; the two are disjoint and both correct.

3. **The receding-facet legibility floor (W-ICON-PRESENCE) holds through the glide.** As the rail glides, the off-screen-edge facet glyphs recede behind the `<FadingScroll>` feather — but they keep the W-ICON-PRESENCE legibility floor (the receding facet reads, never collapses to an illegible sliver) through the glide, not only at rest. The glide is compositor-safe (the `scroll-behavior` native scroll is the browser's own compositor scroll — no JS layout per frame; `proof:no-layout-animation` holds, but see the scan note below).

4. **PRM drops the glide, keeps the scroll (the vestibular floor).** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the `.smooth-scroll` register DROPS the smooth interpolation (the shipped `scroll-choreography.css` `.smooth-scroll` PRM carve) — the rail jump-scrolls correctly (the scroll still reaches every member, the glide animation is off). The W-ICON-PRESENCE legibility floor is PRM-independent (a legibility cue, not motion — it holds under reduce).
