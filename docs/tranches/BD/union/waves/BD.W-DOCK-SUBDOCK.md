## The mechanism

The persistence is a TERMINAL state of the existing `--dock-split-t` travel: a piece flagged persistent, once it reaches full separation, RE-SEATS as a standing `<DockStack>` (the abstract bottom sub-dock) and the core dock minimizes beside it. 1. **The PERSISTENT-PIECE flag (the register on the shipped orchestrator).** `useDockFission`'s `DockFissionPieceRegistration` gains an additive `persistent?: boolean` (default `false` → byte-identical to today's transient fission). 3. **The bottom-accessory RE-SEAT law (the iOS-26 `.tabViewBottomAccessory`).** When the persistent piece re-seats, the CORE dock MINIMIZES and the sub-dock seats NEXT TO it (not over it, not floating away — the re-seat anchors the accessory in a reserved bottom slot beside the minimized core). 4. **The goo-neck SNAPS at the re-seat (the persistent terminus).** The spanning filament (BF.W-FISSION-FILAMENT — the body-anchored neck on `--neck-t`) runs its full stretch→tense→snap as the accessory detaches, then at the re-seat the neck is GONE (the goo-OR-glass swap at `--neck-break`, the piece is now a discrete standing sub-dock with its OWN crisp glass plate). The neck does NOT persist (a standing accessory is not tethered — the re-seat is the SNAP terminus). The goo rides the regular `filter: url(#dock-fission-goo)` (the WebKit-safe path BF.W-DOCK-INTEGRATE mounts) only during the detach travel; the standing sub-dock is plain glass.
