## 6 · H9-6: R-21's frozen list and its printer

### 6.1 · What landed

- `harness/sweep-frozen.json`: the words of Crit §3's sweep, `tint, darken, clamp, luma, bucket, observer, knee, sampled, backdrop mode`, with R-21's text. Per R-21 it is frozen: a critic does not add to it.
- `harness/prose-sweep.mjs` (SWEEP). Over every prose line (DEL's `proseLines`: comments, `<!-- -->`, test titles, markdown) of **every file the patch touches that exists in the tree**, it prints each line carrying (1) a frozen word in DEL's word form (a word start, inflections, so "retint" is not "tint"); (2) a name of `--declare`'s declaration, exact and (unless ruled exact-only) in its word form; or (3) a changed ladder's old tuple, or a rung's old number after its word (H9-2's readings). Each line prints with the reasons it printed and ±1 line of context (`--context N`).
- **`--rulings <json>`** (`[{ at: "file:line", says: "<text>", why }]`) records the lines read and ruled true. With it, a printed line no ruling covers is `✗ unread`, and a ruling whose line no longer prints or no longer contains `says` is `✗ stale`. Either one FAILs. Without it, the printer is a list: exit 0, `LIST`. A line ruled false is rewritten and drops out of the list, so a ruling only ever says "true".
