**H5-5a · the self-deadlock, cured.** The BL WebKit lock (`driver/webkit-lock.mjs`, added 2026-10-15, after H4's self-test) is taken by a process at its first WebKit launch. The self-test process takes it in its own captures, then spawns `glance.mjs --engine webkit`, which calls `capture.mjs` in-process and waits for the same lock, held by its own parent, until the lock's 2,400 s deadline. Observed: the GLANCE WebKit PASS row sat 31 min with the child idle (`(waiting for the BL WebKit lock, held by 20178 selftest.mjs)`); the child was stopped, and that row printed BAD. Cure (`lib.mjs holdWebkitLock`, used by `capture.mjs:272` and `glance.mjs:115`): a process whose parent owns the lock runs under the parent's hold; anyone else takes it as before. The parent is blocked in `spawnSync`, so one WebKit run stays one. No measurement changes. Every later GLANCE WebKit row in the same run loaded the cure and ran at once (the WebKit self pair: FAIL 6/6 as expected, 12:45). **The missed row was not re-run.** `H5-run.sh selftest GLANCE` queued at 13:05:40, waited 1 h 52 min for the WebKit lock, then held it waiting for process headroom (1,570-1,681 processes machine-wide against the guard's 1,400, mostly other sessions' `vitest-vscode`, R and esbuild), and was cancelled at 15:18 so the lock went back to the queue. The row is owed: `H5-run.sh selftest GLANCE` (both engines, about 5 min of browser time).
