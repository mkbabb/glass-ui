## Library faults met

- **New, Carousel (Patch).** `v-model:active` is one-way.
  - `useProvideCarousel` reads the model once (`useDeck(count, { initial: active.value })`, `src/components/carousel/useCarousel.ts`), and nothing feeds a later write back into the deck.
  - So a `PagerDots` bound to the same ref, the composition the old page shipped and the component's own comment calls "the ONE authority", changes the dots but never moves the strip. Measured: after pressing dot 3, every slide still read `active,next,next,next,next` and `scrollLeft` stayed 0.
  - The stage drives the dots through the default slot's documented `deck` and `index` instead.
  - Cure: watch the model and call `deck.go` when it differs from `deck.index`.
- **F3 (muted ink in light).** The untracked ToC rows on the old page used the muted rung. The rebuilt list sets every entry in `--foreground` and the tracked entry in `--primary`, so this page meets no muted run of its own. SegmentedTabs' unselected labels and the underline's inactive tabs are the library's own ink, and were not measured against F3 here.
- **Observed, not a fault.** At a coarse pointer, PagerDots grows each hit cell to 44 px, and it has no overflow behaviour of its own. Eight or more dots on a phone need `window-fit`.

## Scaffolding notes (not patched)

- **`StageSwap`'s 120 ms leave** reached 0.39 of its travel in one frame at 390, just under P0a's 0.40. A busier phone could cross it. A longer leave would give it room.
- **`SectionPreviewCard`** gives its specimen no block size. A stage that fills its box (`block-size: 100%` on a tall stage) gets `auto` on a card, and any scroller inside it then grows to its whole content.
  - The ToC stage now bounds itself with `max-block-size: var(--story-stage-tall)` and a `minmax(0, 1fr)` row.
  - A later tall stage with an inner scroller will meet the same thing. The card could give its specimen the tall stage's height.
- **The probe's `legs()` helper** (P7's `probe.mjs`) dropped any leg that held flat in the middle, as a fade does when it pauses at 0. My copy splits legs at reversals and ignores flat frames (`shots/P8/tools/probe.mjs`).
