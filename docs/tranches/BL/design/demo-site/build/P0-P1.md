## P0, Shell

- **Ground.** `vite.dark-stamp.ts` puts a `<style>` ahead of the dark-mode script: `rgb(43 34 27)` dark, `rgb(252 241 230)` light. `AppShell.vue` mounts a static `.shell-ground` plane on every route, and the live Aurora mounts only where `route.meta.ownsCanvas` is false.
- **Rail.** Vertical, open by default, folds after a press in the page and opens on hover. Folded, it is one disc showing the current section's icon. The foot holds the dark toggle and the gear. The facet menu is gone. The column is fixed at 80 px.
- **Pager.**
  - Folded by default; it opens on hover, or on press for a coarse pointer.
  - It is laid over the end of `main`, with clearance `5.75rem + safe area`.
  - The folded face names the page.
  - Previous and Next walk every page on the site in order and wrap at the ends, so neither is ever disabled.
  - Its plate tier is the one `--dock-*` override (F6).
- Deleted: `DockFacetMenu.vue`, `dock-layer-contexts.ts`, `useContextualDockLayers.ts`, `useShellNavDock.ts`. `focal.ts` shrank to `OWN_CANVAS_ROUTES` and `ownsCanvas()`.
- **Brand face.** `index.html` preloads the Plus Jakarta Sans Latin woff2. Vite rewrites the link to the same hashed asset the `@font-face` uses.

## P1, Page scaffolding

- **Manifest.** One `routes.ts` per section, assembled in `manifest.ts`. Ledes are 16 words or fewer, section ledes 12 or fewer. The intro row is merged into `/`.
- **Chassis.** New: `StoryPage`, `StoryStage` (tall or fit, on a paper-wash plate with the floating edge; fit is content height with a 16rem floor, the instance centred on both axes), `StoryVariants`, `StoryVariantRow`, `StageSwap`, `StoryCode`, `StoryEditor`, `StoryEditorRow`.
