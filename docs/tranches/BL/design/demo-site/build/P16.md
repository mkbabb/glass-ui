## 2. A focus ring on a stage's control draws whole

**What was wrong.** `.stage-swap { overflow: clip }` (`StageSwap.vue:48`) clipped whatever the swap holds to the swap's box at all times. Where the swap shrinks to one button (Dialog, Popover), the button's focus ring, 2 px wide at a 2 px offset, was cut to its four corner arcs.

**What the clip was hiding.** Read on the P15 build before the change, two ways:

- Boxes (`.p16/survey.mjs`, `shots/P16/lift/survey-p15.jsonl`): on the 20 routes whose stage uses StageSwap, in every variant option, and on the section pages whose cards mount those stages, at 1440 and 390, one descendant box reaches past its swap: Timeline's last mark at 390 with touch (`li.tl__mark-seat`, `button.tl__mark`), 14 px past the right edge. It is the mark's touch seat and paints nothing: the stage shot with the clip lifted is the same pixel for pixel outside the swap (`lift/unclip/timeline-m390-pair.png`).
- Pixels (`.p16/lift.mjs`, `lift/lift-p15.txt`): each page shot with the clip as built and again with it lifted, compared outside each swap's box inside its stage or card, in three cells. Of 258 reads, 23 change. They are the focus rings on Dialog's and Popover's triggers (all three cells), and the drop shadows of Cards and plates inside the swap, cut flat at its edge: Empty States, Command, Surface at Overlay and Opaque, Music Staff, Dialog's Delete button, and the Empty States, Command and Music Staff cards (1440 light, where the shadows show; `lift/unclip/cmp-*.png`, the clipped edge above, the whole shadow below). The last two are 6 and 4 px of a one-pixel row above the Colors stage's swap in its card on `/` (the Foundations card) and on `/foundations`, at 390.

None of it is a leaving pane, a scrollbar or a painted overhang. Out-in mode means the two panes are never on the stage together.

**The cure.** The swap needs the clip only while its box is shorter than its new content, which is while its height travels. So `StageSwap` clips only then: `travelling` is set when the height animation starts and cleared on its finish or cancel (a newer travel cancels an older one, and only the current run clears the flag), and the style is `.stage-swap[data-travel] { overflow: clip }`. A switch with no height change, or with reduced motion, never clips. `overflow: clip` makes no scroll container, so lifting it changes no box: Dialog's ring box, stage top (153) and stage share (0.256) read the same on both builds.

An `overflow-clip-margin` sized to the ring was the other road. It would still cut every shadow listed above, and would need the ring's size kept in step with the library's focus style.

### 3. The shadow edge at the end of a height travel
