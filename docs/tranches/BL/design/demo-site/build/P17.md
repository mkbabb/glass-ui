In Chromium an ancestor with opacity below 1, or with a clip-path, stops a descendant's `backdrop-filter` sampling what is behind that ancestor; the element's own opacity does not, nor does an overflow clip. The pane faded whole, and the travel clipped with `clip-path`, so a card's glass went unfrosted for the switch and frosted in one frame when the travel's clip lifted. WebKit samples through both.

The cure, in StageSwap: the fade moves each glass element by its own opacity, and each part that holds no glass, passing through the bare wrappers between them (a wrapper that paints no fill, image, edge, shadow, text or pseudo-element); a pane with no glass, or with text of its own, still fades whole. The travel's clip is `overflow: clip` with `overflow-clip-margin: 3rem` where the engine has it (the same cut 3rem out as before, so a shadow is never cut), and `clip-path: inset(-3rem)` where it does not (WebKit, whose glass samples through a clip-path).
