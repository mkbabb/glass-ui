## The pages

All four are editor pages: header, then the studio and nothing after it. Each has one canvas of its subject, presets as the editor's chip row, layers that open one at a time, and one control per row with no description line.

- **Aurora.** One field. Ten presets: Setting sun, Dusk, Sky, Meadow, Pastel, Oil, Van Gogh, Oil pastel, Crayon, Metal. Three layers (Field, Motion, Finish), 24 rows. Seed, Harmony and Stops derive a new palette. The stage holds the field and one pause control. Gone: the thumbnail ribbon and `usePresetThumbnails.ts`, the hint lines, the second heading, the nucleus rings, the renderer badge, the pointer caption, the probe query hooks.
- **Blob.** One Blob. Six presets, Plain among them (flat fill, no light, no shadow). Three layers (Character, Surface, Satellites), 16 rows. Pause is one button on the stage. Gone: the second stage, the pause section, the renderer badge, the mood and click read-out, Reset clicks, Poke.
- **Constellation.** One field. Five presets: Drift, Warp, Wander, Gravity well, Pinned. Two layers (Field, Behaviour), 9 rows; the strength ceiling is a slider. Gone: the six mounts and the four `window.__constellation*` hooks. No pause control, because the component has none (L1 below).
- **Fourier Field.** One field. Nine presets, one per traced shape: Generated, Script F, Heart, Star and the five figures. Three layers (Machine, Curve, Head), 13 rows. The stage holds the field and one pause control. Gone: the 115-word blurb, the transport bar and its read-out, the renderer badge. Scrubbing is the field's own pointer and keyboard.

Each page has a `<id>.stage.vue` (the subject alone) and a `<id>.tile.vue`. A tile is needed because the card shows the bare substrate, parked (FORM section 3). It mounts the same stage inert, lets it draw for 1.6 s, then parks it until the card is hovered or holds focus. Parking is `pause()` on Aurora and Fourier Field, `paused` on Blob, and a frozen remount on Constellation (L1).

Words: 9 on each page, 14 on Fourier Field. Ledes: 7, 7, 7, 11 words.
