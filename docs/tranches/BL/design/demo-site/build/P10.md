## Library faults met (none patched)

- **New: at phone width every Toaster position anchors to the top.** In `src/components/toast/Toaster.vue`, `VIEWPORT_ANCHOR`'s bottom arms are `top-0` with `flex-col-reverse` below `sm`, and the top arms are top at every width. The viewport is full width. On `/feedback/toast` at 390, four toasts cover y 16 to 446: the header, the stage and its triggers. A press on a covered trigger cannot land until the toasts leave; Playwright's click waited about 5 s, hence the 1, 2, 1, 2, 3 count. A consumer has no prop for a bottom anchor on phones. Release: 11.2.0 if it adds an option, Patch if the bottom arms are made bottom on phones.
- **Observed, not filed:** each toast's close control sits on the toast's top-left corner, half outside its plate. This is the library's design, recorded only so a reviewer reading the raised crops does not take it for a demo fault.
- **F3 (muted ink in light)** was not met in text this packet authored. Runs inside the library's Alert description (`text-card-foreground/90`) and ToastDescription were not measured for contrast by the checker (line 9 reads header and labels only), and I did not measure them separately.

## Scaffolding notes (not patched)

1. **Checker line 8** counts words by whitespace, so ROUTES.md's own labels written with a middle dot ("Text · Avatar · Card", 5 tokens) fail it while "Vertical · Liquid" (3 tokens) passes. The checker should count words the way it does elsewhere (its `WORD` regex), or ROUTES should drop the dots.
2. **Checker line 15** is not in the checker. I measured it in the probe (switch and code frames above).
3. **`StoryVariantRow`'s items row is content-sized,** so an instance with a percentage width (a Progress bar is `inline-size: 100%`) collapses to nothing. Pages must give such instances a fixed width. A note in the scaffolding, or a minimum item width, would save the next packet the trip.
