### Choices

- **Auth Shell's words are off the field.** The brand side is the Aurora alone. Every word sits on the form's `--card` surface. The route is already in `OWN_CANVAS_ROUTES`, so the shell's field stays static and the page has one live canvas.
- **Settings' tabs are Account · Display · Alerts.** "Appearance" and "Notifications" ran off a 358 px stage. The appearance controls are honest controls: they hold their own state and do not restyle the site.
- **Empty States' message is a `div`, not a `p`.** The section page's card check L4 ("no card paragraph") counts the card's own message, which is the component's content, not a blurb. A `div` keeps the check meaningful for blurbs.
- **The empty state's glyph is a bare Lucide icon in a section colour, not a Chip.** `Chip shape="icon"` crushes its glyph (library fault L-P12-1 below). The page does not override the chip's padding locally.
- The old mascot Blob on Empty States and the old page-wide grid wash on Settings and Auth Shell are gone.

## Library faults met (none patched)

- **L-P12-1, new: `Chip shape="icon"` crushes its glyph at every size.**
  - Cause: `src/components/chip/chipVariants.ts` emits the size's `px-* py-*` beside the icon shape's `p-0`. `joinClassValues` joins and does not merge, and in Tailwind's order `px-5` wins over `p-0`.
  - Effect at `size="lg"`: the 48 px circle keeps 40 px of inline padding, so the glyph gets 8 px. Measured on the crop of the first build of `/compositions/empty-states`.
  - Cure: the icon shape drops the size's padding (emit no `SIZE` padding when `shape === "icon"`).
  - Release: Patch, since it removes no public name.
- **L-P12-2, new: the SegmentedTabs indicator lands off its tab under an ancestor `scale`.**
  - Cause: `src/composables/motion/morph/useSelectionIndicator.ts:151-152` places the indicator from `getBoundingClientRect()` deltas. Those are in scaled client space, while the indicator's translate and width apply in the unscaled box.
  - Effect: on `/compositions`, the Settings card (scaled to fit by `SectionPreviewCard`) draws the underline across "Account" instead of under it (`shots/P12/compositions-d1440.png`). Any card whose stage holds a SegmentedTabs shows the same.
  - Cure: measure in layout space (`offsetLeft`/`offsetWidth`), or divide by the ancestor scale.
  - Release: Patch.
  - Not papered over with a tile.
- **F3** (muted ink in light) was not met in text this packet authored. Text inside the library's Label, Input placeholder and text-emphasis Button was not measured for contrast (checker line 9 reads the header and labels only).

## Scaffolding notes (not patched)

1. **Checker L4** ("no card paragraph") cannot tell a blurb from a component's own paragraph. A stage that is a card with a message (an empty state, an alert) must avoid `<p>` to pass. Scoping L4 to text outside the card's preview would be truer to FORM.md.
2. **`SectionPreviewCard`'s `scale` fit** interacts with any library geometry measured in client space (L-P12-2). The cure is the library's, but the scaffold is where every section meets it.
