There is no `<id>.tile.vue`. Every stage runs small and inert on its card.

- **Springs, the editor.** There are two layers, Spring (Response, Damping) and Easing (Start x, Start y, End x, End y, Duration). Exactly one is open at a time, and the open layer is the curve being played. Choosing a preset opens its layer, wipes the plot on and plays the curve. Moving a slider replays it 200 ms after the last change. There is no read-out, no copy button and no badge.
- **Springs' curve** is computed in the demo from published surface: `springTimingFunction` and `SpringProgress` from keyframes.js, and `SPRING_PRESETS` from `@glass/composables/motion`. It does not read `springProjection`, which the library does not export.
- **Deck.** The keyboard is bound to the stage's own box (`useDeckKeyboard`'s `target`), not to `window`, so the card on `/motion` cannot take the page's arrow keys. A coarse pointer shows four dots in the window instead of six. That fixes the sideways overflow at 390: the controls row is 324 px wide with a 324 px scroll width, and `main` does not overflow.
- **Text Motion.** The Typewriter loops on its own, so Replay recounts the figures. They tween down to zero and then count up again; they are never reset in one frame.

## Library faults met

- **New, HandMark (Patch).** It measures with client rects, which include transforms, and draws in local CSS pixels.
  - Geometry comes from `Range.getClientRects()` and `getBoundingClientRect()` (`src/components/handmark/HandMark.vue:135`, `:160`, `:242`). Those rects include every ancestor transform, while the SVG it draws sits in the element's own untransformed pixels.
  - Under an ancestor `scale` (the landing card scales a stage down to fit), every mark is drawn short and toward its origin. Measured on `/motion` before the fix: the underline covered "pay", the highlight covered "rea".
  - The `ResizeObserver` it re-measures on does not fire when an ancestor's scale changes.
  - The demo did not patch it. The stage now fits a card at scale 1, so the card shows the marks whole. The fault stays for any consumer that scales a HandMark.
  - Cure: divide the measured rects by the element's own transform scale (`offsetWidth / getBoundingClientRect().width`), or measure from layout boxes.
- **Observed, not filed.** No published composable reports a scrolling element's 0..1 progress.
  - `useScrollProgress` (`motion-core`) maps the viewport's scroll.
  - `useScrollScene`, which takes an element, is not exported.
  - The page computes the column's progress in one line (`scrollTop / (scrollHeight − clientHeight)`) and hands it to `ScrollProgressRim`.
- **F4a family, met.** Deck's Previous and Next are disabled at the ends and painted by the library's disabled ink. Not recoloured.
- **F3.** Text the page authored is all `--foreground`: header min CR 12.15 dark and 12.80 light. Runs inside SegmentedTabs, the Replay button's quiet emphasis and EasingCurve's axis labels are the library's own and were not measured against F3 here.
