**The rules, as built.**

- **E-11.** The grammar decides what each cell expects:
  - A settle that CSS reads as a positive `<time>` is TAKEN, else REFUSED.
  - An easing that `new KeyframeEffect(null, null, {easing})` takes is TAKEN, with its normal form, else REFUSED.
  - On the carry, an easing may be either.
  - TAKEN means every travel runs on CSS's own clock for the row, `calc(var(--spring-<row>-settle) * var(--motion-tempo, 1))` on a probe, to 1 ms, with the parser's easing and no error. REFUSED means at least one console.error naming the token, and no travel.
  - Always: 0 uncaught errors, and a later hold engaged, shown and pinned.
  - The travels are read by a per-frame recorder of WAAPI animations on the lens, the root and under the root (no CSS transitions or animations).
  - Verdicts name the miss: THROWS, MISREAD CLOCK, NOT EXACT, NOT TAKEN, TRAVELS ON A REFUSED TOKEN, SILENT.
- **E-10 hostglide.**
  - Pass: the root's painted centre on the mutation's frame is ≤ 0.5 px from where it was painted, and its largest move over any 16.7 ms is ≤ the move × the glide's row peak + 1 px, both printed. A glide must have run.
  - The `animating` cells pass when no translate starts on the root in 6 frames, and the root's place in C's frame moves ≤ 0.5 px across the mutation's frame.
- **E-10 reach.**
  - Pass: the painted engaged width is within 1 px of min(block grow, 1 + 2·max(0, room − gutter)/width), with every term painted, and the box is no nearer the edge than the painted gutter − 1 px.
  - UNMEASURED when the figure is not gutter-limited.
- **The fixture carries the cured forms.**
  - `clockOf` reads the settle as a `<time>` in its unit. `easingOf` uses the parser. Both are loud by name.
  - The reach is written as a number by the library's rule, with the room over the frame.
  - The host glide runs through the offset chain. Each offset is brought into painted px through its own box's frame (each box's offset is in its own CSS px), and the sum is taken over the root's frame. The chain is read every frame and is instant under reduce.
  - The clipstale carry runs on the travel's clock.

**Engine facts the cells rest on** (`H12/framefacts.mjs`, library-free; `logs/framefacts-chromium.log` and `logs/facts-webkit.log`). The figures are identical in both engines:

- An element's `offsetTop` is in its own CSS px (painted ÷ its zoom).
- A zoom alone puts no box in the offset chain.
- A scaled box becomes the offsetParent.
- A running WAAPI scale changes no offset.

So flowone and hostzoom separate only on a positioned box with another zoom in the chain (the `chain` cells), and glideframe separates only when a per-frame frame read meets a running scale (the `animating` cells).
