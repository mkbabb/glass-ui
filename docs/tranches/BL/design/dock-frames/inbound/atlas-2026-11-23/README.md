# Atlas's docks, filmed frame by frame (O-420 inbound)

**Site** the Connectivity Atlas, production deployment 00fff429 (entry `assets/index-tbcspDpM.js`; atlas `f79d949`, sci-report `34c8fe31`), served from its byte copy `.u-build/out/deploy-u` on port 9288. **glass-ui** 10.1.2, the line atlas ships (`GlassDock`, `DockControl`, `DarkModeToggle`), its source read at `.u-build/sp-integ-glass` `8b4d8f82b`. Atlas source read at `.u-build/o18-atlas` `62405c0`, whose one commit after `f79d949` touches CI files only. **Engines** Chromium 148.0.7778.96 headless shell (Playwright 1.60.0, `--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu-rasterization`) and Playwright 1.60.0's WebKit 26.4 (build 2287), one run. **Widths** 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, a mouse at both. **Schemes** light and dark. Filmed Mon 2026-11-23, 15:20 to 17:28 ET, at a 1-minute load of 20 to 90.

The docks:

- **The rail** (`platform/chrome/dock/Dock.vue`, 768 px and up). On a dashboard it rests as the 44 × 44 crest disc at the top left and opens to a 72 × 531 rail on hover or keyboard focus. On `/` from 1024 px it rests open, 312 px wide.
- **The phone bar** (the same file, under 768 px). 358 × 56 on the bottom edge. It never collapses. It slides out after more than a viewport of downward scroll and back on an upward one.
- **The figure docks** (`charts/frame/PlateDock.vue`), one in each figure's head row: 4 on `/sci`, 6 on `/ecf`, 8 on `/usf`. Each rests as its leading seats (the figure's dials, Table, Enlarge) and More, and opens a tray (Filters, Options, then Raw data or Download) from its own corner. Twelve sit at the start of their row and grow rightward. Six sit at the end of a head and grow leftward: `ecf-charters`, `ecf-district-choropleth`, `usf-fund-ledger`, `usf-net-retention`, `usf-strip` and `usf-breakeven` (each dock is named here by its `data-subject`). Three of their trays (`ecf-district-choropleth`, `usf-fund-ledger`, `usf-net-retention`) are no wider than the dock at rest.

## What the frames show

- **Closing, a figure dock gives its row the open tray's width until glass's morph settles.** From the close's first frame the dock's seat in the page's flow is the tray's width wider (33 to 104 px). It stays so through the whole close and the 275 to 292 ms after the dock looks closed, then gives the width back in one frame, glass's settle frame. Atlas lifts the open dock out of the flow on `[data-expanded]`, which lets go when the close starts; glass lays the dock out at its open width until it settles. This holds on all 14 figure docks whose tray is wider than their rest, at both widths, in both schemes, by every way of closing.
- **At 390 that width wraps two rows, and the dock and the figure jump.** On `sci-bandcake` the dock jumps 13.44 px up and the key and the whole figure jump 64 px down in the close's first frame. On `ecf-charters` the dock drops 83.77 px to a line of its own and the figure drops 64 px. Both hold for about 500 ms and snap back in one frame, 282 to 292 ms after the dock stopped moving. This is the bar moving up and down when an item is pressed (a pick that closes the tray wraps the row the same way: `ecf-charters`' Filters and Raw data at 390), and the collapse's last frames jumping.
- **End-anchored figure docks slide their resting seats sideways.** Table and Enlarge ride the plate's moving start edge, 104 px at 1440 and 88 px at 390, on every open and every close of `ecf-charters`, `usf-strip` and `usf-breakeven`.
- **The rail's crest slides under the pointer as the rail opens**: 14 px right and 14 px down, and back as it closes. The rail's own left and top edges hold to 0.00 px.
- **The rail's close holds the crest off its rest, then snaps it.** The disc lands at 44 × 44 about 270 to 310 ms after the input with the crest 1.16 px above its rest. Nothing moves for 262 to 292 ms. Glass's settle frame then drops the crest 1.16 px.
- **A press on the rail's disc goes home.** By mouse and by touch, the pointer's arrival opens the rail before the press lands, so the press follows the crest's home link. The frames show the homepage's open rail growing out of the disc (+268 px wide, +588 px tall), and the page is `/`.
- **Hovering or pressing a control moves no dock.** Over 600 hover and press motions on every control of every dock, in both engines, the dock's outer box moved 0.00 px on both axes. A pressed figure-dock control squashes, mostly in height (0.8 to 3.0 px), its centre within 0.01 px. The theme toggle in the rail and the bar holds 0.00 px under hover and shrinks 1.3 px under a press, its centre fixed. Toggling the theme moved nothing else in either dock.
- **Long frames take whole steps of a close.** When a pick closes the tray and mounts a surface in the same moment (the filter drawer, the raw data viewer, a panel), a 40 to 177 ms frame lands inside the close and the plate covers 39 to 99 px in one step. It is worst on `/usf`.

**Dark matches light.** Every fault above holds in the dark cells to the pixel; only frame timing differs (see the table under *Numbers per cell*).

**WebKit** 26.4, one run (K, 135 motions): the rail, its touch case, `sci-bandcake` and `sci-schoolmap` at 1440 light; the bar, `ecf-item-mix` and `ecf-charters` at 390 light. Every fault it covered comes out to the same pixels as in Chromium: A1 (103.67 px at 1440; 47.56 and 88 px at 390), A2 on `ecf-charters` (the dock 83.77 px down, the figure 64 px down), A3 (88 px), R1 (14 px right, 14 px down), R2 (1.16 px), R3 (the press lands on `/`), G1 (0.33 to 0.45 px), G2 (−1.28 to −1.31 px) and L1 (41 to 134 ms frames carrying 23 to 89 px of width). Its settle frames land later, 587 to 698 ms after Escape or an outside press, still 265 to 288 ms after the plate stopped. Over 60 hover and press motions the dock's box moved 0.00 px. Under the load WebKit drew at 20 to 45 ms frames, so its steps are larger. On the bar's slide out its frames ran 190 to 250 ms, and the whole 92 px fell between two samples; this film cannot say what the compositor drew in between.

## How it was filmed

`film-probe.mjs` drives one headless browser per run and a new context per case; `sampler.js` samples in the page and `analyse.mjs` reduces each motion. In every rendered frame the sampler reads `getBoundingClientRect` of the dock's outer box (`.glass-dock`) and plate, a figure dock's wrapper and its seat in the row (`.plate-dock-seat`), every control in the dock (its opacity multiplied up to the dock, so a control in a faded pane counts as unseen), and the neighbours: a figure dock's row, the row's siblings, the plate, the head, the key, the figure and the next block; the rail's section pager and `main`. It also reads the dock's computed width, height, `transform`, `scale`, `translate`, radius and pads, its posture, `[data-morphing]` and `--dock-morph-t`, and logs input events on the same clock. Figure docks are read in page coordinates, so a scroll alone moves nothing.

The 1440 light cell (runs A to C) read in our own `requestAnimationFrame` callback. Every later cell read in a `ResizeObserver` callback on a 1 px probe that the rAF loop toggles, after layout and after glass's own observers, as glass's harness does, so a row holds what the frame paints. A second film of the 1440 light rail and three figure docks (`sci-bandcake`, `ecf-charters`, `usf-normflip`) under the `ResizeObserver` read (run A2, 89 motions) matched the first on every box, seat, neighbour and step; settle times moved by up to 160 ms with the load. One frame differed. On the Dashboards pick, the rAF read saw the rail at the new dashboard's height (619 px) for one frame before glass's resize morph started it from 531 px. The `ResizeObserver` read never sees that frame, so it is not painted, and it is not reported.

Motions, per dock:

- **Rail** (`/sci` at 1440): open by hover; hover every control of the open rail; the theme toggle's hover, press and press back; close by the pointer leaving (after the linger), by Escape (opened by keyboard focus), by an outside press (opened by focus, and opened by hover with the press at once), and by scrolling (the pointer leaves and wheels 900 px); pick a section rung, then leave; open and close past the crown, beside the section pager; a press on the disc; open the Dashboards list and pick the Emergency Connectivity Fund. A second press is no way out: the open rail's crest is the home link. A touch case at 1440 taps the disc.
- **Homepage rail** (`/` at 1440): hover every control and the theme toggle; press the theme toggle and press back.
- **Phone bar** (`/sci` at 390): hover every cell; press the theme toggle and press back; press Dashboards and close with Escape, Sections and close with an outside press, Filters and close with Escape; slide out (6 wheel steps of 400 px) and back (one of −300 px).
- **Figure docks**: open by hover intent onto More and close by the pointer leaving; open by a press and press the same spot again; open by a press and close by Escape, by an outside press, and by scrolling 260 px; hover then press every control at rest (each approached from 56 px above it, and put back after); open by a press, then hover and press every tray control (a pick). In the dark cells the figure docks skipped the outside press, the scroll and the tray picks.

Caveats:

- The load lengthens frames, so settle times and long frames are load-sensitive. Box drifts are not.
- Device scale factor 1, and a mouse at 390 (no touch emulation; glass's record used touch at 390), so hover is measured at 390 too. The one touch case is the rail's, at 1440.
- A pointer leaving waits out atlas's linger (half the dwell, 0.4 to 2.4 s) before the close starts. Times count from the input.
- `usf-decade`'s Play at 390 shrank the page above the dock by 72 px while the years played. The browser's scroll anchoring held the dock within 0.48 px on screen, so it is no dock motion and is left out of the hover-and-press tally.

## Checked and clean

- **Hover and press.** The dock's outer box held 0.00 px on both axes through every hover and press on every control of the rail, the homepage rail, the bar and the 18 figure docks (600 motions: 540 in Chromium, 60 in WebKit). The only control whose hover opens anything is More (the tray, by design).
- **The rail's edges.** Its left and top edges held 0.00 px through every open and close; it grows only right and down. Its visible corner stays one radius in every frame (the computed radius is counter-scaled: 36 × 265.5 px at a 0.611 × 0.083 scale draws 22 px on both axes).
- **The bar.** No horizontal motion in any motion (0.00 px). Theme, Dashboards, Sections and Filters move nothing but their own press (theme −1.3 px, the select cells −1.76 px, both centred). The slide out and back moves only vertically (92 px). The slide back lands in 8 ms steps of 0.8 to 2.0 px, with no end jump. The slide out takes 54 to 162 ms frames of 9 to 33 px while the page scrolls under the wheel.
- **Toggling the theme.** The rail and the bar held every seat at 0.00 px through the flip, in both directions, both widths and both schemes. Glass's record found every seat on fourier moving 4 px in one frame on the theme press; atlas's seats do not move.
- **Opening a figure dock.** The seat held its rest width (0.00 px) through every open on every dock but `sci-scatter-stage` (A4); the dock's anchored edge held 0.00 px.
- **Ways out.** Escape, an outside press and the pointer leaving close every figure dock and the rail. Scrolling does not close a tray opened by a press (36 of 36: the press pins it); a hover-opened rail closes on its linger while the page scrolls. A second press on More's spot is no way out: once the tray is open that spot holds its last seat, and the press acts on it (Filters 22 times and Raw data 12 times, which close the tray; Options 36 times and Download twice, which keep it open).
