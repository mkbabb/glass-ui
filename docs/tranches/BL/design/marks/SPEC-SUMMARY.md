# Marks: the spec in one paragraph

Three seats wrote W-B.16 (all claude-fable-5-2, 2026-11-12): the spec seat ran nothing and quotes `AB.md`; the challenge (`challenge.md`) broke nine things on the winning prototype in paint; the fold seat (`fold.md`) amended the prototype for each and measured the amendments in Chromium headless shell and Playwright WebKit. Proposal A is the design: the mark is a CSS text decoration and the component, `TextMark` at `./text-mark`, only draws it in. Two shapes survive, underline and highlight, as the stylesheet classes `mark-underline` and `mark-highlight`, so a mark needs no script and reads the same on the server, in print and under forced colours. A was the only candidate to pass every hard constraint the A/B harness could witness and paint a no-script mark equal to rest (coverage 1.00): 0 descender crossings, 0 layout reads in the mount task, `transform` alone on the `present` spring, one mark per line box, RTL from the right, and the smallest entry (2,854 bytes gzip against the control's 7,301; 2,978 with the fold's amendments). B's ink-tight ends (0.5 px) were not taken because its fallback changes shape on hydration and its ends cannot be grafted onto a decoration; atlas's proposal failed the mount-read and no-script constraints, but its diagnosis of "by program" (O-265) is right and is answered by a third class, `mark-clear`, that the author chooses per phrase: one unbroken rule 0.26em under the baseline with nothing skipped (1 piece and 0 crossings in 96 of 96 rows in each engine; the spec's first seat of 0.2em met the descenders and broke). The fold changed seven more things: a selected highlight keeps its band and its words (its `::selection` repeats the decoration over the band as ground); a mark draws when any part of it is in view, so a clipped one is no longer left blank; a host that is not an inline box stands drawn with a dev warning; a larger child no longer shifts the copy; the copy carries only `class` and `style` and its layer is `inert`; a draw lands when the page's fonts are ready; and the band's chroma follows the ink's, so a black ink no longer gives a pink band. Known shortfalls, stated in the spec: the ends follow the advance box (3.5 px past the ink of "4" at 112 px, and one letter-space more on positively tracked type), seats of 0.10 to 0.16em are unsupported because the engines' skip-ink fails there, 0.26em is a constant and not a font metric, Chromium keeps a 2.5 px stub after a final descender, the host's `textContent` holds the copies while a mark draws, WebKit's stall cannot be measured by this method, and the stall, still-state and scroll-stop gates were not rerun on the amended build. The release splits: `TextMark` and the classes ship in 11.2.0 beside an untouched HandMark, and HandMark, `./handmark`, the strike and the ring are removed in 12.0.0. The wave carries three static gates, five in vitest and nine browser cells (seven in both engines), and the owner's page (`OWNER-READ.md`) asks four questions with defaults.
