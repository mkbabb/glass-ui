# Horizontal bounding: the study

The owner, 2026-11-23, in atlas's dark mode: "Many items can horizontally scroll, like the fiters view, which should not--a design study should be done for every interactive element for this bounding error, too--communicate to value.js for their pages, glass-ui, etc".

Three faults were looked for in every interactive part:

- **(a)** content wider than its box: a `scrollWidth` over `clientWidth` where no sideways scroll is meant, or a child past its parent's edge;
- **(b)** a scroller that pans sideways under a vertical swipe, or accepts a horizontal pan it should not;
- **(c)** a page wider than the viewport.

A deliberate horizontal strip (a carousel, a long dock run, a table, a code block) may pan by a horizontal gesture, never by a vertical one. The cure is at the root and the smallest that works. `overflow-x: hidden` or `clip` on a wrapper to hide a box that is too wide is a mask, not a cure, unless the record shows the box is right and only its paint spills.

Read on glass `release/11.1.1` (4f1fa667e). `cure.patch` (v6) applies to it with `git apply` and is what 11.1.2 takes from this study: round 1's cure (v4, kept as `cure-v4.patch`), value.js's items 1 to 3 (round 2), and round 3's answers to the second critique (below). Round 2's patch, with value.js's item 4, is kept as `cure-v5.patch`; item 4 is now the owner's choice 9. **Chromium** is Playwright's Chromium headless shell 149.0.7827.55; **WebKit** is Playwright's WebKit 26.5, not Safari; **Safari** was not run, and no iPhone was read. A number with no engine named is Chromium's. The method, every table and the record are in `SWEEP.md`; the critique of the first cure and the rulings on it are in `critique.md` and `fold.md`.

## Round 2: value.js's items

value.js's census of color, keyframes, fourier and daraksha (`inbound/value-js-2026-11-23.md`) named four items at v11.1.0. Each was re-found in the 11.1.1 tree, built into v5 (now `cure-v5.patch`), and read on the base (RED), v4 and v5 (GREEN) in Chromium at 390×844 by touch and at 1440×900, on cells added to the fold page (`/ovf.html?fold`, `tools/app/Stress.vue`; record in `record/inspect/witness5*.json`, tables in `SWEEP.md` §6).

### Item 1: a dock run whose seats all show

The census placed the cause at the reserve boxes (`run.css:426-432`). Those boxes are right; the run around them is 4 px short. A horizontal run's width is its content box (`width: 100%` on the full layer, `layers.css:395-397`, with `box-sizing: content-box`, `run.css:355`). Its padding gives the ring its room and its negative margins hand that room back at both ends (`run.css:356-357`), so its clip reaches the reserve past its slot. Under the reserve boxes the end padding was set to 0 (`run.css:422-424`), and the end margin still took the reserve back: the clip stopped at the slot's end. A run whose seats fill their slot then had the trailing box's 4 px as a scroll range, and its last seat's ring was cut at rest.

v5 keeps the end padding and gives the trailing box an end margin of its own length, so the box lies in the end padding, the padding's twin, as the leading box is the leading padding's. The run's outer box is its slot again.

The vertical arm's form (the reserve yields to the room the seats leave) was not taken. It would remove the range by cutting the end seats' rings by up to the reserve. v5 removes the range and keeps the ring's whole room wherever the seats fill their slot. Where the host is 1 to 8 px narrower than the dock asks, the seats pass their slot, and in Chromium the run keeps a range of that overrun (4 px for a 4 px squeeze, half the base's 8), which is what lets the run's reach bring a focused end seat's ring into view (read: the run scrolled 4 px and the ring had 4.4 px). Playwright's WebKit differs there ("In WebKit" below). `check-overflow.mjs` now reports a strip that shows all its content and still scrolls, so a site sees this case.

### Item 4: vertical scrollers that pan sideways (not in 11.1.2; choice 9)

A box with `overflow-y: auto` computes `overflow-x: auto`, so one pixel of spill (a scaled child, a rounded width) makes it a sideways scroller. v5 set `overflow-x: hidden` beside `overflow-y: auto` on every such box in glass's source. Round 3 takes that out of 11.1.2 and gives it to the owner, because it hides a box the study keeps too wide.

The boxes v5 changed, which choice 9 would change: `.fading-scroll--y` (`base-misc.css:73-74`; it carries the Select viewport, the Command list and the Configurator's controls column, and its comment at `:47-50` says the modifier sets the other axis, which the code does not do); the Sheet's scrolling region (`sheet/styles.css:129-133`) and its detented body rows (`:191-199`); `.card-scroll-host` (`card/scroll.css:40-42`); DialogContent with `scroll` (`DialogContent.vue:218`); the reflowed deck slide (`deck/styles/stage.css:149`); the vertical Deck strip (`:101`, `:106-109`) and the vertical Carousel strip (`carousel/styles.css:21`, `:29-34`); and `.fading-scroll--x`, which would take `overflow-y: hidden`. Already hidden at 11.1.1: the overlay plates (`overlay-plate.css:68`, `:97`, `:146`), the Command list (`command/styles.css:87`) and the vertical dock run (`run.css:480`). Left alone in any case: the Table container, the textarea, and the horizontal Carousel and Deck strips.

Why it is not built:

- The law allows a clip only where the record shows the box is right and only its paint spills. The record has one real case that is not paint: choice 1's default keeps a long word whole in a Button, Chip or toggle item, and the word runs past the control. In the scroll Dialog at 390 touch, a Button labelled with the stress token ends 7.1 px past the plate's padding edge. On the base a 200 px sideways swipe pans the plate 7 px and the word's end shows. On v5 the plate is still 355/348 and the same swipe pans 0, so the end of the word cannot be reached (`record/inspect/round3.chromium.json`; the critic's own run read the same). At 1440 nothing overflows there.
- The paint v5 stopped in glass's own scrollers was a 1 px block the witness planted, and F7's Chip scale, which is itself choice 5. No real pan in a glass scroller was read in either round, apart from value.js's report of 1 px in daraksha's colour bench.
- `clip` is no way out: beside a scrolling axis it computes to `hidden` (CSS Overflow 3).

So the default keeps 11.1.1's `auto`: a word kept whole stays reachable by a sideways pan, and the check reports the pan as fault (b), which points at the control. If the owner picks choice 1's alternative (the word breaks inside the control), the case goes away and choice 9 hides paint only.

v5's readings stand for the alternative: with a 1 px spill planted in each box, a 150 px sideways swipe panned the base and v4 1 px in the FadingScroll, the card host, the vertical Carousel and Deck strips, the Sheet region and the detented body row, 13 px in the reflowed deck slide (390 touch), and 13 and 21 px in the base Dialog; v5 panned 0 in each, with the spill still in `scrollWidth` (301/300).
