# Horizontal bounding: the sweep (O-422 item 2)

Recorded 2026-11-23 by claude-opus-5-5. The owner, in atlas's dark mode: "Many items can horizontally scroll, like the fiters view, which should not--a design study should be done for every interactive element for this bounding error, too--communicate to value.js for their pages, glass-ui, etc". Atlas's Filters view is a non-modal right `SheetContent` whose body is atlas's own `overflow-y: auto` box (`atlas/src/filter/ui/FilterPanel.vue:314`, branch `glass-10.1` at 069708a). A box with `overflow-y: auto` computes `overflow-x: auto` too, so anything inside it that is wider than the body turns the body into a sideways scroller.

Every reading names its engine. **Chromium** is Playwright's Chromium headless shell 149.0.7827.55. **WebKit** is Playwright's WebKit build 26.5 (the version the browser reports). It is not Safari. No Safari reading was taken.

The build is glass `release/11.1.1` at 4f1fa667e, taken by `git archive` into `trees/` (never a worktree) and built with the demo's own config plus one stress page, `/ovf.html`. The cure is `cure.patch`, which applies to `release/11.1.1` with `git apply`. `critique.md` read cure v3; `fold.md` rules on each of its objections, and the patch is now cure v4 (below).

## 1. Faults

With the demo's own content nothing pans sideways and no page widens: in the base, opens and variants passes (Chromium: the 78 demo routes at 390 touch and 1440; WebKit: 21 demo routes at 390 touch and 18 at 1440, less the passes its crashes cut short, §4), no box the design does not mean to pan could pan. Every other fault shows once a part holds a longer label than the demo carries, a long phrase or a long word, which is what a consumer's real content brings: atlas's option and measure names, district names, file names.

### 1.1 F1 SegmentedTabs

The strip is an `inline-grid` with `grid-auto-columns: 1fr`. A `1fr` column's floor is its tab's min-content, and the tab is `white-space: nowrap`, so the strip is at least the sum of its labels, with no bound from its container. Inside an `overflow-y: auto` box (a drawer body, the demo's main column) the strip makes that box a sideways scroller; on the stress page, where the strip sits in a plain 200 px column, the document widens instead.

The cure bounds the strip and lets it pan its own tabs, the form the dock run already has: `max-inline-size: 100%`, `overflow-x: auto`, `overscroll-behavior-x: contain` and `scrollbar-width: none`. The columns stay `1fr`, so a strip that fits keeps equal tabs, and every tab keeps its whole label. The selection engine already scrolls a selected tab into view (`useSelectionGroup`), and the indicator already measures through a scroll (`useSelectionIndicator`). v3 ended each label in an ellipsis inside a `minmax(0, 1fr)` share instead; for eight tabs in 267 px that left every tab 32.6 px wide with its label cut (32.4 at 1440), under the coarse 44 px target, and the tab has no inline floor (`segmented.css:189` sets only a block floor). The fold read it and ruled it a mask (`fold.md`, B2).

The fold's eight-tab cell, Chromium (`record/inspect/witness4*.chromium.json`): at 390 touch the box reads 651/267 and a horizontal swipe on it panned it 206 px; with v4 the box reads 267/267 and does not pan, the strip reads 654/267 with each tab 68 to 102 px wide, the same swipe panned the strip instead, three vertical swipes that start on the strip (straight, 16 px and 48 px drift) left its `scrollLeft` where it was, and selecting the eighth tab scrolled it into view with the indicator on it. At 1440: 854/267 → 267/267.

How to read these rows. The page width is the document's `scrollWidth`: the stress page is 487 px wide at 390 and 1,501 at 1440 before the cure, from the strip and the long words in its 200 px column (c). The last value is the box's `scrollLeft` after a 150 px horizontal swipe on it. Playwright's WebKit has no touch-move input, so the swipe did nothing there and WebKit's value is always 0; the WebKit rows show the box's sideways room (`scrollWidth` past `clientWidth`), not a pan. The drawer body is 339 px wide in Chromium at 390 and 266.5 px in WebKit: Chromium's `isMobile` emulation lays the page out at the document's width (487 px) when the document is wider than the screen, and the fixed sheet takes its width from that. After the cure the document is 390 px wide and both engines give the body 266.5 px.

The underline variant (`segmented.css:245`) is a flex row of the same tabs and takes the same rule (`/compositions/settings`, underline: +1,421 px at base). The vertical variant (`:84`) scrolls on either axis its box limits. The strip clips its content at its padding box now. A focused tab's outline (the browser's own; the tab sets none) has the pill's track trim to draw in (3 px, 4 px from 640 px); the underline material has no trim, so where its outline passes the strip's edge it is cut. That is read from the CSS; no pixel was measured. A consumer who would rather collapse an overfull strip to its Select has the `responsive` prop, which today keys on the viewport, not the box.

### 1.2 F2, F2b, F2c: a single grid column with no floor

The Sheet's region, the detented Sheet, the Dialog plate and the Toaster's body are each a one-column grid with no column template. The implicit column is `auto`, whose floor is the widest child's min-content, so one long word or one wide row sets the column wider than the plate. In the detented sheet the overhang is cut by the sheet's own `contain: layout paint` (the record at `styles.css:538`: "a narrow side rung clips along that axis"). `minmax(0, 1fr)` gives the column a floor of 0: the rows lay out at the plate's width, and the line law breaks what is still too long.

The line law is two rules in `typography/semantic.css`. The surfaces that hold prose (overlay plates, Select content, Command, Dialog, Sheet, Toast, disclosure content) and the Label take `overflow-wrap: break-word`, which breaks a word only where it would overflow and leaves every intrinsic size alone, so a short word in a narrow table cell inside a dialog never splits. The rows a plate gives its width to (menu and Command rows, `.glass-menu-row`; the Accordion trigger, `.disclosure-group-trigger`; the Select trigger) take `anywhere`, which also gives the word up in their min-content: the label is a flex item, and a flex item cannot shrink below a word that will not break. Their width comes from outside, so `anywhere` splits only a word wider than the whole row.

v3 gave `anywhere` to Buttons, Chips, ToggleGroup items and Labels too. Those size to their own label, so wherever a layout reads their min-content, `anywhere` lets it narrow them to a letter a line: the fold read v3 on a Table of Chip and Button cells in a 300 px box (Chromium), and the table stopped scrolling (327/300 → 300/300) with "Download" on 3 lines and each Chip on 2; four Buttons in a 240 px row went from 1, 3, 1, 1 lines to 8, 10, 6, 6 at 390 touch and 6, 5, 5, 5 at 1440. The library ruled the same of Badge (`src/components/badge/index.ts:22-24`). v4 takes them out, and both cells read as the base.

What v4 leaves: a Button, Chip or ToggleGroup item whose label is one word wider than its container runs past it, as in the base (Chip 258/200, Button 275/156). A cure that keeps both would put the label in its own box with `min-inline-size: 0` and `overflow-wrap: break-word` and cap the control at its container (`max-inline-size: 100%`; Button has it, ToggleGroup item now has it, Chip does not): the control's min-content stays its longest word, so a table scrolls and a squeezed row keeps its words, while a control capped at its container breaks a word wider than that. It changes each part's markup (the slot moves into a span, which also changes how a slotted icon sits), so it is not built. The Label reading is the demo's: `.inputs-stage-field` is a one-column grid with no template (the F2 pattern in demo CSS), and its `auto` column holds the label's word open.

### 1.5 F4b PagerDots, not cured

PagerDots draws every dot unless the consumer passes `windowFit`. Seven coarse targets need 266 px; in a 200 px column the row centres them and spills 33 px past each side, past the screen's left edge where no scroll can reach. A rule cannot choose how many dots to show. Two cures for the owner to pick from: PagerDots measures its box and windows itself when `count × pitch` passes it (a default `windowFit`), or the consumer passes `windowFit`. `justify-content: safe center` alone would keep the row off the left edge but still spill right, so it is not offered as the cure.
