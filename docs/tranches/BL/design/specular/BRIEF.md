# The specular dot: brief

Owner (2026-11-15, relayed): "the glass specular dot for buttons and the like needs refinement." His frame is `owner-frame-118.png` here (a 160x80 crop of daraksha's theme seat in dark: a gold crescent in a gold ring; enlarge it). Earlier, on a keyframes stage pane (13 November): "hovering over a pane introduces a strange circular specular item."

Standing law that binds the answer: "NO overuse of shading, transition, or markup--this is an AI smell. Have touch like apple."; nothing changes instantly; glass in proportion; cut small bits and tells. `docs/precepts/restraint.md` is read first.

What it is in source: the pointer-anchored catch-light, one shared `::before` radial recipe (`glass-specular-track`, the `v-specular` directive, `--glass-specular-size`; dock controls set 22 %).

The family: dock seats and DockControl, icon buttons, Button, slider thumbs, the seal, tabs, Card and panes (the 13 November case), anything carrying `glass-specular-track` or the material group's `::before`.

Owed: what Apple does on small glass controls (read, not assumed); two or three variants, one of which is "none at small sizes"; each shown on a real dock seat, an icon button and a slider thumb at 1x and 2x, dark and light, at rest, on hover and on press; the choice's cost in tokens and CSS; the release.
