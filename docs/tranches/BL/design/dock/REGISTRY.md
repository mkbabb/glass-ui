### D2-F · Discrete rungs, carried by the user agent: RETIRE, 28%

**Refutation.** The prototype's own status was "BLOCKED under D-4" (F-proto header), because WebKit has no element-scoped View Transitions (S-27; standards position #611 open). The critic measured that the missing primitive explains only one defect. The other five were measured in Chromium, which has it, and each is specified behaviour (F-crit §7):
- a click during a rung change falls through the whole scope (up to 1,216×122 px around a 56 px pill) to the page;
- the lens is flat for the whole morph (sharpness 29-31 against 0.16-0.29 at rest);
- no retarget: +299.03 px in one frame on a click-away at +90 ms;
- a content change cannot be routed, because a transition must start before the DOM changes (179.33 px in one frame);
- the snapshot model paints three stadiums at once, with doubled glyphs;
- one transition per scope, so two docks in one parent pre-empt or abort (`InvalidStateError`).

These contradict D-1, D-2, W3's content cells and E-2, so the centre is refuted on its best engine. The instant-commit WebKit path is a masked fallback behind a `console.warn` (E-2).

**Open gaps, as recorded** (F-crit §8 items 1-20, source of record). They are not pass-2 work: the centre is retired.

**Re-trigger** (F-crit §7): a user-agent morph carrier that retargets with velocity, keeps hit testing on its subject, keeps `backdrop-filter` live inside the group, and ships in both Chromium and WebKit. None is on a public roadmap.

## 6 · New family: D2-G · the sliding aperture (compositor-only extent)

**Where it comes from.** Four pass-1 readings point at one formulation that no family tried:
- every clip animation runs on the main thread, and a 1.2 s block freezes the plate while transforms keep moving (S-17, D-crit §4.7);
- B′'s carrier animates layout insets, which canon P5 forbids without an amendment (Q-6);
- a box `scale` squashes the corner (W-8), which is why every family left it;
- W §10.4 names an unmeasured hybrid: a script solves the damped spring in closed form from the current position and velocity at each retarget and hands a new `linear()` to WAAPI, which keeps the compositor path and the velocity.

**Charter.** Everything that moves is a transform.
- **The aperture.** The plate is one element at the reserved footprint, and it carries the dock's one `backdrop-filter` (floor row 6). It sits inside two nested clip boxes, one per moving edge. Each clip box is `overflow: clip` with `border-radius: calc(var(--dock-cross) / 2)` on the full cross axis, and translates along the main axis while its content counter-translates by the same amount (the sliding-door pattern). The visible window is the intersection of the two boxes: a stadium with an exact cross/2 corner at every frame, and no scale anywhere.
- **The anchor.** The leading door moves by (1 − a)·Δ and the trailing door by a·Δ, with a ∈ {0, ½, 1} (K-11).
- **Seats.** Seats FLIP by `translate` on the same solved spring, so they travel with the edges. The per-seat obligation (F-9) applies here as it does to B′.
- **The carrier.** Each moving quantity keeps (x, v) in script only at a retarget. The script solves the spring from that state and starts a WAAPI animation with the solved `linear()`. Between retargets the compositor runs the motion.
- **Everything else** comes from the floor: endpoints (F-5), P2's default (Q-2 a), P4 (F-4), the rim as a plate child (F-2).

**Centre:** compositor-carried geometry with velocity handed off at each retarget. **Optimizes:** canon P5 without an amendment, a morph that keeps moving under a main-thread stall, and a corner that is exact by construction.

**Questions it must answer first, in order** (each is also its BLOCK test):
1. **The lens.** Does the plate's `backdrop-filter` stay live and clipped round inside two nested, translating, rounded `overflow: clip` boxes? The backdrop-root triggers in Filter Effects 2 list no `overflow` and no transform, but S-28 found a `clip-path` ancestor kills the blur in Chromium, so this is measured before anything else. Chromium is measurable headless; WebKit needs real Safari (S-3). If the lens dies in Chromium, **BLOCK**: the missing primitive is a compositor-carried rounded aperture compatible with `backdrop-filter`.
2. **The compositor claim.** Under a 1.2 s main-thread block (S-17's calibration), does the extent keep moving in both engines?
3. **The velocity handoff.** At a mid-flight reversal, is the velocity continuous (H-3c's reversal row), against S-16's wall? If a WAAPI replacement cannot carry velocity, the carrier degrades to B′'s, and G is **RETIRED** as dominated.
4. **Edge and elevation.** The border and drop shadow must follow the moving silhouette with one `glass-plate` element and no nested translucent `color-mix()`. The plate's own border sits at the footprint's edges, which A-proto found a clip cuts off. Candidates: D's `evenodd` complement on a sibling layer carried by the same doors, or the doors painting their own rounded edge. Seams where the two doors overlap on the cross axis are a named risk.
5. **Hit testing.** Does a rounded `overflow: clip` clip hit testing to the window in both engines? That would answer Q-7 (a) with no extra mechanism.

**Engines.** Transforms, `overflow: clip`, `border-radius` and WAAPI with `linear()` easing ship in both engines (`linear()`: Chrome 113, Safari 17.2, W §10). The composition is unmeasured everywhere.

**Known risks.**
1. Two extra wrapper boxes, with counter-translation arithmetic on every moving edge (P-1).
2. Seams on the cross-axis edges where the two doors overlap.
3. A static clip on an ancestor of the plate, if an engine treats it as a backdrop root (question 1).
4. The closed-form hybrid has no prior art (W §13).
