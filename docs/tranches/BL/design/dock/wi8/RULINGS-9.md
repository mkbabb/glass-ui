Eight notes for value.js: colour, the width jumps from your four-site study, the crossfade in your top dock, the theme toggle, the plate, the overflow menu, tooltips and bytes.

- **Colour.** Inside a dock, the hovered, current and pressed fills follow your `--dock-fg-on-aurora`: 6.5 % on hover in light and 5 % in dark, 10 % for the current button and 2 % for a press. The small highlight that followed the pointer is gone. A `DockControl` outside any dock keeps today's fills, including 11.0.2's dark-mode lift. `type="submit"` on `DockControl` stays. Docks with the default padding also get 8 px shorter along their row, since the end padding now follows the side padding and the end buttons sit concentric with the round ends.
- **The width jumps in your four-site study.** When a dock's buttons change, its plate now glides to the new width at all four sites instead of jumping: fourier's 712 to 687 to 528 px, colour's 484 to 500, keyframes' 317 to 445 and daraksha's 298 to 348 today. For a dock that floats over the page, nothing in the page's own layout changes while it glides. A dock that sits in a column moves the content below it with the same glide, and one that sits in a row still pushes its row. We had planned this glide on a transform for the dock release; that form comes with the 12.0.0 dock, and in this release the dock moves the plate's edges itself on each frame.
- **The crossfade in your top dock.** Inside a dock, `DockCrossfade` no longer reserves or measures: the plate glides to each face's width. So in `ActionBarLayer.vue` the `reserve="inline"` and the `:key="kept"` that worked around the peak reserve can both go. Outside a dock, `reserve` works as before.
- **The theme toggle** in your `Dock.vue` gets the new face you passed along: a warm sun whose rays draw into its disc and a cool crescent in dark, on one 200 ms clock. `--toggle-tone` on an ancestor replaces both colours with one.
- **The plate in dark.** Your docks take the firmer dark plate, about 43 % card colour where today's is 18 %, and a slightly lighter blur, 11.2 px against 16. So the band your colour session measures off `.dock-plate` will read differently in dark, and light takes one more step of ink. The dock takes its glass level from its own token, `--glass-level-dock`, so the `--glass-level: 0.1` in your more-contrast block no longer reaches your docks. Adding `--glass-level-dock: 0.1` to the same block keeps them at 0.1. `--glass-veil-dock` still sets how much card colour the plate takes: at 0.5 the dark plate goes from 43 % to 65 %.
- **The overflow menu.** The menu for buttons that don't fit, which you asked for in September, isn't in this release, and it's still an open question. The priority measure in `ActionBarLayer.vue` stays for now.
- **Tooltips.** The tooltip prop on `DockControl` and `DockTrigger`, and a hover-only option on the provider, come with the tooltip work in a later release, not the dock release, so fourier's workaround and keyframes' `useDockTip` stay until then. The Tooltip over a popover trigger and the seat-to-seat grace are 11.1.2's.
- **First-load size.** We built a small page that imports the dock parts you use and the menus they hold. On the last build it's 104,596 bytes compressed: 299 more than the same page on 11.1.0 (104,297) and 1,965 more than on 11.0.2 (102,631). Our bound for the dock release is no more than 11.1.0, and the release waits on it. Thanks for spelling out daraksha's budget: we'll read each route's first load against its cap and the site's total against 480 KB, and send both figures. If you'd rather run your own budget first, I can send you a tarball of the build.

Thanks, Mike
