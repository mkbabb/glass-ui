# W-I.8: the requirement ledger

The ledger seat, claude-opus-5-5, 2026-11-13. No browser was launched for this file and nothing was measured here. Every number cites the record that measured it, and that record stays the source. The design seat works from this file; where a row and a record disagree, the record wins and the row is wrong.

**Read in full:** `waves/W-I.8.md` (the 2026-11-10 body and every section after it, to the O-307 piece); `audit/INBOUND.md` (every row that names the dock or W-I.8, O-129 to O-336); the five notes under `design/dock/inbound/`; the dock sections of `audit/inbound-critiques/fourier-for-glass-2026-11-12.md` and `color-for-glass-2026-11-12.md`; `audit/animation/AUDIT.md` and `audit/animation/record/dock/RECORD.md`; `design/dock/LANDING.md` and the ruling tables of `design/dock/REGISTRY.md` (§1 questions Q-1 to Q-11, the P13 and P14 summaries); `waves/W-B.17.md` (the plate, the glyph, presence, follow, the authorized fades, the no-snap check); `docs/precepts/motion-canon.md`, `affordance-map.md` ("Intent first"), `restraint.md`; the dock source on master; the two release worktrees read-only (`release-11.0.2` at `14dd41fea`, `release-11.1.0` at `a6b92fbc3`): their CHANGELOGs, their `constants.ts`, and 11.0.2's orchestrator notes.

**Paths.** Under `docs/tranches/BL/` unless they start with `src/` or `~`. "11.0.2" and "11.1.0" mean those worktrees as read today, not as planned.

**The two bounds, binding on every row.**
1. Nothing changes instantly. Every change is tweened from its beginning to its end, with weight (`motion-canon.md` P0, the owner's words of 2026-11-12: "Growing instantly in height or other states is not acceptable ever--all items should be tweened").
2. "NO overuse of shading, transition, or markup--this is an AI smell. Have touch like apple." (owner, 2026-11-12, `waves/W-B.17.md` line 7). One shadow per surface, one blur level, no gleam, no fade-up on every element, one primary motion per interaction plus its coupled fade (`restraint.md`, Shading and Motion).

Beside them: Safari-compatible techniques, compositor properties, no layout per frame (the task's bound; P5). P5 and W-I.8 §1.2 disagree on one point, row L-16.

**How to read a row.** *Words* are the owner's or a consumer's, verbatim, with the row they came in. *Measured* is the value and the file. *Who* is the consumer or person who needs it. *Branch* says whether 11.0.2 or 11.1.0 already answers it ("no" means neither does). *Conflict* names the other row and says which wins: the latest owner word wins, and the row says so.

**The shell** (`GlassDock.vue`)
- Prop defaults: `collapse: "closed"`, `expandOn: "hover"`, `anchor: "center"` (`:83-89`).
- The state machine is constructed at `:144-165`; the one provide, `provideDockContext({ id, orientation, keepOpen, release, held, expanded })`, at `:179-186`.
- The measure (`useDockExpandedSize`) at `:208-214`; the morph orchestrator at `:227-242`.

**The state machine** (`composables/useDockState.ts`)
- States `collapsed | hover | pinned` (`:35`, `:128-132`). `expandOn: "press"` silences hover, focus and the timer (`:125-126`).
- Two counts: `keepOpenCount` (posture) and `graspCount` (a hand on a control) (`:145-148`).
- Hover intent: a 60 ms timer on enter, cleared by a leave (`:165-172`, `:385-411`).
- A press holds the posture until it resolves (`:201-285`); a press re-arms an armed idle window (`:278-281`).
- The idle window: `scheduleCollapse` (`:328-336`), armed by `onMouseLeave` (`:413-436`; a leave into a dock-owned portal is not a leave, `:431`) and by `onFocusOut` (`:453-473`); a press-opened dock closes at once when focus leaves (`:462-467`). A dock mounted open arms it at mount (`:589`).
- `collapse()` and `expand()` set the state at once (`:356-381`); the morph eases what they set.
- A press on the folded face pins (`:477-486`). `keepOpen` / `release`, with `release` waiting `min(collapseDelay, 800)` ms before the whole window again (`:490-512`).
- Click-away on the document in the capture phase, installed one frame after the expand, skipped while the morph runs (`:522-567`).

## 3 · What a design must contain, by piece

Each piece names its rows. A design that leaves a row out says why.

1. **States and timings.** Rest, open by hover, focus or tap, pinned as a declared state; the linger by dwell with a floor of at least 3.6 s and a ceiling and curve as tokens; re-entry cancels; explicit dismissals skip the linger and still ease; hover intent settable and gated on movement; the touch fold; the iframe fold read from 11.1.1. Rows L-19 to L-25, L-38. Source: `useDockState.ts` throughout; `constants.ts:45, 61, 73`.
2. **The fold and unfold, first frame to last.** Width and height on one spring with eased ends; round in every frame for every shape; no tick, no shadow swap, no sliver; content inside the plate or fading with it from the first frame; one opacity formula both ways; a reversal from where it is, without waiting out the intent; under reduced motion the geometry seats and the contents fade in 144 ms or less. Rows L-1, L-3 to L-8, L-12 to L-18, L-55. Source: `useDockMorph.ts`, `dockMorphMeasure.ts`, `shape.css:104-109`, `shell.css:318-323`, `morph.css:161-191`, `layers.css:178-297`.
3. **The layer swap.** The plate glides to the new width (and height) while the faces fade inside it, the fade done before the stretch; a third switch fades from its painted value; the owner's pick between strip A and strip B, and the windows (the both-at-zero instant is the open conflict). Rows L-2, L-9 to L-11, L-56 to L-58. Source: `crossfade.css:48-88`, `DockCrossfade.vue:115-219`, `useDockMorph.ts:9-11`.
4. **The fission seat with its neck.** One GlassDock; the theme seat on its own plate of the dock's surface, to the right; parting and fusing as goo through a neck that keeps the backdrop in WebKit; on the morph's own clock; containment for hover, focus and click-away; the centring question; the forced-colours outline for two plates. Rows L-40 to L-47, L-39. Source: none on master (`GlassDock.vue:609-610`); the nearest seat is `#persistent-end` (`:591-596`).
5. **The labelled seat.** Icon at rest; the name larger, to the seat's left, on hover or focus; neighbours shift by `translate` inside a plate whose size does not change; flat colour; one rest-fill token. Rows L-28, L-29, L-31, L-32, L-33. Source: DockControl and `icon-button.css`.
6. **The outlet for controls from outside the tree.** A named dock registry by id, a component that teleports into a dock and provides that dock's context to its slot, so Popover, menu, Select and Slider holds and closes work; no DOM lookup. Rows L-50, L-5, L-48. Source: `dockContext.ts:84-94`, `participation.ts:109-155`, `useDockHold.ts`.
7. **Overlays that close with the dock.** Every overlay a seat of the open dock opened closes on the fold, with its own exit, whether the seat is in the tree or arrived through the outlet; the persistent seat's overlay stays its own; the options panel's containment read. Rows L-48, L-49. Source: `participation.ts:125-130`, `GlassDock.vue:321-335`.
8. **The figure dock's three states.** Rest chips, a seat that unfolds when reached and stays until the fold, pinned by a press with its exits, seats that yield by priority, the dock that grows down, an optional run that does not scroll, a dock at rest that runs nothing; as a GlassDock preset. Rows L-25 to L-27, L-15, L-30. The owner question in L-26 (whether "keeping the dock the same size" binds the unfold) is open.
9. **Dividers and the Home seat.** Where a divider belongs in the standard dock and how it fades through the fold; Home first; one documented way for a free-standing control to take the dock plate; the one page-level plate in the README. Rows L-35, L-36, L-51, L-44. Source: `DockSeparator.vue`, the README.
10. **The published progress property and the timing tokens.** The progress siblings follow (is it `--dock-morph-t`, `--dock-expand-t`, or one more; what it reads at rest); `[data-morphing]` on content episodes, flipped with the class in one write; the three timings as tokens, with the 840 ms window named for what it is. Rows L-52, L-53, L-22, L-54.
11. **Touch.** The tap-opened dock's own linger; the coarse press-and-hold that 11.1.0 gives a persistent seat; 44 px targets; the tap that pins a figure dock; the iframe blur from 11.1.1; the menu's text size on a coarse pointer. Rows L-23, L-24, L-25, L-38, and G-6's coarse hit box. Source: `useDockState.ts:201-285`, `:522-567`.

Across every piece: the two bounds in the header, compositor properties, no layout per frame except what L-16 resolves, and the W-B.17 pieces used as they are (the plate, the glyph, presence, the authorized fades, the no-snap check).
