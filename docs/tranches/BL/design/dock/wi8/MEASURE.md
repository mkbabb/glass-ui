## The judge

`judge3.mjs` changes four things in `judge.mjs`, each RU-19's, and one count:

- (a) every edge bound fails only when the row is also over it by more than 0.25 px;
- (b) a turn: the edge must leave the old travel's path (differ by more than 0.25 px from the unturned preset run from its place and velocity) within 34 ms, and reverse within 1.15 times the preset's own stopping time from that velocity plus one row; both derived per edge;
- (c) a blank plate is timed, 17 ms, or 34 ms when a mark lies inside the travel;
- (d) a resize segment is read by R-1 (the plate moves on one row);
- the count: `judge.mjs` counted a face as a face only when its opacity changed by more than 0.5 in the segment, so a face that showed at 0.2 to 0.3 through a third switch was left out and its rows were read as blank. That was most of P2's "12 rows with no face showing". `judge3.mjs` counts every face that changes and reaches 0.1. It also reads a response (the first row no more than 34 ms after a press, a tap, a key or a swap); an opening by the pointer is left to the clock.

P2's real blank was about two rows; in WebKit the third switch also cut a face by 0.93, which was the carrier's (above).

## Findings the rulings do not cover

1. **Seat 3 at the outlet dock's trailing lane** (G-2, G-4, G-8). The outlet dock has `#persistent-end`; on a fold that region rides the plate's end edge leftward over the run's leaving face, which is held where it painted. The run's lane ends at that region's painted start edge, so the last seat is cut by the lane while it is still inside the plate and above 0.1 (one row, both engines). Lanes (no overlap) and "visible until covered" cannot both hold here with the current windows. Fourier's seat 2, the other P2 case, passes now. I did not change the lane.
2. **The turn on a small retarget** (G-4). On a third switch the plate's target moves by a few pixels on some edges. A spring turned toward a target only ΔT px away departs from its old path by about ω²ΔT t²/2, so it takes about 20 ms to depart by 0.25 px when ΔT is 3 px (derived, ω = 20.9). The rows read 35 to 59 ms, longer than that, and I have not found why. The reversal bound fails by 1 to 7 ms on the same cells.
3. **A menu does not hold the dock** (G-8). R-2 says every glass overlay opened from the dock holds it; the DropdownMenu has no hold, and the Popover holds only with `keep-dock-open`. Read in both engines.
4. **A seat that joins or leaves paints in one row** (G-4, P0). R-3's entering window for a control that joins is not built; a consumer's `v-if` seat leaving is W-B.17's presence. Nothing in the judge read it, because the seat's own opacity never changes.
5. **The trigger frame has a fourth layout** (G-11), and its dirty objects rose to 33 to 62. Both appear with RU-17, where the hidden face gains its box in that frame. RU-17's stop rule asks whether the unfold's first row then paints late: by the tap cells it does not. The tap-to-first-row times on this build and P2 overlap (Chromium 24 to 42 ms against 24 to 42, WebKit 12 to 65 ms against 12 to 83), with the transport the one cell slower in Chromium (25 to 41 ms) and faster in WebKit (83 to 65 ms).
6. **O-358** (`INPUT-many-seats-2026-11-15.md`) asks for one rule for a dock whose seats do not fit at 360 to 390. No ruling covers it, and this pass built nothing for it.
