### 6.1 · D4-F · the anatomy declares the tile

**Where it comes from.**
- PORTFOLIO question 2 asks for a holder that is "correct by construction rather than by a per-site class". No advancing family answers it:

- W §7 found a third honest form that §2.1 missed: a structural rule, `:has(> * + *)`, which works in both engines with no script. It reads anatomy, not geometry.
- B's divergence record reduces to anatomy (B Q3). B's critic names the anatomy on D's host as the point where D becomes B (re-trigger (c)).

**Charter.** The tile is the content's structure. ToggleGroupItem becomes a cell (the 16 px field corner, a column, the pad pair, body leading) when its content holds a second row, and stays a pill under TF-7's no-wrap law when it holds one. No prop. The consumer cannot declare the shape wrong, because the shape is not declared. A group's layout stays the consumer's (X §4.1 G; D-crit CX-5).

**Mechanism.** Two candidate readings, in order:
1. **Structural, over the consumer's markup.** A `:has()` over the item's direct children that counts rows and ignores inline adornments, for example two non-glyph element children. The unmigrated K1 item (a name span over a readout span) and K2 item (a stage over a name) would become cells with no consumer edit beyond deleting their overrides.
2. **Anatomical, over library slots.** The item renders `#meta` and `#media` as marked rows, and the cell rule reads their presence. The slots also give the library the meta ink lift (B Q4) and a selected-ink token for the media (X-2, D-crit gap 11).

**Centre:** content structure. **Optimizes:** correct by construction with no script and no declaration, the coarse pointer (16 px at every rung, where A1 paints 30), and the unmigrated consumer.

**Questions it must answer first, in order** (each is also its BLOCK test):
1. **Discrimination.** Does a structural selector tell a second row from an adornment in both engines? It must then show it is not "B on ToggleGroup under another name": one item with two content models is the design-idioms §9 test. If it cannot, F merges into B's anatomy on D′'s host and closes.
2. **The mixed grid.** A one-row tile stretched to its two-row neighbour's height stays a stadium beside a card (W §7). Does a group-level `:has()` (any cell item makes every sibling a cell) hold in both engines, and does it leave single-line pill rows alone?

### 6.2 · D4-G · the selection is a body

- it is not an outline, so it cannot read as the ring (CX-11);

- the content is never re-rendered to paint state (X-3).

The body commits by travel on the spring, with velocity (D-1), in one of Q-11's two forms: a glide between tile rects, or a lift-and-land that crosses no gap. `type="multiple"` has no single body and keeps the floor register (TF-2).
