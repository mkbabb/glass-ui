### 1.1 · H3-1: the stall's root cause

Facts, all read from the unified log (`H3/h31/`):

- **Every http(s) main-frame navigation in Playwright WebKit asks macOS AppSSO first.** WebKit's `SOAuthorizationCoordinator::canAuthorize` calls `canPerformAuthorizationWithURL` and waits. `appsso-probe.mjs` (`logs/appsso-probe.log`, load 30→28) counts 5 calls for 5 `goto` navigations, 5 for 5 `location.assign` navigations, and 0 for 5 `about:blank` navigations. No other navigation form avoids the call.
- **On 2026-10-07 the call stopped answering in three Playwright processes, each at about its 90th navigation** (`syslog-appsso-2026-10-07-1300-1432.txt`):

  | pid | calls | answered |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | 11741 | 91 | 90 |
  | 24625 | 90 | 89 |
  | 66362 | 91 | 90 |

  In pid 66362 (`syslog-pid66362-1427-1432.txt`), call #90 at 14:27:40.872 was answered at 14:28:40.881, which is 60 009 ms, the length of `page.goto`'s timeout. Call #91 at 14:28:41.241, H2's re-open in the same process, was never answered. The run was killed at 14:32:01 after 6.5 min idle (`stall-2026-10-07-1425-t3-webkit-fsrc.log`). All three processes ran in the boot that ended at 14:32:54 (`kern.boottime`).
- **This explains "routed" and "always preset-contents".** H2's routed T-3 adds the migrated arms, which put `preset-contents (light)` at navigation #90. The unrouted T-3 opened about 80 pages and never reached the wall.
- **After the reboot the stall does not reproduce.** These are the traced routed and unrouted T-3 runs on 2026-10-08, between 11:15 and 11:39 (`h31-*.verdict.log`, `*.navigations.txt`, `syslog-appsso-2026-10-08-1115-1139-per-process.txt`). Every AppSSO call was answered in every run:

  | run | navigations | tally | load |
  |---|---:|---|---|
  | `head-froute` | 147 | T-3 2/75 | 12→40 |
  | `f-src3` (the F prototype, routed) | 147 | 42/85 | 38→45 |
  | `f-src3` with the fd sampler | 147 | 42/85 | 56→74 |
  | the old `f-src` build | 122 | 22/65 | 37→42 |
  | the old `f-src` build under the committed H2 harness, as a control | 105 | migrated 15/18 | 33→40 |

  `preset-contents (light)` loaded as navigation #106 in 64 ms.

**Verdict.** The stall is an AppSSO reply that stopped arriving after about 90 navigations of one WebKit process. It held in that boot epoch and is gone after the reboot. Why the macOS service stopped answering is outside this harness, and it cannot be reproduced on demand now. The cure addresses the mechanism, not the symptom:

- **Retirement.** A process never reaches 60 navigations, against an observed wall of 89-91. Every T-3 cell still reads under retirement (`navcap`: 97/97 at a cap of 5, 29 relaunches). At HEAD the baseline retired once in T-3 and once in T-6, with 0 stalls.
- **Re-open on a new process.** H2 re-opened in the dead process, so its second attempt could not succeed.
- **A named cause.** When a stall does happen, the error carries the AppSSO count from the log: "AppSSO pending" if it is this stall, "AppSSO answered" if it is something else, as the `stall` plant shows.
