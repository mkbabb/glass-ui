### 1.5 · H7-5 · the kick's spring, named (B-5; reported)

REPORT's kick read also records the animations running on the committed tile at the commit's first frame: each one's kind, name, duration and easing. A CSS animation's easing is the item's `animation-timing-function`; a transition's is its effect's.

It then traces the spring the frames draw: the scale per frame, the peak over the settle, the dip under it after the peak, and ζ from their ratio, ζ = −ln r / √(π² + ln² r) with r = dip / peak. **`kick-spring` passes** when a scale animation is named and the frames overshoot and bounce under the settle (ζ < 1: weight and bounce, D-1). A failing read on janky frames (median interval > 20 ms) is re-read twice, and the cell prints REPRODUCED or NOT-REPRODUCED. The kick cell itself is unchanged and stays reported (B-5, floor-owned). **Plant** `h47-spring-glide`: the fixture's pop overshoots and glides back with no undershoot.
