## 0 · In brief

1. **The portfolio's lead site moved under it.** keyframes.js `9262899b` (2026-09-23 21:46) rebuilt the Spring preset tiles: the live track and its 60 Hz ball painter are gone, `rounded-pill` is gone from the item, and the consumer now sets `border-radius: var(--radius-field)` itself in scoped CSS (`SpringPhysicsFacet.vue:197-198`). The easing specimens did the same (`EasingTarget.css:115-116`). T-1 is now hidden at both keyframes.js sites by a consumer override, and T-7 is still RED, only in the other direction: the consumer paints the card rung the library should give it. The portfolio's S1 "verbatim" scene (`SpringPhysicsFacet.vue:93`, `:293-309`) transcribes the retired version (§2).
2. **Eleven selection sets in four repos hold more than one line, a thumbnail, a live readout or an interactive child.** Three are one-of-N on a glass primitive (two ToggleGroups, one Chip set). Eight are hand-rolled on `Button`, `<button>` or `<div role="button">`, and six of those eight put an exclusive or multi choice on `aria-pressed` or on nothing (§1).
3. **Every consumer that needed a visible selected state painted its own**, and five spent a channel the library reserves: `outline` (keyframes.js presets), `box-shadow` / Tailwind `ring` (value.js Mix palettes, fourier-analysis HarmonicLevelGrid and GalleryCard), or `--ring`, the focus token, as the selection colour (GalleryCard). The consumers' carriers split into four kinds: an edge (border or outline), a tint on the fill, the content's own ink (glyph stroke, label colour and weight), and a mark (chicago's check icon). No consumer relies on the library's 0.12 fill alone (§1, §4.2).
4. **Two sets carry a second state on the same tile.** fourier-analysis HarmonicLevelGrid has `active` (the selection) and `is-bound` (the low and high slider ends) as independent facts (`HarmonicLevelGrid.vue:33-36`, `:256-269`). GalleryCard has `data-selected` and `data-tier`, and the tier rules come later in source at the same specificity, so a selected featured card paints no selection (`GalleryCard.vue:225-240`). A tile family needs one selected channel that a sibling state cannot overwrite.
5. **Two sets put interactive content inside the selectable box.** keyframes.js matrix cells wrap a text `Input` and select on click (`MatrixEditor.vue:8-40`). fourier-analysis GalleryCard is a `role="button"` holding a Checkbox, a like Button and admin Buttons (`GalleryCard.vue:75-160`). ARIA makes `radio` and `button` children presentational (W §0 item 5), so neither can become a radio tile. Both are grids or lists with their own controls, and a tile family has to say so rather than absorb them.
6. **The grid-arrow gap (T-5) already cost a consumer its layout.** fourier-analysis EasingPicker had a three-column grid of easing thumbnails and collapsed it to one column because the menu's keys are one-dimensional ("the old 3-column grid made ArrowDown mean 'one to the right'", `EasingPicker.vue:36-40`). keyframes.js's two ToggleGroup grids keep the grid and live with dead Up and Down.
7. **Of the 18 F-30b rows** (17 O-59 plus O-58), five name a selectable set (UIA-KF-046, UIA-V-60, UIA-F-85, UIA-F-141, UIA-V-136), one names a wrapped static chip (UIA-V-180), and twelve are the radius canon on a non-selectable control: badges, dock pills, action buttons, the disclosure trigger, the stepper, matrix fields. D4 owns the first six; the rest ride F-30b's canon row (§3).
