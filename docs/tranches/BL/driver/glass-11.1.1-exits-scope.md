# glass-ui 11.1.1: the exits patch (scope)

Owner (2026-11-15): "The value.js pages pop-up from the dropdown on mobile instantly transitions out, and is not glassy--many of our popups and the like are like this, and many of our glass-ui components instantly transition out like this. Audit and mark." Standing law: nothing changes instantly, ever; every change is tweened in and out with weight; and "NO overuse of shading, transition, or markup--this is an AI smell. Have touch like apple."

11.1.1 is this and nothing else: every surface that opens leaves on a tween a person can see, turns from where it is, is glass where its siblings are, and fades under reduced motion. A patch: no public name removed, no prop's meaning changed. Everything else that was booked for 11.1.1 is 11.1.2 (`glass-11.1.1-scope.md`, kept under its old name; read its exits rows O-313, O-319, O-321, O-350, O-351, O-361 here).

Evidence to read first: `audit/exits/TABLE.md` (glass's own table, every surface by form and dismissal route; when present), `audit/inbound-critiques/valuejs-popup-marks-2026-11-15.md` (four production sites: 74 of about 125 cells leave instantly, 32 are not glass), `audit/last-ten/FINDINGS.md` (the reversal table, LT-5, LT-8 to LT-10), hassio's and gaggle's rows in INBOUND (O-361, O-363).

## Rows, in order

1. **The phone sheet jumps on close** (the owner's case, first). A Select or menu in its sheet form at 390 re-anchors to its trigger in the tick `open` turns false (`sheet-width.ts:25-35`): a 445 px jump and a change of surface in one frame. The form a popup opened in is the form it closes in: latch it for the whole exit. Witness per frame, both engines, on a Select inside a GlassDock at 390 with real touch, by pick, press outside and Escape: no frame moves the plate more than its own exit travel.
2. **A phone popup is glass** (ruling). Below 30rem Select and DropdownMenu content is an opaque plate by design (`SelectContent.vue:131-132`, `DropdownMenuContent.vue:84-85,105`, `surface-axis.css:81-88`). It becomes the firm overlay glass tier, tinted with its backdrop. Gates: text and the highlighted row at 4.5:1 over a busy ground in dark and light; under `prefers-reduced-transparency` it is solid as today; no blur nested inside a blur.
3. **The exit is long enough to see and does not front-load.** Menu, Select, Popover and Tooltip exits run a 120 ms clock that reaches opacity 0 at its 60 % stop on ease-out (`animations.css:157-171`, `reveal.css:112,199`, `scheme-spring.css:115`): 34 % gone in the first frame. One exit curve for floating surfaces: opacity reaches 0 at the end of its clock, no single frame takes more than a fifth of the travel at 60 Hz, duration from the motion tokens and shorter than the entrance. Dialog and Sheet exits on the same family, shorter than their entrances (O-321c, hassio L2).
4. **Every dismissal route leaves the same way.** Pick, press outside, Escape, the trigger again, focus leaving, a route change: none cuts. A surface unmounted before its exit ends is the defect class; find each (the exits table marks them per route).
5. **Reversal.** A surface reopened while closing, or dismissed while opening, turns from where it is: Popover, DropdownMenu, Select, Tooltip, Toast, Accordion and Collapsible (O-313, O-319, LT-8 to LT-10, hassio L8); a trigger press while a Dialog, Sheet or Select closes reopens it (LT-5, O-321a); a press on the page while a sheet leaves is not swallowed (O-363).
6. **Tooltips enter and leave on a tween** (O-313b, LT-23), and hand over between neighbours without a flash.
7. **CommandDialog** keeps its list through the exit; the plate does not fall (416 px today).
8. **Reduced motion is a fade** (O-350): opacity and colour up to 150 ms pass, travel stops, the blanket near-zero rule goes so a consumer's fade no longer loses; the dock's fold under reduce cross-fades (O-364).
9. **ExpandableContainer** has an entrance and an exit, reverses, and takes Escape from its first frame (O-351, O-351a items 8 and 9).
10. **Small CSS cures that ride along if their witness is ready:** a state change moves no layout box (`audit/hover-shift/TABLE.md`, when present); the pointer highlight leaves every control if the owner picks that variant (`design/specular/SPEC.md`, V-A).
11. **A "leave finished" event on every surface that opens** (O-373, value.js 2026-11-15; an ADDITION this scope names). Dialog, Sheet, Popover, DropdownMenu, Select, ContextMenu, HoverCard, Tooltip and Toast content emit one event, the same name on all, once, after the exit's last frame (at once under reduced motion when the fade ends; never when the close is reversed mid-exit). Read first what exists: color relies on a `closed` event the dialog fires as its content unmounts; if that is glass's, its name is the name, and it must fire after the exit, not before. Documented in the README beside the line about closing by the open prop. Witness: the event's timestamp is at or after the last frame whose opacity is above 0, in both engines; a reversed close emits nothing. Retires fourier's LeaveSignal helper and color's reliance on unmount timing. Seat B builds it after row 9. **Shape (hassio, 2026-11-15; ruled):** besides the Vue emit, the surface's own content element dispatches a DOM `CustomEvent` that bubbles and is composed, so a listener on a shadow host or an overlay layer inside a shadow root hears it; `detail.kind` is the surface (dialog, sheet, popover, menu, select, tooltip, toast, hover-card), no reason. It fires once per close: after the exit tween ends, when reduced motion ends the fade, and also when the exit is cut short by an unmount mid-exit (fired at the unmount, so a host never waits on an event that cannot come). A close reversed by a reopen fires nothing. No "enter started" event in this release.
12. **A regression 11.1.0 shipped, cured here** (O-380, value.js 2026-11-15, measured on keyframes): on a touch screen a segmented switch that was 40 px tall grows to 50 px and takes the bar shape (12 to 15 px corners) where the 11.1.0 changelog promises round ends; fourier's tabs growing from 32 to 50 px keep theirs. Find why one keeps its ends and the other does not (the size variant that crosses a radius threshold, most likely), make the strip, the tabs and the selected plate round-ended at every size on coarse pointers, and say whether 50 px is the intended touch height or the 44 px floor overshot. Witness: RED on v11.1.0 and GREEN on the branch in both engines at 390 with touch, for each SegmentedTabs size. The first fix seat builds it if seat B has finished.
13. **Two faults Gregg measured on 11.1.0 in production** (O-383, 2026-11-15; probes read-only in `~/Programming/gregg/.shots/`). (a) Under prefers-reduced-motion the dock's run mark (`useDockRunMark`) moves 165 to 169 px in one frame with no fade (`p44s-p0-probe.mjs`, cell 1440-reduce): under row 8's law it cross-fades, old place out and new place in, within 150 ms. (b) WebKit still raises a ResizeObserver loop error at 1440 light about 2.1 s into load; the last writer is `useDockMorph`'s plate observer writing `--dock-plate-cross-px` on the dock root inside its own callback (`p44s-ro-probe.mjs`). Reproduce it on glass's own demo dock in WebKit 2287 first; cure it at the root (the observer does not write a property that resizes what it observes in the same callback: write in the next frame, or only when the rounded value changed), keeping 11.1.0's resize-mid-morph witness green. Each: RED on v11.1.0, GREEN on the branch. If (b) does not reproduce on glass's demo, record the reading and leave it for 11.1.2 with Gregg's probe as the witness.

## Gates

- Every row has a per-frame witness RED on v11.1.0 and GREEN on the branch, in Chromium and Playwright WebKit 2287, at 390 with real touch and at 1440.
- A standing cell from here on: for every surface in the exits table, by every route, no exit completes in under two frames and no frame takes more than a fifth of the travel.
- Frame-rate claims, if any, on the GPU lane with the renderer recorded (O-362). Behaviour witnesses need no lane.
- No public name removed; the release specs of 11.0.x and 11.1.0 stay green or are restated with a biting plant.
