# Popups: leaving instantly and not glassy (marks)

The audit for the owner's order of 15 Nov 2026: *"The value.js pages pop-up from the dropdown on mobile instantly transitions out, and is not glassy--many of our popups and the like are like this, and many of our glass-ui components instantly transition out like this. Audit and mark."*

It covers four live sites: color.babb.dev, keyframes.babb.dev, fourier.babb.dev and daraksha.babb.dev (public pages only). Each was read at phone 390 (touch) and desktop 1440, dark, on the GPU lane. Every number below is a per-frame measurement. Every surface marked "leaves instantly" has a filmstrip in `films/`, and a sample of them was opened and confirmed by eye.

## Summary

**The one-line answer.** Almost every popup on all four sites runs the same glass-ui exit: a 120 ms keyframe whose opacity reaches 0 at its 60 % stop, on an ease-out curve. The surface loses a third of its opacity in the first frame and 90 % within about 42 ms. Below 30rem, glass-ui also deliberately makes dock menus and selects opaque, and the select's bottom sheet jumps to its trigger on the first frame of the close. The pages dropdown on color.babb.dev at 390 has all three.

Counts. A cell is one surface at one width. "Leaves instantly" means gone in one frame, or a jump of more than 40 px in one frame, or 90 % of the opacity gone within 80 ms of the exit's start.

- The faults are nearly all in glass-ui:
  - 68 of the 74 "leaves instantly" cells are glass-ui faults. 6 are site faults: color's Report dialog x2, fourier's phone search, fourier's desktop search pick, and the daraksha lock popover, whose enter is the site's.
  - 29 of the 32 "not glass" cells are glass-ui's own opaque phone rung. 3 are daraksha's `surface="opaque"`.
- Daraksha has few instant cells because it has no Select and only one dock menu. Its 7 popovers leave in 117 ms with 90 % gone at 82 to 84 ms, just outside the mark.
- **Reduced motion:** a cut in and out on every overlay on every site (glass rule B). The Toast is the exception: its vaporize blur still runs.
- **Reverse:** no surface on any site continues from where it is when reopened mid-close (glass rule E and reka's unmount). The Dialogs, on their script spring, are the partial exception: the keyframes shortcuts dialog and the fourier search palette continue.
- **Coordinator column:** no site passes a class, style or prop to a glass content element that sets background, backdrop-filter, animation, transition, border or radius, with four exceptions, all on daraksha:
  - `veil.css:164` sets a `transition`. Disabling it brings the glass enter back.
  - `surface="opaque"` on three surfaces. Flipping it brings the blur back, at α 0.22.
  - Everywhere else the site classes are width, height, padding, font or layer. The glass faults are not caused by any site rule.
- **WebKit** (color pages dropdown at 390 only; Playwright 1.60.0, WebKit 26.4 build 2287, renderer "Apple GPU", `guarded --exclusive webkit`): the same 452 px jump and an 88 ms fade on Escape. On a page pick the listbox is removed whole after a 176 ms stalled frame. Real Safari was not measured.

### The color pages dropdown at 390: the exact cause
It is glass-ui's `Select`. The site (`demo/shell/dock/DockViewSelect.vue:119`) passes only `min-w-[12rem]`.

- **Not glassy:** below 30rem `SelectContent.vue:131-132` stamps `data-form="sheet"` and `data-surface="opaque"`, and `surface-axis.css:81-88` paints a solid `--card` with no backdrop blur (bg α 1.00).
- **Leaves instantly**, in two steps:
  1. `sheet-width.ts:25-35` drops the sheet form synchronously when `open` turns false. In the same frame the listbox re-anchors under the dock and turns glass: a **445 px jump** (y 514 to y 69, between +22 and +39 ms).
  2. `glass-reveal-out` (`animations.css:157-171`, opacity 0 at the 60 % stop) runs on the 120 ms `--spring-present-exit-duration` with `--ease-out` (`reveal.css:112, 199`): 34 % gone in the first frame, 90 % in 50 ms, gone at 79 to 127 ms.
  - Film: `films/color-dock-view-select-p390-outside.jpg`: the sheet at the foot at +30 ms, a glass ghost under the dock at +60 ms, nothing at +120 ms.

## Faults

### glass-ui faults (one cause each; every site inherits them)

Lines are from glass-ui `v11.0.2`, the version color, keyframes and fourier ship. Daraksha ships 11.0.1, which has the same lines for these rules.

- **G1. The exit is a 72 ms fade.** This is the owner's "instantly transitions out" on every Select, DropdownMenu and Tooltip, at both widths.
  - `src/styles/glass/reveal.css:199` runs `glass-reveal-out` on `--reveal-exit-clock`. For the menu and tooltip registers that clock is `--spring-present-exit-duration` (`reveal.css:112, 124`; `src/styles/tokens/scheme-spring.css:115`): `clamp(.12s, .2s * .6, .25s)`, read live as **120 ms**.
  - The keyframe's opacity reaches 0 at its **60 %** stop (`src/styles/animations.css:157-171`), so the fade is 72 ms long.
  - The curve is `--ease-out` `cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1)` (`motion-registers.css:148`), applied to that segment. It takes 34 % of the opacity in the first frame and 90 % in 41 to 50 ms.
  - Popovers and Dialogs (overlay register, `--exit-overlay-duration`, 250 ms) lose 90 % in about 83 ms: fast, but just outside the mark.
  - The rubric's exit band is 120 to 250 ms of *visible* motion. The cure is in the exit register (the clock, the 60 % stop, the curve), not in any site.
- **G2. Phone dock menus and selects are opaque by design.** This is the owner's "not glassy" at 390.
  - `src/components/select/SelectContent.vue:55-61, 131-132`: below 30rem a select takes its sheet form, `data-form="sheet"` and `data-surface="opaque"`.
  - `src/components/menu/DropdownMenuContent.vue:79-85, 105`: a dock menu at sheet width takes `data-surface="opaque"`.
  - `src/styles/glass/surface-axis.css:81-88` paints the solid `--card` with `backdrop-filter: none`.
  - The rationale ("a sheet hides the page") is in `_shared/overlay/sheet-width.ts:4-8`, a glass ruling (U/B7 D-6). The owner's order reverses it.
- **G3. The phone sheet jumps to its trigger as it leaves** (AUDIT.md row 11, rule F).
  - `sheet-width.ts:25-35` recomputes the form synchronously (`flush: "sync"`) from `open`. When `open` turns false, the closing listbox re-anchors (`SelectContent.vue:72-93`) and loses its opaque rung in the same frame.
  - It jumps 142 to 445 px (color 445, fourier 387 to 394), then fades by G1.
  - The dock menu's opaque rung is derived the same way (`DropdownMenuContent.vue:84-85`), so it turns translucent on its first exit frame.
- **G4. Nothing reverses from where it is** (AUDIT.md row 6, rule E).
  - `glass-reveal-out` has a fixed `from` (`animations.css:158-162`).
  - reka unmounts the content after the exit, so a reopen either flashes the same node 0 to 1 in one frame or starts a new node from about 0.15 while the old one is dropped mid-fade.
- **G5. The Tooltip enters whole** (AUDIT.md row 6, rule D). Its content is `delayed-open` or `instant-open`, never `open`, so `reveal.css:136-161` gives it no from-state.
- **G6. Reduced motion is a cut in and out on every overlay** (AUDIT.md row 13, rule B). `src/styles/utilities/a11y-overrides.css:46-54` zeroes every duration with `!important`, and that also silences the reduced-motion fades the sites author.
  - The Toast is the one exception: its vaporize blur still runs.
- **G7. CommandDialog unmounts its list on the first frame of the close** (AUDIT.md row 12, rule F, `components/command/CommandDialog.vue`). The plate drops 416 to 511 px, then the stub fades (daraksha surah index).
- **G8. Dock layer faces swap whole** (AUDIT.md rows 3 and 22): the color login seat and action bar. Already ranked by glass; measured here only as context.

## Method

- Live production, read-only: every non-GET/HEAD/OPTIONS request and every `/admin` URL was aborted in the browser (the only ones seen were Cloudflare's `cdn-cgi/rum` beacons). No sign-in, no submit, no upload, no compute job, no private mode.
- Chromium headless shell from Playwright 1.60.0 on the GPU lane (`--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu --ignore-gpu-blocklist`). Renderer, read in a fresh WebGL context each run: `ANGLE (Apple, ANGLE Metal Renderer: Apple M5 Max, Unspecified Version)`. All runs went through `~/.claude/guard/guarded` (never `--light`), one browser per run, a new context per surface and viewport, closed in `finally`.
- Viewports: phone 390x844 (touch, DPR 3; presses are touch taps) and desktop 1440x900 (mouse). Dark scheme throughout. Reduced motion is a separate context with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- The recorder (`probe/lib.mjs`) samples every rAF frame (about 120 Hz): for each popup-like node (`role=dialog|alertdialog|menu|listbox|tooltip`, `[data-slot$=-content]`, toasts, plus a site selector), its effective opacity (the product over its ancestors, 0 when hidden or zero-sized), its box, and its transform, scale and translate. The surface is the largest node visible at rest that was not visible before the opening press.
- ENTER is read from the opening press to rest. LEAVE is read from the closing input (the input event's own timestamp) until the node is gone. Each close way starts from a fresh open.
- "**Leaves instantly**" means one of: the surface is gone in one frame; it jumps more than 40 px in one frame as it leaves; or 90 % of its opacity is gone within 80 ms of the exit's start. The last rule is what the eye reads as a cut: on the films, every surface that meets it is a ghost by +60 ms and gone by +120 ms. The rubric's exit band is 120 to 250 ms.
- REVERSE: open, close, then press the trigger again at about 45 % of that surface's own measured close time. "No" means it jumped, or the closing plate was dropped and a new one started from zero.
- GLASS is read at rest from the computed style of the surface and its `::before`/`::after`: `glass` means a backdrop blur with a translucent fill; `tinted-opaque` means a solid fill, with or without blur; `plain` means no blur and no fill.
- Films: screencast frames, keeping the latest frame at or before 0, 30, 60, 120 and 240 ms after the closing input (each tile is labelled with the real frame offset). They were made for every close, and opened and looked at for the surfaces marked instant.

## color.babb.dev (value.js demo)

Live production, 15 Nov 2026. Source value.js `origin/master` `216d0e066`, on `@mkbabb/glass-ui` **11.0.2**. Data: `data/color.jsonl` (Chromium), `data/color-webkit.jsonl` (WebKit). Probe: `probe/sites/color.mjs`. Films: `films/color-*.jpg`.

### The pages dropdown at 390: the exact cause

The owner's first case is the dock's **Select view** (`demo/shell/dock/DockViewSelect.vue:77-180`). It is a glass-ui `Select`. The site passes only `class="min-w-[12rem]"` to the content (`:119`), so both faults are glass-ui's.

1. **Not glassy: by glass's own rule.** Below 30rem, `SelectContent` opens in its *sheet form* and stamps `data-form="sheet"` and `data-surface="opaque"` (glass `src/components/select/SelectContent.vue:55-61`, `:131-132`; the rationale is in `_shared/overlay/sheet-width.ts:4-8`).
   - `src/styles/glass/surface-axis.css:81-88` then paints a solid `--card` with `backdrop-filter: none`.
   - Measured: bg α 1.00, no blur; a brown slab across the foot of the screen (`films/color-dock-view-select-p390-outside.jpg`, tiles +0 and +30).
2. **Leaves instantly: two glass mechanisms in series.**
   - **(a) It jumps away first.** The sheet form is derived from `open` (`useSheetWidth(() => selectRoot.open)`, `SelectContent.vue:61`). `sheet-width.ts:25-35` recomputes it synchronously (`flush: "sync"`) the moment `open` turns false, so on the closing input the listbox:
     - loses `data-form="sheet"` and `data-surface="opaque"`;
     - re-anchors to the trigger (`SelectContent.vue:72-93`);
     - jumps **445 px**, from y 514 at the foot of the screen to y 69 under the dock, in one frame (trace: +22 ms y 514, +39 ms y 69);
     - turns glass while doing so.
     - AUDIT.md row 11, rule F.
   - **(b) Then it fades in about 70 ms.** The exit is `glass-reveal-out` (`src/styles/animations.css:157-171`), whose opacity reaches 0 at the **60 %** stop. It runs on `--reveal-exit-clock`, which for the menu register is `--spring-present-exit-duration` (`reveal.css:112`, `scheme-spring.css:115`), read live as **120 ms**, on `--ease-out` `cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1)` (`reveal.css:199`).
     - So the fade is 72 ms long, front-loaded: 34 % of the opacity goes in the first frame and 90 % within 50 ms.
   - Close by outside press: gone at 95 ms. Escape: 79 ms. Picking a page: 127 ms.
   - On the film the sheet is at the foot at +30 ms, a faint glass ghost under the dock at +60 ms, and nothing at +120 ms. The eye reads that as a cut.
3. **No reverse.** The listbox has faded out before a reopen at 43 ms can catch it, and reka unmounts it. A reopen is a new entry from zero. At 1440 a reopen at 30 ms drops the closing plate at opacity 0.31 and starts a new one from 0.15.
4. **Reduced motion.** It cuts in and out (glass `utilities/a11y-overrides.css:46-54`, rule B).
5. **WebKit** (Playwright 1.60.0, WebKit 26.4 build 2287, renderer "Apple GPU", `guarded --exclusive webkit`, about 3 min including the queue). Real Safari was not measured.
   - Escape: the same 452 px jump to the dock, and gone in 88 ms.
   - Picking a page: one 176 ms stalled frame (the route swap), then the listbox is **removed whole**, with no exit frames.
   - The outside tap did not close it under WebKit's touch emulation, so it was not judged.
6. **Route change** (`location.hash` changed while it is open): the sheet stays open over the new page at both widths (`DockViewSelect.vue:37`; the parent's mutex does not close on route).

Every glass Select and dock menu on the site shares causes 1 and 2b at 390, and 2a in its select form. The 1440 forms are glass but leave in 67 to 82 ms through cause 2b.

### Not reached, and why
- Colour-name badge popover (`demo/picker/ColorPicker.vue:94-105`): the badge was not in the DOM for the colours on load.
- Colour input popover (`demo/shell/dock/ColorInput.vue:136-152`): "Open color input" focuses the field, but no popover opened.
- Browse "Show all N tags" popover (`demo/palettes/inspector/PaletteCardMeta.vue:44-53`): no production palette has overflowing tags.
- Tag edit popover, the delete confirmations (`PalettesPane.vue:176, 197`; `BrowsePane.vue:199`), the admin dialogs: they need an owner or admin session.
- Version history drawer (`demo/palettes/browser/dialog/VersionHistoryDrawer.vue`): not in an anonymous card menu.
- Image eyedropper dialog (`ImageEyedropper.vue`): it needs an uploaded image.
- **Source-read only:** the version drawer and the eyedropper are both mounted with a `v-if` that is cleared on close, so both leave in one frame, the same as the Report dialog:
  - `BrowsePane.vue:173` `v-if="versionPalette"` with `:367` `versionPalette.value = null`;
  - `ExtractWorkbench.vue:264` `v-if="session.eyedropperOpen.value && …"`.
- Toasts: the site has no toaster.

### Coordinator column (site overrides on the content element)
- **No site class sets background, backdrop-filter, animation, transition, border or radius on any popup content.** Every class passed is width, height or font: `min-w-[12rem]`, `min-w-menu font-display`, `min-w-[17rem]`, `max-h-[16rem]`, `max-w-56`, `pointer-events-auto`, `w-52 p-2.5`.
- The channel tooltip also gets `:style="tipInkStyle(…)"`, which sets ink colours only.
- The site's global `.glass-floating` rule (`demo/styles/shell.css:385-405`, and `:414-426` for dialogs) sets only ink custom properties (`--foreground`, `--muted-foreground`, and so on). Its `prefers-reduced-transparency` block (`demo/styles/foundation.css:1033-1060`) does not apply under a normal preference.
- So there was no rule to disable, and the material and the exit of every glass surface here are glass-ui's.
- The site's one exit fault is a JavaScript unmount, not a style. A style rule cannot test it; it is confirmed by the node detaching in one frame (`detached: true`) and by the film.

## keyframes.babb.dev: popup exits and glass (POPUPS audit, 15 Nov 2026)

Live production, read-only. Source: keyframes.js `origin/master` `f7d10f51`, on `@mkbabb/glass-ui` **11.0.2**. Every non-GET request and every `/admin` URL was aborted in the browser (only Cloudflare RUM beacons were caught). No sign-in, no writes. "Clear all & reload" was opened to its confirm dialog and never confirmed.

**Lane.** Playwright 1.60.0 headless shell, `--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu --ignore-gpu-blocklist`, every run through `guarded`, one browser per run, a new context per cell, everything closed in `finally`. Renderer read in-page on every run: `ANGLE (Apple, ANGLE Metal Renderer: Apple M5 Max, Unspecified Version)`. Dark, 390x844 touch (DPR 3) and 1440x900. Reduced motion is a separate context with `reducedMotion: reduce`.

**Method.** Each frame (about 120 Hz) records every candidate surface's effective opacity (the product over ancestors), box and transform. ENTER runs from the opening input to rest. LEAVE runs from each closing input until the node is gone. REVERSE closes, then reopens at about 45 % of the measured exit. GLASS is read from computed style at rest, on the element, its pseudo-elements and its first children. Films are CDP screencast frames at +0/30/60/120/240 ms after the closing input, in `POPUPS/films/keyframes-<surface>-<vp>-<way>.jpg`.

**Rule (one rule for every site).** An exit is INSTANT if (a) it is gone in one frame, (b) it jumps more than 40 px in one frame, or (c) 90 % of its opacity is gone within 80 ms of the exit's start. "90% in N ms" is measured from the exit's first moving frame.

**Site overrides (the coordinator's column).** No keyframes surface gets a site class, style or prop on its content element that sets background, backdrop-filter, animation, transition, border or radius. Every site class on a content element is width, padding, type or layer (cited per row). Site CSS that reaches a portalled surface (`ControlsPaneWrapper.css:58-107`, `design-idioms.css:553-576`) lays out the sheet's body or sets `user-select`, nothing more. So no disable-and-retest was needed. **Every exit and glass fault below is glass-ui's.**
