# The drag family, frame by frame

Recorder: claude-opus-5-5. Read on 2026-11-12 and 13.

Components: slider, tabs, sheet, sortable-list, carousel, pager-dots.

## How this was read

- Trees: the published v11.0.1 (`e42bc2705`) and the 11.0.2 tip (`648d70e59`), each served from the harness build.
- Engine: headless Chromium 149 with GPU compositing. Every cell rendered at a median of 8.3 ms a frame.
- Cells:
  - 1440 fine pointer, light.
  - 390 coarse pointer (real CDP touch), light, for the gestures a finger makes.
  - 1440 reduced motion, once per component.
  - No component in this family animates colour on a dark-only path, so no dark cell ran.
- Scenarios: `scenarios/*.json`, generated by `scenarios/make.mjs`. The batches ran through `run.sh` under one guard slot each.
- Rows: `rows/<scenario>.<tree>.chromium.<cell>.rows.json`. Each run's analyser output sits beside it as `.faults.txt`.
- Traced runs: `rows/trace/`. WebKit runs: `rows/webkit/`.
- Strips: `strips/`. Only the strips cited below are kept.
- `rd.mjs <rows> <from> <to> <as.key>...` prints any channel per frame. The frame tables below come from it.
- Frame numbers are row indices in the named rows file. One row is one rendered frame.

What the tip changes in this family:
- `slider/styles.css`: the thumb is the band's height, with one derived radius. This is geometry, not motion.
- `pager-dots/worm.ts`: the pip diameter is now read in px. This changes motion.

Tabs, sheet, sortable-list and carousel source is byte-identical between the two trees. Their readings on the tip match v11.0.1 within run noise (the same frames, plus or minus 3).

### Faults

**S-1 · slider · key step, Page, Home, End · FIRST-FRAME JUMP (lands whole)**
- Rows: `rows/slider-keys.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.
- The thumb and the fill land in one frame on every key:

| Key | Frame | Thumb centre |
|---|---|---|
| ArrowRight | f56 | 185.6 → 190.0 (4.4 px) |
| PageUp | f178 | 198.9 → 243.3 (44.4 px) |
| End | f239 | 243.3 → 452.0 (208.7 px) |
| Home | f300 | 452.0 → 8.0 (444.0 px) |
| marks ArrowRight | f421 | 24.4 px |

- The tip is the same, and so is reduced motion.
- Cause: the reka thumb and range are placed by percentage. `slider/styles.css` gives `.slider-thumb` a transition on `transform` only (`:234`) and the range none on its box.
- Smallest cure: a position transition (`left`/`right`/`inset-inline`, or a transform carrier) on the dock spring, active only when not `[data-held]`, so a drag still tracks 1:1.

**S-2 · slider · track press and click · FIRST-FRAME JUMP (lands whole)**
- Rows: `rows/slider-press.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.
- A click at 85 % moves the thumb 204.2 px in one frame (f8).
- A press at 15 % moves it 319.7 px in one frame (f88, 389.8 → 70.2). The fill does the same.
- Strip: `strips/slider-press.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.seg2.start.png`.
- Same cause and cure as S-1. The tip is the same.

**S-3 · slider · loupe raise · APPEARS (whole), census 14 confirmed**
- On every press the loupe mounts at opacity 1 and scale 1 (f8 and f88 in `slider-press`; f8 in `slider-drag-md`). The 0.13 → 1 present spring never runs.
- On release it leaves well: opacity 1 → 0 and scale 1 → 0.13 over 120 ms (f150 to f165).
- The clone's "CUT" lines are false: its parent is already at opacity 0 when it unmounts.
- Strip: `strips/slider-drag-md.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-down.png`.
- Cause: `Slider.vue:333-346` mounts the loupe with `data-engaged` already true, and `styles.css:477-537` has no `@starting-style`.
- Smallest cure: `@starting-style { .glass-loupe[data-engaged] { scale: 0.13; opacity: 0 } }`.

**S-4 · slider · disabled press · error (the grasp optic answers a disabled control)**
- Rows: `rows/slider-drag-disabled.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.
- The value never moves (the thumb holds at 130.0 through the drag).
- But the press sets `data-held` (f8) and raises the loupe at opacity 1, the focus veil and the fill squish. The fill smears with the pointer for the whole drag (range.w 145.2 → 145.5 while the pointer runs 202 → 210, f60 to f64).
- Strip: `strips/slider-drag-disabled.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-down.png`.
- Cause: `Slider.vue:81` takes `isHeld` from `useDockHold` with no disabled check, and `:130` opens `grasping` from it.
- Smallest cure: gate `isHeld` (or the watch and `data-held`) on `!props.disabled`. The tip is the same.

**S-5 · slider · press · DISCRETE (the keyboard ring on a pointer press)**
- On a mouse press (f8) the track's inset rim becomes the focus ring (`0 0 0 2px` at 30 % plus an 8 px glow) in one frame. It stays after release, until blur.
- The 390 touch cell does the same at f9.
- The fill's alpha also steps 0.88 → 1 → 0.6 in the press frame (`range.c:background-color/3`, f8): the hover rung and the grasp rung land in one frame.
- Cause: `styles.css:262` keys the ring on `:has(:focus-visible)`. Chromium reports `:focus-visible` after reka moves focus to the thumb from script inside the press.
- This is measured in headless Chromium only. Whether a desktop Chrome or Safari press shows it is unconfirmed.
- Smallest cure: a box-shadow transition on the track ring (the ring is census 2), and a focus call from the press path that does not raise `:focus-visible`.

**S-6 · slider · release · MISMATCH (loupe against carriers), low**
- The loupe finishes fading 75 ms before the grasp carriers do (120 ms against 210 ms), at f150 to f165 and f174.
- Nothing visible depends on it.

Read and clean: the drag itself.
- On md, sm, lg, rtl, range, marks, vertical and vertical-inverted, at 1440 and 390, the thumb trails the pointer's grip point by about 1 px and never moves against it.
- The alternating fill-width steps (f132 to f166) are the 60 Hz pointer against 120 Hz frames: a 4.4 px value step, then 0.1 px of smear. They are not jitter.
- Release: the thumb stays where it is let go, and the fill's punch settles in 300 ms.
- Spectrum: the press moves the thumb 2.5 px (f8), and its halo changes shape in one frame (DISCRETE, f7 to f8). Both are minor.

As a whole: a drag on the slider reads as one gesture. A key step, a track press and the loupe's arrival do not; they have an end and no beginning.

### Faults

**T-1 · tabs · drag release onto another tab · JUMP (teleport) and a second glide**

This is the error the owner names. It is the worst visible fault in the family.
- Rows: `rows/tabs-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.
- The pill is released between List and Kanban at f132.
- It settles onto List by about f180 (cx 136.62, `transform` translate 88.41) and rests there for about 230 ms.
- At f205 the drag offset is dropped and the box jumps back to Grid: cx 136.62 → 48.20, −88.41 px in one frame.
- It then glides to List a second time on the CSS clock (f205 to about f231).

| f | ind.cx | transform | translate | List `data-active` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 204 | 136.62 | translate 88.41 | 4px 4px | no |
| 205 | 48.20 | none | 4px 4px | yes |
| 206 | 49.42 | none | 5.2px 4px | |
| 212 | 80.75 | none | 36.6px 4px | |

- The same fault appears wherever the release changes the tab:
  - third2, released two-thirds of the way to List: f151, −88.41.
  - fling to Timeline: f131, −265.2 px.
  - vertical: f189, −34.6.
  - 390 touch: f210, −67.8.
  - The tip: f205 / f152 / f130, the same values.
- Releasing back onto the starting tab (third1) is clean: 245 ms, no jump.
- Strips:
  - `strips/tabs-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-up.png`
  - `strips/tabs-drag.tip.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-up.png`
  - `strips/tabs-drag-fling.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-up.png`
- Cause, `composables/motion/morph/useDragMorph.ts:196-205` (`commitIfSettled`):
  - `onSnap` writes the model.
  - `nextTick(() => morph.clear())` is queued before the indicator writer's own `nextTick(updateSliders)` (`useSelectionIndicator.ts:174-177`), which the model watcher schedules inside the same flush.
  - So `clear()` releases the transition lock and removes the offset first. The box then sits at Grid's base `translate`.
  - `updateSliders` then writes List's `translate` with the transition live, and `segmented.css:159-166` glides it.
- Smallest cure: write the base before clearing.
  - Clear inside the writer's tick, after it: two `nextTick`s, or call the writer from `onSnap`.
  - Keep `lockSpatialTransition` held across the base write, so the base lands under the offset in the same frame and the offset is removed in that frame.

**T-2 · tabs · drag release · FIRST-FRAME JUMP at release, and lag while held**
- While held, the pill trails the pointer's grip point by a median of 11.7 px, 30 px at most (`DRAG` table, f26 to f131).
- On the fling it trails by up to 72 px.
- At release it jumps:
  - 1440: −13.9 px at f132 (182.7 → 168.8).
  - Fling: +88.1 px at f32.
  - Vertical: −6.4 px at f121.
  - 390: −10.7 px at f138.
- The `Draggable` re-seats the spring at the release point, so the lag collapses in one frame.
- The settle then runs 826 ms for 32 px (f133 to f231) and 1048 ms on the fling.
- Cause: the follow is the dock spring chasing the pointer, not a 1:1 offset (`useDragMorph.ts:183`, `armDraggable`), against motion-canon's rule that the thing in the hand follows 1:1 (the sortable states it at `styles.css:202-204`).
- Smallest cure: write the offset 1:1 while held and hand the spring the release velocity only. The tip is the same.

**T-3 · tabs · every glide · LAST-FRAME SNAP (the dock curve's tail)**
- Rows: `rows/tabs-click.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.
- The last step is larger than the one before it on every glide:

| Segment | Frames | Step before | Last step |
|---|---|---|---|
| seg2 | f116 to f117 | −2.75 px | −4.04 px |
| seg3 | f192 to f193 | 2.65 px | 3.01 px |
| seg4 | f279 to f280 | −2.37 px | −5.10 px |

- Cause: `--spring-dock` (`styles/tokens/scheme-spring.css:61`) is generated over a 2 % settle band. It ends at 0.97317 at 97.96 % and then steps to 1. The last 2.7 % of the travel (4.8 px on a 177 px hop) lands in the curve's final 4 ms, inside one frame.
- `--spring-press` (0.97581) and `--spring-present` (0.97550) have the same tail. On a 120 ms press it is invisible. On the dock row it is not, because the dock row carries long travel: tab indicators, sortable rows, pager pips, sheet grip, carousel fade.
- Strip: `strips/tabs-click.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.seg2.end.png`.
- Smallest cure: generate the dock row over the 0.005 band, as panel, bloom and world already are (springPresets LAW 0b names a long stroke as the case). Its clock lengthens from 210 ms to about 300 ms. No API changes.
- The tip is the same.

**T-4 · tabs · reversal mid-glide · INTERRUPTION (the velocity turns in one frame)**
- The click on Timeline, reversed to Grid:
  - At 30 %: no jump.
  - At 50 % (f255) and at 80 % (f425): the position is continuous, but the velocity flips from +3.5 px a frame to −9.8 and −12.9 px a frame in the next two frames (f426 to f427). It reads as a bounce off a wall.
- The tip shows it at 50 % and 80 % (−18.0 at f264, −14.0 at f430).
- Strip: `strips/tabs-click.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.seg4.start.png` (the 50 % reversal).
- Cause: a CSS transition retargets from its current value with zero velocity and a fresh 210 ms clock (`segmented.css:159-166`).
- Smallest cure: route the click glide through the same `SpringProgress` the drag already owns, which carries velocity. That is a wave's work, not a patch.

**T-5 · tabs · glide · MISMATCH (label against pill)**
- The arriving label finishes its colour 115 to 174 ms before the pill lands (seg2: 851 against 966 ms; seg6: 3559 against 3733 ms).
- The label leaving starts 85 to 150 ms late on a reversal.
- This is a different clock and a different curve: 200 ms bezier against 210 ms spring plus the tail.
- Smallest cure: put the label colour on the indicator's clock, or delay it as A K2 does (STUDY §1, "colour waits for shape").

**T-6 · tabs · panel content swap · FIRST-FRAME JUMP (lands whole)**
- The text panel under `tabs-panel` changes height 27.9 → 55.8 px in one frame (f6, and back at f82).
- Its text is cut, not faded.
- SegmentedTabs ships no panel (the panel is the consumer's `<p>`), so this is the consumer's cut. The library offers no tween for it.
- It is recorded so the gestalt seat can rule whether tabs owe a panel recipe (census rows P3 and P4).

Read and clean:
- Key steps: ArrowRight, End, Home, and three ArrowRights at 80 ms. The indicator glides 210 ms per step with no snap or reversal flagged, and the rapid steps chain without a jump (`tabs-keys`, no faults).
- Uneven-width tabs: the width tweens with the translate (one clock).
- The underline mark: no fault.
- Release back onto the starting tab: clean (third1).

As a whole: a click reads as one gesture with a small hitch at the end (T-3). A drag that changes the tab reads as two gestures: a slow settle, a 230 ms stop, a jump back, and a second glide.

### Faults

**H-1 · sheet · second drag release · JUMP (stale spring)**
- Rows: `rows/sheet-detent-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.
- The first drag (from 0.4, a third of the way to 0.7, released) falls back cleanly.
- The second drag carries the sheet to t = 0.6 (540 px). At release (f487) the sheet drops to 373.4 px in one frame, −166.6 px (t 0.6 → 0.415). It then rises to 0.7 (630 px) over about 500 ms.

| f | sheet.h | `--detent-t` |
|---|---|---|
| 486 | 540.00 | 0.6 |
| 487 | 373.41 | 0.4149 |
| 488 | 390.19 | 0.4336 |
| 490 | 424.98 | 0.4722 |

- 390 touch: −162.1 px at f500. The tip: f481, −166.6. In the earlier smoke run: f484.
- Strip: `strips/sheet-detent-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-up2.png`.
- Cause, `sheet/detents/use.ts`:
  - `settle()` (`:121-130`) reuses the live spring when the leg is unchanged (`spring && leg === nextLeg ? spring : seatSpring(...)`).
  - A drag writes `t` directly and only `stop()`s the spring (`:183`), so the reused spring still holds the previous rung (0.4) as its value.
  - The first release changes the leg (travel → rung) and re-seats, which hides the fault once. Every later release onto a non-zero rung, and every key step after a drag, plays from the old rung.
- Smallest cure: in `settle()`, re-seat whenever a drag ran (or whenever `spring.value !== t.value`), at `(t.value, release velocity)`.
- Related: the release velocity is used only to pick the rung. The re-seated spring starts at the old spring's velocity (0), so a throw stops dead before it travels. Pass `velocity` (the drag's, in fraction/s) to `seatSpring`.

**H-2 · sheet · detented open · FIRST-FRAME JUMP (lands whole at the floor)**
- The sheet mounts at 206.9 px and opacity 1 in its first frame (f7), while `--detent-t` is 0.011.
- It does not move until t passes 0.23 (about f25, 150 ms in), then grows to 360 px.
- 57 % of the travel lands whole and the first 150 ms is still.
- Strip: `strips/sheet-detent-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.seg1.start.png`.
- Cause: the `min-block-size` floor in `sheet/styles.css:534-544` (the action's operable minimum) holds the box while the scalar runs under it.
- Smallest cure: below the floor, carry the remaining travel as a `translate` along the anchored axis (the side sheet's own law), so the floor-sized box slides in rather than appearing.

**H-3 · sheet · detented close and drag-to-dismiss · CUT (the sheet and the scrim vanish)**

This is the worst sheet fault.
- Escape on a sheet at rung 0.25 (`sheet-open-close`, f896): the box shrinks 225 → 206.9 px in three frames (f896 to f898). It then stands still for 440 ms with the scrim held at 0.28, while `--detent-t` runs to 0 invisibly.
- At f951 the sheet (206.9 px, opacity 1), its grip, its footer and the scrim (0.28) are removed in one frame.
- Drag-to-dismiss (`sheet-detent-drag`, release at f672) does the same: still at 206.9 px from f672, gone at f728 with the scrim at 0.28.
- The finger stops being followed once the box reaches the floor.
- 390: f747.
- Strips:
  - `strips/sheet-detent-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.after-up3.png`
  - `strips/sheet-detent-drag.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.seg4.end.png`
- Cause: the same floor (`styles.css:534`, whose comment says the exit is the unmount), plus `scrimDetentOpacity` (`sheet/motion.ts:50-52`), which floors the scrim at 0.28 at t = 0.
- Smallest cure: the H-2 translate below the floor, and a scrim that runs to 0 on the close leg (`scrimOpacity` on the travel leg).

**H-4 · sheet · every rung change · MISMATCH (grip on a second clock)**
- The grip's width ends 273 to 523 ms after the sheet does. Open: 722 against 390 ms. Each key step: 307, 256 and 523 ms late. Dismiss: 467 ms late.
- The grip creeps sub-pixel for 587 ms after the sheet rests (f16 to f86).
- Cause: `sheet/styles.css:284-286` transitions `inline-size` and `opacity` on `--spring-dock` while `--detent-t`, which they read, already changes every frame. Every frame restarts a 210 ms transition, so the grip trails the sheet.
- Smallest cure: delete the transition. The scalar is already the tween.

**H-5 · sheet · every detent motion · LAYOUT IN FRAMES**
- Traced run, `rows/trace/sheet-detent-drag...`: 82 of 82 frames lay out on open, 170 of 186 on the first drag, 134 of 148 and 102 of 116 on the next.
- That is 1.2 to 1.6 layouts a frame, 21 to 33 ms of layout per motion.
- `block-size` is animated (against P5). No frame was dropped in this page, but the cost grows with the sheet's content.
- Cure: a wave, not a patch. Scale or clip a fixed box and correct the content, the R E1 technique in STUDY §1.

Read and clean:
- The side sheet slides in 342 ms and out 173 ms, the out at half the in (STUDY §1 pattern 2), and it is off-screen when it unmounts.
- Escape at 120 ms into an opening side sheet turns from where it is (1131 px, f266 to f268), with no jump.
- The first drag's fall-back to its rung is clean.
- The drag follows 1:1. Its first-step flags (f163, f412, f623) are the pointer's own 60 Hz steps after the pause.

Reduced motion:
- The side sheet and its scrim appear whole (f7) and leave whole.
- The detented sheet appears whole at 360 px (f1474).
- Nothing fades.

As a whole: the side sheet reads as one gesture. The detented sheet has no beginning (it appears at the floor) and no end (it stops, then vanishes), and its second release jumps.
