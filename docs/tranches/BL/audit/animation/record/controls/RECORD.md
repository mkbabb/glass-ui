# The controls family, frame by frame

Recorder: claude-opus-5-5. Read on 2026-11-13.

Components: button, checkbox, switch, radio-group, toggle-group, chip, dark-mode-toggle (and the page re-tint it causes), input, textarea, number-field, labeled-field, label.

## How this was read

- Tree: the published v11.0.1 (`e42bc2705`), built by the harness.
- Page server: one preview on port 5396, serving `harness/tree-v11.0.1/dist-demo-controls`.
  - That build is the harness demo plus one page of this recorder's, `?c=controls-state` (`mount/controls-state.vue`). The page binds Button `disabled` and `loading`, LabeledField `invalid` and `description`, Label `requirement`, Input `invalid` and the bits' `disabled` to `window.__ctl`, so a scenario can flip a prop that no story flips.
  - `tools/build-controls.sh` built it, and `tools/serve-controls.sh` served it. The shared `dist-demo` was not touched.
  - Every other page is the component's story (`?c=<name>`). Switches and radios are on the `forms/checks` story (`?c=checkbox`, `?c=radio-group`), because the `forms/toggle` story holds only toggle groups.
- Engine: headless Chromium 149 with GPU compositing. Rows are a median 7.6 to 7.8 ms apart.
- Cells:
  - 1440 fine pointer, light, for every motion.
  - 390 coarse (real CDP touch) for the press and toggle gestures.
  - 1440 dark for the Button hovers.
  - 1440 reduced motion, once per component.
  - The theme flip records light to dark and back in one run.
- Scenarios: `scenarios/*.json`, generated by `scenarios/make.mjs`, 41 in all. They ran in three guarded batches through `tools/run.sh`.
- Rows: `rows/<scenario>.v11.0.1.chromium.<cell>.rows.json`, each with its analyser output (`.faults.txt`, `.faults.json`). Traced runs are in `rows/trace/`, and a repeat of chip-select in `rows/rerun/`.
- `logs/summary.txt` is every faults file with the noise struck by `tools/sum.mjs`:
  - colour hue channels under 0.03;
  - pixel channels under 1 px;
  - colour L/a/b "lands whole" where that colour's alpha runs to or from 0, which is not paint.
- Frame tables come from `tools/rd.mjs <rows> <from> <to> <as.key>...`.
- Pseudo-elements: the sampler cannot read `::before` or `::after`. Where a control paints there (the Button and Chip gleam, the Chip glow), the scenario installs a per-frame mirror that copies the pseudo-element's computed opacity into `--pb-op` and `--pa-op` on the host. The rows then carry them as numbers, at the cost of one inline style write per frame. A value of `-1` means the pseudo-element has no content.
- Strips: `strips/`, holding only the cited ones. A strip starts at the first changed frame, so the frame before a jump is the previous strip tile or the row table.
- Frame numbers are row indices in the named rows file. One row is one rendered frame.
- WebKit confirmation was not reached. Playwright's WebKit build aborted on load (`libc++abi: terminating`, SIGKILL) for dmt-flip and toggle-single (`logs/batchC-webkit.log`). This matches the known glass-ui crash in that build; it is not Safari. No WebKit rows exist.

**B-2 · button · press and hold · MISMATCH (two clocks on one channel), CREEP**
- Rows: `rows/button-press-primary.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.rows.json`.

| f | ms | press-t | scale (x y) | `::before` op |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 53 | 0 | 1 1 | 0 (down) |
| 25 | 190 | 1.006 | 1.0068 0.979 | 0.004 |
| 47 | 352 | 1 | 1.0254 0.930 | 0.008 |
| 63 | 474 | 0.897 | 1.0322 0.9125 | 0.054 (up) |
| 79 | 591 | −0.002 | 1.0242 0.933 | 0.110 |
| 97 | 725 | −0.001 | 1 1 | 0.139 |

- The JS spring reaches its target by f25 (190 ms). The painted scale keeps creeping for the rest of the hold, 0.979 to 0.9125 in Y, and is still moving at release.
- On release, press-t is back at 0 by f79, but the scale takes until f97, 250 ms after the pointer left.
- `anims` lists a new CSS `scale` transition (120 ms, the press `linear()`) and a new `::before` opacity transition (300 ms) on nearly every frame from f9 to f79: 44 of each.
- The same reading holds on secondary, quiet and text, and at 390 coarse. On the NumberField stepper during a 1.8 s hold (`rows/nf-hold…`), the gleam takes 1.9 s to reach rest.
- The amount is a 9 % vertical squash and 3 % widening, on a control.
- Strips: `strips/button-press-primary.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-fine-light.start.png`, `…end.png`.
- Cause:
  - `useLiquidPress` writes `scale` inline per frame (`Button.vue` `hostStyle`).
  - `.button` declares `transition: … scale var(--spring-press-duration) var(--transition-liquid-spatial)` (`src/components/button/styles.css:105`). Each JS write starts a fresh CSS transition, so the paint chases the drive.
  - The gleam's `:active::before` intensity reads `--glass-btn-press-t` (`button/styles.css`, last rule), and its 300 ms opacity transition restarts every frame for the same reason.
- Smallest cure: one owner per channel. While `[data-press-armed]` (JS owns scale), drop the `scale` leg (`transition-property` without `scale`), and drop the `::before` opacity transition under `:active`. The drive is already a spring; the CSS clock only delays it. Whether a 9 % squash belongs on a command is restraint's call (C-39), not this record's.

## Family patterns

**F-1 · the focus ring lands whole on Button, the bits and Toggle items, and cuts on every control.** B-3, C-3 and I-1 are one fault in three registers. `outline-style` is set only under `:focus-visible`, so it cannot tween. The field register already rests the colour at transparent and so fades in; nothing rests the style. The cure is one rule per register: rest at `outline: <w> solid transparent; outline-offset: 2px` and tween `outline-color` only.

**F-2 · reduced motion is a cut on every component of this family.** Every motion lands in one frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`:
- hovers (`button-hover-quiet…1440-rm`, f7 and f411);
- toggles (checkbox f8, switch f7, radio f8, toggle-group f8);
- focus (input f408);
- the theme flip (f8);
- the chip select (f7, and the glow at f15).

The control-bit sheet's own reduced-motion arm asks for an opacity fade (`src/styles/glass/control-bit.css:369-373`, `transition: opacity var(--duration-fast)`). That fade never runs: the universal carve sets `transition-duration: 0s !important` (`src/styles/utilities/a11y-overrides.css:46-53`), and the rows show the mark's opacity landing whole (`rows/checkbox-toggle…1440-rm`, f8, f264). This confirms census point 4.

Strip: `strips/checkbox-toggle.v11.0.1.chromium.1440-rm.seg1.start.png`.

Cure: the carve keeps opacity and colour transitions at a short clock (P6) and zeroes only transform, translate, scale and rotate.

**F-3 · the spring `linear()` curves end on their largest late step.** The switch thumb (0.55 px after 0.12, S-2), the theme icon (4.95° after 1.56°, D-2), the bit marks (C-4) and the field colours (3 % last steps) each take a final step two to four times the one before it.
- The `--spring-*` `linear()` tokens are sampled to the 2 % band of the spring's settle time (P4), so the last 2 % lands in one step.
- At these sizes it is under half a pixel except on the theme icon.
- Cure, if it is wanted: let the generator end each curve with a short sampled tail to 1 (a few more points between 98 % and 100 %), in `scripts/regen-spring-tokens.mjs`. That is a token change across the library, so it belongs to a wave, not a patch.

## Read and found clean

Read as a whole:
- The control bits (checkbox, radio, switch) each read as one gesture with a beginning and an end: fill and mark move together, out mirrors in, and a reversal turns where it stands.
- The Button press does not. It begins on time but has no end you can point to: the squash is still deepening when the finger lifts, and the light keeps arriving after the shape has settled.
- The theme flip reads as two events, a cut and then a fade.
- The toggle group, the field lines and the loading ring read as no motion at all.
