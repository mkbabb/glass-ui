## Cures

**K1, The spring tokens' linear() curves jump to 1 in their last stop.** Root cause: the curve is sampled up to the settle horizon, where the spring is still 2.3 to 2.7 % short of 1 (0.4 to 0.5 % on the 0.005-band rows; panel is 0.47 % over). The last stop is then pinned to 1, so the whole residual closes in one 1/49 segment. Smallest cure, in glass and not in the keyframes package (keyframes' default 4x-response horizon leaves a residual near 1e-10; glass's short horizon is what exposes the pin): in springProjection.ts:48-68, take v(T) from SpringProgress.tickToTime(settleSeconds) (the call :35-41 already makes) and divide every interior value by it, keeping the final 1. keyframes 6.0.0 exports only springLinearStops and springTimingFunction, both pinned, so glass rescales the interior values itself, both in the string at :64 and in the timing function and peak at :56-60 (the last LUT segment must lerp to 1, not to 1/v(T)). Then rerun regen-spring-tokens.mjs. Divide by v(T), not by the last interior sample as AUDIT.md:178 words it (INBOUND O-311 already says the value at settle time). Names and clocks do not change. Cured last-to-previous ratios: press 0.91, present 0.91, dock 0.90, panel 0.89, bloom 0.86, world 0.88. Panel's peak goes from +4.2 % to +3.7 %, the mirror line regenerates, and LAW 0 holds. Correction to the earlier figure: t90 moves earlier on five rows (press 94.9 -> 91.4 ms, present 136.2 -> 128.2, dock 158.3 -> 150.2, bloom 227.7 -> 225.3, world 297.3 -> 293.1) and 1.3 ms later on panel (169.7 -> 171.0, because v(T) > 1 shrinks it). The 'CSS_t90 == JS_t90' lines (:79-80, :93-95) need restating. Witness: a static vitest over each --spring-* linear() in scheme-spring.css, requiring the last segment's slope to be at most 1.5x the previous segment's, with the same sign. RED on v11.1.0 for all six (4.31, 9.65, 6.72, 3.88, 3.76, 6.95), GREEN after. springProjection.test.ts:24-44 stays GREEN after the regen, provided the peak comes from the normalized timing function. keyframes' unchanged pane-slide at 120 Hz is the consumer's witness.

**K2, A tooltip opens on the focus a closing Sheet or Dialog hands back to its opener.** Root cause: the tooltip has no keyboard-focus check. reka ships one (ignoreNonKeyboardFocus, a :focus-visible test), but it is off by default and glass's Tooltip leaves it off. So every scripted focus, from either return path above, opens the hint at once. A coarse-pointer check would be the wrong fix: it would leave a mouse-closed dialog raising the tooltip, and it would hide a real keyboard focus on a touch device that has a keyboard. Smallest cure: Tooltip.vue:29-36 passes :ignore-non-keyboard-focus="true" to RekaTooltipRoot. No prop is added (the d.ts test at :76 stays), and no consumer could choose the old value through glass, so it is a patch. The cure also updates tooltip.contract.test.ts: the assertion at :174 flips to true, and the two tests that open the tooltip with a synthetic focus event (:77-87, :104-126) must really focus the trigger. happy-dom matches :focus-visible on any active element (SelectorItem.js:341-345), so the scripted-return case can only be shown in a browser. Witness, in Chromium and WebKit at 390 with hasTouch: a Sheet and a Dialog whose trigger sits inside TooltipTrigger as-child. Open each by tap and close it three ways: tap Close, swipe the sheet down, and a scripted close. After each, focus is on the opener and no [role=tooltip] mounts within 1000 ms. Repeat with a mouse-click close at 1440. Tab onto the opener, and a close by Escape, still open the tooltip. RED on v11.1.0, GREEN after. The WebKit swipe leg matters because engines differ on :focus-visible after a scripted focus. Chromium carries the state forward from the previous focus. WebKit, as I recall its rule (unverified here), looks only at whether the last focus came from a click, so reka's scripted focus on open followed by the scripted return can match :focus-visible there. If that leg stays RED, the fix moves to the two returns glass owns (path 1 and path 2 above): after a pointer close they ask for a focus that is not visible (FocusOptions.focusVisible: false, once engine support is checked). It does not move to a pointer-type check.

**K3, The reduced-motion rule turns every site fade into a cut.** Root cause on v11.1.0: the blanket guard is a layered !important (`@layer components`, a11y-overrides.css:45-54), which no unlayered rule can beat. Cure built on the tip at faf12109d: a normal declaration in a last `reduced-motion` layer (a11y-overrides.css:20-30, index.css:1), with glass's own fades as carves. Witness: reduce spec `site` (release-11-1-1-reduce.spec.ts:107), RED on v11.1.0 and GREEN on d0f85bd29 in Chromium and WebKit at 390 and 1440.

**K4, A detented Sheet wearing sheet-animate shows its plate again between the exit fade and unmount.** Root cause: the published `sheet-animate` out leg (tw-animate `animate-out`, fill none) ends at --duration-fast while glass's detented spring holds the mount to about 480 ms, and the detented arm, unlike the sliding arm, leaves CSS animations on. The utility also fades only the plate, never its sibling frost sampler.

Smallest cure, all in the utility:
- Add `motion-safe:data-[state=closed]:fill-mode-forwards` (btn.css:99-104). It is gated to motion-safe because a forwards-filled 0.01 ms keyframe under reduce would beat 26e2fea69's inline fade and make it a cut.
- Put the same fade, on the same clock and fill, on the plate's next sibling (`& + [data-slot="glass-graded-halo"]`, the span authored right after the content at SheetContent.vue:458), so the frost leaves with the plate.

No export, token or unchosen default changes. Making the detented exit a fade in normal motion would change the default look (a minor) and is not needed.

Witness: a bottom detented SheetContent with class `sheet-animate`, at 390 touch and 1440, in Chromium and WebKit, closed by the ✕, Escape, an outside press and a flick, filmed per frame in normal motion with both the plate's and the halo's opacity. The halo stays within 0.05 of the plate on every frame. Once the plate reads ≤ 0.02, no later frame before unmount paints either above 0.02.
- RED on v11.1.0: the plate is back to 1 at about 200 ms on the floor stub and the halo holds 1 throughout.
- RED on d0f85bd29: the plate is back to 1 at about 200 ms while sliding, and the halo holds 1.
- GREEN after.

A reduce leg keeps the out leg a monotone fade of at least two frames within 200 ms.

**K4b, A Sheet's frost sampler stays whole through its reduced-motion fade, then goes in one frame.** Root cause: the Sheet's glass is two boxes, the plate and its sibling frost sampler, and the reduced-motion fade is written on one. Smallest cure: SheetContent writes the same fade value on the halo span. One fade value (detented `sheetDetents.opacity`, sliding `1 - p` under reduce) is read by both springStyle and a `:style` on the span at :458-465.

Witness: the reduce spec's Sheet right, Sheet bottom and detented Sheet cells also film `[data-slot="glass-graded-halo"]`. On every frame it is present, its opacity is within 0.02 of the plate's, and it leaves at ≤ 0.02 and enters from ≤ 0.02. Chromium and WebKit, 390 and 1440.
- RED on v11.1.0: plate and halo both cut.
- RED on d0f85bd29: the halo reads 1 on every frame of the plate's fade and goes in one frame.
- GREEN after.
