### R10-01-01: the release re-seats the spring from its pre-drag value (F-78, EXTENDS, HIGH)

- **Mechanism**: the drag writes the scalar directly (`sheet/detents/use.ts:206`, `t.value = …`) after only stopping the spring (`:179`, `spring?.stop()`). On release, `settle()` reuses the live spring whenever the leg is unchanged (`:140-141`, `spring && leg === nextLeg ? spring : seatSpring(…)`), and `SpringProgress`'s `target` setter re-seats "from the current `(value, velocity)`" — the spring's own, which is where it stood before the drag (`keyframes.d.ts:3599-3604`). When the target equals the spring's settled target, the setter is idempotent and `play()` emits the settled value once. The leg is unchanged on a drag-to-dismiss after an open (both `travel`), on every rung-to-rung drag after the first, and on a catch mid-settle. This is F-78's "restarts from … the previous target": the drag is an interruption of the spring, and the release maps it onto the spring's from-rest state. The right shape is in the library already (`useSpringMount` re-targets from its current value; the leg-change branch here, `seatSpring(nextLeg, live, …)`, does too). Source-only sub-clause: even on a leg change the new spring takes `spring?.velocity` (`:139`), the stopped spring's, not the finger's `velocity` (`:167`), so the throw's energy picks the rung but never reaches the spring.
- **Measured** (`res-ladder2-chromium.json`, `res-ladder2-firefox.json`, `res-reuse-chromium.json`, `res-reuse2-firefox.json`, `res-catch-chromium.json`):
  - Drag-to-dismiss from `d=1`, released at t = 0 with the plate at the floor: the next frame paints t = 0.973, top 22.61 at 390×844 (a 650.39 px step) and top 10.45 at 844×390 (175.08 px), then the close replays from there (16 of 16 Chromium touch and mouse; 4 of 4 Firefox mouse, the same 650.38 / 175.07 px). Visual: `dismiss-p390-a-finger-at-bottom-before-release.png` (plate at the floor, finger at the bottom) against `dismiss-p390-b-40ms-after-release.png` (40 ms later the plate is back at t = 0.58).
  - Second drag, released near the same rung (from 1 down to t = 0.70, a crawl-out release): 253.2 px in one frame at 390×844 and 117 px at 844×390, no settle at all (`0@1.000` on the next frame; 8 of 8 Chromium, 4 of 4 Firefox).
  - Second drag, released toward another rung (to t = 0.50): the next frame is t = 0.968 (395.16 px portrait, 173.12 px landscape), then it travels down to 0.36 (8 of 8 Chromium, 4 of 4 Firefox).
  - Catch mid-settle: fling up from 0.36, press at +70 ms, drag 120 px down, release: 232.4 → 83.6 px (portrait) and 123.7 → 15.2 px (landscape) in one frame, back to where the spring stood at the catch (8 of 8 Chromium). The control, a catch with no finger travel, is continuous (8 of 8). Firefox: 1 of 2 portrait reproduces (149.1 → 19.2); the landscape Firefox fling runs off the viewport in this probe and is not counted.
- **Born-RED**: across every release the largest per-frame plate step is at most 2× the median step of the settle that follows (HEAD: 650.39 px against a median of 10.07 px on the portrait dismiss; 253.2 px against 0 on the same-rung return).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: W-INTERRUPT, the Sheet row. Every release re-seats a fresh spring at `(t.value, release velocity in fraction/s)` whatever the leg (`seatSpring` unconditionally on release, or `spring.reset(t, v)` before setting the target). This row is ahead of W-DETENT-FLOOR in order: it is live on every detented Sheet at every viewport, the floor only where a rung is under it.

### R10-01-02: the floor swallows motion, not only rest (F-65, EXTENDS, HIGH)

- **Mechanism**: R9-02-02's two authorities, in motion. The engine drags, flings and animates the scalar in viewport fractions (`use.ts:83-87`, `:206`, `:250`), and nothing it computes knows the floor. The paint takes `max(t·100dvh, floor)` (`sheet/styles.css:530-540`). While `t·vh` is under the floor the plate does not move, so the finger, the open spring and the close spring all run through a band the paint does not show. The keyboard and the fling address rungs by index, so a merged rung stays selectable (`SheetContent.vue:198-244`: `rungIndex`, `onGripKeydown` → `snapTo`).
- **Measured**:
  - Drag dead zone (finger travel with the plate edge frozen, the finger kept inside the viewport; `res-ladder2-*`): up from 0.12 — 157.2 px at 844×390, 61.6 px at 390×844; down from 1 to dismissal — 154.4 px and 130.8 px. 16 of 16 Chromium (touch and mouse), 8 of 8 Firefox (156–157, 155, 62, 131 px). The arithmetic agrees: (204.5 − 0.12·390) = 157.7 px.
  - Open and close (`p-pop.mjs`): at 844×390 for both rungs, and at 390×844 for 0.12, the first painted frame is already the whole floor plate (204.5 / 171 px), the last painted frame is the same plate, and 0 frames change size across the open, the rest and the close (16 of 16 cells, Chromium and Firefox). The plate pops in and pops out while the scrim fades. After a dismissal the plate holds still at the floor for 583–651 ms before it unmounts (`res-ladder-chromium.json`).
  - Merged stops selected (`res-fling-chromium.json`, 844×390): a 625 px/s fling up from 0.12 lands on 0.36 in 4 of 4 cells, and the plate is identical before and after (top 185.53, 204.5 px). A 250 px/s nudge down from 0.36 lands on 0.12 in 3 of 4. The grip's ArrowDown from 0.36 selects 0.12 in 4 of 4 (Chromium and Firefox): the plate does not move, `aria-valuetext` changes to "12%, position 1 of 3" and the grip mark narrows 39.19 → 34.39 px.
  - The invisible rung changes the next gesture: the same plate has a 157.7 px dead zone above 0.12 and 64.2 px above 0.36 (the same arithmetic; the 0.12 figure measured above).
- **Placement**: F-65's round-9 correction already holds the rest half and says the drag and the fling were not probed. This is that half; the mechanism (the model in fractions, the paint floored) is unchanged.
- **Born-RED** (extends W-DETENT-FLOOR's): at 844×390, every frame of a drag from the lowest offered rung toward the next moves the plate edge by at least ½ of the finger's step (HEAD: 56 frames and 157.2 px of finger with 0 px of plate), and the open to the lowest offered rung paints at least 6 frames of size change (HEAD: 0).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: W-DETENT-FLOOR, widened. Resolving the rungs to `max(rung·vh, floor)` and deduplicating is not enough for motion: the drag and both legs must run in the resolved space, so the scalar's zero is the floor-sized plate entering from the edge (a translate for the entry and exit legs below the floor, or a `t` that maps `[0, rung₀]` onto `[off-edge, floor]`). The grip's keyboard steps across the deduplicated ladder only.

### R10-01-03: every end of the detent drag commits, or never arrives (F-78, EXTENDS, HIGH)

- **Mechanism**: the engine binds `pointermove`, `pointerup` and `pointercancel` on the host (`use.ts:190-192`) and routes `pointercancel` to the release path (`onPointerup`, `:209-226`), which projects and commits, dismissal included (`:220-223`). It listens to no `lostpointercapture`, window `blur`, `visibilitychange` or `pagehide`, and reads no `buttons` on a move (`:195-207`). `onPointerdown` returns while a drag is held (`:171`, `pointerId !== null`). R4-01-04 and R5-01-01's two halves on a machine F-78 does not list.
- **Measured** (`res-cancel-chromium.json`, `res-bfcache-chromium.json`, `p-latch.mjs`):
  - `touchCancel` at t = 0.660, after a drag up from 0.36: the sheet commits to 1 (4 of 4, both cells). `touchCancel` at t = 0.060, after a drag down from 0.36: the sheet **closes** (4 of 4).
  - Page hide (bfcache round trip mid-drag, the release while hidden; `pagehide(persisted)` → hidden → visible → `pageshow(persisted)` logged): the restored page is still dragging (`data-dragging`, t = 0.660, between rungs) in 8 of 8. Mouse: `lostpointercapture` arrives, and six moves with `buttons: 0` carry the sheet from 0.660 to 0.482 at 390×844 (150 px) and to 0.275 at 844×390 (4 of 4; `bfcache-mouse-p390-after-wander.png`).
  - Touch: three fresh touches (pointer ids 3, 4, 5), each a 100 px drag, move nothing, and the sheet stays at 0.660 with `data-dragging` (4 of 4; `latch-touch-390x844-after-3-drags.png`, `latch-touch-844x390-after-3-drags.png`). Only a close clears the latch (`:254`).
- **Instrument note**: a background tab does not hide a page in new headless (R5-01's finding, re-measured: `visibilityState` stayed "visible" and the touch ended normally), so the hide ran through bfcache with the default-args ban on bfcache lifted.
- **Born-RED**: a `touchCancel` mid-drag leaves the model and the plate at the gesture's origin rung (HEAD: 0.36 → 1, and 0.36 → closed); after a bfcache round trip mid-drag, the next touch drags the sheet (HEAD: 0 px over three drags).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: W-INTERRUPT's one shared end-of-gesture helper (round-5 correction), with the Sheet's detent engine as a consumer: `pointercancel` and every lifecycle edge spring back to `startT`'s rung and never dismiss; the latch clears on any of them.

### R10-01-04: the release velocity never decays (F-46, EXTENDS, MED)

- **Mechanism**: `velocity` is updated only inside `pointermove` from the last pair of samples (`use.ts:197-201`) and read at release without regard to the time since that move (`:214-219`). A finger that stops before lifting sends no move (the coordinate has not changed), so the release carries the last in-flight velocity. R3-01-02's shape ("nothing feeds zero at `scrollend`, so `--member-lag` keeps its last in-flight value"), here on the throw.
- **Measured** (`p-hold.mjs`): a 90 px pull down from 1 in four 16 ms moves, then 600 ms with the finger still, then the release. At 844×390 the sheet sits at t = 0.769, whose nearest rung is 1, and it lands on 0.36 in 6 of 6 cells (Chromium touch 2, mouse 2; Firefox mouse 2). At 390×844 (t = 0.893) it lands on 0.36 in 3 of 4 Chromium cells and 0 of 2 Firefox cells, which tracks the last event pair's timing.
- **Born-RED**: after 150 ms without a move, the release resolves to the rung nearest the position (HEAD: 0.769 → 0.36).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: F-46's round-3 law on the gesture: the release velocity is a short windowed estimate (the last ~80–100 ms of samples, iOS-style), decayed to 0 by the age of the last sample.

### R10-01-05: the body drag is decided twice, and the UA decides first (F-65, EXTENDS, MED)

- **Mechanism**: the engine lets a press in the body drag the sheet when the body is at its leading edge, "the iOS contract" (`use.ts:89-101`, `:177`). The UA decides the same question from CSS before any script runs: `touch-action: none` is set on the grip and the header only (`sheet/styles.css:96-101`), so the body computes `touch-action: auto` and the browser takes the pan and fires `pointercancel`, which the engine then commits (R10-01-03). One decision with two authorities is F-65's widened mechanism (round 6: "any quantity with two sources"; round 9: a choice the engine offers is resolved by another authority). F-78 was weighed: the cancel's commit is its row; the ownership split is not an interruption.
- **Measured** (`p-body.mjs`, the "Body row 1" text, `elementFromPoint` → `sheet-content-region` with `touch-action: auto`): under touch, 3 moves then `pointercancel` + `lostpointercapture`, and the sheet does not follow in 8 of 8 cells (up from 0.36 and down from 1, both viewports, twice). Under the mouse the same press drags the sheet in 8 of 8 (e.g. t 0.36 → 0.538 mid-drag at 390×844).
- **Born-RED**: under CDP touch at 390×844, a 150 px pull up from "Body row 1" with the body at scroll 0 moves the plate at least 100 px (HEAD: 0 px, `pointercancel` after 3 moves).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: one authority for the gesture: the body's `touch-action` follows the engine's rule (`pan-y` only while the region can scroll away from its leading edge, `none` otherwise, set on the region when its scroll position changes), or the body drag is struck from the engine and its comment.

### R10-01-06: the drag span is read once, the paint reads live (F-65, EXTENDS, LOW)

- **Mechanism**: `span = dragSpan(side())` reads `innerHeight` once at `pointerdown` (`use.ts:183`, `:83-87`), and the plate reads `100dvh` live (`sheet/styles.css:530`). When the layout viewport shrinks mid-drag (a `resizes-content` keyboard), finger and plate stop agreeing.
- **Measured** (`p-kbd.mjs`; the keyboard emulated by `setViewportSize` mid-drag, which resizes the layout viewport as `interactive-widget=resizes-content` does; no real keyboard): 390×844 → 390×544: finger-to-edge offset 35 → 212.8 px at the resize, then 0.645 px of plate per finger px. 844×390 → 844×240: the floor (204.5 px) fills the 240 px viewport until t > 0.85, so 0 px of plate per finger px, and the release settles at 1. 8 of 8.
- **Disposition**: FOLD into W-DETENT-FLOOR (the span and the floor both read the live viewport the paint reads). **Wave shape**: span re-read per move from the visual viewport, or `t` written from the finger's distance to the anchored edge.

### R10-01-07: the overshoot gap on all four sides; the detented arm is immune (F-74, EXTENDS, MED)

- **Mechanism**: unchanged (`SheetContent.vue:151-158`, `motion.ts:17-28`: an unclamped `p` → `p·100%` translate on a sheet with no bleed). The registry names `right: 0` or `bottom: 0`; `top` and `left` carry the same `-p·100%` arms.
- **Measured** (`res-over-chromium.json`, `res-over-firefox.json`; open then close, gap = distance from the anchored edge to the viewport edge): plain Sheet, maximum gap 12.29–12.32 px on every side at 390×844, 13.74 px (top, bottom) and 16.16 px (left, right) at 844×390; 32–34 of ~250 frames over 1 px in Chromium, 4–17 of 75–167 in Firefox (its frame rate), peak within 0.6 px of Chromium's. 2 of 2 in each cell. Detented Sheet: 0.00 px on all four sides at both cells, both browsers (the size law has no translate).
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-SHEET-BLEED, scoped to the sliding arm; the probe "anchored-edge gap ≤ 1 px" runs on all four sides at both cells.

### R10-01-08: the non-modal Sheet under a top dock cannot be dragged from its grip (F-32, CONFIRMS, + F-22)

- **Mechanism**: F-32's (`sheet/styles.css:90-91`, the live-behind sheet at `z-dock − 1`, nothing insets it by the dock's extent). At `d=1` the grip's centre is under the dock plate, so the press lands on the dock. R9-02-03 measured the hit test; this is the gesture.
- **Measured** (`p-dock.mjs`, a top dock, `modal=0`): the grip centre (195,35 / 422,43) hit-tests to the dock in 8 of 8 cells. Touch: a 0.5·vh drag from there moves nothing (4 of 4). Mouse: the press dismisses the sheet (4 of 4), F-22's R9-02-04 (dock chrome dismisses the non-modal Sheet). With `modal=1` the same drag reaches 0.5 and settles at 0.36 in 8 of 8. Captures: `dock-nonmodal-chromium-390x844-after-grip-drag.png`, `…-844x390-…`.
- **Disposition**: BUILD under F-32's round-9 born-RED (the grip hit-testable under a top dock at `d=1`); add "and a drag from it moves the sheet".
