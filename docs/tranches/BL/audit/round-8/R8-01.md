## Findings

### R8-01-01 (MED, EXTENDS F-83): a closed ConfiguratorLayer is outside find and copy, a third disclosure that W-FINDABLE's three roots do not reach

- **Mechanism**: ConfiguratorLayer is a disclosure (a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`), but it does not use reka's Collapsible. Its closed state is `grid-template-rows: 0fr` plus `inert` plus `aria-hidden` on the `role="region"` body (`ConfiguratorLayer.vue:161-167`). Inert subtrees are excluded from find, text fragments, selection and the AX tree, so nothing can reveal the closed content, and nothing listens for `beforematch`. F-83's wave names three roots (`Collapsible.vue`, `Accordion.vue`, `AccordionItem.vue`), not this one.
- **Probe**: `probes/reveal.mjs`, output in `reveal-chromium.json` and `reveal-firefox.json`, cell `configurator-closed`. `/containers/configurator`: I closed "Field" and searched for "Diffusion radius" (the Bloom row's description). In the settled state the button reads `aria-expanded="false"` and the region is 0 px tall and `inert`. The needle is in the DOM once. `window.find` returns false. Select-all copy pasted into a contenteditable holds it 0 times, in both text/plain and text/html. The AX snapshot holds it 0 times. The state is unchanged after find, so no reveal ran. Same in both engines, 2 of 2 each. On `/substrates/aurora` a closed layer ("Source", "Palette", "Warp mode", …, `census.json`) is withheld the same way. No text-fragment cell could run, because the layer's default is open (`defaultOpen: true`, `:59`); `configurator-closed-tf-*.png` shows the default-open control.
- **Relation**: EXTENDS F-83. The mechanism is the same (closed is expressed by `inert` rather than by the platform's `hidden="until-found"`), on a disclosure the family's wave does not list.
- **Disposition**: BUILD (a W-FINDABLE row). **Wave shape**: the closed region carries `hidden="until-found"` instead of `inert` + `aria-hidden`, and `beforematch` writes `open` through the layer's model (uncontrolled or `@update:open`). The `0fr` grid reveal stays the motion, and the closed rung is the platform's state. Born-RED: closed, `window.find("Diffusion radius")` returns true, the layer reads `aria-expanded="true"` after the match, and the needle is inside the viewport.

### R8-01-04 (MED, EXTENDS F-42): the deck's global key handler reads no modifier, so text-selection keys and the browser's own chords page the presentation

- **Mechanism**: `handleDeckKey` switches on `e.key` alone (`deck/composables/useDeckKeyboard.ts:60-98`). Its only guard is the focused-control test (`:67`), and `useDeckKeyboard` binds it to `window` (`:119-122`). Outside a control, Shift+Arrow (extend a selection), Shift+Space (the platform's scroll back), Alt/Option+Arrow (move by word), Cmd/Ctrl+Arrow, Cmd/Ctrl+digit and Shift+End all reach the paging switch, which calls `preventDefault` (`:96`). The only modifier reads in `src` are in the shortcut registry (`composables/keyboard/useKeyboardShortcuts.ts:148-156`). The deck, the dock run, the selection group, PagerDots and Timeline read none (`grep -rn 'shiftKey\|metaKey\|ctrlKey\|altKey' src`).
- **Probe**: `probes/deckmods.mjs`, output in `deck-modifiers.json`. On `/motion/deck` I double-clicked a word in slide 1's body, so "advances" was selected, then pressed each chord. Shift+ArrowRight, Shift+Space, Alt+ArrowRight, Cmd+ArrowRight and Cmd+2 each page to slide 2, and Shift+End jumps to slide 6. In Chromium the selection is dropped every time. In Firefox it is sometimes kept, now inside a slide that is inert. Both engines, 2 of 2 per chord (24 cells). With Firefox caret browsing on (`accessibility.browsewithcaret`, `probes/caret.mjs`, output in `caret-deck-firefox-2.json`), ArrowRight with the caret in slide text pages the deck, and the caret is left at x −814 inside the now-inert slide 1 (2 of 2). The unmodified arrow is the presentation register by design. Its effect on a caret-browsing reader is recorded here but not counted as a member.
- **Relation**: EXTENDS F-42. The round-6 correction states the class: "a key handler acts on `e.key` without reading the event's own state" (`event.repeat`, IME). Modifier state is the third leg of the same predicate. The dispatch seat here is the one global binding in `src` that stands where the document's text keys land.
- **Disposition**: BUILD (a row in F-42's one keystroke predicate). **Wave shape**: `handleDeckKey` returns false when `e.altKey || e.ctrlKey || e.metaKey`, and for Shift with any key except Space (Shift+Space pages back, the platform's meaning). The predicate that already takes `isComposing` and `repeat` takes the modifier set once and is shared with the element-scoped handlers. Born-RED: with a word selected in a slide, Shift+ArrowRight extends the selection and `active` stays 0, and Cmd+2 leaves `active` at 0 with `defaultPrevented` false (HEAD: pages, 24 of 24).

### R8-01-05 (MED, EXTENDS F-41): the deck and its documented pager each own the step announcement, so every step is announced twice

- **Mechanism**: `DeckStage.vue:91-93` always renders a polite live region with `deck.liveMessage`. `PagerDots.vue:412-418` renders its own `role="status"` polite region from the same index, and `announce` defaults to true (`:79`). The deck story composes the two exactly as the deck README describes. Its header claims "the rail owns the announcement, so nothing here re-authors a live region" (`demo/stories/motion/deck.vue:14-15`), while the README says the stage "hosts the `aria-live` region" (`deck/README.md:60-61`). Two components each claim the one channel, and neither defers to the other.
- **Probe**: `probes/misc.mjs` and `probes/live.mjs`, output in `misc.json` and `live-regions.json`. On `/motion/deck` there are two exposed polite regions, `deck-stage` and `pager-dots`, both reading "Slide 1 of 6: Welcome". After two Next clicks both read "Slide 3 of 6: Focus-guarded". Both are in the AX snapshot (2 hits), and select-all copy carries the phrase twice (`reveal-*.json`, `slideAnn: 2`). Both engines, 2 of 2. Control: on `/navigation/carousel` the two "Slide 1 of 5" regions belong to two different carousels, so that page is not a duplicate.
- **Relation**: EXTENDS F-41. The family's announce-channel leg is one event, two exposed announcers, because two components each render the event: R4-01-19's two Toasters and R6-01-13's two app roots. The deck is the same mechanism outside the toast store. Weighed against F-84: these are two announcements, not a presentation copy, so their cure is ownership, not the text register. Weighed against F-43: F-43 owns an arrival's motion and scroll, not its announcement.
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: W-TOAST's rule of one announce channel per event becomes the library's rule. The deck substrate announces once: the stage's region is the owner, and a PagerDots inside a `DeckStage` context defaults `announce` to false, or the reverse, stated in one place. The story's header and the README then agree. Born-RED: on `/motion/deck`, one exposed live region holds "Slide N of 6" after each step (HEAD: 2).

## Gaps

- **Headed Ctrl+F with the real find bar was not driven.** The find bar is browser UI. Playwright's synthesised keys reach the page, not the browser's accelerators, and CDP has no `Page.searchInPage`. Driving it would mean OS-level keystrokes into a desktop window, which the fence rules out. `window.find` and text fragments run the find matcher, and text fragments fire `beforematch` (R7-01's control). Neither shows find's own reveal of `hidden="until-found"`, because the library renders none.
- **Reader mode**: not automatable in either engine, and no Readability module is in `node_modules`. Not driven.
- **Chromium caret browsing** (F7) is a browser setting that Playwright cannot toggle, so only Firefox's `accessibility.browsewithcaret` pref ran. In it, one observation is recorded and not filed: two ArrowDowns from slide text put the caret inside the demo shell's inert `dock-layer--summary` at a 0×0 rect, where later presses do not move it (2 of 2). Whether an inert subtree should take a caret at all is engine behaviour, so it is not placed.
- **AX**: Playwright's `ariaSnapshot` ran in both engines, and CDP's full AX tree in Chromium only. No screen reader was driven.
- **No real Safari**, and no Playwright WebKit cell was run.
- **Text-fragment cells for closed configurator layers** could not run, because the layer is open at load by default and a fragment applies only at load.
