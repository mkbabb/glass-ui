## Findings

### R8-02-01 (HIGH, EXTENDS F-65)—the library's own root scope splits the dark arm on a dark OS

- **Mechanism**: `:root { color-scheme: light dark }` (`styles/tokens/scheme-motion.css:29`) lets the UA's used scheme follow `prefers-color-scheme`. The 70 `light-dark()` tokens (`tokens/light-dark.css`) follow it. The class-keyed colours do not: `--on-glass-muted` and `--input-on-glass` are light at `tokens/on-glass-fg.css:35-37` and dark only under `.dark` (`tokens/dark-arm.css:301-303`). A consumer that has not installed the `.dark` controller (no `useGlobalDark`, no sync script) paints a split on any dark-OS machine: `light-dark()` ink on the dark arm over class-keyed plates on the light arm. This is R2-08-12's mechanism (two dark arms, a scope only one reaches), but the scope is the library's default, not a consumer's scoped `color-scheme`.
- **Probe**: `probes/p-split.mjs`, OS dark and no class, against OS dark with `useGlobalDark` ("gd-dark"), 2 runs × 2 sessions.
- **Evidence**: `captures/R8-02/split-osdark-none-r1.png`, `-r2.png`, `split.json`. Input: computed `color` rgb(233,230,226) on a `background-color` of rgb(243,236,226). The typed value paints at **1.07:1** (the class-keyed cell: 9.49), and the caret inherits the same ink. The card description paints at **2.05** (12.23). Inactive SegmentedTabs labels resolve `oklab(0.4676 …)`, the light-arm muted ink on a dark track. The OS-light cell with no class is correct (15.08 / 6.83).
- **Disposition hint**: FOLD into W-DARK-ONE-ARM. This is the family's broadest paint consequence: every consumer without the controller is affected, so the family's maximum rises from MED to HIGH.
- **Wave shape**: every colour through `light-dark()`, and non-colour dark values through an inherited custom property keyed off the used scheme, as the round-6 correction states. Alternatively the root states one scheme until the controller runs. Born-RED: no class, OS dark: the Input's value is at least 4.5:1 on its well (HEAD 1.07) and the card description at least 4.5 (HEAD 2.05).

### R8-02-02 (MED, EXTENDS F-65)—Chrome's Auto Dark Mode double-darkens the half that already flipped

- **Mechanism**: under Auto Dark Mode for web contents (`Emulation.setAutoDarkModeOverride`), Chrome resolves `light-dark()` to the dark arm even with the root's inline `color-scheme: light` from `useGlobalDark` (`composables/dark/useGlobalDark.ts:171`), then applies its darkening filter, which assumes an undarkened light page. The class-keyed half stays light and is darkened once. The `light-dark()` half is darkened twice: its already-dark ink is inverted over its already-flipped ground. The two arms of R8-02-01 meet a third actor.
- **Probe**: `probes/v-autodark.mjs` (consumer, both themes, with and without the override, ×2) and `probes/v-demo-ro.mjs` (`/forms/inputs` in the demo, ×2).
- **Evidence**: consumer, light theme: the Badge computes lilac `oklch(0.739 0.134 318.1)` with dark ink (its dark-arm pair) and paints white on lilac at **2.42:1**. It is 16.58 without the override and 7.16 in dark (`v-autodark.json`, `v-autodark-light-ad-r1.png`). The card's computed `color` moves to rgb(233,230,226) while its veil stays the light-arm `color(srgb 0.204 0.148 0.083 / 0.14)`. Demo `/forms/inputs`: the Read only field falls from **15.23 to 1.41** and the Disabled field from 2.83 to 1.55 (`v-demo-readonly.json`, `v-demo-ro-autodark-r1.png`; screenshot `v-demo-autodark-light-forms-inputs.png`). In the dark theme the override is a no-op (identical numbers), because Chrome skips a used-dark page. The command-line `--enable-features=WebContentsForceDark` did nothing in this headless build (`forcedark-flag-*.png` equal the base), so the CDP override is the measured cell.
- **Disposition hint**: FOLD into W-DARK-ONE-ARM as the forced-dark cell. One arm alone does not cure it: the Badge's pair is already one arm and still fails.
- **Wave shape**: the page declares its stance toward UA forced dark. Either it serves its own dark arm whole when the UA forces dark, so the filter skips a used-dark page, or it pins `only light` on an explicit light pick. Born-RED under the override on `/forms/inputs` and the consumer battery: Read only at least 4.5 (HEAD 1.41) and Badge at least 4.5 (HEAD 2.42).

### R8-02-03 (MED, EXTENDS F-22)—the dock's `pointer-events: auto` re-opens the page under a modal layer, to the pointer and to spatial navigation

- **Mechanism**: a reka modal layer (Select, DropdownMenu) blocks the outside with an inherited `body { pointer-events: none }` plus `aria-hidden` on the siblings, not `inert`. The dock re-declares `pointer-events: auto` on its interactive run: `.dock-plate` at `components/dock/styles/dock.css:66`, `.dock-controls` at `dock.css:190`, the active layer at `layers.css:149`, and `crossfade.css:51`. Under an open layer its controls stay hit-testable while `aria-hidden`. Chrome's spatial navigation only takes hit-testable candidates, so the same rule also lets arrow keys walk out of the open listbox. This is R6-01-11's seam in the other direction: a foreign top layer inherits the `none`, while the library's own chrome overrides it. The layer contract has no outside-interaction leg.
- **Probe**: `probes/p-dockpe.mjs`, `p-escape.mjs`, `p-dockmodal3.mjs`, `v-spatpe.mjs`, `v-dkchain.mjs`.
- **Evidence**: demo `/dock/overview` with the dock's "Dock view" Select open: "Next story" computes `pointer-events: auto` under `body: none`, sits inside `[aria-hidden=true]`, and a click on it changes the route to `/dock/layers` (2 of 2, `dock-pe-under-select.json`). With `--enable-spatial-navigation`, ArrowRight from the open option moves focus to "Next story" (`inHidden: true`), and Enter changes the route (2 of 2; without spatial navigation focus stays on the option, 2 of 2) (`escape-dock-select-ArrowRight.json`, `escape-dock-select-ArrowRight-spat1.png`). Consumer: the `#persistent` DockControl (chain `.dock-persistent: auto` ← `.dock-controls: auto` ← `.glass-dock: none`, `v-dock-pe-chain.json`) fires its handler on a click under an open Select (2 of 2) and an open Menu (2 of 2). The Dialog's scrim blocks it (0 of 2) (`dock-under-modal-consumer-persist.json`). Isolation: a plain page button with inherited `none` is never reached by spatial navigation (2 of 2). The same button with `pointer-events: auto` is reached from the open listbox while `aria-hidden`, and Enter fires it (2 of 2, `v-spatnav-pe.json`).
- **Disposition hint**: BUILD, in F-22's layer-contract wave.
- **Wave shape**: the seam gains its fourth leg, the outside. While a modal layer is open, the outside is `inert`, or the dock's `auto` rules are scoped off a modal-open signal the seam sets. Born-RED: with a Select or Menu open, a click on a DockControl fires nothing, and spatial navigation from the listbox does not leave it (HEAD: the handler fires, focus reaches "Next story", and Enter navigates).

### R8-02-04 (LOW, EXTENDS F-23)—ink is not calibrated to the UA's own selection ground

- **Mechanism**: `src` has 0 `::selection` rules (grep, re-checked), so selected text paints the UA's highlight under ink derived for the plate. The selection composite is a painted ground no ink rung was derived against: F-23's mechanism on a ground the UA supplies.
- **Probe**: `probes/p-sel.mjs` (select-all across the consumer battery, both themes, plain and field grounds, ×2); `probes/selcon.py`.
- **Evidence**: `sel-contrast.json`, `sel-dark-f0-r1.png`, `sel-light-f0-r1.png`. On the plain ground, page muted text falls from 5.38 to **3.60** in light and from 7.31 to **3.14** in dark. In dark the Badge falls from 7.16 to **3.32** and the card description from 12.23 to 5.25. In light the Badge falls from 16.58 to 4.25.
- **Disposition hint**: FOLD into W-INK-COMPOSITE.
- **Wave shape**: an owned selection register (a `::selection` background and ink token per theme, derived against the plates), and the rendered-contrast invariant samples one selected muted run per theme. Born-RED: selected muted ink at least 4.5 in both themes (HEAD 3.60 / 3.14).

### R8-02-05 (LOW, CONFIRMS F-70)—a consumer following the dock README mounts a collapsed dock

- **Mechanism**: the struck props `always-expanded` and `start-collapsed` (`components/dock/README.md:43-44`; `composables/useDockShellProps.ts:5-8` folded them into `collapse`) fall through silently. The dock mounts collapsed, showing only `#persistent`, and its default layer is `visibility: hidden`. This reproduces L05b-11 and R2-02-17 in a fresh consumer. It is filed only because this seat's harness hit it by following the README.
- **Evidence**: `v-dk-rest.png`, `probes/v-dkrest.mjs` (Home: `pointer-events: none`, `visibility: hidden`, opacity 0).
- **Disposition hint**: FOLD (W-DOC-TRUTH and the F-70 typed-fallthrough wave). No new wave.

## Cells driven with no defect filed

Each cell below ran in both themes, twice.

- **User stylesheet past 1.4.12** (`* { font-family: "Courier New" !important; line-height: 2; word-spacing: 0.5em; letter-spacing: 0.12em }`): no text leaves its box across 20 probes. Buttons hold 40 px over a 29.6 px line, and the Badge grows from 22 to 34 px (`cell-usercss-f0.json`).
- **User default font size 24** (`Page.setFontSizes`, not the minimum): the consumer battery scales 1.5× through `rem` with no spill, and 10 demo routes add 0 spills over the 16 px baseline (`cell-deffont24-f0.json`, `deffont-demo.json`). The minimum-size clause stays with R7-01-11 in F-29.
- **`prefers-contrast: less`, `prefers-reduced-data`**: `src` has no arm for either (0 hits), and the paint is byte-identical to the base. Not filed: no normative requirement, and no shipping engine exposes reduced-data.
- **Image blocking**: the library ships no raster images. The paper grain is a `data:` SVG (`styles/paper.css:97`), and the consumer battery is unchanged. The Avatar's image-to-fallback path was not reached (gap).
- **`accent-color`**: native controls follow `--accent-color` (`tokens/light-dark.css:157`, re-declared under `.dark`) in both themes, with no loss.
- **Caret**: it inherits the ink and is lost only in R8-02-01's split cell.
- **Spatial navigation on SegmentedTabs**: the strip's keydown listener makes the group container a stop (`role=group`, platform ring). ArrowRight then enters the tabs, and selection follows (2 of 2, both themes, `v-spatnav-tabs.json`). Not filed: an extra stop, and the missing ring is already F-39's.

## Gaps

- Real Safari: not automatable (owner-blocked). No WebKit cell ran in this seat, so no Safari or WebKit cell is claimed.
- OS text scaling and `text-size-adjust`: not drivable from CDP. The consumer carries Tailwind preflight's `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%`, and `src` has 0 hits.
- Chrome's real "Auto Dark Mode for Web Contents" flag and Android's "Darken websites" were not measured on hardware. The CDP override is the measured cell, and the headless `--enable-features` flag had no effect.
- The Avatar fallback under image blocking, and auto dark across more than one demo route.
