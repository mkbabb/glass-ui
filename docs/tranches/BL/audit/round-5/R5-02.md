### R5-02-01 (HIGH, EXTENDS F-37): in a consumer, a pointer cannot pick a CommandDialog item, and clicking one closes the palette

**Mechanism.** `CommandList` renders reka's `ComboboxContent` (`src/components/command/CommandList.vue:34-48`), which is a `DismissableLayer`. Inside `CommandDialog`, that layer enters reka's layer set below the Dialog content's modal layer. reka then styles every layer below the highest pointer-disabling layer with `pointer-events: none` (`node_modules/reka-ui/dist/DismissableLayer/DismissableLayer.js:56-61` computes the index; `:122` writes the style). The list and its items stop hit-testing, and a press on an item lands on the `.command` wrapper. That wrapper is inside the dialog but outside the list's layer. The list layer reads the press as a pointer-down outside and dismisses. `CommandDialog` forwards the Command's `update:open` to the Dialog (`CommandDialog.vue:97-100`), so the palette closes.

This is F-37's mechanism, reka's Combobox content mounted with all of its popper-and-modal side effects (the round 4 correction). R4-01-12 is the keyboard edge, where Escape is swallowed. This row is the pointer edge.

**Evidence.**
- One-palette consumer (`?only=cmd`: one Button, one CommandDialog). Vite, webpack, rspack and esbuild, 2 runs each at load 8.2-9.9 (`cmdonly.json`, `cmdonly-*-open.png`). All eight runs gave the same result:
  - the list carries an inline `pointer-events: none`, and the Dialog `auto`;
  - the item's computed `pointer-events` is `none`, and `elementFromPoint` at the item's centre is `.command`;
  - a mouse click on "README.md" leaves `open` false and `picked` null;
  - ArrowDown then Enter picks "design".
- The full battery in all three mount modes (`bind-csr.json`) and in webpack, rspack and esbuild (`bind-bundlers.json`, plus an esbuild re-run): `page.click` on the item times out, because it is not hit-testable.
- The built demo's own palette does **not** reproduce it (`demo-palette.json`, 2 of 2 at 1280 and 2 of 2 on the phone). There the list's inline `pointer-events` is `auto`, and a click picks `file:readme`. The demo page also mounts an inline `Command`, whose layer changes the registration order. So only a consumer, not a demo route, shows the defect.

**Disposition.** BUILD, in W-COMMAND.

**Wave shape.** W-COMMAND's non-modal listbox root removes the list's DismissableLayer, and with it both edges. Born-RED, run in the consumer fixture and not in the demo: a page with one Button and one CommandDialog; a mouse click on the first item gives `picked` = that item's value and exactly one `update:open(false)`.

### R5-02-02 (HIGH, EXTENDS F-67): SSR hydration diverges on coarse-pointer and reduced-motion clients, and Vue keeps the server's attributes

**Mechanism.** Two setup-time reads of the client environment fork the render. Both are `false` on the server.
- `Popover.vue:81-85` reads `matchMedia("(pointer: coarse)")` in setup and picks `RekaHoverCardRoot` or the Popover root from it (`:98-114`).
- `useMotionAxis` resolves motion with `readReducedMotion()` in setup (`src/components/_shared/useMotionAxis.ts:51-53,95`). That value drives `:data-motion` (`Slider.vue:272`, `SegmentedTabs.vue:252`) and SegmentedTabs' `dragEnabled` (`SegmentedTabs.vue:168`), which adds the `glass-drag-grabbable` class (`:285`).

Vue's hydration reports class and attribute mismatches but never patches them: "this mismatch is check-only. The DOM will not be rectified in production". The client-only Popover root also calls `useId` once more, which shifts every id rendered after it.

**Evidence.** `hydrate-matrix_touchPopover_1*.json` (hydrated DOM against a fresh CSR mount, cell by cell), `touch-hydrate-ssr-dev.json`, `idshift.json`, `prm-tabs.json`, `nuxt.json`.

- **Phone.** Vite SSR prod and dev, all three mount modes, and Nuxt 4:
  - The hover Popover's trigger hydrates with no `id`, no `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and no `aria-expanded="false"`. The CSR mount has all three. AX at rest: `button "Open popover"` with no hasPopup or expanded.
  - The DropdownMenu trigger keeps the server's `id="reka-dropdown-menu-trigger-v-8"`, while the client's content points `aria-labelledby` at `…-v-10`. The opened menu is `menu ""` in AX. The CSR mount gives `menu "Open menu"`, and so does the SSR page at desk.
- **PRM.** Desk and phone, Vite and Nuxt:
  - The SegmentedTabs indicator keeps `glass-drag-grabbable`. It computes `cursor: grab` and `touch-action: none`, and the active tab computes `pointer-events: none` (`tabs/styles/drag.css:22-25,92-95`). A tap at the active pill's centre hits the indicator, which has no drag handler under PRM.
  - The CSR mount under PRM gives `auto`/`auto`, and the tap hits the tab.
  - The Slider's `data-motion="reduced"` is missing too. That is inert today: only `.glass-reveal[data-motion]` reads it (`src/styles/glass/reveal.css:283-301`).
- **Clean cells.** Desk with default preferences, and desk dark: 0 mismatches in every mode. A GlassDock hydrates clean; only the SegmentedTabs inside it diverges.
- **Proof it is the environment read.** `useTabResponsive` binds `matchMedia` in `onMounted` behind an SSR default (`tabs/composables/useTabResponsive.ts:93-115`) and does not diverge.

**Disposition.** BUILD, in W-PEER-TRUTH's SSR leg.

**Wave shape.**
- Reads that change the render move behind mount, the `useTabResponsive` pattern, so the server and the first client pass render from one default.
- The consumer matrix's SSR leg hydrates rather than only smoke-rendering: {fine, coarse} × {no-preference, reduce}.
- Born-RED: 0 hydration mismatches (HEAD: 3 on the phone, 1-2 under PRM), and every `aria-labelledby` and `aria-controls` in the hydrated battery resolves to an element (HEAD: the menu's does not).

**Placement note.** F-67 has carried SSR since R2-09-08 ("a capability the source advertises and the artefact fails"). The source advertises SSR-safety here (`useReducedMotion.ts:50`, "SSR-safe"; `useTabResponsive` "SSR-default"), and W-PEER-TRUTH's matrix carries the check. If the registrar keeps F-67 scoped to packaging claims, this row, a render forked by a client-only read at setup, is the one mint candidate in this report.

### R5-02-03 (MED, EXTENDS F-67): DarkModeToggle under SSR fails differently by host

**Mechanism.** `useGlobalDark` is a `createGlobalState` singleton (`src/composables/dark/useGlobalDark.ts:79`). Its immediate watcher writes `document.documentElement.style` (`:168-174`), which is R2-09-08.
- With no app error handler (bare Vite SSR), the throw escapes the factory, the singleton never initialises, and every render throws.
- Under Nuxt, the throw goes to Nuxt's error handler, so the request 500s but the factory completes. The singleton then lives for the process, shared by every later request.

**Evidence.** On `:5452`:
- Vite SSR `/ssr/?dark=1`: 500, then 500 again. The body reads `ReferenceError: document is not defined at …/dist/dark-pONY3x93.js:34`.
- Nuxt, freshly restarted: `req1 500`, `req2 200`, `req3 200`. `nuxt.json` shows the 200 page hydrating with the toggle.

**Disposition.** BUILD (small); it folds into R2-09-08's row.

**Wave shape.** The watcher moves to the client. The SSR smoke invariant renders on a **cold** process: under Nuxt, a warm process passes after the first failure, so a warm smoke test misses it.

### R5-02-04 (MED, EXTENDS F-70): CommandDialog emits `update:open` 2-3 times per close

**Mechanism.** `CommandDialog` forwards `update:open` from both the Dialog (`CommandDialog.vue:67`) and the inner Command (`:99`), and one close runs both paths. This is F-70's forwarded channel with no runtime witness. Here the channel fires more than once rather than not at all.

**Evidence.** `cmdonly.json`, 4 bundlers × 2 runs, counting a consumer `@update:open` beside `v-model:open`:
- Escape: 2 emits on first open, 3 on reopen;
- keyboard pick: 2;
- pointer press: 2.

Dialog, Popover, DropdownMenu, Tooltip and Collapsible each emit once per close (`bind-csr.json`). A consumer toggling state on the event (`@update:open="open = !open"`) ends in the wrong state.

**Disposition.** BUILD.

**Wave shape.** One owner for the forwarded channel. The runtime witness list adds "exactly one `update:open` per logical close".

### R5-02-05 (MED, EXTENDS F-70): seven overlay roots drop consumer attributes with no dev warning, and Toaster's source says they land

**Mechanism.** Two paths:
- `inheritAttrs: false` with no `$attrs` rebinding: `CommandDialog.vue:8`, `Select.vue:26`, `DropdownMenu.vue:50`, `Tooltip.vue:18` (also `TooltipProvider`, `DropdownMenuSub`).
- A renderless reka root: Dialog, Popover, and Toaster, whose root is `ToastProvider`.

`inheritAttrs: false` also switches off Vue's extraneous-attrs warning, so a dev build is silent. `Toaster.vue:141-142` says "The `$attrs` fall through to reka's Primitive, landing on the rendered region element". They do not. Toaster declares only `position` (`:19`), so the region's name cannot be set at all.

**Evidence.** `mount-csr-dev+ssr-dev.json`, dev and prod builds, all three modes: `data-r5` and `class` are absent from the DOM for Select, Dialog, Popover, DropdownMenu, Tooltip, CommandDialog and Toaster, with 0 Vue warnings.
- CommandDialog's `aria-label="Palette dialog"` is gone; AX shows `dialog "Command palette"`, named from its title.
- The Toaster region keeps reka's English default, `region "Notifications (F8)"`.

Every other root lands its attributes, including Collapsible, Accordion, Label and each Content part.

**Disposition.** BUILD.

**Wave shape.** On a compound root, attributes land on the element that carries the role (CommandDialog to DialogContent, Toaster to the viewport), or the root warns in dev. Gate: the strictTemplates consumer fixture adds an attribute-landing assertion per exported root.

### R5-02-06 (MED, EXTENDS F-42): `<Label for>` plus `<Slider id>` names nothing, and the dev guard for it is compiled out

**Mechanism.** Slider forwards `id` to the `SliderRoot` span, which is not labelable. The focusable is the thumb. The label idiom that names Checkbox, Switch, RadioGroupItem, Input and SelectTrigger in the same battery names nothing here.

The guard written for exactly this case (`Slider.vue:236-255`, "no accessible name") is gated on `import.meta.env.DEV`. The library build resolves that gate, so dist has no such string (R2-07-07, F-67).

**Evidence.** `axtree.json` gives `slider "" val=40`. The same `<Label for>` gives `checkbox "Accept"`, `switch "Wifi"`, `radio "Small"`, `textbox "Name"` and `combobox "Fruit"`. The DOM root carries `id="r5-slider"`, and the thumb has no `aria-labelledby`. The CSR dev consumer build, with dev Vue, logs 0 warnings.

**Disposition.** BUILD (small).

**Wave shape.** Slider forwards `id`, or the Label's `aria-labelledby`, to the thumb. The guard moves to `process.env.NODE_ENV` under F-67's dev-guard row. Born-RED: the Label-for Slider's thumb has a non-empty AX name.

### R5-02-10 (MED, EXTENDS F-41): one polite announcement per toast in Chromium, and no path to an assertive one

**Mechanism.** reka's `ToastRootImpl` renders a `ToastAnnounce` with `role="alert"` (`reka-ui/dist/Toast/ToastRootImpl.js:130-133`). `ToastAnnounce` wraps it in `VisuallyHidden feature="fully-hidden"` (`ToastAnnounce.js:27-30`), which stamps `aria-hidden="true"`. The only exposed live region is Toaster's `aria-live="polite"` viewport (`Toaster.vue:145-148`). `ToastOptions` (`toast/use-toast.ts`) holds title, description, action, tone, surface and duration, with no urgency, so an error-tone toast is announced politely.

**Evidence.**
- `axtree.json`, toast state: AX exposes `list "" live="polite" relevant="additions text"` and one `listitem ""`. The alert copy is `SPAN[aria-hidden=true][role=alert]` (probe `dbg2.mjs`) and absent from the AX tree.
- The FCD tree is identical.

**Narrowing.** L08-11 and R3-02-17 ("announced twice") hold for the DOM but not for Chromium's tree, where the second copy is hidden. VoiceOver is unverified from this seat.

**Disposition.** BUILD, in W-TOAST.

**Wave shape.** One announce channel owned by the library: polite by default, assertive for an error tone. The toast item is named from its title.

## Clean results

Recorded so that no later seat re-derives them.

- **Mount modes are equivalent.** A template, a hand-written `h()` and `<component :is>` build the same library DOM. CSR template against `:is` is byte-identical after normalisation; `h()` differs only in this harness's own empty `class=""` on a section. All 19 outside writes and 26 gestures behave the same in all three modes (`bind-csr.json`, `mount-*.json`). No binding is a no-op in one mode and live in another.
- **Bundlers are equivalent.** Paint was compared by the computed style of every battery element, at rest and with Dialog, Popover, DropdownMenu, Tooltip and Select open, light and dark (`css-delivery.json`, `css-*.png`):

  | bundler | against Vite |
  |---|---|
  | esbuild (+ Tailwind CLI) | 0 differences |
  | webpack | colour serialisation only, ≤ 0.001 in L (the minifier's rounding) |
  | rspack | colour serialisation only, ≤ 0.001 in L (the minifier's rounding) |

  None differs in layout, radius, blur, opacity, transform or display. The binding census is identical across the four (`bind-bundlers.json`). Per-subpath CSS still arrives only through the stylesheet, as R2-07-06 found; that holds in every bundler.
- **Hydration is clean at default preferences.** At desk, with default preferences and in dark: 0 mismatches in all three modes and in Nuxt. Toaster's teleport hydrates.
- **Forced colours do not change the AX tree.** The FCD tree matches the unforced tree node for node in 9 states (rest, the Dialog, the Sheet, the Popover, the menu, the Tooltip, the palette, the Select, a toast; 55-63 nodes each). Every state change flips its AX property: checked, pressed, expanded, value and mixed. In FCD every overlay keeps an edge at ≥ 3:1 against Canvas (299-1,426 px).
- **A dev consumer build logs no Vue warnings.** That is also why R5-02-05 and R5-02-06 give no dev-time signal.

## Not reproduced

- **Stuck Sheet, esbuild.** In the first esbuild battery run (load 13.1-13.7), the Sheet stayed mounted after an outside `open = false` write. The page was then unclickable for the rest of the run. It did not recur:
  - the esbuild re-run was clean (load 17.5);
  - a dedicated sheet-close probe, 4 runs across esbuild, Vite and webpack at load 15.9-18.5, unmounted the Sheet within 750 ms every time (`dbg5.mjs`).

  Verdict: NOT-REPRODUCED.

## Method notes

- **State separation.** A pixel diff of the control's box plus 8 px, rest against state. `changed` counts pixels whose largest channel moved by more than 10. `c3` counts pixels whose contrast against the rest pixel is at least 3:1. `perim2` is 2 px times the box perimeter. This is R3-02's method, so the rows are comparable.
- **Hydration.** The hydrated DOM is read after mount and compared attribute by attribute with a fresh CSR mount in the same client cell. Style whitespace, empty `style`, and `value=""` on inputs are normalised. The one remaining serialisation difference (CSR writes `required="false"` on radio buttons, SSR omits it) is harmless and not filed.
- **Mismatch messages.** The prod SSR client was built with `__VUE_PROD_HYDRATION_MISMATCH_DETAILS__`, so prod mismatches are named; the dev build confirms them.
