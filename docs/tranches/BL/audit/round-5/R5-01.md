## 1. How each transition was driven

| transition | how | what the engine does here |
|---|---|---|
| hide and show | bfcache navigation away and back. | network | CDP `emulateNetworkConditions`: slow-3G (2,000 ms RTT, 50 KB/s), fast-3G (562 ms, 180 KB/s), broadband (40 ms, 10 Mbit/s), offline, across a route push to an uncached chunk | |

| long session | two sessions in parallel, **30.05 and 30.08 minutes of real time**, 565 and 566 steps. ## 3. Findings

### R5-01-01 (HIGH, EXTENDS F-78): a held drag outlives a page hide

- **Mechanism**: three drag machines end only on `pointerup` or `pointercancel`. None subscribes to `lostpointercapture`, window `blur`, `visibilitychange` or `pagehide`, and none reads `buttons` on a move (grep: 0 hits in the three files). When the page hides mid-drag (a tab switch, Back, a system sheet), the release happens while it is hidden. The restored page receives `lostpointercapture` and then moves with `buttons: 0`, and never a `pointerup` or `pointercancel` (pointer log, 3 of 3 runs). So the machine stays in its held state. This is F-78's interruption with no transition of its own, reached through a lifecycle edge that no machine listens to.
- **Evidence**:
  - EasingPicker (`src/components/easing/EasingPicker.vue:228-245`, bound at `:452-455`; `onMove` tests only `dragIndex`). After restore the handle follows the released pointer across six moves with no button held, from (1152.2, 387.6) to (1224.2, 423.6). The next ordinary click, 120 px below, writes the curve to the click point (1132.2, 525.7). Control (a normal release): the handle stays at (1152.2, 387.6).
  - SortableList (`src/components/sortable-list/drag.ts:503-523` binds document `pointermove`/`pointerup`/`pointercancel`; `:549` checks `pointerId` only; `:574-582`). The ghost plate stays lifted and glued to the pointer, from (185, 250) to (257, 286). The next click anywhere drops it and reorders the list: "Draft spec" moves from first to third (3 of 3). Control: the ghost flies home and the order is unchanged. The list already has a `cancel()` path bound to `pointercancel`; the lifecycle edge never reaches it.
  - Tabs drag morph (`src/composables/motion/morph/useDragMorph.ts:335-364`; the keyframes.js `Draggable` binds its move, up and cancel on the element under capture, `node_modules/@mkbabb/keyframes.js/dist/keyframes.js:562,606`). The indicator strands at x = 410.2 on "Timeline" while the model and the caption stay "Grid" (x = 155), 255 px apart. A later click elsewhere does not recover it (3 of 3). Control: it snaps to List (235.5).
  - The library's right shape: `useDockHold` releases on window `blur` (`src/components/dock/composables/useDockHold.ts:126-137`), and reka's Slider ends on `lostpointercapture`.
  - Captures: `drag-bfcache.json`, `drag-bfcache-r2.json`, `drag-bfcache-r3.json` (loads 30.2-35.1), `drag-{easing,sortable,tabs}-lost-release-bfcache-stuck.png`, controls `drag-*-control*.png`; probe `probes/drag-bfcache.mjs`.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-INTERRUPT.
- **Wave shape**: every held gesture ends on its lifecycle edges: `lostpointercapture` on the capturing element, window `blur`, `visibilitychange` to hidden, `pagehide`, and a move with `buttons === 0`. The end runs the cancel transition (spring back to the origin; never commit). One shared helper next to `useDockHold`'s release. Born-RED: a bfcache round trip mid-drag, six moves with no button held and one click elsewhere leave the handle, ghost and indicator at the origin and the order unchanged. HEAD: the handle is carried 72 px right and 36 px down, the ghost stays glued and the click reorders the list, and the indicator rests 255 px off its tab.

### R5-01-02 (HIGH, EXTENDS F-43): a route push waits on the network inside the frozen view-transition update

- **Mechanism**: `routeTransition` calls `startViewTransition(update)` with `update` = `await mutate()` (`src/composables/motion/route/useRouteTransition.ts:120-142`). Its documented `mutate` is `await router.push(to)` (`:46`, `:94`), and the demo does the same (`demo/chassis/routeTransition.ts:105-106`). The demo's `router.beforeResolve` loads the lazy chunk inside that push (`demo/router.ts:111-121`), although its comment says it runs "before the View Transition update callback" (`:108-110`). The engine suppresses rendering from the old-state capture until `update` settles, so the page draws no frame while the chunk downloads, and after 4 s Chrome skips the transition. The substrate hides both outcomes. `vt.ready?.catch(() => {})` (`src/composables/motion/core/useViewTransition.ts:168`) silences the TimeoutError, and `finished` maps a rejection to success (`:170-173`). So a failed push resolves exactly like a completed one. It is the router seam's arrival: the transition starts before the arrival exists.
- **Evidence** (`probes/net.mjs`):
  - slow-3G: no frame for 4,016-4,017 ms, starting 3-10 ms after the key press (2 of 2, loads 38.4 and 42.8). `ready` rejects with "TimeoutError: Transition was aborted because of timeout in DOM update", and the route lands with no transition at +4.1 s.
  - fast-3G: no frame for 1,206-1,216 ms (2 of 2); the transition survives.
  - broadband (40 ms, 10 Mbit/s): 141-159 ms (2 of 2). Unthrottled: a 65 ms update and no gap over 100 ms.
  - offline: the push fails inside `update` ("Unable to preload CSS …"). The URL does not change, nothing is shown, and `finished` resolves. Once the network is back, the same route fails again at once ("Failed to fetch dynamically imported module"), because the module map keeps the failure until reload. The demo has no `vite:preloadError` handler, so that half is consumer-side.
  - Captures: `net.json`, `net-r2.json`, `net-bb.json` (the unthrottled and broadband runs), `net-b.json` (offline), `net-*-t2s.png`, `net-*-after.png`.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-ARRIVAL (the router seam).
- **Wave shape**: resolve first, then transition. The helper takes an async `prepare` that runs before `startViewTransition` (router resolve plus the matched lazy components) and a synchronous commit inside it. The substrate reports a skipped or failed transition (`finished` resolves `{ ok, reason }`, or rejects) rather than swallowing it, and the demo router's comment is corrected. Born-REDs: an uncached push under slow-3G draws a frame at least every 100 ms while the chunk loads, and its transition runs (HEAD: 4,016 ms, then skipped); an offline push hands the caller a failure (HEAD: resolves).

### R5-01-03 (HIGH, NEW, proposed family "the print medium has no owner"): the deck's print block claims every printed page of every consumer

- **Mechanism**: `src/components/deck/styles/capture.css:112-170` declares, inside `@layer components { @media print { … } }`:
  - `@page { size: 1280px 720px; margin: 0 }`
  - `html, body { overflow: visible !important; margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; print-color-adjust: exact }`
  - `html, body, #app { block-size: auto !important; min-block-size: 0 !important }`

  Nothing gates these rules on a deck being present. `src/styles/index.css:272` imports the deck stylesheet, so the root `@mkbabb/glass-ui/styles` export carries the block (built: `dist/styles/index.css` imports `../components/deck/styles/index.css`), and so does the demo's main CSS (CSSOM path: `index-*.css > @layer components > @media print > @page`). Every page a consumer prints is laid out on 1280×720 px sheets, with zero margin and backgrounds forced.
- **Evidence**:
  - Print media, reader defaults, three non-deck routes (`/forms/slider`, `/containers/dialog`, `/foundations/typography`) in light and dark. As shipped, every PDF is 960×540 pt, with `print-color-adjust: exact` on `body`. With that one rule deleted through CSSOM, the same pages print on Letter with `economy` (6 of 6 pairs). In dark, the shipped pages print full-bleed dark grounds.
  - No other component in `src` writes `@page`, `html`, `body` or `#app` rules (grep).
  - Captures: `print.json` (loads 24.7-41.5), `print-{light,dark}-*-{shipped,deck-print-removed}-p1-1.png`; probes `probes/print.mjs`, `probes/page-size.mjs`.
- **Why NEW**:
  - F-21 is a shared rule overriding a component's own declaration; this is the reverse, a component's rule taking document state.
  - F-52 (payload does not shake) would drop the block for consumers that never import the deck. An app that shows one deck route (the demo is one) still has the block claim every other route, so F-52's wave does not carry the cure.
  - F-56 is file placement, not selector reach.
- **Disposition**: BUILD.
- **Wave shape**: W-PRINT. The deck's page geometry moves to a named page (`@page deck { size: 1280px 720px; margin: 0 }`, with `page: deck` on `.deck-stage` in print capture mode), and its `html`/`body` rules gate on `:root:has(.deck-stage)`. The library states one print arm (R5-01-04). Born-RED: a non-deck route prints on the reader's paper with economy colour (HEAD: 960×540 pt, exact).

### R5-01-04 (MED, NEW, same proposed family; F-23 is the other candidate): the dark theme reaches paper, and the library has no print arm

- **Mechanism**: the only print rules in `src` are the paper grain's hide (`src/styles/paper.css:137,175`) and the deck block. Under print, the theme keeps its screen scheme. The substrate canvases print, because replaced content survives "background graphics off". The engine's economy darkening assumes white paper, so in dark the near-white ink (233, 230, 226) darkens to about (157, 146, 144), which is the colour of the printed field (154, 142, 139).
- **Evidence**:
  - `/forms/slider` in dark on Letter, deck rule removed, printed pixels at 96 dpi: h1 1.05:1, the "Volume" label 1.05:1, the blurb 2.41:1, the back link 2.38:1. The same page in light: 14.28, 14.28, 5.90 and 4.43.
  - Computed under print media: the h1 is `rgb(233, 230, 226)`, and `print-color-adjust` is `economy` on every ancestor (`probes/pca.mjs`, `probes/h1ink.mjs`).
  - Capture: `print-dark-slider-letter-header.png`.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-PRINT.
- **Wave shape**: `@media print` sets `color-scheme: light` and the light ink ramp, hides the substrate canvases (or paints the paper ground in their place) and flattens the glass rungs. Born-RED: in the dark-theme print of three routes, every text run is at least 4.5:1 on printed pixels (HEAD: h1 1.05).
- **Placement note**: if the registry reads this as "ink calibrated to a ground other than the one painted", it folds into F-23; the cure still lands in the print arm.

### R5-01-05 (HIGH, EXTENDS F-35): the brand face never paints on a load, and a restore swaps it in with a reflow

- **Mechanism**: both Plus Jakarta Sans faces are `font-display: optional` (`src/styles/fonts.css:81-86, 97-102`), and nothing preloads them: there is no `rel=preload` in `index.html`, `dist-demo/index.html`, `src/styles` or `README.md`. Without a preload, Chromium keeps the fallback for the life of the page. The calibrated fallback (`src/styles/typography/scale.css:22-50`; here Arial at `size-adjust: 104.9796%`) matches vertical metrics but not advance widths: `ch` resolves 25% narrower and display headings 23% narrower. Two comments claim otherwise: "the swap is no-shift on geometry" (`fonts.css:77-79`) and "metrically neutral (no CLS)" (`scale.css:18-21`).
- **Evidence**:
  - CDP `CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode` on the story text reports Arial on the first load and on reload (3 routes, and 5 of 5 runs of `/containers/popover`, unthrottled and at 150 ms RTT). In the unthrottled runs the woff2 finishes 35-86 ms before FCP (`font-first.json`, `font-used.json`).
  - Control page with the same woff2 (`font-control.json`): `optional` without a preload paints Arial (2 of 2); with a preload it paints Plus Jakarta Sans (2 of 2); `swap` paints Plus Jakarta Sans.
  - After a bfcache restore the demo paints Plus Jakarta Sans; the control page stays on Arial. On `/containers/popover` the h1 goes from 342.3 to 419.8 px, `max-w-prose` (65ch) from 631.7 to 792.3 px, and the header blurb from 2 lines to 1, so everything below it moves up 23-47 px (`ch-restore.json`; `life-aurora-c-0pre.png` against `life-aurora-c-after1500.png`). `probes/shift-find.mjs` names the blurb as the first box that changes.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in F-35's wave (one path, preloaded).
- **Wave shape**: preload the latin face. Then either use `swap` with a fallback calibrated on the advance widths the layout reads (`ch` and the display weights), or keep `optional` and strike the no-shift claims. Born-REDs: the first load paints Plus Jakarta Sans (platform font); a bfcache round trip moves no h1 width and no `65ch` measure by more than 0.5 px (HEAD: +77.5 px and +160.6 px).
- **Severity**: HIGH, because the brand face is absent from every first load measured. Playwright Chromium headless only; no Safari cell.

### R5-01-06 (MED, EXTENDS F-38): a restored page keeps a modal layer open, with focus on BODY

- **Mechanism**: the engine clears focus on a bfcache restore; a control page with no library code also lands on BODY (`probes/focus-control.mjs`). The layers keep their logical open state (`body` `pointer-events: none; overflow: hidden`, `data-aria-hidden` on the page). Focus is placed only at mount, though, so nothing re-seats it on `pageshow`.
- **Evidence**: with a Dialog, a DropdownMenu or a Popover open across a bfcache round trip, focus is on BODY in all three (`focus-restore.json`, `life.json`). In the menu, ArrowDown twice does nothing and the highlighted item is lost. Tab brings focus back into the layer in all three.
- **Disposition**: FOLD into W-FOCUS-LIFECYCLE.
- **Wave shape**: on `pageshow` with `persisted`, an open modal layer re-seats focus on its last focused descendant. Born-RED: after the round trip with a menu open, `activeElement` is inside the menu and ArrowDown moves the highlight (HEAD: BODY, and the highlight does not move).

## 5. Examined and not filed

- **The toast timer across a park.** reka pauses on window `blur` and resumes on window `focus` (`reka-ui/dist/Toast/ToastViewport.js:104-105`). In headless, the restored page did not always receive `focus`. When it did not, the toast never dismissed (2 of 2 runs, 16 s polled); after a click that gave the page focus, it dismissed at +8.1 s (`toast-life2.json`). This depends on headless focus delivery, so it is not filed.
- **A Dialog printed while open.** Only the Dialog prints. Removing the scrim element (`fixed inset-0 z-overlay bg-overlay-scrim`) brings the page back, and making its background transparent does not (`print-dialog*.mjs`, `print-overlays.json`: page coverage 0.028 with the Dialog open against 0.41-0.50 in every other state). The engine mechanism is unresolved, so it is not filed.
- **The demo shell prints one page.** `<main>` is the scroller, so `/foundations/typography` (3,859 px of content) prints 1 page. This is demo-side, a consumer note for W-PRINT.
- **dt after a park.** `useRAFLoop` resets its frame time on hide (delta 0). The blob clamps to 50 ms (`blobSimulation.ts:114`), the Fourier clock to 50 ms (`fourier-field/clock.ts:95`) and the lead trail to 40 ms (`useLeadTrail.ts:67,207`). The aurora, Fourier and constellation loops see a delta at or below 0 after the resume rebase, and they reset the pointer field. R2-04-14's unclamped attractor is reachable only by a long main-thread stall on a visible page (F-44), not by a lifecycle transition.
- **Listener, timer and animation balance** across every restore: unchanged. Timeouts drain, and no animation count grows.
- **The restore reflow** comes from the font swap (R5-01-05). A `ch` control page with `swap` does not change across a restore.

## 6. Coverage and gaps

- Hide and show ran only as bfcache hide + freeze + show. New-headless offers no same-document hidden state, and CDP freeze does not freeze a visible page in this build (measured).
- Device sleep was not driven.
- Every cell is Playwright Chromium; no Safari or WebKit cell.
- The long session ran 30 minutes of real time, in parallel with a WebGL2-arm control; it did not include touch, a pen or a text entry.
- The Sheet (off-screen at HEAD, F-21) was not driven through any transition.
