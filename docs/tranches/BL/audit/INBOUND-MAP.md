## How to read

- **family**: the registered family whose mechanism names the row's cause. `F-30a`/`F-30b` are F-30's two rows. `—` means no family holds it.
- **status**:
  - `FAMILY`: the producer half is live or unmeasured at HEAD, and the family's wave carries the cure. The row's surface or witness joins that wave's born-RED set.
  - `CURED-AT-HEAD`: the cure is in the 10.0.1 bytes, cited by file and line. The consumer adopts it by repin, so the family column reads F-68 (W-LANDING).
  - `CONSUMER-SIDE`: the producer half is empty. The family column names the row the answer rides (F-71 ANSWER, or an F-68 migration-packet row) or reads `—`.
  - `NOT-REPRODUCED`: a HEAD probe ran (R4-01, or R2-02 for KFA-112) and did not reproduce the row as stated. The note names the nearest live mechanism.
  - `NEW`: no family's mechanism covers the row, so it mints. **None this round.**
- **API asks.** O-59 carries 24 rows that ask for a surface the library does not have (a tone axis, a loading state, FileDrop, Pagination, an anchor export). They carry no defect mechanism. They sit in F-71 as decide rows under E-5 (BUILD only if a consumer names it), as O-61 R-2 and R-3 did in round 2, so W-MAIL-INTAKE rules each CURE, ANSWER or DECLINE. They cannot mint.
- **"(re-checked)"** marks a row whose cited source I opened at `9b5aa09c` for this map. Rows citing R4-01 or R4-02 rest on those seats' HEAD measurements, whose sources I re-checked for the registry (REGISTRY §2, round 4 re-check log).
