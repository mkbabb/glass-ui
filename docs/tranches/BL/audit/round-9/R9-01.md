### R9-01-01: Sheet content inside the insets (F-85, CONFIRMS, HIGH)

- **Mechanism**: the Sheet anchors flush (`sheet/styles.css:456-465` `top: 0` / `bottom: 0` / `left: 0` / `right: 0`, re-checked) and pads with `padding-inline/-block: var(--space-family)` (`:557-560`, re-checked). Neither reads `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, so the content inherits the plate's device edge.
- **Measured** (Chromium, `?inj=fixed`, 4 of 4 per cell, `data/res2/`):
  - Portrait bottom sheet: "Done" `[787,831]` against a safe bottom of 810, **21 px** inside the home indicator. Same at detents 0.25, 0.5 and 1, and in the non-modal arm.
  - Portrait top sheet, and left and right sheets: the title at y 13 to 43.5, **34 px** into the 47 px top inset. Bottom sheet at detent 1: the grip `[13,57]` is 34 px in.
  - Landscape bottom and top sheets: title, field and grip at x 21 to 823, **26 px** into both 47 px notches; "Done" 26 px into the right notch. A left sheet puts its content 26 px into the left notch, a right sheet 26 px into the right.
- **Capture**: `p-sheet-bottom-light.png` ("Done" straddles the home-indicator line), `p-sheet-top-light.png`, `l-sheet-bottom-light.png`, `l-sheet-left-light.png`, `p-sheet-bottom-det1-light.png`.
- **Playwright WebKit**: `env()` cannot be overridden there (no CDP), so it gives only a zero-inset baseline. The anchors are identical to the pixel (`data/res3/wk-*`: plate `[635.5,844]`, action `[787,831]`), so the producer geometry does not depend on the engine. No Safari cell is inferred.
- **Wave correction to W-SAFE-EDGE**: F-85's wave says "every edge-anchored surface offsets by `max(own gap, token)`". For the Sheet that is wrong: an offset would lift the plate off its edge, and the iOS register bleeds the plate under the indicator. The Sheet's cure is padding on its anchored edge and on both inline edges, `padding-block-end: max(var(--space-family), var(--safe-block-end))` for `side=bottom`, and likewise for the other sides. The detent floor (`:531-543`) must add the same term, or the floor comes out 34 px short. Born-RED: at 390×844 with `{top 47, bottom 34}`, the bottom sheet's action bottom is ≤ 810 at every rung, and the top and side sheets' title top is ≥ 47. At 844×390 with `{left 47, right 47}`, every field sits within [47, 797]. HEAD: 21 / 34 / 26 px inside.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-SAFE-EDGE (the Sheet leg).

### R9-01-02: Toaster at phone width sits in the top inset (F-85, CONFIRMS, HIGH)

- **Mechanism**: `VIEWPORT_BASE = "fixed top-0 … max-h-screen"` (`toast/Toaster.vue:72`). The three bottom positions reach the bottom only through `sm:bottom-0 … sm:top-auto` (`:82-93`), so below 640 px all six positions anchor at `top: 0` with `p-4`. No arm reads an inset.
- **Measured** (`data/res/`, 4 of 4): portrait, all six positions: the viewport is `[0,138]` and the toast `[16,122]`, **31 px** into the 47 px top inset. The corner-straddling close disc (`ToastClose.vue:37-46`, `glass-corner-affordance`) paints at y ≈ 6 to 30, entirely inside the status-bar zone, where iOS routes a tap to scroll-to-top. Landscape: bottom-right 5 px into the bottom inset and 31 px into the right notch; bottom-left and top-left 31 px into the left notch; top-right 31 px into the right.
- **Capture**: `p-toast-bottom-right-light.png` (the toast crosses the top safe line, and the × sits above it), `l-toast-bottom-right-light.png`.
- **Born-RED correction**: F-85's born-RED checks "the block-end edges of … the Toaster sit at or above `innerHeight − 34`". At phone width no Toaster position is at the block end, so that assertion passes vacuously. It should read: at 390×844 the toast's top and its close disc's top are ≥ 47 for all six positions, and at 844×390 its inline edges are within [47, 797]. HEAD: 16 and 6.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-SAFE-EDGE (the Toaster leg: `p-4` becomes `max(1rem, token)` per edge).

### R9-01-03: ExpandableContainer's fullscreen host is the Configurator's edge surface (F-85, EXTENDS, MED)

- **Mechanism**: the expanded host is `position: fixed; inset: 0` (`expandable-container/styles.css:6-9`), and its collapse trigger is `position: absolute; inset-block-start: 0.5rem; inset-inline-end: 0.5rem` (`:55-58`, `:81-83`, re-checked). No inset is read.
- **Measured** (`<Configurator expandable :expanded="true">`, `data/res2/`, 4 of 4): in portrait the collapse trigger is at `[8,68]`, **39 px** into the top inset, and the Configurator plate top at 21 is 26 px in. In landscape the trigger's right edge is at 836, 39 px into the right notch, and the plate 14 px into both notches. After a portrait-to-landscape rotation the result is the same (`data/res3/`, `rot-cfg`).
- **Capture**: `p-cfg-light.png` (the collapse disc straddles the top safe line), `l-cfg-light.png`, `chromium-rot-cfg-light.png`.
- **Surface-list correction**: F-85 names "the Configurator stage overlay slot". Master has no such slot. `#stage` renders in-flow inside `.configurator-stage` (`Configurator.vue:406-409`), and the slot is a 10.1.0 (`release/10.1`) surface. At HEAD the edge-anchored Configurator surface is ExpandableContainer's fullscreen host, which every `expandable` consumer gets. W-SAFE-EDGE's list should name ExpandableContainer (padding on the host, and the trigger's `inset-*` as `max(0.5rem, token)`), plus the 10.1 slot once it lands on master.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-SAFE-EDGE.

### R9-01-04: popper content in the landscape notch (F-85, EXTENDS, MED; F-22 edge leg is the carrier)

- **Mechanism**: reka's collision boundary is the layout viewport, and the library passes no `collisionPadding` (`popover/PopoverContent.vue:56-62`, `menu/DropdownMenuContent.vue:55-62`, `grep collisionPadding popover menu` → 0; only `SelectContent.vue:56` passes a literal 16). When the content collides it shifts to the viewport's own edge, which is inside the inset.
- **Measured**, with the trigger placed inside the safe rect (`edge=rs|ls` → `right/left: env(safe-area-inset-*)`, `data/res4/`, 4 of 4): landscape Popover `[508,844]` with its trigger at right 797 goes **47 px** into the right notch, and at left `[0,336]` 47 px into the left. DropdownMenu at right `[684.5,812.5]` goes **15.5 px** in. At the top and bottom edges (portrait `ts`/`bs`, landscape `bs`) the content stays inside, because it opens away from the edge. With the trigger itself at the raw edge (`data/res/`), content goes 47 px into the notch too, but there the consumer's placement shares the blame, so only the safe-trigger cells are claimed.
- **Capture**: `safe-trigger-l-pop-rs-light.png`, `safe-trigger-l-pop-ls-light.png`, `safe-trigger-l-menu-rs-light.png`.
- **Placement**: the unread input is F-85's (a device inset that no surface reads). F-85's placement paragraph kept the dock, Sheet, Toaster and slot out of F-22 because they are not popper content. It did not say that popper content is free of the inset, and this row shows it is not. F-22's round-2 correction already puts an edge-clearance token in `overlayContentAttrs`. The cure is one line in that seam: `collisionPadding` as a per-side object `{ top: max(clear, --safe-block-start), … }` read from W-SAFE-EDGE's tokens. Filed in F-85 by input, carried by F-22's seam, and counted once.
- **Born-RED**: at 844×390 `{left 47, right 47}`, a Popover and a DropdownMenu opened from a trigger flush with the safe right edge keep `right ≤ 797`. HEAD: 844 and 812.5.
- **Disposition**: FOLD into W-SAFE-EDGE, with the F-22 seam line.

### R9-01-05: the dock leg has no producer placement (F-85, CONFIRMS narrowed, MED)

- **Measured** (the consumer wraps the dock `position: fixed; bottom|top|left|right: 12px`, the demo's `--sp-3` gap; `data/res5/`, 4 of 4): portrait bottom 172×60 plate `[772,832]`, **22 px** into the 34 px inset, matching fourier's O-74 number. Top 35 px in. Landscape left and right 35 px into the notch, bottom 9 px.
- **Narrowing**: F-85 says "the dock's placement … read[s] none", and its wave "offsets the dock's placement". `src` has no dock placement: GlassDock's six props (`useDockShellProps.ts:5-8`) have no position, and `dock/styles/dock.css` has only `position: absolute` internals (`:54`, `:165`). The consumer places the dock, as the demo does (`demo/shell/dock-nav.css:34`). The producer gap is that the library publishes no safe-edge token for that placement, and the README gives no placement idiom (`dock/README.md:7-15`). The dock leg of W-SAFE-EDGE is therefore "publish `--safe-*` and document the placement with it, and the demo shell consumes the published token", not an offset inside GlassDock.
- **Capture**: `p-dock-bottom-light.png` (from the collapsed run; the `:collapse=false` re-run is in `data/res5/`), `l-dock-left-light.png`.
- **Disposition**: BUILD (small), inside W-SAFE-EDGE.

### R9-01-06: the Dialog overflows a keyboard-shortened viewport (F-22, CONFIRMS, LOW)

- **Mechanism**: the Dialog's block cap applies only on the `scroll` arm (`dialog/DialogContent.vue:183` `props.scroll ? "max-h-[calc(100dvh-2rem)] overflow-y-auto" : ""`). This is A2-FO-L2-8, which the round-8 map placed in F-22.
- **Measured** (`interactive-widget=resizes-content`; a 180 px landscape keyboard emulated as the layout shrink to 844×210 after focusing the field; `data/res3/`, 4 of 4 in Chromium and in Playwright WebKit): the plate is `[-15,225]`, 15 px past each edge, with `scrollHeight == clientHeight` and `overflow-y: visible`, so the clipped band cannot be reached. Portrait 390×508 fits (`[150,358]`).
- **Capture**: `chromium-kb-l-dialog-light.png`.
- **Relation**: CONFIRMS F-22's inbound row, with a second way in: the keyboard, not only a short viewport. There is no new mechanism. Born-RED addition for F-22: at 844×210 the Dialog's plate lies within the viewport or scrolls. HEAD: −15 / 225.
- **Disposition**: FOLD into F-22's wave.

## Gaps

- **Viewport segments**: `Emulation.setDisplayFeaturesOverride` is accepted, but Chromium 149 (headless shell and new headless, with and without `--enable-experimental-web-platform-features` and `--enable-features=ViewportSegments,DevicePosture`) reports one segment, and `env(viewport-segment-right 0 0)` stays unresolved (`probe/seg.mjs`). `src` reads no `viewport-segment` (grep → 0). Not measured, not filed. The Dialog, the bottom Sheet, a bottom-center toast and the dock all straddle a 400 px hinge in the unsegmented cell (`chromium-seg-*.png`), which is the expected paint if segments were live.
- **Keyboard overlays-content / resizes-visual, and the dynamic toolbar**: headless has no virtual keyboard and no dynamic toolbar (`svh = lvh = dvh`, and `visualViewport` equals the layout viewport in every cell). `src` reads no `visualViewport` and no `env(keyboard-inset-*)` (grep → 0). The Toaster's `max-h-screen` (100vh = lvh) is the one lvh-sized fixed surface, from source only. The dock search was not driven. The safe-area override persists through the emulated keyboard shrink, where a real device would drop the bottom inset, so the keyboard cells assert against the viewport, not the inset.
- **Playwright WebKit**: there is no inset override, so it gives only zero-inset baselines (sheets, toasts, popovers, menus, Dialog, deck and Configurator match Chromium to the pixel). Every GlassDock cell crashes the Playwright WebKit page ("Page crashed", 8 of 8 dock cells and 4 of 4 `seg-dock`; `probe/wk.mjs` isolates it to the dock). L08 saw this engine-build crash on another route. It is not filed, and no Safari cell is inferred.
- **Pointer to R9-02**: in landscape (390 tall) the detented Sheet's floor (`sheet/styles.css:531-543`) resolves rungs 0.25 and 0.5 to the same 204.5 px box (`data/res2/`, `l-sheet-bottom-det0.25/0.5`). That is the "Sheet's landscape ladder" in R9-02's charter, and it is not filed here.
