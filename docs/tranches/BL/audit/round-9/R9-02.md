### R9-02-02: detent rungs collapse under the operable floor (NEW, HIGH)

- **Mechanism**: a detented bottom or top sheet sizes as `block-size: calc(clamp(0, var(--detent-t), 1) * 100dvh)`, floored by `min-block-size: calc(2·--space-family + seam + --touch-target + 2·--space-body + --space-body + seam + 1lh + --space-body + seam + --touch-target)` (`sheet/styles.css:530-543`). The rungs are fractions of the viewport, while the floor is an absolute sum. Any rung whose fraction × `innerHeight` falls under the floor paints at the floor, so two such rungs paint the same sheet. The engine keeps them as distinct stops: `--detent-t` reads 0.12 and 0.36, and the model emits both. The floor also grows as the viewport gets shorter. The width query (`tokens/sizing.css:333-342`) transposes `--space-family` 12 → 20 and `--space-body` 8 → 12 above the phone width, so 844×390 gets the larger pads (`p-floor.mjs`: floor 171 px at 390×844 and 204.5 px at 844×390, the same at fine and coarse pointers).
- **Measured** (`res/sheet.json`, `fixed-*`, 2 of 2):
  - 844×390, ladder `[0.12, 0.36, 1]`: 0.12 (46.8 px requested) and 0.36 (140.4 px requested) both paint [185.5, 390], 204.5 px. The ladder has two visible rungs, not three. This is the letter's "peek and 0.36 are the same 204 px".
  - Portrait: 0.12 paints at the 171 px floor in every cell (20.3% of 844, 21.9% of 780, 18.3% of 932, against a requested 12%). 0.36 is above the floor, so the ladder stays distinct there.
- **Capture**: `sheet-fixed-l844-d0.12.png` and `sheet-fixed-l844-d0.36.png` (identical plates), `sheet-fixed-p390-d0.12.png`.
- **Placement weighed**: F-32's statement is the dock covering the live-behind sheet's action (round-2 correction). Its evidence is hit-testing, not rung arithmetic. F-29 names the width query moving pads, which is why the floor rises in landscape, but it names no detent and no rung merge. F-21's containment row holds clips, not sizes. No family names a fractional ladder merged by an absolute floor, so this is NEW. The width-query half is cross-cited to F-29.
- **Map correction**: split A2-KE-L2-3. The ladder half is this family's. The rises-under-the-top-dock half stays in F-32 (R9-02-03).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. **Wave shape**: W-DETENT-FLOOR. The engine resolves each rung to `max(rung · vh, floor)` at layout, and deduplicates rungs that resolve within one grip height of each other, so every stop it offers moves the sheet. The floor's pads read the height-aware rung, or the landscape arm steps them down. Born-RED: at 844×390 with `[0.12, 0.36, 1]`, every pair of adjacent offered stops differs by at least 44 px of plate (HEAD: 0 px between 0.12 and 0.36).

### R9-02-04: dock chrome dismisses the non-modal Sheet, and Chromium touch does it through focus (F-22, EXTENDS, MED)

- **Mechanism**: F-22's dismissal leg ("which press is outside"). A tap on a dock control while a non-modal detented sheet is open counts as outside. The route depends on the input. Mouse goes through reka's `pointerDownOutside`, which fires at `pointerdown` (`DismissableLayer/utils.js:32-54`). In Chromium touch the button takes focus before the deferred click, so the sheet is dismissed through `focusOutside` (`focusin` on the control), and `pointerDownOutside` is never emitted.
- **Measured** (`res/nm5.json`, Chromium, 4 cells × 2 runs, event log per cell):
  - A dock control with no handler (`c3`): the sheet closes in 16 of 16 (tap and click). The log reads `fo:focusin/c3, io:focusin/c3` for touch and `pdo:mouse, io:pointerdown` for mouse.
  - A dock control that raises the sheet to 0.36 (`c2`): the sheet ends open at 0.36 in 16 of 16. Under touch it is dismissed by focus and re-opened by the item's click; under mouse it is dismissed by `pdo` and re-opened by the click. The letter's symptom (touch leaves 0.12, mouse gives 0.36) is **NOT-REPRODUCED** in a clean consumer. Its cause is the order between keyframes' own handler and reka's deferred click, which this consumer does not share.
- **What this changes**: the letter's cure ("does not outside-dismiss on presses on dock chrome") would be written on `pointerDownOutside`. In Chromium touch that event never arrives, and `focusOutside` dismisses first. The exemption belongs on `interactOutside` (both routes), or the tethered chrome joins the layer's branch set. On iOS a tapped button does not take focus, so real Safari probably takes the deferred-click route the letter describes. That is not inferred here: WebKit crashed.
- **Capture**: `nm-chromium-p390-c3-tap.png` (sheet gone after a tap on a dock control), `nmsheet-raise-p390-tap.png`.
- **Map correction**: A2-KE-L2-1 stays FAMILY in F-22. The symptom is NOT-REPRODUCED at HEAD in a clean consumer, and the mechanism is reproduced in 16 of 16.
- **Disposition**: FOLD into F-22's dismissal leg. Born-RED: with a non-modal detented Sheet open, a tap and a click on a DockControl leave the sheet open at its rung in Chromium (HEAD: closed, 16 of 16), and the guard sits on `interactOutside`.
