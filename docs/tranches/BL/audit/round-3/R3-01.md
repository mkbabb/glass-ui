## Evidence

### R3-01-01—Carousel `v-model:active` is read once (HIGH, EXTENDS F-70)

- **Mechanism**: `Carousel.vue:24` declares `defineModel<number>("active")` and hands it to `useCarousel.ts:35-42`, which seeds the deck once (`initial: active.value`) and writes the model outbound from `onChange` (`active.value = to`). Nothing watches the model inbound, so any writer other than the deck's own scroll (a bound PagerDots, an autoplay, a sibling rail) changes the model and the strip stays put. This is E-11's shape: a declared binding that no-ops with no error. The deck demo avoids it (`demo/stories/motion/deck.vue:58-62` binds PagerDots through a computed get/set over `deck.index`), which isolates the defect to Carousel's model edge.
- **Probe** (`/navigation/carousel`, `carousel` scenario, `probe-carousel*.mjs`):
  - Worm exhibit, click dot 4: the worm travels 24 → 144 px and the dot reads current, while the strip's `scrollLeft` stays 0 on every frame and item 0 stays `data-state="active"`. Identical in L120, D120, L60 and RM120 (`metrics.json` `car_dot-3.strip_sl_range [0,0]`).
  - Hero exhibit (autoplay 4,200 ms, `demo/stories/navigation/carousel.vue:49-64`): after 9 s the dots read "Go to slide 4" current and the strip still shows slide 1, light and dark (`carousel-probe.json` `hero2`).
  - Rail, ArrowRight ×2: rail current 2, strip 0 (`carousel-probe2.json`, load 44.7).
  - Control: the CarouselPager chevrons page the strip 0 → 127 px (`peekAfterNext`), because they call the deck directly.
- **Capture**: `strip-carousel-dot3-worm-vs-strip.png`.
- **Owner**: BDF B3-C3 (carousel transitions "more glassy, distortion, inertia"): on 4 of 5 exhibits there is no page transition to judge, because the strip never moves.
- **Wave shape**: W-CAROUSEL-MODEL (small): `watch(active, (i) => i !== deck.index.value && deck.go(i))`, routed through `useDeckSnap.scrollTo`. Born-RED e2e: bind `v-model:active` to PagerDots, click dot 3, expect `scrollLeft = 3 × pitch` within 1 s. The model joins F-70's runtime-witness list (a write to the model must move the view).

### R3-01-02—Carousel member lag holds at rest (MED, EXTENDS F-46)

- **Mechanism**: `useDeckSnap.ts:103-112` computes velocity from consecutive `scroll` events and calls `deck.feed(position, velocity)` (`useDeck.ts:129-134`) only on those events. The last event of a gesture carries non-zero velocity, and nothing feeds zero at `scrollend` or `snapchanged`. `CarouselContent.vue:59-66` writes `--member-lag = memberLag(deck.velocity)` (`projection.ts:65-71`, which is `trailOffset` at `useLeadTrail.ts:73-76`), and `carousel/styles.css:56` translates each slide's content by `lag × 100%`. At rest the content keeps its last in-flight offset. F-46's mechanism exactly: state advanced on velocity ticks, stale when the ticks stop, position never consulted.
- **Probe**: wheel on the exhibits strip, then 1.5 s idle. `--member-lag` at rest: −0.0409 (L120), −0.0419 (D120), −0.0284 (L60), −0.0424 (L120 second run); `carousel-probe.json`: −0.0446 light, −0.0391 dark. The content box sits −14.51 px (light) and −12.72 px (dark) from its item: the left rounded corners clip and a strip of track shows on the right. RM120 is clean (the PRM arm does not write the lag).
- **Captures**: `carousel-rest-before-after-light.png`, `carousel-rest-after-wheel-dark.png`, `strip-carousel-wheel-residual-lag.png`.
- **Wave shape**: in F-46's wave, one law for both consumers (with R3-01-03): the lag is a function of position (distance to the settling snap), or velocity is zeroed on `scrollend`. Invariant: 100 ms after `scrollend`, every `--member-lag` is 0.

### R3-01-03—The pager worm's trail runs behind its origin (MED, EXTENDS F-46)

- **Mechanism**: `useLeadTrail.ts:176` places the trail edge at `x − trailOffset(v, τ, ceil)`. The offset is clamped by a ceiling, not by the distance travelled, so at launch, when `v` is large and `x` has barely left the origin, the trail edge lands behind the origin, and the trailing body steps backward before it follows. Same law as R3-01-02 (`projection.ts:18`).
- **Probe** (`/navigation/pager-dots`, `pager` scenario): trail edge behind the origin by 12.81 px on a 28 px hop and 24.61 px on a 56 px hop (L120); 12.79 / 24.12 (D120); 12.83 / 21.61 (L60); 10.74 / 24.65 (L120 second run); 0 in RM120.
- **Masked today by F-36**: the neck scales to 301-1,056 px, so the worm reads as a smear and the back-step is lost in it. With F-36 cured in the page only (`--pager-dot-size: 12px` injected by `addStyleTag`; no source edit), the trailing body kicks back 11.5 px in one frame (674 → 662.5 px at +70 ms) and reaches 13.7 px behind its origin (light) and 12.9 px (dark) (`worm-f36-cured-hop.json`, loads 56.6 and 45).
- **Captures**: `strip-pager-hop1-trail-behind.png`, `strip-worm-f36-cured-trail-behind-origin.png`, `worm-fixed-rest.png`.
- **Wave shape**: the trail is a first-order follower of the lead's position, bounded between the origin and the lead, decaying to the lead at rest. Born-RED after F-36 lands: min(trail edge) ≥ origin on every hop. Order: F-36 first, or this defect cannot be seen.

### R3-01-04—The Alert liquid entrance plays inside the route view transition (MED, EXTENDS F-43)

- **Mechanism**: a route push runs a 578-581 ms view transition (`gl-route-slide-in`, `gl-vt-route-in`, `gl-route-world-push-in` on the route window). The six Alerts on `/feedback/alert` carry `.liquid-enter` (`alert/index.ts:56`) and play `gl-liquid-enter` (200 ms on `--spring-present`) from +103 to +303 ms after the click: every frame of the entrance lands inside the incoming snapshot's own slide and fade, starting from near-zero page opacity. Two engines own one arrival and nothing decides between them. `liquid-enter.css:11-13` says "Route changes use native View Transitions, not this recipe", and `:7-9` says every mount surface opts in on the `--i` stagger; Alert is the only consumer in `src/` and `demo/`, sets no `--i`, and all six alerts run `animation-delay: 0s` in unison.
- **Probe** (`alertNav` scenario, sidebar click from `/feedback/toast`): frames where an alert's opacity, scale, translate or filter changed, and how many of them fell inside a frame with a running view-transition animation: 25/25 (L120), 25/25 (D120), 12/12 (L60) in the third run (`alertNavr3/`, loads 31.0-31.5); 24/24 and 25/25 in the first two runs (loads 44.0, 50.8). First frame: opacity 0.024, scale 1.0585 × 0.9463, translate 7.8 px, blur 3.9 px. RM120: no view transition, the reduced fade completes in 2 frames (see R3-01-12).
- **Capture**: `strip-alert-entrance-inside-route-vt.png`.
- **Owner**: locates the "double animated" site of N-8d (R2-06 recorded it as unlocated); measures BDM A3 and D-entrance (the squish, blur and fade above). A3's "replicate generally" is not built: one consumer.
- **Wave shape**: widen W-ROUTE-POPSTATE to the route seam: one owner per arrival. Mount entrances under a route change wait for the transition to finish or are suppressed while it runs, and the `--i` stagger is either written by the consumer (list index) or struck from the header. Probe: 0 `.liquid-enter` frames overlap view-transition frames on a push.

### R3-01-05—HandMark draws twice when fonts land after mount (MED, NEW family N-A)

- **Mechanism**: `HandMark.vue:329-338`: `onMounted` calls `run(0)` in `nextTick` and again on `document.fonts.ready`. `run()` (`:263-284`) resets `stroke-dashoffset` and starts a fresh WAAPI draw from the full length each time; nothing checks whether a draw already played.
- **Probe** (`hm-fontdelay.mjs`, `/motion/handmark`, woff2 responses delayed 450 ms): guide 0 draws to 0.1 by +462 ms, resets to 166 (undrawn) at +706 ms and draws again by +938 ms, with two animations stacked. With no delay the second run starts at +348 ms before the first has moved, so the two overlap without a visible reset (`handmark-fontdelay.json`, loads 65.7-69.6). The reset is a logic outcome (a second `run(0)`), not timing jitter; it reproduced on both runs.
- **Why NEW**: no registered family holds an entrance whose trigger fires twice. F-43 is the route seam and F-51 is mount-path cost.
- **Wave shape**: one draw per mount. On `fonts.ready`, re-measure only; replay only if the draw has not started (or continue from the current offset). Probe: one draw per mount under a 450 ms font delay.

### R3-01-12—The universal PRM carve cancels the recipe fades (MED, EXTENDS F-21; doc half F-63)

- **Mechanism**: `a11y-overrides.css:45-54` sets `animation-duration: 0.01ms !important; transition-duration: 0s !important` on `*` inside `@layer components`. The recipe arms that keep a fade under reduce are not `!important`: `reveal.css:317-327` (`transition-duration: var(--duration-fast)`) and `liquid-enter.css:215-220` (`gl-liquid-enter-reduced var(--duration-fast)`). Only the enumerated authorized set carries `!important` and survives: `transitions.css:128-162` (Vue fades) and `dissolve.css:275-288` (vaporize). So under reduce a toast enters 0 → 1 in one frame and an alert's reduced fade ends in 2 frames (8 ms), while the toast's exit (vaporize, authorized) still runs 240 ms. This is F-21's mechanism on a motion property (R2-02-04 folded the same): a shared rule replaces the component's own declaration.
- **Docs**: `reveal.css:313` ("The OPACITY fade SURVIVES"), `liquid-enter.css:207-209` ("the entrance keeps the FADE"), `transitions.css:120-123` ("the fade survives") all describe arms that do not run.
- **Probe**: `toast/RM120.json` (load 30.1), `alertNav/RM120.json` (load 28.2, `alert_enter_frames 2`).
- **Wave shape**: one PRM table. Either the recipe fade arms join the authorized set, or the docs say PRM snaps. Probe: under reduce, a toast's enter opacity runs for `--duration-fast` ± one frame, or its docs say otherwise.

### R3-01-15—`/dock/overflow` voids the run's snap lattice (MED, EXTENDS F-18)

- **Mechanism**: `run.css:270-273` makes only direct children of `.dock-run` snap targets (`> * { scroll-snap-align: start }`), and `:155-158` warns that a wrapper would make seats grandchildren. The library's own overflow story wraps every seat in one `div.flex role="radiogroup"` (`demo/stories/dock/overflow.vue:69-75`), so the run has a single 524 px snap area over a 316 px port, and `x mandatory` cannot hold a rest inside an area wider than the port. Rests at 37, 60 and 120 px were accepted; the pitch lattice forbids all three.
- **Probe** (`probe-dockrun*.mjs`, `dockglide/gesture.json` at 420 × 800, load 34.1): run `scrollWidth` 524 / `clientWidth` 316; synthesized drags of 120 and 60 px track 1:1 and stop dead at 120 and 60 px, with no snap settle. `dockglide/L120.json` (load 46.3): wheel legs, same result.
- **Owner**: BDU R56. The glide constant and composable the row names (`SCROLL_PX_PER_SLOT`, `useLiquidRail`) have 0 hits; the lattice that superseded them is void on the one story that demonstrates overflow. Same run as the owner witness on F-18 (4652670d).
- **Wave shape**: the story seats its controls as direct children (roving tabindex on the seats), and a born-RED probe asserts every rest after a gesture sits at P/2 (mod P).
