## The two findings from the interim tokens

- **On `:host` the hover hooks lose in dark.** Confirmed: `tokens/property-regs-specular.css:38-44` sets `--glass-specular-intensity-hover`, `-active` and `--glass-specular-capsule-hover` again under `.dark`. A `.dark` element below the host wins over a value set on `:host`. Setting them on `.glass-root` and `.glass-root.dark`, as the dashboard does, outranks it. Both rows go when 11.1.2 ships.
- **`--glass-specular-intensity-active` must stay.** Confirmed: it also lights a checked, on, pressed, selected, expanded or current surface at rest (`styles/glass/material.css:368-375`), and the press release (`:179`, `:313`). Setting it to 0 would dim the selected dock seat and an on toggle with nothing in their place. 11.1.2's specular removal is held to the same line: the pointer-following light goes from every surface on hover and on press, and a state's rest light stays until the dock redesign shows selection by fill steps instead.

## Round 14's amendment (2026-11-24): LF-56 and LF-57

| Ask | Verdict | Rides with | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| LF-56 | glass defect | 11.1.2, the dock-frames cure's challenge round | Confirmed on release/11.1.1. The dock's press-outside test runs at `document` in capture and asks `root.contains(e.target)` (`dock/composables/useDockState.ts:652`, and `:400` for a seat's claim). The teleport check asks `target.closest(...)` (`_shared/overlay/isTeleportedTarget.ts:18-20`). Inside shadow roots, the target at `document` is retargeted to the outermost host, so a press on a seat reads as outside: the dock folds in 9 to 12 ms and the click is lost. No glass source uses `composedPath()`. The cure: both checks ask `e.composedPath().includes(root)`, and `isTeleportedTarget` walks `composedPath()` for a portal. The sweep takes the other document-level `contains(event.target)` in `src/` too: `composables/dom/useTouchGate.ts:28` (`dock/composables/useDockRun.ts:349` runs on the dock's own listener, so its target is not retargeted). Witness: a dock inside an open shadow root, where a press on a seat opens its page and doesn't fold the dock, in both engines and in a unit test. Until 11.1.2, Home Assistant holds `collapse: false`. |
| LF-57 | glass defect | 11.1.2 | Confirmed: the Slider's root is `touch-action: none` (`slider/styles.css:31`), and a press sets the value, so a vertical swipe begun on a slider changes it and never scrolls the page. The cure, on a coarse pointer: a horizontal slider's root is `touch-action: pan-y`. No value is set until the pointer has moved 10 px more along the track than across it; a move across it goes to the page's scroll. A press let go inside the threshold sets the value at the release point, emitting `update:modelValue` and `valueCommit` once. A vertical slider mirrors it on the other axis. The expanded view (O-426, the design study's tranche) grows on the press as designed; the value waits for intent. Witness: at 390 with touch, a vertical swipe from a slider scrolls the page and leaves the value unchanged; a horizontal drag sets it from the 10 px point; a tap sets it once at its release. Until 11.1.2, the rail's own touch rule stands. |
