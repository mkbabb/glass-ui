## The proposed family

**F-NEW-DIR (band F, max HIGH): direction and writing mode are not inputs to axis code.** Every horizontal surface resolves its axis as physical x with "next" at larger x. A pitch measured from `offsetLeft` or a centre difference is negative in RTL, a scroll offset from `scrollLeft` is ≤ 0 in RTL, and a key table maps ArrowRight to "next". Code that assumes a positive pitch or offset then clamps against the wrong bound, guards the whole path off, or writes a value the engine clamps to 0. Key tables built on physical arrows move focus away from the arrow's direction. reka resolves direction from its own `dir` prop or ConfigProvider and defaults to `ltr`, stamping it on the root, so an inherited `dir="rtl"` never reaches reka primitives. The library ships no ConfigProvider and forwards no `dir` except DropdownMenu's own prop (`menu/DropdownMenu.vue:80,93`, default undefined).

No registered family holds this. F-19 is the dock's seat resolution and naming, F-42 is absent keys and names, F-36 is a unit parse and F-46 is a velocity-only offset; none of them names direction or writing mode, and none of their waves reads the resolved direction. The round-3 rule mints when no registered mechanism names the cause.

The library already has the correct shape in five places, which the wave reuses:
- `tabs/composables/useTabRovingFocus.ts:166-179` reads `getComputedStyle(currentTarget).direction` and swaps the arrows. Measured: SegmentedTabs in RTL keeps the indicator exactly on the selected tab, including the scroll offsets −18, −113 and −549 px, and ArrowRight moves to the physically right tab (`tabs-*-rtl-last.png`).
- The dock caps are logical keyframes (`dock/styles/run.css:557-600`, `border-start-start-radius` and so on). Measured in RTL: at rest `10 24 24 10` (TL TR BR BL; the cut on the left, where the hidden seats are), scrolled to the end `24 10 10 24`.
- `fading-scroll/composables/useFadingScroll.ts:35-60` normalises RTL scroll types.
- `progress/Progress.vue:191` and `switch/styles.css:57` carry `:dir(rtl)` arms, and neither root is stamped by reka, so both apply.
- The dialog content inherits `rtl` (no stamp).

Wave shape, W-DIRECTION: one resolved direction per axis site (`getComputedStyle(el).direction` plus `writing-mode`, as `useTabRovingFocus` reads it). Pitches and offsets become magnitudes with a separate sign: clamps take `|pitch|`, and paint applies the sign. Scroll reads and writes go through one logical-offset helper (the `useFadingScroll` normaliser, lifted). Key tables and swipe map prev and next through the resolved direction. The library passes the resolved `dir` to every reka root, or wraps its reka roots in one ConfigProvider seam. Directional glyphs mirror under `:dir(rtl)`. The carousel snaps on `inline`/`block`, not `x`/`y`. One RTL cell and one vertical-rl cell join the browser battery, each with a born-RED per member below.

### R4-02-01—RTL pager: the rail rests as one solid bar (HIGH, NEW)

- **Mechanism**: `measure()` sets `geometry.pitch = centreOf(second) − origin` (`pager-dots/worm.ts:128`), which is −28 px in RTL. `elongationCeil(span, pitch)` (`worm.ts:49-54`) returns `hi = 3·pitch = −84` for every span, because `want = |span|·0.55 ≥ 0 > −84`. `trailOffset(v, τ, ceil)` (`composables/motion/morph/useLeadTrail.ts:73-76`) then returns `ceil` for any `raw > ceil`, including `v = 0`, so at rest `trail = x − (−84) = x + 84` (`:182-192`). The paint (`worm.ts:195-214`) puts body B 84 px right of the active dot, off the rail, and scales the neck by `gap / D`. `D` is F-36's `parseFloat(".75rem") = 0.75`, so the neck scale is 84 / 0.75 = 112 at opacity 1.
- **Probe** (`pager.mjs`, `carousel.mjs`): on `/navigation/pager-dots` at rest in RTL the rail is [524, 676], the active dot [588, 612], the bodies [594, 606] and **[678, 690]**, and the neck **[−30, 1314]** with `scale: 112 1; opacity: 1`. In LTR both bodies sit at [674, 686] and the neck is at opacity 0. The carousel's bound pager does the same: body B is +84 px at rest, and at dot 3 after a click. Captures `pager-pager-dots-0-rtl.png` (a solid dark bar with no dots visible) and `pager-pager-dots-0-ltr.png`; `pager-carousel-rtl-dot2.png`.
- **Relation to F-36 and F-46**: F-36's parse turns an 84 px worm into a full-rail bar, but the RTL defect stands with F-36 cured (a 7× neck and a body off the rail at rest). F-46's round-3 invariant "min(trail edge) ≥ origin" is direction-blind, and in RTL it would pass on a broken worm; it should read "the trail lies between the origin and the lead".
- **Born-RED**: in RTL, at rest, neck opacity 0 and both bodies within 0.5 px of the active dot's centre (HEAD: +84 px, scale 112).

### R4-02-02—RTL carousel: every programmatic page is dropped (HIGH, NEW)

- **Mechanism**: `useDeckSnap.measure()` takes `extent = second.offsetLeft − first.offsetLeft` (`deck/composables/useDeckSnap.ts:84`), which is negative in RTL. Two guards written for "not yet measured" then fire on every call: `onScroll` returns on `extent <= 0` (`:104`), so the continuous feed (member scale, member lag, velocity) never runs, and `scrollTo` returns on `extent <= 0` (`:145`), so `deck.go`, `next` and `prev` change the index and never move the strip. The arithmetic below the guard would have been right: `index · extent` is the correct negative RTL offset. The same engine drives `DeckStage.vue:56`.
- **Probe** (`chevron.mjs`, `lag.mjs`): on `/navigation/carousel`, the peek carousel's "Next slide" pressed twice. LTR: `scrollLeft` 0 → 127 → 255, and the view follows. RTL: `scrollLeft` stays **0** with slide 1 in view while the items' `data-state` reads `prev,prev,active,…` (the state says slide 3). A native wheel still pages RTL (−351, `scrollsnapchange` commits the index). During an RTL wheel the member-lag offset is 0 in every frame (23 moving frames), while LTR trails by 13 to 25 px, and member scales stay at the discrete 1 and 0.965 instead of the continuous 0.9994 and 0.9656. Captures `carousel-peek-rtl-afterNext2.png` and `-ltr-`.
- **Not this row**: dot clicks not paging is R3-01-01 (the inbound `v-model`, F-46's neighbour), in both directions.
- **Born-RED**: RTL CarouselPager "Next" → `scrollLeft = −pitch` within 1 s, and `--member-lag` is non-zero during a wheel.

### R4-02-03—RTL dock: End focuses a seat outside the port (HIGH, NEW)

- **Mechanism**: the key table is `["ArrowLeft", "ArrowRight"]` = [prev, next] in DOM order (`dock/composables/useDockRun.ts:251-253`), so in RTL ArrowRight moves focus to the seat on the left. Reach computes a positive anchor `i·pitch − pitch/2` and clamps it to `[0, scrollMax]` (`:149`), then writes it through `run.scrollLeft = value` (`:120-123`). RTL `scrollLeft` lives in `[−max, 0]`, so the engine clamps every write to 0: reach can return to the start and can never go anywhere else. `occluded()` (`:157-168`) is physical and correct.
- **Probe** (`dockkeys.mjs`, `rtlcap.mjs`): `/dock/overview`, dock #9 (the `shape-card` run, 424 px of seats in a 296 px port). LTR: ArrowRight from Home goes to Search at x 580 (right); End goes to Settings at [788, 828], inside the port [532, 828], with `scrollLeft` 128. RTL: ArrowRight from Home at 708 goes to Search at **660 (left)**; End puts focus on Settings at **[324, 364], outside the port [452, 748]**, with `scrollLeft` **0**, a focused control the user cannot see. After a manual scroll to −128, Home does reach (0 is the one reachable value). Captures `dock-overview9-rtl-end.png` and `-ltr-end.png`. CDP AX exposes the toolbar with `orientation="horizontal"` and no direction, so the key map is the only direction carrier, and it is LTR.
- **Born-RED**: in RTL, End puts the last seat inside the port, and ArrowRight moves focus to the physically right neighbour.

### R4-02-04—reka roots stamp `dir="ltr"` inside an RTL page (MED, NEW)

- **Mechanism**: reka's `useDirection` resolves `dir` prop → ConfigProvider → `'ltr'` and writes it on the root. The library passes no `dir` to `SliderRoot` (`slider/Slider.vue:259-275`), RadioGroup, Select, Combobox or Popover content, and ships no ConfigProvider. Its own `.glass-slider:dir(rtl)` arm (`slider/styles.css:58-64`, the range origin flipped to the right) can therefore never match unless a consumer passes `dir` on the component. The demo does exactly that (`demo/stories/forms/slider.vue:175`), so the story shows RTL working.
- **Probe** (`dircensus.mjs`, `slider.mjs`, `rtlmisc.mjs`, `ovz.mjs`): across the 80 story routes in an RTL document, **154** nodes carry `dir="ltr"` on **17** routes: `span.glass-slider` 129, `button.control-surface[combobox]` (Select trigger) 17, `div.radio-group` 3, `button.dock-trigger[combobox]` 2, `div.command` 1, and 2 others (`dircensus.json`). The page mirrors (track [821, 1150]), but the slider's fill runs from the left (`left: 0%; right: 58%`), a click 20% from the left edge sets 20, and ArrowRight increments. The radios lay out LTR (x 417, 491, 572) and ArrowRight goes to index 1. Opened in RTL, the DropdownMenu and Popover content compute `direction: ltr`: labels are left-aligned, shortcuts sit right, and the submenu chevron points right toward the viewport edge, with the menu flush at x 1024..1280 over the sidebar (`overlay-RTL-dropdown-menu.png`). Captures `slider-rtl-*.png`, `radiogroup-rtl.png`.
- **Born-RED**: a Slider inside `dir="rtl"` with no prop has its fill anchored right and ArrowLeft increments; 0 `[dir="ltr"]` nodes inside an RTL document on the battery routes.

### R4-02-05—Key tables, swipe and chevrons hard-wire Right = next (MED, NEW)

- **Mechanism**: `PagerDots.vue:291-292` and `deck/composables/useDeckKeyboard.ts:70-71` map ArrowRight to next with no direction read. `useDeckSwipe.ts:56` maps a leftward swipe to `next` (in RTL, a leftward swipe reveals the previous member), and `Timeline.vue:214-215` does the same with arrows. - **Probe** (`carousel.mjs`): on the RTL carousel pager, with focus on dot 3, ArrowRight moves the worm to dot 4 at x 957, i.e. **left** (LTR: dot 4 at 323, right). The shell's BottomDock strip arrows (demo, same glyphs) point inward in RTL (`overlay-RTL-dropdown-menu.png`, bottom). The swipe and the Timeline are source-read, not driven.

### R4-02-06—Vertical writing mode (LOW, NEW)

- **Mechanism**: `.carousel-strip { scroll-snap-type: x mandatory }` (`carousel/styles.css:23`) and the lag `translate: calc(var(--member-lag) * 100%) 0` (`:56`) are physical, and `useDeckSnap` reads `scrollLeft` for the horizontal axis (`useDeckSnap.ts:93-96`). Under `writing-mode: vertical-rl` the strip's inline axis is vertical, so its overflow is on y, where no snap type applies. SegmentedTabs keeps `aria-orientation="horizontal"` on a strip that now runs vertically, and its key table follows the prop, not the writing mode.
- **Probe** (`vwm.mjs`): the carousel strip under vertical-rl scrolls 5280 px on y, and a 90 px wheel rests at **`scrollTop` 90** (off-lattice, the item 90 px from its snap). The tabs indicator tracks the selected tab exactly; ArrowDown on the vertical-rl strip scrolls the page instead of moving focus; AX orientation is "horizontal". The dock (#9) re-flows vertically with logical caps (`24px` ×4, no overflow), and End reaches Settings (no defect found). Captures `vwm-*.png`.
- **Disposition**: in W-DIRECTION: `scroll-snap-type: inline mandatory` (`block` for the vertical axis) and a logical lag translate; `aria-orientation` derived from the writing mode, or the writing mode is declared unsupported in the component docs.

### R4-02-10—HandMark: a wrapped mark's frame sits on the wrong line and pans the page (MED, EXTENDS F-76)

- **Mechanism**: `measure()` reads one rect per fragment (`Range.getClientRects()` in `slotRects`, `handmark/HandMark.vue:126-150`) and draws each fragment's geometry relative to the first fragment's origin (`:222`, `x: r.x − origin.left`). Every `svg.hm-mark` is `position: absolute; left: 0; top: 0` of the inline host (`:447-453`) with `width = frame.width`. The paint lands on the right fragment through negative coordinates and `overflow: visible`, but the SVG's layout box is the second fragment's width placed at the first fragment's origin. That box counts as scrollable overflow. This is F-76's shape with the sign reversed: the absolute layer's box, not its paint, is what the reserve leaves out.
- **Probe** (`hm.mjs`, `svgw.mjs`, `wide.mjs`): at 320×256, "Hpqjy really matters" wraps into [203..272 @1289] and [41..204 @1320]. Its frames are [203..272 @1289] and **[203..366 @1289]**, and `main.scrollWidth` is **366 against 320**: the page pans 46 px. At 390 it does not pan, and under 1.4.12 spacing it pans **79 px** (the frame at [258..469]). Capture `z400-handmark-pan.png`.
- **Born-RED** (added to W-EXTENT-OWNED): each frame is positioned at its own fragment (`left`/`top` = the frame's offset), or the host clips layout overflow; `main` scrollWidth equals clientWidth at 320 on `/motion/handmark`.

### R4-02-11—SegmentedTabs re-measures only when the container resizes (MED, EXTENDS F-43 with R4-01-08)

A label or spacing change that grows the selected tab without resizing the strip leaves the stored width and translate stale. The underline strip is `display: flex` at the parent's width, so it never resizes on a label change; the pill strip is `inline-grid` and resizes, unless the consumer pins its width. This is R4-01-08's trigger set, reached by content rather than a host transform. If the registry keeps R4-01-08 in F-43, this row joins it there. If it does not, the two rows share one mechanism (the re-measure triggers exclude the measured subject's box) and belong in one place.
- **Probe** (`tabs-late.mjs`, `tabs-late2.mjs`): selected-tab width minus indicator width, measured 1 s after the change. 1.4.12 spacing injected after mount: the underline strips are **−14 and −22 px** and the pills 0. Capture `tabs-late-german-0.png`.
- **Born-RED**: after a label change on the selected tab, the indicator is within 0.5 px of the tab (observe the selected button, not only the strip).

### R4-02-13—Inline Command: 8 of 13 rows cannot be scrolled to (MED, EXTENDS F-21's containment row)

- **Found in the LTR baseline**, outside this seat's axes; not in the registry (`grep command__group` returns nothing).
- **Mechanism**: reka's `ComboboxContentImpl` writes an inline `display: flex; flex-direction: column` (`reka-ui/dist/Combobox/ComboboxContentImpl.js:207`) on the list, which `CommandList.vue:41` bounds with `max-height: var(--overlay-max-block)` (`command/styles.css:81`). Each `.command__group` is `overflow: hidden` (`:94-95`), and a flex item whose overflow is not visible has an automatic minimum size of 0. The groups therefore shrink to fit the 320 px port instead of the list overflowing it, and the list, the only scroller, has nothing to scroll. The groups' own clip then hides the rows.
- **Probe** (`cmd.mjs`, `cmd2.mjs`, `cmd3.mjs`, `/containers/command` at 1280 LTR, at rest): the groups are 74, 110 and 110 px tall over 169+ px of rows; the list is **320/320**. Hidden: DESIGN.md, tokens.css, Switch branch, Install package, Publish release, Customize palette, Account settings, General settings. The keyboard highlight reaches them because reka's `scrollIntoView` scrolls the clipped group programmatically; a wheel or pointer cannot. The RTL sweep shows the same (`sweep-RTL/_containers_command.jpg`); capture `command-inline-group-clip.png`.
- **Born-RED**: on `/containers/command`, every group's `scrollHeight` equals its `clientHeight`, and the list scrolls when its rows exceed the bound.
