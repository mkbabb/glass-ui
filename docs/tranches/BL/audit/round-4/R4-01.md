## The proposed family

**F-78 (band F): interruption has no transition of its own.** Every stateful surface here is written as a pair of from-rest transitions: open and close, press and release, expand and collapse. An interruption (a reverse before the first transition lands, a `pointercancel`, a close request during a morph, a hold that ends while the pointer is still present) gets no transition of its own, so the machine maps it onto one of the pair. It either restarts from a fixed endpoint (the keyframe `from`, or the previous target), commits the interrupted gesture as if it had completed, drops it, or infers "the pointer left". So the settled state, or the path to it, depends on when the input arrived, not on what the surface was doing at that moment.

The library already has the correct shape in two places. The dock morph reverses by complementing both position and velocity (`useDockMorph.ts:73-81`, measured continuous: 130.3 → 143.2 → 144.1 → 142.3 px across the reversal frame, `dock-rev-warm.json`), and the Dialog's `useSpringMount` re-targets its spring from the current value (Escape at +40, 90, 150 and 220 ms of the enter reverses continuously, `dialog-seq.json`). The family is every other stateful surface, which lacks that shape.

Why it mints: no registered mechanism names reversal, cancel or overlap. F-16's layer-swap clause and R2-02-01's poisoned endpoint have a similar shape but are scoped to the dock extent, and W-DOCK-EXTENT cannot carry a reveal, disclosure, tab or drag cure. F-44 is the time authority (integrators, dt, `linear()` tails), not reversal. F-76 is the extent a motion owns, not its start value. F-43 is arrival ownership, and its reopen half is placed there (R4-01-07, -08). If the registrar prefers a narrower mint, members -01 to -04 (motion from a fixed endpoint, and a cancel committed) form the family, and -05 and -06 (the dock posture machine reading edges, not present state) can sit beside F-16 as a posture-machine row. The mechanism statement above covers all six.

**Wave shape (W-INTERRUPT)**: each surface takes its interrupt from the in-flight value. (1) The reveal register moves enter and exit to one engine that re-targets: either the spring mount kernel the Dialog already uses, or a transition in both directions with Presence gated on `transitionend`. (2) The disclosure does the same, with the size re-targeted from the current `block-size`. (3) The indicator deform reads the painted position, not `lastIdx`. (4) `pointercancel` springs back to the gesture's origin value and never commits. (5) The dock's click-away is never gated for a target outside the root. (6) `release()` re-reads present state (`root.matches(':hover')`, `:focus-within`) before scheduling a collapse.

Born-RED invariants, one per member:
- The largest per-frame step across a reversal is at most 2× the median step. At HEAD the Popover opacity jumps 0.71 and the Collapsible 179.6 px.
- A 0→3→1 retarget at +40 ms paints no wider than a direct 0→1 hop (HEAD 172.5 vs 139.6 px).
- A `touchCancel` mid-drag leaves `v-model` unchanged (HEAD Grid → Kanban).
- An outside click at +120 ms of an expand collapses the dock (HEAD stays pinned).
- 7 s after a keep-dock-open Popover closes under a resting pointer, the dock is still expanded (HEAD collapses at +4.4 s).

## Evidence

### R4-01-01—Reveal register: a close mid-enter jumps to the open pose, and a reopen mid-exit lands open in one frame (HIGH, NEW, proposed F-78)

- **Mechanism**: the enter is a transition from `@starting-style` (`src/styles/glass/reveal.css:136-160`). The exit is a keyframe animation bound on `[data-state="closed"]` (`reveal.css:167-199`), which reka's Presence needs because it awaits `animationend` (the docblock at `:180-199`). `@keyframes glass-reveal-out` starts `from { scale: 1 1; opacity: 1; filter: blur(0) }` (`src/styles/animations.css:157-178`). A close before the enter lands therefore restarts from the fully open pose. A reopen during the exit removes the animation, and the element lands at its open computed style in the next frame, with no enter.
- **Probe** (`overlay-seq.mjs`; `popover-seq.json`, `menu-seq.json`):
  - Popover (`/containers/popover`, Dimensions), Escape at +40 ms: opacity 0.286 → **1.000** and scale `0.99696 0.90575` → `1 1` in one frame, then the fade. At +90 ms: 0.506 → 1.000. At +160 ms: 0.706 → 1.000. On a 320×~330 px plate the y-scale step is about +30 px.
  - Popover reopened (Enter on the trigger) 80 ms into the exit: opacity **0.203 → 1.000** and scale y 0.933 → 1 in one frame. At 140 ms: 0.007 → 1.000.
  - DropdownMenu, Escape at +40 ms: opacity 0.513 → 1.000 and scale y 0.938 → 1 in one frame. At +90 ms: 0.838 → 1.000. Reopened 80 ms into the exit: 0.000 → 1.000.
  - The translate slide stays a transition and is continuous in every run (−6.75 → −7.36 px), so the discontinuity is exactly the keyframe's channels.
- **Members by source**: every `.glass-reveal` surface (`_shared/overlay/content.ts:53-60`): Popover, DropdownMenu and submenu, Select (`data-reveal="menu"`), Tooltip's exit, HoverCard, and Toast (the overlay register, `reveal.css:94-96`). Measured: Popover and DropdownMenu. The Tooltip exit plays the same keyframe (`tooltip-frames.json`), and its enter is R4-01-18.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-INTERRUPT (1).

### R4-01-02—Disclosure: a reversal jumps up to 179.6 px in one frame (HIGH, NEW, proposed F-78)

  - Reopen at +43 ms of the close: 153 → **0** (−153 px), held at 0 for 2 frames. At +94 ms: 79.4 → 0, held for 3 frames. The second capture gave 138, 49.4, 179.6 and 79.6 px.

### R4-01-03—Tab indicator: a retarget computes its deform from the previous target (MED, NEW, proposed F-78)

- **Mechanism**: `squishOnTravel` measures travel from `buttonRefs[lastIdx]`, where `lastIdx` is the previous target and not the painted position (`src/composables/motion/morph/useSelectionIndicator.ts:313-332`). It then writes `--stretch`, `--eyeglass-span` and `--eyeglass-swell` from that fraction (`:343-399`). A retarget mid-glide gets the deform of the long hop it never makes. The translate is a CSS transition and re-targets continuously.
- **Probe** (`tab-reverse.mjs`, `/navigation/tabs` first pill strip; `tab-reverse-r1.json`, `-r2.json`):
  - A direct 0→1 hop peaks at a painted width of 138.8 (and 139.6) px, span 1.6.
  - 0→3, then back to 1 at +40 ms (a shorter physical hop): span **1.94**, peak **172.1 (172.5) px**, which is 33 px (24%) wider.
  - 0→3, then back to 0 at +40 ms (about 13 px of physical travel): height swells to 41.2 px from 34.6 (+19%).
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-INTERRUPT (3).

### R4-01-04—`pointercancel` commits a drag as if released (MED, NEW, proposed F-78)

- **Mechanism**: `useDragMorph.ts:359-364` binds `pointercancel` to `onPointerUpCapture` (`:344-352`), which arms the commit-side `onSnap`. `useSpringMount.ts:246-247` maps `onPointercancel: releaseDrag`, which dismisses past the 30% threshold (`:220-232`; published through `composables/motion/index.ts:20`). `EasingPicker.vue:455` binds `@pointercancel="onUp"`. - **Probe** (`tabdrag-cancel.mjs`, CDP touch on the draggable pill, 112 px drag; `tabdrag-cancel.json`): release gives Grid → **Timeline**; `touchCancel` after the same drag gives Grid → **Kanban**. Expected: Grid. The event log shows `pointercancel:touch` delivered.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-INTERRUPT (4).

### R4-01-05—Dock: a click-away that lands during the morph is dropped (MED, NEW, proposed F-78)

- **Mechanism**: `onPointerDownOutside` returns early on `isTransitioning` (`src/components/dock/composables/useDockState.ts:368-379`, `:373`). The comment's rationale is about clicks inside the dock landing on the parent. The gate also swallows a genuine click outside the root. `isTransitioning` spans the morph plus its ~300 ms settle tail (F-16's R2-02-05).
- **Probe** (`dock-rev.mjs`, `dock-rev-warm.mjs`; `/dock/overview` "Starts compact"): click the collapsed pill, then click the page at +80, +160 or +260 ms (cold), or +120 ms (warm). In **5/5** runs the dock settles expanded and pinned (`E`, 227 px) with focus on BODY, and stays that way until another outside press (`dock-rev.json`, `dock-rev-warm.json`, `dock-rev-*-outside-*.png`).
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-INTERRUPT (5). Test the root first, and never gate a target outside it.

### R4-01-06—Dock: releasing a hold collapses the dock under a resting pointer (MED, NEW, proposed F-78)

- **Mechanism**: `release()` arms an 800 ms grace timer, then `scheduleCollapse()` (3600 ms), whenever the count reaches 0 in hover posture (`useDockState.ts:342-358`). It never checks whether the pointer or focus is still inside. The only thing that cancels it is an incidental `focusin` (`:301-308`).
- **Probe** (harness `?scene=dock`, `hdock-release.mjs`; `hdock-release.json`, `-r2.json`): hover-expand, open the keep-dock-open Popover, close it by clicking its trigger again, and leave the pointer on the trigger. The dock collapses at **+4.45 s** (twice), sliding the trigger out from under the pointer (`trigger-hovered=false`). Closing with Escape instead returns focus to the trigger, `focusin` clears the timer, and the dock stays expanded. The control, the pointer resting with no overlay, stays expanded for 8.5 s.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, W-INTERRUPT (6). The same `release()` path serves the Slider's grasp hold (`useDockHold.ts:121-136`), so a drag that ends over the dock collapses it 4.4 s later.

### R4-01-07—SegmentedTabs: every mount glides the indicator in from the strip origin (MED, EXTENDS F-43)

- **Mechanism**: the indicator's rest style before the first measure is `translate: 0px 0px; width: 0px; opacity: 0` (`useSelectionIndicator.ts:163-170`). The first measure (`onMounted` → `nextTick(updateSliders)`, `:462-465`) writes the real box, and `.segmented-indicator--js` transitions `translate, width, height, …, opacity` (`src/components/tabs/styles/segmented.css:250-262`). The mount therefore plays the travel glide from the strip's corner while fading in. The arrival is owned by the travel engine, which is F-43's "one entrance per mount" clause.
- **Probe** (`tabs-entry.mjs`, `tabs-glide.mjs`; `tabs-entry.json`, `tabs-mount-glide-strip7.json`): on `/navigation/tabs` (a hard load and a push), all 10 strips start at the origin. Strip 7 reads −189.8 px at opacity 0, then **−83.4 px at opacity 0.71** and −46 px at 0.87, landing after ~130 ms. The configurator's PresetEditor strips show the same glide at 60 ms (`cfg-tabs-settled.png`; −85.9 px at opacity 0.50).
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-ARRIVAL. The first measure is written with transitions suppressed (seated, not travelled). Born-RED: 0 frames with the indicator visible (opacity > 0.05) and more than 1 px off the active tab after mount.

### R4-01-08—UIA-V-50 at HEAD: the indicator is measured inside the host's entrance and never re-measured (HIGH, EXTENDS F-43)

- **Mechanism**: `updateSingleSlider` reads `getBoundingClientRect()` for the container and the button (`useSelectionIndicator.ts:223-238`), which is transformed space. When the strip mounts inside a host whose entrance transform is live (the reveal's squish, the Dialog's bloom), that transform's scale is baked into `width`, `height` and `translate`. The only re-measure triggers are the model, the options and a `ResizeObserver` on the container (`:426-465`). The transform ending changes none of them, so the scaled values persist at rest. O-59's confirm note found the consumer's error anisotropic: height and y wrong, width right. That is the reveal squish's signature (`scale: 0.9964 0.8883`).
- **Probe** (harness `?scene=overlays&nocmd=1`, `htabs.mjs`; `htabs.json`, `htabs-*.png`), measured 1.5 s after open. The inline style is identical at +40 ms and at rest, so the value was never re-measured.
  - SegmentedTabs in a Popover: indicator **30.7 px tall** in a 34.6 px button (−3.9 px, ×0.888), width −0.4 px. At 390: 23.3 vs 26.3 px.
  - In a Dialog (uniform 0.95 bloom): **7.1 px narrow** and **7.2 px left** of the active tab, and 1.6 px short.
  - The shell configurator (a translate-only entrance) settles correctly, as translation cancels in a difference.
- **Relation to O-59**: UIA-V-50 is confirmed by mechanism at HEAD (value.js measured 21 px low and 48 px tall; the sign and size depend on the consumer's entrance). The row's claim that "a pure translate cancels" holds, and scale is the live channel.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-ARRIVAL. Measure in layout space (`offsetLeft`/`offsetTop`/`offsetWidth`/`offsetHeight` relative to the container), or re-measure on the host's `transitionend`/`animationend`. Born-RED: a SegmentedTabs inside Popover and Dialog has its indicator within 0.5 px of the active tab at rest (HEAD −3.9 and −7.1 px).

### R4-01-09—A reopen inside the exit window never moves focus into the reopened content (HIGH, EXTENDS F-38)

- **Mechanism**: reka's open focus is a mount side-effect (FocusScope auto-focus on mount). The library holds the content mounted through its exit (the reveal keyframe for menus and popovers, `reveal.css:167-199`; the spring hold for Dialog and Sheet, `DialogContent.vue:106-108`). A reopen inside that window reuses the mounted node, so no open focus runs. This is F-38's lifecycle defect with the direction reversed: focus is keyed to mount and unmount, not to the logical open and close.
- **Probe** (`overlay-seq.mjs`; `menu-seq.json`, `popover-seq.json`):
  - DropdownMenu reopened by Enter 30 or 80 ms into its exit settles **open and modal** (`body` `pointer-events: none; overflow: hidden`, 9 `data-aria-hidden` nodes), with **focus on the trigger**, which sits in the hidden page. Reopened after the exit ends (140 ms), focus goes to the first item as on a fresh open.
  - Popover reopened 30, 80 or 140 ms into the exit keeps focus on the trigger. A fresh open focuses `INPUT#w`.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-FOCUS-LIFECYCLE. The open focus runs on the logical open edge. Born-RED: open, Escape, and Enter at +30 ms leaves `activeElement` inside the content.

### R4-01-10—Double-clicking a menu trigger strands focus on BODY under an open modal menu (HIGH, EXTENDS F-38)

- **Mechanism**: the first press opens the modal menu and focuses its content. The menu sets `body { pointer-events: none }`, so the second press lands on `HTML`, which blurs the content to BODY. F-38 already records the trapped FocusScope's exemption for `relatedTarget === null` (`FocusScope.js:57-61`), and here it lets focus leave an open modal layer. The menu stays open.
- **Probe** (`menu-dbl.mjs`; `menu-dblclick.json`, 3/3 runs): `pointerdown menu-trigger` → `focusin menu-content` (+32 ms) → `pointerdown HTML` → final `state: open`, `activeElement: BODY`. ArrowDown then highlights nothing, and focus stays on BODY. Escape still closes it through the window listener. The Popover double-click toggles closed, which is the expected outcome.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-FOCUS-LIFECYCLE. Born-RED: after `dblclick` on a menu trigger, the menu is closed, or `activeElement` is inside it.

### R4-01-12—An inline Command inside a Dialog swallows Escape (HIGH, EXTENDS F-37)

- **Mechanism**: `CommandList.vue:34-45` renders reka's `ComboboxContent`. That wraps a `DismissableLayer` (`reka-ui/dist/Combobox/ComboboxContentImpl.js:181`), which is present from mount, and it calls `useHideOthers` (`:142`), which is F-37's mechanism. Mounted inside the Dialog, it is the highest layer. reka's Escape handler fires only for the highest layer (`DismissableLayer.js:77-82`), so every Escape dismisses the list, a no-op, and never the Dialog.
- **Probe** (harness; `escape-dialog-command.json`): layers `dialog-content:open`, `command-list:closed`. After two Escapes the Dialog is **still open**, with 11 `data-aria-hidden` nodes. The same Dialog without the Command closes on the first Escape.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-COMMAND. The non-modal listbox root F-37 already calls for also removes the dismissable layer. Born-RED: Escape closes a Dialog that hosts an inline Command.

### R4-01-13—UIA-KF-015 confirmed: the global Escape shortcut fires with every overlay dismissal (HIGH, EXTENDS F-22)

- **Mechanism**: Escape has two dispatchers and no shared stack. The shortcut registry listens on `window` `keydown` (`src/composables/keyboard/useKeyboardShortcuts.ts:351`). It is registered at the app's first use, before any overlay mounts, and it exempts Escape from the modal barrier (`:300-308`). reka's DismissableLayer also listens on `window` (`onKeyStroke("Escape")`, `DismissableLayer.js:77`), but registers later. The registry therefore fires first, sees `defaultPrevented` false (`:298`) and runs the app's Escape, and then reka dismisses the top layer. The docblock claims the registry "MATCH[es] the reka DismissableLayer stack" (`:311-316`). No overlay registers its Escape in the registry, so the stacks never meet.
- **Probe** (harness, `registerShortcut("escape", …)`; `escape-dispatch.json`): each of these fires the global handler once — Escape closing a page Select, a Popover or a dock DropdownMenu; Escape on a Dialog; and Escape closing only the inner Select or inner Popover of an open modal Dialog. The only exception is Escape typed in the Command input, which is filtered as editable.
- **Why F-22**: F-22 is the overlay seam carrying no stacking contract (host-relative z, edge clearance). The dismissal stack is the same contract on the key axis, and the cure lands in the same seam (`overlayContentAttrs` / `useDockParticipation` register the open layer). A second home would be F-42.
- **Disposition**: BUILD. Open overlays enter the registry's Escape stack (register on open, release on close) or `preventDefault` after dismissing. Born-RED: the global Escape count stays 0 across those six dismissals (HEAD 1 each).

This is O-59 area 4's "hover and keyboard highlight light two rows", measured at HEAD.

### R4-01-15—UIA-F-80 at HEAD: a coarse tap reaches the dock's hover posture (HIGH, EXTENDS F-77)

- **Mechanism**: the dock's posture handlers are modality-blind. On touch, a tap on the collapsed dock produces a compat `mouseenter` (→ `onMouseEnter`, the 60 ms intent) and focuses the control under the finger (→ `onFocusIn`, the keyboard-parity path that goes straight to `hover`) (`GlassDock.vue:347-350`; `useDockState.ts:251-273, 301-308`). The tap-to-pin path (`onClickCollapsed` on the summary, `GlassDock.vue:490`) is not reached. When the consumer authors no `#collapsed` slot, the summary "Expand dock" is **0×44 px**, so the only tap target is the persistent control. That tap also activates the control. This is F-77's mechanism on the script side: a fine-pointer state entered from a coarse pointer.
- **Probe** (`dock-touch.mjs`; `dock-touch.json`), `/dock/overview` "Starts compact" at 390×844 @3: summary rect `[217,534,0,44]`, persistent Home `[173,534,44,44]`. The tap delivers `pointerdown:Home → mouseenter ×4 → focusin:Home → click:Home`, the posture becomes `E` (not pinned) at +51 ms, and Home's click fires. The harness (`hdock-touch-*.png`) reproduces it with "Edit contour", the persistent control O-59 names.
- **Also measured**: UIA-F-10 and UIA-F-11 are **not reproduced** at HEAD. With a DropdownMenu, a Select and a keep-dock-open Popover open in a collapsible dock, the dock held for 6.5 s on touch and on a fine pointer (`hdock.json`). Two docks' holds stay independent (`hdock-two.json`).
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-HOVER-GUARD's script half. On `(hover: none)` the posture reads the tap (a pointerdown with `pointerType` touch or pen pins), and `focusin` from a pointer press does not enter hover. A dock with a `#persistent` slot and no `#collapsed` slot gets a real expand target. Born-RED at P: a tap on the collapsed dock pins it and does not activate the persistent control (HEAD: hover posture, and Home fired).

### R4-01-16—Holding Enter on an opener activates the control focus moved to (MED, EXTENDS F-42)

- **Mechanism**: the opener moves focus to an activatable control (the Dialog auto-focuses its Close ✕; the menu focuses its first item), and nothing distinguishes `event.repeat`. reka's `MenuItem.js:62-66` clicks on any selection-key `keydown`, and a native button clicks on a repeated Enter.
- **Probe** (`dialog-seq.mjs`, `overlay-seq.mjs`: keydown Enter, then repeats at 33 ms after a 500 ms hold):
  - Dialog: opens (2002 ms), then closes **42 ms** later through the auto-focused Close; it settles closed (`dialog-enter-held.png`).
  - DropdownMenu: open → item selected, closed → reopened → selected, closed → reopened, oscillating while held, with **2 item activations** in 10 repeats (`menu-seq.json` `enter-held`).
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in the a11y sweep. Activation ignores `event.repeat` at the item and close seams. Born-RED: a held Enter on a Dialog trigger settles open, and on a menu trigger fires 0 items.

### R4-01-17—UIA-V-55 (limit) and V-192 at HEAD: the WebGPU field fails where WebGL2 renders, and never re-arms (HIGH, EXTENDS F-47)

- **Mechanism**: the shared device is requested with no `requiredLimits` (`grep -rn requiredLimits src` returns 0 hits; `useWebGPUCanvas.ts:137-163`), so `maxTextureDimension2D` is the default 8192, though this adapter offers 16384. No backing-store sizing reads `device.limits` (`grep -rn maxTextureDimension2D src` returns 0 hits; the only clamp is the DPR, `aurora/constants/budget.ts:11,25`). A field taller than 8192 / DPR fails configure validation, and the lifecycle parks without a re-arm edge. When the host later fits, the canvas resizes and the loop stays dead, showing the static ground. This is F-47's capability-parity clause (the WebGL2 arm on the same GPU renders the size) plus its masking-fallback clause (the static ground hides the dead primary).
- **Probe** (harness `?scene=aurora`; `haurora.json`, `haurora2.json`):
  - Host 1392×9000 @1: the console warns `Texture size ([Extent3D width:1392, height:9000]) exceeded maximum texture size ([Extent3D width:8192, height:8192…])`, `onInitError` fires twice (`GPUValidationError`), and rAF is **0/s**.
  - With `navigator.gpu` stubbed out (WebGL2): the same 1392×9000 renders at **120 rAF/s**.
  - The host shrunk to 600 px: canvas 1392×600, rAF **0/s**, no new errors. After a 10-step resize storm across the limit: still 0.
  - The screenshot pair `haurora-h600-dpr1.png` (live painterly field) vs `haurora-h9000-dpr1-then-shrink.png` (static gradient ground).
- **Not reproduced at HEAD**: UIA-V-55's "retries init every frame" and UIA-V-337's "about 2000 warnings in 5 s". HEAD parks after 2 errors, with 0 messages in the following 2 s.
- **Disposition**: BUILD, in W-ONE-ENGINE. Request `requiredLimits.maxTextureDimension2D: adapter.limits.maxTextureDimension2D`, clamp the backing store to `device.limits` (scale the DPR down, never fail), and re-arm on the next size that fits. Born-RED: a 9000 px host renders live, and a shrink after a failure re-arms (HEAD 0 rAF/s).

### R4-01-18—Tooltip never enters (MED, EXTENDS F-70)

- **Mechanism**: the reveal entrance (`@starting-style`) sits inside `.glass-reveal[data-state="open"]` (`reveal.css:136-160`). reka's Tooltip emits `data-state="delayed-open"` or `"instant-open"`, and `grep -rn "delayed-open\|instant-open" src` returns 0 hits. The hint therefore matches neither the open rule nor the closed rule while shown, and appears at opacity 1 in one frame. On close it gets `closed` and plays the exit keyframe (R4-01-01). This is a binding to the primitive's state vocabulary that no-ops silently, F-70's class; the consequence (an arrival with no owner) is F-43's.
- **Probe** (`tt3.mjs`; `tooltip-frames.json`): the first content frame reads `delayed-open | opacity 1 | scale none | animation none`. The exit runs over 15 frames (`closed`, opacity 1 → 0).
- **Disposition**: BUILD. Key the recipe on `:is([data-state="open"], [data-state="delayed-open"], [data-state="instant-open"])`, with a test that asserts each overlay's emitted states against the recipe's selectors.

### R4-01-19—Two Toasters render every toast twice (LOW, EXTENDS F-41)

- **Mechanism**: the toast store is module-global (`toast()`), and every `<Toaster>` renders the whole store, so no single viewport owns a toast.
- **Probe** (harness `?scene=toasters&toasters=2`; `htoasters.json`, `htoast-2.png`): one `toast()` yields 2 `OL` viewports with 1 toast each, 2 `role=status/alert` nodes and 6 live regions (3 with one Toaster). The two plates stack exactly, and the doubled veil paints a darker plate. F-41's double announcement becomes four.
- **Disposition**: FOLD into W-TOAST: one viewport owner per store, or a dev warning on a second mount.
