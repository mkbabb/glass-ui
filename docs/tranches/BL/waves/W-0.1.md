## 3 · The cure

D3 owns the design. This wave builds the part of D3's converged specification that is **minor-lawful**, and nothing else:

1. **The light veil at the token.** The light arm of the veil (the ink `--glass-veil-ink`, the base and step at `tokens/glass.css:34,45-52`, and whatever pole or α rungs D3's converged family names) takes the converged values, so every light `.glass-*` level composites as a light frost over paper. The law comment at `:29-34` is rewritten to state the new law; the owner revised the old one (`FORMATION-PROGRESS.md:48`). The dark arm (`tokens/dark-arm.css:278-279`) is not touched in this wave.
2. **One field surface.** A filled `SelectTrigger` and a filled `Input` reach paint through one composite, in both themes, so that the trigger sits on the Input's field surface. Which element changes (the trigger composing the field register, or `.control-surface` dropping its floor layer) is the converged spec's call. The gate below does not depend on that choice. ToggleGroupItem also composes `.control-surface` (`toggle-group/ToggleGroupItem.vue:84`), so the spec states what the toggle paints afterwards and W-0.2 is measured on it.
3. **The menu row off the veil-ink plate.** `--menu-row-bg` (`_shared/menu/menu.css:10`) stops reading `--glass-plate-quiet`. The light highlighted row is carried by an accent tint or accent ink, never by a darkening of the plate (O-66 §1's ask).

**What "minor-lawful" admits.** Token values, one element's composite recipe, and a rewritten comment. No token, class, utility, prop or export is removed or renamed, and the `.published-roster` diff is empty (`scripts/verify-export-types.mjs:344-366` pins the names). Any part of D3's converged answer that needs a removal is not built here: the dark ladder, the level-0 escape, the transmissive well, the retirement of `--input-on-glass`, the sampler deletion (M-5), and `contain: paint` (M-7) land in W-H.1 or W-H.2, with their MIGRATION rows there. The converged spec's "minor split" table names each row's wave. A row the spec leaves unassigned is W-H.2's by default.

**The entry gate (the loop's convergence).** D3 meets the charter's criterion (`CHARTER.md:82,86`): at least three full passes, zero enumerated open gaps, survival of a fresh adversarial audit by seats that did not author the winning spec, and two consecutive clean passes. Its battery must also be met (PLAN §4, `design/material/PORTFOLIO.md:631-670`): M-1..M-8 green in both themes over paper, the aurora at ceilings 1 and 0.5 and the hostile grounds, plus the converged family's own witness (one of W-A..W-F), plus the O-66 §1 witness in paint.
