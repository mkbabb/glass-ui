## 2 · The state at HEAD

**The size holds.** `.card-title` computes to body × 1.272 at the clamp floor, the fluid arm and the ceiling (fix-2's probe, scene `card`):

| viewport | body px | `.card-title` px | body × 1.272 | engine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320 | 16 | 20.352 | 20.352 | Chromium and Playwright WebKit |
| 1440 | 18.608 | 23.669 | 23.669 | both |
| 2560 | 21.632 | 27.516 | 27.516 | both |

**The name collides.** `--type-title` is the φ^(3/2) display rung, `2.058rem` (32.928 px in both engines), declared at `typography/scale.css:121` and read by `typography/semantic.css:148`, `theme/bridges.css:37` (`--text-title`), `metric/styles.css:110` and `tokens/manifest.ts:27`, and by three consumers: slides (`src/styles/deck.css`, `src/styles/deck-theme.css`, `src/decks/feedback-coder/theme.css`), speedtest (`SpeedtestResults.vue`) and keyframes.js (`EditorStartScreen.vue`). Redefining it as body × 1.272 moves every one of those readers from 32.928 px to 23.669 px at 1440 (9.26 px) in a minor. That is a value break the ruling did not intend (it rules "the size is unchanged"), so the token takes the distinct name `--type-card-title` (§1).

## 3 · The cure

1. `typography/scale.css` declares the rung beside the body ladder: `--type-card-title: calc(var(--type-body) * 1.272)`.
2. `card/styles.css:217` reads `font-size: var(--type-card-title)`. The comment at `:210-214` states the rung's ladder in one sentence.
3. `tokens/manifest.ts` lists the new token.

`--type-title` keeps its name and value; no rung is renamed in 10.2.0 or 11.0.0.
