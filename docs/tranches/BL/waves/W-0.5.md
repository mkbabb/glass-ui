## 3 · The cure

The 7.0.0 section takes two entries, placed before the table that holds the `PaperBackdrop` row. The text below is the wave's text. G1 checks it word for word, so it is written to land as it stands:

_`--paper-aged-texture` is removed_

The SVG-noise "aged" paper token `--paper-aged-texture`, and the `frequency="aged"` value of `<PaperBackdrop>` that read it, left in this cut. The SVG-noise look has no successor, by owner ruling. The warm paper register is the raster `--paper-grain-tooth`, with the optional `--paper-grain-relief` layer (`styles/paper.css`). _`Button` `variant` splits into `emphasis` and `tone`_

`variant` is gone. `emphasis` sets visual priority (`"primary" | "secondary" | "quiet" | "text"`, default `"secondary"`), and `tone` sets intent (`"neutral" | "destructive"`, default `"neutral"`). Each row below was judged in paint: the successor is the emphasis whose rest plate, shadow and ink sit nearest the old variant's in light theme. Dark theme does not separate `primary` from `secondary` at rest.

**How the table was judged (G2).** A Button specimen was built twice with the band-0 build pattern. The first build used v6.0.0's `src`, extracted with `git archive v6.0.0 src` into the scratch worktree. v6 imports `class-variance-authority`, `clsx` and `tailwind-merge`, which are no longer in the repo's `node_modules`, so they were fetched with `npm pack` into the probe's own `extra/` directory. The second build used HEAD's `src`, extracted the same way. The v6 build renders the 11 v6 variants and the HEAD build renders the four emphases at `tone="neutral"`. Each was measured in Chromium at DPR 1, in light and dark: the plate strip, the shadow band under the button against the page, and the glyph-core ink. The successor is the emphasis with the least structure score (plate ΔE_OK plus the difference in shadow strength), with the ink ΔE_OK breaking any tie within 0.005. Tone is intent, so paint never chooses it. Results at `30d6d12a`:

At rest in v6, every glass variant painted the same plate (`#f2eee8` over paper `#fbfaf8`, shadow 0.0571 in light). That is why `accent`, `primary-audacious` and `gold-audacious` map to `secondary` and not to `primary`, where atlas guessed `primary`. The atlas guess and the paint agree on `ghost`, `glass` and `outline`, and disagree on `glass-wash` and `accent`. Dark's primary and secondary plates (`#1b140b`, `#1a140c`) are the same plate for the purposes of this table. That is the dark-ladder separability defect (M-6), which is W-H.2's, and the table does not wait on it.

`solid` and `destructive` are not in v6's map, so they are not paint-judged. `solid` was the opaque primary fill (its 4.1.0 row), and the nearest successor is the heavier plate, `primary`. `destructive` keeps its 5.0.0 ruling, `tone="destructive"`, over the default emphasis.
