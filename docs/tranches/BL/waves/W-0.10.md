## 2 · The defect at HEAD

`vue-tsc --noEmit` with `vueCompilerOptions.strictTemplates: true` over a probe SFC that binds `:reference` on both contents, resolving `@glass/*` to the worktree's `src` (the repo's own path alias, `tsconfig.json:17-19`):

```vue
<Popover><PopoverTrigger>t</PopoverTrigger><PopoverContent :reference="reference">p</PopoverContent></Popover>
<TooltipProvider><Tooltip><TooltipTrigger>t</TooltipTrigger><TooltipContent :reference="reference">h</TooltipContent></Tooltip></TooltipProvider>
```

At `183e021a`: **exit 2**, `Probe.vue(7,61) TS2353 … 'reference' does not exist` and `Probe.vue(8,78) TS2353 … 'reference' does not exist`. The 10.1.0 tarball's `dist/components/popover/PopoverContent.vue.d.ts` and `tooltip/TooltipContent.vue.d.ts` carry 0 lines naming `reference`. At runtime the attribute falls through untyped: W-L.1's B3 probe seated a virtual reference at (700, 400) with the content top at 414 and centre x 705 (`W-L.1.md` §2), so paint works and the declaration is the defect. `PopoverTrigger.vue:25` and `TooltipTrigger.vue:21` strip a `reference` attribute from the trigger's fallthrough, which stays.

## 3 · The cure

`PopoverContent` and `TooltipContent` declare `reference?: ReferenceElement` (reka's type) and pass it to `PopperContent`. No `PopoverAnchor` part is added: one door for the anchor (E-1). The READMEs of both components show the virtual-reference form.
