## Scope

Two distinct substrate areas:

1. **Dock edge-fade retire** (`099d51e`) — remove the `mask-image` linear-gradient scroll-affordance feather from horizontal `.dock-layers` and vertical `.glass-dock.vertical`. The feather was a leftover from the `overflow-x/y: auto` era; after Z.W2.T2 made docks grow-to-fit, the content is never clipped, so the mask only damaged the first and last dock items with a transparent ramp.

2. **Timeline continuous-variant rewrite** (`3cb70db`) — replace per-region gradient with a single rail-spanning stitched gradient (each phase's colour is a stop anchored at its weight-centre; regions window into the gradient via `background-size` / `background-position-x`). Fix terminus rounding (anchored to first/last region only; interior regions stay square). Upgrade the continuous-dot to a glass primitive (translucent tinted fill + backdrop blur + hairline ring) with CSS-custom-property knobs.

3. **Dock-layer hit-test fix** (`beec35e` dock portion) — `.dock-layer-item-host` and `.dock-layer` inactive state gains `visibility: hidden` with a delayed transition, keeping the layout box in flow for FLIP width measurement while removing inactive layers from the paint + hit-test tree.

`beec35e` is a dual-scope commit (`fix(toggle,dock)`) that straddles W2 and W3. Its dock.css hunk is attributable to W2; its toggle CVA hunk is attributable to W3.
